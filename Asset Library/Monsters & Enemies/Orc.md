Orcs are warriors first, last, and always. They do not farm, they do not trade, they do not negotiate from a position of weakness. Orc culture is built around the One-Eyed — the one-eyed god of slaughter and conquest — and his demands are simple: fight, kill, take, survive. Orcs who cannot do these things are cast out or killed. Orcs who do them exceptionally well become warchief material.

What makes orcs dangerous at even low tier is their frightening resilience. Relentless Endurance turns one fight-ending blow into a speed bump. An orc at 0 HP that drops to 1, then burns its action on an attack, has traded your big hit for a free attack before going down properly. In a mob, this means the "last orc standing" moment keeps getting deferred. Parties that try to focus-fire a single orc down will often find it refuses to die at the worst moment.

The Blind Prophet is a priestly warrior-caste — orcs who have torn out their own right eye in emulation of their god and received divine power in return. They are more dangerous than regular orcs not because of their spells, but because their presence makes *every other orc* more effective and more savage.

### Campaign Integration: Investigation & Clues

Orc raids leave a specific signature that experienced frontier rangers recognize instantly.

- **The Brazier and the Banner:** Orcs mark conquered territory with fire and a planted weapon — a spear driven into the ground, sometimes bearing a severed head. Finding a planted weapon means an orc warband considers this area theirs.
- **The Speed of the Raid:** Orcs don't linger. A location that shows signs of rapid, violent entry, total destruction of anything edible or wearable, and rapid departure suggests an orc raid rather than an occupation. If the orcs are still present in strength, something unusual is keeping them here.
- **The Eye's Mark:** Victims of a Blind Prophet's presence show unusual wounds — curse effects, magical burns, or injuries consistent with a *bane* or *bless* effect. A DC 13 Religion check confirms divine magic was involved. A Blind Prophet is running this warband.

### 1. Orc Warrior

The rank-and-file of any orc warband — brutally strong, toughened by constant warfare, and possessed of a terrifying last-ditch survival instinct.

_Medium Humanoid (Orc), Chaotic Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 13 (hide armor)
- **Hit Points:** 15 (2d8 + 6)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 16 (+3) | **DEX:** 12 (+1) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 7 (−2) | **WIS:** 11 (+0) | **CHA:** 10 (+0)
- **Skills:** Intimidation +2
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common, Orc
- **Challenge:** 1/2 (100 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Aggressive:** As a Bonus Action, the orc can move up to its speed toward a hostile creature it can see.
- **Relentless Endurance (1/Day):** When the orc is reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, the orc drops to 1 hit point instead.

**Actions**

- **Greataxe:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 9 (1d12 + 3) slashing damage.
- **Javelin:** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.

### 2. Orc Berserker

A warrior who has fully surrendered to the One-Eyed's battle-fury — entering a frenzied state that makes them virtually unstoppable for short bursts. The berserk orc ignores pain, exhaustion, and self-preservation entirely.

_Medium Humanoid (Orc), Chaotic Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 13 (hide armor)
- **Hit Points:** 28 (3d8 + 15)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **STR:** 18 (+4) | **DEX:** 12 (+1) | **CON:** 20 (+5) | **INT:** 7 (−2) | **WIS:** 8 (−1) | **CHA:** 9 (−1)
- **Skills:** Intimidation +3
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9
- **Languages:** Common, Orc
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Aggressive:** As a Bonus Action, the orc can move up to its speed toward a hostile creature it can see.
- **Relentless Endurance (1/Day):** When the orc is reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, the orc drops to 1 hit point instead.
- **Reckless:** At the start of its turn, the berserker can gain Advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls during that turn, but attack rolls against it also have Advantage until the start of its next turn.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The berserker makes two Greataxe attacks.
- **Greataxe:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 10 (1d12 + 4) slashing damage.

### 3. Orc Blind Prophet

A consecrated warrior-priest who has sacrificed their right eye to the One-Eyed in exchange for divine power. The Blind Prophet transforms a warband from a mob into a holy army.

_Medium Humanoid (Orc), Chaotic Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 16 (chain mail)
- **Hit Points:** 45 (6d8 + 18)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 16 (+3) | **DEX:** 12 (+1) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 9 (−1) | **WIS:** 13 (+1) | **CHA:** 12 (+1)
- **Skills:** Intimidation +3, Religion +1
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** Common, Orc
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Aggressive:** As a Bonus Action, the Prophet can move up to its speed toward a hostile creature it can see.
- **God's Fury:** When the Prophet hits a creature with a weapon attack, it can deal an extra 4 (1d8) necrotic damage.
- **Relentless Endurance (1/Day):** When the Prophet is reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, it drops to 1 hit point instead.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The Prophet makes two Spear attacks.
- **Spear:** _Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage, or 7 (1d8 + 3) if used with two hands in melee.
- **Spellcasting:** The Prophet is a 3rd-level spellcaster (Wisdom is its spellcasting ability, DC 11). It has the following spells prepared:
  - Cantrips (at will): *guidance*, *resistance*, *thaumaturgy*
  - 1st level (4 slots): *bane*, *bless*, *cure wounds*, *inflict wounds*
  - 2nd level (2 slots): *hold person*, *spiritual weapon*

### d10 The Raided Ground (Investigation & Clues)

| d10 | Discovery | Investigative Hook |
|:----|:----------|:------------------|
| **1** | **The Planted Spear** | A spear has been driven into the center of the room, wrapped in a strip of animal hide bearing the One-Eyed's mark. This territory has been formally claimed. A warband will return to check on their claim. |
| **2** | **The Battle Map** | Scratched into the dirt floor is a crude tactical map of the dungeon — with the party's *current location* marked. The orcs had advance intelligence about the layout. |
| **3** | **The Eye's Altar** | A rough stone altar covered in dried blood and one-eyed symbols. The Blind Prophet performed a sacrifice here to receive divine guidance. A DC 13 Religion check determines what the ritual was asking for — answers about a specific location, enemy, or timing. |
| **4** | **The Tally Wall** | Hundreds of notches cut into a stone wall. A DC 12 History check estimates the warband has been staging out of this location for at least three months. |
| **5** | **The Survivor Account** | A barely-alive victim can describe the warband's size, equipment, and direction of travel after the raid. They also mention one orc that stood apart — pale-skinned, quiet, and giving orders to the others. Not a typical orc. |
| **6** | **The Abandoned Gear** | Quality weapons and armor left behind — not discarded in retreat, but *piled* carefully. The orcs left their heavy equipment here to move fast. They're traveling light and coming back. |
| **7** | **The Branded Prisoner** | A captive bears an orc brand — a crude mark burned into their arm. The brand is a clan insignia, and a DC 14 History check identifies which clan. | **8** | **The Tribute Collection** | A pile of specific items in the corner — not random loot, but a careful selection: iron tools, grain sacks, two horses' worth of saddlebags. This warband was collecting specific materials for a larger operation. |
| **9** | **The Message Stone** | A flat stone with a message scratched in Orc runes. A DC 12 Languages or party member with Orc translates it: "Site confirmed. Send the Prophet's battalion by the new moon." Someone is organizing multiple warbands. |
| **10** | **The Half-Orc Witness** | A half-orc slave or prisoner in the camp who was taken in a previous raid.
