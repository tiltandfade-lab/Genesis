## Lanes (stop-and-report after each)

1. **Cave morphology (geological).** Karst show caves — chambers, breakdown piles,
   flowstone/columns, sinkhole entrances · lava tubes — smooth bores, collapsed skylights
   · sea caves — tidal floors, siphons · ice caves. Extract: mass grammar (how chambers
   actually shape and connect), floor/ceiling relations, natural light events, water
   relations. Deliverable: annotated contact sheets + a cave-mass-grammar note.
2. **Animal architecture.** Badger sett cross-sections · bear dens · fox earths · raptor
   eyries · beaver lodges (the underwater entrance — the flooded-siphon analogue) ·
   termite/ant colony casts. Extract: network topology and chamber specialization —
   nest, larder, latrine, **bolt-holes** (natural secret exits; the W4 secrets tie) —
   entrance strategy, chamber-count ranges. Deliverable: warren-topology typology sheet.
3. **Adopted voids.** Abandoned mines and quarry galleries — timbering going feral,
   collapse, flooding. The dug-by-someone-else origin, the uninhabited-lair case, and
   the mishap-entry fiction (falling through into old workings). Extract: how built
   geometry decays toward natural; where the mine↔cave hybrid boundary reads.
4. **FFT cave cohort (the local corpus).** From the five-angle corpus — now in-repo at
   `Reference/FFT-Guard-Post-Study/study-archive/maps/five-angle/` (moved 2026-07-24;
   121 maps × 5 views, files `<map>_<view>.gif`) — identify the underground / cave /
   rock-interior subset and run the guard-post comparison method: matched views,
   relations induced, never layouts copied. Extract: how FFT fakes cave irregularity in
   low-poly · ceiling treatment under the fixed camera · mouth/daylight handling ·
   ledge/rim usage · water placement · anti-rules. Deliverable: the lair's ten-rule
   candidate grammar, guard-post format.
5. **Touchstones.** BG3 owlbear cave + Underdark entries (the confirmed deck touchstone)
   · Diablo 1 darkness / torch-radius presentation (the confirmed causal-light
   touchstone). Extract: what precisely makes each work at gameplay scale — light
   gradient, prize placement, the route around the rim.
