| **d12** | **The Immediate Situation**                                                                                 |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1       | Sentinels have tracked your approach for miles and are waiting for you to cross the threshold.              |
| 2       | You arrive amidst a chaotic event (a brawl, a delivery, a frantic repair) and are completely ignored.       |
| 3       | The entrance is barred and silent; it takes significant effort just to get someone to acknowledge you.      |
| 4       | You spot a patrol or lookout before they see you, giving you the choice of how to reveal yourself.          |
| 5       | The entrance is entirely unmanned and open, leaving the interior feeling strangely vulnerable.              |
| 6       | A local is waiting specifically for a traveler like you and offers a guarded but necessary piece of advice. |
| 7       | You arrive just as a major social or religious rite is beginning, forcing you to participate or wait.       |
| 8       | You find a discarded or stashed supply cache near the entrance containing a vital curative or tool.         |
| 9       | A friendly NPC mistakes you for someone else and hands you a useful item or a letter of passage.            |
| 10      | The entrance is blocked by a recent accident or debris, requiring a physical test to finally enter.         |
| 11      | You find a coin-purse or a small trinket of value dropped in the dirt right at the finish line.             |
| 12      | You are greeted as an honored guest and offered immediate rest, a hot meal, and a minor boon.               |
