# The Spice Ruler — the entry bar per band

**The diagnosis it fixes: BAND INFLATION** — content wearing a band one hotter than it earns
(a faction recruiter is civic LIFE, not Textured; long shadows are ATMOSPHERE, not Strange).
Voice anchors calibrated register but not temperature; this ruler is the thermometer. **A row
enters a band only if it clears the bar. When in doubt, file DOWN — the tail must be earned.**

| Band | The reader's reaction | The entry bar | Calibration rows (the floor of the band) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GROUNDED** | *"huh."* | Life. Explicable, ordinary, no eyebrow raised. | *The inn changed hands.* · *A recruiter has set up a permanent table by the gate.* |
| **TEXTURED** | *"oh? tell me more."* | A STORY is visibly underway — stakes, power, motive, a question with teeth. Fully natural. A traveler would mention it in a letter home. | *The garrison hanged three deserters at dawn; nobody will say which army they deserted from.* · *The tithe doubled the week the old priest stopped appearing in public, and his handwriting on the notices has changed.* |
| **STRANGE** | *"wait — WHAT?"* | The natural explanation has RUN OUT. One impossible thing, contained, undeniable — a priest is consulted. Mood is never enough; the impossibility must be checkable. | *The well's water went mineral-sharp overnight and the oldest dogs won't drink it; the dowser left without her fee.* · *Every clock in one household keeps a different, private time — consistent inside, wrong to everyone else.* |
| **VOLATILE** | *"oh no. What do we do NOW?"* | The wrongness or violence is LOOSE and MOVING. People are in danger tonight; the place has visibly reorganized around it. | *A street is gone — burned or bought and razed; both camps are afraid of the same name.* · *Half the town has fled or is packing, and the ones staying watch the road with a specific kind of dread.* |
| **MYTHIC** | *"we will speak of this forever."* | The world's rules visibly, permanently bend. A generation will date time by it. **And it bends at the SCALE OF ITS SURFACE** — a town table's Mythic bends the town (the FLOOR); a world-scale surface's Mythic bends the WORLD (the CEILING, currently missing from the corpus — Adam's calibration, 2026-07-02). | FLOOR (place-scale): *The town has a second shadow at noon — everyone's, cast wrong, pointing at the same hill.* CEILING (world-scale — Distant Word, Region Identity, pressures/dooms, Shrine & Omen, breach tables MUST reach here): *A god moved the Teeth of Heaven into the sky last month — the whole range, roots dripping; no message was left.* · *The dragon's writ runs over an entire nation now; the coronation was filmed in its shadow and the old king reads the proclamations aloud himself.* · *Something in the drowned deep is marching an army north — ten thousand strong, and not one of them knows they're walking.* |

**Working rules:** 1) Faction/civic activity is GROUNDED until it draws blood, costs someone
visibly, or poses a question with teeth (then Textured). 2) Atmosphere/mood caps at Textured —
Strange requires a CHECKABLE impossibility. 3) Volatile requires MOTION — a contained
impossibility, however dark, is Strange. 4) Mythic requires PERMANENCE — a crisis that can end
is Volatile. 5) When two bands argue, the row files DOWN. 6) **Mythic scales to its surface** —
place tables bend the place; world surfaces bend the WORLD (gods doing geology, nations held by
a single will). 7) **Mythic is WORLD-budgeted, not encounter-budgeted** — the game may show a
L4 party a god's work; it does not owe them a winnable fight. Witness, flee, scheme, serve,
survive — the Tier-2 cap means mythic forces are weather, and the T3/T4 future is where you
become climate.

**Absorption:** (a) this ruler is LAW in every batch-3 authoring brief (breach/nightmare
tables, skin re-authors, kit tints, drift Effect pass, hot-tail authoring); (b) the ELEVEN
wave-1/2a tables get a **re-band + hot-tail pass** in batch 3 — existing rows RELABEL to their
honest band (content preserved verbatim; mostly Textured→Grounded, Strange→Textured), and
~15–20 NEW rows per table are authored to the ruler for the vacated tails (PROVISIONAL, Adam
skims the new rows only); (c) the future craft sweep grades the 210 ungraded against this
ruler.
