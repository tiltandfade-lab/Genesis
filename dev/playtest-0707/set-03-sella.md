## Synopsis

A second pure social/intrigue set — no combat, no HP ever at risk — that took Sella's "last day" and its central question (how does a marked woman get off a patient man's list?) all the way to a bittersweet, earned close. Night 1 she read and shed Corran's post-Tree tail clean (Stealth adv 20) and went to ground in the **Refugee Quarter**, where — working the fire-circles as one uncounted among the uncounted — she learned the town's real machine: the Circle doesn't kill you, it *stops counting you*, and the quarter survives by an inverse trick (struck names hide in Saltwidow-Row rooms let to corpses). She tailed a still-faced man into the dark, read him true (Insight 15) as **Frederick Brackett**, one of the erased — subverting the "Circle spy" telegraph — but her Persuasion gambit to buy the survival-network with a secret **failed** (11 vs 15, adv): she spent her Circle-mark disclosure for nothing. She conformed to the **Shared Sleep** taboo and, sleeping east-faced, was let into the warren's shared dream — the set's deepest lore drip: the crater *calls*, oil is the tithe you pay so it doesn't take *you*, and the Sunns were its priests. She woke (the long rest engine-rolled a WILD rest-risk: *a threat already inside*) to a **Circle dawn flood-count** — a good coat + a clerk striking names off the high-ground roll as the river rose, sentencing Susannah's whole row to drown — read the whole engine of it unseen (Stealth 20), and then reversed her earlier failure by *deed*: bringing the quarter a true, no-ask warning **flipped Frederick and opened the network** (the thing words couldn't buy). Susannah refused to run alone ("save the row, not the woman"), so Sella brokered the rescue with her Iron-Strap ally: she pitched **Maddan** the one cause his whole penance is for and won it (Persuasion 18, adv) — he committed the Strap and reshaped it into the network's own corpse-room trick turned to flood-work. The climax was the un-winnable target: she faced **Corran on open ground with truth-as-leverage** (her Tree lesson: no lie to catch) — and **failed** (11 vs DC 17, adv), because the one bluff in her true play (a dead-man's-switch she didn't have) is exactly the seam a 20-year reader probes; he called it, kept his leash, and made her his top item, the crowd her only armor. So in the closer she took the harder exit she'd assembled all set: she **struck herself off the book** cleaner than his chalk — checked Sella Voss into a dead man's Saltwidow room, "drowned in the flood" (`fact_canonized`, +1 XP) — and *ran* the dusk rescue, counting the struck row (the boy among them) up into the graves the Circle dug. She ends her last day having lost her name and kept her life, Maddan's successor within the uncounted, the traitor-ace still unspent, and Corran left an empty leash to one day puzzle over.

**Threads now:** Sella Voss is legally dead — struck from the town book ("drowned in the flood"), living uncounted as a dead-name in the Saltwidow network, now **Maddan's committed successor** in the Iron-Strap cause (she still holds, unspent, his secret that he's the Sunns' original traitor); the **dusk rescue ran** — Susannah's struck row + Batgal (a Sunn) moved into the dead-man rooms, so the Circle strikes an empty row at dawn with no name to blame; **Corran** kept his leash and marked her his one clean loose end but was left an empty leash + a dead woman on a book he'll "one day work the sum of again — the Maw is small" (residual patient threat, deferred); **Frederick** (Friendly, opened the network) and **Susannah** (Friendly, the row's voice) are new allies among the uncounted; fronts moved — **Ironwood Circle 3/6→4/6**, **Iron-Strap 1/6→2/6**, the **flood/external front 0/6→2/6** (still rising, the live danger into any next session); the crater's deep "why" (it calls; oil is the not-taking tithe) dripped a notch via the Shared Sleep dream but is still not hard canon; the **Navigator's Lodge** (0/6, "bury/drag-into-light a dangerous truth") remains wholly untouched — a loaded, unopened faction.
