# Unwilling Accomplice

Type: Narrative Device  
Purpose: Complicate moral clarity by revealing coerced participation in wrongdoing.

## Core Concept
An individual assists harmful actions under pressure, coercion, debt, threat, or deception.

## Typical Uses
- Kidnapping logistics
- Information leaks
- Smuggling
- Ritual preparation
- Political sabotage

## Attach To
- Stablehands
- Guards
- Minor officials
- Tavern staff
- Apprentices
- Family members of power figures

## Coercion Sources
- Threat to loved ones
- Financial debt
- Blackmail
- Addiction supply control
- Magical compulsion
- Fear of authority

## Revelation Paths
- Confession under pressure
- Inconsistent behavior
- Secret meeting observed
- Payment trail
- Emotional breakdown

## Escalation Options
- Accomplice seeks redemption.
- Accomplice doubles down out of fear.
- Coercer eliminates accomplice.
- Party must decide fate of coerced party.

> Design Note:
> Unwilling Accomplice introduces ethical decision points.
> Use to soften binary good/evil structures.
