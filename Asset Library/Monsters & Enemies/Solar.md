*p. 288 MM 2024*

Of all the celestial beings that serve the gods of good, solars stand closest to divinity. They are the generals of the upper planes, the stewards of cosmic law, and the final response when lesser servants have failed. Only twenty-four are said to exist. The few that are known to mortals act as direct stewards of specific deities; the rest rest in a state of deep contemplation, waiting for the day their full power is needed to stave off a threat to the order of existence.

On the battlefield, a solar's greatsword fights independently at the solar's command, and a single arrow from a solar's longbow can kill outright. So great is a solar's celestial might that even demon princes are said to flinch at its resonant commands.

A solar is not an errand angel. Encountering one means one of two things: either the party has stumbled into something of cosmic importance, or they have transgressed against something that matters deeply to a god.

### Solar

_Large Celestial (Angel), Lawful Good_

- **Armor Class:** 21 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 297 (22d10 + 176)
- **Speed:** 50 ft., fly 150 ft.
- **Initiative:** +20 (30)
- **STR:** 26 (+8) | **DEX:** 22 (+6) | **CON:** 26 (+8) | **INT:** 25 (+7) | **WIS:** 25 (+7) | **CHA:** 30 (+10)
- **Skills:** Perception +14
- **Immunities:** Poison, Radiant; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
- **Senses:** Truesight 120 ft.; Passive Perception 24
- **Languages:** All; telepathy 120 ft.
- **Challenge:** 21 (33,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +7

**Traits**

- **Divine Awareness.** The solar knows if it hears a lie.
- **Exalted Restoration.** If the solar dies outside Mount Celestia, its body disappears, and it gains a new body instantly, reviving with all its Hit Points somewhere in Mount Celestia.
- **Legendary Resistance (4/Day).** If the solar fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.
- **Magic Resistance.** The solar has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack.** The solar makes two Flying Sword attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Slaying Bow.
- **Flying Sword.** _Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:_ +15, reach 10 ft. or range 120 ft. _Hit:_ 22 (4d6 + 8) Slashing damage plus 36 (8d8) Radiant damage. _Hit or Miss:_ The sword magically returns to the solar's hand or hovers within 5 feet of the solar immediately after a ranged attack.
- **Slaying Bow.** _Dexterity Saving Throw:_ DC 21, one creature the solar can see within 600 feet. _Failure:_ If the creature has 100 Hit Points or fewer, it dies. It otherwise takes 24 (4d8 + 6) Piercing damage plus 36 (8d8) Radiant damage.
- **Spellcasting.** The solar casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (Spell Save DC 25):
    - _At will:_ `Detect Evil and Good`
    - _1/day each:_ `Commune`, `Control Weather`, `Dispel Evil and Good`, `Resurrection`

**Bonus Actions**

- **Divine Aid (3/Day).** The solar casts _Cure Wounds_ (level 2 version), _Lesser Restoration_, or _Remove Curse_, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

**Legendary Actions**

The solar can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Regains all expended uses at the start of its own turn.

- **Blinding Gaze.** _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 25, one creature the solar can see within 120 feet. _Failure:_ The target has the Blinded condition for 1 minute. _Failure or Success:_ The solar can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
- **Radiant Teleport.** The solar teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see. _Dexterity Saving Throw:_ DC 25, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the solar at its destination space. _Failure:_ 11 (2d10) Radiant damage. _Success:_ Half damage.

### d10 Why Is the Solar Here? (Purpose & Tone)

A solar does not appear at random. Its presence is a statement. Roll or choose one to define the encounter's stakes before the party arrives.

| d10 | The Solar's Presence | Campaign Tone & Execution |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **1** | **The Last Warning** | **Gravitas:** The solar appears to a mortal who has been chosen by a god to fulfill a great purpose — and has been ignoring the call. The solar delivers one final, unambiguous message: answer, or be replaced. |
| **2** | **The Celestial Judge** | **Moral Weight:** A character in the party has committed an act that violated a divine covenant they may not even know they made. The solar has come to adjudicate, not immediately punish. It gives the character a chance to make it right. |
| **3** | **The Warden of the Seal** | **Dungeon:** The party discovers a sealed vault, tomb, or planar rift. A solar has been standing perfectly still in front of it for centuries. It does not speak first. It simply watches. |
| **4** | **The Demon Prince's Pursuer** | **High Fantasy:** The solar is hunting a demon prince or archdevil that has manifested in the mortal realm. It regards the party as irrelevant obstacles unless they can prove useful. It is not gentle. |
| **5** | **The Grieving Steward** | **Tragic:** The solar's god has been diminished, imprisoned, or killed. The solar is adrift — its cosmic purpose severed. It is not dangerous in a hostile way, but it is deeply, frighteningly lost. |
| **6** | **The Test** | **Psychological:** The solar is evaluating whether the party — or one specific member — is worthy of a divine boon, artifact, or ascension. Everything that happens in this encounter is a test. It will not announce this. |
| **7** | **The Corrupted** | **Horror:** An impossibly rare event: a solar has been partially corrupted by prolonged exposure to a necrotic artifact or demonic ritual. Its eyes are black instead of golden. It still believes it is doing good. It is not. |
| **8** | **The Resurrection Mandate** | **Epic:** A solar arrives specifically to resurrect someone the party has failed to save — but the price it names in exchange is something the party will deeply not want to pay. |
| **9** | **The Cosmic Witness** | **Atmospheric:** The solar is simply present, hovering at the edge of a climactic moment. It watches. It does not intervene. It knows the outcome already, and its expression tells the party nothing. |
| **10** | **The Fallen General** | **Dark Fantasy:** This is not a battle the party can win. The solar has come to fight a Balor or a pit fiend that has taken root nearby, and the party is caught directly between two CR 20 entities. Survive. |

### d10 What the Solar Knows (Revelation Table)

Solars have Truesight, cannot be lied to, and have observed the mortal world for centuries. If the party talks to one, they can extract information — but the solar trades in truth that cuts.

| d10 | What It Reveals | Campaign Hook |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **1** | **The Traitor's Name** | Someone the party implicitly trusts is not who they claim to be. The solar states this as simple, verified fact. |
| **2** | **The Imminent Doom** | An entity of enormous power will arrive in this region in exactly 30 days. The solar doesn't know why. That should terrify everyone. |
| **3** | **The Party's Flaw** | It names, with eerie specificity, the one thing each party member is most afraid of about themselves. Not as an attack. As a fact. |
| **4** | **The Dead Aren't Gone** | A specific NPC the party lost is not in the afterlife. Something is holding their soul. The solar does not know what — and that is extraordinarily unusual. |
| **5** | **The Covenant** | One party member made a promise in a moment of crisis they may have forgotten. A god has not. The solar is here to remind them. |
| **6** | **The Artifact's True Purpose** | Something the party is carrying or seeking is not what they have been told. The solar will not touch it. It takes one step back when the item is produced. |
| **7** | **The Deity Is Watching** | A specific deity has been observing one party member for a long time. The solar refuses to say whether the god's interest is favorable or not. |
| **8** | **The Rift** | There is a planar rift within 10 miles of the current location. It is sealed. It has been sealed for 400 years. Something is testing the seal from the other side. |
| **9** | **The Price of Victory** | The party can succeed in their current goal. The solar has seen it. But it also knows what that victory costs — and it will tell them plainly, without softening the truth. |
| **10** | **Nothing** | The solar has assessed the party and found them unworthy of its time. It turns and departs. What they do with that judgment is up to them. |
