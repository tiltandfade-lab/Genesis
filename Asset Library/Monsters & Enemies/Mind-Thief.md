### Mind-Thief

_Medium Aberration, Lawful Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 15
- **Initiative:** +4 (14)
- **Hit Points:** 71 (13d8 + 13)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., fly 15 ft. (hover)
- **STR:** 11 (+0) | **DEX:** 12 (+1) | **CON:** 12 (+1) | **INT:** 19 (+4) | **WIS:** 17 (+3) | **CHA:** 17 (+3)
- **Skills:** Arcana +6, Insight +5, Perception +5, Stealth +3
- **Gear:** Breastplate
- **Senses:** Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 15
- **Languages:** Deep Speech, Undercommon; telepathy 120 ft.
- **Challenge:** 7 (2,900 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +3

**Traits**

- **Magic Resistance.** The mind-thief has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Actions**

- **Tentacles.** Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft. Hit: 22 (4d8 + 4) Psychic damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14). While Grappled this way, the target has the Stunned condition until the grapple ends.
- **Mind Blast (Recharge 5–6).** Intelligence Saving Throw: DC 15, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. Failure: 31 (6d8 + 4) Psychic damage, and the target has the Stunned condition until the end of the mind-thief's next turn. Success: Half damage only.
- **Spellcasting.** The mind-thief casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15): At Will: *Detect Magic, Detect Thoughts, Disguise Self, Mage Hand* (the hand is Invisible); 1/Day Each: *Dimension Door, Fireball* (level 5 version), *Plane Shift* (self only), *Sending*.

**Reactions**

- **Shield (2/Day).** The mind-thief casts *Shield* in response to that spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

### Mind-Thief Arcanist

_Medium Aberration, Lawful Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 16
- **Initiative:** +6 (16)
- **Hit Points:** 143 (26d8 + 52)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., fly 30 ft. (hover)
- **STR:** 11 (+0) | **DEX:** 12 (+1) | **CON:** 14 (+2) | **INT:** 21 (+5) | **WIS:** 17 (+3) | **CHA:** 17 (+3)
- **Skills:** Arcana +9, Insight +7, Perception +7, Stealth +5
- **Immunities:** Psychic; Charmed, Frightened
- **Gear:** Breastplate
- **Senses:** Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 17
- **Languages:** Deep Speech, Lightning Bolt; telepathy 120 ft.
- **Challenge:** 11 (7,200 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +4

**Traits**

- **Magic Resistance.** The mind-thief has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack.** The mind-thief makes three Arcane Tentacles attacks.
- **Arcane Tentacles.** Melee Attack Roll: +9, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. Hit: 27 (4d10 + 5) Psychic damage, and the mind-thief can teleport the target up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space the mind-thief can see on a surface or liquid large enough to support the target. If this damage reduces the target to 0 Hit Points, the mind-thief kills it and magically devours its brain.
- **Mind Blast (Recharge 5–6).** Intelligence Saving Throw: DC 17, each creature in a 40-foot Emanation originating from the mind-thief. Failure: 41 (8d8 + 5) Psychic damage, and the target has the Stunned condition until the end of the mind-thief's next turn. Success: Half damage only.
- **Spellcasting.** The mind-thief casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17): At Will: *Detect Magic, Detect Thoughts, Disguise Self, Mage Hand* (the hand is Invisible); 1/Day Each: *Major Image, Project Image*.

**Reactions**

- **Shield (2/Day).** The mind-thief casts *Shield* in response to that spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
