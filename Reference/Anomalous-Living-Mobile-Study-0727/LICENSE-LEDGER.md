## Binding statement

**Reference evidence only.** Nothing listed here may become a Genesis production asset,
texture, mesh source, or promotional image. These images inform internal visual direction
and grammar induction. Before any image is copied, modified, redistributed, embedded, used
as a texture, or used in promotional material, its exact license and attribution
requirements must be reviewed *for that use*. The safe Genesis production path remains
original procedural geometry and original materials.

Every image below is from **Wikimedia Commons**. Attribution strings are as supplied by the
Commons file's own metadata (queried through the Commons API, `extmetadata`); the linked
Commons page is authoritative if this table and the page ever disagree.

Images were fetched at 1100–1280 px (Commons-rendered thumbnails), not full resolution —
enough to read substrate morphology, small enough to keep the study cheap. Two Centralia
files are served at their native 960 px because no larger rendition exists.

**Every image in this ledger was opened and read before its "what it evidences" line was
written.** No entry describes an image sight-unseen.

## What was deliberately NOT downloaded

- **No FFT map images were copied into this study.** The FFT cohort (see `fft-cohort.md`)
  read the existing in-repo corpus at
  `genesis/Reference/FFT-Guard-Post-Study/study-archive/maps/five-angle/` **in place**. No
  new copy, no derived contact sheet, nothing committed. This follows the FFT BOUNDARY in
  `docs/GOLDEN-SITES-CATALOG.md` and the precedent set by the Lair Study's Lane 4.
- **No game screenshots of any other title.** Same boundary reason as the Lair Study: this
  packet lives *in the repo*, so downloading them here would violate the boundary by
  construction.
- **No images of the Shimmering Maw / Dorsal Market / Living Hive as "concept art."** This
  study is a *real-world* substrate lane. Fantasy illustration would have contaminated the
  grammar induction with someone else's answer.
- **No PD-only substitutes were forced.** The honest license mix is recorded below; it
  contains no CC0 or public-domain item. That is a real gap in the ledger, flagged rather
  than papered over — a later pass should add at least one PD survey/section drawing
  (e.g. a historical boat-mill engraving or a drift-station plan) so the packet is not
  wholly dependent on attribution-required photographs.
