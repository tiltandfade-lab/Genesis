# Set 01 — Sella Voss "the Seam" · The Shimmering Maw

_Continuation of the campaign save `dev/playtest-saves/sella-shimmering-maw`. Day 2, Coalstack Row / the Throat. 10-turn headless session, Opus set-runner playing both seats._

## Synopsis

A pure social/intrigue set — no combat, no dice touched HP; ten turns of a charlatan threading a three-way faction war. Sella began Day 2 owing Corran Vale a "mouth" by dusk (who leaked the buried Sunns) that she never actually had — she'd deduced it — and spent the morning building one: she flipped the chandler Rell into a frightened ally (banking the mercy she'd shown him), extracted by inference that the Iron-Strap collector skimming the well is Maddan the netmender, then went to hear the *other* wolf's side before selling anyone. At the Tanner's Reach she uncovered the spine of the whole world — the Sunns were the hereditary well-keepers (the town's east-facing taboo is their well-religion), the boy Batgal hiding at Dessa's loft is the last Sunn heir, and Iron-Strap's patient chief Maddan is (on a natural-20 Insight read) the Sunns' *own original traitor* now doing thirty years of penance and hunting a successor. Maddan armed her with a sacrificial mouth (Dannad, a real Circle-side leak) and made the boy their mutual hostage. At the dusk climax under the Traitor's Tree she delivered Dannad to Corran — but **blew the Deception (9 vs DC 16)** on his precision-trap ("describe the carrier's hands" → "the carrier's a woman"), so Corran marked her a loose end and pointedly revealed he knows the loft. In the closer she refused to lead the Circle to the boy, and instead **spent her best skill (Sleight of Hand 17 vs DC 15)** to dead-drop Iron-Strap a warning to move him — succeeding, saving the last Sunn at the cost of her own standing and safety. Her hidden ace (Maddan = the traitor) is unspent; she ends marked, double-leashed, and — per the fiction — cleaner than she's ever felt.

**Threads now:** Sella is a marked loose end of the Circle (Corran knows the loft, will close Dannad, promised a "better name tomorrow"); Batgal (last Sunn) moved to safety by Iron-Strap, unaware; Maddan now a Friendly ally who thinks he's found his successor (she holds his traitor-secret unspent); Rell a Friendly stabilized asset; the well-war (Circle vs Iron-Strap over the Throat) escalating (internal front clock 1/6→2/6); Corran's dusk-mouth debt PAID via Dannad; the second name still owed tomorrow (Day 3, her last); the east-facing taboo now explained as the Sunn well-rite (its deepest "why" — what the crater does if you face it — still undripped).

**Session rating: 5/5** — Every DM turn held the charter: agency clean throughout (uncertain actions → rollRequest → STOP, never spoke/acted as the PC, never coached), degrees-of-failure graded tight (a near-miss by 1, a fail by 4, a nat-20 spike, a clutch success), lethal danger always telegraphed and patient rather than fiat, and codex persistence verified in state every turn (dm-merges accumulate, attitude commits, branch isolation clean, front clock resolves). Caveat on breadth, not craft: it was almost entirely a social/deception exercise — combat, economy, inventory, rest, and leveling systems got zero exercise this set.
