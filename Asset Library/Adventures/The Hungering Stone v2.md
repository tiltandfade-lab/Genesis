Here is the "Best of Both Worlds" compilation. It marries the macro-level pacing and organization of classic, high-end commercial modules (like _Eye to Eye_ and _Eberron_) with the highly tactical, table-ready "Run Surface" formatting of our CORE v1.5 layout.

Crucially, it preserves your generated encounters verbatim, but upgrades the **True Nature of the Stone** table to the fully expanded, mechanics-rich version you provided in the sources.

### II. The Adventure Framework (Macro Level)

**Premise & Hook** Vesper, a spy for the Sapphire Circle, went missing three days ago while investigating "The Prime Cut," an elite butcher shop in the Civic District. The party is hired by Vesper's partner (The Tracker) to find the spy. The investigation reveals a horrific truth: corrupt nobles are using the butcher shop to smuggle the city's unwanted down into an ancient aqueduct, feeding them to a dark artifact called the Devouring Relic.

**What Happens if the PCs Ignore It** If the party walks away, the local lord's ritual succeeds. The artifact reaches its Escalation Threshold (often hatching a Shadow Dragon or opening an Abyssal rift), devastating the Civic District. the local lord uses the chaos to seize political control of the ruins.

**The Factions & Cast**

- **The Cult of the Stone:** Led by two Cult Priests in Node 5. They are arrogant dabblers playing with forces they don't understand.
- **The Corrupt Patrons:** Wealthy nobles, represented by the cowardly the local lord (Node 6) and the paranoid butcher Kinto (Node 1), who fund the operation.
- **The Sapphire Circle:** An investigative faction represented by the invisible spy Vesper (Node 3) and the surface Investigator (Node 1).

**Dynamic Pacing: "The Events"** To make the dungeon feel alive (rather than static rooms waiting to be cleared), use these DM-controlled triggers:

- _The Wheelbarrow Timer:_ If the PCs spend more than 10 minutes in Node 2, two thugs from Node 3 open the portcullis to retrieve wheelbarrows, initiating combat at the gate.
- _The Watcher's Eye:_ The Cult Leader actively observes Node 4 via _Clairvoyance_. The players' actions here dictate whether the final boss in Node 5 is surprised or prepared.

### III. The Encounter Nodes (Micro Level)

## Node 1: The Prime Cut

- **Adventure Promise:** The party investigates a pristine storefront where an artisan covers up damning graffiti and a drunk babbles the truth. Bypassing the paranoid butcher leads to a flooded meat locker and a hidden descent.
- **Pressure Level:** Low/Medium (Driven by social tension and NPC paranoia; the butcher will not call the guards for fear of exposing his crimes).
- **Rewards & Consequences:** Access to the underworks, an alliance with a magical investigator. Consequences include triggering the gargoyle's assassination protocols.

**The Set Up**

- **Basic Narrative:** "The Prime Cut" is an elite butcher shop. The locked basement is a slaughterhouse where a massive, 5-foot-wide disposal chute dumps animal carcasses and bodies down to the cult below. Because the cult is performing a massive ritual, this chute is clogged, flooding the back room with bloody sludge. Outside, an artisan covers up graffiti ("The Nobility Are Eating Us") while a Sapphire Circle investigator sweeps the street with _Locate Object_. A Gargoyle on the roof silently acts as surveillance.
- **Starting NPCs:** Kinto the Butcher, 1 Artisan, 1 Babbling Vagabond, 1 Investigator (The Tracker), 1 Gargoyle.

**d6 The "Exotic Meats" Closet Contents**

1. A heavy iron cage containing a living Rust Monster.
2. Crates of alchemical preserving fluid that causes hallucinations if imbibed.
3. A ledger detailing the exact cuts of humanoid meat preferred by specific nobles.
4. A crate of perfectly preserved humanoid hands packed in salt.
5. Several vials of _Oil of Taggit_ (used to sedate kidnappings).
6. A silver signet ring belonging to a missing magistrate.

**The Run Surface (Segments)**

- **Segment 1: The Pristine Facade:** The PCs arrive. They can coordinate with the Investigator, walk in to intimidate Kinto, or sneak into the back alley. _Skill Checks:_ DC 13 Wis (Perception) to notice the Gargoyle's eyes moving. DC 15 Dex (Sleight of Hand) to pick the alley locks. The Gargoyle only attacks if the PCs cause a violent public scene.
- **Segment 2: The Meat Locker & The Chute:** The back room is flooded with offal (Difficult Terrain). _Skill Checks:_ DC 11 Con Save or be _Poisoned_ by the stench for 10 mins. DC 14 Cha (Intimidation) breaks Kinto's resolve, yielding the key to the Exotic Meats closet. DC 15 Int (Investigation) locates the clean lift hidden behind the hanging meats. Jumping down the clogged chute requires a DC 13 Dex save to avoid 2d6 bludgeoning damage.

**Toolbox**

- **Clues:** DC 13 Wis (Medicine) reveals the hanging meat is legitimate animal refuse; humanoids are only used for special rituals.
- **Kinto the Butcher (Cult Fanatic):** _Use 2024 Core Rules Cult Fanatic._ (CR 2, Medium Humanoid. AC 13, HP 33. Multiattack: two melee attacks. _Meat Cleaver (Sickle):_ +4 to hit, 1d4+2 slashing).
- **Gargoyle:** _Use 2024 Core Rules Gargoyle._ (CR 2, Medium Elemental. AC 15, HP 52. Speed 30 ft., Fly 60 ft. _False Appearance:_ While motionless, it is indistinguishable from an inanimate statue).
- **Swarm of Rats:** _Use 2024 Core Rules Swarm of Rats._ (CR 1/4, Medium Swarm of Tiny Beasts. AC 10, HP 24. _Bites:_ +2 to hit, 2d6 piercing damage).

## Node 2: The Holding Pens

- **Adventure Promise:** Descending into a mud-slicked slaughterhouse, the party discovers a caged teenager who warns them the cultists are right next door. They must navigate hanging green slime and silently operate a lift to ambush the thugs.
- **Pressure Level:** Low (No active enemies initially, but noise ruins the ambush in Node 3).

**The Set Up**

- **Basic Narrative:** This grim industrial pen transfers heavy loads into the main aqueduct. The floor is coated in deep, bloody mud. A rusted iron cage holds Vulfio, a 15-year-old street kid. Vulfio explains an invisible spy (Vesper) fell down the chute days ago and just snuck into the corpse wagon next door. A patch of deadly _Green Slime_ hangs over the counterweight winch needed to open the portcullis.
- **Starting NPCs:** Vulfio (Vengeful Teenager).

**d6 Vulfio's Chalk Drawings**

1. A crude drawing of a massive, jagged egg with teeth.
2. Stick figures of thugs prying gold teeth from corpses.
3. A drawing of the wagon, with a faint blue outline of a person hiding inside it.
4. A warning: a stick figure melting under a green blob.
5. Tally marks counting dozens of bodies.
6. An accurate map of the Aqueduct (Node 3).

**The Run Surface (Segments)**

- **Segment 1: The Stench & The Stash:** _Skill Checks:_ DC 11 Con save or be _Poisoned_ for 1 hour. DC 14 Int (Investigation) in the mud uncovers the thugs' skim stash (gems and bloody gold teeth). The mud is Difficult Terrain; standing up from Prone costs _all_ movement.
- **Segment 2: The Teen & The Slime:** Voices echo from beyond the portcullis. _Skill Checks:_ DC 15 Str (Athletics) bends Vulfio's cage bars. DC 14 Int (Investigation) balances the counterweights to silently raise the gate. Operating the winch without removing the slime triggers a drop (DC 13 Dex save or 2d8 Acid damage). Failing the winch puzzle alerts the thugs in Node 3.

**Toolbox**

- **Vulfio:** _Use 2024 Bandit if given a weapon._ Wants revenge on the thugs.
- **Green Slime:** 2d8 Acid damage initially, and 2d8 at the start of each turn until scraped off (Action) or destroyed by cold/fire/radiant damage.

## Node 3: The Illuminated Ambush

- **Adventure Promise:** A bottleneck encounter where a massive corpse wagon is stuck in the mud, causing an argument between cult thugs and trapping an invisible spy.
- **Pressure Level:** High (Combat engagement is immediate).

**The Set Up**

- **Basic Narrative:** A draft horse pulling a massive wagon of corpses is stuck in the mud of a vaulted sewer aqueduct. Five cult thugs are arguing over dropped loot because they now have to carry the bodies by hand to Node 4. Vesper is hiding invisibly in the wagon.

**d4 What fell off the wagon?**

1. A heavy iron casket spilling stolen silver.
2. A corpse wearing an expensive noble's signet ring.
3. A sealed urn containing volatile Alchemist's Fire.
4. A spell scroll case.

**The Run Surface (Segments)**

- **The Ambush:** If entering silently from Node 2, the PCs gain Surprise. If they failed the puzzle, the thugs are behind cover.
- **Vesper's Reveal:** On Initiative Count 10 of Round 1, Vesper drops invisibility, casts _Faerie Fire_ on the thugs, and shouts, "Take the ones on the left!"
- **Aftermath:** Vesper confirms they are a Sapphire spy, admits they don't know the layout of the inner sanctum, and volunteers to walk point to prove their loyalty.

**Toolbox**

- **Vesper (Sapphire Spy):** _Use 2024 Spy._
- **Cult Thugs (5):** _Use 2024 Thug._

## Node 4: The Watcher's Gate

- **Adventure Promise:** A lethal marching-order trap tests the party's guide under the gaze of a magical sensor, leading to a tense social encounter with a telepathic Flumph.
- **Pressure Level:** High.

**The Set Up**

- **Basic Narrative:** A 20x20 waterlogged antechamber. Origami birds hang from the ceiling. A glowing _Clairvoyance_ sensor watches the room. A sunken pressure plate triggers a crossbow trap. A glowing red Flumph blocks the exit winch, projecting warnings into the PCs' minds.

**d4 Origami Trap Trigger Table**

1. **Psychic Backlash:** Attacking the Flumph animates **1d4+2 Stirges**.
2. **Spite:** Dispelling the sensor animates **1d4+2 Stirges**.
3. **Blood Scent:** Taking piercing/slashing damage animates **1d4+2 Stirges**.
4. **Obeisance:** Forcing the winch clockwise animates **1d4+2 Stirges**.

**The Run Surface (Segments)**

- **Segment 1: The Marching Order Trap:** The DM asks for a marching order. The point character triggers the trap (+6 to hit, 1d10 piercing, 2d10 poison). Anyone taking damage on the slick guano floor must make a DC 13 Dex save or fall Prone. If the PCs don't block the sensor, the Cult Leader gains +2 Initiative in Node 5.
- **Segment 2: The Flumph:** DC 14 Cha (Persuasion) calms the Flumph, earning psychic intel about the Stone.

**Toolbox**

- **Flumph / Stirge:** _Use 2024 Core Rules._

## Node 5: The Devouring Relic

- **Adventure Promise:** The party enters a ritual chamber just as priests activate the artifact. As the room decays into an expanding sphere of necrotic ash, they must navigate the shrinking battlefield, rescue a captive, and stop the ritual.
- **Pressure Level:** High (Environmental Timer).

**The Set Up**

- **Basic Narrative:** An oval room with hanging meat hooks (one holding a live captive). In the center, the Devouring Relic rests on a pedestal. Two priests chant symmetrically, causing a sphere of necrotic ash to expand from the center, destroying the floor.

**d6 The Captive (What is on the hook?)**

1. **Loyal Mastiff:** A dependable guard dog.
2. **Injured Druid:** Appears to be a wounded wolf.
3. **Blink Dog:** Can teleport to help PCs bypass the ash.
4. **Stolen Mount:** An undersized pony in expensive barding.
5. **Innocent Deer:** Rewards the PCs with a _Charm of Animal Friendship_.
6. **Weird Sidekick:** A captive Pseudodragon.

## Node 6: The Velvet Vestry

- **Adventure Promise:** Stepping out of the ritual cavern, the party enters an opulent robing room. They must confront the cult's financial backer and solve the logistical nightmare of escaping with his impossibly heavy treasure hoard.
- **Pressure Level:** Low (The climax has passed. The pressure is entirely logistical).

**The Set Up**

- **Basic Narrative:** An opulent room reeking of lilac perfume. the local lord is frantically packing the cult's ledgers to flee up a secret spiral staircase hidden in a wardrobe. If cornered, the cowardly noble will trade the contents of his hidden vault for his life.

**d6 The Cult Masks (Found in the wardrobes)**

1. Bone mask carved into an eyeless frog.
2. Gilded tiger mask with actual feline fangs.
3. Heavy iron mask depicting a weeping skeletal child.
4. Rat mask crafted from stitched human leather.
5. Cracked porcelain mask of a five-jawed skull.
6. Featureless oval of black glass that adheres to the face.

**The Run Surface (Segments)**

- **Segment 1: The Perfumed Antechamber:** The PCs catch Vane packing. _Skill Checks:_ DC 13 Wis (Insight) reveals Vane will surrender if his two guards fall. DC 14 Dex (Initiative) is required to beat Vane before he uses Disengage to reach the secret door.
- **Segment 2: The Vault Trap:** Vane unlocks the vault in his surface cellar. _The Catch:_ The hoard contains 7,000 silver pieces (weighing 140 pounds) and 2,100 gold pieces (weighing 42 pounds). Vane's estate is in the heavily patrolled Civic District. Walking out with bloody armor and sacks of jingling coins will trigger immediate arrest.

**Toolbox**

- **The Ledger:** Details the financial transactions. The notes prove the nobles were foolish dabblers who had no idea what the artifact truly was.
- **Vane's Vault (Treasure Hoard):** 2,100 gp, 7,000 sp, 105 pp, 2 Art Objects (250gp each), _Potion of Healing (Greater)_, _Scroll of Invisibility_.
- **the local lord:** _Use 2024 Noble._
- **Private Guards (2):** _Use 2024 Guard._
