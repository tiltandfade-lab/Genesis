*p. 129 MM 2024*

Gelatinous cubes are the ultimate subterranean scavengers, perfectly adapted to the architecture of worked dungeons. They scour corridors in silent, predictable patterns, leaving unnervingly clean stone paths in their wake. Because they consume organic material but cannot digest metal, stone, or glass, a well-fed cube often resembles a bizarre, floating museum display, with the bones and equipment of past victims suspended in its clear jelly.

Depending on the tone of your campaign, a gelatinous cube can be played for slapstick comedy—such as a bumbling goblin walking face-first into an invisible wall of jelly—or for sheer, claustrophobic horror as a trapped player slowly dissolves while their friends frantically try to pull them free from the acid.

### Gelatinous Cube

_Large Ooze, Unaligned_

- **Armor Class:** 6
- **Hit Points:** 63 (6d10 + 30)
- **Speed:** 15 ft.
- **STR:** 14 (+2) | **DEX:** 3 (-4) | **CON:** 20 (+5) | **INT:** 1 (-5) | **WIS:** 6 (-2) | **CHA:** 1 (-5)
- **Damage Immunities:** Acid
- **Condition Immunities:** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Prone
- **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 8
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Ooze Cube:** The cube fills its entire space and is transparent. Other creatures can enter that space, but a creature that does so is subjected to the cube's Engulf and has Disadvantage on the saving throw. Creatures inside the cube have Total Cover, and the cube can hold one Large creature or up to four Medium or Small creatures inside itself at a time. As an action, a creature within 5 feet of the cube can pull a creature or an object out of the cube by succeeding on a DC 12 Strength (Athletics) check, and the puller takes 10 (3d6) acid damage.
- **Transparent:** Even when the cube is in plain sight, a creature must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check to notice the cube if the cube hasn't moved or attacked. A creature that tries to enter the cube's space while unaware of the cube is surprised by the cube.

**Actions**

- **Pseudopod:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 12 (3d6 + 2) acid damage.
- **Engulf:** The cube moves up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks. The cube can move through the spaces of Large or smaller creatures if it has room inside itself to contain them (see the Ooze Cube trait). *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature whose space the cube enters for the first time during this move. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) acid damage, and the target is engulfed. An engulfed target is suffocating, can't cast spells with a Verbal component, has the Restrained condition, and takes 10 (3d6) acid damage at the start of each of the cube's turns. When the cube moves, the engulfed target moves with it. An engulfed target can try to escape by taking an action to make a DC 12 Strength (Athletics) check. On a success, the creature escapes and enters the nearest unoccupied space. If there is no unoccupied space, the target fails the save instead. *Success:* Half damage, and the target moves to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the cube.

### d10 Gelatinous Cube Debris (The Suspended Loot)

Because the cube is transparent, adventurers will often see the treasure floating in the corridor _before_ they see the monster holding it. _(Roll or choose one to determine what tantalizing bait is floating at eye level)._

|d10|Suspended Debris|Campaign Tone / Integration|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Perfect Lure**|**Suspense:** A beautifully ornate treasure chest is suspended perfectly in the center of the cube. Alternatively, the chest is actually a Mimic that was engulfed and is now trapped in a symbiotic nightmare.|
|**2**|**The Watching Eyes**|**Creepy:** A cluster of strangely smooth, round rocks that look disturbingly like eyeballs are floating near the top of the cube, shifting as the jelly moves to give the illusion the cube is "looking" at the party.|
|**3**|**The Jailer's Keys**|**Tactical:** A heavy iron ring holding the only set of keys to the dungeon's next gate. The PCs must risk taking acid damage by reaching into the cube to pull them out, or kill the cube without scattering the keys in the dark.|
|**4**|**The Floating Greataxe**|**High Fantasy:** A glowing, golden greataxe hangs perfectly suspended in the middle of a dark hallway. Because the cube is entirely transparent, it simply looks like a magical weapon floating in mid-air, waiting to be grabbed.|
|**5**|**The Famous Adventurer**|**Tragicomedy:** The fully intact, perfectly clean skeleton of a legendary, boastful hero the PCs have heard rumors about. Their gleaming trademark armor and signature weapon are still strapped to their bones.|
|**6**|**The Mysterious Tablet**|**Investigation:** A stone tablet etched with mysterious runes or a cipher. The PCs must follow the cube around the dungeon at a safe distance, trying to read the moving tablet through the distorting jelly.|
|**7**|**The Magic Potion**|**Dynamic/Humorous:** The cube recently engulfed a thief who was carrying an unstoppered _Potion of Spider Climb_. The cube has absorbed the magic and is now defying gravity, slowly crawling up the walls and across the ceiling.|
|**8**|**The Bizarre Amulet**|**Wondrous:** A skeletal rider wearing an _Amulet of Ooze Riding_, a magical item that projects a force field to protect the wearer from acid, allowing them to literally steer the cube like a sluggish, subterranean tank.|
|**9**|**The Everburning Torch**|**Atmospheric:** An engulfed _Everburning Torch_. The cube acts as a massive, 10-foot-tall wandering lantern, casting eerie, shifting blue or green shadows down the corridor as it approaches.|
|**10**|**The Still-Living Captive**|**Heroic:** A terrified goblin, rival adventurer, or lost NPC is trapped inside, currently holding their breath and slowly dissolving. The PCs have only a few rounds to pull them out (DC 12 Strength check) before they succumb to the acid.|

### d10 Encounter Scaffolds & Traps

Gelatinous cubes are frequently utilized by clever dungeon builders as biological traps, waste disposal systems, or perimeter security.

|d10|Encounter Premise|Setup & Execution|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Sticky Pit Trap**|A 30-foot deep pit trap. However, a gelatinous cube has been balanced on a 1-foot-wide ledge exactly 10 feet down. Victims fall 10 feet into the cube (taking acid damage and getting stuck) instead of hitting the bottom.|
|**2**|**The Portcullis Squeeze**|The party steps on a pressure plate, causing a heavy iron portcullis to slam down, dividing the party. A gelatinous cube is resting exactly on the other side of the bars, immediately surging forward to press against the trapped PCs.|
|**3**|**The Cleanest Floor**|**Clue:** The PCs enter a dungeon where one specific hallway has absolutely no dust, debris, or rat droppings. A DC 13 Survival or Investigation check reveals this perfectly sterile path is the patrol route of a massive cube.|
|**4**|**The Dead End**|The party is fleeing from a horde of goblins or a powerful monster. They turn down a narrow 10-foot corridor to escape, only to run directly into a transparent wall of jelly blocking the entire passage.|
|**5**|**The Ooze Cavalry**|Cultists or Underdark denizens are using the cubes as mounts. Protected by magical saddles or force-field amulets, they sit inside the cubes, firing crossbows out from the safety of the jelly while the cube advances.|
|**6**|**The Loot Pinata**|The cube is completely stuffed with thousands of copper and silver pieces. Killing the cube causes the gelatin to lose cohesion, unleashing a massive, deafening tidal wave of loose coins that makes stealth impossible.|
|**7**|**The Stunjelly Mutant**|Instead of a cube, the party encounters a _Stunjelly_—a mutated ooze bred by an ancient mage to look exactly like a mundane, 10-foot section of masonry wall, waiting for someone to lean against it.|
|**8**|**The Necromancer's Marbles**|A necromancer uses the cube as a mobile storage unit for their undead. The cube is filled with 1d4 skeletons. The skeletons are immune to the acid and simply wait inside the jelly to attack anyone who gets engulfed.|
|**9**|**The Divided Threat**|The cube has been exposed to a massive electrical trap or lightning bolt, causing it to undergo asexual fission. The PCs are faced with two smaller, highly aggressive cubes flanking them from both sides of the room.|
|**10**|**The Invisible Bridge**|A chasm blocks the path. A perfectly stationary gelatinous cube is lodged between the walls of the gap. The PCs can try to carefully walk _over_ the top of the cube, requiring DC 15 Acrobatics checks to avoid sinking into the jelly.|
