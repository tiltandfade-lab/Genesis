### II. The Adventure Framework (Macro Level)

**Premise & Hook** The party traverses a sweltering, glaringly bright swamp. The geographic layout forces them toward a 30x40 basin of rushing water, bisected by a seemingly safe path of firm wet sand. Opportunistic scavengers have baited this land-bridge, using the harsh light, low-hanging mist, and deep mud to trap and drown intruders.

**What Happens if the PCs Ignore It** The scavengers continue to trail the party at a distance. They will not attack head-on, but will wait to strike when the party is distracted by another wilderness hazard or attempts to take a Short Rest.

**The Factions & Cast**  **The Artillery (Swamp Kobolds):** Cowardly scavengers utilizing high ground and total cover. Goal: Pin down prey with arrows and force them off the safe path.  **The Frontline (Dire Swamp Rats):** Feral beasts working in tandem with the kobolds. Goal: Grapple physically weak targets and drag them into the submerged mud.

**Dynamic Pacing: "The Events"**  **The Bait is Sprung:** If a PC interacts with the basket of rotten apples in the center of the bridge, or if the party reaches the halfway point, the ambush is triggered.

### III. The Encounter Nodes (Micro Level)

### Node 1: The Colossus of the Sun-Fog

_The Set Up_ _Map: A 30x40 rectangular basin of rushing water. A 10-foot-wide path of firm sand crosses it diagonally. A 15x15 stone hand sits near the center-left, and thick-trunked trees form a 5-foot choke point at the far exit._

 **Adventure Promise:** The party must survive a crossfire ambush while navigating treacherous footing, grappling beasts, and hidden submerged mud traps.  **Pressure Level:** Medium. Driven by environmental hazards, obscured visibility at ground level, and enemies utilizing total cover.,  **Rewards & Consequences:** Rewards: Fresh spring water, safe passage, salvaged loot from past victims. Consequences: Getting dragged into the mud and suffocating, alerting deeper swamp denizens.  **Basic Narrative:** Scavengers spring a trap using the environment and bait to separate and drown the party's physically weakest members, utilizing a stone relic for impunity.

**The Run Surface (Segments)**

**Define Physical Space**  **The Space:** A 30' x 40' rectangular basin.  **The Footing:** A 10-foot-wide land-bridge of firm wet sand crosses diagonally (Normal Movement). The surrounding water is deep and rushing. A hidden 10x10 submerged hazard zone contains sucking mud.  **The Atmosphere:** Glaringly bright sunlight beats down, but the rapid evaporation creates a clammy, creeping mist that clings to the ground, obscuring the water's surface.  **The Feature:** A colossal 20-foot-tall stone hand reaching upward (provides Total Cover and multi-level ledges). Thick-trunked trees at the far end form a 5-foot bottleneck (Three-Quarters Cover).

**Starting NPCs**

1. **Swamp Kobolds (3):** Hiding within the curled fingers of the stone hand, arrows nocked.
2. **Dire Swamp Rats (3):** Submerged in the rushing water near the land-bridge, waiting to strike.

**Segment 1: The Sun-Fog Ambush**

 **Narrative Flow:** The kobolds pop out of the fingers, loose arrows, and drop back into Total Cover. Simultaneously, the rats surge from the water to Grapple the party's spellcasters, attempting to drag them off the firm sand and into the submerged mud hazard.  _Read Aloud: "The glaring sun beats down on the swamp, evaporating the moisture into a clammy, low-hanging mist. Ahead, a firm sand bridge crosses a basin of rushing water. Jutting from the water is a colossal, 20-foot-tall stone hand. In the exact center of the bridge sits a basket buzzing with flies. Suddenly, the twang of bowstrings echoes from the stone fingers, and the water beside the bridge churns!"_

 **Skill Checks & Revelations:**  **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception):** Spots the ripples of the submerged rats beneath the creeping mist, preventing Surprise for those who succeed.  **DC 15 Intelligence (History):** Recognizes the architectural style of the stone hand, revealing it is part of a larger, sunken colossus.  **DC 10 Strength or Dexterity (Saving Throw):** Required when stepping or being dragged into the 10x10 mud hazard to avoid being _Restrained_.

 **Escalation & Pressure Keys:** The players' natural instinct will be to get off the exposed bridge and charge the hand. If they do, they risk plunging into the deep rushing water or the submerged sinking mud. If they retreat to the 5-foot choke point, the rats will bottleneck them.

### IV. Encounter Mechanics & Appendices

#### Difficulty Dial: Scaling by Tier

 _The Base Encounter: Tier 1 (Levels 1-4)_  **The Controller:** 3x Kobolds (CR 1/8). Tactics: Pop out, shoot, drop into Total Cover.  **The Minions/Pets:** 3x Giant Rats (CR 1/8). Tactics: Grapple (Speed becomes 0) and drag into the mud hazard.  **The Pressure:** DC 10 saves for the mud hazard. Ranged attacks against the kobolds are impossible without readying an action or climbing the hand.

 _Tier 2 (Levels 5-8)_  **The Controller:** 3x Goblin Bosses (CR 1). Use Nimble Escape to fire and hide.  **The Minions/Pets:** 3x Swarms of Poisonous Snakes (CR 2).  **Tactical Shift:** The enemies push characters into the water rather than just grappling. The mud hazard save increases to DC 14.

#### Mechanic: The Sucking Mud (2024 Core Rules)

Any creature entering the 10x10 submerged mud zone must make a DC 10 Strength or Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, the creature is **Restrained**. A Restrained creature can use its Action to make a DC 10 Strength (Athletics) check, freeing itself on a success.

#### Mechanic: Rotten Apples (2024 Core Rules)

The basket of apples sits exposed on the land-bridge. A character can use an Object Interaction to grab an apple, and an Action to throw it as an improvised weapon. A hit forces the target to make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw to maintain Concentration due to the disgusting, fly-blown rot.

#### Unresolved Mysteries & Clue Chains

_(Open-ended hooks for the DM to integrate into the wider campaign)_

- **The Architect:** Who built the colossal hand? Does it belong to a buried construct, a ruined temple, or an ancient god?
- **The Apple Source:** The fresh (if rotting) apples imply a nearby road, an ill-fated merchant caravan, or a bandit camp deeper in the swamp.
- **The Springs:** Clear, cold spring water wells up around the hand despite the fetid bog, suggesting the statue is tapping into a subterranean aquifer or magical leyline.

#### Treasure & Rewards

**Total Value:** ~25 gp. Scattered coins, waterlogged adventuring gear, and a pristine, unsullied trinket (roll on the Trinket table) stashed inside a hollow of the stone hand by the kobolds.
