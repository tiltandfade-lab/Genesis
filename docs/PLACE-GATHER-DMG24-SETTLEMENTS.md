## Settlements — framing prose (p.93)

- Settlements are places characters visit and may adopt as home base (or build Bastions
  near, per chapter 8).
- The Settlements by Size table gives population ranges for villages/towns/cities AND the
  GP value of the most expensive item the settlement is likely to have for sale. Adjust
  for special circumstances — e.g. a *Potion of Healing* (50 GP) exceeds a village's
  normal cap, but a village with an alchemist, herbalist, or potion brewer might stock
  a few anyway.
- **Settlement Tables and Tracker:** the following tables flesh out a settlement; the
  accompanying Settlement Tracker sheet records the important information.
- Sidebar "Do I Need a Settlement Map?": usually no — describing it is sufficient; a map
  helps only when building/location positions matter. Example map: Crossroads Village,
  appendix B.

## Settlement Tracker sheet — field list (p.95)

The photocopiable sheet is the settlement data-model. Fields, in sheet order:

1. **Settlement Name** (line)
2. **Size** — one radio of three: Village (pop. up to 500) / Town (pop. 501–5,000) /
   City (pop. 5,001+)
3. **Defining Trait** (free-text box)
4. **Claim to Fame** (free-text box)
5. **Current Calamity** (free-text box)
6. **Local Leader** (free-text box)
7. **Noteworthy People** (multi-line list, left column)
8. **Noteworthy Places** (multi-line list, right column)
9. **GP Value of the Most Expensive Item for Sale** (line)

Note the sheet's singular slots: exactly one defining trait, one claim to fame, one
current calamity, one leader per settlement — the tables above map 1:1 onto the sheet's
boxes, with Noteworthy People/Places left free-form (no tables provided for them; the
Tavern Names and Random Shops tables feed them indirectly).
