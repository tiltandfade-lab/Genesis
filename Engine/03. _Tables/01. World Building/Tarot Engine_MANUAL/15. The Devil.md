### **XV. The Devil: Trigger Effects**

The Devil represents bondage, materialism, and the shadow-self. It is the card of addiction, temptation, and the chains we forge for ourselves. Under this card, the dungeon becomes a place of oppressive luxury, corruption, and the perversion of the elements: **spirit, air, fire, water, and earth**.

---

**The Constant: "The Gilded Chain"**

In the Devil’s domain, every desire comes with a tether.

- **The Effect:** Any time a player receives a beneficial effect (healing, a buff, or Advantage), they gain a "Link." Each Link imposes a non-cumulative **-1 penalty** to all Saving Throws.
    
- **The Release:** A player can "break a link" by dealing damage to an ally or destroying a valuable item (worth 50+ gp), representing the sacrifice of connection or wealth for freedom.

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

**The Constant: "The Iron Monopoly"**

The faction has moved beyond war and into "Ownership." They no longer kill enemies; they buy their debts, their land, and eventually, their souls.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**Faction Behavior (The Devil)**|**Narrative Implication**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Debt-Collectors**|The faction begins foreclosing on local businesses and temples, demanding "service" in lieu of payment.|
|**6-9**|Textured|**The Shadow-Trade**|The faction begins trading in **Spirit** energy, using captured souls to power their cities and machines.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Soul-Mandate**|The faction passes a law that every citizen’s soul is legally the property of the State upon birth.|

### **Trigger: Accepting Binding Agreements**

**The Constant: "The Hidden Clause"**

The bargain is incredibly generous upfront, but the "Fine Print" allows the Patron to possess the player’s body for 1 minute at a time of the DM’s choosing.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**The Bargain's Mutation (The Devil)**|**The "Gift"**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**Wealth of the Pit**|You gain 5,000 gold immediately. The cost: Every time you spend gold, you take 1 point of necrotic damage.|
|**6-9**|Textured|**Elemental Mastery**|You can cast _Fireball_ or _Wall of Water_ at will. The cost: You can no longer touch or be near **Spirit** (holy) ground.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The DM’s Favor**|You can "buy" a Natural 20 at any time. The cost: The DM gets to choose when you roll your next three Natural 1s.|

### **Trigger: World Shifts**

**The Constant: "The Age of Indulgence"**

The world enters a period of moral decay and sensory overload. Law and order collapse not through violence, but through a total lack of restraint.

| **d10** | **Magnitude** | **The Nature of the Shift (The Devil)** | **World Change**                                                                                                                            |
| ------- | ------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1-5** | Grounded      | **The Golden Plague**                   | A new "Currency" is discovered that is highly addictive. People will kill or starve just to hold a single coin.                             |
| **6-9** | Textured | **The Shackle-Storm**                   | It begins raining literal iron chains (**Earth**). Anyone caught outside is pinned to the ground and "Claimed."                             |
| **10**  | **Insane**    | **The Hell-Merge**                      | The material plane begins to overlap with the Lower Planes. Demons become the new merchant class and "Spirit" is the only thing with value. |
