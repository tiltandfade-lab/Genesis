### Segment: Hive-Mind Square & The Sentient Gallows

#### Header

- **Title:** Hive-Mind Square & The Sentient Gallows
- **One-line use:** A public horror set-piece where the party must cut through a magically controlled crowd to stop a mass execution fueled by cursed architecture.
- **Adventure Promise _(30-second pitch)_:** A hive-minded crowd has gathered around a freshly built gallows in a residential square, dragging multiple prisoners to execution. The party must cut through the spectacle, identify the hidden curse source, and stop the killings before the Sentient Gallows claims its victims or the square turns fully hostile.
- **Adventure Scale _(Levels 1-5, 6-10, etc..)_:** Levels 3–5 (See Difficulty Dial for Tier 2 scaling).
- **Tags:** Environment: Urban / Encounter type: Rescue, Puzzle-Combat, Crowd Control / Tone: Social Horror, Occult Corruption.
- **Prep Time:** Low (Review Swarm mechanics and Gallows trap).
- **Pressure Level:** High (Crisis & Ticking Clock).
- **Rewards & Consequences:**
    - _Outputs:_ Ally rescued, enemy faction identified, occult anomaly contained.
    - _Follow-ups:_ Rooftop Pursuit, Smuggler Interrogation, Safehouse extraction.

#### The Set Up

- **Basic Narrative:** The PCs enter an urban square to find hundreds of citizens acting as a single, silent organism. They are watching blank-faced attendants drag thrashing prisoners toward a gallows that seems to be moving on its own. The party must navigate the dense, unnatural crowd to locate the "controller" and break the curse before the trapdoors drop.
- **Why the PCs Care:** The condemned prisoners possess vital information, or the party recognizes innocent locals being marched to the noose.
- **Pre-roll tables to define scene:**
    - _buildings:_ Looming, soot-stained tenements with accessible wooden balconies and rooftops.
    - _scene architecture:_ A densely packed cobblestone plaza centered around a massive, groaning wooden gallows.
- **Define maps and tables used within Adventure:** The d4 Curse Source Table, d6 Mid-Encounter Twists (Appendices).
- **Starting NPCs:** The Condemned Prisoners, The Hive-Mind Crowd, The Execution Attendants.
- **What happens if the PCs ignore it (The Antagonist's Timeline):** If the execution succeeds, the cursed relic fully anchors itself. The entire residential square becomes a permanent "dead zone" controlled by the occult faction. The PCs lose all contacts in this district, and the city guard barricades the streets, permanently altering the city map.

#### Segment Descriptions

- **Narrative of each segment and likely play flow:** The encounter begins as a tense social-horror standoff. The PCs must first figure out how to navigate the densely packed crowd without triggering a violent swarm response. Pushing through requires physical skill (**DC 14 Athletics**), while slipping through the gaps requires finesse (**DC 15 Acrobatics**). Failure means taking 1d4 bludgeoning damage from the crushing bodies and treating the area as Difficult Terrain. During their approach, the PCs must actively observe the crowd (**DC 15 Insight or Investigation**) to pinpoint the anomaly—the single entity or object broadcasting the curse (determined by the d4 Curse Source Table). If the PCs attack the crowd or the attendants indiscriminately, the entire square acts as a hive defense mechanism, grappling and overwhelming them. **Initiating combat with the Gallows:** The Sentient Gallows acts as a passive hazard until provoked. It enters the combat order (acting on Initiative count 20) the moment a PC steps within 15 feet of the platform, attacks the gallows itself, or attempts to pull a prisoner free. Once provoked, it actively attacks PCs instead of the prisoners. The optimal path requires the party to surgically strike the Curse Source or physically jam the Sentient Gallows. Destroying the source instantly shatters the hive-mind, plunging the square into mundane panic and allowing the PCs to rescue the prisoners amidst the chaos.
    
- **What the PCs notice immediately:** An unnerving, absolute silence from a crowd of hundreds; the synchronized, unblinking stares of the citizens; thrashing prisoners being dragged toward a wooden gallows; wheelbarrows staged for bodies.
    
- **What does the DM read aloud?**
    
    _(Section 1: The Bottleneck & The Crowd — Read as the PCs enter the square)_ _
    >**"At a four-way intersection in a modest residential square, a crowd has packed itself around a prepared gallows, blocking the main route completely. The air smells faintly of lamp oil, and the dim light leaves faces visible but hard to read—until you realize they are all wearing the same strange expression. Several prisoners are being dragged or marched toward execution, shouting names into the crowd and begging for their lives, but the people only stare, transfixed, as if thinking with one mind."**_
    
    _(Section 2: The Environment & The Threat — Read as the PCs move closer or survey the hazards)_ _
    >**"Wheelbarrows have already been staged to haul away bodies. Some spectators have climbed onto nearby rooftops to watch, but loose tiles are sending people skidding and clattering toward the street below. Somewhere in the spectacle, something is driving the crowd, and if it is not stopped, the execution will go forward."**_
    
    _(Section 3: The Escalation — Read if the PCs hesitate or the Sentient Gallows activates)_ _
    >**"Suddenly, the heavy hemp nooses hanging from the gallows begin to twist and sway like striking snakes. Reaching downward as if alive, one of the ropes violently lashes out, wrapping around an exhausted prisoner's neck and hoisting them onto their tiptoes."**_
    
- **Skill Checks/revelations:**
    
    - _Spotting the Source:_ DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) or Intelligence (Investigation) to notice the single person or object the crowd is subtly deferring to.
    - _Jamming the Mechanism:_ DC 14 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) or Strength (Athletics) to break the gallows' trapdoor levers.
    - _The Architect's Cache (Secret):_ DC 15 Investigation or Stealth to crawl under the platform and extract a hidden lockbox containing faction clues without alerting the crowd.

- **PC likely choices:**
    - _Surgical Strike:_ Assassinate the controller or destroy the relic to instantly break the mob's cohesion.
    - _Brute Force:_ Attack the executioners directly, risking the wrath of the entire crowd.
    - _Distraction/Illusion:_ Use magic to create a massive diversion, buying time to free the prisoners.
- **Tables for variants:** See **d4 Curse Source Table** and **d6 Mid-Encounter Twists** (Appendices).

- **References to any procedural elements:** The crowd acts as a dynamic environmental hazard. Moving through them requires skill checks; treating them as a Swarm if provoked.

- **Instructions to corral players if necessary:** If the party hesitates and keeps their distance, the Sentient Gallows takes action against a prisoner (Read Section 3 of the Read Aloud text) to prove the threat is immediate. If a PC steps within its 15-foot reach to intervene, the gallows happily targets the PC instead, treating them as a substitute sacrifice.

- **Escalation and pressure keys:**
    - _Round 1-2:_ Attendants secure the prisoners. The crowd physically impedes the PCs.
    - _Round 3:_ The executioners pull the levers.
    - _Trigger (The Swarm):_ If a PC deals lethal damage to a commoner, the Hive-Mind Crowd immediately swarms, attempting to Grapple the party.
    - _Trigger (The Gallows):_ Damaging the wooden structure, stepping onto the platform, or freeing a prisoner causes the Sentient Gallows to enter the initiative order and actively target PCs with its Lashing Noose.

- **Consequences of escalation and pressure:** Failing to stop the execution results in the loss of key NPCs. A mismanaged combat turns the residential square into a bloodbath, drawing massive heat from the city guard later.
    
- **Difficulty Dial (Scaling Bracket):**
    
    - _Tier 1 (Levels 1-2):_ The Swarm only blocks movement and pushes PCs back; it deals no damage. The attendants use simple club attacks.
    - _Tier 2 (Levels 5-7):_ The Swarm deals 2d6 trample damage to grappled targets. The Sentient Gallows gets two _Lashing Noose_ attacks per turn instead of one.

#### Clues & Handouts

- _Portable, location-agnostic clue:_ A shattered piece of the cursed relic bears a stamped shipping manifest label from the _city's elite docks_, linking the occult magic directly to a wealthy smuggling cartel.
- _Physical Handout (The Warning):_ A hastily scrawled, blood-stained note found on the edge of the square: _"Don't look at the tuning fork. If you hear the hum, plug your ears and run. They are all dreaming."_

#### Stat Blocks & Encounter Metrics

- **Execution Attendants (Use Cult Fanatic)**
    
    - _Race, alignment:_ Medium Humanoid, Lawful Evil
    - _AC:_ 13 (Leather) | _HP:_ 33 | _Speed:_ 30 ft.
    - _Saving throws, skills:_ Religion +4, Deception +4
    - _Tactic:_ They ignore the PCs entirely until the PCs interfere with the gallows. If attacked, they cast _Hold Person_ on the biggest melee threat.
    - _CR:_ 2 (450 XP)
- **The Hive-Mind Crowd (Use Swarm of Zombies/Commoners)**
    
    - _Race, alignment:_ Huge Swarm of Medium Humanoids, Unaligned
    - _AC:_ 10 | _HP:_ 45 | _Speed:_ 20 ft.
    - _Immunities:_ Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Prone, Restrained.
    - _Tactic:_ The crowd does not make standard weapon attacks. Instead, they move into PC spaces and use the Grapple action with advantage. Any creature starting its turn Grappled by the crowd takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage from being trampled.
    - _CR:_ 3 (700 XP)
- **The Sentient Gallows (Complex Hazard)**
    
    - _Initiative:_ Acts on Initiative count 20 (enters combat order when provoked).
    - _Action:_ _Lashing Noose_. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. _Hit:_ Target is Grappled (Escape DC 14 Athletics/Acrobatics) and begins suffocating. A noose has AC 11 and 10 HP.

#### Reuse Hooks & Rumors

- **True Rumor:** "People in the crossroads district are walking in their sleep, gathering lumber in the middle of the night to build something terrible."
- **False Rumor:** "The city watch has sanctioned public executions for curfew breakers."
- **Reuse Hook:** This encounter can be dropped into any courtyard, temple district, or roadside camp. Simply swap the 'Execution Attendants' for 'the Shadow Syndicate Thugs' or 'Corrupted City Watch' to fit the current faction you want to highlight.

#### d4 What Curse Drives the Crowd?

_(Roll to determine the entity or object controlling the Hive-Mind Crowd. This changes the tactical objective of the encounter)._

|d4|Curse Source|Description|Tactical Objective|
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Crimson Scapegoat**|A blood-red moon or similar cosmic sign has blurred reason into collective rage. The crowd seeks a scapegoat for a local disaster, whether guilty or not. Best for paranoid witch-hunt energy and a false accusation angle.|Cast _Dispel Magic_ on the executioner or break their line of sight to the moon/sky.|
|**2**|**The Juggernaut’s Backlash**|A ruler or noble once empowered by a dark artifact has broken faith with it, and the magic now unites the wronged into a singular execution mob. Best for political revenge and anti-tyrant tension.|Destroy the glowing royal signet ring worn by the lead attendant.|
|**3**|**The Final Muse**|An occult, cursed instrument (a hurdy-gurdy or bone-flute) plays by itself on a nearby balcony, compelling the crowd to create a "masterpiece" of tragedy.|Reach the balcony and destroy the instrument or cast _Silence_ on the area.|
|**4**|**The Sentient Gallows**|The wood of the gallows was harvested from a corrupted, blood-drinking tree in a notorious local swamp. It feeds on blood and controls the crowd to bring it meals.|Set fire to the central support pillar of the gallows (AC 12, HP 30, Vulnerable to Fire).|

#### d6 Mid-Encounter Twists & Complications

_(Roll or choose one when the PCs first take a hostile action, or at the start of Round 3, to dynamically alter the battlefield)._

|d6|Complication|Mechanical/Narrative Effect|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**Structural Collapse**|The sheer weight of the entranced onlookers causes a nearby tenement balcony or rooftop to give way. Debris crashes into the square. _Effect:_ A 15-foot square area becomes Difficult Terrain, and anyone in that area must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take 2d6 bludgeoning damage.|
|**2**|**The False Rescue**|A patrol of six City Watch guards marches into the square with weapons drawn. _Effect:_ Give the players a moment of hope before describing that the guards' eyes are perfectly blank. The guards do not attack the PCs, but they lock shields and form a physical barricade to protect the gallows.|
|**3**|**Oily Rain**|A sudden, unnatural squall moves over the district. _Effect:_ Heavy, foul-smelling rain lightly obscures the entire square (disadvantage on Perception checks). The cobblestones become slick; any PC who takes the Dash action must make a DC 12 Acrobatics check or fall Prone at the end of their movement.|
|**4**|**The Zealous Break**|The curse mutates in one of the commoners. _Effect:_ A bystander suddenly snaps out of their silent trance, but instead of running, they begin screaming fanatical praise for the execution. They actively attempt to grapple and drag a PC toward the gallows.|
|**5**|**The Faltering Trance**|A localized ripple of resistance passes through the hive-mind, causing a pocket of commoners near the scaffolding to temporarily wake up in pure panic. _Effect:_ The waking crowd surges against the base of the gallows, physically hindering the cultists. Attack rolls against the Execution Attendants have Advantage until the end of the next round.|
|**6**|**Mechanism Jam**|The dark magic surging through the gallows causes the freshly cut wood to violently warp and twist. _Effect:_ The iron levers to the trapdoors jam in their tracks, or the reaching nooses tangle themselves into a knot. The "Ticking Clock" on the execution is paused for 1d4 rounds, granting the PCs a desperate window of extra time.|
