**Jack Torver (Economic Advisor)** 
_Archetype: Polished Kingpin — Projects an aura of absolute, immaculate professionalism. He is polite, highly organized, completely indispensable to the community, and utterly, chillingly detached from the human cost of his actions._

Notes for Play

- **Mannerism & Quirk (Quick Play):** He is the picture of respectable pragmatism. He never raises his voice, speaks in perfectly measured, polite sentences, and uses a lot of sanitized bureaucratic buzzwords (e.g., calling a kidnapping an "unfortunate reallocation of civic assets"). He has a fastidious visual quirk: he is constantly, unconsciously straightening his cuffs, adjusting his rings, or squaring the ledgers on his desk to ensure perfect order.
- **Ability & Role (Quick Play):** As an Economic Advisor, he is a "Local Leader (Unofficial)" who wields massive power without wearing a crown. His **High Intelligence** makes his schemes airtight and layered in plausible deniability, but his **Low Strength** means he absolutely avoids physical confrontation, relying entirely on his criminal intermediaries.
- **Secret & Flaw (Quick Play):** He ordered the kidnapping of Vaaldi Estapaar’s niece to gain social leverage over the council. His Flaw is his terrifying detachment: he views people purely as data points and leverage. He genuinely does not care—nor does he bother to find out—what his thugs actually do to the victims once they are taken.
- **Hook & Complication (Quick Play):** Torver publicly "hires" the PCs (or offers them civic funding) to help find Vaaldi's missing niece. The complication is that he is doing this to monitor their investigation and ensure they only find the intermediaries, keeping his own plausible deniability perfectly intact.

Catchphrase

_(Spoken with a polite, helpful smile while perfectly aligning a stack of papers on his desk)_: "It is a tragedy about Councilwoman Estapaar's niece. Rest assured, I have authorized the guard to spare no expense. We all must make sacrifices for the stability of the frontier town."
