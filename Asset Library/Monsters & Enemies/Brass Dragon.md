*p. 54 MM 2024*

Brass dragons crave conversation, sunlight, and attention. They are the most gregarious of dragons, but they do not take "no" for an answer. If an intelligent creature tries to leave their presence without indulging their thirst for small talk, the dragon will aggressively incapacitate them to force an audience.

**Campaign Integration Hooks:**

- **The Hostage Audience:** A missing scout or vital courier hasn't been murdered by cultists—they were knocked out by sleep gas and buried up to their neck in loose earth by a Brass Wyrmling. The dragon has kept them there for three days just to gossip. The party must negotiate for the scout’s release without insulting the dragon and triggering a fight.
- **The Loud Liability:** A Brass Wyrmling decides the party is "interesting" and begins following them through a highly dangerous, monster-infested zone. It refuses to stop talking, ruining all Stealth checks and drawing random encounters. The party must figure out how to bribe it, trick it, or safely trap it without killing a "good" creature.

### d10 Meeting Circumstances (The Discovery)

_(How the party stumbles across the wyrmling. These setups emphasize the danger of being small, curious, and entirely too talkative in a hostile environment)._

|d10|Encounter Circumstance|Setting & Tactical Integration|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**In Over Its Head**|The wyrmling attempted to initiate an "epic quest" by attacking a local monster lair (such as a hag or a troll) entirely alone. The PCs arrive to find the dragon pinned down, injured, and shouting defiant, cliché heroic banter.|
|**2**|**The Extorted Captive**|A local gang of bandits or poachers managed to trap the dragon in a heavy iron cage. They are currently arguing over whether to sell it to an underground fighting ring or harvest its scales. The dragon is trying to strike up a conversation with its captors.|
|**3**|**The Forced Audience**|The PCs find a local scout or traveler buried up to their neck in loose earth or mud, frantically yelling for help. The wyrmling used its sleep breath to incapacitate them and buried them simply to force them to tell it stories.|
|**4**|**The Eavesdropping Stowaway**|During a Long Rest, the PC on watch hears rustling. The wyrmling has sneaked into the edge of the firelight. It doesn't want to attack; it was drawn by the sound of the PCs talking and wants to listen to their campfire tales.|
|**5**|**The Rival Claimant**|Just as the PCs solve a puzzle or breach a sealed tomb to claim an objective, the wyrmling drops from the ceiling or burrows through a wall. It proudly declares that it is claiming the site in the name of its own grand adventure.|
|**6**|**The Misguided Guide**|The party is navigating a hazardous wilderness zone. The wyrmling swoops down, claiming to know a "much more interesting" shortcut. Following it leads directly into an environmental hazard or a secondary monster territory.|
|**7**|**The Annoying Spectator**|While the PCs are attempting a tense Stealth infiltration, the invisible or hiding wyrmling spots them. It begins following them, loudly whispering questions about their tactics, weapons, and what they plan to do next, threatening to ruin their stealth.|
|**8**|**The Wounded Survivor**|The dragon took a bad hit from a larger apex predator (like a wyvern or manticore) while exploring a place it shouldn't have been. It crashes near the PCs, needing a _Cure Wounds_ spell or a healer's kit before the predator circles back to finish the job.|
|**9**|**The Baffled Scholar**|The wyrmling is sitting in the middle of the road or a ruin, utterly fascinated by a mundane, mechanical object left behind by travelers (like a broken wagon wheel, a compass, or a music box). It refuses to let the PCs pass until they explain how the object works.|
|**10**|**The Ambush Bait**|The wyrmling is happily wandering into a very obvious, lethal trap set by cultists or hunters, completely oblivious to the danger. The PCs have only one round to intervene before the ambush is sprung.|

### d10 Adventure Hooks (The Seed)

_(Once the PCs engage the wyrmling, it immediately attempts to leverage them into a quest. These hooks use the dragon's thirst for adventure and growth to drive the campaign forward)._

|d10|The Dragon's Hook|Adventure Promise & Reward|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Eager Apprentice**|The wyrmling is so impressed by the PCs that it demands to join their party to learn "how to be real heroes." It offers its tactical support (and breath weapon) as a temporary follower, but its reckless curiosity will constantly complicate their current mission.|
|**2**|**The Unfinished Story**|The dragon heard a fascinating, half-finished legend from a traveler about a lost ruin or magical artifact hidden nearby. It refuses to leave the region until it knows how the story ends, and hires the PCs to escort it to the dangerous location to find the truth.|
|**3**|**The Molting Catalyst**|To shed its wyrmling scales and grow into a "proper" dragon, it needs to consume a highly specific, rare elemental mineral or magical flora. It asks the PCs to delve into a hazardous cavern to retrieve it, promising a favor from its older kin in return.|
|**4**|**The Missing Storyteller**|A local bard, hermit, or commoner who regularly told the dragon stories has been abducted by an aggressive local faction (like goblins or cultists). The dragon lacks the strength to breach the fortress alone and needs the PCs as muscle for a rescue mission.|
|**5**|**The First Hoard**|The dragon proudly buried its very first, tiny treasure hoard under a recognizable landmark. Unfortunately, a dangerous burrowing monster (like an ankheg or grick) has nested right on top of the X. It needs the PCs to exterminate the squatter.|
|**6**|**The Proving Ground**|To prove its independence to its family, the wyrmling has sworn to slay a specific, dangerous local beast (like a basilisk or a hill giant). It wants the PCs to help it track the beast and soften it up, provided they let the wyrmling land the "heroic" killing blow.|
|**7**|**The Diplomatic Envoy**|The intensely curious dragon wants to walk right into a hostile faction's camp (like a bandit hold or an orc war-camp) just to interview them and learn their culture. It promises the PCs a magical trinket if they will act as its heavily armed bodyguards during the "peaceful" meeting.|
|**8**|**The Stolen Souvenir**|A shiny but completely mundane item (like a polished silver mirror or a brass telescope) was stolen from the dragon by a group of mercenaries. The dragon is deeply offended by the theft of its "prized treasure" and demands the PCs help launch a raid to recover it.|
|**9**|**The Ancient Puzzle**|The dragon has discovered a sealed, magically warded door in the wilderness that it cannot open. Its curiosity is driving it mad. It offers to split whatever is inside 50/50 if the PCs can pick the lock or solve the arcane cipher keeping it shut.|
|**10**|**The Mythic Mentor**|The wyrmling is obsessed with a local legend about an ancient, sleeping metallic dragon buried somewhere in the region. It has a single clue (a scale or an ancient map) and wants the PCs to help it follow the breadcrumbs to wake the legendary creature.|
