# Starting State — World Depth
> **The foreshadowing fuel.** Foreshadowing requires the world to *know its own future*, so at founding the world is pre-rolled **deeper than the player can yet see**. This set adds two layers to the existing Starting State (factions + pressures): the region's **buried truths** (the mysteries the player can uncover) and its **over-the-horizon threats** (distant menaces that loom long before they press). Charter: `docs/DM-CHARTER.md` §4 (the slow drip) + §8.4 (pre-generated depth).
>
> **The policy — soft until contact, then hard forever.** Pre-generated material is **malleable** until the player gets close, then **locks to write-once canon on first contact.** Pre-generated but soft; observed, then permanent. This is how Genesis keeps "generate lazily, the script owns canon" *and* "the DM must know what it's foreshadowing" both true.
>
> **At founding, roll:** 1d2 **Deep Secrets** + 2–3 **Over-the-Horizon Threats**, behind the screen, alongside the faction web and the two pressures. The DM plants tells for them **long before** the player arrives at the truth (`§4`). Player sees only Fragments and clues — never the rolled row (the over-reveal discipline, `§8.1`).
>
> **These differ from `[[Starting State - Pressures]]`:** a *pressure* is an **active front already pressing** on the town (it has a clock that ticks now). A *deep secret* is a **static buried truth** waiting to be found; an *over-the-horizon threat* is **not yet pressing** — it's the thing the DM foreshadows for a long arc before it becomes a pressure.
>
> **v1 DRAFT — flagged for the table-improvement pass.** Bands are first-pass; expand to d100 + refine the spice grading against playtest.

## Deep Secrets (the region's buried truths) — Commitment · Grounded→Mythic
> What the place is hiding under its ordinary face. The foreshadowing *targets* — leak them by the revelation chain (`§4`). Strange+ rows may route to the `[[Starting State - Pressures]]` concretization tables (Buried Power / Curse / Becoming / Intrusion) for their concrete form.

|d20|Beneath the ordinary face…|
|---|---|
|1–2|the founding was a crime — stolen land, a massacre, a betrayal — papered over for generations|
|3–4|the prosperity has a price: the harvest, the trade, or the safety depends on something terrible, quietly maintained|
|5–6|the one in charge isn't who they claim — a figurehead, an impostor, or someone else pulls the strings|
|7|a rightful heir or true claimant exists — hidden, exiled, or not yet aware of it|
|8|something is buried *under* the town — a tomb, a structure, a sealed thing the walls were really built around|
|9|a past disaster was covered up — plague, fire, a vanishing — and the cover-up is starting to fray|
|10|half the town is complicit in something the newcomers aren't — a shared secret kept by silence|
|11|the local faith is a front — the temple serves something other than what it preaches|
|12|there's a traitor inside the dominant faction, feeding a rival or an outside power|
|13|an old pact with a non-human power — fey, fiend, dragon, or the dead — is quietly coming due|
|14|the "monster problem" is manufactured — someone profits from the fear, and stokes it|
|15|the town has done this before — it resets, repeats, or has happened in another shape, and a few half-remember *(Strange)*|
|16|the dead here don't quite stay dead, and everyone has silently learned to accommodate it *(Strange)*|
|17|a beloved figure is a thing wearing a face — replaced, possessed, or never human *(Strange)*|
|18|the whole settlement is bait — built to lure something, by someone, long ago *(Volatile)*|
|19|the town is a wound or a seal: its very existence holds something far worse at bay *(Mythic)*|
|20|it is all a recurrence — founder, threat, and the soul that keeps returning are one story repeating, and the narrator knows *(Mythic)*|

## Over-the-Horizon Threats (the distant looming) — Commitment · Grounded→Mythic
> Menaces too far off to press *yet* — the things the DM foreshadows across a long arc. When one finally arrives, it becomes an active pressure (roll it into `[[Starting State - Pressures]]` as a front with a clock).

|d20|Far off, and coming…|
|---|---|
|1–2|a warlord is rising in a distant region, swallowing settlements one by one|
|3–4|a cult is metastasizing town to town — still leagues away, but nearer each season|
|5–6|a plague-front is moving this way; the refugees will arrive ahead of it|
|7|an exile with a grievance is coming home — slowly, deliberately, on their own schedule|
|8|a rival power eyes this region for conquest; scouts have already been seen|
|9|a famine or killing season is building beyond the horizon|
|10|an old empire or order is reawakening and reclaiming its former reach|
|11|a debt or treaty between great powers is coming due, and this region is the collateral|
|12|a migrating horde, herd, or swarm is on a years-long road that ends here|
|13|a slow wrongness spreads across the far map — the world's border thinning, distant but nearing *(Strange)*|
|14|something is hunting a specific person or bloodline across the world, and the trail bends toward here *(Strange)*|
|15|a sleeping god or old power dreams louder each season, and its reach is lengthening *(Strange)*|
|16|a war between powers larger than any town is about to break, and the armies' road runs through this region *(Volatile)*|
|17|an apocalyptic cult has set a date — and the date is coming *(Volatile)*|
|18|the dead are gathering somewhere distant, and they have begun to march *(Mythic)*|
|19|something is *unmaking* the far edge of the world and working inward *(Mythic)*|
|20|a returning cosmic cycle — the thing the oldest myths warned of — is finally, exactly on schedule *(Mythic)*|

---

> **Concretizing (behind the screen).** As with pressures, the player may see only a vague fragment, but the DM fixes **what it actually is** the moment it's established and writes it to the Ledger as **write-once canon** — soft until first contact, hard forever after (`§8.4`). *What it is* is fixed; *how it ends* is play's to decide.
>
> **Routing:** a Deep Secret or Threat that reads Strange+ can borrow a concrete form from `[[Starting State - Pressures]]` — **The Buried Power** (something waking under the land), **The Intrusion** (a thinning border), **The Becoming** (a place transforming), **The Curse** (a haunting with a lever). Don't leave a Strange+ truth as a mood; give it a real shape with a stated way to be resolved.
