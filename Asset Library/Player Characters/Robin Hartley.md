**The Life (rolled):** Born at home; raised by an aunt, uncle, or both; modest upbringing. Others thought him strange, so he had few companions. When the moment came for someone to stand up, he found it was him — and a weathered tracker took him on and taught him the wild's hidden grammar. Life event: he committed a crime, or was blamed for one — *burglary; cleared after being accused.*

**Hook for play:** A quiet, watchful tracker carrying the faint stain of an accusation he was cleared of. Could arrive as a wary companion on the trail, or a rival who got to the bounty first.
