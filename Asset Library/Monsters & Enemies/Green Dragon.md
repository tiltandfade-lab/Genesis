*p. 152 MM 2024*

Green dragons are the most cunning and treacherous of the true dragons. They are nasty-tempered and take special pleasure in subverting and corrupting the good-hearted. Rather than relying on brute force, they use misdirection and trickery to gain power with as little effort as possible.

**Campaign Integration Hooks:**

- **The Puppet Master:** The PCs are hired to take down a gang of highwaymen or smugglers. Upon breaching their hideout, the PCs discover the "bandit captain" is actually a terrified hostage, and the true mastermind is a highly intelligent Green Wyrmling whispering orders from the rafters.
- **The Poisoned Well:** The water supply in a poor district or frontier camp has suddenly turned toxic. The wyrmling has claimed the subterranean cistern and is using its venom to extort the locals for their meager wealth.

### d6 The Wyrmling's Stage (Tactical Terrain)

Green dragons use their incredibly long legs to step effortlessly over underbrush, allowing them to navigate dense forests silently. They rely on heavy vegetation, toxic flora, and traps rather than fighting fair. _(Roll or choose one to establish the tactical frame)._

|d6|Forest Terrain & Hazard|2024 Mechanical Integration & Tactics|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**Poison-Coated Brambles**|The wyrmling has deliberately exhaled on the thick briar patches. The brambles provide Half Cover. A character pushed or moving through them takes 1d4 Piercing damage and must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or take 1d4 Poison damage and become **Poisoned** until the end of their next turn.|
|**2**|**The Chlorine Fog**|A perpetual, acrid green fog hangs in the air, creating a Heavily Obscured area more than 15 feet away. The dragon uses this to mask its approach, striking from the mist and then using the Disengage action to vanish back into the fog.|
|**3**|**The Canopy Snatch**|The forest canopy is extremely low and thick. The wyrmling uses its 80 ft. Fly speed and long neck to perch directly above the trail. It attacks with its bite from above, attempting to grapple the weakest PC and drag them up into the branches.|
|**4**|**Deceptive Footing**|The wyrmling has dug concealed pits or masked natural sinkholes with fallen leaves. Characters who take the Dash action must succeed on a DC 12 Survival or Perception check, or fall 10 feet into a pit, landing **Prone**.|
|**5**|**The Hallucinatory Spores**|The area is choked with toxic, overgrown fungi. Whenever a character misses an attack and hits the scenery, the fungi burst. All creatures within 5 feet must succeed on a DC 11 Wisdom save or use their Reaction to make a melee attack against a random adjacent creature.|
|**6**|**The "Safe" Clearing**|The battlefield is a wide, perfectly clear circle with no cover, ringed by incredibly dense, impassable trees. The dragon refuses to enter the clearing, circling the edges and using its Poison Breath to fill the bowl, forcing the PCs to push through the difficult terrain of the tree line to reach it.|

### d6 The Wyrmling's Ruse (The Encounter Setup)

Unlike a White or Black wyrmling, a Green wyrmling rarely attacks on sight. It wants to evaluate the party, figure out what they want, and trick them into doing its dirty work. _(Roll or choose one to determine the wyrmling's current scheme)._

|d6|The Manipulation / Hook|Adventure Promise & Resolution|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Captive "Bandit Leader"**|The PCs raid a bandit camp only to find the human "leader" is a terrified puppet. The wyrmling is hiding in the rafters or canopy, whispering orders. The human begs the PCs to surrender so the dragon doesn't execute his captive family.|
|**2**|**The False Guide**|Because green wyrmling scales are incredibly dark (almost black), it pretends to be a Black Dragon wyrmling. It offers to help the PCs navigate the swamp to kill a rival monster, intending to betray the PCs once they are weakened.|
|**3**|**The Extortion Racket**|The wyrmling has poisoned the local water supply or a vital logging camp's rations. It promises to provide the antidote, but only if the frontier settlement provides it with a weekly tribute of hand-crafted art and fresh meat.|
|**4**|**The Cult of the Forest**|The dragon has thoroughly corrupted a local circle of druids, goblinoids, or elf foragers. The humanoids genuinely believe the wyrmling is a divine messenger of the woods. The PCs must break the psychological hold the dragon has on the locals without slaughtering innocent dupes.|
|**5**|**The Trapped Envoy**|A local noble, renowned bard, or faction leader has gone missing. They haven't been eaten; the wyrmling is keeping them as a "living treasure," forcing them to write songs about its greatness or teach it political history.|
|**6**|**The "Helpful" Informant**|The wyrmling casually approaches the PCs' camp under a flag of truce. It offers them highly accurate, devastating blackmail material about a corrupt city official. It asks for no payment—it simply wants to watch the PCs destroy the official so it can move into the power vacuum.|

### d4 Clues, Captives, & Windfalls (The Investigation Layer)

Green dragons love sculpted art, musical instruments, and the subjugation of intelligent creatures. Looting a green dragon's territory is less about finding piles of gold and more about uncovering stolen secrets and kidnapped artisans.

|d4|Macabre Discovery|Clue / Campaign Hook|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Suborned Ledger**|A waterproof satchel containing a ledger that proves the wyrmling has been successfully bribing a captain of the local Watch to look the other way when caravans are ambushed.|
|**2**|**The Masterwork Instrument**|A beautifully carved, magical wooden instrument (like a _Mac-Fuirmidh Cittern_ or a _Pipes of Haunting_). It is slightly etched by chlorine gas but fully functional. Returning it to the Bard's College earns the party a massive political favor.|
|**3**|**The Traumatized Scout**|The PCs find a local elf scout tied to a tree, physically unharmed but psychologically tortured. The wyrmling has spent days simply whispering horrors to them to break their mind. Healing the scout provides the PCs with a flawless map of the region.|
|**4**|**The Stolen Repertoire**|Tucked into a hollow log is a collection of intricate woodcarvings and highly classified missives stolen from a traveling diplomat. The documents reveal the troop movements of a rival faction, giving the PCs a massive tactical advantage in the broader campaign.|
