# Ambush Bridge Thoroughfare Crisis
![[Pasted image 20260307163847.png]]
> *Urban High. A prepared bandit ambush on a contraband wagon — the party must cross a rickety bridge directly into it.*

## 30-Second Run

A 20-foot rickety wooden footbridge crosses a small 8-foot canyon onto the main road. As the party reaches it, horses scream and a wagon tips — crashing sideways across the thoroughfare. Rotten wheat spills open to reveal hidden contraband beneath. Bandits rise from cover behind the overturned wagon. A scout watches from the rooftop to the right. The party is already in the kill zone before they know it. A ledger page from the crash names a corrupted noble.

---

## Setup

**Space:** Rickety wooden footbridge (20 ft long, 5 ft wide) crossing an 8-ft canyon. The main road beyond runs along the drop edge. Crates staged in advance shape the ambush zone. A floor grate near the crash drains to lower caverns.
**Atmosphere:** Bright daylight, warm air. Maggots in the rotten wheat. A faint draft from the grate below.
**NPCs Present:** 4 Bandits (behind overturned wagon, left flank), 1 Rooftop Scout (right flank, crossbow), Wagon Driver (incapacitated in crash).
**If Ignored:** Bandits take the contraband and vanish. The ledger page is recovered. The noble's connection is never found.

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Bridge Crossing

The bridge is the chokepoint — and it announces the party's arrival.

**Skill Checks**
- **Bridge (rickety wood):** Every creature crossing must succeed on **DC 10 Acrobatics** or make noise on entry. Failure means the ambushers get a free reaction.
- **DC 12 Perception:** Spot the rooftop scout before crossing (from the canyon edge). Grants the ability to plan a flank.
- Moving through the rough, deeply rutted road beyond the bridge: DC 10 Acrobatics or Speed halved.

**Escalation:** The bandits don't wait — they open fire once the first PC steps off the bridge.

---

### Segment 2: The Crashed Wagon

The overturned wagon is strong cover for the bandits (+5 AC from behind) and blocks the thoroughfare. The rotten wheat covers the true cargo.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 15 Investigation (in combat):** A torn sheet of financial records is caught in the rusted fence beside the grate. Blackmail material — a corrupt local noble's name and connection to the shipment (roll d6 Noble Contraband for what it is).
- **DC 13 Athletics:** Shove the wagon upright or clear enough of the road to create a new movement lane.
- **Floor grate (DC 10 Perception):** Faint draft. DC 12 Athletics to lift and drop below into lower caverns — escape route, or ambush from below.

**Escalation:** The rooftop scout has full line of sight to the bridge and the road. +5 to hit, Longbow, 1d8+3. Uses Half Cover behind chimney (+2 AC to scout).

---

## Mechanics

1. **Bridge:** Noisy crossing without DC 10 Acrobatics. 5 ft wide — two creatures can cross side by side but no more.
2. **Canyon Edge (road beyond bridge):** 8-ft drop. Shoved creatures make DC 13 Athletics or fall — 1d6 bludgeoning + Prone below.
3. **Hazard Lane:** The crash zone along the wagon's impact path is Difficult Terrain (churned road, broken crates, rotten wheat) — 20 ft length.
4. **Crate Cover:** Half cover (+2 AC) for both bandits and PCs if reached.
5. **Ledger Page:** DC 15 Investigation to retrieve from the rusted fence. Found post-combat automatically on a search of the crash site.

---

## Difficulty Dial

**Tier 1 (Lvl 2–4):** 3 Bandits + 1 Scout. No reinforcements.
**Tier 2 (Lvl 4–6):** As written + the wagon driver is alive and turns out to be a cult informant. He flees via the grate.
**Tier 3 (Lvl 7+):** The bandit leader is a Bandit Captain. The scout is a Cult Assassin (+6 to hit, 1d8+4 piercing + 2d6 poison).
