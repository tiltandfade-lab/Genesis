#morale-outcome
> **What a broken creature *does*** — rolled on a **failed morale check** (a Wisdom save vs. the severity of the trigger: Bloodied, an ally dropped, its leader fell; `docs/SOCIAL.md` §3). **This replaces the DM deciding by fiat whether a losing fight continues or defuses** — the single most important narrative question combat asks: blood or words. The four channels route to live systems: **flee** → a chase / `[[Monster Behavior if Hunted]]` read · **surrender** → a Hostile-but-compliant prisoner (killing it now is a `kill{victimClass}` with full escalation, `docs/DIFFICULTY.md`) · **parley** → drops into `[[Creature Parley - What It Wants]]` (and *if talks fail, combat resumes exactly where it left off*) · **fights on, desperate** → the fight continues but the fiction changed. The DM narrates the *shape*; this die sets the *size* (`DM-CHARTER.md` §6.6). Spice-graded — the top rows are genuinely Volatile/Mythic forks. A creature that by nature does **not** break (mindless, fanatical, cornered-with-young) never rolls here; that's a `[[Monster Motivation]]`/`[[NPC If Cornered]]` read.

| d20 | Band | Channel | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grounded | Flee | **Breaks and runs.** Drops what it's carrying, turns tail, and bolts for the nearest cover or exit. |
| 2 | Grounded | Flee | **Fighting withdrawal.** Backs away covering itself, trading ground for distance until it can run clean. |
| 3 | Grounded | Flee | **Scatters the group.** Bolts and the others go with it — the whole engagement comes apart at once. |
| 4 | Grounded | Flee | **Lures as it goes** — flees *toward* a hazard or its lair, hoping pursuit costs the PC more than the chase is worth (`[[Monster Behavior if Hunted]]`). |
| 5 | Grounded | Surrender | **Drops its weapon and yields.** Hands up, eyes down — it would rather be a prisoner than a corpse. |
| 6 | Grounded | Surrender | **Begs.** Goes to its knees and pleads — for mercy, for its life, for a chance to explain. |
| 7 | Grounded | Surrender | **Surrenders and sells out** — yields *and* immediately offers up its allies, its master, or its cache to buy goodwill. |
| 8 | Grounded | Surrender | **Plays dead / fakes the yield** — surrenders in body, watching for one opening to bolt or strike (a `[[Monster Behavior if Hunted]]` mind). |
| 9 | Grounded | Parley | **Calls for a parley.** Throws up a hand and shouts (or signs) for a *truce to talk* — drop into `[[Creature Parley - What It Wants]]`. |
| 10 | Grounded | Parley | **Names its price.** Stops fighting and states, plainly, the one thing that ends this without more blood. |
| 11 | Grounded | Parley | **Offers a trade** — information, a way out for you, a rival's head — in exchange for its life. |
| 12 | Grounded | Parley | **Pleads its case** — insists this is a *misunderstanding*, that you've the wrong creature or the wrong quarrel. |
| 13 | Textured | Fights on | **Desperate, all-in.** No escape it can see — it fights recklessly now, holding nothing back. |
| 14 | Textured | Fights on | **Cornered and vicious** — flight failed, so it turns and savages whatever's closest, fear sharpened to fury. |
| 15 | Textured | Flee | **Routs and abandons the wounded** — saves itself, leaves its own to be cut down or captured. |
| 16 | Textured | Surrender | **Surrenders the whole group** — its break drags the others into yielding with it; the fight is simply *over*. |
| 17 | Textured | Parley | **Switches sides on the spot** — offers to turn on its own master/pack *right now* if you'll spare it and take it on. |
| 18 | Strange | Fights on | **Snaps — a death-frenzy.** Past terror into something worse; it will not flee, will not yield, and fights until it or you is dead. |
| 19 | Volatile | Flee | **Flees to bring back something far worse** — escapes specifically to wake, summon, or lead a greater threat to you. Mark the clock. |
| 20 | Mythic | Parley | **Breaks open and *bargains with everything*** — terror cracks it down to the core, and it offers a desperate, binding pact (its true name, a curse lifted, a door opened) that reshapes the fight into a far larger story. Write any pact to the Ledger as canon (`DM-CHARTER.md` §6.6). |
