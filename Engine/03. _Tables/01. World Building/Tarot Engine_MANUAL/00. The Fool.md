# Dungeon Effects

---
### 01. The Constant

**The Constant: "The Fool’s Leap"** The party moves with a strange, aloof lightness, seemingly protected by their own recklessness.

- **The Effect:** Movement does not provoke opportunity attacks, as enemies are baffled by the party's casual gait.
    
- **The Precariousness:** However, any time a player ends their turn within 5 feet of a ledge, pit, or trap, they must roll a "Luck Die" (d20).
    
    - **1-5:** You stumble; fall prone or trigger the hazard.
    - **6-19:** No effect.
    - **20:** You "accidentally" find a pouch of 1d10 gold pieces while regaining your balance.

# World Effects

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

**The Constant: "The Blind Lead"**

The faction is currently operating with total disregard for consequences, led by a figurehead who is either brilliant or a total idiot.

| **d10** | **Magnitude** | **Faction Behavior (The Fool)** | **Narrative Implication**                                                                                                   |
| ------- | ------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1-5** | Grounded      | **The New Brand**               | The faction throws a massive, expensive gala and invites their enemies, claiming "all is forgiven."                         |
| **6-9** | Textured | **The Great Reset**             | The faction burns all their ledgers and records, deciding to start their history "from Day Zero."                           |
| **10**  | **Insane**    | **The Musical Mandate**         | The faction decides that all official business and combat must be conducted while wearing fancy dress and rhythmic dancing. |


### Trigger: Accepting Binding Agreements
**The Constant: "The Fool’s Bargain"**

Deals offered under this card are designed to appeal to the unwise—high immediate reward with a "vague" future cost.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**The Bargain's Mutation (The Fool)**|**The "Gift"**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Golden Handshake**|You receive 1,000 gold immediately. However, you can never again refuse a request for a "small loan" from an NPC.|
|**6-9**|Textured|**Ignorance is Bliss**|You are immune to the Frightened condition, but you are also unable to see "Danger" signs or hear "Warning" shouts.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**Plot Armor**|You cannot die unless the death is "dramatically appropriate." The DM decides what is dramatic.|

### **Trigger: World Shifts**

**The Constant: "The Age of Innocence"**

The world experiences a sudden, lighthearted shift that makes the mundane feel precarious and the absurd feel normal.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**The Nature of the Shift (The Fool)**|**World Change**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Grand Carnival**|A month-long festival is declared worldwide. Work stops; fancy dress is mandatory.|
|**6-9**|Textured|**The Floating Market**|All shops and stalls in major cities begin to levitate 10 feet off the ground.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Reset Button**|For 24 hours, everyone in the world forgets their professional skills and must rely on "Beginner’s Luck" (flat d20 rolls) for everything.|
