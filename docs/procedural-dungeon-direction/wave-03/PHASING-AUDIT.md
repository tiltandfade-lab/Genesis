# Wave 3 Prototype/MVP Phasing Audit

This audit separates Wave 3's architecture, graph-realization, culture/material-history, and evidence destinations
from the smallest honest proofs and playable-MVP obligations. It follows the global
[phasing framework](../PHASING-FRAMEWORK.md), the [Clay Proof Ladder](../CLAY-PROOF-LADDER.md), and the
[Feature-Promotion Ledger](../FEATURE-PROMOTION-LEDGER.md). The chronological authority remains
[Wave 3's running record](01-questionnaire-and-triage.md). No implementation or dependency is authorized.

## Pre-alpha-critical foundation

| Decision family | Minimum critical form | Why it cannot wait |
|---|---|---|
| Canonical architecture versus rendering | Stable ids and provenance for mechanically meaningful cells/volumes, boundaries, portals, elevations, materials, supports/attachments, zones, condition, and committed mutation; every visual view derives from them | Later renderers, saves, destruction, remount, and DM narration cannot recover mechanical truth from decorative mesh. |
| Graph and circulation preservation | Every implemented canonical node/edge maps to typed space/connector evidence; no invented or silently dropped adjacency; rejected or unsatisfied output remains explicit | A beautiful layout that changes the dungeon graph is a different dungeon and corrupts navigation, discovery, and persistence. |
| Deterministic commitment and remount | Accepted plans, culture constitutions/imprints, structural identities, attachments, and durable history persist with versions/provenance and rebuild the same truth | Discovered architecture or culture cannot restyle, reroll, or lose player-created changes on revisit. |
| Reservations and honest failure | Implemented thresholds, routes, interaction fronts, supports, deployment/objective space, and hard canonical obligations have typed ownership; collision degrades in a named order or reports unsatisfied | Later dressing or solver convenience cannot consume required play space or fabricate a catastrophe. |
| Scale truth | Implemented inhabitants and structures use compatible scale domains or an explicit causal mismatch/adaptation fact; renderer scaling cannot counterfeit fit | Oversized universal rooms or visual overlap would make movement, occupation, and narration false. |
| Action-created structure | Every implemented anchor, moved device, break, repair, improvised route, or similar action commits through certified game/action authority to stable local structure and lifecycle data | Renderer physics or prose cannot decide durable world state independently of the game. |
| Fixed-camera tactical honesty | The one production camera family, governed focus, and cutaway/ghosting expose required known tactical truth without changing mechanics or leaking secrets | Removing player rotation concentrates all comprehension responsibility in one view; a bad production view cannot be escaped by the player. |

## Reconciled debts and exclusions

- No exact solver, floorplanning package, geometry utility, WFC library, Material Maker graph, generated payload, or
  cloud service is admitted by Wave 3. Each future dependency must pass its own license/runtime/determinism/value
  gate after an evidenced need.
- Exact schemas, stamp catalogs, numeric solver budgets, final camera constants, visual thresholds, and portfolio
  order are specification/evidence work. Their owners and promotion triggers are named; they are not omitted product
  decisions.
- Fixed camera removes four-yaw gameplay/equal-yaw beauty from the MVP. Optional nonproduction views may diagnose
  reusable geometry, but cannot restore player rotation or become a hidden production-art matrix.
- The twelve structural composition dimensions overlay rather than replace Wave 2's twelve named golden sites.
  One fixture may cover several risks; no structure is generated merely to satisfy a quota.
- Full destruction, structural propagation, and every possible physical interaction remain Wave 8 feature breadth.
  Wave 3 locks stable identity, material/support/condition seams, and one narrow exercised family so later breadth
  extends rather than replaces the architecture.
- No wave/design closure, build authorization, implementation state, CI claim, or release-readiness claim follows
  from this phasing audit.
