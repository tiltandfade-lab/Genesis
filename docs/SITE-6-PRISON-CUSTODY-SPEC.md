## Plain-English promise

A prison turns people, possessions, time, and permission into controlled flows.

Whether the player arrives at the public desk, sneaks in to free someone, or wakes in a
cell without their gear, the same committed place should reveal who controls each
threshold, what routine moves through it, where confiscated property went, what will
happen when the clock advances, and several materially different ways to change the
situation.

The intended first impression is pressure with legibility: the player feels contained
or watched, but can read enough of the institution to begin forming a plan.

## What makes it a site family

Prisons are not defined by stone bars, one corridor, or one justice system. They range
from a shared lockup behind a sheriff's office to a city prison, military stockade,
hostage house, penal institution, suspended cage, warded breach bank, or repurposed
ruin.

The family survives those changes when it preserves:

- involuntary or conditional custody;
- unequal permissions across thresholds;
- observation/control and an operator routine;
- a credible custody path for people and their property;
- time pressure or disposition;
- staff, prisoner, visitor, supply, and emergency relationships appropriate to scale;
- precommitted opportunities to alter or escape custody; and
- consequences for success, failure, exposure, damage, release, or transfer.

A cage with no operator, disposition, property trail, or playable way to change the
situation is a prop or encounter feature, not a complete Prison/Custody site.

## Required invariants

- **REQUIRED — one committed map, multiple entry states.** Outside arrival and captured
  arrival use the same canonical topology, mechanisms, occupants, property locations,
  and clocks. Entry state changes knowledge, position, permissions, and carried gear,
  not the map.
- **REQUIRED — custody boundary.** At least one controlled threshold separates a held
  population from free exit, with a named operator or active replacement system.
- **REQUIRED — functional oversight.** The current operating model explains observation,
  keys/permissions, routine, staffing or automation, and what happens when control
  fails.
- **REQUIRED — causal property custody.** Confiscated PC property has exact instance
  ids, a custodian/location/state, a recovery path, and a ledger trail. It never becomes
  an unbound prose promise that “the gear is somewhere.”
- **REQUIRED — pressure.** A captured start has a finite disposition or equivalent
  consequence clock. Waiting indefinitely is not a safe universal solution.
- **REQUIRED — plan plurality.** Every captured-start fixture admits at least three
  viable plan families: one social/legal, one routine/covert, and one
  force/disruption or environmental plan. Their risks and capacities differ.
- **REQUIRED — precommitment.** A hidden route, loose fixture, missed tool, compromised
  wall, corrupt relationship, or schedule opening is generated before the player
  exploits it. Player attention may reveal or promote it; the DM cannot invent it to
  rescue a stalled scene.
- **REQUIRED — force has truth.** Bars, doors, anchors, walls, chains, wards, and
  restraints have material, support, lock, durability, noise, time, and consequence
  facts. “Unbreakable” requires a licensed law and cannot erase all other plans.
- **REQUIRED — recoverable failure.** A failed attempt changes alert, access, damage,
  relationship, time, or disposition causally. It does not silently reset the site.
- **REQUIRED — legal bodies.** Every holding space, route, restraint, and evacuation
  path is compatible with its intended occupants or records a different accommodation
  domain.
- **REQUIRED — honest services.** Food, water, sanitation, medical support, waste, and
  staff rest may combine or externalize only through named realizations and
  dependencies appropriate to scale/state.
- **REQUIRED — retained source lineage.** World, place, building, walk, capture,
  occupant, material, history, and adapter facts retain `rollerLineage` and
  `sourceRollRefs`.
- **DEFAULT — repeated restraint.** Ordinary prisons express custody through repeated
  cells, pens, beds, cages, or wards. A single exceptional holding is legal when
  capacity and disposition support it.
- **LICENSED — strange containment.** Grown wood, coral, sentient silk, portals,
  inverted gravity, living wards, or impossible rooms require world/realm/history
  provenance and mechanical consequences.

## Identity, culture, and circumstance

### Site identity

Custody, unequal access, oversight, routine, property control, disposition, and
precommitted counterplay.

### Cultural expression

Culture and institutional doctrine may change:

- who may imprison whom and what counts as lawful custody;
- whether charges, witnesses, oaths, kin, debt, ransom, labor, classification, or
  supernatural danger organize the population;
- whether authority is centralized, household, communal, military, religious,
  mercantile, or magical;
- which thresholds use keys, seals, names, sponsors, schedules, wards, witnesses, or
  reciprocal obligations;
- how prisoners are seen, separated, fed, washed, exercised, worked, visited, judged,
  released, mourned, or disappeared;
- how personal property and civic evidence are recorded and stored; and
- construction rhythm, outward face, repair, marks, light ownership, and staff
  organization.

Culture weights compatible solutions. It never selects one prison mesh or palette by
name.

### Local circumstance

Settlement size, legal authority, war, wealth, labor demand, captor motive, capacity,
overcrowding, climate, material, water, terrain, inherited shell, current occupants,
corruption, disease, damage, realm pressure, and supply reliability decide the actual
expression.

Original function and current use stay separate. A bandit-held city gaol retains its
cellblocks and civic evidence trail under a new hostage/loot overlay; a monastery used
for detention records the circulation mutation rather than becoming “a prison” through
furniture alone.

## The prison deck

The prison's deck is the **control position that governs two or more custody
relationships**: cell fronts and yard gate, intake and secure corridor, transfer route
and block doors, or several suspended holdings.

It may realize as:

- a raised keeper landing;
- an overlooking gallery or crosswalk;
- a central guard station;
- a gatehouse walk;
- a ward-control platform;
- a winch station above hanging cells; or
- a non-elevated control threshold when a tiny lockup cannot honestly support a deck.

The deck is not free dominance. It requires access, staffing/automation, sight or ward
coverage, capacity, support, and counterplay. Captured players may seek to reach,
neutralize, deceive, isolate, or bypass it. Outside rescuers may need to avoid or seize
it.

**RULED — PR2 (Adam, 2026-07-25):** build the raised keeper landing first because it
proves the general elevation-as-control relationship visibly. Retain the grade-level
control threshold as the Rung-A degradation.

## Five-expression family

| Expression | Family relation | Distinguishing facts | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| One-room lockup | Smallest believable custody | One shared barred room or 1–2 cells; oversight/records combined; property cabinet; external meals; yard privy or degraded sanitation | `VARIANT`, Rung A |
| Four-cell civic jail | Ordinary complete institution | Public office/intake; one correlated cell assembly; keeper threshold/deck; secure yard; property cabinet/closet; real rear service | `DEFAULT` first build (PR1), Rung B |
| Prison wing / stockade | Large/advanced expression | Repeated blocks or pens; separate intake/visitation; records/evidence; shifts; kitchen/sanitation/medical/work or exercise | `VARIANT`, Rung C |
| Breached, corrupt, abandoned, or repurposed prison | Degraded/current-use expression | Original custody model remains legible under broken control, occupant seizure, cover-up, riot, neglect, haunting, or new use | `VARIANT` at any rung |
| Suspended living-ironwood solitary cell | Unusual supported expression | Hanging cage/holding + suspended world context + growing architecture; property and escape handles remain elsewhere in the active walk/site | `RETAINED-FIXTURE`, `RC-PRISON-01` |

The family comparison must also admit a city/realm prison with multiple blocks or
breach-linked capacity at Ideal. The five rows above are concept comparisons, not a
fixed distribution.

### Scene ladder

| Rung | Canonical capability | Typical realization |

| A — Lockup | custody 1–4; one operator/control relationship; property; disposition; minimal services | office plus shared cell room, tiny gatehouse cell, cage/pit with nearby keeper, household/surety holding |

## Working spatial specification

### Required zones

Every active prison realizes these as rooms, assemblies, distributed systems, or honest
external services according to rung:

1. exterior/world relation and free exit;
2. public/visitor/claim threshold where the institution admits one;
3. intake, transfer, or custody-assumption point;
4. oversight/control position;
5. one or more custody assemblies with stable held-space children;
6. staff-only circulation or permissions;
7. property/evidence custody realization;
8. water, food, sanitation, waste, and health relationships appropriate to load;
9. routine destination such as yard, work, exercise, interview, chapel, hearing,
   washing, or meal distribution where the selected model requires it;
10. release/transfer/emergency path;
11. at least three precommitted plan/opportunity families for a captured start; and
12. protected discretionary/Spice capacity with one juicy player handle.

A tiny lockup may combine zones 2–4 in one office and express zone 8 through providers
and a secure-yard privy. A city prison may separate every one and repeat several.

### Provisional grid and accommodation hypotheses

These are clay questions, not rulings:

- 5-foot tactical cells remain the planning unit where ordinary combat scale applies;
- a human solitary child begins at roughly `1×2` cells only when the intended fixtures,
  door swing, body, interaction, and emergency handling fit;
- shared, accessible, large-body, medical, or high-duration custody receives a larger
  accommodation domain rather than shrinking occupants;
- a public/intake or transfer area needs enough width for the intended bodies, escort,
  search/property interaction, and door states;
- the Rung-B keeper landing begins as a visibly raised `2×2` or larger control position
  with legal access and counterplay;
- the secure yard begins large enough for a small group, staff control, sanitation, and
  a real movement decision rather than a decorative rectangle;
- a one-cell corridor is legal only when its capacity and inability to pass are
  intentional facts; high-throughput or two-way staff routes widen;
- bars/grilles, doors, walls, chain gaps, railings, and wards carry honest collision,
  sight, projectile, reach, squeeze, sound, and cover classes; and
- suspended, giant, portal, aquatic, or other unusual holding domains declare their own
  dimensions, gravity/support, transition, and rescue/evacuation requirements.

Clay must test small, six-foot-human, presentation-large, and largest-admitted bodies,
including a deliberate refusal when a rolled holding cannot accommodate the intended
prisoner.

### Route promises

- **Public/legal route:** free exterior → public threshold → oversight/intake →
  authorized release, interview, hearing, visitation, or refusal. It does not grant
  accidental access to cells or property.
- **Staff/control route:** staff entry/rest/service → property/evidence and control →
  custody/routine destinations. Its permissions and schedule are explicit.
- **Prisoner routine route:** held space → meal/water/sanitation/yard/work/transfer as
  licensed. It changes with shift, escort, lock, and alert state.
- **Covert/secret route or handle:** a committed hidden edge, blind spot, compromised
  fixture, contraband/tool chain, key opportunity, social relationship, or schedule
  opening. Not every seed needs a crawlable tunnel.
- **Force/disruption route:** break, lift, burn, cut, climb, overpower, riot, sabotage,
  environmental failure, ward interruption, or structural leverage over truthful
  materials and consequences.
- **Emergency/release route:** operators need a way to evacuate, transfer, reinforce,
  isolate, or lawfully release occupants. This route may overlap staff circulation but
  cannot be invented after a fire, riot, flood, or breach begins.

Every route records actor/body capacity, permission, barriers, noise/exposure, time,
required tool/relationship, reversibility, and consequence. One-way and
observer-specific access stay typed rather than collapsed into one depth value.

### Tactical furnishing

Furnishing supports custody and choice:

- cell fixtures: sleeping/support surface, water/sanitation realization, restraint or
  privacy facts, owned light if any, and occupant expression allowance;
- control: desk/record surface, key/permission system, alarm/signal, observation aids,
  barrier controls, and staff cover only when physically present;
- intake/property: search surface, containers, tags/seals, secure storage, transfer
  space, and claimant records;
- routine/service: meal/water containers, carts, laundry, waste, tools, medical
  supplies, work equipment, visiting barriers, and emergency gear; and
- local handles: ordinary stool, blanket, hook, bucket, papers, marks, loose material,
  improvised tool, or contraband when licensed.

Anything that changes collision, cover, sight, restraint, route, reach, lock state,
light position, capacity, or escape mechanics is geometry or a mechanism. Ordinary
clutter may remain dressing and be promoted when play depends on it.

## Eight operating circuits

### 1. Custody and permission

Tracks held identities, legal/claimed authority, cell/zone assignments, door/grille/
restraint states, escorts, visitors, release/transfer permissions, and breaches.

### 2. Observation, keys, and control

Tracks guard/automation coverage, blind spots, key/seal/ward custody, control-position
access, alarms, signals, and failed observation. Visibility is not omniscience:
occlusion, distraction, shift, darkness, social permission, and damaged systems matter.

### 3. Time, routine, and disposition

Tracks shift change, meals, water, sanitation, yard/work/interview/hearing/transfer
windows, curfew, count, and the captured PC's finite disposition clock. Routine creates
predictable openings and predictable consequences.

### 4. Property and evidence custody

Tracks four distinct classes:

1. attributable prisoner property removed on admission or later held;
2. crime/case evidence held for a court, watch, or investigation;
3. attributed or unattributed contraband/unauthorized property; and
4. institution property issued or controlled by the site.

Each record retains exact item identity, source/owner, custody reason, container,
location, custodian, tag/seal when used, access, transfer, sale/destruction risk,
recovery hook, release authority, and return/disposition.

Captured inventory uses exact instance ids and stores a recovery-grade snapshot. If the
current item event cannot restore identity-preserving instances, implementation must
add a versioned reconciliation path; it may not remove by id and later mint lookalike
items by name.

At Rung A/B, several classes may share a secure cabinet or closet only when capacity,
hazard, ownership, and access permit; they remain distinct records. At Rung C, intake/
search, attributed prisoner property, records, and case evidence separate according to
actual throughput. A dedicated “evidence room” is never assumed merely because the site
is a prison.

### 5. Supply, water, sanitation, and health

Tracks providers, route, cadence, local buffer, distribution, waste destination,
washing, disease/quarantine, medical capacity, and failure pressure. A service can be
external without being imaginary.

### 6. Population and relationships

Tracks prisoners, staff, visitors, factions, classification, solidarity, conflict,
fear, corruption, leverage, refusals, informants, dependents, and possible helpers.
No escape-lever NPC is mechanically predetermined to help or betray.

### 7. Containment integrity and force

Tracks material/ward/support condition, lock state, damage, noise, tools, fire, water,
structural hazard, breached edges, repair, reinforcement, and causal operator response.
Damage does not disappear because the attempt failed.

### 8. Knowledge, records, and aftermath

Tracks charges/claims, warrants, transfer ledgers, maps, observed routines, known and
unknown routes, discovered property location, guard knowledge, escape evidence,
casualties, released occupants, faction response, searches, and later repairs.

The circuits are factorized. `alert`, `lockdown`, `escaped`, or `abandoned` is a readable
summary over them, never a single switch that rewrites every subsystem.

## Strategic gameplay contract

### Captured-start plans

- **Social/legal:** persuade or bargain with a guard, official, fellow prisoner,
  claimant, witness, faction, visitor, or possible lever; challenge a record, pay or
  promise ransom, exchange information, expose corruption, arrange sponsorship, or
  earn a lawful/conditional release.
- **Routine/procedural:** exploit count, meal, water, sanitation, work, yard, transfer,
  visitation, hearing, medical, shift, or service delivery. This spends time and may
  move the PC without immediately breaking custody.
- **Covert/secret:** use a precommitted key opportunity, missed tool, contraband chain,
  blind spot, compromised fixture, concealed edge, old route, acoustic clue, or
  relationship to change access while limiting witnesses.
- **Force/disruption:** break or lift a barrier, sever support, overpower an operator,
  start or survive a riot, sabotage alarm/ward/winch, exploit fire/flood/attack, or use
  a body/spell/tool capability against declared mechanics.

At least three are viable in the committed situation. A high-security prison may make
one family extremely costly, but it may not erase every plan behind narrator fiat.

### Outside-start plans

- **Public/institutional:** visit, investigate, deliver, negotiate, inspect, claim
  property, challenge custody, or use authority.
- **Service/staff:** use delivery, shift, waste, medical, repair, records, or property
  relationships.
- **Covert/spatial:** approach a licensed alternate relation, hidden edge, roof, drain,
  yard, adjacent host, compromised wall, or insider.
- **Direct/force:** breach, assault, sabotage, create evacuation pressure, seize the
  control position, or rescue during transfer.

The same locks, people, routes, records, property, schedules, and containment states
govern both sets. Outside rescuers cannot discover a convenient route that does not
exist for the captured PC, though their permissions, tools, knowledge, and body
capabilities may make different edges usable.

### Site levers

Keys/seals/true names · warrants/charges/records · guard and prisoner relationships ·
shift/count/routine · property/evidence containers · food/water/waste/medical flows ·
yard/work/transfer movement · light/observation/alarm · wall/bar/chain/ward condition ·
contraband/tools · population pressure · disposition clock · external attack, fire,
flood, breach, or authority change.

Every demonstrated lever changes at least one circuit, one access view, and one
understandable consequence.

### Required rejection checks

Reject when:

- custody has no controlled boundary, operator/system, disposition, or release/transfer
  truth;
- a captured replay changes topology, occupants, property, history, or mechanisms;
- the only available plan is waiting, narrator mercy, or one impossible check;
- there is no viable social/legal or routine/covert plan;
- force surfaces have no material/support/durability/noise/consequence facts;
- an “unbreakable” containment law removes all counterplay or lacks provenance;
- a hidden edge, crawlable drain, loose bar, key, missed tool, or helper is invented
  after commitment;
- confiscation removes by name, loses instance ids/snapshots, has no custody location,
  or returns lookalike replacements;
- evidence/property is accessible from prisoner circulation without a committed breach
  or failure state;
- intake/public flow crosses secure custody accidentally;
- staff cannot feed, count, wash, treat, transfer, reinforce, evacuate, or release the
  held population according to the selected operating model;
- external meals, sanitation, medical, evidence, or staffing lacks a provider, route,
  cadence/reliability, access, buffer, or failure consequence;
- repeated cells reroll their whole identity independently, lose stable child ids, or
  multiply major Spice/secrets by child count;
- uniform cells provide no local state/discovery opportunities;
- a culture or realm changes only palette/props while its claimed doctrine has no
  access, routine, construction, permission, property, or service consequence;
- strange material is cosmetic and does not change construction, maintenance, force,
  hazard, light, growth, or containment behavior;
- a holding or route cannot fit its intended body/escort/evacuation need;
- practical light has no owner, task, fuel/power, mount, schedule, or supernatural
  source;
- fixed camera or cutaway hides the custody boundary, control position, required route,
  standee, or force surface; or
- the retained suspended-cell result requires a named-row, material-id, world-id, or
  seed branch.

### Deterministic fallback ladder

1. remove uncommitted loose dressing and reduce crowd/ambient clutter;
2. simplify cosmetic child-cell variation while retaining every stable child and
   discovery promise;
3. shift ordinary furniture within owned reservations and simplify nonmechanical
   history marks;
4. simplify the discretionary room/annex expression while retaining its causal handle
   and any committed route;
5. combine legally compatible administration/support functions with a recorded reason;
6. externalize an eligible service only when a real provider/dependency already passes;
7. simplify an optional alternate approach while preserving at least three genuinely
   different captured-start plan families;
8. reduce repeated assembly count and operational capacity together;
9. degrade one scene rung while preserving custody, oversight, property, pressure,
   services, variation, and both entry-state replays;
10. reject and reroll with typed diagnostics.

Never save a candidate by deleting confiscated property, fabricating a release, removing
the disposition clock, erasing sanitation/service needs, overlapping cells, shrinking
bodies, making walls abstractly unbreakable, converting a secret handle into free
knowledge, or moving committed content for the captured start.

## Culture and custody-doctrine cards

The evidence-backed card record lives in
`../Reference/Prison-Custody-Study/culture-doctrine-cards.md`.

Every card answers:

1. Who claims the authority to hold, and how is that claim made visible/testable?
2. How are prisoners classified, separated, counted, and identified?
3. What controls access: keys, seals, sponsors, names, schedules, witnesses, wards,
   rank, oath, kinship, payment, or another licensed mechanism?
4. How are charges, claims, testimony, property, and release recorded or remembered?
5. Who provides food, water, washing, waste removal, medical support, and staff relief?
6. What movement is routine—yard, work, worship, hearing, visit, bath, meal, transfer?
7. What may occupants alter, keep, display, trade, hide, or inherit?
8. What is considered legitimate force, punishment, restraint, privacy, and care?
9. How do construction, outward face, observation, repair, and emergency release answer
   those practices?
10. What happens when custody is disputed, corrupted, breached, or transferred?

First comparison pair, **SELECTED FOR PROOF**:

- **`PR-DOC-01 — Keeper-House Civic Custody`:** custody is entrusted to a named keeper
  whose public room, household/service edge, knowledge, keys, reputation, and failure
  risk are attached to the holding. The compact form concentrates authority and force
  while making keeper, kin/witness, household routine, yard/service access, and a small
  number of hard boundaries strong counterplay targets.
- **`PR-DOC-02 — Ledger-and-Shift Civic Custody`:** custody belongs to a recorded
  process that survives staff changes through commitment/release authority, numbered
  children, attributed property containers, role-bound access, count/handoff, clearer
  intake/search, and a staff/service route. Record, classification, shift, property,
  and permission mismatches become strong counterplay targets.

The comparison must change at least four construction, circulation, permission,
property, routine, service, relationship, force, or release facts. One must be geometry
or organization, one access/interaction, and one operating consequence. Palette and
symbols are supporting signals only.

Later evidence-backed candidates are separate observation, congregate work-yard,
perimeter/transport, and debt/liberty custody. Hostage/surety and quarantine remain
`RESEARCH-NEEDED`; warded exceptional containment is a licensed world adapter. These
are purposes and institutional answers, not moral endorsements or culture stereotypes.

The earlier `Surety/household custody` proposal is retired as the first-pair card. Its
social/sponsor relationships remain legal occupancy and plan inputs, but the doctrine
itself lacks enough direct evidence for MVP promotion.

## Capture, property, and continuity law

Site 6 does not replace the current capture contract:

- capture in an active non-prison walk reuses a holding-shaped segment or mints one
  minimal holding node in that walk;
- that result may itself be a complete Rung-A holding capsule, including
  `RC-PRISON-01`, without requiring a full civic prison;
- capture does not silently instantiate a nearby jail or teleport the PC into a new
  Golden Site;
- transfer to a real prison requires a causal transition to an already committed or
  explicitly generated Site 6 record; and
- when the active walk is already a prison, capture points into one of its compatible
  stable holding children and preserves the site's existing topology.

On capture:

1. choose/reuse the compatible holding child and record position/knowledge;
2. open or attach the finite disposition clock;
3. choose a possible social lever without deciding allegiance;
4. remove exact carried instance ids through the canonical inventory event;
5. store their full recovery-grade snapshots under `propertyClass: prisoner-property`;
6. record missed/held-on-person exceptions explicitly;
7. commit custodian, location, access, transfer/sale/destruction risk, and recovery hook;
8. expose only what the PC could know.

On recovery/release:

1. validate the actual property container/custodian or lawful return authority;
2. restore the recorded instances without name-based substitution;
3. retain condition, quantity, curse/identity, attachments, and observed history;
4. resolve or advance the recovery hook; and
5. leave an auditable item/property ledger trail.

Generic civic evidence and PC property may share a secure realization only when
capacity, hazard, access, and record rules allow it. They remain distinct custody
records.

## Repeated holding assemblies

The parent assembly owns:

- count, layout, construction era, material, dimensions, grille/door/ward family;
- control sightlines, access, light, ventilation, sanitation, meal, count, and emergency
  method;
- intended classification/capacity and current occupancy pressure;
- shared history/condition and any root event;
- allowed child roles and variation budget; and
- child seed/id derivation and lazy-detail contract.

Each child has a stable id and may vary sparsely through:

- position/role;
- capacity/form;
- occupant/current use;
- condition/history;
- relationship/refusal;
- contraband/property/local discovery;
- sound/smell/light/neighbor information;
- independent bounded Spice; or
- manifestation of a shared root event.

The compiler reserves required roles—accessible, quarantine, observation, overflow,
disciplinary, empty, service-adjacent, or other selected functions—then assigns ordinary
children through deterministic coverage/anti-repeat controls over editable rolls.

Three identical quiet cells in a row may be truthful. Twelve independent demonic murder
mysteries are not.

## Lighting, roofs, ceilings, and cutaway

- Exterior/daylight belongs to world orientation, openings, yard, time, and weather.
- Keeper, intake, property, transfer, medical, work, count, and night-watch lights
  require owners, tasks, mounts, fuel/power, and schedules.
- Cells may be dim or dark. Readable ambient value separation, exposure floor, AO,
  contact, silhouettes, reflected environmental light, and honest shadows are
  presentation support, not fictitious lamps.
- A captured player's view may reveal only a hard doorway pool, corridor rhythm,
  neighboring shadow, or moving guard light while preserving usable geometry nearby.
- Strange materials emit, absorb, grow, hum, heal, or fail only when their source facts
  say so.
- Suspended holdings retain ceiling/anchor/winch/chain/support, drop distance, rescue,
  fall, sway, capacity, and access truth even when the overhead shell is omitted.
- Roofs and ceilings remain world facts. Cutaway hides/ghosts licensed camera-facing
  occluders and caps sections; it does not expose secrets, remove bars, alter line of
  sight mechanically, or redesign the prison for readability.
- Night changes staffing, routine, witnesses, open services, and permissions as well as
  light.

or enclosing street compiles as a separate `EXTERIOR_ARCHITECTURAL_PRECINCT`. A cutaway
