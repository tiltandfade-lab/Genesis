# Tarot Rules
Every party chooses a character that is the party's cartomancer. When these events are triggered, I will ask them to pull a tarot card. If I am playing with real players, the cartomancer will be given my deck during the duration of play
### Dungeon Begins
The cartomancer pulls a card before entering the dungeon. This may increase difficulty, bounty, or create problems indicated by the tarot card. 
### Faction Arc Begins
If a player has a major entanglement with a faction the cartomancer will pull a card to reveal the future behavior of that faction. 
### Accepting Binding Agreements/Bargains
- Swearing an oath to a god, spirit, or ideal
- Accepting a blessing that comes with conditions
- Making a deal with:
    - A demon, fey, hag, or undead power
    - A living faction with long memory (church, order, guild)
- Binding a ghost or spirit into service
- Accepting land, title, or stewardship with obligations
- Promising protection, secrecy, or vengeance _in a witnessed way_
- Taking payment that implies loyalty or exclusivity

# Dice Rolling
## Criticals - Magnitude of Absurdity

### Rolling a 1 - Critical Fail
- 1 is crit failure followed by a d20
- a second 1 will result in catastrophic world changing results
  Example: Patrick rolled a 1 -> 6 and the thermal detonator he tried to kick out the front door went straight above his head, melted reality and teleported all characters into a 1990's synthwave universe where explosions of the thermal detonator continued to ripple and cause all characters to make CON saves periodically
- 20 is comical and a safe failure

### Rolling a 20 - Critical Success
- 20 is crit success followed by d20
- a second 20 will results in paradigm shifting, ludacris positive results 
  Example: (Elias rolled a 20 -> 14 and the blessing of the dawn god changed the entire trajectory of an NPCs life causing him to weep, and tell the PCs everything he knew about the bandit threat, the light of the dawn god still shines on this spot to this day.)
- a second 1 is still a solid win

# Dungeon Engine

## Enemy Generation
- Determine **Faction or Creature Type**
    - Who claims this space?
- Determine **Enemy Posture**
    - Unaware
    - Alert
    - Fortified
    - Hunting intruders
- Determine **Distribution**
    - Static guards
    - Patrols
    - Lair clusters
    - Reserve force
- Establish **Morale Rules**
    - When do they flee, surrender, or reinforce?
## Treasure Generation
Triggered after overcoming danger, meaningful player actions, hidden locations

1. Determine **Treasure Type**
    - Coin
    - Gear
    - Relic
    - Information
    - Favor / access
2. Determine **Condition**
    - Damaged
    - Cursed
    - Incomplete
    - Tracked
    - Contested
3. Determine **Narrative Weight**
    - Mundane
    - Symbolic
    - Faction-linked
    - World-significant
## Problem Generation
### Types of problems
- Traps
- Puzzles
- Environmental threats
- Moral dilemmas
- Resource drains
- Time pressure 
### Procedure
1. Identify **What Is Being Tested**
    - Attention
    - Patience
    - Greed
    - Mercy
    - Coordination
2. Identify **Cost of Failure**
    - Damage
    - Alerting enemies
    - Lost time
    - Broken gear
    - Escalation
3. Identify **Partial Success Outcome**
    - Progress with consequence

## Scenery Generation
For each zone or cluster of rooms:

1. Establish **Material State**
    - Ruined, maintained, corrupted, overgrown, sanctified
2. Establish **Sensory Dominant**
    - Sound, smell, light, texture, temperature
3. Establish **Environmental Hazard (Passive)**
    - Poor footing, low visibility, spores, unstable ceilings, echoes
4. Seed **Clues**
    - Signs of enemies
    - Past violence
    - Failed explorers
    - Ritual use

## Layout Generation

- Determine **Dungeon Scale**
    - Small (5–8 rooms)
    - Medium (9–15 rooms)
    - Large (16+ or layered)
- Determine **Topology**
    - Linear (A → B → C)
    - Branching
    - Looping
    - Hub-and-spoke
    - Vertical (drops, climbs, shafts)
- Establish **Choke Points**
    - Doors, bridges, tunnels, stairs
    - These matter for ambushes, retreats, reinforcements
- Place **One Secret or Optional Path**
    - Shortcut, bypass, hidden room, risky route

# Reality Breach / Intrusion Procedure

_(Optional Chaos Layer)_

## Purpose

This procedure introduces **rare, non-mandatory reality intrusions** into play. These intrusions are intentionally incoherent with setting, genre, and tone. They are not balanced, justified, or explained.

They exist to provoke player reaction, emergent meaning, and disruption of expectation.

Use sparingly.

---

## Core Principle

**Normal results are replaced, not supplemented.**  
When a breach occurs, ignore the standard result entirely.

---

## When This Procedure Is Available

This procedure may be invoked when interacting with:

- Containers (treasure chests, crates, bags, vending machines, lockers)
    
- Rewards (loot, gifts, quest payments)
    
- Locations (rooms, dungeons, sites)
    
- NPC possessions (at DM discretion)
    

The DM decides whether the situation is eligible.  
The procedure is never mandatory.

---

## Step 1: Roll the Intrusion Die

When an eligible interaction occurs, the DM may roll an **Intrusion Die**.

Choose one:

- **d20** → Breach on a **1**
    
- **d100** → Breach on **01–02**
    

If the roll does **not** trigger a breach:

- Resolve the interaction normally.
    
- Do not mention the Intrusion Die result.
    

If the roll **does** trigger a breach:

- Proceed to Step 2.
    

> Optional: Players may be informed that an Intrusion Die exists, but not when it is rolled.

---

## Step 2: Ignore the Normal Result

When a breach triggers:

- Do **not** roll on the normal table.
    
- Discard any previously rolled result.
    
- Do not combine outcomes.
    

Proceed directly to the Reality Breach Table.

---

## Step 3: Roll on the Reality Breach Table

Roll once on the **Reality Breach Table**.

Results on this table:

- May violate genre, technology level, or logic.
    
- May reference modern, mundane, or impossible objects.
    
- Are not required to be useful, magical, or relevant.
    

The result **exists as-is**.

---

## Step 4: Presentation Rules (Critical)

When presenting a breach result:

- Do **not** explain its origin.
    
- Do **not** justify it with lore, planes, time travel, or magic unless the players do so themselves.
    
- Do **not** editorialize.
    

Describe only:

- What it looks like
    
- Its physical state
    
- Any immediately obvious properties
    

If asked whether it is magical:

- Answer honestly based on detection.
    
- Mundane items are mundane.
    

If asked where it came from:

- You do not know.
    

---

## Step 5: Mechanical Handling

Unless otherwise stated:

- Breach items are **non-magical**
    
- They function exactly as their real-world counterparts would, if applicable
    
- They do not auto-scale to usefulness
    

Any mechanical significance emerges only through:

- Player action
    
- Creative application
    
- Consequences adjudicated at the table
    

---

## Table Separation Rule

**Reality Breach Tables must never be merged with normal tables.**

They exist in parallel and are only accessed via this procedure.

This prevents dilution and preserves the shock value of intrusion.

---

## Design Notes (For the DM Only)

- Breaches should feel _rare but absolute_.
    
- One breach per session is usually plenty.
    
- Long stretches without breaches increase their impact.
    
- Players will assign meaning even when none is provided. Let them.
    

Do not rescue the fiction.  
Let the object sit in the room.

---

## Summary (Quick Reference)

1. Eligible interaction occurs
    
2. Roll Intrusion Die (low chance)
    
3. On success, discard normal result
    
4. Roll Reality Breach Table
    
5. Present without explanation
    

---

If you want next steps, the natural extensions are:

- a **Reality Breach Item Table** (pure Gemini-style chaos)
    
- a **Breach Escalation Variant** (multiple breaches distort play)
    
- or a **Tarot–Breach Interaction Rule** (cross-contamination of systems)
    

But this procedure, as written, is already complete and stable.
