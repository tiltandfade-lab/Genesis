### **IX. The Hermit: Trigger Effects**

The Hermit represents introspection, solitude, and the search for inner truth. When this card is drawn, the world pulls inward. Distractions fade, the environment becomes quiet and vast, and the power of the individual is magnified—at the cost of the strength found in numbers.

---

**The Constant: "The Solitary Path"**

The Hermit rewards those who stand alone and punishes those who rely too heavily on the "crowd."

- **The Effect:** Any creature that starts its turn with no allies within 15 feet gains a **+2 bonus** to all attack rolls and ability checks.
    
- **The Toll:** Any creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of an ally takes a **-2 penalty** to all attack rolls and ability checks, as their "inner light" is crowded out by the presence of others.

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

**The Constant: "The Great Withdrawal"**

The faction has ceased all external communications. They have retreated to a hidden or inaccessible location to focus on their internal purity or a singular, secret goal.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**Faction Behavior (The Hermit)**|**Narrative Implication**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Hidden Fortress**|The faction abandons its outposts and fortifies a single, remote mountain peak or deep cave.|
|**6-9**|Textured|**The Vow of Poverty**|The faction destroys all its gold and material wealth, believing it is "poisoning" their spirit.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Ascended Mind**|The entire faction enters a collective meditation and vanishes from the material plane entirely.|
