## Lanes (all in one pass for a background run; report once)

1. **Market halls + plaza typology.** Historic market halls (open-sided ground floors,
   upper chambers), plaza proportions, stall arrangements, fountain/center-object
   placement. Extract: the market hall's structural read at gameplay scale · plaza-edge
   grammar (stalls hug edges? aisles?) · how the fountain claims the center.
2. **THE DRESSING CENSUS (the heaviest lane — the reason this brief exists).** From
   street photography/paintings/plans of pre-industrial towns: enumerate what actually
   dresses a street, and SORT every item into the game's three bins — **material** (a
   §8-style demand slot: plaster color families, brick bonds, timber-frame patterns,
   paving varieties, roof tile/slate/shingle mixes) · **decal/paint** (signage, painted
   trim, grime bands, wheel ruts, doorstep wear, water staining under gutters) ·
   **prop** (awnings, stall goods, barrels/crates, laundry lines, lanterns, pot
   plants, hitching posts). Note which lanterns/lights exist and who owns them (the
   causal light law comes to town). Deliverable: **the urban material + dressing
   possibility roster in STRUCTURE-KIT-CATALOG §8 format** — the direct input for the
   next Codex material brief.
3. **Hillside + stair streets (the terrain-street deck's precedents).** Lombard Street
   itself, stepped streets of Mediterranean/hill towns, sunken lanes, streets
   overlooking streets. Extract: how level streets relate to descending ones (retaining
   walls? railings? building entries on two levels?) · stair-street construction · what
   makes the overlook read at gameplay scale. This lane pressure-tests Adam's ruled
   third deck expression.
4. **Frontage + party-wall construction.** How shoulder-to-shoulder buildings actually
   meet: shared walls, jettied upper storeys, shopfront bay anatomy (stall board,
   sign, door, display), balcony construction, exterior stairs. Extract: the frontage
   bay's geometry/paint split per §5 · the multi-storey street wall's piece list.
5. **FFT trade-city cohort.** The corpus at
   `Reference/FFT-Guard-Post-Study/study-archive/maps/five-angle/` (view 0 = plan;
   id→name at `study-archive/tools/heretic/src/map.c`; exclude test maps 116–120, 125).
   Study the trade cities (22 Gariland · 42 Warjilis · 47 Zarghidas · 99 Lesalia main
   street) plus the slums set (32, 40, 48 — already classified by the camp study;
   reuse, do not reclassify) and 103 Windmill Shed. Extract: FFT's street-slice
   composition (how much street, how much building) · frontage rhythm at gameplay
   scale · where FFT spends dressing vs paint in towns · rooftop/balcony usage ·
   anti-rules.
6. **Touchstones (documentation only, no images).** BG3's city market/street fights if
   documented · any well-documented hillside-town battle scene. Extract what works at
