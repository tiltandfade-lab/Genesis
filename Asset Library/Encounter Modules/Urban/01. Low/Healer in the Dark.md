## 30-Second Run

The streetlamps are smashed. Ankle-deep murky water. Two armed factions standing in darkness behind aqueduct pillars, weapons out — but not fighting. A glowing arcane ward burns in the flooded street, and inside it an exhausted healer is frantically bandaging a wounded civilian. The PCs walk into what looks like an ambush and find something else entirely.

---

## Setup

**Space:** Eastgate crossroads. Pitch black (streetlamps deliberately smashed). Ankle-deep murky water across all paving stones. Thick aqueduct pillars provide heavy cover on all sides.
**Atmosphere:** Broken glass crunches underfoot. The only light is the warm glow of the ward. The silence between armed people is louder than shouting would be.
**NPCs Present:** Exhausted Apothecary (inside ward, working), 4 Eastgate Guards (pillar cover, east side), 4 Gang Members (pillar cover, west side). All weapons drawn, none attacking.
**If Ignored:** Party bypasses via a longer, muddier route. Misses the supplies and the Heroic Inspiration trigger. The factions eventually disperse.

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Approach

The PCs step into the water and hear glass crunch. Every read of the tactical terrain screams combat. A guard steps from behind a pillar and holds up an empty hand: *"Put the steel away, travelers. Nobody else bleeds tonight."*

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 12 Insight:** Both factions are too exhausted and ashamed to fight. Anyone who starts violence now will be viewed as a monster by both sides.
- **DC 13 Perception:** The faction members aren't watching the PCs at all — their eyes are locked on the healer.

**Escalation:** Any hostile action (drawn weapons, magic, raised voices) breaks the truce. Both factions pivot to the PCs. Standard combat — 4 Guards + 4 Gang Members.

---

### Segment 2: The Ward

**DC 13 Medicine (inside the ward):** A PC can assist the apothecary, speeding her work and earning immediate gratitude. If no PC assists, she succeeds alone — it just takes longer and costs her more.

The waterproof satchel is tucked behind pillar cover on the west side — left behind in the initial panic. The apothecary nods toward it if the PCs have been respectful. *"Take it if you need it."*

**Escalation:** If the party waits or helps without starting trouble, the victim stabilizes (see below).

---

### Segment 3: The Stabilization

When the victim takes a deep, ragged breath — faction members exhale. A watchman silently hands a flask to a gang member. The moment lands.

**Every character present immediately gains Heroic Inspiration.**

The scene then ends via the Dispersal table below.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Pitch Black:** All areas beyond the ward's light are Heavily Obscured. Creatures without Darkvision have Disadvantage on attack rolls and Perception checks.
2. **Broken Glass / Water:** Moving at full speed requires DC 10 Acrobatics or the character makes noise — alerting both factions to their position.
3. **Arcane Ward:** The glowing Magic Circle is not a trap. Any creature entering it peacefully is protected from hostile magic (as *Protection from Evil and Good* while inside).
4. **Waterproof Satchel:** Contains 2× Healer's Kit, 3× Antitoxin, 1× Potion of Healing.

---

## Difficulty Dial

**Any Level:** Pressure zero. Adjust escalation threat only if needed.
**Escalated Version:** If the truce breaks, treat the combined faction as 8 Guards (CR 1/8 each). Add the apothecary retreating into the ward for cover.

## Stat Blocks

**Eastgate Guard** — *Human Guard* — CR 1/8
AC 16 | HP 11 | Speed 30 ft.
**Spear.** +3, 1d6+1 piercing.
**Tactic:** Holds cover; won't break the truce unless attacked first.

**Gang Member** — *Human Thug* — CR 1/2
AC 11 | HP 32 | Speed 30 ft.
**Shortsword.** +4, 1d6+2 piercing.
**Tactic:** Mirrors the Watch's tension level. Withdraws if the Watch withdraws.

**Apothecary** — *Human Commoner (Healer)* — CR 0
AC 10 | HP 4 | Speed 30 ft.
**Medicine +4.** Stays inside the ward. Will not fight.
