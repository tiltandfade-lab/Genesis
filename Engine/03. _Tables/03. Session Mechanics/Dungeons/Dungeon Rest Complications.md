| **d20** | **Result**                  | **Narrative & Tactical Impact**                                                             |
| ------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1       | **Assaulted**               | The rest is violently interrupted by a nearby Threat. No recovery benefits.                 |
| 2       | **Encroaching Presence**    | A Threat has set up an ambush or blockade just outside your current room.                   |
| 3       | **Marked**                  | You wake to find a symbol or sign left by an inhabitant; you are being tracked.             |
| 4       | **The Watch Fails**         | A Threat is already inside the room when you wake, having slipped past the sentry.          |
| 5       | **Structural Shift**        | A minor collapse or shift in masonry has blocked the exit. You must find a new way out.     |
| 6       | **Temporal Drag**           | You slept far longer than intended. Roll on the Spatial/Temporal table.                     |
| 7       | **Stagnant Air**            | Poor ventilation makes breathing difficult. Regain only half of your usual health/energy.   |
| 8       | **Local Pests**             | Biting insects or small vermin infested your gear. Disadvantage on your next check.         |
| 9       | **Echoing Horrors**         | Distant, rhythmic sounds prevented deep sleep. Partial recovery only.                       |
| 10      | **The Bone-Chill**          | You wake up stiff and shivering from the damp. Penalty to your next physical action.        |
| 11      | **Heightened Paranoia**     | You keep seeing eyes in the dark. You cannot be surprised in the next segment.              |
| 12      | **Auditory Hallucinations** | You hear voices in the stone. Gain a cryptic lore clue, but no mental recovery.             |
| 13      | **Stiff Joints**            | Sleeping on cold stone has left you clumsy. Disadvantage on your next initiative or reflex. |
| 14      | **Vivid Omens**             | Dreamt of the dungeon’s past. Gain an "Insight" but lose your next Boon.                    |
| 15      | **Damaged Records**         | A leak or moisture has blurred some of your recent journal entries or sketches.             |
| 16      | **Heavy Silence**           | The dungeon is unnaturally still. The rest is successful, but the atmosphere is tense.      |
| 17      | **Secure Position**         | Your barricades held perfectly. You wake feeling refreshed and safe.                        |
| 18      | **Natural Solace**          | You found a surprisingly dry or soft spot. Regain one additional hit die or resource.       |
| 19      | **Hidden Relic**            | While clearing the floor to sleep, you found a small, interesting trinket or old coin.      |
| 20      | **Inspiring Respite**       | The rest was perfect. Gain a temporary Boon for the duration of the next segment.           |
^dungeon-rest-complications
