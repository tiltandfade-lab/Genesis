#child-saw# Child Saw — the witness table (d50)

> PARTIAL-NPC HOOK ATOM ([[NPC-PARTIALS]]) — a child's contribution to the plot is what they
> **witnessed**: they see what adults miss and don't know which part matters. This is the kid's
> hook-carrier roll, kid-framed — a real hook delivered in a child's literal, significance-blind
> voice. It is a lever/witness atom, NOT a full situation: no Band/Pressure/If-Ignored, so do NOT
> tag `table_family: situation` (same exemption as [[npc-want]] / [[child-want]]). Rows run roughly
> **mundane → uncanny** as the die climbs — the low 30 are human secrets a sharp kid could really
> notice; the tail (32+) leans Strange→Mythic for Amblin/Stranger-Things realms. The DM reads which
> part matters. **Kin-agnostic / recontextualize** per convention — "the widow," "the stranger,"
> "the old line" resolve against rolled atoms + codex. TONE: children witness horror but are never
> its graphic subject ([[NPC-PARTIALS]] safety rule). Tags in-format.
