## Dungeon Art Motif — d20

| d20 | Motif | What it looks like (and what it’s good for) |
|---:|-------|----------------------------------------------|
| 1  | **Ward Geometry** | Sigil lattices, circles/squares, boundary lines. Misalignment = hidden panel/route hint. |
| 2  | **Procession Relief** | Figures carrying offerings/tools. One figure subtly points toward a mechanism. |
| 3  | **Beast & Halo** | Local beast under a crown/halo. Foreshadows guardians/hazards. |
| 4  | **Hands & Knots** | Hands tying cords/prayer knots. Knot pattern can encode a simple clue. |
| 5  | **Map Fragment** | Partial plan/shoreline/riverline. “Missing piece” implies a concealed space. |
| 6  | **Saints of Utility** | Holy icon + tools/keys/lanterns. Tool implies interaction method. |
| 7  | **Astral Diagram** | Stars/constellation lines/celestial rings. Alignment *hint*, not required puzzle. |
| 8  | **Votive Marks** | Thousands of scratches/prayers/tallies. Fresh marks = recent activity. |
| 9  | **Warning Glyphs** | Prohibition/taboo symbols. “Forbidden” side often hides the good stuff. |
| 10 | **Chains & Locks** | Locks, shackles, barred doors. Keyhole motif indicates false lock/real latch. |
| 11 | **Masks & Faces** | Repeated faces; one defaced/missing. Missing face hides a compartment. |
| 12 | **Mirror Inlay** | Polished tiles/metal strips. Reflections reveal seams/hidden writing. |
| 13 | **Overpainted Layers** | Old mural under new paint/graffiti. Scraped spot reveals truth/route. |
| 14 | **Civic & Trade Marks** | Stamps, cargo brands, registry seals. Smuggler cache / underworks clue. |
| 15 | **Roots & Serpents** | Tangling roots/serpents. “Root line” leads to a seam/hidden slit. |
| 16 | **Salt & Tide Stains** | Mineral bloom, flow lines. Reveals drainage paths, voids, hidden cavities. |
| 17 | **Fungal Rosettes** | Spore prints/mycelium blooms. Growth patterns indicate airflow/cavities. |
| 18 | **Lightning Glass Veins** | Petrified lightning glass threaded in rock. Veins “point” in low light. |
| 19 | **Runic Palimpsest** | Runes overwritten by newer marks. Older layer reveals original function. |
| 20 | **The marsh’s Curdle** | Bruised-purple staining bands. Where staining stops marks a ward line/threshold. |
^dungeon-art-motif
