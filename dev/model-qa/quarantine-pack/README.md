# The Off-Angle Pack — 78 free game sprites

78 creature/character sprites with transparent backgrounds, sorted by camera angle:

- `high-angle/` (30) — creatures seen from a raised/overhead camera
- `top-down/` (7) — straight overhead
- `other/` (41) — flying swarms, amorphous spirits, unusual compositions

They come from **Genesis**, a solo-TTRPG video game project, where the art law is
strict eye-level pixel art — these sprites were generated off-angle and cut from the
main game rather than deleted. They're coherent, styled, and ideal for a **top-down
or experimental project**: 12 themed "realms" (ash wasteland, cosmic, noir, frontier,
suburbia, high seas, and more) in a pixel-art register.

`manifest.json` describes every sprite: realm, subject label, family
(animal/monster), camera view, and approximate in-world size band.

## Honest provenance

These are **AI-generated images** (image-model output, sliced and keyed
programmatically). If your project or community excludes AI-generated assets,
this pack isn't for you — no hard feelings.

## Known quirks

- Cut from generation sheets with content-aware cropping; a few sprites carry a
  small sliver of a neighboring sprite at one edge.
- Backgrounds were chroma-keyed with un-blending; edges are clean at pixel-art
  scale but haven't been hand-audited.
