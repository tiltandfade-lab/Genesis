| d20 | Tactical Setup |
| :------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1**   | **Narrow alley chokepoint** — funnels combat through a two-abreast gap between buildings; forces melee engagement, shoves and grapples dominate, ranged attacks at disadvantage if attacker in alley |
| **2**   | **Market stall maze** — dense array of vendor booths and carts create waist-high cover; movement halved, ranged attacks from booth-tops, scrambling enemies can vanish into crowd, civilian casualties likely |
| **3**   | **Rooftop flat expanse** — bare stone/tile roof with no cover, exposed to arrows and spells; high ground advantage to whoever secures ladder/stairs, falling 20-40 ft to street below, dangerous if guards pursue |
| **4**   | **Bridge crossing** — narrow span over water/chasm, railings waist-high, flanking trapped (pushed = fall + drowning check), retreat only from bridge ends, barricade points at center, water hazard below |
| **5**   | **Tavern brawl interior** — multi-level common room with tables, bar counter (half cover), overturned furniture, patrons scattering as mobile obstacles, second floor balcony for ranged support, tipsy crowd unpredictable |
| **6**   | **Temple sanctuary** — soaring ceiling (range increases), stone columns provide full cover, upturned pews (half cover), holy font/altar as hazard, noise triggers other patrols, pursuit may violate sanctuary rules |
| **7**   | **Warehouse stacks** — towering crates 15 ft high (can climb, hide behind, push over as hazard), shadowed narrow aisles, second-tier catwalks, fire/oil hazard if crates breach, enemy climbing advantage |
| **8**   | **Courtyard fountain square** — open with central fountain (half cover), building facades with few windows, circular sightlines, mounted guards can control perimeter, nowhere to hide from archer towers |
| **9**   | **Sewer grate district** — ground level broken by 10-ft grates/grating (hidden pits, 15 ft deep), slime-slick surfaces (difficult terrain, DEX saves to avoid slipping), stench and darkness (dim/obscured), branching escape routes |
| **10**  | **Sloped tile rooftop** — pitched roof at 30-45 degrees, movement halved, DEX saves to avoid sliding down, adjacent roof-gap (15 ft across), possible weather hazard (rain/snow), archers have elevated firing line |
| **11**  | **Fortified gate plaza** — barricade of carts/logs at street entrance, defenders on wall above (20 ft), force attacker to assault position, heavy cover for defenders, siege timing matters, mounted charge blocked |
| **12**  | **Bell tower combat** — multiple narrow floors (spiral stairs, ladders), vertical elevation every 20 ft, ranged fighters separated by floors, falls deal extra damage (confined spiral = prone), allies can't easily reach top |
| **13**  | **Merchant wagon formation** — three-four large wagons in defensive circle, wooden sides (half cover), wheels and undercarriage (hazard to crawl under), drivers can crack whips as improvised weapons, mobile if someone takes reins |
| **14**  | **Sewer tunnel network** — low ceiling (8-10 ft, tall creatures prone/squeezed), murky water (movement difficult, cold hazard), branching passages (escape routes), vermin swarms if disturbed, sound echoes (stealth hard) |
| **15**  | **Scaffolding construction site** — wooden platforms at 10-ft intervals, unstable (DC 10 DEX to balance), planks can break (15 ft fall possible), narrow walkways (shove attempts common), building materials as half-cover |
| **16**  | **Outdoor market arcade** — cloth awnings overhead (dim lighting, hanging ropes), vendor chains/ropes create half cover, civilians packed densely, smoke/spice cloud hazard (obscurement), rooftop access via stacked boxes |
| **17**  | **Crumbling building interior** — partial walls, holes in floors (10-20 ft drops), structural collapse risk (loud noise = instability check), wooden beams as climbing routes, shadows and rubble (half cover), escape via side streets |
| **18**  | **Canal-side dock** — narrow wooden planks on either bank (slippery, DEX save), water 15 ft below (cold, current pulls), stacked barrels (half cover, moveable), rope moorings (climbing/swinging options), ship ramp access |
| **19**  | **Guard tower interior** — spiral stairs, arrow slits (cover for defenders, ranged only), confined floors, bell-rope hazard (yanks = alarm rounds), multiple levels (vertical surprise), siege ladder at base |
| **20**  | **Crowd-choked festival street** — dense throng of citizens (half-cover AND difficult terrain), pickpockets and bystanders as hazard, music/noise (stealth impossible), merchant tents overhead, escape into alleys, collateral damage unavoidable |
