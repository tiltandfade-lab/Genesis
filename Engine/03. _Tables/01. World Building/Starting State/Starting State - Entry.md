# Starting State — Entry
> The PC↔world bridge, rolled when a soul is breathed into a world. Connects the rolled character to the rolled town's *present* — so play opens on a situation already in motion, not a cold drop. The five-slot bundle (**Enemies / Friends / Complications / Things / Places**) is **assembled by the procedure**, which fills each slot by *preference order* — (1) the PC's rolled backstory seeds, (2) the world's factions, (3) the standing pressures, then (4) a fresh roll — so the opening reincorporates what already exists. These tables are the framing + the fresh-fallback rolls. Original, IP-clean; standard pipe tables. Orchestrated by `Starting State Procedure v1.0.md`.

## Why You're Here — Fork
> The arrival reason. **Procedure: prefer to derive this from one of the PC's open threads/seeds first** (chasing the vanished lover, lying low because wanted, sent by the former employer); roll here only when nothing pulls.

|d12|You came here…|
|---|---|
|1–2|for work and coin — you go where the work is|
|3|for refuge — lying low, or starting over|
|4|just passing through, or so you told yourself|
|5|sent or summoned — an errand, an order, a request|
|6|searching for someone, or something|
|7|carrying something to deliver|
|8|because a debt or obligation called you|
|9|drawn by a rumor — of wealth, of work, of wonder|
|10|stranded — the road, the ship, or your luck ran out|
|11|on a pilgrimage or under a vow|
|12|recruited — someone wanted you, specifically, for this|

## A Foot in the Door — Fork (one starting asset, here)
> Gives the first lead. **Procedure: prefer a PC seed or a faction the PC already ties to**; else roll.

|d12|You arrive with…|
|---|---|
|1|an old acquaintance or contact in town|
|2|a room already paid for|
|3|a letter of introduction, or a token of trust|
|4|a lead or rumor only you hold|
|5|a small debt owed *to* you here|
|6|a skill the town badly needs right now|
|7|a cache or an item waiting for you|
|8|a patron's eye already on you|
|9|a shared enemy with someone local|
|10|kin here — for better or worse|
|11|a faction that, for now, tolerates you|
|12|nothing — only what you carry|

## Standing with the Local Power — Fork (relation to a rolled faction)
> Wires the PC into the dominant (or a rolled) faction. **Procedure: if a backstory seed already names a tie, use it.**

|d10|To the local power, you are…|
|---|---|
|1–2|a stranger — unknown, unproven|
|3|useful — they mean to use you|
|4|in their debt|
|5|owed by them — a favor on your side of the ledger|
|6|watched, or marked|
|7|mistaken for someone — for good or for ill|
|8|kin, or an old tie, to one of their own|
|9|an old enemy of theirs|
|10|wanted by them for something specific|

## The Opening Tension — assembled, not rolled here
> The first clock the PC feels. **Procedure: aim the nearest-to-firing **Pressure** at the PC** — the internal or external front whose next Grim Portent is closest, re-framed as something the PC will be forced to answer soon. This is where the standing pressures become *this character's* problem. The existing "Trouble at Hand" (world-genesis) remains the immediate first-five-minutes hook layered on top; the Opening Tension is the slow burn it sits within.
