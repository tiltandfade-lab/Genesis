> [!info] T3 Loot Budget Reference (Lv 11–16)
> Based on DMG 2024 tier targets (per-party cumulative across the tier): 5 Common, 7 Uncommon, 4 Rare, 2 Very Rare, 2 Legendary. Budget below is one dungeon's share (same per-dungeon-share logic as T1/T2; tunable in play). The generator computes from segment count; deepest rooms get rarest items; boss gets the top-rarity slot.

| **Dungeon Size** | **Segments** | **Common** | **Uncommon** | **Rare** | **Very Rare** | **Legendary** |
| ---------------- | ------------ | ---------- | ------------ | -------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| Small | 5–8 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| Medium | 9–12 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| Large | 13+ | 2 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 1 |

> **Distribution:** Legendary → Boss room. Very Rare → second-deepest cluster. Rare → mid-depth. Uncommon → shallow rooms. Common → remaining. All other rooms receive coin only (see Dungeon Loot - Coin Cache). Artifacts are story placements, not budgeted (see Dungeon Loot - Artifact).
