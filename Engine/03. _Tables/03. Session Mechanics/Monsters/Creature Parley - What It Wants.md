#creature-parley-wants
> **The lever that makes a creature stand down** — the concrete thing it would *take* in exchange for not fighting: a deal, a bribe, a way out, a thing returned. The non-NPC analog of `[[NPC Want]]`/`[[NPC Leverage]]`, and **what the social resolver checks against** when the PC tries to talk past, buy off, or scare off a creature (`docs/SOCIAL.md` §4). **This replaces the DM inventing, on the spot, whether a monster can be reasoned with and what it would take.** Complements `[[Monster Motivation]]` (its *goal*) and `[[Monster Behavior if Hunted]]` (how it *runs*): Motivation seeds the creature's opening attitude, this seeds the *price*. If the PC can **give the thing**, it can auto-shift one step (decisive leverage, `SOCIAL.md` §2.1). A truly mindless creature, or one whose want *is the PC* (a starving predator, a bound construct), has **no entry here** — the DM says so honestly and it stays a fight-or-flee problem (`docs/DM-CHARTER.md` §5). Skill is the DM's read by creature (Cha presence vs. Wis/Animal-Handling); the resolver is identical either way.
