> **PROVISIONAL — Adam skim pending.** The THEATER realm's item universe (docs/BREACH.md §2d,
> BATCH3-GUARDRAILS.md J2 restructure). **Re-authored 2026-07-03 to ADAM-REVIEW-2 §3/§3b**
> (doers-only — pointers extracted to `docs/REALM-PLOT-ITEMS-PARKED.md`; coherence laws: wired
> physics only, cross-breach R1s, no undefined referents, Volatile = live-danger items). d50;
> breach draws inside a `theater`-tagged walk roll THIS table; legacy Outlandish d300 stays the
> anachronism-intrusion grab-bag. Register per J3b: War, any war — never named, never flagged;
> shapes without nations, real atrocity never loot (docs/BREACH.md §2d content-safety,
> BATCH-GUARDRAILS G0/G9). This realm carries the **era-lens** sub-table (`data/realms.js`
> `REALMS.theater.eraLens` — trench/hedgerow/legion/musket/longship/jungle/siege): every row is
> written era-AGNOSTIC (no named nations, no named conflicts, no real atrocity depicted or
> looted) so a DM can present it through whichever lens the current breach rolled; where an
> item leans toward one lens it's noted, but nothing requires a specific lens to make sense.
> Every item names its FRAME (existing `data/items.js` id); none invented. **Band shares run
> hotter than H2's baseline by construction** (see Frontier's frontmatter). Rank ladders sit on
> enchanted + signature rows; reality-breaking rungs floor at L9. d50, Commitment ceiling.
