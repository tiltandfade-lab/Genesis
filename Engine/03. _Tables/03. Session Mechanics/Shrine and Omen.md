> **PROVISIONAL — Adam spot-check pending.** A walk/interior/place dressing lane
> (`[[TABLE-GAPS-070126]]` §5) — rows bind to the WORLD'S OWN rolled myth and taboo by reference,
> not by inventing new lore: the row is a frame, and the world's already-rolled myth/taboo fills
> the bracketed slot at roll time (`[the myth]` / `[the taboo]`). This is how the myth finally
> circulates — a shrine or omen the party actually meets, carrying content the world-genesis roll
> already committed to canon. Spice-graded d100, Commitment ceiling (66 Grounded · 20 Textured · 9
> Strange · 4 Volatile · 1 Mythic). Five band-anchor rows (marked ★) are Adam's approved samples
> from `TABLE-GAPS-070126.md` §6, included verbatim; the rest extend their register.
