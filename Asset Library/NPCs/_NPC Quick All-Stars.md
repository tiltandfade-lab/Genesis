It is no trouble at all! Adding last names is actually a great way to ground these characters in the setting.

For the **Humans**, I have assigned surnames based on the official ethnic groups found along the frontier coast and the Forgotten Realms (Heartlander, Northfolk, Highlander, Southerner, Old-Blood, Steppefolk, Shou, and Desertfolk).

For the **Non-Humans**, I have pulled from the official Clan, Family, and House names specific to their races (such as Dwarven clans, Elven family names, and Halfling families). Since Half-Orcs, Half-Elves, and Tieflings often adopt the naming conventions of the culture they were raised in, I have given them appropriate Human or Elven surnames.
