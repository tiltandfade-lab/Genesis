# Vera Valen

*(Asset: guard_noble_chokepoint_instability)*

## Level 1 — Immediate Table Presence

**Race / Background:** Human (Heartlander); Minor noble investing in shipping ventures  
**Role:** Guard / Watch (authority-adjacent, limited power)  

**Visual Quirk:** Small ritual mark drawn in ash at hairline  
**Mood:** Slightly afraid, masking it  
**Demeanor:** Clenches jaw when challenged  
**Hook:** Needs a rare ingredient today  
**Talent:** Trained militia drill leader  
**Unrealized Potential:** Yes  

---

## Level 2 — Structural Pressure

**Formative Trauma:** Accidentally caused someone serious harm  
**Formative Grace:** Helped rescue someone successfully  

**Want:** Finish an obligation before consequences arrive  
**Fear:** Breaking tradition (believes consequences are real even if unproven)  

**Faction Tie:** Financially tied to local merchants  
**Influence Weight:** 2 — Small Circle (crew, watch, merchant contacts)  
**Resource Control:** Control over a choke point (bridge, gate, or primary dock access)  

**If Ignored (Personal):** Reprioritizes small tasks to reduce stress  
**If Ignored (Regional):** Dock Guild begins arming members quietly  

---

## Level 3 — Secrets

**Secret:** The secret is far larger than they realize; its full implications are unknown even to them  
**Leverage:** Has the means to sabotage infrastructure  
**If Cornered:** Escapes using a pre-planned route  

---

## Potential Quest / Adventure Tie-Ins

- Rare ingredient needed for ash ritual is tied to forbidden maritime rites.
- Merchant investors pressure her to restrict dock access selectively.
- Players discover sabotage plans hidden within infrastructure oversight.
- The harm she caused in the past connects to dock guild radicalization.
- Her “unknown larger secret” ties into cult manipulation or foreign leverage.

---

## Notes for Play

- Jaw tightens visibly when challenged or questioned.
- Keeps tone professional but guarded.
- Touches ash mark unconsciously when stressed.
- Talks about “tradition” often — avoids explaining why.
- Has contingency plans for retreat.
