# Character Genesis

> Rolls a **soul** into being the way `_START_New World` rolls a world into being. Two interlocking layers (full spec: `CHAR-CREATION.md`). Player chooses + rolls openly (DM-agency); the AI DM guides the player through the chain and weaves. The app rolls + records; the chat narrates. Backstory people/threads auto-seed the World State Ledger as canon.

## Layer 1 — The Sheet (guided L1, mechanical)
Player **chooses** (open): Species (9 SRD) · Class (12) · Background (4 SRD → grants the origin feat + ability bumps).
Player **rolls** (open): ability scores — **4d6 drop lowest ×6** (`CHAR-CREATION.md` locked method). Assign by the SRD "Standard Array by Class" priority as a *suggested* default; player/DM may override.
App **computes** (cheap): modifiers · HP (class base + Con) · base AC (10 + Dex) · Prof Bonus +2 · Passive Perception (10 + Wis [+2 if proficient]) · save/skill proficiency lists.
App **points** (never duplicates): L1 features, starting-equipment A/B, feat + spell text → `Reference/SRD-Data/` (`classes.md`, `equipment-*`, `spells.json`, `character-origins-feats.json`), resolved by the DM at weave-time.

## Layer 2 — The Life ("Life & Origins")
Roll the chain in [[Life & Origins]] — the genericized, public-safe biography suite (the XGE transcription that seeded its structure is heritage-only in `zz_Archive/`). Player rolls each table openly, DM weaves:
1. **Origins** — Parents → Birthplace → Siblings (Number, Birth Order) → Family (→ Absent Parent) → Family Lifestyle → Childhood Home → Childhood Memories.
2. **Personal Decisions** — Background "I became…" (keyed to the chosen background) + Class Training "I became…" (keyed to the chosen class).
3. **Life Events** — Life Events by Age → roll Life Events that many times → branch to the secondary tables (Adventures / Arcane / Boons / Crime+Punishment / Supernatural / Tragedies / War / Weird Stuff).
4. **Supplemental** — Alignment / Cause of Death / Class / Occupation / Race / Relationship / Status, called by other rows or on demand.

## Seeding — the soul writes the world
At bind-time, promote backstory people + threads into the World State Ledger as **write-once canon** (see [[Life & Origins]] §Seeding map), tagged to the originating character so a successor's past never overwrites a predecessor's. These become live fuel for reincorporation, NPC life-events, and the lie/motive systems.

## Successor
Death rolls the existing **fate d20 (≥11 = successor)**. The successor is generated by **this same procedure** — a new soul, optionally arriving near the predecessor's unresolved Ledger threads. The generator *is* the successor generator.

## Headline
Derive a one-line card label from the rolled layers (e.g. "a {background} {species} {class} who {strongest life-event hook}"). The depth lives in the layers; the headline is just the strip label. The old flat `SPARK` list is retired.
