
> Not all content is equal.  
> Not all content is permanent.

---

## 1. the Vale 
(Playtest Environment)

Purpose:
- Engine testing
- Procedural stress tests
- Mechanical experimentation
- Wild scenario trials

Rules:
- Canon is unstable.
- Retcons are allowed.
- Systems may break.
- Characters may exist only as test entities.
- World logic can be temporarily ignored to test mechanics.

Do NOT:
- Over-invest emotionally in continuity.
- Polish lore beyond what is needed to test a system.
- Migrate content to Firmament prematurely.

Promotion Rule:
Content only moves to Firmament if:
- It runs cleanly.
- It produces strong play.
- It survives at least one real session without structural cracks.

---

## 2. Firmament (Canon World)

Purpose:
- Stable campaign continuity.
- Long-term character arcs.
- World consequence tracking.
- Narrative gravity.

Rules:
- Retcons require justification.
- Systems must be stable.
- No experimental mechanics unless tested in the Vale.
- Canon changes persist.

Content enters Firmament only through promotion.

No direct design here without prior testing.

---

## 3. Asset Library (Modular Content Bank)

Purpose:
- Reusable dungeons.
- Neutral NPC frameworks.
- Plug-and-play towns.
- Encounter modules.
- Tables stripped of setting-specific lore.

Rules:
- No deep lore.
- No canon relationships.
- No timeline anchoring.
- No faction entanglement unless abstracted.

Assets must be:
- Setting-neutral.
- Mechanically stable.
- Easily skinnable.

Example:
"Bandit Captain guarding ruined watchtower"
NOT
"Ser Garion, disgraced knight of the frontier town who betrayed the Silver Order"

---

## 4. Promotion Path

the Vale → (Refactor & Strip Lore) → Asset Library → (Skin & Contextualize) → Firmament

Never skip layers.

---

## 5. Design Priority

Primary Goal:
Build durable, modular assets.

Secondary Goal:
Build canon narrative.

Never reverse this priority.

Reusable assets are long-term power.
Canon is a layer applied on top.

---

## 6. Refactor Philosophy

When moving content:
- Remove specific names.
- Remove setting references.
- Replace factions with archetypes.
- Keep stat blocks intact.
- Preserve mechanical integrity.

Lore is paint.
Structure is steel.
