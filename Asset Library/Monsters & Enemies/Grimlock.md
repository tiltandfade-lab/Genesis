*p. 160 MM 2024*

Grimlocks were once human. Their worship of mind flayers over generations of life in the Underdark transformed them into blind, monstrous cannibals. Their eyes withered and sealed shut long ago, leaving only covered eye sockets behind. What they lost in sight, they more than compensated for in their other senses: grimlocks hear the faintest footfall or whisper echoing through stone passageways, track by scent like a bloodhound, and speak in tones too low for most humanoids to detect. The odors of sweat, flesh, and blood awaken hunger, and they leave deliberate scent trails — piles of dung, viscera — in their territories to warn their own kind and track intruders.

For a grimlock, sightlessness is not a weakness. They are completely unaffected by visual illusions, magical darkness, or the Blinded condition. They move through their Underdark territories with the fearlessness of creatures that have never needed to see, and they are nearly impossible to detect before they strike.

Grimlocks still venerate mind flayers and serve them whenever possible. They also recall, in a tribal cultural memory, the ancient war in which they were driven underground. To them, it has never ended. They continue to surface periodically to abduct mortals for their illithid masters — and for their own consumption.

### d10 The Pack's Tactic (Encounter Setup & Combat Behavior)

A single grimlock is not the threat. The threat is that wherever there is one grimlock, there are eight more in positions the party cannot see, oriented entirely by sound, waiting to be given permission to close in. Run them as a coordinated sensory net, not a brawl.

| d10 | Pack Behavior | Setup & Mechanical Execution |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **1** | **The Silence Ambush** | The grimlocks have been crouching in total darkness for hours, conserving all sound. They cannot be heard with passive Perception. The first sign of their presence is a club connecting with the rearmost PC. |
| **2** | **The Sound Funnel** | The grimlocks have spent time filling a passage with debris that will crumble loudly when stepped on. Every step the party takes generates a locating sound the grimlocks triangulate without error. They know exactly where each PC is. The party does not know where the grimlocks are. |
| **3** | **The Torch Hunters** | Light is a targeting beacon, not a comfort. Any PC carrying a light source in grimlock territory is giving every grimlock within 30 ft. a free Perception check to pinpoint their exact location. The grimlocks will close on the light-bearer first, specifically to eliminate the party's psychological comfort. |
| **4** | **The Dung Trail** | The party has unknowingly walked through a grimlock scent marker — a smear of viscera on the floor that signals "intruder." Every grimlock in the dungeon now knows which corridor they're in and roughly what they smell like. DC 15 Survival to recognize the marker before stepping on it. |
| **5** | **The Illusion Immunity** | A spellcaster in the party tries a *minor illusion*, *silent image*, or *major image*. The grimlocks don't blink. They redirect toward the sound of the spellcaster's voice and footsteps, completely ignoring the illusion. |
| **6** | **The Surface Raid** | The grimlocks have surfaced at night to abduct livestock or people from an isolated farmstead. They are unnerved by the open space and move in tighter formations than normal — but they can still track by scent across a dirt field better than any tracker the party has. |
| **7** | **The Mind Flayer's Vanguard** | The grimlocks are escorting a mind flayer through the Underdark toward a humanoid settlement. The flayer stays 30 ft. back and directs the grimlocks telepathically. Killing the grimlocks does not stop the flayer. The flayer is watching everything. |
| **8** | **The Captive Caravan** | A chain of four abducted humanoids — hands bound, heads covered — is being herded through the tunnels by a group of grimlocks. The grimlocks respond to any liberation attempt by immediately scattering into the dark with their captives. |
| **9** | **The Deafening Option** | A creative party might use a *thunderwave*, a bell, or an improvised loud noise to temporarily Deafen all grimlocks in range, reducing their Blindsight from 30 ft. to 10 ft. The grimlocks respond to this by immediately closing to melee range — they know what it means when they can't hear. |
| **10** | **The Tracker's Nightmare** | The party killed a grimlock earlier and continued into the dungeon without concern. Grimlocks can smell the blood of their own dead from 60 ft. The entire local pack has been converging on the party's trail since that kill. They arrive at the end of this encounter as reinforcements — from behind. |

### d10 The Grimlock Lair (Investigation & Signs of Occupation)

Grimlock territories in the Underdark are navigable to those who know what to look for — and deeply unpleasant to those who don't.

| d10 | Environmental Sign | What It Means |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **1** | **The Smooth Wall Path** | A specific corridor has had all sharp edges and loose stones meticulously removed at shoulder height — a clearly navigated path made safe by touch over years of use. DC 12 Survival to recognize this as a cleared travel route. |
| **2** | **The Scent Boundary** | A horizontal smear of viscera across the tunnel at chest height. DC 13 Survival: this is a territorial marker. Something on the other side of this line considers this its space. |
| **3** | **The Sound Map** | Small pebbles have been wedged into specific cracks in the walls, each producing a subtly different tone when the air pressure changes. DC 15 Investigation to realize the entire cavern is instrumented as an acoustic early warning system. |
| **4** | **The Bone Graffiti** | Bones from past kills have been wedged into wall crevices in a pattern — not decorative, but functional. DC 14 Arcana or DC 12 with Undercommon: these are navigation markers, telling grimlocks which tunnels lead to water, food, or their masters. |
| **5** | **The Missing Stones** | Certain floor stones in the corridor are absent — removed and placed 5 ft. away. The gaps are barely visible in low light. Stepping in one: DC 12 Dex save or turn an ankle (Disadvantage on Dex checks for 1 hour). The grimlocks know exactly where each gap is. |
| **6** | **The Stripped Campsite** | A previous party's campsite, stripped of everything edible or scented. The sleeping rolls remain. The food sacks, the tallow candles, and the party's rope (soaked in sweat) are gone. The grimlocks took everything they could smell. |
| **7** | **The Fresh Blood** | A trail of human blood leads deeper into the dungeon. DC 12 Medicine: the blood is from multiple people, dripping at irregular intervals — consistent with a stumbling prisoner being herded. This is recent. Within the last two hours. |
| **8** | **The Mind Flayer Shrine** | A crude altar of stacked stone bearing a carved tentacled face. Grimlocks leave fresh kills here — sometimes still moving — as offerings. The offerings are always gone by the next day. |
| **9** | **The Echo Chamber** | A natural cavern in the territory has extraordinary acoustics — voices from the entrance can be heard anywhere in the cavern. The grimlocks use this as their meeting point. Any conversation the party has here is overheard by every grimlock within range. |
| **10** | **The Survivor** | A captured adventurer, half-mad from days in absolute darkness, has managed to hide in a crevice the grimlocks can't reach. They have been surviving on dripping water. They know the layout of this section of the dungeon better than anyone alive — and they are desperate enough to tell the party everything if freed. |
