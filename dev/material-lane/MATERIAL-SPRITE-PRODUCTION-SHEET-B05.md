# Batch 5 — Makeshift / Tribal Vernacular

Six complete albedo authorities were produced with built-in ImageGen, one call per asset, under
Adam's binding sprite-first rule. MM adds restrained structural depth but preserves exact albedo.

| Material | Selected authority | Seam route | X/Y p95 |

The wattle and thatch prompt retries retained good internal construction but failed vertical phase.
Their selected sprites therefore use a complete repeating unit extracted from the richer retry and
rebuilt modularly. Structural joints were never edge-blended. Mud daub is the only continuous
field and received the narrow toroidal lock.

All six MM v001 graphs pass source identity, seam inheritance, measurable height/normal signal,
Godot 4 ORM, and dual-run byte determinism. The review fixture is 5.2 × 1.65 with UV repeat
3.1515 × 1 and X/Y texel scale 1.000.
