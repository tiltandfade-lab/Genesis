# GOLDEN VIGNETTE VISUAL GUIDE

## 0. Purpose and authority

This is the visual front door for the unified Procedural Vignette Synthesizer. Its
target is:

> **FFT's composition economy + Triangle Strategy's material and presentation
> discipline + Genesis's persistent, roll-authored visible consequence.**

The sentence describes a transfer of principles, never a transfer of layouts, meshes,
textures, palettes, or other protected content.

This guide turns Adam's binding art rulings and the retained
`Reference/FFT-Triangle-Strategy-World-Study/` evidence into one review grammar. It
does not replace its owners:

- `ART-DIRECTION-CANON.md` remains the verbatim founder law.
- `GOLDEN-SITE-PROCEDURAL-VIGNETTE-MASTER-PLAN.md` owns build order and wave gates.
- `BATTLEMAP-TOWNTRAY-COMPOSITION.md` owns composition and surface assembly.
- `TERRAIN-PROGRAM.md` owns terrain features and terrain synthesis.
- `MATERIAL-LANE.md`, `GROUND-MATERIALS-PROGRAM.md`, and
  `TRIM-SHEET-PIPELINE.md` own material production.
- `ASSETFORGE.md` owns the primary factory envelope: typed manifests, quarantine,
  specialist-tool orchestration, proofs, receipts, and explicit admission.
- `GOLDEN-SITE-WORLD-CONTEXT-PROJECTION.md` owns the world beyond the active window.
- `ART-DEPARTMENT.md` owns the pixel-figure register.

The research packet remains evidence, including its estimates and unanswered founder
questions. Numbers promoted here are marked **hard**, **soft**, or **provisional**.
They do not become universal law merely because a reference-game cohort measured that
way. The machine-readable companion preserves the same distinction.

## 1. The authoring order

Beauty is multiplicative, but it is not unordered:

1. **Story and play truth:** role, purpose, operating state, claim, route, objective,
   interaction, persistence, and source provenance.
2. **Composition:** one spatial sentence, ranked masses, approach, decision, quiet
   ground, height, and an honest edge.
3. **Geometry:** connected natural land, deliberate construction, causal joins,
   affordance-bearing thickness, and navigable connectors.
4. **Faces and materials:** coherent surface families, value hierarchy, construction,
   culture, condition, causal wear, trim, markings, and evidence.
5. **Light and atmosphere:** a readable key, motivated practicals, restrained grade,
   weather, and floor-safe focus.
6. **Citizens and objects:** figures, props, effects, and state marks that share the
   world's value, palette, light, and contact.
7. **Context:** true continuation, support, and far premise without false affordance.
8. **Persistent consequence:** instance-bound traces that make revisited places
   remember.

A later layer may reveal a weakness in an earlier one; it may not conceal it. Texture
cannot create a stair, fog cannot rescue a shapeless skyline, props cannot replace a
spatial sentence, and post-processing cannot make disconnected tile events into a
hill.

## 2. Compact composition

### 2.1 One spatial sentence

Every active window should be describable in one short relationship:

- a road climbs under a shoulder to a guarded crown;
- a stream cuts between two fighting shelves;
- a street rises between frontage and a roof lane;
- a switchback crosses a hillside below a ruined tower; or
- a gate spans the route while an undercroft offers a second approach.

If the sentence is only a noun inventory, the composition is not done.

### 2.2 Ranked masses and quiet ground

The FFT study found a durable **soft target** of two to four broad masses, ranked
rather than evenly noisy. One mass leads, one answers, and any remainder supports.
Natural and constructed masses may interlock, but their origin must remain legible.

Quiet ground is reserved composition. It creates deployment space, contrast, actor
readability, route anticipation, and relief from the engine demonstrating itself on
every cell. Dressing does not receive permission to consume it merely because the
space is empty.

### 2.3 Begin at the decision

The first meaningful choice should normally arrive within roughly one to three cells
of entry (**soft calibration**, not a universal cap). Arrival may reveal an immediate
route split, cover choice, height commitment, interaction, or threat. Long mandatory
approaches and backtracking belong in prose, linked windows, or compressed travel
unless decisions justify the walked distance.

### 2.4 Worldfulness without acreage

Large places are persistent networks of compact windows. Scale comes from height,
depth, cutaways, linked frontiers, sibling windows, visible context, remembered state,
and relationships—not empty traversal. Extra-large Golden Site overview images remain
site-premise studies, not playable-footprint goals.

## 3. Natural ground, construction, height, and joins

### 3.1 The controlled mixture

The target is neither smooth soup nor universal blocks:

- natural hills, hollows, shoulders, saddles, banks, ravines, gullies, dips, and
  ordinary rises are connected responsive ground;
- human and creature construction may be chunky, thick, stepped, jointed, repaired,
  cut, and visibly assembled;
- creature-made lairs may use constructed logic without imitating human masonry; and
- cliffs, canyon walls, crevices, collapses, root undercuts, and other natural hard
  breaks remain legal when a visible cause owns the discontinuity.

A pure cavern does not imply a lair. A lair requires a claimant or ecological purpose,
and its shaping, wear, debris, access, and markings should look caused by that creature.

### 3.2 No height cap

There is no one-quantum, five-foot, or two-storey global terrain limit. Height is
licensed by the composition, routes, support, tactical surfaces, camera, and compact
window. Spend verticality on a hillside, crown, drop, switchback, gallery, roof lane,
tower, or scenery-only mass when it strengthens the spatial sentence.

Tall architecture and centerpiece masses begin camera-far in the authored starting
bearing by default. The other quarter-turn views exist partly to reveal the ground they
occlude.

### 3.3 A hill is a field, not a tile collection

Neighbouring natural cells negotiate shared edges and gradients. Tile facets may vary
and the grid may remain lightly readable, but the terrain must carry hills, bumps,
dips, rises, and falls across cells. Independent random tile slopes, exposed voxel
risers at ordinary grade changes, alternating diagonal zipper seams, repeated parallel
sedimentary bands, and a cosmetic smooth skin over discontinuous logic are hard
rejections.

### 3.4 Switchbacks and transitions

A switchback is a route-shaped landform: graded run, turn landing, cut bank, fill
shoulder, drainage, reverse slope, and a relationship to its destination. Its turns
must create tactical and visual consequences. It is not a sawtooth ribbon or a stack of
equal ramps.

Meso-scale transitions make macro form believable: shoulder, saddle, foot, crest,
bench, cut, fill, talus, bank, retaining toe, spill, spoil, and root heave. Material
detail should reinforce those transitions only after the geometry carries them.

### 3.5 Causal joins

Every natural/constructed boundary names a relationship such as `CUT_INTO`,
`RESTS_ON`, `RETAINS`, `BRIDGES`, `PINS`, `ABUTS`, or `BURIES`. Use retaining faces,
footings, cuts, spill, drainage, coping, supports, and accumulated debris to explain
how masses meet. A building or wall simply perched on an unrelated terrain cell has
failed even if collision is legal.

## 4. Faces and materials

### 4.1 Materials are identity and history, not decoration

Geometry owns shape, support, collision, cover, sight, walkability, interaction reach,
and openings. Materials own much of what lets the player recognize:

- what a surface is made from;
- who made, maintains, claims, repairs, or defaces it;
- what work or weather acts on it;
- which face is exposed, sheltered, wet, worn, burned, repainted, or rebuilt;
- where the eye should land; and
- which changes belong to this persistent instance.

That is why material work cannot be a generic texture randomly assigned after a map is
done. It also cannot be fully requested before the geometry has produced real faces.

### 4.2 The two-stage demand

**Stage 1 — `SEMANTIC_IDENTITY_RESERVATION`, before topology.** The semantic plan reserves
recognition opportunities: a custody threshold must communicate purpose; a guard post
needs a readable controlled approach; a lair needs claimant-shaped evidence; a ruin
needs physical-state evidence without implying that it is dormant or unoccupied. A
reservation names the communication obligation, likely visibility, interaction, and
priority. It does not invent a wall, material id, decal, or production lane.

**Stage 2 — `SURFACE_MATERIAL_DEMAND`, after geometry.** Once terrain and construction
emit actual faces with stable local frames, each relevant face can request exact
surface treatment. The demand binds to a `surfaceRef` or `faceRef` and records:

- face role, local origin/normal/tangent/bitangent, mask, and texel scale;
- mechanical truths that treatment may clarify but never change;
- realm, culture, construction method, material family, and maintenance doctrine;
- age, condition, moisture, damage, repair, magic, use, and emphasis channels;
- trim, joint, crest, signage, heraldry, decal, or shallow-extrusion reservations;
- causal source facts for wear and mutation;
- candidate production lanes and a truthful fallback; and
- projection/camera visibility requirements.

The material resolver may then choose an admitted existing surface, Material Maker
output, ground recipe, trim atlas, decal, shallow extrusion, procedural fallback, or
unresolved demand. The semantic role never hard-codes a vendor or asset filename.

### 4.3 Canonical face roles

The shared vocabulary must cover at least:

| role | typical job |
|---|---|
| `WALKABLE_TOP` | ground, floor, landing, platform, street, plaza |
| `NATURAL_SLOPE` | connected hill, shoulder, bank, hollow |
| `CUT_FACE` | excavated earth/rock, road cut, trench, quarry face |
| `RETAINING_FACE` | constructed or creature-made restraint of grade |
| `WALL_FIELD` | primary vertical construction field |
| `CROWN_COPING` | cap, parapet top, exposed crown, wall protection |
| `JAMB_REVEAL` | opening thickness, threshold side, window/door reveal |
| `ROOF_DECK` | roof, upper fighting lane, canopy top |
| `WATERLINE_EDGE` | wet/dry transition, bank, tide or flood evidence |
| `UNDERSIDE_SUPPORT` | bridge underside, pier, beam, vault, overhang support |
| `MOUNT_FACE` | sign, crest, fixture, door, lever, claimant mark |
| `SCENERY_FACE` | visible nonplayable continuation or vertical premise |

More specialized roles may refine these. They must retain a stable relation to the
shared family rather than generating a camera-direction texture taxonomy.

### 4.4 Small coherent families

Three to six broad material families per active window is a useful **soft budget**,
not a hard count. A family spans compatible ground/floor, structural field,
riser/retaining, roof/deck, trim/edge, support, and decal roles as appropriate.
Variation comes from construction, panel choice, value/tint bands, normals,
roughness, joints, wear masks, trim, and instance-bound state—not a new texture for
every cell, wall, compass direction, or map.

Materials within one scene should share pixel DNA, texel density, value logic, realm
palette, and light response. A sprite, wall, roadway, trim strip, and prop need not
share literal colors; they need to look as though the same world and display process
made them.

### 4.5 Value, color, roughness, and focus

The environment remains quieter than actors and decisive accents, but “quiet” does
not mean uniformly dark or low-contrast:

- protect readable separation between tops, vertical faces, recesses, trims, and
  figures through value, temperature, roughness, and edge rhythm;
- allow one controlled accent family to lead the eye;
- allow a sun-keyed face or motivated practical to claim the highlight ceiling when
  the realm and lighting state support it;
- keep broad terrain less saturated than focal figures and accents; and
- test in grayscale and at gameplay/contact-sheet scale.

The study's `p98 >= 85` daylight proposal and `±25%` texel-density experiment are
**provisional A/B calibration**, not universal shipping thresholds. Dark realms,
night scenes, interiors, snow, metal, and emissive conditions need their own
evidence-based envelopes.

### 4.6 Causal wear and persistent mutation

Wear must answer “what did this?” Foot traffic follows routes and thresholds; water
collects by grade and drainage; soot follows combustion; impact damage follows
exposure; moss follows moisture and shade; maintenance interrupts decay; claimant
marks follow authority; creature abrasion follows bodies and repeated access.
Uncaused evenly distributed grunge is a rejection.

Persistent state belongs to the affected object or surface instance. Scorch, repaint,
repair, occupation, flooding, defacement, and claimant marks use instance-bound
receipts and must not mutate a shared parent material or every sibling instance.

### 4.7 Ruin is one axis

`RUIN` is a physical state, not an operating model. A ruined place may be active,
occupied, repaired in part, contested, sacred, inhabited, or dormant. Material and
condition channels may show collapse, weathering, salvage, patching, and age; they
may not infer vacancy, abandonment, or faction ownership without source facts.

## 5. Light, atmosphere, and photographic framing

Use one legible key plus motivated practicals and restrained atmosphere. Lighting
should describe planes, joins, materials, routes, figures, and the focal hierarchy.
Practicals must have plausible mounts and causes. Weather and atmosphere should
modulate the same place rather than replace it.

The active tactical floor remains the sharpest and most legible depth band. Depth of
field, haze, bloom, particles, and grade may enrich the miniature photograph; they may
not obscure legal movement, contacts, objectives, connector geometry, or the five-foot
grid. Normal and ORM detail should become visible under the light without producing
sparkle, noisy microcontrast, or a photographic texture pasted onto low-frequency
forms.

## 6. Four governed camera bearings

Genesis owns four canonical bearings separated by exactly 90 degrees. This is a
quarter-turn camera over one immutable place, not a free orbit and not four generated
maps.

- The authored starting bearing establishes the primary silhouette and keeps tall
  masses camera-far where practical.
- Each other bearing should reveal useful hidden ground, an opposite face, an interior
  relation, a route, or a material/condition story.
- Rotation may change projection, overlap, cutaway state, and visible information.
- Rotation may not reroll, mirror, rebuild, or mutate geometry, routes, objects,
  mechanics, state ids, or tactical fingerprints.
- Writing, heraldry, one-sided damage, drainage, and directional construction remain
  world-oriented. They do not flip to face the camera.

Tall-centerpiece and final dressed proofs require four-bearing captures plus any
necessary cutaway/ghosting views. A beautiful starting view does not excuse false,
unfinished, or unreadable backsides.

## 7. Context and honest edges

The active board sits inside a larger world:

- a thin near band may supply portal apron, support face, foreground framing, or
  truthful adjacency;
- the active tactical floor remains sharp and mechanically authoritative;
- a far band may establish settlement, terrain, weather, horizon, monumental scale,
  or another rolled premise; and
- linked windows preserve exact continuation where future play can cross.

Context has no secret mechanics and creates no false exit, reachable roof, hiding
place, door, bridge, or tactical surface. It must remain semantically truthful at all
four bearings. A black void is not the default edge; neither is decorative infinity.

## 8. Citizens, props, and effects belong to the world

Standees remain the stars, but they should not look pasted on. The floor contact,
plinth, cast shadow, local light, value hierarchy, texel register, and atmosphere bind
them to the scene. Props are sparse and relational: they explain work, maintenance,
occupation, claim, ecology, route use, or recent consequence.

Effects amplify an already readable state. Fire must have a source and light response;
water must meet a bank; smoke must rise from a cause; spell residue must bind to an
event or instance. Particle abundance is not material richness.

## 9. Asset routing

Route by physical and gameplay need:

- **Assetforge:** the primary production front door around every generated or compiled
  visual candidate; it owns manifests, exact lineage, quarantine, deterministic
  assembly, negative controls, proof packets, receipts, and admission—not taste,
  mechanics, or duplicate specialist algorithms;
- **engine geometry:** terrain fields, structure, openings, sockets, thickness,
  supports, collision, cover, sight, access, and tactical envelopes;
- **admitted existing assets:** the first choice when they satisfy the semantic demand;
- **Meshy donors:** demand-backed complex volumes whose silhouette cannot be expressed
  economically by the procedural kit;
- **sprite extrusion/faced boxes:** shallow identity, fronts, signs, reliefs, ordinary
  furniture, and replaceable props;
- **material tools:** reusable surface families, normal/ORM response, construction,
  realm identity, causal condition, and state variants;
- **trim/decal tools:** seams, caps, joints, edges, markings, repair, wear, evidence,
  and instance-bound consequence; and
- **truthful proxy:** the minimum honest expression when production art is unavailable.

The Golden-specific Assetforge application plan lives in
`intel/golden-vignette-assetforge-applications-v1.json`. Its first material-critical
path is `surface-demand -> material-family -> surface-project`: bind semantic
reservations to real faces, assemble a coherent role family from canonical material
owners, then prove world-locked projection over actual connected terrain. Identity
faces and the governed proof packet complete the Wave-2 loop; causal state, join kits,
and context enter only with their owning Golden waves.

`MATERIALIZE_NEW` means that activation would require committing a new persistent site
or window; it does not mean “generate a map immediately.” `DECORATE_LOCAL` means realize
a licensed local feature, trace, fixture, or encounter inside the current window; it
does not mean cosmetic dressing or mechanically irrelevant clutter.

## 10. Review and rejection controls

### 10.1 Hard rejection

Reject a candidate when it contains any of the following:

- independent random tile terrain or an ordinary grade expressed as voxel risers;
- visible diagonal zipper triangulation or repeated sedimentary ridge bands;
- a global terrain/storey height cap;
- a traversable height change without a mechanics-owned connector;
- a material, decal, or model that invents an affordance;
- constructed mass with no causal terrain/support join;
- false context exits or mechanically suggestive unreachable surfaces;
- camera rotation that changes world or tactical truth;
- uncaused wear or a physical-state material that invents operating state;
- persistent mutation that bleeds through a shared material parent;
- atmosphere or depth of field that hides active-floor truth;
- decoration that consumes protected quiet ground or interaction space; or
- a semantic identity obligation with neither a realized face treatment nor an
  explicit truthful fallback.

### 10.2 Soft scoring

Score rather than blindly reject:

- clarity of the one spatial sentence;
- hierarchy among roughly two to four broad masses;
- arrival near a meaningful decision;
- height-for-acreage and route consequences;
- reserved quiet ground;
- natural/constructed causal integration;
- three-to-six-family material economy;
- value, palette, roughness, and texel coherence;
- all-four-bearing legibility;
- world context without false affordance;
- sprite/world citizenship; and
- visible persistent consequence.

### 10.3 Required review packet

A dressed Golden proof should retain:

1. one mechanics/clay view with routes, connectors, objectives, and face roles;
2. the authored starting-bearing beauty capture;
3. the other three quarter-turn captures;
4. a gameplay-scale and 50%-contact-sheet read;
5. a grayscale/value read;
6. a material-family and semantic-identity overlay;
7. context off/on comparison when context is present;
8. state before/after comparison when mutation is present;
9. changed-seed siblings that preserve the recipe's defining qualities; and
10. a receipt naming hard rejections, soft scores, fallbacks, unresolved demands, and
    the exact expression/material revisions shown.

## 11. Working definition of success

A successful Golden vignette looks composed before it looks decorated. Its natural
ground reads as a place-wide landform while its construction reads as made. Height
earns tactical and visual meaning inside a compact window. Materials make faces,
purpose, culture, exposure, maintenance, use, and history legible without inventing
mechanics or noise. Citizens belong to the same light and world. The camera can
quarter-turn around one persistent truth. The edge suggests a larger world without
lying about what is playable. A return visit can show what happened here.

That is the standard the procedural engine must reproduce across seeds—not a single
hero map assembled by an agent or by Adam.
