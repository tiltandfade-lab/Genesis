### **II. The High Priestess: Trigger Effects**

This card represents mystery, stillness, and the bridge between the seen and unseen. It is the guardian of the subconscious and the mistress of the elements: **spirit, air, fire, water, and earth**. Under this card, the campaign shifts into a space of hidden knowledge and muffled truths.

---

**The Constant: "The Veil of Silence"**

The presence of the Priestess dampens the mundane world to amplify the spiritual.

- **The Effect:** Sounds are muffled as if underwater. Verbal spell components can be performed as a whisper. All Wisdom (Insight) and Intelligence (Investigation) checks are made with **Advantage**.
    
- **The Toll:** However, the physical world feels "distant." All Strength and Athletics checks are made with **Disadvantage** as the body feels weightless and disconnected.
