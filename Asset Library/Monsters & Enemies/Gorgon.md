### Gorgon

_Large Construct, Unaligned_

- **Armor Class:** 19
- **Initiative:** +0 (10)
- **Hit Points:** 114 (12d10 + 48)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **STR:** 20 (+5) | **DEX:** 11 (+0) | **CON:** 18 (+4) | **INT:** 2 (-4) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 7 (-2)
- **Skills:** Perception +7
- **Immunities:** Exhaustion, Petrified
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 17
- **Languages:** None
- **Challenge:** 5 (1,800 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +3

**Actions**

- **Gore.** Melee Attack Roll: +8, reach 5 ft. Hit: 18 (2d12 + 5) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature and the gorgon moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target has the Prone condition.
- **Petrifying Breath (Recharge 5–6).** Constitution Saving Throw: DC 15, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. First Failure: The target has the Restrained condition and repeats the save at the end of its next turn if it is still Restrained, ending the effect on itself on a success. Second Failure: The target has the Petrified condition instead of the Restrained condition.

**Bonus Actions**

- **Trample.** Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 16, one creature within 5 feet that has the Prone condition. Failure: 16 (2d10 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. Success: Half damage.

### Brazen Gorgon

_Large Construct, Unaligned_

- **Armor Class:** 19
- **Initiative:** +2 (12)
- **Hit Points:** 161 (17d10 + 68)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **STR:** 18 (+4) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 18 (+4) | **INT:** 2 (-4) | **WIS:** 14 (+2) | **CHA:** 7 (-2)
- **Skills:** Perception +10
- **Immunities:** Fire; Exhaustion, Petrified
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 20
- **Languages:** None
- **Challenge:** 9 (5,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +4

**Traits**

- **Flame Aura.** At the end of each of the gorgon's turns, each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from the gorgon takes 13 (3d8) Fire damage. Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 14. Success: Half damage.
- **Illumination.** The gorgon sheds Bright Light in a 10-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 10 feet.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack.** The gorgon makes two Gore attacks.
- **Gore.** Melee Attack Roll: +8, reach 5 ft. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Fire damage. If the gorgon has a creature Grappled, the target is pulled into the gorgon's space and has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14); if the gorgon already has a creature Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition instead. Until the grapple ends, the creature has the Restrained condition. When the gorgon moves, the Grappled target moves with it, costing no extra movement.
- **Smelting Charge (Recharge 5–6).** The gorgon moves up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks and can move through the spaces of Medium or smaller creatures. Each time a creature enters a space for the first time during this move, that creature is subjected to the following effect. Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 16. Failure: 13 (3d8) Fire damage, and the target is pulled into the gorgon's space and has the Grappled condition (escape DC 14); if the gorgon already has a creature Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition instead. Until the grapple ends, the creature has the Restrained condition. When the gorgon moves, the Grappled target moves with it, costing no extra movement. Success: Half damage only.
