#puzzle-failsafe
> **The stuck-solo-player escape valve** — the single most important table in the puzzle toolkit, because Genesis is *one player, no party to brainstorm with.* Every generated puzzle MUST roll one of these so a player who can't crack `[[Puzzle Solution Path]]` is **never hard-blocked.** This is `plumbing`: the player doesn't see the failsafe; the DM holds it and *deploys it on a cadence* — let the player genuinely try, then, as frustration mounts, drip the listed relief. The governing rule (DM-Charter §3, *rarely impassable*): a wall is allowed to cost something, never to dead-end the game. Three flavors are spread across the rows — **degrade-to-hint** (the puzzle teaches you over time), **pay-a-cost bypass** (skip it for a price), and **brute alternative** (an off-book way through the DM honors). d20, spice-graded (12 Grounded / 5 Textured / 3 Strange).

| 4 | Grounded | Degrade-to-hint | A bound spirit, talking statue, or carved face will answer one yes/no question per failure — grudging, riddling, but honest. |
