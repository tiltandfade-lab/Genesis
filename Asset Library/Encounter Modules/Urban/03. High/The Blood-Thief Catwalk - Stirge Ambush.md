### II. The Adventure Framework (Macro Level)

**Premise & Hook** The PCs are pursuing a cell of kidnappers through the city's chaotic and transient caravan district. To avoid the crowded, livestock-filled streets below, the cult utilizes a series of grand stairs and elevated wooden catwalks as a smuggling highway. The PCs must cross a pitch-black, 5-foot-wide gantry where the cult intentionally maintains a dangerous biological alarm: a roost of starved stirges that attack any who bring light or make noise.

**What Happens if the PCs Ignore It** If the heroes find another route, they lose the cult's trail. The kidnapped victims are successfully smuggled out of the city, and the cult fortifies their operations, leading to further disappearances.

**The Factions & Cast**

- **The Sanguine Chain (Cult):** A secretive faction that traffics humanoids and harvests blood for dark rituals. They move in absolute darkness to bypass their own hazards.
- **The Swarm:** A roost of 2d6 Stirges nesting beneath an awning. They are accustomed to being fed scrap meat by the cult and ruthlessly attack strangers.

**Dynamic Pacing: "The Events"**

- **The District Patrol:** If the PCs spend more than 10 minutes debating how to cross the catwalk, a city watch patrol passes through the noisy caravan pens below holding bright bullseye lanterns. The light casts up, instantly waking the Stirge roost and sending them into a frenzy right as the PCs try to move.

### III. The Encounter Nodes (Micro Level)

### Node 1: The Blood-Thief Catwalk

**The Set Up** _[Encounter Map Placeholder: A 20' x 40' set of ascending stone grand stairs leading to a 10' x 40' elevated wooden balcony. From the balcony, a 5-foot-wide wooden catwalk spans a 30-foot gap over an alley, connecting to an adjacent warehouse roof. Above the balcony is a canvas awning.]_

![[Pasted image 20260312132419.png]]

**The Run Surface (Segments)**

- **Adventure Promise:** A claustrophobic, high-wire battle where the party must fend off swarming parasites on a narrow bridge without falling to the streets below.
- **Pressure Level:** High. (The catwalk is a severe bottleneck, and a single misstep or Prone condition risks a 30-foot drop into the chaotic pens below).
- **Rewards & Consequences:** Success provides the PCs with direct evidence or a rescued captive tied to the cult. Failure results in a deadly fall, blood drain, and losing the cult's trail.
- **Basic Narrative:** The party ascends the grand stairs in complete darkness, smelling the campfires and livestock from the outlander district below. As they reach the narrow catwalk spanning the gap between buildings, they spot something resting in the center of the gantry—bait left by the cult. Stopping to investigate (or using a light source) awakens the stirges roosting directly above them.

**Define Physical Space**

- **The Space:** The 20'x40' stairs lead to a 10'x40' balcony. The primary feature is the 5-foot-wide, 30-foot-long Catwalk spanning the alley.
- **The Footing:** The stairs and balcony are stable, but the Catwalk is narrow. Being knocked **Prone** on the catwalk requires a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw to avoid falling off the edge.
- **The Atmosphere:** The area is completely Dark. There is no ambient light. The air is thick with the smell of campfires and unwashed beasts from the caravan pens 30 feet below.
- **The Feature:** A heavy canvas awning hangs over the balcony, creating _Total Cover_ from above and housing the Stirge roost.

**Starting NPCs**

1. **2d6 Stirges:** Clinging to the underside of the awning in a torpor, completely hidden in the darkness.

**Segment 1: The Biological Alarm**

- **Narrative Flow:** The PCs spot the "Bait" (roll on the Player Table) resting on the catwalk. If they approach with a light source, or if any PC wearing Heavy Armor fails a Stealth check on the wooden stairs, the Stirges awaken and dive-bomb the party on the narrow bridge.
- **Read Aloud:** _"The stench of dung and woodsmoke rises from the caravan pens thirty feet below you. The grand stairs terminate at a wooden balcony shrouded in absolute darkness. Spanning the gap to the next roof is a rickety, five-foot-wide catwalk. Hanging from the center of the catwalk is a suspicious shape. Above you, in the dark rafters of the awning, comes a sudden, wet chittering sound, followed by the drone of leathery wings."_
- **Skill Checks & Revelations:**
    - **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception):** If the PCs have Darkvision, a success allows them to spot the writhing mass of Stirges under the awning before they step onto the catwalk, avoiding the Surprised condition.
    - **DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth):** Required to cross the stairs and balcony without waking the Stirges (if no light sources are used).
- **Escalation & Pressure Keys:** The Stirges aim to attach and drain blood. Because they occupy the target's space when attached, characters using area-of-effect spells or wide weapon swings risk hitting their allies. If a PC attempts to Shove a Stirge off an ally, a failure by 5 or more accidentally pushes the ally instead.

### IV. Encounter Mechanics & Appendices

#### **Difficulty Dial: Scaling by Tier**

**Tier 1: Local Heroes (Levels 1–4)**

- _The Base Encounter:_ This tier utilizes the encounter as written.
- **The Minions:** 2d6 **Stirges** (CR 1/8).
- **The Pressure:** The fall from the catwalk is 30 feet (3d6 Bludgeoning damage).

**Tier 2: Heroes of the Realm (Levels 5–10)**

- **The Controller:** A **Cult Fanatic** (CR 2) stands on the opposite roof, casting _Hold Person_ on characters crossing the catwalk.
- **The Minions:** Replace the Stirges with 1d4+1 **Cloakers** (CR 8) or heavily mutated Dire Stirges.
- **The Pressure:** The Cloakers use their moans to induce the **Frightened** condition, forcing PCs to flee back across the hazardous, narrow catwalk.

**Tier 3: Masters of the Realm (Levels 11–16)**

- **The Controller:** A **Vampire** (CR 13) cult leader waits at the end of the catwalk.
- **The Minions:** The roost contains 1d4 **Vampiric Mists** (CR 3) and 2d4 **Vampire Spawn** (CR 5).
- **The Pressure:** The magical darkness is so thick it acts as the _Darkness_ spell. The fall is into a pit of spiked iron fencing (deals 10d6 Piercing damage).

#### **Mechanic: The Precarious Catwalk (2024 Core Rules)**

1. **The Chokepoint:** The catwalk is only 5 feet wide. Creatures cannot squeeze past hostile targets without using the Tumble or Overrun actions.
2. **The Drop Hazard:** If a creature takes damage that knocks them **Prone** while standing on the catwalk, they must immediately make a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw.
3. **The Fall:** On a failed save, the creature slips off the edge, falling 30 feet into the caravan pens below, taking 10 (3d6) Bludgeoning damage and landing **Prone** in a pile of animal refuse.

#### **Stat Blocks & Tactics**

**Stirge** _Tiny Beast, Unaligned_

- **Armor Class:** 14 (Natural Armor)
    
- **Hit Points:** 2 (1d4)
    
- **Speed:** 10 ft., fly 40 ft.
    
- **Attributes:** STR 4 (-3), DEX 16 (+3), CON 11 (+0), INT 2 (-4), WIS 8 (-1), CHA 6 (-2)
    
- **Skills:** Stealth +5
    
- **Actions:** _Blood Drain._ Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage, and the stirge attaches to the target. While attached, the stirge doesn't attack. Instead, at the start of each of the stirge's turns, the target loses 5 (1d4 + 3) hit points due to blood loss. The stirge can detach itself by spending 5 feet of its movement. It does so after it drains 10 hit points of blood from the target or the target dies. A creature, including the target, can use its action to detach the stirge.
    
- **Tactics:** The Stirges swarm the first source of light or the loudest target on the catwalk. They attempt to attach to lightly armored targets. Once a Stirge has drained 10 hit points, it detaches and lazily flies back into the dark rafters.
    

#### **Treasure & Rewards**

Total Value: ~25–100 gp.

- **The Bait:** If the dropped ledger or cult mask is recovered, they can be sold to rival factions or city authorities for 50 gp.
- **Cultist Belongings:** If a hostile decoy or sniper is present, they carry 25 gp in assorted coins, a shortsword, and a vial of basic poison.
- **Roost Refuse:** Searching the dark balcony beneath the awning reveals the desiccated corpses of previous victims, yielding a _Potion of Healing_ and a silver ring worth 15 gp.
