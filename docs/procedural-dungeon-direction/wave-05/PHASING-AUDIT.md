# Wave 5 Prototype/MVP Phasing Audit

This audit separates Wave 5's functional furniture, dressing, clutter, empty-room, container, touched-state,
validated-improvisation, visual-realization, budget, and evidence goals from the smallest honest proofs. The
chronological authority remains [Wave 5's running record](01-questionnaire-and-triage.md). It follows the global
[phasing framework](../PHASING-FRAMEWORK.md), [Clay Proof Ladder](../CLAY-PROOF-LADDER.md), and
[Feature-Promotion Ledger](../FEATURE-PROMOTION-LEDGER.md). No implementation or dependency is authorized.

## Pre-alpha-critical foundation

| Decision family | Minimum critical form | Why it cannot wait |
|---|---|---|
| Canonical assembly truth | Every supported assembly has stable identity/provenance, required/optional functional roles, explicit members/relations, shared state, and honest satisfied/degraded/unsatisfied reporting | A pretty prop bag cannot prove a cell, guard room, shrine, or workstation functions. |
| Shared placement legality | Architecture commits first; furniture consumes the same sockets, footprints, supports, access faces, clearances, portal/circulation/focal reservations, and scale truth | Independent placement can block doors, make containers unusable, or contradict the DM's description. |
| Layer and density honesty | Function, evidence/history, tactical interaction, and optional dressing stay distinct; sparse/crowded/stripped/ceremonial/ruined/empty states have causal profiles | Art cannot silently satisfy missing mechanics, and quiet rooms cannot be treated as unfinished frames. |
| Search, holdings, and custody continuity | Exact consequential contents, bounded fungible holdings, aggregate search surfaces, depletion, and discovered emptiness obey closed Wave 1/2 owners | Furniture cannot become a loot generator or erase conserved inventory truth. |
| Touched-state continuity | Attention/action promotes the same lightweight identity through relevant state, location, custody, persistence, and projection; broad propagation escalates to Wave 8 | A stool, spill, paper pile, or embedded tool cannot vanish, reroll, or remain cosmetic once play depends on it. |
| DM-seat transaction boundary | An ordinary proposed detail commits minimum mechanical truth before establishment; rejected/late/invalid proposals receive honest fallback; breadth grows by conformance evidence | Provider prose must not create a different dungeon, solution, treasure supply, or save state. |
| Culture/asset/budget invariance | Wave 3 culture truth selects indexed arrivals; exact, primitive, extrusion, card, and marker tiers preserve identity/mechanics; aggregation reduces cost rather than facts | Missing art or a slower device cannot restyle culture, change affordances, or delete promised objects. |

## Explicit prototype/MVP exclusions

- The first proof does not build the full guard post, all common recipes, all twelve sites, or a universal furniture
  ontology. It retains one guard room and only the micro-assemblies/relations its actual obligations require.
- Basic structural geometry, portals, circulation, reservations, and tactical composition precede furniture.
  Furniture cannot repair a weak or illegal clay plan.
- The prototype uses a tiny allowlist, not the ideal latent contextual reserve. It still records mechanical state;
  literal prose-only provider invention remains excluded.
- The prototype does not simulate every cup, paper, drawer, or material continuously. Untouched interchangeable
  matter may remain aggregate/re-derivable; touch and consequence promote only sufficient state.
- Broad burn/break/support/topology/fire propagation and general destructibility remain Wave 8's responsibility.
- Primitive-plus-solid/procedural material is expected for most first props. Full 3D or sprite extrusion enters only
  when the retained fixture demonstrates a semantic/readability need; exact art is never required for mechanics.
- Exact numeric clutter caps, solver weights, tag/state catalogs, provider pass rates, latency ceilings, and corpus
  thresholds remain measured specification/workbench work. They may not weaken the accepted authority laws.
