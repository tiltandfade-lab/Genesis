#festival-and-holy-days
> **PROVISIONAL — Adam spot-check pending.** Rolled on arrival drift (a Textured+ drift row can BE
> a festival) or downtime (`[[TABLE-GAPS-070126]]` §4) — the urban life beat the walk-skins only
> flirt with. Carries real market effects (a price swing, lodging running full), a custom the DM
> narrates, and a complication hook for the session to catch on. Spice-graded d100, Commitment
> ceiling (66 Grounded · 20 Textured · 9 Strange · 4 Volatile · 1 Mythic). Five band-anchor rows
> (marked ★) are Adam's approved samples from `TABLE-GAPS-070126.md` §6, included verbatim; the
> rest extend their register.
