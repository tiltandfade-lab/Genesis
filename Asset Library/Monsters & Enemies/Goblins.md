### The Goblins: The Cowardly Horde

Goblins are highly adaptable, prolific humanoids that lair in abandoned mines, despoiled dungeons, and dismal forests. Individually weak, they rely on overwhelming numbers, clever ambushes, and their natural stealth to survive in a world filled with larger predators.

Because they are cowardly and pragmatic, their behavior shifts wildly depending on who is leading them. When left to their own devices, they are disorganized scavengers or comical bandits. However, when dominated by hobgoblins, bugbears, or cruel tyrants, they become a dangerous, expendable military force. They are notorious for building lairs riddled with alarms, simple snares, and escape tunnels sized only for them.

### 4. Goblin Hexer

_Small Fey (Goblinoid), Chaotic Neutral_ _Rare spellcasters among goblin-kind. They use dark, debilitating magic to control the battlefield while hiding behind their allies._

- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Hit Points:** 45 (10d6 + 10)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR** 8 (-1) | **DEX** 16 (+3) | **CON** 12 (+1) | **INT** 16 (+3) | **WIS** 11 (+0) | **CHA** 10 (+0)
- **Skills:** Sleight of Hand +5, Stealth +7
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common, Goblin
- **Challenge:** 3 (700 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Actions**

- **Multiattack.** The goblin makes two Hex Stick attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting.
- **Hex Stick.** _Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 12 (2d8 + 3) Psychic damage.
- **Spellcasting.** The goblin casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13):
  - At Will: _Minor Illusion_
  - 1/Day Each: _Blindness/Deafness_, _Faerie Fire_, _Grease_

**Reactions**

- **Jinx.** _Trigger:_ A creature the goblin can see hits it with an attack roll. _Response — Wisdom Saving Throw DC 13:_ The triggering creature. _Failure:_ The attack misses instead.

### d10 The Goblin Warband (Tone Variance Table)

Because goblins are easily bullied, their encounters can range from deeply comical to genuinely threatening based on who or what is motivating them.

|d10|Encounter Premise|Campaign Tone & Mechanics|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The "Tollbooth"**|**Humorous:** The goblins have stretched a single piece of rope across the road and demand a toll. They are easily intimidated, bribed, or distracted, and their traps are hilariously shoddy.|
|**2**|**"Bree-Yark!"**|**Lighthearted/Action:** The goblins attack bravely, but the moment the Boss is killed, the rest throw down their weapons, scream "Bree-Yark!" (which means "we surrender"), and beg to serve the PCs.|
|**3**|**The Pet Keepers**|**Swashbuckling:** A tribe that specializes in taming vermin. They ride into battle on Giant Rats, or hurl clay pots filled with Swarms of Rot Grubs or Centipedes.|
|**4**|**The Nilbog's Prank**|**Chaotic:** One of the goblins is possessed by a Nilbog—a trickster spirit. Whenever the PCs try to hit it, they must make a DC 12 Charisma save or be forced to praise the goblin instead.|
|**5**|**The Meat Shields**|**Tactical:** The goblins are commanded by a Hobgoblin Warlord or Bugbear. The larger monsters actively throw the goblins into the PCs' area-of-effect spells or use them as living cover.|
|**6**|**The Hit-and-Run**|**Suspense:** The goblins refuse to engage in melee. They hide in dense thickets or dark caverns, shoot arrows, and immediately use _Nimble Escape_ to vanish, slowly whittling the party down.|
|**7**|**The Slavers' Net**|**Gritty:** The tribe acts as a gathering force for a deeper Underdark empire. They use nets, lassos, and poisoned darts specifically to capture travelers alive to sell to Duergar or Drow.|
|**8**|**The Desperate Flight**|**Mystery:** The PCs encounter a massive horde of goblins running _away_ from something. They trample through the PCs' camp not to attack, but because something horrifying (like a dragon or mind flayer) claimed their lair.|
|**9**|**The Murder Holes**|**Lethal:** The goblins fight in a 3-foot-tall, cramped tunnel system. Medium-sized PCs must squeeze (fighting with Disadvantage), while the goblins navigate the treacherous terrain perfectly.|
|**10**|**The Bane Fanatics**|**Dark Fantasy:** Indoctrinated by an Evil Cleric, these goblins have overcome their cowardice. They fight to the death with frightening zeal, blowing screeching war horns and employing suicide tactics.|

### d10 Goblin Status Symbols (Loot & Clues)

Goblins value bizarre objects, and their caste system dictates that higher-ranking bosses display their status with strange, stolen trophies.

|d10|Discovered Symbol|Implication / Clue|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Pet Rat**|A special breed of rat kept on a piece of twine. This goblin was a "Gatherer," searching for food and traps.|
|**2**|**The Umbrella**|A cloak or umbrella fashioned from the leathery remains of a dead Darkmantle.|
|**3**|**The Snack Jar**|A glass jar filled with live, wriggling beetles kept for mid-battle snacking.|
|**4**|**The Boot Hat**|A stolen gnome or dwarf's heavy leather boot, worn proudly on the goblin's head as a helmet.|
|**5**|**The Bloody Hands**|The goblin's hands are dyed bright red, indicating they are a "Lasher"—the enforcers who whip the lower castes into shape.|
|**6**|**The Ear Notches**|The goblin has specific notches cut into its ears or wears earrings made of bone, detailing a tally of defeated enemies.|
|**7**|**The Raccoon Belt**|A belt made from several poorly-cured raccoon pelts, marking them as a scout or "Hunter."|
|**8**|**The Stolen Crest**|A tattered tabard bearing the royal crest of a local lord, which the goblin boss has fashioned into a dirty cape.|
|**9**|**The Ogre's Toenails**|A pouch filled with the clippings of an allied Ogre, proving the tribe has a massive giant-kin brute guarding their main camp.|
|**10**|**The Petrified Kin**|A bracelet made from pieces of former goblin rivals who were turned to stone by a Basilisk or Medusa (hinting at the dungeon's true boss).|
