# Guard Post Cultural Mutation — MVP and Ideal Tier

## Founder-confirmed constitution

Adam confirmed on 2026-07-23:

1. The same-thing invariants recommended for the Guard Post are sound.
2. The first paired cultural construction systems are:
   - **Institutional Frontier Works**
   - **Upland Vernacular Station**
3. The first road-control posture is a guardroom beside a genuine hill-shoulder narrowing, with a
   gate or barrier that controls the pass-through road.
4. MVP culture may mutate construction while preserving functional topology. Deeper spatial
   mutation belongs after that proof.

These names describe original fantasy construction logics informed by historical evidence. They
are not literal Roman and Alpine cultures.

## Founder-confirmed physicalization boundary

Adam confirmed on 2026-07-23 that the first cultural proof uses the very-low-poly construction
language of FFT maps and keeps the majority of furnishings narrative. Furnishing facts may remain
DM-visible and world-persistent without physical models until the architecture and tactical scene
are proven. If a furnishing changes collision, cover, sight, support, practical-light position, or
exact interaction reach, it must receive the smallest truthful physical proxy; invisible tactical
furniture is not permitted.

The paired cultures must therefore be legible through construction, silhouette, threshold,
opening, roof/lookout, repair, and material-role distribution with all ordinary furnishings hidden.

## Tier boundary in one sentence

> **MVP proves that culture can make the same legal Guard Post read as a different coherent
> construction tradition without changing its gameplay topology. Ideal allows culture to reshape
> the site's spatial solution and therefore requires the engine to solve and validate it again.**

## Same-thing invariant capsule

The paired MVP variants hold these facts constant:

- small, operating, externally supplied checkpoint;
- maintained overgrown old stone, not abandonment;
- hill-shoulder site with a real road narrowing;
- pass-through road with the same entry and exit continuations;
- barrier/gate spans or controls the same threshold;
- one occupied guardroom beside the threshold;
- one licensed observation position;
- the same workstation, storage, readiness, access, and circulation roles;
- the same approach, threshold, lookout, flank, retreat, and camera obligations;
- the same canonical repair event;
- the same weather, time, lighting, envelope, composition seed, and tactical reservations for the
  paired comparison.

Working MVP repair fixture: **recent slope-pressure and drainage repair**. The event is invariant;
each culture expresses how its builders perform the repair.

## MVP — required cultural expression

MVP is not a palette swap. Each culture must differ through a correlated construction profile with
at least one shape/silhouette channel and one material/assembly channel visible at gameplay scale.

### Culture A — Institutional Frontier Works

| Channel | MVP expression |
|---|---|
| Planning posture | Low, rectilinear, standardized occupied mass aligned deliberately to the threshold |
| Wall system | Roughly squared/coursed stone with repeatable lifts and explicitly dressed corners, openings, caps, and gate supports |
| Weather/lookout silhouette | Low roof or drained roof-deck profile plus a standardized timber observation deck/parapet assembly |
| Observation | Narrow or modular opening family plus the raised observation socket |
| Threshold | Squared gate supports and a replaceable, standardized timber barrier with legible forged fittings |
| Repair grammar | Measured stone replacement, dressed drain, and standardized bolted brace or support |
| Material roles | Organized coursing, restrained timber sections, repeated iron pattern, maintained operational edges |
| Narrative furnishing binding | Standardized work/record, readiness, and supply language bound to the same canonical roles; not a physical MVP cue |

### Culture B — Upland Vernacular Station

| Channel | MVP expression |
|---|---|
| Planning posture | Compact roofed guardroom fitted into the hill and retaining edge rather than visually imposed across them |
| Wall system | Irregular local rubble fields with dressed stones reserved for openings, corners, caps, drains, and threshold wear |
| Weather/lookout silhouette | Compact pitched or strongly shed weather roof with a deep eave and a roof-protected observation posture |
| Observation | Deep shuttered opening plus the same licensed observation obligation |
| Threshold | Local heavy-timber barrier and adapted ironwork occupying the same threshold socket and clearances |
| Repair grammar | Packed local-stone buttress, timber cribbing, and adapted drainage where the slope presses against the post |
| Material roles | Broader stone variation held in coherent value masses, heavier local timber, less regular but purposeful ironwork |
| Narrative furnishing binding | Locally made work, readiness, and storage language bound to the same canonical roles; not a physical MVP cue |

## MVP — what may and may not change

| May change by culture | Must remain invariant in MVP |
|---|---|
| Visible wall construction and bounded wall thickness/profile | Road and mechanics route graph |
| Roof, eave, parapet, and observation silhouette within the allotted support envelope | Entry/exit continuations and threshold location |
| Opening profile and legal socket choice | Room and furnishing semantic roles |
| Barrier geometry and hardware inside the same operating clearance | Barrier function and usable clearances |
| Stair/ladder realization inside licensed access sockets | Reachability and observation mechanics |
| Repair realization of the shared event | Repair event, age, and operating condition |
| Culture-specific material-role bindings | Maintained-overgrowth state and physical growth rules |
| Narrative furnishing bindings and DM vocabulary | Tactical reservations, collision promises, cover, sight, and reach obligations |

The MVP paired fixtures should use byte-equivalent committed `TacticalCompositionPlan` and
mechanics plans. Their culture-realization and surface-assembly plans may differ only through the
declared culture-owned profiles above. Culture-owned geometry must remain inside prelicensed
support envelopes so it cannot close a road, move a route, or change tactical advantage.

## Explicitly outside MVP

- culture-specific road layouts or alternate route graphs;
- culture changing the number or purpose of rooms;
- culture-specific staffing, faction, law, or canonical site nouns;
- a courtyard, second building, tower compound, bridge complex, or full gatehouse;
- multiple roof families per culture;
- multiple repair-event families;
- culture × realm mutation matrices;
- culture × season or weather matrices;
- symbolic dressing as the primary differentiator;
- large prop catalogs, unique hero props, or authored cultural set-pieces;
- all twelve golden sites expressing both cultures.

These are not rejected. They are Ideal-tier work that must not obscure whether the first
construction mutation succeeds.

## MVP evidence bundle

Working minimum:

- one paired hero seed rendered as neutral clay, materialed structure, architectural cutaway, and
  gameplay view with ordinary narrative furnishings hidden;
- six paired changed seeds using the same two culture profiles;
- two paired adversarial envelopes stressing slope direction, threshold orientation, and camera
  occlusion;
- deterministic replay receipts for every fixture;
- one fallback capture for each culture demonstrating truthful degradation without passing the
  cultural visual-acceptance gate.

This is 18 generated site realizations—nine shared inputs, each expressed by both cultures—not 18
authored maps.

## MVP acceptance gate

MVP passes only when:

1. both variants are unmistakably the same operating Guard Post;
2. both variants are culturally distinguishable at gameplay scale without names, flags, signage,
   hue shifts, or a unique decorative prop;
3. the distinction survives a neutral/clay silhouette-and-construction view;
4. material projection strengthens an already visible construction difference;
5. route graph, collision, tactical reservations, interaction roles, and operating clearances
   remain equivalent;
6. maintained overgrowth obeys the same exposure, drainage, traffic, and repair logic on both
   construction systems;
7. the identity of each culture survives all changed seeds;
8. neither profile wins by collapsing every seed toward one layout;
9. the renderer performs no independent culture roll;
10. no seed, fixture, culture id, or accepted capture receives a special-case geometry patch;
11. both cultures remain legible with narrative furnishings hidden, and no invisible furnishing
    changes collision, cover, sight, support, light position, or interaction reach.

## What MVP should teach Genesis

- which cultural channels can be swapped after composition without invalidating mechanics;
- which construction changes need prelicensed support envelopes and sockets;
- whether coherent correlated profiles read more strongly than isolated asset variants;
- whether culture remains legible when terrain and road layout change;
- how a single canonical history fact is expressed through different repair grammars;
- which semantic slots require culture-specific geometry and which can share a neutral realizer;
- what must be re-solved when the system later permits spatial mutation.

## Ideal tier — deeper cultural expression

Ideal begins after MVP proves the culture-profile contract.

### Spatial mutation

Culture may influence:

- footprint proportions and handedness;
- whether the post sits across, beside, above, or partly within the road edge when the canonical
  road-control posture licenses alternatives;
- room adjacency and interior circulation;
- lookout placement and vertical-access path;
- terrace, yard, retaining-wall, and gate composition;
- degree of orthogonality versus terrain-following construction;
- one-building versus small compound realizations where canonical scale permits.

These changes require the engine to generate new candidates, rerun all constraints, score the
result, and commit a new composition plan. They are not renderer swaps.

### Deeper material economy and craft

- geology-aware stone selection and block-size distributions;
- culture-specific quarrying, coursing, plaster/render, joinery, roofing, and forged-hardware
  practices;
- multiple construction campaigns and accretive additions;
- builder, occupier, and repairer layers that may differ while remaining historically coherent;
- climate-compatible roof, eave, drainage, foundation, and opening systems;
- condition masks that respond correctly to the culture's geometry.

### Deeper operation and interior expression

- culturally distinct arrangements of the same workstation, records, readiness, rest, storage, and
  light roles;
- inspection and queueing posture;
- privacy, hospitality, authority, and defensive doctrine expressed through circulation and
  thresholds;
- substitutions driven by cultural manufacturing and supply networks;
- symbolic marks and ornament used as a confirming layer, never the only cue.

### Cross-system expression

- one culture profile recognizably expressed across several golden-site types;
- controlled culture × realm interactions;
- controlled culture × prosperity, age, damage, occupation, and repair layers;
- related cultures, local variants, institutional imports, and hybrid border construction with
  explicit provenance rather than arbitrary mixing;
- culture-specific composition weights that preserve site truth and generator diversity.

## Promotion law

An Ideal feature may be promoted into the reusable culture system only after:

1. it is visible and meaningful at gameplay scale;
2. its canonical owner is explicit;
3. its table/profile representation is comprehensible;
4. its geometry and material effects are deterministic;
5. it has compatibility rules, budgets, and a named fallback;
6. it survives changed seeds without destroying site identity;
7. it works on a second golden-site type without becoming a copied Guard Post motif.
