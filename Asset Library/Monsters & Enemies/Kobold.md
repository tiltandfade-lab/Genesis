*p. 177 MM 2024*

Kobolds are small, cowardly, and individually weak — which means they are individually pathetic and collectively terrifying. A kobold alone will flee at the first sign of danger. A kobold pack of twenty, with a trap-setter who has been engineering this corridor for six months, is a TPK waiting to happen. The central principle of running kobolds is: they never fight fair, and they never have to.

Kobolds venerate dragons with a devotion that borders on the religious. A kobold lair within a dragon's territory is fanatically loyal — they will die without question in service to their "patron," and they will fight more aggressively and recklessly in the dragon's presence, or if they believe a dragon is watching. Outside of dragon territory, kobold packs are still organized around a pack leader, and they treat their tunnel network as sacred space — every inch of it is trapped, mapped, and known.

Winged kobolds (urds) are a rarer subspecies, born with small wings capable of sustained flight. They are deeply resented by their wingless kin in some tribes and elevated as divine messengers in others. In combat, winged kobolds bomb from above with fire pots and rocks, refusing to engage in melee unless cornered.

### Campaign Integration: Investigation & Clues

Kobolds leave the most *elaborate* dungeon sign of any creature. Finding a kobold lair means finding an engineering project.

- **The Trap Evidence:** Kobolds can't resist traps. Even a crude trip-wire with a rock above it is a kobold calling card. A DC 12 Investigation check finds the trap before it fires. A DC 15 Investigation check finds the *second* trap behind the first one.
- **The Tunnel Network:** Kobolds dig connecting tunnels between the "official" passages and their own system. Small openings (2 ft. wide) at floor level are surveillance ports — kobolds are watching the party from inside the walls.
- **The Dragon Shrine:** Any kobold lair will contain at least a crude altar to a dragon — real or aspirational. The scale, color, and markings indicate which dragon lineage they serve (or wish to serve). This is the single most valuable intelligence about a kobold group.

### 1. Kobold

The standard pack creature — cowardly alone, vicious in numbers, utterly dependent on Sunlight Sensitivity and Pack Tactics to contribute to a fight.

_Small Humanoid, Lawful Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 5 (2d6 − 2)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 7 (−2) | **DEX:** 15 (+2) | **CON:** 9 (−1) | **INT:** 8 (−1) | **WIS:** 7 (−2) | **CHA:** 8 (−1)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 8
- **Languages:** Common, Draconic
- **Challenge:** 1/8 (25 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Pack Tactics:** The kobold has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the kobold's allies is within 5 ft. of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.
- **Sunlight Sensitivity:** While in sunlight, the kobold has Disadvantage on attack rolls and on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

**Actions**

- **Dagger:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.
- **Sling:** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 4 (1d4 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

### 2. Kobold Inventor

The craftiest kobolds develop an obsession with improvised devices — crude grenades, jury-rigged traps, alchemical accidents. They are erratic and dangerous in a completely unpredictable way.

_Small Humanoid, Lawful Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 13 (3d6 + 3)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 7 (−2) | **DEX:** 15 (+2) | **CON:** 12 (+1) | **INT:** 14 (+2) | **WIS:** 7 (−2) | **CHA:** 8 (−1)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 8
- **Languages:** Common, Draconic
- **Challenge:** 1/4 (50 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Pack Tactics:** The kobold has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the kobold's allies is within 5 ft. of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.
- **Sunlight Sensitivity:** While in sunlight, the kobold has Disadvantage on attack rolls and Perception checks that rely on sight.

**Actions**

- **Dagger:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.
- **Improvised Device (Roll d6):** The inventor throws or activates a random device at a point within 30 ft.
  - **1–2 Alchemist's Fire Flask:** DC 10 Dex save or 5 (1d10) fire damage at the start of the target's next turn.
  - **3–4 Smoke Bomb:** 10-ft.-radius cloud of smoke for 1 minute. Heavily obscured.
  - **5 Net:** Large or smaller creature must succeed DC 10 Strength save or be Restrained. (DC 10 Str check to escape.)
  - **6 The Good One:** DC 13 Dex save (one creature) or 7 (2d6) thunder damage and knocked Prone. On a 1 on the d20 attack roll, the device misfires and the inventor takes 3 (1d6) fire damage.

### 3. Winged Kobold (Urd)

A rare subspecies born with functional wings, elevated by most kobold clans as a sign of draconic favor. Urds bomb targets from above and retreat before melee is possible.

_Small Humanoid, Lawful Evil_

- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 8
- **Languages:** Common, Draconic
- **Challenge:** 1/4 (50 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Pack Tactics:** The kobold has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the kobold's allies is within 5 ft. of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.
- **Sunlight Sensitivity:** While in sunlight, the kobold has Disadvantage on attack rolls and Perception checks that rely on sight.

**Actions**

- **Dagger:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage.
- **Drop Rock:** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, range 30/60 ft. (must be elevated above target), one target. _Hit:_ 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

### d10 The Warrens Scene (Investigation & Clues)

| d10 | Discovery | Investigative Hook |
|:----|:----------|:------------------|
| **1** | **The Scale Shrine** | A crude altar bearing a single dragon scale — large, clearly real, and of a specific color. A DC 12 Nature check identifies the dragon type. The scale suggests the kobolds have *met* their patron, not just worshipped at a distance. |
| **2** | **The Trap Blueprint** | A detailed diagram scratched into the wall showing the layout of the next corridor with every trap location marked. Unfortunately, it's in Draconic, and the diagram is oriented from inside the wall, not from the party's perspective. |
| **3** | **The Listening Holes** | Dozens of small holes in the wall at eye-level — for a kobold. They've been watching and listening from inside the stonework. Everything the party has said in this dungeon was overheard. |
| **4** | **The Egg Chamber** | A warm side room contains a clutch of kobold eggs, carefully tended. The pack was *living* here. This wasn't a military installation — it was a home. |
| **5** | **The Captive's Notes** | A prisoner, long since escaped or dead, left scratched notes in the wall of their cell: "Day 12 — they count the eggs every morning. Day 15 — the inventor brings new devices on Tuesdays. Day 22 — the leader talks to the wall in the northwest corner. There's something behind it." |
| **6** | **The Dragon's Mark** | Every room has the same symbol carved above the entrance — a draconic territorial mark. A DC 14 Arcana check identifies it as a specific dragon's personal sigil. The dragon knows about this dungeon. |
| **7** | **The Broken Device** | A kobold inventor's failed experiment lies shattered in the corner. A DC 13 Arcana or Tinker's Tools check determines what it was *supposed* to do — something genuinely impressive, and almost certainly not finished. The ingredients are still scattered around the room. |
| **8** | **The Council Records** | A stack of bark-tablets covered in Draconic describe pack council meetings. The most recent entry records a vote: stay and fight (4), flee to the secondary tunnels (11), surrender to the large ones (1). The vote was three days ago. |
| **9** | **The Trophy Collection** | A small collection of items taken from previous adventurers — a bent holy symbol, a broken wand, a single boot. Each item is labeled in Draconic with a date and a crude drawing of the person who carried it. One of the drawings looks disturbingly like a person the party knows. |
| **10** | **The Secret Route** | Behind a loose stone in the wall is a tunnel exactly 2 ft. wide and 2 ft. tall — too small for most humanoids, but a halfling or gnome could crawl through. The tunnel bypasses the next three rooms entirely. The kobolds use it as an escape hatch. |
