#watcher-effect-pool
> **What the watching thing actually is** — the first **Consequence-Ladder effect pool** (`docs/CONSEQUENCE-LADDER.md`). Rolled when a player investigates a thing tagged `Archetype: Watcher` (a Listening Portrait, a Witness Wall, a Recurring Child, any art/object that *attends* you) and the read succeeds (Perception-gated). **The effect die is a player roll**, revealed slowly through the "Tell". Built to the **Hungering Stone v2 "True Nature"** standard — four columns: what it *is* (+ valence), the **mechanic the player can use**, the slow-reveal **"Tell"**, and its **Escalation Threshold** (the clock / where it goes). Ordered 1→8, mundane → reality-straining; the Escalation column names the **sink** (`dead-end` / one-time boon / bind-to-threat / closed local event / thread-seed on a clock). Shared by *every* Watcher-archetype row across the corpus — pooled, not per-row. The DM may instead **generate a bespoke die on the fly** for a special piece, seeded by this room/world/PC, then **capture the rolled outcome to the codex as canon** (`DM-CHARTER §8.5`).

|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **1** | **Just Craft (none).** No watcher at all — a whispering-gallery curve or a trick of perspective that only *seems* to attend you. | None. | The "attention" evaporates the moment you stop expecting it; a companion beside you feels nothing. | **None — a `dead-end`.** Sometimes a statue is just a statue. |
| **2** | **A Grieving Echo (benign).** It holds the attention of someone long dead — a deaf child, a watchman bound by love to this spot. | Speak the dead one's name and it shows you **one true memory of this place** (a free clue). | A warmth, a half-heard hum, the faint smell of someone long gone. | If its grief is ever *answered* (the memory acted upon), it falls quiet forever — a **one-time boon**, then closed. |
| **3** | **The House-Spirit (good).** A minor guardian bound to the place, watching for harm to its own. | While you mean its people no harm, gain **advantage on the first social check** made within these walls. | Small lucks — a latch opens, a draft steadies your candle, a stair forgets to creak. | Threaten its wards and it **turns**: disadvantage instead, and it wakes an alarm only the faithful can hear. |
| **4** | **The Recording Wall (neutral).** It remembers every word ever spoken before it, without judgment. | Ask it **one question about a past conversation** held here; the DM answers truthfully, in period diction. | Faint overlapping whispers, like a struck tuning fork you feel in your teeth. | Others can ask it too — **a secret you spoke here may surface in the wrong mouth within days** (a social complication, seeded → bind). |
| **5** | **The Ward's Eye (neutral→hostile).** A surveillance-glyph worked into the art; to act before it is to be seen by the power that set it. | None directly — but it can be **blinded** (DC Arcana / Sleight of Hand) to pass unobserved. | The painted eyes track you a half-beat *behind* your real movement. | On the third trespass it has your faces and intent — **binds to this place's threat clock**; the resident now hunts you. |
| **6** | **The Fear-Eater (evil).** It listens for what you dread and feeds it through the wall into the next room. | Name a fear aloud and it will manifest that fear **for an enemy instead** — once. Turn it on them. | The air goes close and personal; the next room is colder exactly where *you* will stand. | Fed three fears, it manifests the worst one bodily — **a closed encounter keyed to the PC's dread**, here and now. |
| **7** | **The Far Ear (very evil).** A two-way conduit: something *elsewhere* has listened to this town through the art for years. | You may listen **back** — eavesdrop, once, on the distant listener (a major lead about a hidden power). | When you speak before it, the room settles a half-second late, as if your words were repeated somewhere first. | It now knows you exist, and where — **a thread-seed on a clock:** the distant watcher begins to *send* for you. |
| **8** | **The Collector (very evil).** Every face it has ever watched, it keeps — and paints into its crowded background. | Ask it to *show* you **anyone it has ever seen** here: a powerful way to find a person who once stood in this place (a tracking lead). | The newest face in its painted crowd is **yours** — a little more finished each day you linger near. | When your painted face is complete it **pulls** — a clocked thread; finished, the original is replaced by the painting. |
