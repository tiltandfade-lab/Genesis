# Foreign Gang in the Arcade
> *Urban High. Non-locals using a bright colonnade as a chokepoint — and something they dropped during the fight tells you exactly who they are.*

## 30-Second Run

A bright open colonnade — columns, arches, covered lane — smells like it's been lived in for days. Torn banner scrap. Chalk arrows on the wall, some crossed out. Someone changed the route. A small group of 3–6 people who are clearly not from this district are using the arcade as a chokepoint. They know its geometry. In the fight, something drops. The evidence tells the party more about this gang than the fight itself.

---

## Setup

**Space:** Long covered colonnade (40 ft+), supported by stone columns. Bright daylight — no shadows to hide in. Drain channel runs along one side. A wall niche that sounds hollow sits near the far end.
**Atmosphere:** Smells of urine, old occupation. A torn banner scrap, stiff with dust, hangs from one column. Chalk arrows — some crossed out — mark the east wall. A beadwork leather panel is hung like a banner fringe: beaten barge motif, repaired with visible patches.
**NPCs Present:** 3–6 Non-local gang members (nature varies by campaign — use Bandits or Cult Thugs).
**If Ignored:** The gang finishes what it's doing and moves on. The hollow niche goes undiscovered. The chalk navigation is never interpreted.

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Standoff

The gang is using the colonnade geometry deliberately — columns as cover, narrow lanes as chokepoints. They're not locals, but they know this space.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 13 Insight:** They're tense but not panicking. They've done this before. They're waiting to see if the party is a threat or incidental.
- **DC 12 Perception:** The chalk arrows — some are crossed out and redrawn. The route was changed recently. Why?
- **DC 14 History or Investigation (chalk arrows):** The crossing-out pattern matches a known gang navigation cipher — or an unfamiliar one. Either way, someone planned this path.

**Escalation:** If the party advances without attempting contact, the gang attacks. If combat starts, they use the columns aggressively — half cover for all attackers on both sides.

---

### Segment 2: The Fight (and What Drops)

During combat — when a gang member is reduced to 0 HP, uses a Dash action to flee, or is knocked Prone — something falls.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 12 Perception (during combat):** Notice the dropped item in the chaos.
- **DC 13 Investigation (dropped item, post-combat):** Identify its faction significance — a token, a mark, a document that says exactly who sent these people and why.

**The hollow niche:** Back wall of the niche sounds thinner than it should (DC 12 Perception to tap and notice). DC 13 Investigation: the back slides. Small hidden space — jammed grate behind it to a sub-route (DC 13 Athletics or DC 12 Thieves' Tools to clear).

---

## Mechanics

1. **Chokepoint Columns:** Half cover (+2 AC) from behind columns — available to both gang and PCs. No line of sight past columns from 30+ ft.
2. **Drain Channel:** Difficult Terrain (10 ft wide). Creatures shoved into it: DC 11 Athletics or Prone + Slowed.
3. **Bright Light:** Disadvantage on Stealth checks throughout. No ambush option for the party.
4. **Alarm Hazard (unused):** Optional — if the gang has a tripwire alarm at the colonnade entrance (DC 15 Dex Stealth to avoid triggering it).
5. **Jammed Grate:** DC 13 Athletics or DC 12 Thieves' Tools to clear. Beyond: hidden route through sub-colonnade to next node.

---

## Difficulty Dial

**Tier 1 (Lvl 2–4):** 3 Bandits. Dropped evidence is a faction token (micro payoff).
**Tier 2 (Lvl 4–6):** 5 Cult Bandits + 1 Cult Thug. Dropped evidence is a sealed letter (medium payoff — names a handler or a meeting location).
**Tier 3 (Lvl 7+):** The gang leader is a Veteran with cult tattoos. Dropped evidence includes a partial map to a location the party hasn't been yet.
