| d6 | Reinforcement Result |
|----|----------------------|
| 1  | 1 Guard or enforcer from the same faction arrives from a nearby street, room, or rooftop. |
| 2  | 2 Minions (CR 1/8–1/4 each) arrive cautiously from the edges of the site. |
| 3  | 1 Veteran/Elite (CR ~1) arrives to investigate the disturbance. |
| 4  | Defensive lockdown instead of creatures: shutters slammed, doors barred, chokepoint established, alarm triggered. |
| 5  | Patrol heard approaching — arrives at start of next round. |
| 6  | Boss sends a scout, runner, or lookout (weak creature/NPC) to assess the situation. |
