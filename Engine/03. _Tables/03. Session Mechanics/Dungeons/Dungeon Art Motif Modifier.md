| d6 | Modifier | Effect |
|---:|---------|--------|
| 1 | **Pristine** | Intact and readable. **Advantage on the first check** to interpret/find a related secret this dungeon. |
| 2 | **Defaced** | Deliberately damaged/erased. Implies **conflict or cover-up**; clue points to *who* did it. |
| 3 | **Overgrown / Corroded** | Nature/salt/rust obscures it. Suggests **water/airflow** routes or hidden voids nearby. |
| 4 | **Repaired / Patched** | Newer material interrupts old design. Patch line often marks a **panel/route**. |
| 5 | **Fresh Marks** | Recently added paint/chalk/scratches. Signals **recent faction presence** (and likely a cache). |
| 6 | **Uncanny** | The motif behaves wrong (glows faintly, “moves,” hums, whispers). Signals **weird phenomenon** or ward failure. |
