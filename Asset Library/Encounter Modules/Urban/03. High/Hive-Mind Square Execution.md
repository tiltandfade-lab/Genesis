## 1) 30-Second Run

> **GM Reads Aloud**
> At a four-way intersection in a modest residential square, a crowd has packed itself around a prepared guillotine or gallows, blocking the main route completely. The air smells faintly of lamp oil, and the dim light leaves faces visible but hard to read—until you realize they are all wearing the same strange expression. Several prisoners are being dragged or marched toward execution, shouting names into the crowd and begging for their lives, but the people only stare, transfixed, as if thinking with one mind. Wheelbarrows have already been staged to haul away bodies. Some spectators have climbed onto nearby rooftops to watch, but loose tiles are sending people skidding and clattering toward the street below. Somewhere in the spectacle, something is driving the crowd, and if it is not stopped, the execution will go forward.

### What the PCs Notice Immediately
- A guillotine or gallows has been deliberately prepared in the middle of the square
- The crowd is blocking the main thoroughfare and pressing inward from all sides
- Multiple prisoners are restrained, resisting, and calling out members of the crowd by name
- The crowd’s faces and reactions feel synchronized or emotionally wrong
- Wheelbarrows are in place to carry away the executed bodies
- Spectators are gathering on nearby rooftops despite the danger
- Loose roof tiles are slipping and falling as people swarm for a better view
- A narrow side passage shows fresh scrape marks, suggesting forced movement or repeated dragging
- A brass plate on a post nearby is worn thin, as though this spot has been formally designated for something
- A drain cuts through part of the square, offering runoff, clutter, and possible footing complications

### What Is Happening
A public execution has become the focal point of a curse-driven crowd mind. In a residential square at a four-way crossing, multiple prisoners are being brought to a prepared gallows or guillotine while the gathered spectators behave with eerie collective purpose. The condemned scream and struggle, naming people in the crowd and insisting they are innocent or betrayed, but the audience remains transfixed. Some have climbed onto nearby rooftops to watch, sliding and clattering across loose tiles as they press for a better view. The party is not facing a normal mob: the crowd is under a curse or psychic influence, and unless that source is identified or disrupted, the square will continue to feed the execution ritual. The default version of the scene assumes the gallows itself is the curse anchor, though alternate curse sources can be rolled below.

### d4 What Curse Drives the Crowd?
| Roll | Result | Curse Source / Play Use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Crimson Scapegoat** | A blood-red moon or similar cosmic sign has blurred reason into collective rage. The crowd seeks a scapegoat for a local disaster, whether guilty or not. Best for paranoid witch-hunt energy and a false accusation angle. |
| 2 | **The Juggernaut’s Backlash** | A ruler or noble once empowered by a dark artifact has broken faith with it, and the magic now unites the wronged into a singular execution mob. Best for political revenge, moral ambiguity, and anti-tyrant tension. |
| 3 | **The Final Muse** | An aberrant influence has turned the execution into a grotesque performance. The crowd is calm, smiling, and eerily delighted rather than enraged. Best for surreal horror and psychic pressure. |
| 4 | **The Sentient Gallows** *(Default)* | The execution structure itself has absorbed communal hatred, ritual certainty, and death-energy. The crowd is synchronized by the apparatus, and attempts to rescue a prisoner risk awakening it as the encounter’s living focal point. Best for the fastest default version. |

### The Sentient Gallows (Default 2024 Mechanics)
| Element | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | A creature attempts to remove a prisoner from the platform, or the crowd’s chanting reaches a fever pitch. |
| **Effect** | The gallows animates. The beams smell of old rot and spilled blood. It uses its Action to make a **Noose Attack** against any creature on its platform. |
| **Noose Attack** | *Melee Spell Attack:* **+6 to hit**, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* **2d6 Bludgeoning damage**, and the target gains the **Grappled** and **Restrained** conditions (**Escape DC 14**). |
| **Suffocation** | A creature starting its turn Grappled by the noose takes **1d6 Bludgeoning damage** and begins suffocating. |
| **Destruction** | The gallows is an object with **AC 13**, **50 Hit Points**, and **Vulnerability to Fire damage**. Destroying it breaks the immediate focal point of the curse, often causing the mob to suffer momentary confusion or panic, giving the PCs a brief window to act or escape. |
