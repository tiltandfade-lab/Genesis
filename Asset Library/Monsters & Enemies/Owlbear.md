*p. 233 MM 2024*

The owlbear is one of the most iconic monsters in the game for a simple reason: it is absolutely terrifying to behold and utterly inexplicable. Nobody knows who made owlbears or why. Sages argue endlessly — was it a wizard's experiment? A divine prank? Natural evolution in a magically saturated environment? The owlbear doesn't know or care. It is an apex predator with the ferocity of a bear, the night-hunting precision of a great owl, and absolutely no social instincts whatsoever.

Running an owlbear as simple "big monster attacks" is a waste of its flavor. The owlbear is territorial, not malicious. It attacks because it perceives an intruder in its range, not because it's evil or hungry. An owlbear that isn't threatened will often withdraw once a perceived threat is neutralized. An owlbear with a cub or an egg nearby will fight to absolute destruction.

The Stunning Screech should be a signature moment every time — the sound of an owlbear shrieking is described in the MM as a horrific combination of owl screech and bear roar, audible for miles. When it fires off, it should *feel* like a special event.

### Campaign Integration: Investigation & Clues

Owlbears are wilderness threats that occasionally move into dungeon corridors wide enough to accommodate them, usually following prey or fleeing something worse.

- **The Feather Scatter:** Owlbear feathers are distinctive — large, barbed, and often blood-stained. A DC 11 Nature check confirms owlbear presence from feathers alone.
- **The Territorial Marking:** Owlbears mark their range by raking deep claw gouges into trees or stone at shoulder height (for an owlbear, about 6 feet). Finding these marks means the party is in an owlbear's established territory.
- **The Cached Kill:** Owlbears cache excess prey — they partially consume a carcass and return for it later. A fresh cache (days old, not weeks) means the owlbear is nearby and will return.

### 1. Owlbear

A massive, ferocious predator that combines the worst traits of an owl and a bear into a single nightmarish package.

_Large Monstrosity, Unaligned_

- **Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 59 (7d10 + 21)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **STR:** 20 (+5) | **DEX:** 12 (+1) | **CON:** 17 (+3) | **INT:** 3 (−4) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 7 (−2)
- **Skills:** Perception +3
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 3 (700 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Keen Sight and Smell:** The owlbear has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight or smell.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The owlbear makes two attacks: one with its Beak and one with its Claws.
- **Beak:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 10 (1d10 + 5) piercing damage.
- **Claws:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 14 (2d8 + 5) slashing damage.

**Bonus Actions**

- **Stunning Screech (Recharge 5–6):** The owlbear unleashes a horrific shriek. Each creature within 30 ft. of the owlbear that can hear it must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be **Stunned** until the start of the owlbear's next turn.

Ancient forests and Feywild demesnes steeped in magic can give rise to particularly large and vicious specimens. These primeval owlbears can fly — albeit poorly — and emit thunderous screeches that can rattle foes and even tear them asunder.

Failure: 27 (6d8) Thunder damage and the Prone condition until end of target's next turn. Success: Half damage only.

### 3. Owlbear Cub

A juvenile owlbear — smaller, faster, and not yet full-grown. Its Stunning Screech is less powerful than an adult's, but it compensates with speed and the fact that an adult is almost certainly nearby.

_Medium Monstrosity, Unaligned_

- **Armor Class:** 12 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 26 (4d8 + 8)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **STR:** 14 (+2) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 14 (+2) | **INT:** 3 (−4) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 7 (−2)
- **Skills:** Perception +3
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Keen Sight and Smell:** The owlbear cub has Advantage on Perception checks that rely on sight or smell.
- **Territorial Distress:** If the owlbear cub takes damage, it emits a high-pitched screech audible out to 300 ft. If an adult owlbear is within that range, it moves toward the sound on its next turn, arriving in 1d4 rounds.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The cub makes two attacks: one with its Beak and one with its Claws.
- **Beak:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.
- **Claws:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 6 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage.
