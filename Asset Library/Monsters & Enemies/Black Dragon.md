*p. 38 MM 2024*

Black dragons are malicious, abusive beasts that disgorge acid and instinctively resent intrusions of any kind. They prefer dismal surroundings and heavy vegetation, but are uniquely drawn to locations with strong ties to the Shadowfell or areas steeped in necrotic magic.

**Campaign Integration Hooks:**

- **The Shadowfell Bleed:** A local graveyard or ruined subterranean vault is leaking necrotic energy. A black wyrmling has claimed the ruins, feeding on the corrupted vermin and creating a hazardous sinkhole of acid.
- **The Cult's Mascot:** A localized occult faction operating in the slums or nearby marsh is feeding a captive wyrmling in exchange for it dissolving the bodies of their victims, creating a perfect, untraceable murder pipeline.

#### d6 The Wyrmling's Ambush Stage (Tactical Terrain)

Black dragon wyrmlings rely heavily on fetid vegetation and dank water to hide from larger threats and ambush prey. They lair in dismal, partially flooded ruins or grottos filled with the stench of decay. _(Roll or choose one to establish the tactical frame of the swamp encounter)._

|d6|Swamp Terrain & Hazard|2024 Mechanical Integration & Tactics|
|---|---|---|
|**1**|**Submerged Ruins**|The water conceals the crumbling foundations of a sunken structure. The water is Heavily Obscured. The wyrmling uses this to break line of sight, forcing PCs to ready actions or enter the water to engage.|
|**2**|**Acid-Pitted Statues**|Decaying statues of forgotten deities jut from the mud. They provide Three-Quarters Cover, but a missed attack that hits a statue causes it to crumble, showering adjacent creatures with residual acid (1d4 Acid damage).|
|**3**|**The Shadowfell Bleed**|Black dragons are drawn to areas where the Shadowfell's influence is strong. The ambient light drops to Dim Light regardless of the sun. Healing spells cast within this zone restore the minimum possible hit points.|
|**4**|**Fetid Peat Bogs**|The ground is deceptive. Characters who move more than 15 feet in a single turn must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or plunge into deep muck, becoming Restrained.|
|**5**|**The Carcass Dam**|A massive, fly-ridden carcass of a giant crocodile or elk dams a sluggish creek. The wyrmling hides inside the hollowed-out ribcage, gaining Total Cover until it bursts out to unleash its breath weapon.|
|**6**|**Floating Algae Mats**|Thick mats of green algae look like solid ground but cannot support the weight of a Medium creature. The wyrmling, being naturally amphibious, swims effortlessly beneath them, tracking PCs by their shadows.|

#### d6 Acid-Scared Clues & Windfalls (The Investigation Layer)

Because black dragons prefer to lair in flooded areas, fragile treasures like paintings or wood rot away quickly. They favor coins, which survive long-term immersion, though they heavily tarnish. _(Roll or choose one to provide an investigative clue about the wyrmling's recent victims)._

|d6|Macabre Discovery|Clue / Campaign Hook|
|---|---|---|
|**1**|**The Fused Lockbox**|A merchant's iron lockbox, half-melted by acid. Inside is a tarnished but intact sum of local coinage and a scorched ledger proving a local official is extorting refugees.|
|**2**|**The Bribed Cultist**|An acid-pitted humanoid corpse wearing the sigil of a local occult faction. The corpse is clutching a tethered goat; the faction was attempting to feed and tame the wyrmling to use as a guardian.|
|**3**|**The Unlucky Scout**|The remains of a missing frontier scout. Their waterproof map tube survived the acid, revealing a safe path through a known swamp hazard or the location of a smuggler's cache.|
|**4**|**The Poisoner's Kit**|A shattered glass vial and an intact, sealed satchel containing 3 doses of potent Serpent Venom. The wyrmling ate a local assassin who was attempting to harvest swamp toxins.|
|**5**|**The Hoarded Gold**|A surprisingly large pile of tarnished gold coins scattered beneath the water. Collecting it requires characters to spend 10 minutes feeling through the muck, exposing them to a wandering monster check.|
|**6**|**The Draconic Molt**|The PCs find a large, intact shedding of the wyrmling's scales. An armorer in town can use this to craft a single shield that grants Resistance to Acid damage.|

#### d4 Scavengers & Sycophants (Secondary Pressure)

A wyrmling's lair is infested with stinging pests, and they are occasionally followed by opportunistic scavengers or lesser monsters seeking protection. _(Roll or choose one to complicate the fight)._

|d4|Symbiotic Threat|Description & Synergy|
|---|---|---|
|**1**|**Lizardfolk Trackers**|Local lizardfolk often worship black dragons. A pair of lizardfolk scouts watch the fight from the trees. If the wyrmling is winning, they hurl javelins. If the wyrmling dies, they flee to warn their tribe.|
|**2**|**Swarm of Insects (Centipedes)**|The damp lair is crawling with venomous centipedes. The swarm avoids the dragon but aggressively attacks any PC who falls Prone or is Restrained in the mud.|
|**3**|**Will-o'-Wisps**|These malevolent lights feed on despair. They bob at the edges of the battlefield, waiting for a PC to drop to 0 hit points so they can use their _Consume Life_ ability.|
|**4**|**Goblin Scavengers**|A trio of goblins are hiding in the reeds, hoping to steal from the wyrmling's hoard while it is distracted by the PCs. They will attempt to snatch a dropped weapon or loose coin pouch and run.|
