# Starting State — Pressures
> The standing situations in motion around a freshly-rolled town — the gap world-genesis didn't fill: **what presses on the town from inside, and from outside.** Each pressure is a **front** (a Danger → a Grim-Portent clock → an Impending Doom). Roll **one Internal + one External** at world-genesis; more may arise in play. Original, IP-clean; standard pipe tables; spice-graded. Orchestrated by `Starting State Procedure v1.0.md`.
>
> **Sources are mixed (Adam 2026-06-19):** a pressure may be a faction's scheme, an impersonal force, the world's own myth/taboo coming due, a neighboring power, or **fallout of the PC's own choices** — pressures are reactive, not only rolled at start. Roll **Pressure Source** first to decide where it comes from, then the matching pressure table.

## Pressure Source — Fork
> Where this pressure comes from. Roll once per pressure (internal & external).

|d12|Source|
|---|---|
|1–4|A **faction's scheme** — use a rolled faction's Agenda + Method as the danger|
|5–7|An **impersonal force** — roll *Impersonal Force* below|
|8–9|The **world's own** — a rolled Myth or Taboo coming due (reincorporate an established fact)|
|10–11|A **neighboring power** — an outside settlement, faction, or claimant (lean External)|
|12|**Fallout** — a consequence of recent events or the PC's own past deeds (reactive)|

## Internal Pressure (inside the town) — Commitment · Grounded→Volatile
> The danger rooted in the settlement itself. Usually tied to its rolled Faction, Taboo, or Trouble.

|d20|Within the walls…|
|---|---|
|1–2|a guild or cartel is tightening its grip on a trade everyone needs|
|3–4|a blood-feud between families or quarters is turning open|
|5–6|a shortage — grain, water, coin, fuel — and someone is hoarding it|
|7–8|a sickness is spreading through the poorer quarters|
|9–10|the leader is corrupt, failing, or dying, and the scramble to replace them has begun|
|11–12|a secret is about to surface that someone will kill to keep|
|13–14|the dispossessed are organizing — labor, class, or creed simmering toward revolt|
|15|a scapegoat-hunt is forming; the mob has chosen who to blame|
|16|a vice or addiction is quietly hollowing the place out|
|17|something preys on the town from its own underbelly — sewers, undercroft, the dark below *(Textured)*|
|18|a schism is splitting the community's faith or loyalty in two|
|19|a haunting or a curse no one will name has settled over a quarter *(Strange)*|
|20|the town itself is, slowly and wrongly, becoming something else *(Volatile)*|

## External Pressure (from beyond the town) — Commitment · Grounded→Mythic
> The danger approaching from the region or the wider world. Tied to a neighboring power, myth, or place node.

|d20|From beyond…|
|---|---|
|1–2|bandits or raiders are thickening on the roads|
|3–4|a rival settlement's ambitions are pressing inward|
|5–6|a trade route is failing or being contested, and the town starves of it|
|7–8|refugees are arriving — ahead of something|
|9–10|a hard season is coming: drought, flood, or a killing winter|
|11–12|a blight or plague is spreading toward the town|
|13–14|a migrating beast, herd, or swarm is on its way through|
|15|a warlord or strongman is rising and gathering the region under one fist|
|16|a cult is spreading settlement to settlement, and the town is next|
|17|something long-buried is stirring in the ruins or the wilds *(Strange)*|
|18|an army or horde is on the march, and its road runs through here *(Volatile)*|
|19|an old power is waking — the land itself beginning to answer *(Volatile)*|
|20|the border of the world is thinning here; something is coming through *(Mythic)*|

## Impersonal Force — Fork (factionless dangers)

|d12|Force|
|---|---|
|1|blight on the crops|
|2|plague or fever|
|3|famine|
|4|drought|
|5|flood|
|6|a killing winter|
|7|wildfire|
|8|quake or landslide|
|9|a failing resource — the mine plays out, the well sours, the soil dies|

|11|a beast or swarm out of the wild|

## Grim Portent (the next worse step) — populates clock segments
> When a pressure's clock ticks, roll or pick the next beat. Each makes the doom nearer and harder to deny.

|d12|The threat…|
|---|---|
|1–2|gains an ally, a patron, or fresh coin|
|3–4|claims a victim — someone mourned, or someone feared|
|5–6|takes ground: a quarter, a road, a holding|
|7|is revealed — denial is no longer possible|
|8|forces an ugly choice on the people|
|9|removes a protector — bought, broken, or driven off|
|10|reaches a deadline: a season turns, a debt comes due, a march arrives|
|11|turns a friend into a foe, or buys one|
|12|crosses a line thought uncrossable *(Textured/Strange)*|

## Impending Doom (if the clock fills) — Commitment · Grounded→Mythic
> What happens if no one intervenes. Write it to the Ledger; the world is changed by it.

|d12|Doom|
|---|---|
|1–2|the faction wins, and its grip closes for good|
|3–4|a death, a hanging, or a massacre|
|5–6|the town falls — taken, occupied, or made to kneel|
|7–8|displacement — the people scatter; refugees take to the road|
|9|a permanent loss — a place razed, a resource gone beyond recovery|
|10|a tyranny installed — a boot that does not lift|
|11|a sacred line crossed; the town is damned in its own eyes *(Strange)*|
|12|transformation — the place becomes something else and will not change back *(Mythic)*|

# Making It Real — concretizing the uncanny
> **The craft rule (Adam 2026-06-19): vague to the player, concrete underneath.** The player may see only a fragment — *"an old power is waking; the land itself answering"* — but that is **presentation, not the truth.** Behind the screen the DM rolls here to fix **what it actually is**: an objective fact, written to the World State Ledger as **write-once canon.** From that moment it is a real thing in the world, not a mood. *What it is* is fixed once established; **how it ends is open and shaped by play** — every entry below carries its own ways to be contained, fed, freed, undone, or embraced, and the player's choices decide which comes true. Never leave a Strange+ pressure unresolved mystery; resolve it the instant it's established (lazy-history: the moment it matters, roll and record it).
>
> **Routing — roll the matching table when a pressure lands in these rows:**
> - *External 17 (stirring in the ruins), External 19 (old power waking), and any "the land answers" reading* → **The Buried Power.**
> - *External 20 (the border thins; something coming through)* → **The Intrusion.**
> - *Internal 20 (the town becomes something else) and Impersonal 12 (a slow change in the land)* → **The Becoming.**
> - *Internal 19 (a haunting or curse no one will name)* → **The Curse.**
> - *Internal 17 (something preys from the underbelly)* → a concrete creature: pull the actual beast from the monster library (`Asset Library/Monsters & Enemies/`), don't leave it "a thing."

## The Buried Power — what is actually waking
|d8|In truth it is…|
|---|---|
|1|a vast sleeper beneath the land — a leviathan or titan; the tremors are its turning, and its dreams seep into those who sleep on the soil|
|2|a pre-human structure surfacing — a drowned city, a buried engine, a stair going down — rising stone by stone through the earth|
|3|a bound thing whose seals are failing — chained by the founders; the old wards are going quiet one by one|
|4|a font of raw power re-pressurizing — a ley-spring, a god's wound, a deep node — bleeding into the water, the crops, the newborns|
|5|the local dead keeping a promise — a barrow-host or a lost legion told they would return, and the day has come|
|6|an apex beast ending a long dormancy — the thing the region's warning-myths were *about*, and it wakes hungry|
|7|an old bargain coming due — a pact the founders struck; the other party has arrived, on schedule, to collect|
|8|the land reverting to an older shape — terrain and all, it is becoming what it was before the town, before the maps|

> **Outcome is play's to decide:** re-seal it · feed or appease it · free or wake it fully · bargain with it · rouse the town against it · flee and let it have the place. The DM tracks which the players push toward.

## The Intrusion — what is bleeding through the thinning border
|d6|On the other side is…|
|---|---|
|1|a mirror-country — a reflection of here where one thing went differently; doubles are crossing over|
|2|the dream-land — where sleep goes; it leaks into waking, and waking leaks back into it|
|3|the deep past — a fold to an earlier age; old beasts, old weather, old peoples wander out|
|4|a country of one element — fire, ice, stone, storm, or salt — and its weather and natives spill in|
|5|the land of the dead — the boundary worn thin; the dead walk back, or the living slip down|
|6|somewhere with no name and no likeness — geometry, color, and law all wrong; simply Elsewhere|

> **Manifestation (DM picks or rolls d4):** 1 a fixed doorway · 2 a wandering fold that moves · 3 a slow tide of overlap spreading from one point · 4 scattered bleed-points with no center. **Outcome is play's:** close it · hold the line · pass through · let it widen · treat with what comes out.

## The Becoming — what the place is turning into
|d8|It is becoming…|
|---|---|
|1|a single substance — glass, stone, metal, salt, fungus, or silk — creeping outward from a source point|
|2|a place out of time — hours loop, age, or freeze; one day keeps happening, or never ends|
|3|a hive or a chorus — the people becoming one mind and one voice, the seams between them dissolving|
|4|an echo of its own myth — reshaping into the image of the story it has always told about itself|
|5|a garden of something — a spore or bloom rewriting flesh and timber into its own kind|
|6|a place where the rules slip — gravity, sound, color, or direction stop behaving, and the change spreads|
|7|a town of replacements — each resident slowly swapped for a truer, falser, or hollow version|
|8|a wound still spreading — the change is a scar from something that already happened here|

> **Outcome is play's:** find and stop the source · slow or reverse it · escape ahead of it · let it complete · learn to live inside the new rules. Spreads on a clock; find the source-point and you can act on it.

## The Curse — what the haunting actually is
|d8|No one will name it because it is…|
|---|---|
|1|a specific dead presence with an unfinished grievance — name it and you know what it wants|
|2|a curse with a trigger and a price — it activates on a condition and lifts for a cost|
|3|a contagion of dreams or memory — it passes sleeper to sleeper; what it takes (or gives) is the question|
|4|a thing wearing a familiar face — it replaced someone, and is still here, still smiling|
|5|a blood-debt the town never paid — the land itself is collecting for an old wrong|
|6|an object that should have been destroyed — still here, still working, still wanting|
|7|a place that should not have been entered — someone did, and brought it back with them|
|8|a word, a song, or a name that wounds when spoken — and it is spreading mouth to mouth|

> **Outcome is play's:** satisfy it · pay the price · break the trigger · destroy the source · expose the impostor · carry it somewhere it can do no harm. Every curse here has a stated lever; the players find and pull it (or don't).
