## Binding statement

**Reference evidence only.** Nothing listed here may become a Genesis production asset,
texture, mesh source, or promotional image. These images inform internal visual direction
and grammar induction. Before any image is copied, modified, redistributed, embedded, used
as a texture, or used in promotional material, its exact license and attribution
requirements must be reviewed *for that use*. The safe Genesis production path remains
original procedural geometry and original materials.

Every image below is from **Wikimedia Commons**, under CC0 / public domain / CC BY / CC BY-SA
as recorded. Attribution strings are as supplied by the Commons file's own metadata; the
linked Commons page is authoritative if this table and the page ever disagree.

Images were fetched at ~1100 px (Commons-rendered thumbnails), not full resolution — enough
to read morphology, small enough to keep the study cheap.

## What was deliberately NOT downloaded

- **No FFT map images were copied into this study.** Lane 4 read the existing in-repo corpus
  at `genesis/Reference/FFT-Guard-Post-Study/study-archive/maps/five-angle/` in place. No new
  copy, no derived contact sheet, nothing committed.
- **No BG3 or Diablo screenshots.** The FFT boundary in `docs/GOLDEN-SITES-CATALOG.md` says
  game screenshots are "local comparison only, never redistributed, never committed," and this
  study lives *in the repo* — so downloading them here would violate the boundary by
  construction. Lane 5 is therefore built from **public written documentation** (community
  wikis, engine-port source discussion) plus prior play knowledge, and cites URLs instead of
  pixels. This is a deliberate, declared narrowing of Lane 5, not an oversight.
- No images of private individuals were sought; a handful of the kept photographs contain
  incidental people at distance, used only for **scale**.
