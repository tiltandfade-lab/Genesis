#animal-kind# Animal Kind (d12)

> PARTIAL-NPC ATOM ([[NPC-PARTIALS]]) — rolled by `rollPartial('animal')`: what kind of beast is
> around. No want/fear/leverage stack — an animal is not a moral agent. It carries a **kind** (this
> table), a **tell** ([[animal-tell]], its one plot function as a living detector), and a simple
> **need** (hungry · guarding · lost · loyal). Exempt from the situation family — do NOT tag
> `table_family: situation`. **Realm-flavor is a reskin note, not yet a full skin table:** whether
> animals get per-realm skin tables the way roles do ([[NPC-ROLE-REALMS]]) is an open question for
> Adam — for now the DM reskins in place off the realm register (Chrome's "pet" is a drone-companion,
> Bright-Kingdom's beasts are a shade too clever, Theater's is a war-mule, Ash's is a rad-scarred
> mongrel). Row 11 is a held reskin slot for that realm-beast. Tags in-format.
