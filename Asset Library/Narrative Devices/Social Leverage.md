# Social Leverage

Type: Narrative Device  
Purpose: Introduce non-combat tension through information imbalance.

## Core Concept
An individual possesses damaging, compromising, or strategically valuable information about another party.

## Typical Uses
- Blackmail
- Political manipulation
- Coerced loyalty
- Silent power shifts
- Controlled scandals

## Attach To
- Nobles
- Merchants
- Clergy
- Council members
- Criminal intermediaries
- Party allies

## Leverage Sources
- Affair
- Hidden debt
- Criminal involvement
- Illegitimate heir
- Cult affiliation
- Smuggling ties
- Forged documents
- Past betrayal

## Escalation Options
- Leverage is revealed publicly.
- Leverage changes hands.
- Leverage is fabricated.
- Leverage is partially true.
- Leverage holder disappears.
- Third party discovers the secret.

> Design Note:
> Social Leverage creates tension without violence.
> It is most effective when the threatened party has visible status or authority.
