# Wave 6 Prototype/MVP Phasing Audit

This audit separates Wave 6's body/form, activity capacity, constrained movement, scale-domain, party-waiting,
CastRoster, carrier, transformation, participation, true-scale projection, persistence, and evidence goals from the
smallest honest proofs. The chronological authority remains [Wave 6's running record](01-questionnaire-and-triage.md).
It follows the global [phasing framework](../PHASING-FRAMEWORK.md), [Clay Proof Ladder](../CLAY-PROOF-LADDER.md), and
[Feature-Promotion Ledger](../FEATURE-PROMOTION-LEDGER.md). No implementation or dependency is authorized.

## Structural persistence and compression boundary

Wave 6 accepts deterministic structural compaction before a replaceable byte codec:

```text
one sparse canonical noun + typed component/owner refs
  -> one materialized current-state projection
  -> append each idempotent event/receipt once
  -> deduplicate immutable capsules/recipes/source/prose by stable id/hash
  -> coalesce only routine cold receipts into typed summaries
  -> retain identity, material turning points, custody, knowledge, causes, obligations, terminal truth
  -> store owner/site/world chunks incrementally
  -> project active causal frontier + deltas + pull-by-id roster/context
```

Full narration/transcripts remain separate optional archives; renderer payloads, search indexes, embeddings,
thumbnails, and provider prompts remain rebuildable/evictable projections. Wave 12 chooses the exact IndexedDB-
backed schema or proven successor, chunk format, codec, quota/recovery policy, and numeric gates from retained Mac and
long-world soak measurements. Codec choice may not weaken the semantic or recovery invariants above.

## Explicit prototype/MVP exclusions

- The proof does not resize, regenerate, replace, or fully normalize the roughly two-thousand-sprite corpus. A
  retained cast is integration and visual QA only.
- The proof does not add continuous collision skeletons, pose physics, emergent crowd packing, universal squeeze
  DCs, or provider-owned fit/risk rulings.
- The playable MVP does not offer a general voluntary split command, independently advancing companion scene,
  split screen, or offscreen companion action menu. A waiting party is self-inert.
- A pre-contact ordinal, latent object reserve, or concept candidate does not receive a full retained noun merely
  because generation or the camera could see it. Required continuity remains compact and player-contact cast remains
  distinct.
- Three semantic detail tiers do not mean three copied object schemas or nine combined detail/activation classes.
  Sparse components and orthogonal active/site/cold plus precision fields carry variation.
- The prototype does not support every mount, vehicle, guise, transformation, crowd, rescue, long body, swarm, or
  participant type. The shared contract applies to any family only when that family's mechanics are promoted.
- The first evidence set is not all twelve sites/eight traces, an exhaustive Cartesian matrix, a new Wave 6 corpus,
  or a claim of public hardware/accessibility usability.
- Exact byte sizes, record caps, compression ratios/codecs, DC catalogs, crowd counts, and final release thresholds
  must be measured. They may not become arbitrary scope cuts that erase canonical truth.
