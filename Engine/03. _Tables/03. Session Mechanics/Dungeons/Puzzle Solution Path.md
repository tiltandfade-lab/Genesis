#puzzle-solution-path
> **The fair, intended solve** — the answer the script *holds* so the DM doesn't have to invent (and then forget) one mid-scene. This is the heart of the anti-drift contract for puzzles: roll it the moment the puzzle is generated, write it to the segment as canon, and the DM honors *that* solution rather than rationalizing whatever the player happens to try. Each row is a **method** the clever, observant player can reach from the puzzle's tells — pitched to be *gettable*, not obtuse. Most key an ability or a tool so a well-built character is rewarded (anchor DCs to the ladder in `[[Tool Proficiency Uses]]`). The escape valve for a stuck player lives in `[[Puzzle Failsafe]]` — this table is the *front door*. d20, spice-graded (12 Grounded / 5 Textured / 3 Strange).

| d20 | Band | The fair solve | Keys / check (guideline) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grounded | Find the key, token, or shaped object nearby — it's hidden in this segment or carried from the last one. | Investigation / Perception to locate; DC 13. |
| 2 | Grounded | Read the order from a clue in plain sight — a worn step, a numbered carving, a faded fresco that shows the sequence. | Investigation to spot the order; DC 13. |
| 3 | Grounded | Weigh the plate correctly — gear, rubble, your own body, or an item of the named mass. | Int to deduce the needed weight; DC 12. |
| 4 | Grounded | Bring the matched halves together — the second is here, or on a body, or back where you came in. | Perception to pair them; DC 12. |
| 5 | Grounded | Answer the riddle — its wording points to one concrete, in-world thing the player can name or fetch. | Player figures it; or History/Arcana hint, DC 15. |
| 6 | Grounded | Apply the room's sorting rule — keep what shares the one true trait, discard the decoys. | Int (Investigation) to find the rule; DC 14. |
| 7 | Grounded | Clear and redirect the flow to its visible endpoint — pull the debris, tilt the channel, open the right valve. | Int or thieves'/artisan's tools; DC 13. |
| 8 | Grounded | Wedge, prop, or rig the hold so it stays without a living hand — a spike, a stone, a clever knot. | Dex/Int with tools or rope; DC 13. |
| 9 | Grounded | Complete the pattern — the missing piece is logically implied by the rest, or physically present to slot in. | Int (Investigation); DC 14. |
| 10 | Grounded | Position the light source so the cast shape resolves — angle a mirror, move a torch, block a beam. | Perception/Int; DC 13. |
| 11 | Grounded | Sound the right tone — match a pitch shown by resonating dust, water, or a tuned companion stone. | Performance/Perception; DC 14. |
| 12 | Grounded | Unscramble the hidden word — anagram, acrostic, or first-letters that spell a name the room wants. | Player figures it; or Investigation hint, DC 14. |
| 13 | Textured | Pay the named toll *correctly* — the small, intended price (a drop of blood, one coin, a true name), not a panicked over-payment. | Read the inscription: Religion/Arcana, DC 15. |
| 14 | Textured | Solve it in mirror — perform the action *reversed*, or in the twinned space, so the echo lands right. | Int to grasp the inversion; DC 15. |
| 15 | Textured | Refuse the bait and take the humble path — ignore the obvious lever; use the unglamorous door the room hoped you'd skip. | Insight/Wisdom to distrust it; DC 15. |
| 16 | Textured | Answer the living warden on its terms — soothe, feed, name, or honestly bargain with it instead of fighting. | Cha (Persuasion/Insight) or a fitting offering; DC 16. |
| 17 | Textured | Perform the rite or speak the cipher — gleaned from carvings, an NPC, a found document, or local custom. | Religion/History to know it; DC 15. |

| 19 | Strange | Change how you observe — look away, look through, look with another sense — so the shifting lock holds still. | Wisdom (Perception) lateral read; DC 16. |
| 20 | Strange | Win by not playing — name the trick aloud, walk away, or do the one mundane thing the room never guarded against. | Player insight; honor the cool (§5), no DC. |
