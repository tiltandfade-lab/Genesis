# Guard Post v015 ImageGen Source Prompt Record

Mode: built-in ImageGen generation.

These are reusable source-sheet prompts, not canonical world-truth declarations. The original
generated files are preserved under `source/`; packaging derives neutral masks and exact physical
modules without overwriting the originals.

## Reusable ivy

Create a transparent-source sprite sheet of modular ivy roots, vertical runners, horizontal
runners, paired wall-corner growth, and small connectors. Use an authored pixel-art 2.5D game
texture language between FFT and Triangle Strategy, crisp hard pixel clusters, no receiver wall,
no mortar, no baked shadow, no camera-specific ground plane, and a solid magenta key background.
Include both broadly reusable connectors and a few more distinctive bounded silhouettes.

## Contact grime

Create a transparent-source sprite sheet of wall-to-ground contact grime: continuous low aprons,
sparse and dense damp bands, left and right closures, and irregular accumulation. It must be
substrate-independent, horizontally useful, crisp pixel art, without bricks, wall shadows, ground
texture, or perspective. Solid magenta key background.

## Wet-edge growth

Create modular damp-edge and algae coverage masks: straight runners, inside/outside corners,
downward drips, outfall fans, pockets, and connector flecks. Substrate-independent, reusable on
stone, gravel, soil, and angled terrain; no baked receiver material or shadow. Crisp authored
pixel clusters on a solid magenta key background.

## Camera-neutral tufts

Create reusable grass and weed tuft sprites designed for four-bearing tactical cameras: radial
sparse, medium, and dense tufts, low connector scatter, plus a limited set of reed and flower
accents. Avoid one-direction ground shadows and camera-dependent bases. Crisp pixel art on a solid
magenta key background.

## Recolorable signifier cloth

Create emblem-free banners, flags, pennants, occupation rags, and blank cloth attachments as
neutral-value reusable masks. Shapes must support recoloring and later world-truth emblems,
paint, blood, or warning marks. No faction symbols, poles, walls, environment, or baked shadows.
Solid magenta key background.

## Architectural relief

Create modular architectural relief sources: horizontal course bands, cornice/cap pieces, quoin,
pilaster, rectangular aperture surround, arch surround, and a limited plaque/ornament set. Keep
universal joins distinct from culturally specific accents. Orthographic reusable source shapes,
crisp authored pixels, no wall receiver or environment, solid magenta key background.

## Defensive masonry

Create one square defensive-masonry parent texture with exactly five visually legible horizontal
courses representing five feet. Use large believable blocks, staggered bonds, rich authored pixel
clusters, restrained warm limestone variation, and no ivy, grime, apertures, lighting vignette, or
perspective. It will be packaged at 160×160 pixels: 32 pixels per foot and 32 pixels per course.
