*p. 65 MM 2024*

A cambion is the offspring of a fiend — usually a succubus or incubus — and a humanoid, usually a human. Even as a youth, a cambion identifies its rightful place as an overlord of mortals, learning quickly to orchestrate others for its own advancement. In cities and towns, cambions gather gangs of humanoids and lesser devils to serve them. In the Nine Hells, they serve as soldiers, envoys, and personal attendants to greater devils, accruing authority through sheer ambition.

A cambion born of Graz'zt — the demon lord with a particular appetite for humanoid consorts — is distinguished by charcoal-black skin, six-fingered hands, and a disturbing, unearthly beauty. These particular cambions tend toward more elaborate cruelty, and are far more likely to be encountered in positions of political influence than on an open battlefield.

Cambions are most dangerous not because of their combat power, which is considerable, but because of their patience. A cambion that cannot overpower the party will simply wait, recruit, and return when the odds are better.

### d10 The Cambion's Scheme (What Is It Actually Doing?)

Cambions are builders of power structures. They are almost never encountered alone — they are encountered at the center of something they have carefully constructed. Roll to determine the operation the party has stumbled into.

| d10 | The Cambion's Play | Campaign Tone & Execution |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **1** | **The Upstart Gang Lord** | **Urban Grit:** The cambion has taken over a criminal organization from the inside. The previous leadership either swore loyalty, disappeared, or both. The gang members don't know what their boss is. |
| **2** | **The Noble's Shadow Advisor** | **Political Intrigue:** Using *alter self*, the cambion has spent years as the trusted confidant of a powerful lord or council member, slowly steering their decisions toward chaos and ruin. |
| **3** | **The False Cleric** | **Religious Horror:** Disguised as a priest of a good-aligned deity, the cambion has built a genuine following among the vulnerable and desperate, harvesting their devotion and feeding it to a devil patron. |
| **4** | **The Pit Fight Promoter** | **Pulp / Action:** The cambion runs an illegal fighting ring in the underbelly of a city. The fighters are charmed, contracted, or blackmailed. The cambion is always betting on the outcome, with currency that isn't gold. |
| **5** | **The Cult Architect** | **Horror:** The cambion isn't the cult's god — it's the administrator. It has organized an entirely human death cult, keeping its own nature secret from even the highest-ranking members. It is using the cult to accomplish a very specific planar objective. |
| **6** | **The Guild Master's Heir** | **Mystery:** The cambion has legitimately inherited control of a prominent trade guild through a combination of charm, assassination, and patient manipulation. It now controls the flow of a critical resource through the region. |
| **7** | **The Mercenary Captain** | **Military / Action:** The cambion commands a disciplined mercenary company with a reputation for extreme effectiveness. It genuinely delivers results — the company wins — which makes it extraordinarily difficult to expose. |
| **8** | **The Planar Contractor** | **High Fantasy:** The cambion has been dispatched to the mortal plane by a greater devil to acquire something specific — a soul, an artifact, a location. It is on a deadline and will deal with the party directly if they can help it meet that deadline. |
| **9** | **The Desperate Defector** | **Moral Complexity:** The cambion is hiding from its fiendish parent, who is hunting it. It has been living among mortals for years and has developed something resembling a conscience — which it despises and can't fully suppress. It needs the party's help. |
| **10** | **The Long Play** | **Slow Burn:** The cambion has been operating in this city for 40 years under different identities. It is not trying to conquer anything. It is methodically corrupting the city's institutions — its courts, its temples, its guilds — one person at a time. |

### d10 The Cambion's Tell (Clues & Investigation)

A cambion disguised as a mortal leaves traces. They are subtle, but a clever party can find them if they know what they're looking for.

| d10 | The Clue | What It Means |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **1** | **The Cold Fireplace** | The cambion's private room never has a fire burning — it doesn't need warmth. The hearth is spotlessly clean and has never been used. |
| **2** | **The Contradictory Records** | A DC 14 Investigation check on the cambion's official history reveals two different ages, two different birthplaces, or a gap of several decades during which it appears in no records whatsoever. |
| **3** | **The Loyal Inner Circle** | Every single person in the cambion's closest circle is magically charmed — a fact that can be confirmed with *detect magic*. Not just the guards. The cook. The secretary. The confessor priest. |
| **4** | **The Impossible Deal** | The cambion has brokered a contract that gave it enormous power in exchange for something absurdly trivial — a single personal secret, a handful of copper, a promise to never reveal a specific name. The other party has come to the party wondering what they missed. |
| **5** | **The Mirror Avoidance** | The cambion never sits with its back to a wall — and it never stands in direct line of sight of a mirror. A DC 12 Perception check notices this consistent behavioral pattern. |
| **6** | **The Burned Letter** | In the cambion's fireplace (which it never uses for warmth) there is always one piece of recent correspondence that has been burned. The ashes of the most recent letter can be partially reconstructed (DC 15 Investigation) and contain instructions in Infernal. |
| **7** | **The Smell** | Cambions carry a faint sulfurous odor masked by expensive perfume. A character with a Keen Smell trait (or a trained animal companion) can detect this automatically. |
| **8** | **The Unreachable Background** | Every person the cambion claims to have grown up with, trained under, or worked alongside is conveniently dead, distant, or otherwise unavailable for confirmation. |
| **9** | **The Fiend's Mark** | One charmed member of the inner circle bears a faint brand on the back of their neck — an Infernal glyph that marks them as the cambion's property. DC 15 Religion to identify. |
| **10** | **The Devil's Invoice** | Somewhere in the cambion's private correspondence is a single document that cannot be explained away: a detailed accounting ledger written in Infernal, tracking souls against services rendered. |
