*p. 122 MM 2024*

In a campaign that embraces a wide spectrum of tones, the lantern-sage is a perfect bridge between the whimsical and the horrifying. Outwardly, they are flying, saucer-shaped jellyfish that communicate via telepathy and express their emotions by changing color. Mechanically, their greatest weakness is getting flipped upside-down.

However, beneath this slightly comical exterior lies a tragic and noble existence. Lantern-Sages feed by siphoning psionic energy, which forces them to experience the thoughts and emotions of their prey. Because they live in the deep underground, they are constantly subjected to the vile, sickening secrets of mind-thiefs, elder deep-things, and other subterranean horrors. They actively seek out good-hearted adventurers to share these dark secrets, hoping to enlist them to defeat the evils of the deep.

Some advanced "monastic" lantern-sages even gather in grand, nest-like cloisters, acting as clerics and sages led by an "abbot" who studies philosophy, mathematics, and religion.

### Lantern-Sage

_Small Aberration, Lawful Good_

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 7 (2d6)
- **Speed:** 5 ft., fly 30 ft. (hover)
- **STR:** 6 (-2) | **DEX:** 15 (+2) | **CON:** 10 (+0) | **INT:** 14 (+2) | **WIS:** 14 (+2) | **CHA:** 11 (+0)
- **Skills:** Arcana +4, History +4, Religion +4
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Psychic
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** Undercommon; Telepathy 60 ft.
- **Challenge:** 1/8 (25 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Advanced Telepathy:** The lantern-sage can perceive the content of any telepathic communication used within 60 feet of it, and it can't be surprised by creatures with any form of telepathy.
- **Prone Deficiency:** If the lantern-sage is knocked **Prone**, roll a die. On an odd result, the lantern-sage lands upside-down and is **Incapacitated**. At the end of each of its turns, the lantern-sage can make a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw, righting itself and ending the incapacitated condition if it succeeds.
- **Telepathic Shroud:** The lantern-sage's thoughts can't be read by any means, and magic can't detect its location or observe it remotely.

**Actions**

- **Tentacle:** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +4, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 4 (1d4 + 2) acid damage. At the end of each of its turns, the target must make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw, taking 2 (1d4) acid damage on a failure or ending the recurring acid damage on a success. A _Lesser Restoration_ spell cast on the target also ends the recurring acid damage.
- **Stench Spray (1/Day):** Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 10, one creature the lantern-sage can see within 15 feet. Failure: The target is coated in a foul-smelling liquid, exuding a horrible stench for 1d4 hours. The target is **Poisoned** as long as the stench lasts, and other creatures are **Poisoned** while within 5 feet of the coated creature. A creature can remove the stench on itself if it bathes during a Short or Long Rest.

### Encounter Scaffolds & Interaction Tables

#### d6 Lantern-Sage Color & Emotion

Because lantern-sages lack facial expressions, they broadcast their emotional state through bioluminescence. A perceptive party can read a lantern-sage's intentions from a distance before ever initiating telepathic contact.

|d6|Color|Emotion / State|Campaign Implication|
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**Soft Pink**|**Amused**|The lantern-sage finds the party's blundering through the dungeon highly entertaining and approaches playfully.|
|**2**|**Deep Blue**|**Sadness**|The lantern-sage is mourning. It recently fed on a psionic creature and absorbed horrifying, tragic memories of captive slaves.|
|**3**|**Vibrant Green**|**Curiosity**|The lantern-sage is intrigued by the party's magical gear or strange surface-world customs, floating close to inspect them.|
|**4**|**Crimson Red**|**Anger**|The lantern-sage has been attacked or deeply offended by an evil act and is preparing to defend itself with its Stench Spray.|
|**5**|**Pale Yellow**|**Contemplative**|Often the default hue of Monastic Lantern-Sages, indicating deep, philosophical thought or mathematical calculation.|
|**6**|**Rapid Flashing**|**Alarm / Panic**|The lantern-sage is frantically warning the party of an approaching, invisible, or telepathic threat that it has detected using its _Advanced Telepathy_.|

#### d4 Lantern-Sage Assistance (The Cloister's Aid)

Lantern-Sages are benevolent beings that organize into cloisters to support one another and any good-hearted travelers they encounter. If the party is battered by the horrors of the deep, a lantern-sage cloister might offer sanctuary.

|d4|Form of Assistance|Narrative Integration|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**Culinary Hospitality (Cooking Meals)**|Using their delicate front tentacles for fine manipulation, a monastic lantern-sage cooks a surprisingly delicious, restorative meal using safe cave flora and purified water. Eating it grants the party the benefits of a _Heroes' Feast_ or allows them to recover extra Hit Dice during a rest.|
|**2**|**Sanctuary & Nursing**|The party is dragged from the brink of death by lantern-sages. The creatures use their knowledge of herbalism and strange cave mosses to bind wounds, curing the party of poisons, diseases, or the _Dazed_ condition while allowing them a safe Long Rest in their hidden bower.|
|**3**|**The Deep Guide**|A brave lantern-sage volunteers to escort the party through a treacherous labyrinth. Because of its _Advanced Telepathy_, it can sense the psychic static of nearby aberrations, allowing the party to entirely bypass ambushes set by mind-thiefs, elder deep-things, or umber hulks.|
|**4**|**The Stolen Secret**|The lantern-sage does not offer physical aid, but shares a crucial tactical secret it learned by feeding on an evil telepath. It provides the password to an enemy vault, the true name of a demon, or the specific weakness of a local boss.|
