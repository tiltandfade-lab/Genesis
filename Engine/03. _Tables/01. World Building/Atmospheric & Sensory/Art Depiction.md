#art-depiction
> **What the local art depicts** — a player-facing, sensory reveal for the murals, carvings, tapestries, and statues of a place. **World-agnostic by design:** rows name *archetypes* (the founding, the war, the catastrophe, the patron-power), never specific people or places — the DM grounds each onto *this* world's rolled founder, faction, beast, or myth. The ladder climbs from ordinary commemorative art → a nameable wrongness in the work or its subject → art that behaves uncannily → art that is *acting on the world* → art that has rewritten what was real. The lower bands describe normal art of grander and grander subjects (the art stays mundane); the high bands are where the **art itself** is wrong. Spice-graded d100, Commitment ceiling (66 Grounded · 20 Textured · 9 Strange · 4 Volatile · 1 Mythic).
>
> **DM-only columns (`Legs` · `Pool`) — the Consequence Ladder (`docs/CONSEQUENCE-LADDER.md`).** `Legs` = story-potential (`dead-end` = narrate & forget · `hook` = a manipulable handle · `thread-seed` = a real lead/front · `canon-shift` = a Ledger-written world fact). Consequence routes on **`Legs`, not band** — most rows are `dead-end` on purpose ("sometimes a statue is just a statue"). `Pool` = the effect-pool archetype the *effect* roll routes to (Watcher / Passage / Oracle / Omen / Predation / Containment / Identity). Surfaced behind a Perception read; the **effect die is a player roll**. Re-authored 2026-06-29 (re-authoring craft pass) from a single-world Forgotten-Realms lore-dump; original archived in `zz_Archive/`.
