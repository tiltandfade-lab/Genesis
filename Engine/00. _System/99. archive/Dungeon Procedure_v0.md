## Treasure System

- *1 chest per segment unless an event calls for more.*
- *Only 1 chest per segment can roll Legendary or higher.*  
- *If another chest hits that range, downgrade one tier.*
- *Add +10 to the composition roll (cap at 100) for boss room treasure.*
