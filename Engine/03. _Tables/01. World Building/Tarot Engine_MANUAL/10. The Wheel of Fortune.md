### **X. The Wheel of Fortune: Trigger Effects**

The Wheel of Fortune represents the ever-turning cycles of life, the unpredictability of fate, and the transition between good and bad luck. When this card is drawn, the dungeon becomes a place of high stakes and constant rotation, where momentum is everything and stability is an illusion.

---

**The Constant: "The Turning of the Wheel"**

Fate is in constant flux. At the start of every round of combat (on Initiative Count 20), the DM flips a coin or rolls a d2.

- **Heads (High Tide):** All players gain a **+2 bonus** to attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws until the start of the next round.
    
- **Tails (the Wayside Tavern):** All players take a **-2 penalty** to attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws until the start of the next round.

|**10**|**Insane**|**The Living d20**|The monster is a floating, 10-foot-tall d20. Its current HP is always equal to its most recent d20 roll multiplied by 10.|

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

**The Constant: "The High Rollers"**

The faction has stopped all long-term planning. They now make every major decision—from troop movements to executions—based on the roll of a die or the flip of a coin.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**Faction Behavior (The Wheel)**|**Narrative Implication**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Boom & Bust**|The faction is currently either incredibly wealthy or completely bankrupt. Their mood and resources change weekly.|
|**6-9**|Less Grounded|**The Fate-Weavers**|The faction believes they can "rig" the wheel of fate. They are hunting for ancient artifacts that control probability.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The RNG Sovereignty**|The faction declares a new "Law of the Day" every morning by spinning a wheel (e.g., "Today, it is illegal to breathe through your nose").|

### **Trigger: Accepting Binding Agreements**

**The Constant: "The Double or Nothing"**

The bargain is a gamble. The "Gift" is massive, but there is a hidden trigger that could cause it to reverse into a "Curse" at any moment.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**The Bargain's Mutation (The Wheel)**|**The "Gift"**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Lucky Streak**|You have Advantage on all rolls for 24 hours. After that, you have Disadvantage on all rolls for 48 hours.|
|**6-9**|Less Grounded|**The Wealthy Swap**|You become the richest person in the room. Somewhere else, a person of your same status becomes a beggar.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Character Swap**|You swap character sheets with the player to your left for one session. All XP gained goes to your original character.|

### **Trigger: World Shifts**

**The Constant: "The Great Turning"**

The world enters a period of extreme instability. What was once certain is now up for debate, and those at the bottom of society suddenly find themselves at the top.

| **d10** | **Magnitude** | **The Nature of the Shift (The Wheel)** | **World Change**                                                                                         |
| ------- | ------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1-5** | Grounded      | **Economic Collapse**                   | All currency becomes worthless overnight. A new currency (e.g., blue shells or memories) is established. |
| **6-9** | Less Grounded | **Seasonal Chaos**                      | The seasons now change every 24 hours. Spring on Monday, Winter on Tuesday, Summer on Wednesday.         |
| **10**  | **Insane**    | **The Re-Roll**                         | The world "resets." Every NPC forgets their name and role, and everyone is assigned a new life by fate.  |
