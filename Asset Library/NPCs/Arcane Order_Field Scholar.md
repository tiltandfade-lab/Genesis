**Ilyan Voss (the Arcane Cabal Field Scholar)** _Archetype: Frantic Savant — Projects an aura of frantic, tightly-wound intelligence. He is intensely observant but completely lacks the social grace to explain his terrifying findings without sounding like a paranoid conspiracy theorist._

Notes for Play

- **Mannerism & Quirk (Quick Play):** He observes people silently before speaking, squinting as if trying to read their aura. When he does speak, he is rushed and slightly impatient, pulling loose, ink-stained parchment from his belt to point at erratic diagrams. He speaks quickly, frequently assuming the PCs understand complex arcane theory.
- **Ability & Role (Quick Play):** As a lower-level field researcher, he is the party's first contact with the Arcane Cabal. His **High Intelligence** means his theories and detections of magical residue are 100% accurate. However, his **Low Charisma** means he is socially awkward, abrasive, and completely incapable of convincing the city council that the threat is real.
- **Secret & Flaw (Quick Play):** His Secret is that he has already confirmed magical residue at the local disappearance sites and knows the cult is using structured ritual patterns. His Flaw (driven by the trauma of losing his mentor to a magical anomaly) is that he is desperate for validation and will pursue this investigation recklessly if the PCs ignore him.
- **Hook & Complication (Quick Play):** He tries to hire the PCs simply to act as credible "witnesses" at a site where he detected arcane disturbances. The complication is that if the PCs brush him off, he will go alone, likely getting himself killed and allowing the cult's ritual to advance without interference.

Catchphrase

_(Tapping an ink-stained finger aggressively against a crumpled piece of parchment)_: "No, no, you aren't looking at the pattern! The council called me paranoid, but the residue is right here. Just—come with me to the site. I just need you to look at it!"
