*p. 323 MM 2024*

The wyvern is what you get when you take a dragon's physical power, remove most of its intelligence, and add a scorpion tail. It is not a dragon — it has two legs rather than four, no breath weapon, no spellcasting, and no treasure hoarding instinct. What it does have is a natural aerial speed that matches mounted warhorses, talons capable of carrying off Large creatures, and a poisonous stinger that can kill an unarmored human in a single strike.

The wyvern is primarily a T2 threat — it shows up when the party has enough HP and saving throws to survive its initial assault but can still be killed by it if they're unlucky. Its combination of a flyby strike pattern and the Sting's potentially lethal poison means a wyvern encounter is always a resource drain even if the party manages it well.

Wyverns are used as mounts by hobgoblin warlords and ambitious warlocks. A ridden wyvern turns a standard wyvern encounter into a cavalry encounter with all the complications of dismounting a skilled rider before dealing with the wyvern itself.

### Campaign Integration: Investigation & Clues

Wyverns are territorial fliers with large ranges.

- **The Shadow Passes:** Frontier settlements describe a large flying shadow that passes over at dawn and dusk — the wyvern making its circuit. These sightings go back weeks or months before a wyvern attack occurs.
- **The Poison Victims:** Wyvern poison is distinctive and recognizable by any herbalist or physician (DC 11 Medicine). Victims of wyvern stings who survived describe intense, spreading numbness from the wound site. Bodies with no obvious wound except a small puncture and extreme pallor are wyvern kills.
- **The Nest Smell:** Wyvern nests reek of carrion and a specific musk the creature secretes for territorial marking. The smell is detectable before the nest is visible. A DC 12 Nature check confirms the source.

### 1. Wyvern

A large, territorial flying predator with a poisonous tail stinger.

_Large Dragon, Unaligned_

- **Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 110 (13d10 + 39)
- **Speed:** 20 ft., Fly 80 ft.
- **STR:** 19 (+4) | **DEX:** 10 (+0) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 5 (−3) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 6 (−2)
- **Skills:** Perception +4
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 6 (2,300 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +3

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The wyvern makes two attacks: one with its Bite and one with its Sting. While flying, it can use its Talons in place of one other attack.
- **Bite:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage.
- **Talons:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 13 (2d8 + 4) slashing damage. If the creature is Large or smaller, it is Grappled (escape DC 15). Until this grapple ends, the wyvern can't use its Talons on another target.
- **Sting:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage. The target must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw, taking 24 (7d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### 2. Ridden Wyvern

A wyvern trained and mounted by a skilled rider. The rider — typically a hobgoblin warlord, a warlock, or a dark knight — directs the wyvern's attacks with unusual tactical precision and makes dismounting attempts far more dangerous.

_Large Dragon, Unaligned_ (with Rider as separate stat block)

- **Armor Class:** 14 (natural armor + saddle rig)
- **Hit Points:** 110 (13d10 + 39)
- **Speed:** 20 ft., Fly 80 ft.
- **STR:** 19 (+4) | **DEX:** 10 (+0) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 5 (−3) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 6 (−2)
- **Skills:** Perception +4
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 8 (3,900 XP, includes rider) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +3

**Traits**

- **Controlled Mount:** While a rider is mounted, the rider can direct the wyvern to use any of its available actions instead of requiring its own action. The wyvern uses its own bonus action (if any) freely.

**Actions** (same as standard wyvern)

**Rider — Hobgoblin Warlord** *(use as separate combatant if dismounted)*

- **AC:** 17 (half plate) | **HP:** 52 (8d8 + 16) | **Challenge:** 3
- **Longsword +4** | **Javelin +4** | **Leadership (Recharge after Short Rest)**

### d10 The Wyvern's Domain (Investigation & Clues)

| d10 | Discovery | Investigative Hook |
|:----|:----------|:------------------|
| **1** | **The Poison Victim** | A body with a single small puncture wound and no other trauma. The victim's expression is frozen in surprise. A DC 11 Medicine check identifies wyvern poison as the cause of death. |
| **2** | **The Stripped Carcass** | A large animal carcass — a horse, a bear, an elk — with its chest torn open and large talon gouges in the hide. The wyvern ate its fill in place. The tracks show it landed to feed, not just struck from above. |
| **3** | **The Territorial Boundary** | Bleached bones arranged at irregular intervals along a trail — the wyvern's cached kills marking its territory edge. A DC 13 Nature check reads this as intentional territorial marking, not coincidence. |
| **4** | **The Nest Site** | High up (cliff face, ruined tower, large cave mouth), a mess of large sticks, animal hide, and bones. The nest is large enough to hold a horse. It may contain eggs. It may be currently occupied. |
| **5** | **The Shadow Report** | Local NPC or journal entry describes a recurring "darkness across the sun, like a very large bird" in the early morning. The sightings all come from the same direction, suggesting the wyvern's hunting route passes overhead at dawn. |
| **6** | **The Saddle Rig** | A wyvern-sized leather harness, custom made, with stirrups sized for a Medium humanoid, is found mounted on the wall of a dungeon garrison. Someone has been flying this wyvern. The harness bears the sigil of a specific warband or noble house. |
| **7** | **The Livestock Losses** | A farming community has lost seven animals in two weeks — taken from open fields, not from pens. The wounds (talon pattern, no blood trail) confirm aerial predation. The locals want it stopped; they're offering a reward. |
| **8** | **The Poisoned Ground** | The soil around a wyvern nest is lightly toxic — the musk secreted for territorial marking accumulates over years. A DC 13 Nature check identifies the cause. The vegetation is dead in a 20-foot radius of the nest entrance. |
| **9** | **The Failed Taming** | A dungeon journal describes an attempt to train a wyvern using a magical bridle. The entries grow shorter and more desperate over two weeks. The final entry is just a date and the words "It is free." The bridle is in this room. The wyvern is somewhere in this dungeon. |
| **10** | **The Warlord's Correspondence** | A sealed letter in a goblinoid warlord's writing describes the wyvern: its name, its hunting patterns, its weaknesses (a scar on its left flank where a prior attacker landed a telling blow), and where to find the keeper who controls it. The letter was addressed to an ally who never received it. |
