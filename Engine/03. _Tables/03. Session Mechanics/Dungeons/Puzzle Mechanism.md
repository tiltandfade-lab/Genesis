#puzzle-mechanism
> **How the puzzle is physically gated and what failure costs** — the concrete machinery wrapped around the `[[Puzzle Type]]`. Roll this to learn *what the player is actually touching* and *what going wrong does to them*, so the DM stops inventing whether a wrong guess hurts. Every row names a **gate** (what bars the way), a **tell** (the honest, perceivable warning per DM-Charter §5 — the puzzle is never a silent deathtrap), and a **failure cost** (what a wrong attempt does — kept Fork-scale: a cost, never an instant kill). Pair with `[[Puzzle Solution Path]]` and `[[Puzzle Failsafe]]`. d20, spice-graded (12 Grounded / 5 Textured / 3 Strange).

| d20 | Band | Gate (what bars the way) | Tell (the honest warning) | Failure cost (wrong attempt) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grounded | A barred door with a mechanism set into its face. | The bar is keyed to the mechanism — you can see the linkage but not force it. | Nothing happens; the mechanism resets with a flat click. Time lost. |
| 2 | Grounded | A pit or gap you can't cross until the puzzle lowers a bridge. | The far anchor-points are visible; the span is retracted. | The bridge jolts and re-retracts; a near miss, no harm. |
| 3 | Grounded | A portcullis or stone slab held up by the solution. | It's already trembling at the top of its track. | The slab drops a hand's width with a boom — a warning, not a strike. |
| 4 | Grounded | A locked chest, font, or reliquary the path needs opened. | Scratch-marks ring the lock; others tried. | The lock seizes harder; the next attempt is at disadvantage until reset. |
| 5 | Grounded | A series of plates, levers, or tiles wired to one door. | One is already depressed and glowing faintly — a started sequence. | The whole sequence resets to zero with an audible *thunk*. |
| 6 | Grounded | A current of water, sand, or light that must be redirected. | You can see where it *wants* to go and where it's blocked. | The flow spills wrong — a slick, a dousing, a snuffed torch. Inconvenience. |
| 7 | Grounded | A weighed plate that won't release the door until balanced. | The plate visibly tilts; a counterweight basket hangs empty. | The plate slams up; anything light on it is flung off. Bruises, not wounds. |
| 8 | Grounded | A spoken/struck lock — say or sound the answer. | The room has good acoustics; it's listening. | A sour echo; a held breath of silence, then nothing. Try again. |
| 9 | Grounded | A grid of symbols you must complete or correct. | The pattern is 90% laid; the gap is obvious, the *fill* is not. | A wrong glyph flares red and goes dark; one strike against you (see Failsafe). |
| 10 | Grounded | A door that opens only while something is held in place. | The hold-point is marked with worn handprints. | The instant you let go, it shuts. Costs you a held hand, not your life. |
| 11 | Grounded | A maze, dial, or routing board to thread to an exit. | The endpoint is lit; the route is fogged. | A dead end; the dial spins back. Pure time. |
| 12 | Grounded | A pair of halves — object, circuit, story — to reunite. | One half is here, fitted with an empty socket for its twin. | The mismatch sparks or grinds and rejects; no damage, just refusal. |
| 13 | Textured | A toll-gate that demands a price paid into it. | An inscription names the coin: blood, breath, a memory, a kept thing. | It takes a *lesser* toll for a wrong offering — a cut, a chill, a small forgetting. |
| 14 | Textured | A mechanism that runs in mirror — your action echoes, reversed. | A polished surface or twinned room shows the inversion plainly. | The echo backfires: a force, a slam, a shove on the mirrored side. A real but survivable hit. |
| 15 | Textured | A door that punishes the obvious move and rewards restraint. | The inviting lever is *too* clean; the safe path looks wrong. | Pulling the bait springs a contained hazard — gas, darts, a cage. Telegraphed, escapable. |
| 16 | Textured | A bound or sleeping warden that *is* the lock. | It stirs; its breathing, chains, or eyes track the room. | It wakes a degree angrier — a threat clock advances toward combat. |
| 17 | Textured | A rite, password, or code of conduct enforced by the room. | Carved etiquette, a watching face, a phrase half-legible. | A rebuke — wards flare, a voice denies you, standing with the place drops. |
| 18 | Strange | A lock that resets the room and your place in it. | A faint déjà-vu; clocks, candles, and wounds snap back on failure. | You're returned to the start — keeping only what you carry in your head. |
| 19 | Strange | A puzzle that rearranges itself as you study it. | It's visibly *moving* — slow, patient, watching back. | It scrambles further the harder you stare; brute scrutiny makes it worse. |
| 20 | Strange | A lock whose answer is to refuse the game it sets. | Everything screams *play me* — and one detail quietly says *don't.* | Playing along deepens the trap by one stage; the way out narrows. |
