#downtime-ledger
> **PROVISIONAL — Adam spot-check pending.** Rolled ONCE per week-long downtime montage
> (`[[TABLE-GAPS-070126]]` §3) — player declares intent (work / carouse / research / train / lie
> low), the montage passes the week (lodging charged), then this table says what the week actually
> yielded. Intent biases how the DM narrates the result, not which row lands — one table, not five.
> Rows carry a `Payout hook` naming the mechanical shape (gold swing scaled by place tier, a
> Distant-Word roll, a contact minted from the ambient pool, a condition, or a thread) for the
> wiring layer to resolve against the actual place/pool at the table. Spice-graded d100, Commitment
> ceiling (66 Grounded · 20 Textured · 9 Strange · 4 Volatile · 1 Mythic). Five band-anchor rows
> (marked ★) are Adam's approved samples from `TABLE-GAPS-070126.md` §6, included verbatim; the
> rest extend their register.
