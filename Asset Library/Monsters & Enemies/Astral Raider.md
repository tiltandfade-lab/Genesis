### Astral Raider Warrior

_Medium Aberration (Gith), Lawful Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 17
- **Initiative:** +4 (14)
- **Hit Points:** 49 (9d8 + 9)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 15 (+2) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 12 (+1) | **INT:** 13 (+1) | **WIS:** 13 (+1) | **CHA:** 10 (+0)
- **Gear:** Half Plate Armor
- **Senses:** passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** Common, Gith
- **Challenge:** 3 (700 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Actions**

- **Multiattack.** The astral raider makes two Psi Blade attacks.
- **Psi Blade.** Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 9 (2d6 + 2) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Psychic damage.
- **Spellcasting.** The astral raider casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability: At Will: *Mage Hand* (the hand is Invisible); 2/Day Each: *Nondetection* (self only)

**Bonus Actions**

- **Misty Step (2/Day).** The astral raider casts *Misty Step*, requiring no spell components and using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

### Astral Raider Knight

_Medium Aberration (Gith), Lawful Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 18
- **Initiative:** +5 (15)
- **Hit Points:** 117 (18d8 + 36)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 16 (+3) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 15 (+2) | **INT:** 14 (+2) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 15 (+2)
- **Gear:** Plate Armor
- **Senses:** passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** Common, Gith
- **Challenge:** 8 (3,900 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +3

**Actions**

- **Multiattack.** The astral raider makes three Silver Sword attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting to cast *Tongues*.
- **Silver Sword.** Melee Attack Roll: +6, reach 5 ft. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage plus 13 (3d8) Psychic damage. Critical: if the target is in an astral body (as with the *Astral Projection* spell), the astral raider can cut the silvery cord that tethers the target to its material body instead of dealing damage.
- **Spellcasting.** The astral raider casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13): At Will: *Mage Hand* (the hand is Invisible); 2/Day Each: *Nondetection* (self only), *Tongues*; 1/Day Each: *Plane Shift*, *Telekinesis*

**Bonus Actions**

- **Misty Step (2/Day).** The astral raider casts *Misty Step*, requiring no spell components and using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

### Astral Raider Dracomancer

_Medium Aberration (Gith), Lawful Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 18
- **Initiative:** +8 (13)
- **Hit Points:** 255 (30d8 + 120)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., Fly 30 ft.
- **STR:** 10 (+0) | **DEX:** 15 (+2) | **CON:** 18 (+4) | **INT:** 17 (+3) | **WIS:** 15 (+2) | **CHA:** 18 (+4)
- **Skills:** Arcana +10, Perception +8
- **Senses:** Blindsight 30 ft., passive Perception 18
- **Languages:** Common, Draconic, Gith
- **Challenge:** 16 (15,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +5

**Actions**

- **Multiattack.** The astral raider makes three Draconic Strike attacks.
- **Draconic Strike.** Melee or Ranged Attack Roll: +10, reach or range 120 ft. Hit: 12 (2d6 + 5) Slashing damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the start of the astral raider's next turn.
- **Conjure Dragon's Breath (Recharge 5–6).** Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 18, each creature in a 90-foot Cone. Failure: 27 (6d8) Fire damage plus 27 (6d8) Force damage. Success: Half damage.
- **Spellcasting.** The astral raider casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18, +10 to hit with spell attacks): At Will: *Mage Hand* (the hand is Invisible); 2/Day Each: *Nondetection* (self only), *Plane Shift*, *Tongues*; 1/Day Each: *Plane Shift*, *Telekinesis*

**Bonus Actions**

- **Misty Step (3/Day).** The astral raider casts *Misty Step*, requiring no spell components and using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
