## Optional Layer: Hidden Clue

An NPC may contain a discoverable secondary layer that advances the mystery.  
The clue is optional and does not gate progression unless deliberately designed to do so.

### Container Type
Where is the clue hidden?
- Lockbox beneath cellar floorboards
- Hidden compartment behind bar shelving
- Wrapped in oilcloth beneath dock planks
- Sewn into coat lining
- Buried near an old milestone
- Inside a hollowed beam or cask

### Clue Format
What form does the clue take?
- Coded letter
- Fragmented map
- Symbol etched in wood or stone
- Signet token tied to a faction
- Child’s drawing with geographic hints
- Prayer scrap referencing a hidden location

### Emotional Tone
What feeling does the clue evoke?
- Hopeful (“I’m still alive.”)
- Desperate (“They’re watching.”)
- Cryptic (“Trust no councilman.”)
- Protective (“Don’t follow me.”)
- Resigned (“I did this for you.”)

### Advancement Function
What does the clue accomplish?
- Reveals next location
- Deepens mystery without resolution
- Confirms political corruption
- Introduces a new faction
- Reframes the missing family’s agency

### Discovery Method
How might players uncover it?
- After clearing a cellar infestation
- Successful Investigation check
- Environmental interaction
- Accidental discovery during downtime
- Triggered by emotional roleplay
- Revealed by third-party confession

> Design Note:  
> The hidden clue is optional depth.  
> It rewards curiosity but does not require discovery to continue the campaign.
