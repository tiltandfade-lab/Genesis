#myth-costs
> **What a legend now demands, attracts, or inflicts** — rolled when a deed, person, place, or event has crossed into myth, to set the *cost* of being mythologized. Spice-graded d100, Commitment ceiling (66 Grounded · 20 Textured · 9 Strange · 4 Volatile · 1 Mythic). Table-improvement pass 2026-06-23: rebuilt from the thin 12-row "Mythic Consequences" to a full 100-unique-row ladder; the originals are in `zz_Archive/`. *(Part of the Myth suite — pairs with `[[Myth Seeds]]` (what kind of myth) and `[[Myth Becomes Geography]]` (how it scars the land). The suite is the situational "a place/deed became legend" payload, NOT the universal mythic-crit answer — see `docs/CRIT-MAGNITUDE.md`.)*
