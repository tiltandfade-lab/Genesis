*p. 132 MM 2024*

Ghouls are the cursed, undead remnants of humanoids who either indulged in cannibalism during their lives or were transformed through profane rituals. Unlike mindless zombies, ghouls possess a feral cunning, hunting in coordinated packs. They are driven by an insatiable, unending hunger; though they gain no actual nourishment from the flesh they devour, they are compelled to eternally gorge on the dead and make fresh corpses of the living.

Lacedons (often called sodden ghouls) are the aquatic cousins of the standard ghoul. They arise from aquatic humanoids or sailors who resorted to cannibalism at sea, or through the deliberate curses of bog hags and sea devils. They haunt reefs, shipwrecks, and foggy coastlines, using their swimming speed to pull paralyzed victims down into the crushing depths.

### 1. Ghoul

_Medium Undead, Chaotic Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 22 (5d8)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 13 (+1) | **DEX:** 15 (+2) | **CON:** 10 (+0) | **INT:** 7 (-2) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 6 (-2)
- **Damage Immunities:** Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Poisoned
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common
- **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The ghoul makes two Bite attacks.
- **Bite:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) necrotic damage.
- **Claw:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing damage. If the target is a creature that isn't an undead or an elf, it must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or have the **Paralyzed** condition until the end of its next turn.

### 2. Lacedon (Sodden Ghoul)

_Medium Undead (Aquatic), Chaotic Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 22 (5d8)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
- **STR:** 13 (+1) | **DEX:** 15 (+2) | **CON:** 10 (+0) | **INT:** 7 (-2) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 6 (-2)
- **Damage Resistances:** Cold
- **Damage Immunities:** Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Poisoned
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common
- **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Amphibious:** The lacedon can breathe air and water.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The lacedon makes two Icy Bite attacks.
- **Icy Bite:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 9 (2d6 + 2) cold damage, and the target's Speed decreases by 5 feet until the start of the lacedon's next turn.
- **Claw:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing damage. If the target is a creature that isn't an undead or an elf, it must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or have the **Paralyzed** condition until the end of its next turn.

**Bonus Actions**

- **Watery Rush:** While underwater, the lacedon moves up to half its Swim Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

### d10 The Charnel Scene (Investigation & Clues)

Because ghouls are driven by hunger rather than malice, they leave behind scenes that look more like an animal's feeding ground than a ritualistic murder.

|d10|Discovery|Investigative Hook|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Cracked Bones**|Skeletons scattered across the floor have been violently snapped in half. A DC 12 Medicine check reveals the long, tough tongues of the ghouls were used to completely lick the marrow clean from the inside.|
|**2**|**The Pristine Chainmail**|A bloody pile of armor and gear. Ghouls cannot digest metal or leather; they have meticulously stripped the armor off a victim, leaving the equipment completely unharmed and ready to be looted.|
|**3**|**The Missing Crew**|A merchant ship is found adrift. The cargo and gold are completely untouched, but the crew is gone, and the wooden rails are covered in wet, jagged claw marks leading up from the sea (Lacedons).|
|**4**|**The Desperate Diary**|A journal found in a trapped mining camp. The last entries detail the miners starving, drawing straws, and deciding to eat their dead friends to survive—explaining exactly how the ghoul pack was born.|
|**5**|**The Elf's Boast**|The half-eaten corpse of an elven scout. If the optional Elven Immunity rule is used, the scout died surrounded by dead ghouls, having fought bravely but eventually succumbing to sheer numbers rather than paralysis.|
|**6**|**The White Dust**|The stonework in the crypt is caked in a strange, white bone-dust. A DC 15 Religion check identifies this as the signature residue of creatures hailing from the White Kingdom of Doresain.|
|**7**|**The Locked Cellar**|A heavy oak door barred heavily from the _outside_. The wood is deeply gouged from the inside, indicating someone locked their infected, cannibalistic family member away as they transformed.|
|**8**|**The Foul Pearl**|Mixed in the gore is a small, black, poorly formed pearl. Necromancers or local alchemists might pay gold for these, as they are sometimes found in the stomachs of elder ghouls.|
|**9**|**The Frozen Paralyzation**|A terrified, living goblin or bandit is found standing perfectly rigid like a statue, sweating profusely. They were struck by a ghoul's claw moments before the party arrived and are currently paralyzed.|
|**10**|**The Missing Tongues**|Several corpses are found relatively intact, but their tongues and organs have been surgically removed by a Flesh Cult to create an "Organ Wretch" or to feed a favored ghoul pet.|
