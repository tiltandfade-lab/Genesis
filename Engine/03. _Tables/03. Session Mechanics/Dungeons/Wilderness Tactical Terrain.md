**How to Use This Table:** When you generate a wilderness map, roll 1d4 times on this table and draw these elements onto the grid. A field of grass becomes highly tactical when you drop a **Natural Trench (5)** in the middle, block line of sight on the left with a **Thick-Trunked Tree (2)**, and place a **Spore Cloud (27)** on the right flank!

|d50|Tactical Feature (Biome Agnostic)|Map Footprint|Mechanical Impact (2024 RAW)|
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**Earthen Berm / Low Ridge:** A raised lip of dirt, snow, or rock.|5' wide, 20' long|**Half Cover** (+2 AC/Dex saves). Drops to Total Cover if a creature falls Prone behind it.|
|**2**|**Thick-Trunked Tree / Stout Monolith:** A massive, solid obstacle.|5'x5' space|**Three-Quarters Cover** (+5 AC/Dex saves). Blocks Line of Sight.|
|**3**|**Copse / Rock Cluster:** A tight grouping of trees or stalagmites.|15'x15' area|**Half Cover** to anyone inside. Blocks Line of Sight for attacks passing entirely through it.|
|**4**|**Dense Canopy / Overhang:** Thick branches or a stone ceiling.|20'x20' overhead|**Tactical Ceiling.** Blocks aerial attacks. Flying creatures cannot ascend more than 10 feet here.|
|**5**|**Natural Trench / Dry Creek:** A recessed channel in the earth.|10' wide, 30' long|**Depression.** Creatures inside have Half Cover from attackers outside, but must spend 5 extra feet of movement to climb out.|
|**6**|**Fallen Log / Toppled Pillar:** A heavy, horizontal barrier.|5' wide, 15' long|**Half Cover.** Vaulting over it costs 5 extra feet of movement.|
|**7**|**Heavy Brush / Thickets:** Waist-high, dense vegetation.|10'x10' patch|**Lightly Obscured** (Disadvantage on Perception) and **Difficult Terrain**.|
|**8**|**Fog Bank / Steam Vent:** A lingering cloud of dense vapor.|15' radius sphere|**Heavily Obscured** (Blocks vision entirely; creatures effectively suffer the Blinded condition looking in or out).|
|**9**|**Hanging Moss / Vines:** Thick curtains dangling from above.|10' wide, 20' long|**Lightly Obscured.** Does not impede movement.|
|**10**|**Tall Reeds / Deep Snow:** Chest-high, yielding terrain.|20'x20' area|Small creatures have Total Cover; Medium creatures have Half Cover. **Difficult Terrain.**|
|**11**|**Sniper’s Perch:** A naturally elevated, hidden shelf.|5'x5' platform, 15' up|**Elevation & Cover.** Grants Three-Quarters Cover against attackers below. Requires DC 13 Athletics to scale.|
|**12**|**Choke Point:** Two massive obstacles leaving a narrow gap.|5' wide gap|**Bottleneck.** Forces single-file movement. A creature dodging in the gap provides Half Cover to allies behind them.|
|**13**|**Narrow Crevice / Fissure:** A tight squeeze through rock or roots.|5' wide, 15' long|Medium creatures must **Squeeze** (Disadvantage on attacks/Dex saves, attacks against them have Advantage, speed halved). Small creatures move normally.|
|**14**|**Terraced Steps:** Natural stairs of rock or packed earth.|Three 5' wide rows|**Elevation.** Each step requires 5 extra feet of movement to climb, but grants Advantage on melee attacks against targets on lower steps.|
|**15**|**Steep Scree / Icy Slope:** A treacherous, angled surface.|15'x15' slope|**Difficult Terrain.** If a creature takes the Dash action here, it must succeed on a DC 12 Acrobatics check or fall Prone.|
|**16**|**Blind Corner:** A sharp, L-shaped bend in a canyon or forest path.|L-Shape|**Line of Sight Blocker.** Impossible to target creatures around the bend with ranged attacks or spells.|
|**17**|**Sinkhole / Crater:** A sudden bowl-shaped drop.|10' diameter, 5' deep|**Depression.** Creatures inside are Prone to attackers outside (attacks from outside have Disadvantage if > 5ft away).|
|**18**|**Stepping Stones:** Solid footing spaced across a hazard.|1d4+2 5' spaces|**Platforming.** Normal movement if walking. Dashing requires a DC 12 Acrobatics check or the creature falls into the surrounding hazard.|
|**19**|**Briar Patch / Razor Rock:** Extremely jagged, hostile terrain.|10'x10' patch|**Hazardous Difficult Terrain.** Moving through costs 1 extra foot, and deals 1d4 Slashing damage per 5 feet moved.|
|**20**|**Sucking Mud / Deep Drifts:** Viscous or collapsing ground.|15'x15' patch|**Severe Hindrance.** Every 1 foot of movement costs 2 extra feet (effectively 1/3 speed).|
|**21**|**Wind Funnel:** A natural corridor that accelerates air.|10' wide, 30' long|**Wind Shear.** Ranged weapon attacks passing through this line have Disadvantage.|
|**22**|**Hollow Tunnel:** A massive rotting log or water-carved tube.|5' wide, 15' long|**Total Cover.** Medium creatures must crawl (Prone rules apply).|
|**23**|**Shattered Debris / Rubble:** Field of broken stone, ice, or timber.|20'x20' area|**Difficult Terrain.** Grants Half Cover to creatures that drop Prone within it.|
|**24**|**Natural Bridge:** A stone arch, fallen tree, or ice span over a drop.|5' wide, 20' long|**Precarious Span.** No cover. Taking damage while on the bridge requires a DC 10 Acrobatics check or fall off.|
|**25**|**Deep Water / Thick Acid:** A pool of opaque liquid.|15'x15' pool|**Swimming Required.** Costs 1 extra foot of movement. Melee attacks made underwater have Disadvantage (except daggers/spears/tridents).|
|**26**|**Glaring Surface:** A patch of highly reflective ice, water, or crystal.|10'x10' patch|**Visual Hazard.** Creatures looking across or standing in this area in bright light have Disadvantage on Perception.|
|**27**|**Spore/Pollen Cloud:** A localized haze of biological irritants.|10' radius sphere|**Lightly Obscured.** Creatures ending their turn inside cannot take Reactions until the end of their next turn.|
|**28**|**Unstable Crust:** Thin ice, brittle stone, or rotted roots.|10'x10' patch|**Trapdoor Terrain.** Breaks instantly if two or more Medium creatures stand on it, dropping them into a 5-foot pit.|
|**29**|**Dead Zone / Antimagic Flora:** A patch of weirdly silent ground.|15'x15' area|**Magical Dampener.** Magical light sources entering the area are suppressed (shed no light) until they leave.|
|**30**|**Echoing Hollow:** A bowl of stone or dense hardwood.|20'x20' area|**Acoustic Amplifier.** Stealth checks made here automatically fail due to extreme echoing.|
|**31**|**Thick Webbing / Sap:** A naturally sticky patch of terrain.|10'x10' patch|**Difficult Terrain.** Creatures entering must make a DC 12 Dex save or be Restrained. (Webs: AC 10, 5 HP, vulnerable to fire).|
|**32**|**Concealing Depression:** A shallow dip obscured by ground cover.|10'x10' patch|**Hideout.** Creatures inside have Advantage on Stealth checks to Hide.|
|**33**|**Clinging Kelp / Grasping Roots:** Active, hindering flora.|15'x15' patch|**Difficult Terrain.** A creature starting its turn here must make a DC 10 Str save or its speed is reduced to 0 for the turn.|
|**34**|**Magnetic Anomaly:** Ground rich in lodestone.|10' radius|**Metal Hindrance.** Creatures wearing metal armor treat this as Difficult Terrain.|
|**35**|**Gushing Waterfall / Silt Cascade:** A curtain of falling matter.|15' wide line|**Heavily Obscured.** Grants Total Cover against ranged weapon attacks (projectiles are knocked down).|
|**36**|**Rampart of Bone/Fossil:** The massive ribs of a dead leviathan.|5' wide, 20' long|**Slotted Cover.** Grants Three-Quarters Cover, but does not block Line of Sight (can see/shoot through the ribs).|
|**37**|**Boiling Puddle / Magma Seep:** A localized thermal hazard.|5'x5' patch|**Hazard.** A creature entering the space or ending its turn there takes 1d6 Fire damage.|
|**38**|**Static-Charged Brush:** Flora crackling with ambient energy.|10'x10' patch|**Hazard.** A creature taking the Dash action through this area takes 1d4 Lightning damage.|
|**39**|**Slippery Ledge:** A 5-foot wide pathway next to a drop-off.|5' wide, 20' long|**Precarious Path.** If a creature is hit by a melee attack here, it must succeed on a DC 10 Dex save or fall off the edge.|
|**40**|**Scattered Caltrops (Natural):** Hardened seed pods or sharp shells.|10'x10' patch|**Hazard.** Moving through at normal speed requires a DC 15 Acrobatics check; failure halts movement and deals 1 Piercing dmg.|
|**41**|**Rubble Ramp:** A collapsed wall or rockfall forming a ramp.|10' wide, 15' long|**Difficult Terrain Elevation.** Allows passage to a 10-foot height without requiring an Athletics climbing check.|
|**42**|**Submerged Drop-off:** Water/Mud that suddenly gets much deeper.|10' line|**Hidden Hazard.** The first 5 feet is shallow (Difficult Terrain), the next step drops into 10-foot deep water (Swimming).|
|**43**|**Shifting Dunes / Loose Dust:** Granular inclines.|15'x15' area|**Difficult Terrain.** Creatures pushed or shoved here slide an extra 5 feet in the direction of the push.|
|**44**|**Crystalline Outcropping:** Jagged, fragile formations.|5'x10' patch|**Half Cover.** If hit by a bludgeoning or thunder attack, it shatters, dealing 1d4 Slashing to adjacent creatures and destroying the cover.|
|**45**|**Shadow-Drenched Thicket:** An area of unnaturally persistent gloom.|20'x20' area|**Dim Light.** Darkvision is required to see clearly. Creatures without Darkvision have Disadvantage on Perception.|
|**46**|**Floating Earth Mote:** A chunk of land hovering mid-air.|10'x10', 10' up|**Airborne Platform.** Grants the high ground. Provides Total Cover to creatures under it.|
|**47**|**Vortex / Maelstrom:** A swirling anomaly in air or water.|10' radius|**Pulling Zone.** Creatures starting their turn within 10 feet are pulled 5 feet toward the center.|
|**48**|**Porous Pumice / Hollow Crust:** Highly brittle ground.|15'x15' area|**Fragile Terrain.** Any spell dealing thunder or bludgeoning damage turns this area into Difficult Terrain instantly.|
|**49**|**Overgrown Ruin Wall:** A 10-foot tall straight barrier.|5' thick, 20' long|**Total Cover.** Can be climbed (DC 10 Athletics). Fighting from the top grants Half Cover to the lower body.|
|**50**|**Perfect Ambush Ring:** A clearing surrounded by high ground and cover.|20'x20' center|**Kill Zone.** The center is completely exposed. The 10-foot perimeter around it is elevated and grants Three-Quarters Cover.|
