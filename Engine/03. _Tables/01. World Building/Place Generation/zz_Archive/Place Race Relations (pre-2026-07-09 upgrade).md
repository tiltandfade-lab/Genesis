#place-race-relations
> **How the peoples here get on** — the social weather between groups (bloodlines, faiths, classes, newcomers vs. old-bloods). Rolled when a PC heads toward a new settlement. Spice-graded d100, Commitment ceiling: most towns are ordinarily mixed and functional; the rare roll is where the divide turns uncanny or breaks.
