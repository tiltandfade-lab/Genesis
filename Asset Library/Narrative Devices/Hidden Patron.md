# Hidden Patron

Type: Narrative Device  
Purpose: Expand scope by revealing unseen influence behind visible actors.

## Core Concept
An antagonist, faction, or operation is secretly supported, funded, or manipulated by a larger hidden force.

## Typical Uses
- Political corruption
- Cult infiltration
- Organized crime
- Regional destabilization
- Long-term conspiracies

## Attach To
- Bandit camps
- Merchant guilds
- Town councils
- Religious sects
- Mercenary companies

## Patron Types
- Corrupt noble
- Distant criminal syndicate
- Cult leader
- Rival city-state
- Exiled aristocrat
- Fiendish influence
- Shadowy financier

## Revelation Paths
- Financial records
- Symbol recurring across scenes
- Arrested intermediary confession
- Distinctive coinage
- Unique spell signature
- Hidden correspondence

## Escalation Options
- Patron eliminates loose ends.
- Patron steps into the open.
- Patron replaces failed subordinate.
- Patron was unaware of full atrocities.

> Design Note:
> Hidden Patron should widen the narrative horizon.
> Use sparingly to avoid turning every problem into a conspiracy.
