### **XXI. The World: Trigger Effects**

The World represents the completion of the journey, the integration of all lessons, and the attainment of wholeness. It is the final destination where **spirit, air, fire, water, and earth** are no longer separate, but a single, unified force. Under this card, the dungeon becomes a place of perfect symmetry, divine success, and the overwhelming feeling that "everything is exactly where it should be."

---

**The Constant: "The Great Work"**

In the domain of the World, all forces are balanced. Resistance and vulnerability are concepts of the past.

- **The Effect:** If a player has resistance to one of the five elements (**spirit, air, fire, water, or earth**), they are considered resistant to all five while inside the dungeon.
    
- **The Toll:** "The Finality." Because this is the end of a cycle, resources do not regenerate easily. Players cannot benefit from a Long Rest until the dungeon’s "Goal" is completed. Every choice must be made with the end in mind.

The faction has reached its final form. They have integrated all their rivals and are now the dominant, undisputed power in the region. They are focused on "Finality" and "Order."
