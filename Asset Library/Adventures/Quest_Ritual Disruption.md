# Ancient Sunken Church

Party Size: 2 PCs  
Level: 2  
Milestone XP upon dungeon resolution  

---

## 1. Hook – The Dawn Priest

The Dawn Priest seeks the party after witnessing or hearing of their recent miracle.

### What He Says
- The veil between living and dead is weakening.
- A ritual is forming at the abandoned sunken church.
- The ward at the church responds only to living faith.
- He cannot break it alone.
- One touched by divine power must stand within the ritual space.

### What He Hides
- The ritual requires divine sacrifice.
- The miracle marks the PCs as ideal fuel.
- Cultists were informed ahead of time.
- The sacrifice must occur inside the ritual chamber during a pulse.

Insight success:
- He is withholding something.
Insight failure:
- He appears sincere.

---

## 2. Travel to the Church (Medium Encounter)

Swamp terrain. Heavy air. Signs of disturbance.

### Combat
2 Zombies emerging from shallow water.

Purpose:
- Resource drain.
- Establish undead tone.
- Teach small-party combat pacing.

After combat:
- First subtle magical pulse.
- Momentary chill.
- Water surface ripples unnaturally.

---

## 3. Upper Level – The Ruined Nave

No required combat.

### Elements
- Collapsed pews.
- Faded mural resembling veiled woman between pillars.
- Cracked basin carved with inverted triangle (water glyph).
- Faint chanting below.

Players can:
- Experiment with symbol (pour water).
- Study mural (possible minor insight blessing).
- Detect descending stair.

Pulse grows stronger here.

---

## 4. Lower Level – Ward Chamber

Alchemical fonts line the chamber walls.
Glyphs correspond to elements.

Pulses occur every few minutes of exploration.

### Interaction
Players can:
- Disable fonts.
- Activate water glyph.
- Investigate ritual geometry.

Incorrect manipulation:
- 1 Skeleton animates.

Purpose:
- Teach interaction under tension.
- Signal that ritual is active.

Clue here:
- This church was originally built to stabilize the veil.
- The ritual inverts its purpose.
- Reference to Mere connection.

---

## 5. Escalation Chamber (Light Combat)

Cultists arrived ahead of the party.

### Combat
1 Cultist
1 Skeleton

During fight:
- One cultist may shout:
  “Praise the Dawn! The offering comes!”

Subtle hint of priest involvement.

Pulse intensity increases.

---

## 6. Optional Secret Room

Hidden chamber beneath collapsed stone.

### Discoveries
- Old sealing relic.
- Historical text confirming church’s original purpose.
- The Book (strange, incomplete ritual text).

The Book:
- Not immediately usable.
- References broader ritual network.
- Possibly reacts faintly to pulses.

No combat here.

---

## 7. Ritual Chamber – Final Confrontation

Circular chamber.
Chalked sigils.
Standing water.
Chanting intensifies.

The Dawn Priest enters (or reveals himself).

### Confrontation
He frames ritual as:
- Reunification.
- Healing.
- Restoration.

He believes:
- Sacrifice transcends.
- The miracle proves divine sanction.

### Combat Composition (2 PCs Safe)
Modified Acolyte (Priest)
1 Skeleton

Pulses every 2 rounds:
- DC 12 Wisdom save.
Failure:
  - 1d4 psychic damage.
  - Priest gains temporary HP.
Success:
  - Insight into next glyph.
  - Advantage on disrupting ritual.

Ritual ends if:
- Priest is defeated.
- Glyph anchors destroyed.
- Chant interrupted during pulse.

---

## Failure Condition

If a PC drops to 0 HP inside ritual circle during pulse and priest completes chant:

Minor spirit manifests permanently.
Regional instability increases.
Campaign consequence seeded.

---

## Aftermath

If Priest dies:
- Tier I cell destabilized.
- Cult network hinted at.
- Book remains.
- Level up (Milestone).

If Priest survives:
- Potential redemption arc.
- Possible recurring antagonist.

If Ritual partially succeeds:
- Spirits begin appearing sporadically.
- Veil thinning becomes recurring campaign pressure.

---

## Campaign Threads Seeded

- Cult structure beyond the frontier town.
- Mere ritual network.
- The Book as future quest hook.
- Question of whether veil is truly a “wound.”

Cartomancer draws 3-card omen for next arc direction.
