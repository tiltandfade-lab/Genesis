#puzzle-type
> **What kind of gated challenge bars the way** — the spine of the puzzle toolkit. Rolled when a dungeon segment wants a *solvable obstacle* rather than a fight, a trap, or a found object. This is **not** an Interactable Object (`[[Dungeon Interactable Object]]`, a thing you fiddle with) or a Secret (`[[Dungeon Secret Type]]`, a thing you find) — a puzzle is a **challenge with a fair solution and a way past if you're stuck.** Roll this for the *flavor* of the lock, then `[[Puzzle Mechanism]]` for how it's gated, `[[Puzzle Solution Path]]` for the intended solve, and **always** `[[Puzzle Failsafe]]` so a solo player is never hard-blocked. Replaces the DM inventing a riddle on the spot (and forgetting its answer). Fork ceiling — a puzzle bends the path, it doesn't end the world. d20, spice-graded (12 Grounded / 5 Textured / 3 Strange).

| d20 | Band | Puzzle Type | The shape of it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grounded | Lock & key | A door wants a thing you don't have yet — a key, a token, a shaped fit. The whole room is the keyhole. |
| 2 | Grounded | Sequence | Press, pull, or step in the correct order. Wrong order resets it, or worse. |
| 3 | Grounded | Weight & balance | A plate, a scale, a counterweight. Put the right mass in the right place and the way opens. |
| 4 | Grounded | Matching | Pair the marks, the colors, the halves. Things that belong together must be brought together. |
| 5 | Grounded | Riddle | Words carved, spoken, or sung. Answer in kind — a word, a name, a deed — and the lock relents. |
| 6 | Grounded | Sorting | Too many of a thing; only some are right. Separate the true from the false by some rule the room teaches. |
| 7 | Grounded | Routing | Guide a flow — water, light, a current — from where it is to where it must go, around what blocks it. |
| 8 | Grounded | Pressure-hold | Something must be held down, held open, or held shut while someone else passes. A two-hands-three-doors problem. |
| 9 | Grounded | Pattern-completion | A design is unfinished. Supply the missing piece — the next symbol, the absent tile, the broken line. |
| 10 | Grounded | Light & shadow | Cast, block, or bend light so a shape, a path, or a word resolves out of the dark. |
| 11 | Grounded | Sound | A tone, a chord, a rhythm. Make the right noise — strike the right stone, name the right note — and it answers. |
| 12 | Grounded | Hidden word | A message is here, scattered or scrambled. Read it correctly and it tells you what to do. |
| 13 | Textured | Toll & sacrifice | The way demands payment — blood, a memory, a possession, a truth spoken aloud. It takes before it gives. |
| 14 | Textured | Mirror logic | What you do here happens *there*, reversed. Solve it backward, or solve it by undoing. |
| 15 | Textured | Trust the room | The puzzle lies to you. The obvious solution is the trap; the safe-looking path is the death. Read what the room *wants* you to do, then don't. |
| 16 | Textured | Living lock | A creature, bound or sleeping, *is* the door — it must be answered, soothed, fed, or outwitted, not broken. |
| 17 | Textured | Social cipher | The lock is a person, a ritual, or a code of manners. Say the password, perform the rite, name yourself rightly — or be turned away. |
| 18 | Strange | Time-loop | The room resets. Each attempt you remember; the room does not. Win by acting on what only you carry forward. |
| 19 | Strange | The puzzle watches | It changes as you study it — adapts, hides, rearranges. Solve it by changing how you *look*, not what you do. |
| 20 | Strange | Self-referential | The answer is the act of solving — name the trick, refuse the game, walk away, or do the one thing the room never expected anyone to try. |
