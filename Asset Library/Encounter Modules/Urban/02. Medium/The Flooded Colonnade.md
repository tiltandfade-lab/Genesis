## 30-Second Run

Recent storms or dark magic have backflooded the sewers beneath the Temple District into a 20×60 covered colonnade. Knee-deep, pitch-black water. The floor is carpeted in broken pottery — the first step auto-fails stealth and scares a raven flock off a corpse wedged in the caved-in sewer grate. The noise brings the crocodile. After the fight, an illusory shrine in a dry alcove hides the trapped iron door to a local criminal syndicate.

---

## Setup

**Space:** Covered colonnade, 20 ft wide × 60 ft long. Stone pillars running down the center. Entire floor is flooded — Difficult Terrain. Caved-in sewer archway at the far end (completely untraversable). Hidden shrine alcove right-side wall.
**Atmosphere:** Pick one before play: muffled chanting from a nearby sanctuary / heavy scent of wet ash / distant mournful bell / complete unnatural silence outside the water sounds.
**NPCs Present:** 1 Crocodile (initial), 2 Crocodiles (reinforcements, round 2–3), 1 Swarm of Ravens (feeding, disturbed immediately).
**If Ignored:** Flooding worsens. Syndicate operates from flooded front unchecked.

They erupt into the air, revealing what they were feeding on: the remains of a humanoid body wedged into the collapsed sewer grate.*
> *The dark water between you and the body begins to churn."*

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Crunch and the Surge

Entry into the colonnade auto-fails stealth. No check possible — the pottery carpet makes silence impossible.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 12 Perception (after ravens scatter):** Faded chalk markings on the pillars just above the water line — a safe path indicator, or syndicate symbols.
- **DC 13 Athletics:** Climb a pillar to get fully out of the water (height 10 ft, croc cannot follow).

**Escalation:**
- The Crocodile attacks on the initiative round after the ravens scatter.
- On **initiative count 0** each round, the water surges — all floating debris (pews, barrels) shifts 10 ft toward the entrance, constantly changing sightlines and cover.
- On **initiative count 0 of Round 2**, the sewer surges. Roll on the Sewer Surge table.

---

### Segment 2: The Eerie Draft and the Syndicate Door

During or after combat, a dry alcove on the right wall holds a modest shelf shrine. A wildly flickering candle points to an impossible draft.

**Read Aloud (alcove):**
> *"Tucked into a small, dry alcove is a modest shelf shrine — cracked offering bowl, heavy wax drips. The single remaining candle is flickering wildly, blown by an eerie draft where the air should be completely still."*

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 15 Perception:** The draft is blowing through the solid stone wall behind the shrine.
- **DC 15 Investigation:** The back wall is a permanent illusion. Behind it: a heavy iron door with a brass knocker.
- **DC 15 Investigation (knocker):** Spot the needle hole in the brass knocker and the pressure plate in the lock — it's trapped.
- **DC 15 Thieves' Tools:** Safely jam the needle mechanism.
- **DC 15 Thieves' Tools (lock):** Pick the lock. Fail by 5+: triggers the trap.
- **Carving on the underside of the offering bowl:** Thieves' cant — *"Knock once for blood, twice for coin."* (Pass-hint for guards inside.)

**Trap — Rigged Door Knocker:** Using the knocker, or failing the lockpick by 5+ = DC 15 Con save. Fail: 2d10 poison damage + Poisoned for 10 minutes. Success: half damage, no condition.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Pottery Carpet:** Automatic Stealth failure on entry. No check removes this.
2. **Knee-Deep Water:** Difficult Terrain throughout. All movement costs doubled.
3. **Moving Cover:** On initiative count 0, all floating debris shifts 10 ft toward entrance.
4. **Crocodile Grapple:** Targets hit by crocodile bite are Grappled (Escape DC 12). While grappled in water, the target risks drowning.
5. **Corpse / Syndicate Tattoo:** Examining the body (DC 13 Medicine, requires Athletics DC 15 to reach through the grate pressure) reveals a tattoo matching the chalk symbols on the pillars.

**Level 1:** 1 Crocodile total. Trap deals 1d10 poison.
