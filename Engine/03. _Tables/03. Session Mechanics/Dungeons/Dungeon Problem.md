### Dungeon Obstacles & Locks (d50)

Five equal categories of ten entries each. Roll to determine the obstacle type; each entry is a distinct spatial situation with two bypass options.

- **01–10 · Architectural Decay** — entropy; the dungeon is falling apart in your path
- **11–20 · Fluid/Environmental Systems** — infrastructure failure; climate and plumbing have broken down
- **21–30 · Infestation/Organic Growth** — colonization; something moved in and claimed the space
- **31–40 · Arcane Systems/Residual Magic** — design intent that outlived the designer
- **41–50 · Mechanical Systems/Constructed Locks** — craftsmanship; something a builder installed deliberately

| d50 | The Obstacle / "Lock" | The "Key" / Bypass Mechanic |

| **01** | **Collapsed Ceiling Choke:** A 10-foot section of corridor ceiling has caved in, filling the passage with rubble up to 4 feet deep. No Medium creature can pass. | **Labor/Skill:** DC 15 Str (Athletics) to drag enough stone aside for passage (20 min), or DC 12 Int (Investigation) to spot a load-bearing gap that can be leveraged with a crowbar. |
| **02** | **Tilted Floor Slab:** A massive flagstone has sunk on one end, creating a 30-degree slope across a 15-foot section. The low end drops into a 6-foot gap. | **Skill:** DC 13 Dex (Acrobatics) to cross the slick surface, or DC 12 Str (Athletics) to wedge a beam under the slab and walk the gap safely. |
| **03** | **Cracked Load-Bearing Arch:** The only passage runs under an arch visibly cracked through its keystone. It will collapse under significant weight or vibration. | **Skill:** DC 14 Wis (Perception) to identify the safe weight limit. DC 12 Dex (Acrobatics) to cross lightly in sequence — not simultaneously. Thunder damage or running triggers collapse: DC 15 Dex save or 4d6 bludgeoning + Restrained. |
| **04** | **Sunken Chamber Floor:** The floor of a chamber has dropped 12 feet, leaving only a 2-foot ledge along the walls. The pit below is dark. | **Skill:** DC 14 Dex (Acrobatics) to traverse the ledge, or DC 12 Str (Athletics) to find a collapsed beam and use it as a ramp across. |
| **05** | **Tipped Decorative Slab:** A massive stone panel has toppled forward, completely blocking the only stairway. It is 8 feet wide and solid granite. | **Labor/Magic:** DC 17 Str (Athletics) to push it off its pivot — two creatures can combine Strength. _Stone Shape_ carves a passage through, or _Mold Earth_ undercuts the base over 30 minutes. |
| **06** | **Buckled Doorframe:** An archway has caved inward; the door is jammed in a skewed frame with rubble piled against it. | **Skill:** DC 14 Str (Athletics) to force the door despite the pressure, or DC 13 Dex (Thieves' Tools) to remove the hinge pins and take the door off sideways. |
| **07** | **Weight-Sensitive Ceramic Tiles:** A 20-foot section has hollow ceramic tiles — original structural testing chambers. Each 5-foot square holds one Medium creature. Two in the same square cracks it, dropping them 10 feet. | **Skill:** DC 13 Wis (Perception) to identify the hollow sound underfoot. DC 12 Int (Investigation) to map a safe single-file crossing pattern. |
| **08** | **Compacted Sludge Mound:** A broken drainage channel has deposited a mound of compacted debris 5 feet high across the passage. | **Skill:** DC 12 Str (Athletics) to climb over, or DC 12 Con save to wade through the reeking material without becoming Poisoned for 1 hour. |
| **09** | **Eroded Staircase:** A stone stair descends 30 feet but the treads have worn to near-frictionless slopes. | **Skill:** DC 13 Dex (Acrobatics) to descend carefully (5 min). Rushing requires DC 14 Dex save or 2d6 bludgeoning + Prone at the bottom. A rope anchored above eliminates the check entirely. |
| **10** | **Wedged Fallen Column:** A massive pillar has fallen diagonally and is lodged in a doorway, leaving a 2-foot gap at the top and a 1-foot gap at the bottom. | **Skill:** DC 15 Dex (Acrobatics) to squeeze through the top gap, or DC 14 Str (Athletics) to shift the column enough to widen the bottom passage. |
| **11** | **Flooded Corridor (Knee-Deep):** A burst cistern pipe has left 40 feet of corridor under 2 feet of cold, murky water. | **Movement Tax:** No check to cross, but movement is halved. Creatures in medium or heavy armor make DC 10 Str (Athletics) or drop to 5 ft. per round. _Water Walk_ bypasses it. |
| **12** | **Flooded Chamber (Neck-Deep):** A chamber holds 5 feet of stagnant water. The exit door is 30 feet away on the far wall. | **Skill:** DC 12 Str (Athletics) to swim across in armor. Creatures in heavy armor must make DC 13 Str (Athletics) or begin suffocating while attempting to walk the bottom. |
| **13** | **Backed-Up Sewer Grate:** The only route forward passes through a grate holding back a pressurized sewage line. Opening it releases a torrent. | **Skill:** DC 14 Wis (Perception) to identify the pressure first. DC 13 Str (Athletics) to brace or DC 13 Dex to leap clear when it opens. Failure: DC 12 Con save or Poisoned for 1 hour. |
| **14** | **Steam Vent Corridor:** A ruptured heating pipe cycles scalding steam across a 20-foot corridor on Initiative count 10 each round. | **Timing:** DC 14 Wis (Perception) to time the cycle. On the correct round, passage is free. Moving through an active vent: DC 13 Con save or 2d6 Fire damage and Blinded until end of next turn. |
| **15** | **Ice Sheet Floor:** Subterranean cold has sheeted a 30-foot section of floor with transparent ice. | **Skill:** DC 12 Dex (Acrobatics) to cross at half speed. Taking damage or being pushed on the ice: DC 13 Dex (Acrobatics) or fall Prone and slide 1d6 × 5 feet in a random direction. |
| **16** | **Acid Seep Ceiling:** A ruptured alchemical pipe above coats the floor of a 15-foot section in shallow corrosive liquid. | **Skill:** DC 13 Dex (Acrobatics) to step between the drips, or DC 12 Int (Arcana/Nature) to neutralize the puddles with ash or chalk found nearby. Failure: 1d6 Acid damage and non-magical footwear is destroyed. |
| **17** | **Subterranean Runoff Channel:** An underground stream has cut a 10-foot-wide, 8-foot-deep channel across the dungeon floor. Current is fast. | **Skill:** DC 14 Str (Athletics) to cross the current. A fallen timber or shield laid flat eliminates the check. _Water Walk_ bypasses it entirely. |
| **18** | **Mineral Gas Pocket:** A colorless, odorless gas from a deep fissure fills a 20-foot chamber. Area is Lightly Obscured; Perception checks relying on smell have Disadvantage. Non-toxic. | **Skill:** DC 12 Con to hold breath through the crossing, or DC 12 Int (Nature) to stuff cloth into the fissure crack and disperse the gas in 2 minutes. |
| **19** | **Drowned Passage:** A 15-foot section of corridor is completely submerged under 6 feet of still water. Entry and exit points are above the waterline. | **Resource:** Requires breath-holding (DC 11 Con) or _Water Breathing_. A 10-minute rope rigging allows non-swimmers to pull themselves across the bottom. |
| **20** | **Pipe-Welded Door:** A fractured heating pipe has warped an iron door shut in its frame. The door is too hot to touch without protection. | **Skill/Resource:** Wait 10 minutes for the pipe to cool (DC 12 Survival to estimate when), or use cold water or _Ray of Frost_ to cool it in 1 round. DC 15 Str (Athletics) to force the warped door regardless, dealing 1d6 Fire damage to the forcing creature. |
| **21** | **Web Mass:** A chamber has been sealed floor-to-ceiling by webbing thick enough to constitute a wall. The web is 10 feet deep. | **Action:** AC 10, 5 HP per 5-foot cube. Fire destroys webs instantly but creates thick smoke (Heavily Obscured, DC 12 Con or Poisoned). Slashing weapons clear 5 feet per minute. |
| **22** | **Fungal Bloom Blockage:** A massive growth has sealed a door in its frame, rhizome tendrils anchoring it to the surrounding walls. Moving it releases spores. | **Skill:** DC 14 Wis (Nature) to cut the safe line avoiding spore sacs. Cutting blindly: DC 13 Con save or Poisoned for 1 hour. Fire destroys it but triggers the same save. |
| **23** | **Rot Grub Crawlway:** The only bypass to a sealed door is a 3-foot crawlway with a rot grub colony living in the floor debris. | **Skill:** DC 14 Wis (Perception) to spot the infestation before entering. DC 13 Dex (Sleight of Hand) to sweep a clean path (10 min), or torch the crawlway first — destroys the grubs but also any Stealth advantage. |
| **24** | **Calcified Hatch:** A floor hatch to the level below is completely sealed by decades of calcified lichen growth. Cannot be lifted by normal means. | **Skill/Tool:** DC 15 Str (Athletics) to break the seal (loud — nearby creatures make DC 12 Perception), or DC 13 Dex (Thieves' Tools) to scrape the seal clean without noise (20 min). |
| **25** | **Vermin Nest in Mechanism:** The crank that operates a portcullis or lift platform has been colonized by insects nesting inside the gear housing. Operating it without clearing them draws an attack. | **Skill:** DC 13 Int (Investigation) to spot the nest before touching the mechanism. Fire clears it in 1d4 rounds. Ignoring it: a Swarm of Insects attacks (+3, 2d6 piercing) while the crank is operated. |
| **26** | **Bone Pile Load-Bearing:** A compacted mass of old skeletal remains is structurally supporting a fractured ceiling section above the only corridor. Moving the bones risks a localized collapse. | **Skill:** DC 14 Wis (Perception) to identify the load-bearing bones. DC 12 Int (Investigation) to determine which can be safely removed. Disturbing the pile without the check: DC 14 Dex save or 3d6 bludgeoning + Restrained by debris. |
| **27** | **Mycelium Bridge:** The only crossing over a pit is a thick mat of interconnected fungal growth spanning 20 feet. Looks solid but cannot support combined weight. | **Skill:** DC 13 Wis (Nature) to assess the weight limit (one Medium creature at a time, no running). DC 13 Dex (Acrobatics) per creature to cross. Two creatures simultaneously or one running: DC 14 Dex save or it tears. |
| **28** | **Root Intrusion:** Underground tree roots have grown through the dungeon wall and fill a 10-foot corridor section, leaving only narrow twisting gaps. | **Skill:** DC 14 Dex (Acrobatics) to squeeze through. Medium creatures in heavy armor cannot pass without cutting a path (30 min, audible). Small creatures pass freely. |
| **29** | **Adhesive Slime Mold:** A non-aggressive ooze variant has colonized the walls, floor, and ceiling of a 20-foot section. Harmless but extremely sticky. Movement costs double; touching a surface leaves a creature Restrained (DC 14 Str to pull free). | **Skill:** DC 13 Dex (Acrobatics) to pass through the center without touching the walls, or _Grease_ to neutralize the adhesion in the target area. |
| **30** | **Egg Sac Blockage:** A massive carrion crawler egg sac is anchored across a corridor — 4 feet wide, ceiling to floor. Intact and unhatched. | **Skill:** DC 13 Wis (Perception) to identify it before approaching. DC 14 Dex (Sleight of Hand) to cut around it without rupturing it. Rupturing it releases 1d4 larvae (Swarm of Insects, paralytic sting: DC 12 Con or Poisoned). |
| **31** | **Ward Line:** A glyph-marked threshold bisects a corridor. Crossing without the original attunement deals 2d6 Force damage and sends a silent signal to 1d4 nearby constructs or guards. | **Magic/Lore:** _Dispel Magic_ suppresses it for 1 minute. DC 15 Int (Arcana) to deduce the original bypass condition. DC 13 Int (History) to recognize the builder's tradition and know the key. |
| **32** | **Dead-Magic Pocket:** A 20-foot-radius area where magic fails entirely. Spell slots cannot be expended, verbal components don't function, magic items become inert. Caused by a residual artifact at the center. | **Mundane Solution:** Must be crossed without magic. Concentration spells drop immediately on entry. DC 14 Int (Arcana) to identify the area before stepping in. Destroying the artifact (AC 13, 10 HP) ends the effect. |
| **33** | **Glyph-Triggered Lockdown:** A long-ago-triggered glyph has sealed all iron doors in the room (as _Arcane Lock_, DC 25 Athletics to force). | **Magic:** _Knock_ opens one door per cast. _Dispel Magic_ vs. DC 15 clears all locks within 20 feet. DC 15 Int (Arcana) to locate the trigger source and potentially reset it. |
| **34** | **Illusory Terminus:** A _Major Image_-level effect makes a continuing corridor appear to end in a solid wall — or makes a 10-foot pit appear to be solid floor. | **Skill/Magic:** DC 15 Int (Investigation) to spot inconsistencies (perfect texture, absent dust). Probing with a pole or thrown object reveals the truth. _Truesight_ bypasses it immediately. |
| **35** | **Gravity Inversion Room:** In a 30-foot-square chamber, gravity pulls toward the ceiling. The exit is on the far wall. Everything loose in the room fell upward when the effect triggered. | **Skill:** DC 13 Dex (Acrobatics) to navigate the inverted chamber and reach the exit. Encumbered creatures have Disadvantage. _Feather Fall_ makes the transition trivial. |
| **36** | **Teleportation Loop:** A 10-foot section of corridor has a spatial fold — walking forward through it returns the creature to the entry point seamlessly. | **Skill/Magic:** DC 15 Int (Arcana) to detect the fold. Bypassed by walking backward, casting _Dispel Magic_, or observing a thrown object loop back. Brute force has no effect. |
| **37** | **Forbiddance Threshold:** An ancient binding marks a doorway. A specific creature type (undead, fiend, or fey — set when placing) cannot cross it. Others feel intense unease: DC 12 Wis save or Frightened for 1 round. | **Lore/Magic:** DC 14 Int (Religion) identifies the binding and what it bars. _Dispel Magic_ suppresses it temporarily. A specific ritual fulfillment (offering, deity name, holy water) can permanently bypass it. |
| **38** | **Shrieking Rune (Silent Alarm):** A pressure-sensitive glyph is flush with the floor. Invisible without magical detection. Triggering it sends a silent arcane signal to a creature elsewhere in the dungeon. | **Skill/Magic:** _Detect Magic_ reveals it clearly. DC 16 Dex (Thieves' Tools) to scratch out the activation sigil. DC 14 Int (Arcana) to know that chalk dust or salt poured over it grounds the effect. |
| **39** | **Time-Slowed Zone:** A 15-foot-radius area where time runs at half speed. Creatures inside move at half speed and act every other round. Sustained by an anchor crystal embedded in the wall. | **Magic/Resource:** _Haste_ counters the effect on one creature. DC 15 Int (Arcana) to identify the anchor. Destroying it (AC 13, 10 HP) ends the effect for everyone. |
| **40** | **Blood Seal:** A door is sealed by a binding keyed to the original builder's lineage. A shallow basin sits before it. No other mechanism is visible. | **Lore/Magic:** DC 15 Int (Arcana or History) to identify the tradition and know any living humanoid blood may substitute (1 HP self-inflicted). _Knock_ bypasses it. DC 17 Dex (Thieves' Tools) to pick the physical lock concealed beneath the magical one. |
| **41** | **Portcullis with Missing Crank:** A heavy iron portcullis bars the passage. The crank mechanism is present but its handle has been removed or broken off. | **Skill/Tool:** DC 17 Str (Athletics) to lift manually — two creatures can combine Strength. DC 14 Dex (Thieves' Tools or Tinker's Tools) to fabricate a replacement handle from available materials and operate the crank. |
| **42** | **Counterweight Bridge:** A stone platform over a 20-foot internal chasm only lowers when 200+ lbs is placed on a pressure plate at the near end. | **Puzzle/Resource:** Place sufficient combined weight on the plate (bodies, gear, gathered stone). A locking pin can be found with DC 13 Investigation to hold the bridge down without the counterweight. |
| **43** | **Lever Array — Wrong Sequence:** Five iron levers control a door mechanism. The wrong sequence drops a ceiling panel. Environmental clues elsewhere encode the correct order. | **Puzzle/Skill:** DC 14 Int (Investigation) to read wear patterns and identify the most-used levers. Wrong sequence: DC 14 Dex save or 3d6 bludgeoning. DC 15 Int (History/Arcana) if sigils suggest a ritual order. |
| **44** | **Chain Hoist Locked at Top:** An iron platform for descending a 40-foot shaft is locked at the top. The release lever is on the platform itself, inaccessible from below. | **Skill:** DC 14 Dex (Acrobatics) to free-climb the shaft walls and reach the platform. DC 14 Dex (ranged, Disadvantage) to knock the lever with a thrown object. _Mage Hand_ operates the lever effortlessly. |
| **45** | **Four-Ring Vault Door:** A circular stone vault door has four iron rings that must be depressed simultaneously from different angles to disengage the lock. One creature cannot do it alone. | **Puzzle/Skill:** Requires two or more creatures operating different rings at once (DC 13 Str each). Alone: DC 16 Dex (Thieves' Tools) to rig a cord-and-pulley system using available materials. |
| **46** | **Stuck Chain Ladder:** A retractable iron chain ladder leads 25 feet to a lower level, but its release mechanism is jammed with rust and grime. The chain is up. | **Tool/Skill:** DC 14 Dex (Thieves' Tools) to free the release catch. DC 15 Str (Athletics) to force the corroded mechanism. DC 12 Int (Investigation) to spot a parallel shaft nearby usable as an alternate descent. |
| **47** | **Puzzle-Ring Door:** A circular stone door has three concentric rotating rings of carved symbols. The correct alignment is encoded in carvings found elsewhere in the dungeon. | **Puzzle/Skill:** DC 15 Int (Investigation) to align the rings using environmental context found nearby. _Knock_ bypasses it. DC 22 Str (Athletics) to force it — possible but audible and takes 10 minutes. |
| **48** | **Crest-Indentation Lock:** A door's only unlocking mechanism is a recessed crest-shaped indentation. The original key was a carved sigil belonging to the builder. | **Skill/Resource:** DC 16 Int (History) to recognize the crest and sketch a replica. DC 14 Dex (any artisan tools) to press a wax or clay impression. _Knock_ bypasses it. The door is otherwise effectively indestructible without siege equipment. |
| **49** | **Raised Internal Drawbridge:** A wooden bridge spans a 30-foot internal chasm. It is raised. The lowering winch is visible and accessible only from the far side. | **Resource:** Flight, teleportation, or DC 18 ranged attack to trigger the winch release. A grappling hook thrown to the winch handle (DC 14 Athletics) creates a pull rope. DC 12 Int (Investigation) to spot a counterbalance rope on this side that was overlooked. |
| **50** | **Displaced Keystone Mechanism:** A load-bearing keystone functions as the door's unlocking mechanism — pressing it inward opens the passage. The keystone has been removed and the arch is now unstable. | **Skill/Tool:** DC 14 Dex (Mason's Tools or Thieves' Tools) to improvise a wedge that re-seats the mechanism safely. DC 12 Int (Investigation) to spot the original keystone on the floor nearby. Re-seating it correctly opens the door; jamming it wrong: DC 13 Dex save or 2d6 bludgeoning from debris. |
