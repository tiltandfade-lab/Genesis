# The Covered Alley Bypass
![[Pasted image 20260310181529.png]]
> *Urban High. A rooftop shortcut interrupted by an elemental assassination — and a demon-summoning trap waiting at the bottom.*

## 30-Second Run

The party crosses a smoky tiled roof as a shortcut. Looking down into the covered alley: a Water Weird has a man in a guard uniform pinned in a horse trough, drowning him. Rotting crates on the roof burst open — rat ambush, cutting off the retreat. At the bottom of the stairs: a small locked desk with a chalk handprint marking it, and a refilled dirt hole beside it. The keys to the desk are in the trough with the dying man. Using Thieves' Tools on the lock triggers *Glyph of Warding* and summons demons.

---

## Setup

**Space:** 15-ft wide × 30-ft long covered alley. Eastern 10 ft = tiled rooftop, elevated 15 ft above the alley floor, connected by a 5-ft wooden staircase. The roof is Difficult Terrain (loose clay tiles).
**Atmosphere:** Tavern chimney smoke, stale beer haze. Dry air for a covered alley. The sound of muffled singing and clinking glass from surrounding taverns. Pick one from the table.
**NPCs Present:** Water Weird (trough, drowning the jailer), Corrupt Jailer (helpless, 3 rounds until death), 2 Swarms of Rats (crates on the roof), Wererat Sniper (optional, rooftop).
**If Ignored:** Water Weird finishes the kill. Jailer found later — district Heat increases. Wererat descends, uses the dropped keys, takes the stash, vanishes.

Suddenly, the rotting crates behind you burst open — a squealing mass of rodents pours out, cutting off your retreat."*

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Rooftop Ambush and the Drowning

Both crises begin simultaneously. The party is split between two urgencies.

**Water Weird Timer:** The jailer dies in **3 rounds** if the Weird is not engaged or dispelled. Fire damage forces the Weird to release its grapple. Sunlight in the alley disrupts it (it cannot act in bright light).

**Roof Tile Movement:** Difficult Terrain throughout the rooftop. Natural 1 on any Dex or Str check on the roof: a tile slides off and smashes on the street — 1d4 City Watch Guards arrive at the alley entrance in 1d4+1 rounds, assuming murder.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 13 Stealth:** Move across roof tiles without alerting the rats or slipping.
- **DC 14 Perception (from the roof):** Notice the heavy iron key ring glinting at the bottom of the trough.
- **DC 14 Acrobatics (roof jump):** Leap 15 ft to the alley floor without the stairs. Fail: 1d6 bludgeoning + Prone in the mud + Weird gets an Opportunity Attack.

**Escalation:** Roll on the Rat Ambush Tactics table to determine how the rats press the attack.

---

### Segment 2: The Trapped Desk

After combat, the alley is quiet. The desk awaits in the corner beneath a modest shrine.

**Read Aloud:**
> *"Tucked beneath a small corner shrine is a sturdy wooden desk. Dust recently disturbed, and a fresh chalk handprint marks the front of the locked drawer. Beside it, a shallow hole in the dirt has been hastily refilled, the soil still loose."*

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 14 Investigation:** Deduce from scuff marks + chalk handprint that the jailer was opening the desk when the Weird attacked — dropped the keys in the trough.
- **DC 15 Arcana:** Detect the invisible 3rd-level *Glyph of Warding* etched into the lock.
- **DC 15 Thieves' Tools (lock):** Pick the physical lock — but this **triggers** the glyph.
- **Dirt hole (DC 12 Investigation):** Find what's buried (roll d6 Dirt Cache).

**Correct Solution:** Retrieve the key ring from the trough (DC 14 Perception or DC 14 Investigation to find in murky water). Insert master key. Bypasses the glyph entirely. Opens the desk safely.

**Trap Trigger (if glyph detonates):** *Summon Lesser Demons*. 1d4+4 Dretches spawn in the enclosed alley, attacking the nearest creatures.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Water Weird Constrict:** +5 to hit, 3d6+3 bludgeoning, target Grappled + pulled into trough. While grappled: target risks drowning. Fire damage forces release.
2. **Key Ring in Trough:** DC 14 Perception or DC 14 Investigation (murky water) to find. Required for safe desk access.
3. **Glyph of Warding:** Detected DC 15 Arcana. Triggered by lockpick attempt or forced open. Effect: *Summon Lesser Demons* (1d4+4 Dretches).
4. **Loose Roof Tiles:** Difficult Terrain + Nat 1 mechanic (tile falls, Watch alert in 1d4+1 rounds).
5. **Chalk Handprint:** A character with the Criminal background or thieves' cant recognizes it as a "Secure Dead Drop" marker.

---

## Difficulty Dial

**Levels 1–2:** Replace Water Weird with 3 Bandits attacking the jailer. Trap deals 1d10 lightning instead of summoning demons.
**Levels 3–4:** As written.
**Levels 5–6:** Add 2 Wererats on the roof with heavy crossbows. Water Weird is pulling the jailer into a full cistern (larger space, faster drowning).

## NPC Dossier

**Wererat Sniper** (Optional)
Goal: Ensure the corrupt jailer dies without being able to talk. Protect the stash.
Leverage: Deeply cowardly. Values its own life over the faction's gold.
Boundary: Will not engage armored PCs in melee.
Tell: Sniffs the air rapidly, twitches its nose before each crossbow shot.

---

## Stat Blocks

**Water Weird** — *Large Elemental* — CR 3
AC 13 | HP 58 | Blindsight 30 ft.
**Constrict.** +5, 3d6+3 bludgeoning; target Grappled + pulled into water (Escape DC 13).
**Vulnerability:** Fire. Sunlight makes it incapacitated.
**Tactic:** Grapples and submerges. Ignores party until grapple is broken.

**Swarm of Rats (×2)** — *Medium Swarm* — CR 1/4
AC 10 | HP 24.
**Bites.** +2, 2d6 piercing. Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, slashing.
**Tactic:** Swarms isolated PCs. Uses terrain per d6 table.

**Dretch (if trap triggers)** — *Small Fiend* — CR 1/4
AC 11 | HP 18.
**Claws ×2.** +2, 1d6 slashing each.
**Fetid Cloud (1/day):** 10-ft radius, DC 11 Con save or Poisoned until end of next turn.
**Tactic:** Attacks nearest creature immediately, no coordination.

## Rewards

- **Jailer's Key Ring** — bypasses *Glyph of Warding* on the desk; unlocks any standard cell or shackle in the district
- **Smuggler's Stash** (inside desk): 50 gp + 2× Basic Poison + forged writ of release (magistrate seal)
- Jailer intelligence (what he knew about the operation, if stabilized before death)
- **Total:** ~50 gp + 2× poison + key ring + writ
