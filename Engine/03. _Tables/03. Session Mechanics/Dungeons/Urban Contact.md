Here is the massive, fully expanded d200 Social Encounter table. Every integer from 1 to 200 has its own row.

Drawing from your original foundation, the _Monster Manual_, and the varied environmental tables in the sources, this list spans every conceivable campaign tone. You will find slapstick comedy, grounded urban grit, high-society intrigue, heartbreaking tragedy, and visceral horror, all presented as actionable, non-combat (or strictly optional combat) social hooks.
