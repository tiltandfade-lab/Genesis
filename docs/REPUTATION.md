# Reputation — the world remembers what it SAW

## §0. Adam's forks (2026-07-01)

| Fork | Call |
| --- | --- |
| Source | **Witnessed or claimed only.** Deeds earn renown when seen by survivors, or when the player CLAIMS them ("let them know it was me" — a real choice with teeth). Stealth stays anonymous; infamy requires witnesses. |
| Decay | **Slow fade, legends stick.** Ordinary renown drifts toward 0 over in-world months (a World-Turn tick); deeds above a magnitude threshold mint PERMANENT epithets. The world forgets small things and mythologizes big ones. |

Pure ledger math — zero DM invention. The twin of `recall`: recall makes the world rhyme;
reputation makes it remember YOU.

## §1. State + sources

- **`w.renown = { factions: {slug:{score, epithets:[]}}, regions: {regionId:{score, epithets:[]}} }`**
  (score float, ±; epithets `{text, deedRef, day}`).
- **Deed events** (in `applyEvent`, after the existing handling): `kill`/`encounter_resolved`
  (weight from CR/`E(L)` — reuse ADVANCEMENT-RETUNE's unit), `front_closed` (front size),
  `clock_fired`-survived, `social_check` decisive outcomes, `gift` (given/received),
  capture-escape, betrayal-class `fact_canonized` (flagged).
- **Witness inference (script default):** a deed is witnessed iff — surviving NPCs in
  `scene.present` · OR the node is `nodeInhabited` · OR **a foe fled alive** (MONSTER-TACTICS
  morale-flee = tales get told; the fled survivor is the attribution vector). DM may override
  per-event with `payload.witnessed:true|false` (it adjudicates the fiction; the default covers
  the forgetting problem).
- **The claim verb:** new event **`claim_deed {ledgerRef}`** — the player announces authorship of
  an unwitnessed deed; renown applies from that day, and the deed becomes Distant-Word-eligible.
  (The DM offers the moment; the player decides. Runbook line.)
- Attribution targets: the deed's faction (foe's `factionId`, the front's faction) and the
  node's REGION (REGIONS-NAMES layer) both move; valence from the deed's direction (killing a
  faction's people = negative with them, positive with their rivals at half weight — the rival
  map exists in the faction web).

## §2. Decay + epithets

- **World-Turn T1/T2 tick:** every score drifts `×RENOWN_FADE` per in-world month elapsed
  (init 0.9); |score| < 0.5 → 0.
- **Epithet threshold:** any single deed with |delta| ≥ `EPITHET_MIN` (init: 2× a
  level-appropriate `E(L)` weight) mints a **permanent epithet** — generated by the DM to a
  contract (Hungering-Stone §8 pattern: ≤4 words, deed-specific, world-voiced — "Copperline-
  Burner", "the Debt-Forgiver") and **captured** via `codex_update` on the PC's record; the
  script requests it (`dm.needsEpithet` flag, same machinery as `needsEffectDie`). Epithets
  never fade; they ride Distant Word.

## §3. Consumers (all existing seams)

- **Social:** faction members' OPENING attitude shifts by `sign(score)×min(2,⌊|score|/RENOWN_STEP⌋)`
  (feeds `codexGetAttitude`'s lazy init — the parley system prices the rest).
- **Economy:** the shop attitude tint already exists — renown seeds the merchant's opening
  attitude like any faction member. No new price code.
- **Distant Word:** attributable deeds join the lens-bindable fact pool (misattribution rows can
  now misattribute onto or off of the PC — delicious).
- **Faction behavior:** score ≤ `HUNTED_AT` (init −6) marks the PC hunted — that faction's
  pressure/clock bearings bias toward the PC's location (WORLD-TURN's rim-bearing machinery,
  pointed inward).
- **Player surface:** the character sheet shows CLAIMED/witnessed epithets + a coarse standing
  word per *revealed* faction (`hated/wary/neutral/known/honored`); never the number.

## §5. Acceptance

Walk into a town wearing a claimed deed and the room already has an opinion. Kill quietly in the
dark and nobody does — until one goblin runs, and three villages later a stranger names you by a
thing you did in the rain. The record is the dice's, not the DM's.
