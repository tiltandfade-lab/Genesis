**High Priest Silas Vane (Cult of the Serpent Cult)** _Analog: Mads Mikkelsen — Projects an aura of terrifying, quiet fanaticism. He doesn't rant or rave; he speaks with the calm, absolute certainty of a man who has looked into the eyes of a god and survived._

Notes for Play

- **Mannerism & Quirk (Quick Play):** He maintains intense, unblinking eye contact when he speaks. The entire left side of his face and neck is heavily scarred by acid burns—a "blessing" he proudly received from the twin dragons.
- **Ability & Role (Quick Play):** As a Cult Leader hiding in plain sight, he operates with **High Charisma** (he is a magnetic speaker who easily lures the desperate or grieving into his flock). However, his acidic "blessings" have left him with **Low Dexterity**; he walks with a pronounced, stiff limp and relies entirely on his fanatical guards for physical protection.
- **Secret & Flaw (Quick Play):** His Flaw is his supreme arrogance: he genuinely believes the twin black dragons view him as an equal partner in their conquest. His Secret is that he possesses a corrupted, acid-fouled scrying bowl that allows him to communicate directly with the dragons deep in the marsh.
- **Hook & Complication (Quick Play):** Silas publicly advocates for the town to "make peace" with the swamp, offering his cult's services to search for the missing people. The complication is that he is actively using the town's panic to recruit new members, and anyone who joins his search parties is quietly fed to the dragons or transformed into a fanatic.

Catchphrase

_(Tilting his acid-scarred face into the light, speaking in a smooth, hypnotic baritone)_: "You fear the dark water because you do not understand it. The Serpent does not take; it _receives_. Come with me, and I will show you how to be received."
