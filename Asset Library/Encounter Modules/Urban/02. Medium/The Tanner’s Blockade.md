### Header

- **Title:** The Tanner's Blockade
- **One-line use:** A logistical puzzle and social chokepoint demanding quick talking, careful footing, or brute force.
- **Adventure Promise:** The party must navigate a fragile, acid-slicked chokepoint in the caravan district while dealing with an invisible magical barrier and a belligerent drunk who mistakes a PC for an old friend.
- **Adventure Scale:** Levels 1–4
- **Tags:** Urban, Caravan District, Chokepoint, Hazard, Social
- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Pressure Level:** Medium (Environmental hazards combined with social friction)
- **Rewards & Consequences:**
    - _Rewards:_ Passage secured, a bizarre rumor acquired, a secret map discovered.
    - _Consequences:_ Non-magical footwear destroyed, time delayed, or combat triggered with local thugs.

### The Set Up

- **Basic Narrative:** The sounds of the city are muffled here. A bottleneck blocked by caravan wagons and an open tanning vat forces the party to cross a fragile, waterlogged plank. Blocking the only exit door is a heavily intoxicated outlander who firmly believes one of the PCs is a long-lost friend.
- **Why the PCs Care:** This alley is the only direct route to their next objective that avoids the heavily patrolled main avenues. Turning back costs valuable time; causing a riot here brings the City Watch down on their heads.
- **Pre-roll Tables to Define Scene:**
    - **d6 Buildings (Shops/Guilds/Residences) Surrounding the Alley:**
        1. The Leatherworker's Vat (Active, smells of lye)
        2. Outlander Waystation (Crowded, smells of roasting meat)
        3. Abandoned Tenement (Boarded up, sagging roof)
        4. Draft Animal Stables (Loud, smells of manure)
        5. The Weaver's Drying Racks (Hanging canvases blocking sightlines)
        6. Illicit Dice Parlor (Muffled shouting behind a heavy iron door)
    - **d6 Scene Architecture:**
        1. Mud and scattered hay
        2. Broken, uneven cobblestones
        3. A narrow, 10-foot high brick wall on the left
        4. Overhanging timber balconies casting deep shadows
        5. Deep wagon-wheel ruts filled with stagnant water
        6. A rusted iron grating covering a sewer drop

### Segment Descriptions

_(Segment 1: The Bottleneck)_

- **What the PCs notice immediately:** Wagons and pens completely block the main street. The ground beyond a trench gleams with acidic slime. A waterlogged plank spans the trench. A drunk stands directly in front of the only exit door.
- **What does the DM read aloud?**
    
    > "The sounds of the city seem strangely muffled here, replaced by the heavy smell of campfires, livestock, and sharp chemicals. A wall of parked wagons and animal pens pinches the thoroughfare into a shadowed bottleneck. To proceed, you must cross a waterlogged, groaning plank spanning an ankle-deep trench. On the other side, an open vat of tanning fluid sits next to a heavy wooden door. Blocking that door is a swaying, heavily intoxicated outlander who suddenly points directly at one of you with a wide, sloppy grin. 'I know you!' they shout."
    
- **Skill Checks / Revelations:**
    - _DC 13 Charisma (Performance or Deception):_ Successfully play along with the drunk's delusion, allowing peaceful passage.
    - _DC 10 Strength (Athletics):_ Overpower the invisible _Unseen Servant_ holding the door shut from the other side.
    - _DC 12 Dexterity (Acrobatics):_ Cross the slime-slicked stones without slipping into the tanning trough.
    - _DC 13 Wisdom (Perception):_ Spot a torn map sticking out of the drunk's pocket or dropped in the muck.
- **PC Likely Choices:**
    - Play along with the drunk to defuse the situation.
    - Shove the drunk out of the way (triggers combat/escalation).
    - Try to climb the wagons to bypass the alley.
- **Variant Table (d6): The Drunk's "Old Friend" Memory**
    1. "You still owe me for that night in the port city!" (Demands 5 gp)
    2. "I heard you died in the Underdark!" (Needs a hug)
    3. "Where is my sister?!" (Becomes sorrowful and weeping)
    4. "You swore you'd never show your face here again." (Hostile paranoia)
    5. "Did you bring the shipment?" (Expecting illegal goods)
    6. "I haven't seen you since we served in the war!" (Wants to arm-wrestle)
- **References to Tables/Maps used:** Use standard 5 ft. grid rules for the narrow 10-foot-wide alleyway. The slime puddle and plank are Difficult Terrain.
- **Instructions to corral players if necessary:** If the PCs attempt to climb over the caravan wagons, heavily armed outlander guards immediately appear from the tents above, leveling heavy crossbows and telling them to "stay on the ground where you belong."
- **Escalation and pressure keys:**
    - If more than one Medium creature steps on the waterlogged plank, it snaps.
    - If the PCs reject the drunk or fail the Charisma check, the drunk becomes belligerent and shoves a PC toward the tanning trough.
- **Consequences of escalation and pressure:**
    - _Plank Snaps:_ PCs fall into the trench. Ruined stealth; footwear ruined by acidic runoff.
    - _Tanning Trough:_ Any creature pushed into the tanning trough takes 1d6 Acid damage.
    - _Violence:_ Attacking the drunk draws 1d4+2 **Bandits** (caravan hands) from the nearby dice parlor in 1 round.

### Clues

- **Clues & NPCs that can reveal them:**
    - _The Bizarre Rumor:_ If the PC successfully plays along with the drunk, the drunk slurs, "Did you hear? The magistrate's new bodyguard doesn't cast a reflection in the silver mirrors at the bathhouse."
    - _The Torn Map:_ A partial map of the immediate neighborhood dropped in the mud. It reveals a safe, un-patrolled route through the heavily guarded noble district.

### Item Stat Blocks

- **Treasures:**
    - _Torn Map:_ Mundane item. Grants Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to navigate the noble district for the next 24 hours.
    - _Dropped Pouch:_ If the drunk is subdued or pickpocketed (DC 14 Sleight of Hand), they hold a pouch containing 3d6 sp and a set of bone dice.

### NPC Dossiers

- **Kaelen the Drunk (Outlander)**
    - **Goal:** To catch up with his "old friend" and relive the glory days.
    - **Leverage:** Readily accepts alcohol, nostalgic stories, or aggressive camaraderie.
    - **Boundary:** Will not let the party pass unless his "friend" answers him.
    - **Tell:** Sways heavily, points an accusatory but joyful finger.

### Stat Blocks

- **Bandit**
    - _Use standard 2024 Core Rules Bandit stat block._ (AC 12, HP 11, Scimitar +3 to hit, 1d6+1 slashing).
- **Unseen Servant (Hazard/Obstacle)**
    - _Mechanic:_ An invisible force holds the exit door shut. It requires a contested Strength (Athletics) check to open. Treat the servant's Strength score as 2. If the PC fails the check, the door inexplicably slams shut again.
