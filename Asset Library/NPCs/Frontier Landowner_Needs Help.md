# Maris Tallowmere

## Level 1 — Immediate Presence

**Race:** Human (Lowlander)  
**Occupation:** Minor landholder leasing farmland near the marsh edge  
**Role:** Authority Proxy — filling in for someone absent  

**Visual:** Keeps boots laced to military precision.  
**Demeanor:** Speaks too loudly for indoor spaces.  
**Mood (Default):** Impatient and short-tempered.  
**Hook:** Wants to hire the party but cannot pay immediately.

**Talent:** Silent tracker.  
**Unrealized Potential:** No.

---

## Level 2 — Structural Position

**Want:** Avoid public scandal or exposure.  
**Faction Position:** Uses religion for civic leverage.  
**Fear:** Isolation — being left alone with consequences.

**Influence Weight:** 1 — Personal Only  
Her actions primarily affect her own land and immediate dependents.

**Resource Control:**  
Ownership of a critical supply line (grain, livestock, marsh access route).

**If Ignored (Personal):**  
Spreads blame subtly to protect herself.

**If Ignored (Regional):**  
Militia recruitment intensifies.

---

## Level 3 — Deep Structure

**Secret:** Is considering leaving town soon.

**Leverage:** Controls entry to a guarded area (her leased land and supply path).

**If Cornered:** Tries to intimidate subtly.

---

## Interpretation Discipline

Maris is not a villain.

She is:

A strained frontier operator trying to maintain dignity.

She may be:
- Covering up crop failure.
- Hiding damage from marsh encroachment.
- Concealing unpaid debts.
- Hiding something found on her land.

Her inability to pay immediately is not deception.
It is pressure.

She hires adventurers because she cannot afford public failure.

---

## Frontier Quest Potential

- Livestock going missing.
- Smuggling route crossing her land.
- Strange signs in marsh grass.
- Ritual residue on boundary stones.
- Militia probing her holdings.
- Rival land claim dispute.
