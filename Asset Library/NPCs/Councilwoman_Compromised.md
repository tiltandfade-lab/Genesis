**(Insert Name) — Councilwoman of the frontier town** _Analog: Gillian Anderson (Claire Underwood from Hannibal / House of Cards) — Projects an aura of absolute, icy composure and distance. She speaks little, letting her silence do the intimidating, but is internally crumbling under massive anxiety._

Notes for Play

- **Mannerism & Quirk (Quick Play):** Her "Public Face" becomes her immediate acting cue. She is composed, distant, and politically silent. She speaks incredibly slowly, taking long, ponderous pauses before answering questions. Physically, she looks exhausted (Low Con), and her hands are rigidly clasped together to hide the fact that they are shaking.
- **Ability & Role (Quick Play):** As a Local Leader, she wields enormous political power. Her High Charisma means she is exceptionally persuasive and impossible to fluster in a debate, but her Low Constitution shows the physical toll the stress is taking on her body.
- **Secret & Flaw (Quick Play):** We collapse her "Private Truth," "Fears," and "Notes" into one dark Secret: She is being blackmailed by Jack Torver over her niece's disappearance and refuses to acknowledge it publicly. Her flaw is that she will act as an Unwilling Accomplice to crimes just to keep the scandal suppressed.
- **Hook (Quick Play):** The players are led to believe she is a corrupt villain orchestrating local crimes (Misidentified Villain). The complication is that exposing her doesn't solve the crime; it just gets her kidnapped niece killed by Jack Torver.

Catchphrase

_(Spoken in a slow, icy, and deliberate tone)_: "I have no idea what you are implying about my family. This council meeting is concluded. Please see yourselves out."
