## Evidence / inference boundary

The packet uses a three-level discipline:

- **OBSERVED:** a measured plan, official record, policy, or site history directly
  supports the relationship stated.
- **SYNTHESIZED:** several observed relationships support a generator rule, but no
  source claims the game mechanic.
- **FANTASY ADAPTER:** Genesis world/material/realm facts license a transformation. It
  must preserve the custody and operating invariants, but historical sources are not
  cited as proof of impossible construction.

No historical institution is treated as a neutral model or moral endorsement. Several
sources document abusive, coercive, colonial, racist, or otherwise harmful systems.
They are used to understand how purpose and power alter space and operation, not to
sanitize those histories.

## Declared gaps

- No direct tactical-RPG prison-map cohort is available in this worktree.
- The measured-plan lane is strongest for British and U.S. examples. The NDL source
  broadens the visual lead but is untranslated here and cannot carry detailed claims.
- Surety, hostage-house, quarantine, religious-penance, and supernatural doctrines do
  not yet have enough direct evidence for MVP promotion.
- Robben Island and Australian Convict Sites add necessary non-building and coercive
  landscape breadth, but they remain colonial institutions rather than a balanced
  global premodern sample.
- Modern rights and operations sources establish minimum relationships. They do not
  make modern prison architecture the universal fantasy default.
- Historical cell dimensions—especially deliberately punitive ones—are not player-space
  targets. Standee, escort, camera, access, sanitation, and evacuation clay tests own
  Genesis dimensions.
- No honest historical analogue was found for the suspended living-ironwood solitary
  cell. It remains a current-roller fantasy adapter whose support, access, service,
  property, force, and fall consequences must be proved directly.
- No linked image is approved as shipping art.
