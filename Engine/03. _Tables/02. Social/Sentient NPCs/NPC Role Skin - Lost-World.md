# NPC Role Skin — Lost-World (deep-time saurian dominion)

> Realm skin over [[npc-role-spine]] ([[NPC-ROLE-REALMS]] approach C). **DRAFT RE-KEY 2026-07-08
> (`craft/lost-world-rekey-draft`, docs/LOST-WORLD-REKEY.md).** Lost-World is now the **saurian-court
> skin**: the scaled court rules the valley and does not count mammals as people, so nearly everyone
> the party moves among is the mammal underclass in the hills — forager, delver, tithe-bearer, or
> rebel — with the ruling scales appearing as overseers and scholars who deign to walk among the
> stock. It takes **one hard drop** — Pampered-elite (mammal society under the tithe has no idle
> insulated class, and the court's own pampered nobles don't mix into the mammal-facing NPC pool;
> when a scaled elite is wanted, use the overseer/scholar/rebel Adds) — and **reweights hard** (the
> same numeric weights as the prior key, re-justified): settled bulk-labor thins (Land-worker,
> Feeder, Clothier — the hill clans are small, marginal, feeding themselves under a boot, not
> running a market), while the ruin-and-tithe economy swells (Delver, Thief, Wild-provider, Trader,
> Hired-blade, Outlaw) and clan faith / forbidden scholarship swells with it (Rite-keeper,
> Hidden-fanatic, Secret-scholar) — this realm's true demography. Soul/note/class inherit from the
> spine; only the LABEL changes. Blank Weight = spine default, `0` = drop. Kin-agnostic /
> recontextualize per convention. Lever/atom — exempt from the situation family (do NOT tag
> `table_family`).
