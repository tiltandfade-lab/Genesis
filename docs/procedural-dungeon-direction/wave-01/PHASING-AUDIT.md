# Wave 1 Prototype/MVP Phasing Audit

This is the accepted implementation-horizon audit of the closed Wave 1 direction. It does not reopen Wave 1's
semantic answers. Adam accepted the revised cut and small-pass delivery reframe on 2026-07-21. Terms follow the
global [phasing framework](../PHASING-FRAMEWORK.md).

## Pre-alpha-critical foundation

These are narrow invariants, not promises of full mature breadth:

| Decision family | Minimum critical form | Why it cannot wait |
|---|---|---|
| Site identity and provenance | Stable original purpose, builder/operator doctrine, current use, and provenance ids/fields | Rooms otherwise become unrelated rolls and later history cannot attach to stable causes. |
| Canon commitment | A seed/commit boundary; committed and action-created facts never reroll; latent detail resolves deterministically | A persistent world cannot tolerate facts changing because a room is revisited or a model is called again. |
| Truth versus knowledge | Canonical truth, viewpoint knowledge, and unverified claims/beliefs are distinct authorities | Retrofitting this after prose and saves accumulate would leak secrets and corrupt continuity. |
| Functional coherence | A site-scale envelope and a small required-capability/room-obligation roster | The first dungeon must feel capable of its stated function rather than like a themed random-room list. |
| Stable discovery identity | Discoverable facts/opportunities have stable ids and viewpoint-safe state | Clues and callbacks otherwise duplicate, disappear, or become impossible to reconcile. |
| Honest impossibility | Overconstrained combinations fail or relax through visible governed rules | Silent contradiction is not an acceptable scaffold. |

## Proof/MVP scaffold -> feature goal

The `Pre-alpha form` column below means the **playable MVP**, not a disposable proof prototype. Under the global
borderline rule, the MVP retains a minimal cross-room promise, meaningful procedural contrast, normal party
participation, and at least one visibly operating resource/obligation flow.

| Decision family | Pre-alpha form | Accepted feature goal | Seam retained now | Promotion trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operating model and resources | A small list of functional guarantees, weighted room tags, and at least one live stock/obligation flow whose success or failure visibly changes play | Rich institutional operation, supply/depletion, staffing, and resource ecology | Typed capability and resource ids; explicit owner and cadence fields | More than one site family cannot express meaningful differences with the small catalog. |
| Historical transformation | One to three typed transformations plus a current-use overlay | Deep, time-indexed causal cascades with subordinate/repurposed architecture | Ordered transformation ids and before/after obligations | Playtests need callbacks or conflicts that the shallow history cannot explain without invention. |
| Repeated functions | Simple family templates with stable instance ids and bounded local variance | Hierarchical repeated assemblies with multi-scope repetition control | Family/instance relation and variance policy | A larger site makes repetition feel cloned or loses operational legibility. |
| Spice coherence | One scoped site root with conservative child inheritance and no causal cascade beyond what is stored | Band-first budgets, multi-level inheritance, and a final flavor pass | Scope, parent, budget, provenance, and deterministic selection seam | Multiple scopes or high-Spice sites show uncontrolled clustering or bland averaging. |
| Secrets and discovery | Stable local opportunities plus at least one cross-room promise/callback expressed through Gemini prose and clicked inspection where relevant | Secret networks, multiple interlocking promissory discoveries, graded remounting, and richer contract-card pressure | Discovery ids, knowledge state, causal parent, legal reveal conditions | Players miss or cannot pursue multi-room promises despite correct clues and one working chain. |
| Occupation and claims | Holder/occupant/claim tags sufficient to distinguish original and current use | Dynamic multi-domain fronts, competing claims, and doctrine-driven modification | Actor/group ids and typed relation to place/object | Multiple active groups make the simple relation model ambiguous. |
| Current d200 content | Keep useful rows as source material and map only used rows into explicit typed recipes | Fully decomposed typed rollers with replacement-proof coverage | Provenance back to source row; semantic result stored, not re-rolled | Reuse, contradictions, or coverage gaps make row-level mapping the main authoring bottleneck. |
| DM priority contracts | A small explicit queue of site obligations and unresolved discoveries | Strategic card hands with eligibility, slack, expiration, and richer pressure | Typed obligation id, owner, eligibility, completion/retirement state | Gemini loses obligations or over-repeats them in longer play. |
| Party split and extended bounded places | Normal co-located party/companion participation, stable participation tags, and local adjacency; only exotic remote or simultaneous split cases remain conservatively unsupported | Full split-party, remote participation, and complex bounded-place support | Actor/place identity and participation authority | A vertical slice deliberately includes split or simultaneous remote play. |
| Rich inventories and multi-beat social places | Consequential unique objects, simple stocks, a bounded NPC/group summary, and at least one persistent dungeon relationship that can carry across multiple beats | Broad inventory ecology and sustained social-place operation | Exact unique-item custody plus typed aggregate stock and relationship seams | The core loop proves greater social/economic depth is needed for replay rather than just more content. |

## Recommended Wave 1 pre-alpha slice

The proof prototype may begin with one small site family. The playable MVP must demonstrate meaningful procedural
contrast: at least two contrasting site configurations—either two families or one family across materially
different purpose/history/current-occupation seeds. Each has a recognizable functional roster; one or two historical
changes that alter present rooms; a live resource/obligation flow; current occupants who use or misunderstand the
structure; normal party participation; and at least one cross-room promise/payoff. Preserve stable ids,
truth/knowledge separation, exact commitments, and honest failure. Do not require the full institutional simulator,
complete d200 decomposition, deep-time cascade, or mature DM card economy to make those dungeons playable.
