|d20|Condition|Visual & Tactile Effect|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**Pristine / Unnaturally Clean**|Looks as if it were placed here moments ago. It is entirely untouched by the surrounding elements, dew, or dust.|
|**2**|**Overgrown**|Heavily choked by creeping ivy, thick moss, or dense local lichen. It must be pulled free to examine clearly.|
|**3**|**Sun-Bleached / Wind-Scoured**|All color has been leached away. The surface is scoured completely smooth by blowing sand, snow, or wind.|
|**4**|**Scorched**|Blackened, charred, and brittle. It smells strongly of old ash and crumbles slightly when handled.|
|**5**|**Frozen / Rime-Covered**|Coated in a thick layer of frost or encased entirely in a shell of clear ice. Cold to the touch.|
|**6**|**Waterlogged / Sodden**|Soaking wet, spongy, and dripping. It is resting in a shallow depression of mud, puddle, or stagnant water.|
|**7**|**Infested**|Teeming with harmless local vermin (ants, centipedes, maggots, or tiny crabs) that scatter when approached.|
|**8**|**Partly Buried**|Half-swallowed by the natural terrain. It requires digging through dirt, sand, snow, or thick leaf litter to extract.|
|**9**|**Shattered / Torn**|Violently broken into multiple pieces or shredded, as if struck by a massive, blunt blow.|
|**10**|**Rusted / Rotted**|Utterly succumbed to the elements; metal flakes away at a touch, and wood/cloth/bone crumbles to dust.|
|**11**|**Gnawed / Clawed**|Covered in deep, distinct tooth or claw marks from a passing predator or scavenger.|
|**12**|**Petrified / Calcified**|The object has been turned entirely to stone, or is thickly coated in hard, crusty mineral deposits.|
|**13**|**Defiled**|Smeared with dried blood, foul ichor, or animal droppings.|
|**14**|**Resonant / Glowing**|Emits a faint, harmless magical light (Dim Light in a 5-foot radius) or a low, vibrating hum that can be felt in the teeth.|
|**15**|**Web-Choked**|Wrapped thickly in dense, mundane spiderwebs or the sticky, cocoon-like silk of a local insect.|
|**16**|**Fungal Bloom**|Sprouting small, colorful, harmless mushrooms, molds, or thick bracket fungi.|
|**17**|**Makeshift Nest**|Small, harmless wildlife (birds, mice, snakes, or frogs) have repurposed it as a home or shelter.|
|**18**|**Revered / Arranged**|Deliberately and carefully placed. It sits on a crude pedestal of stacked stones or is encircled by a neat ring of pebbles/twigs.|
|**19**|**Melted / Corroded**|Slagged and deformed by extreme heat, or visibly pitted and splashed by a highly acidic substance.|
|**20**|**Flickering / Phasing**|Shifts slightly out of phase with reality. It is translucent, casts no shadow, and feels unnaturally cold to the touch.|
