*p. 32 MM 2024*

A banshee is the undead remnant of an elf who was so consumed by vanity, bitterness, or cruelty in life that their death left a spiritual wound too raw to close. The resulting spirit retains an awareness of its former beauty and an overwhelming, howling grief over its loss. Banshees are not aggressive in the calculating way of a wight or intelligent undead — they are in constant, sustained anguish, and they lash out at the living because the living serve as a reminder of what the banshee was and can no longer be.

The Corrupting Touch and the Horrifying Visage are powerful, but the Wail is what defines a banshee encounter. It affects every creature within 300 feet that can hear it — a radius that, in an underground space with good acoustics, can include an entire dungeon. Any creature at fewer than half HP when the Wail fires drops immediately to 0 HP. A healthy party can survive it. A party that has already taken significant damage may see multiple members drop in a single action. The wail recharges on a 1 in 6 per round — it can happen twice in a combat.

Banshees are always elven. This is not arbitrary flavor — it's a narrative hook. If a banshee is present, there is an elven story somewhere in the dungeon's history. Finding that story is often the key to the banshee's vulnerability or, in campaigns willing to go there, its resolution.

### Campaign Integration: Investigation & Clues

Banshees leave a spiritual residue that affects the living.

- **The Mournful Sound:** The banshee's presence produces a low, distant wailing audible at the edge of perception — a sound that makes the listener feel inexplicably sad or anxious. NPCs in the area describe a "sound like crying in the walls" for weeks before anyone actually encounters the banshee.
- **The Elvish History:** Any location with a banshee has an elven past. A ruined elven estate, an elven tomb, an old elven trading post — something happened here to an elf of significant ego or cruelty. The historical record almost always survives somewhere.
- **The Visage Effect:** Creatures who have encountered a banshee's Horrifying Visage and survived describe it for weeks — a face they can't forget, a sadness that follows them. This is a lore trail back to the banshee's location.

### 1. Banshee

An undead elven spirit of shattering grief, whose Wail can drop every wounded creature in earshot.

_Medium Undead, Chaotic Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 58 (13d8)
- **Speed:** 0 ft., Fly 40 ft. (hover)
- **STR:** 1 (−5) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 10 (+0) | **INT:** 12 (+1) | **WIS:** 11 (+0) | **CHA:** 17 (+3)
- **Saving Throws:** WIS +2, CHA +5
- **Damage Resistances:** Acid, Fire, Lightning, Thunder; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** Cold, Necrotic, Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common, Elvish
- **Challenge:** 4 (1,100 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Detect Life:** The banshee can magically sense the presence of living creatures up to 5 miles away. She knows the general direction they're in but not their exact locations.
- **Incorporeal Movement:** The banshee can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. She takes 5 (1d10) force damage if she ends her turn inside an object.

**Actions**

- **Corrupting Touch:** _Melee Spell Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 12 (3d6 + 2) necrotic damage.
- **Horrifying Visage:** Each non-undead creature within 60 ft. that can see the banshee must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be **Frightened** for 1 minute. A Frightened target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, with Disadvantage if the banshee is within line of sight, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a target's saving throw is successful or the effect ends for it, the target is immune to this banshee's Horrifying Visage for the next 24 hours. A creature that fails by 5 or more ages 1d4 × 10 years. *(Optional; see Age rule.)*
- **Wail (1/Day):** The banshee releases a mournful wail, provided she isn't in sunlight. This wail has no effect on constructs and undead. All other creatures within 300 ft. that can hear her must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, a creature drops to 0 hit points. On a success, a creature takes 10 (3d6) psychic damage.

### d10 Banshee Encounters (Tone Variance Table)

| d10 | Encounter Premise | Campaign Tone / Mechanics |
|:----|:------------------|:--------------------------|
| **1** | **The Wail Warning** | **Tactical Priority:** The party hears the banshee's ambient sound in the next room. They have one minute to heal to full HP before engaging — because the Wail is coming and they are currently at 60% HP. |
| **2** | **The Mournful Plea** | **Social Layer:** The banshee doesn't immediately attack. It speaks — in Elvish — a broken account of what was taken from it. A party member who understands Elvish can hear a name, a betrayer, and a request. Whether the party acts on it is their choice. |
| **3** | **The Wail Cascade** | **Horror Moment:** The Wail fires on round two. Two PCs are below half HP. The DM rolls the Constitution saves in full view of the table. One PC drops. The rest of the table feels what the spell intended. |
| **4** | **The Hidden History** | **Investigation Hook:** The party found the banshee's former identity in the dungeon's records before reaching her. They know her name. Using it in conversation causes her to momentarily stop — she hasn't heard it spoken in decades. It creates an opening for something other than pure combat. |
| **5** | **The Age Effect** | **Dramatic Consequence:** A PC fails the Horrifying Visage save by 5 or more and ages 30 years. They're fine mechanically — but they look elderly now, which creates social complications and a quest motivation to find a way to reverse it. |
| **6** | **The Protect the Wounded** | **Escort Danger:** An NPC the party is protecting is already wounded. If the Wail fires, the NPC dies instantly unless the party can prevent the banshee from using it — perhaps by keeping the banshee in melee, disrupting her action. |
| **7** | **The Tomb Discovery** | **Setting Context:** The banshee guards a tomb that contains exactly what the party came for. She won't allow anyone near it. The tomb is her former crypt. What she's protecting is her own memory — her grave goods, her portrait, her written history — and she will die before she lets it be touched by strangers. |
| **8** | **The Detect Life Tracker** | **Dungeon Dread:** The party learns the banshee can detect life at 5 miles. She has known they were in the dungeon since they entered. She chose her moment. Everything the party has done in the past hour was observed. |
| **9** | **The Resolution Path** | **Campaign Arc:** A DC 15 History check, combined with the dungeon's existing records, reveals what the banshee needs to rest — an apology delivered to her grave by a named living descendant of her betrayer, or the destruction of a specific object. The party can solve this without fighting. |
| **10** | **The Dual Visage** | **Two-Phase Encounter:** The banshee opens with Horrifying Visage. Most of the party passes. One PC is Frightened. Then, before the Wail, she uses Corrupting Touch twice on the same target. Then the Wail fires. She was targeting the most wounded party member from round one with a specific kill sequence. |

### d10 The Mournful Site (Investigation & Clues)

| d10 | Discovery | Investigative Hook |
|:----|:----------|:------------------|
| **1** | **The Portrait** | A painted portrait in the dungeon depicts an elf of striking beauty, clearly from a significant noble family. The face is the banshee's former face. A DC 12 History check identifies the family name and whether they still exist. |
| **2** | **The Grief Record** | A historical account of an elf who died in this location — the cause of death, the manner of it, and, critically, whether anyone responsible was ever held accountable. They weren't. |
| **3** | **The Ambient Sadness** | Any creature that spends more than 1 hour in the banshee's territory without engaging in active combat must make a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or gain the Frightened condition toward an unrelated object or person of their choosing for the next 8 hours. The banshee's grief is contagious. |
| **4** | **The Mirror** | A large, ornate mirror — elvish craftsmanship, clearly old — faces outward in the banshee's chamber. It is the only object she has maintained. It is also the only object she avoids looking at directly. |
| **5** | **The Descendants** | Cross-referencing the banshee's name with local records reveals living descendants. One of them is known to the party — a contact, an NPC, or even (in extreme cases) a PC with the right backstory. |
| **6** | **The Sound Map** | NPCs in the region have been describing the "crying sound" for years — and their accounts pinpoint different locations. The banshee moves. She patrols this dungeon. The sound pattern maps her route. |
| **7** | **The Last Poem** | In the banshee's former quarters, scratched into the wall in Elvish: a poem, unfinished. The last line cuts off mid-word. The poem is about loss, beauty, and being seen. Someone who reads it and speaks Elvish must make a DC 11 Wisdom save or feel deeply sad for 1 hour. |
| **8** | **The Betrayer's Name** | A contract, a letter, a court record — something naming the person or faction that wronged the elf who became this banshee. The party now has a name. Whether that name connects to the wider campaign is the DM's call. |
| **9** | **The Fading Object** | The banshee has been here so long that objects tied to her memory are disintegrating under her influence. A journal, a painting, a keepsake — each one crumbling. When the last one is gone, something will change about her. She will become more dangerous, or less coherent, or finally slip into complete dissolution. The party found this just in time. |
| **10** | **The Request Fulfilled** | If the party performs whatever act would allow the banshee to rest (see Resolution Path above), they find her chamber empty afterward. On the floor, where she last stood, is a single elven-made object of value — a gemstone, a ring, a scroll — left as thanks. It's the only thing she could give. |
