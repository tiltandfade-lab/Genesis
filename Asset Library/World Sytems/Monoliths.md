# Monolith
> Asset Type: Ancient Arcane Node  
> Alignment: Neutral  
> Origin: Pre-the Arcane Cabal Civilization  
> Function: Magical Amplifier

---

## Core Function

A Monolith is a dormant arcane amplification node.
It does nothing on its own.
It increases the potency of magic cast nearby.
Can later be used to create portals for fast travel around the world if players build alliance with [[Cult of the Broken Veil]] or [[the Arcane Cabal]]

---

## Amplification Field

Radius: 30 ft
While within the radius of an active monolith:
When a creature casts a spell of 1st level or higher, they may choose one:
- +1 bonus to spell attack rolls for that spell
OR
- +1 bonus to the spell save DC for that spell
This benefit does not stack with other monoliths.

---

## Activation

Most monoliths are dormant.
They require ritual attunement (Arcana check DC 15, 10 minutes) to awaken temporarily.
On failure by 5 or more, arcane feedback may trigger an Obelisk Glitch effect (see Arcane Memory asset).

---

## Physical Description

Constructed of unknown ancient stone, often veined with crystallized arcane channels.
Surface carvings are geometric rather than linguistic.
They predate the Arcane Cabal.

---

## Design Boundary

Monoliths amplify magic.
They do not:

- Restore spell slots
- Break concentration rules
- Stack infinitely
- Override class mechanics
