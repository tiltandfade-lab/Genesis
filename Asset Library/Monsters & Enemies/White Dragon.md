*SRD 5.2.1*

White dragons are savage, heartless beasts driven entirely by hunger and greed. What they lack in intelligence, they make up for in sheer ferocity. They blow in with cold weather, looking for food and easy treasure, making them highly unpredictable threats.

**Campaign Integration Hooks:**

- **The Flash Freeze:** An arcane anomaly has caused a sudden, unnatural drop in temperature in a specific urban warehouse or alleyway. A White Wyrmling, drawn by the magic, has taken up residence, freezing the local merchants' goods and killing anyone who comes to investigate.
- **The Hunted Hunters:** The PCs find a pair of frozen, shattered bodies belonging to a rival adventuring party or mercenary squad. The wyrmling is treating the local roads as its personal hunting ground, and the PCs are next.

#### d6 The Wyrmling's Stage (Tactical Terrain)

White dragons favor labyrinthine ice caves, deep crevasses, and high mountain vales accessible only by flying. They love vertical heights, often clinging to the ceiling to drop onto prey. _(Roll or choose one to establish the tactical frame)._

|d6|Arctic Terrain & Hazard|2024 Mechanical Integration & Tactics|
|---|---|---|
|**1**|**The Macabre Meat Locker**|The walls of the cave or ravine are lined with the frozen corpses of humanoids, giants, and beasts. These horrific trophies provide Half Cover to characters, but any missed attack that strikes a frozen corpse causes it to shatter, creating a 5-foot patch of jagged, Difficult Terrain.|
|**2**|**Concealing Snowdrifts**|The ground is covered in deep, powdery snow that counts as Difficult Terrain for the PCs. The wyrmling uses its 15 ft. burrow speed to tunnel _under_ the snow, gaining Total Cover until it bursts up beneath a character to attack.|
|**3**|**Slippery Ice Shelves**|The battlefield consists of tiered, slick ice shelves. Characters moving more than half their speed or taking the Dash action must succeed on a DC 12 Acrobatics check or fall Prone. The dragon uses its _Ice Walk_ to ignore this and easily scales the walls to drop its breath weapon from above.|
|**4**|**Thin Ice over Freezing Water**|The combat takes place over a frozen lake or subterranean pool. If a creature takes fire damage or massive bludgeoning damage, the ice breaks. Characters falling in must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw each round or take 1d4 Cold damage. The wyrmling has a swim speed and uses the water to reposition.|
|**5**|**The Howling Whiteout**|A sudden, brutal squall blows through the mountain pass. Wind and driving snow make the area Heavily Obscured beyond 15 feet. The dragon uses its 10 ft. Blindsight to perfectly track targets in the immediate blinding snow.|
|**6**|**Icicle Stalactites**|The cavern ceiling is covered in massive, razor-sharp icicles. When the wyrmling uses its Cold Breath or roars, the vibrations cause 1d4 icicles to drop. Characters in random squares must succeed on a DC 11 Dexterity saving throw or take 2d6 Piercing damage.|

#### d6 Frozen Clues & Windfalls (The Investigation Layer)

White dragons love the cold sparkle of ice and favor diamonds, but they also collect the survival gear and magical items of overconfident adventurers who tried to hunt them. Because they freeze their hoards, retrieving the loot takes time.

|d6|Macabre Discovery|Clue / Campaign Hook|
|---|---|---|
|**1**|**The Rime-Encrusted Chest**|A sturdy lockbox encased in a thick layer of transparent ice. It takes 10 minutes of chipping or magical fire to free it. Inside are 2 _Potions of Healing_ and a perfectly preserved map to a lost dwarven silver mine further north.|
|**2**|**The Giant's Warning**|The cracked, frozen bones of a Frost Giant. Clutched in its skeletal hand is a massive, crude iron rune. If returned to a local giant tribe, it acts as a token of safe passage, proving the PCs avenged their fallen kin.|
|**3**|**The Frozen Courier**|A royal messenger, completely flash-frozen mid-stride. Buried in their heavy furs is a sealed, waterproof dispatch tube containing explosive political blackmail about a noble in your city.|
|**4**|**The Ivory Cache**|A pile of heavily carved walrus and mammoth tusk ivory. It is highly valuable (worth 200 gp), but it is incredibly heavy and awkward to transport back to civilization without a sled or pack animal.|
|**5**|**The Diamond Lure**|Scattered across the icy floor are several rough diamonds that glitter like stars. However, the bloodstains beneath the ice suggest the wyrmling deliberately scattered them to lure greedy travelers into the center of the room.|
|**6**|**The Surviving Captive**|A severely frostbitten scout or trapper is buried in a snowbank, kept alive only because the dragon wanted them to freeze naturally before eating them. If the PCs warm them up and use a Healer's Kit, the scout becomes a loyal, knowledgeable guide for the northern wilderness.|

#### d4 Scavengers & Nuisances (Secondary Pressure)

Because the white dragon is such an effective killer, its territory naturally attracts creatures hoping to scavenge its leftovers, or beings foolish enough to try and worship it.

|d4|Symbiotic Threat|Description & Synergy|
|---|---|---|
|**1**|**Kobold Worshippers**|A band of 1d4+1 winged kobolds worships the wyrmling. They don't fight the PCs directly, but wait on the high ledges to drop rocks or nets on the party, hoping to cripple them so the "Great White Wyrm" can eat them.|
|**2**|**Winter Wolves**|A pair of starving winter wolves or dire wolves are waiting on the edge of the battlefield. They are too scared to attack the dragon, but the moment a PC drops to 0 hit points, the wolves rush in to drag the unconscious character away into the snow.|
|**3**|**Ice Toads**|Giant amphibious toads that secrete a hallucinogenic toxin. They hide in the snowdrifts. If a PC steps on one, it releases a cloud of gas that applies the _Poisoned_ condition, making them easy prey for the dragon.|
|**4**|**The Ticking Clock (The Mother)**|White wyrmlings are often abandoned, but sometimes a mother lingers. The wyrmling lets out a shrill, echoing cry when bloodied. The PCs hear a massive, terrifying roar in the distance. They have exactly 10 rounds to finish the fight, loot the area, and hide before an Adult White Dragon arrives.|
