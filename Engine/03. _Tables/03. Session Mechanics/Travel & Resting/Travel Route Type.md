|**d12**|**Route Type**|**Movement & Footing Character**|
|---|---|---|
|1|Maintained Road|Level, packed surface; clear of debris and easy to follow.|
|2|Abandoned Road|Cracked stone or overgrown dirt; nature is reclaiming the path.|
|3|Narrow Trail|Single-file path; requires constant attention to stay on the track.|
|4|Animal Path|Winding and low; requires ducking under limbs or pushing through brush.|
|5|Trackless Terrain|No discernable path; requires active navigation and constant effort.|
|6|River Route|Following a bank or bed; footing is often wet, sandy, or slippery.|
|7|Elevated Path|Ridge lines or cliff edges; precarious footing with high exposure.|
|8|Low Ground|Valleys or basins; soft, yielding earth that clings to boots.|
|9|Dense Cover|Movement through thickets or ruins; line of sight is extremely limited.|
|10|Open Expanse|Flat and featureless; easy movement but nowhere to hide.|
|11|Ruined Infrastructure|Broken bridges, aqueducts, or walls; requires climbing and jumping.|
|12|Unstable/Shifting Path|Scree, dunes, or melting ice; the ground moves under your weight.|
