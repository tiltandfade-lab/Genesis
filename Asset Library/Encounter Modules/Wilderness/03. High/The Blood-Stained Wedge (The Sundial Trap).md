## 30-Second Run

A wedge-shaped box canyon reeks of old blood and rusted iron. A stone sundial on a raised ledge at the narrow end glows with a magical light source that should be impossible in stormy weather. The moment the party enters, the light snaps on, and the sundial's shadow elongates toward whoever is closest. On initiative count 20, every round: fire erupts in a 10-ft radius around the nearest creature, 3d8 fire. The center path is thick reeds. The perimeter is slick ice and algae. The hammock above the sundial is the fastest solution.

---

## Setup

**Space:** 50×50 ft triangular box canyon. 20-ft sheer cliff walls on all sides. 20-ft wide entrance. Narrow 10-ft ledge at the far point, 10 ft off the ground, holds the sundial.
**Atmosphere:** Dim, stormy weather. Intermittent squalls. Smell of rusted iron and old blood. The magical pale light on the sundial is the only constant source in the space.
**The Terrain:** Center 20×20 area — Tall Reeds (Difficult Terrain, Half Cover for Medium, Total Cover for Small). Outer perimeter — slick algae and ice (DC 10 Acrobatics or Prone).
**If Ignored:** Magical squalls continue to deny rest. The hidden war chest remains lost.

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Gauntlet

Initiative is called the moment the party enters. The sundial acts on count 20 each round.

**The Hunting Shadow:** The sundial's shadow rotates to point at the creature physically closest to it. On initiative count 20: fire erupts centered on that creature. Every creature within 10 ft makes a **DC 14 Dex save** — fail: 3d8 fire damage. Success: half.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 10 Acrobatics:** Required when first entering the slick perimeter on a turn. Fail: Prone.
- **Squall Penalty:** Ranged weapon attacks within the canyon suffer −2 to hit. No standing-back shooting solution.

**Escalation:** The shadow prioritizes whoever runs ahead. Spread the party horizontally through the reeds to keep the shadow from finding a consistent target.

---

### Segment 2: Disabling the Sundial

Three ways to end the encounter. A character must be adjacent to the sundial (climb the 10-ft ledge — DC 12 Athletics).

**Option 1 — Arcane Solution:** Two cumulative DC 14 Arcana or Sleight of Hand checks (Actions). Each failure by 5+: 1d8 fire to the hacker.

**Option 2 — Brute Force:** Attack the sundial directly. AC 17 | HP 40 | Damage Threshold 5 (attacks below 5 damage do nothing). Immune to poison and psychic.

**Option 3 — Environmental:** A rotting canvas hammock hangs from vines directly above the sundial. Attack the vines (AC 11 | HP 5). Dropping the canvas blocks the magic light source — instantly neutralizes the trap. No check required.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Hunting Shadow:** Initiative count 20. Targets nearest creature. 10-ft radius, DC 14 Dex save, 3d8 fire / half.
2. **Tall Reeds (Center):** Difficult Terrain. Half Cover (+2 AC, +2 Dex saves) for Medium. Total Cover for Small.
3. **Slick Ice/Algae (Perimeter):** DC 10 Acrobatics on entry. Fail: Prone. Standing costs half movement.
4. **Ledge Climb:** DC 12 Athletics to reach the sundial ledge (10 ft vertical).
5. **Stash Cavity:** Defeating or breaking the sundial opens a cavity in its base.

---

## Difficulty Dial

**Tier 1 (Lvl 1–4):** As written.
**Tier 2 (Lvl 5–8):** Sundial fires twice per round (once on count 20, once on count 10). Reeds become Heavily Obscured as well as Difficult Terrain.
**Tier 3 (Lvl 9+):** Add a second smaller sundial on the opposite ledge. Both must be disabled simultaneously (requires splitting the party).
