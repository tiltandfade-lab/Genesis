## Specificity law

Specific assets are not rejects merely because they are specific. Specificity controls:

1. how many related assets production should generate;
2. how broadly the procedural selector may place them;
3. how much world-truth evidence is required before selection;
4. whether the asset is a default primitive, family variant, or bounded accent.

| Tier | Generation budget | Procedural eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Universal primitive | High | Any compatible parent/culture after material and geometry checks |
| Family variant | Moderate | Related programs, cultures, climates, or condition histories |
| Specific accent | Low | Explicit world truth, authored site history, or rare variation socket |

All three tiers remain in the library.

Specificity is therefore a production throttle, not an art-quality verdict. A strong specific form
may be retained indefinitely and used for a landmark, authored history beat, culture socket, or rare
procedural result. It simply must not consume the same generation volume or default-selection
probability as a universal connector.

## Placement-ready runtime candidates

The source sheets are not themselves runtime atlases. The packaging pass compiles only a small,
semantically named subset into `v015/runtime/`; every output has a placement socket and remains
`RUNTIME_CANDIDATE_NOT_APPROVED`.

| Primitive | Socket | Routing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low contact-grime apron | Wall/ground contact, repeat X | Universal; taller generated bands remain bounded neglect variants |
| Paired ivy faces A/B | Shared-left/shared-right two-face corner, ground rooted | Universal connector pair |
| Horizontal ivy runner | Horizontal seam, repeat X | Family variant |
| Wet-edge runner | Causal wet edge, repeat X | Universal mask |
| Radial grass tuft | Ground point, crossed card or extrusion | Universal form |
| Long blank banner | Wall or pole hang; world truth supplies color/mark | Universal attachment |
| W10 relief band | Ten-foot wall-course band | Universal architectural join |

This compile does not delete the stronger source silhouettes. It keeps them available for bounded
authored or truth-gated use while preventing them from becoming ubiquitous procedural wallpaper.

## Cleanup contract

- The original ImageGen files remain under `v015/source/`.
- Chroma-keyed intermediate albedo-alpha sheets remain reversible.
- `*-neutral-mask-hard-alpha.png` candidates are grayscale, palette-quantized, binary-alpha, and
  contain zero visible magenta-family pixels.
- No generated mask includes a parent wall, mortar course, ground plane, or baked receiver shadow.
- Runtime recolors coverage masks; world truth and culture determine final meaning.
- Nearest sampling and no mipmaps remain mandatory for authored albedo/mask channels.
