# Realm surfaces — DRAFT for approval (floors per breach realm)

> Follows the monster model: each realm surface reskins one of the 12 base floor materials (or flags **net-new** — a new procedural texture to build). Drafted 2026-07-04.

## Net-new base materials to build (8) — the future procedural-texture queue

| Realm | Surface | New base texture |
|---|---|---|
| `chrome` | Grated Decking | perforated metal walkway texture — repeating diamond or square punch-pattern with visible negative space/see-through gaps, subtle rust-bleed at seams |
| `noir` | Fire-Escape Grating | riveted steel grate lattice, rust-streaked, rendered as a repeating diamond-punch metal pattern with visible see-through gaps |
| `noir` | Wet Asphalt Crossing | fine-grain rolled asphalt with painted crosswalk striping worn to ghost-white, distinct from cobble's jointed block pattern |
| `ash` | Grating Walk | perforated steel floor grating — a repeating diamond/square lattice of raised metal ribs over dark negative space, catches light along the rib edges |
| `cosmic` | Star-Flecked Void-Floor | a near-black translucent field patterned with tiny bright pinprick specks at irregular density (a starfield scatter, not a grid) plus faint slow nebulous cloud mottle beneath — reads as standing on open night sky |
| `theater` | Grated Decking | riveted steel grating/decking plates — dark gunmetal grid pattern with rust bleed at the seams and bolt-head studs, a heavy industrial-military floor panel texture |
| `high-seas` | Wet Rope Matting | a woven-cordage deck-matting texture (crosshatched rope/net pattern, salt-stiffened fiber) for gangways and cargo netting stretched over deck gaps |
| `bright-kingdom` | Grated Gumdrop Decking | hex-panel decking — raised interlocking hexagonal tiles, seams that rattle underfoot, a base pattern none of the 12 currently cover |
