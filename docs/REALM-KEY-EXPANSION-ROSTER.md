# Realm-key expansion roster — NPC/monster additions per re-keyed realm

Draft additions to match the 2026-07-08/09 realm re-keys (chrome, gloom, suburb, lost-world)
plus the new ash key direction. **Concepts + role slots only** — no CR/tier/stat work yet.
Everything here is serial-numbers-filed-off: the vibe, never the trademark.

Legend: `(R)` = recruitable/ally · `(S)` = social threat, parley-first · `(B)` = boss-tier
· `(M)` = Mythic-band · `(seam)` = cross-realm bleed entry.

## GLOOM — the town that made a deal

### The Collector (the payment-taker — always "an IT")
The payment-taker is **always the same *kind* of thing**: a shapeshifting fear-eater on a feeding
schedule whose surface form rolls off the town-secret d20. (Legal read: the folkloric shape-shifting
fear-eater is unowned; the specific clown expression is what's protected — our form is table-rolled.)
Internal name **the Collector** to avoid "IT" prose collisions.

### The demand ladder (mechanic seed — engine-ownable)
As the feeding clock advances toward payment-due, the town's *ordinary things turn*, band by band:
1. **Rabid dogs** — animals first; the town explains it away.
2. **Possessed dolls** — killers' souls in vintage toys (Chucky/Puppet Master lane).
3. **The jealous vintage car** — restored beauty that hates whoever its owner loves (Christine lane).
4. **Autonomous diesels** — trucks with a mind, no drivers needed (Maximum Overdrive lane).
5. **The returned-not-whole** — pets and relatives back from the dead, wrong in small ways first.

Each rung = 2–4 creature entries; the clock band selects which rungs are active. Ties directly to
GLOOM-KEY's feeding clocks — spec hook, not built here.

### Evil children (children carve-out honored — depiction rule, not content ban)
Kids as villains are in-bounds; **graphic depiction of/by children is the sole carve-out**, so every
entry carries an authored non-graphic defeat-out.
- **The corn congregation** — rural-edge kid cult with a child prophet; defeat-out: the thing they
  serve takes them back / the adults finally *see*.
- **The bad seed** `(S)` — one perfect child, singular, patient; a social-tier threat.
- **The good son's shadow** `(S)` — paired-kid entry: one believed, one not.

### Slashers + the dream system (system hooks)
- **The dream-stalker** `(M)` — Nightmare-on-Elm-Street lane; **activation home for the dream system**
  (hurt in dreams = hurt for real; sleep becomes a resource). Spec hook — needs its own unit.
- **The lake revenant** — drowned-camp slasher, returns on a date.
- **The masked shape** — the empty-house slasher; walks, never runs, keeps regular hours (fits gloom's
  register verbatim).

### Friendly monsters (Monster Squad lane) `(R)`
- **The gentle flat-head** — Frankenstein's-monster-shaped protector who likes the kids.
- **The reformed wolf-man** — knows the schedule, fights the Collector's pull monthly.
- **The gill-man in the creek** — shy, territorial, tradeable-with.
- **Harry the hairy neighbor** `(seam)` — Bigfoot in the garage (Amblin lane; also plays in suburb).

## ASH — new key direction (Fallout-shaped: mutation + relic-worship)

Adam's sprite session already generated supermutant/ghoul analogs — **cast them into the roster:**
- **The overgrown** — supermutant analog ladder: brute (mook) → warlord `(B)`; made, not born —
  somebody's still making them (realm mystery hook).
- **The withered** — ghoul analog, two lanes: *lucid withered* `(S)(R)` — pre-fall memories intact,
  the realm's best lore NPCs; *feral withered* — the mook horde.
- **The glow-called** — feral withered drawn to radiation sources; environmental-hazard creatures.

### The ember-stone faction (from the sprite generator's glowing-ember accident — Adam wants it)
- **The Cindermarked** — a faction of NPCs who found a **magic ember-stone**; each carries a shard
  that glows through the skin (the sprite artifact becomes the identity). Draft concept for the
  stone's power (Adam to pick/re-taste): **it burns fuel that isn't there** — a shard warms a body,
  powers a dead machine, or cooks a meal without consuming anything… and the faction is quietly
  *aging into ash* around their marks (payment on the other side). Roster: shard-bearer (mook) ·
  ember-speaker `(S)` · the First Kindled `(B)` · the stone itself as a plot item/doer.
- Fits ash's unclaimed lane: **scavenger civilization + cargo-cult relic worship** — chrome took
  urban decay, theater took war; ash is "the world after the world," and the ember-stone is its
  first native miracle.
