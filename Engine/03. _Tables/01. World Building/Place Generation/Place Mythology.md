#place-mythology
> **The town's living myth** — the legend, superstition, or folktale that shapes how it sees itself. Rolled when a PC heads toward a new town. Spice-graded d100, Commitment ceiling. Table-improvement pass 2026-06-22: re-graded from the old 3-tier to the full 5-band ladder — Adam's named myths preserved, a **Textured** tier added, weird tiers regraded by honest weirdness, a **Mythic** capstone added. Originals in `zz_Archive/`. *(Pair with `[[Truth vs False]]` to set whether the myth is actually true.)*
