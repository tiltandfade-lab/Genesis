*p. 43 MM 2024*

In a gritty, investigation-forward campaign, blights are not just random wilderness encounters; they are an active, spreading corruption. They are awakened plants that drink darkness from the soil, rapidly overrunning trails, ruining buildings, and turning healthy forests into toxic, bramble-choked wastelands.

Here are the official 5e stat blocks and campaign-agnostic integration hooks for the three core blights, followed by the source lore for the Gulthias Tree to help you anchor your investigation.

---

### 1. The Twig Blight

Twig blights are incredibly dry, woody shrubs that pull their roots free to form humanoid-looking limbs. Because they rely on ambush, they specifically seek out campsites, watering holes, or piles of firewood to blend in.

**Campaign Integration Hook:**

- **The Campfire Mimic:** The party finds an abandoned campsite where the previous occupants were seemingly dragged away. When the PCs attempt to use the stacked firewood, the "wood" suddenly animates. Because they are exceptionally dry, using fire against them is highly effective, but risks burning the camp.

#### Twig Blight

_Small Plant, Neutral Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 14 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 7 (2d6)
- **Speed:** 20 ft.
- **STR:** 6 (-2) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 11 (+0) | **INT:** 4 (-3) | **WIS:** 8 (-1) | **CHA:** 3 (-4)
- **Skills:** Stealth +4
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire
- **Condition Immunities:** Deafened
- **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 9
- **Languages:** Understands Common but can't speak
- **Challenge:** 1/8 (25 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **False Appearance:** While the blight remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a dead shrub.
- **Pack Tactics:** The blight has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the blight's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.

**Actions**

- **Claw:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing damage.

### 2. The Needle Blight

In the shadows, needle blights look like shuffling, hunched humanoids covered in quivering clumps of conifer-like needles. They operate as the scouts and infantry of a corrupted forest.

**Campaign Integration Hook:**

- **The Pollen Alarm:** Needle blights do not fight alone. When they detect a threat, they release a wind-carried pollen that alerts all other needle blights in the forest. If the PCs fail a Stealth check and trigger a Needle Blight, you can start a literal environmental timer as waves of blights converge from all sides to drench their roots in blood.

#### Needle Blight

_Medium Plant, Neutral Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 12 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 16 (3d8 + 3)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 12 (+1) | **DEX:** 12 (+1) | **CON:** 13 (+1) | **INT:** 4 (-3) | **WIS:** 8 (-1) | **CHA:** 3 (-4)
- **Condition Immunities:** Deafened
- **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 9
- **Languages:** Understands Common but can't speak
- **Challenge:** 1/4 (50 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Actions**

- **Claw:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 6 (2d4 + 1) slashing damage.
- **Needles:** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +3 to hit, range 30/60 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 6 (2d4 + 1) piercing damage.

### 3. The Vine Blight

Vine blights appear as masses of slithering creepers that animate the undergrowth around them to entangle prey. Crucially, they are the _only_ blights capable of speech.

**Campaign Integration Hook:**

- **The Echoing Interrogator:** Because vine blights are connected to the evil spirit of their progenitor tree, they speak in a fractured version of their dead master's voice. A Vine Blight can be used to taunt the PCs from the shadows, dropping cryptic lore hints or terrifying the party by speaking in the voice of an NPC who recently went missing in the woods.

#### Vine Blight

_Medium Plant, Neutral Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 12 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 19 (3d8 + 6)
- **Speed:** 20 ft.
- **STR:** 15 (+2) | **DEX:** 8 (-1) | **CON:** 14 (+2) | **INT:** 5 (-3) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 3 (-4)
- **Skills:** Stealth +1
- **Condition Immunities:** Deafened
- **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common
- **Challenge:** 1/2 (100 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **False Appearance:** While the blight remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a tangle of vines.

**Actions**

- **Constricting Vine:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 6 (1d8 + 2) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 12). Until the grapple ends, the target takes 4 (1d8) bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns, and the blight can't make Constricting Vine attacks.
- **Entangling Plants (Recharge 5-6):** The blight casts the *Entangle* spell, using Constitution as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12).

### Tree Blight

_Huge Plant, Neutral Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 15
- **Initiative:** +3 (13)
- **Hit Points:** 115 (10d12 + 50)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 23 (+6) | **DEX:** 10 (+0) | **CON:** 20 (+5) | **INT:** 6 (-2) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 3 (-4)
- **Immunities:** Deafened
- **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Understands Common and Druidic but can't speak
- **Challenge:** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)

**Traits**

- **Siege Monster.** The blight deals double damage to objects and structures.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack.** The blight makes two Branch attacks and uses Grasping Root.
- **Branch.** Melee Attack Roll: +9, reach 15 ft. Hit: 16 (3d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage.
- **Grasping Root.** Strength Saving Throw: DC 17, one Large or smaller creature within 15 feet. Failure: The target is pulled up to 10 feet straight toward the blight and has the Grappled condition (escape DC 16) from one of six roots. Until the grapple ends, the target takes 13 (2d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns.

**Bonus Actions**

- **Gnash.** Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 17, one creature Grappled by the blight. Failure: 19 (3d8 + 6) Piercing damage. Success: Half damage.

### Gulthias Blight

_Gargantuan Plant, Neutral Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 20
- **Initiative:** +5 (15)
- **Hit Points:** 264 (16d20 + 96)
- **Speed:** 50 ft.
- **STR:** 25 (+7) | **DEX:** 10 (+0) | **CON:** 22 (+6) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 18 (+4) | **CHA:** 12 (+1)
- **Skills:** Perception +9
- **Resistances:** Fire, Necrotic
- **Immunities:** Deafened
- **Senses:** Blindsight 120 ft., passive Perception 19
- **Languages:** Common, Druidic
- **Challenge:** 16 (XP 15,000; PB +5)

**Traits**

- **Blight Seeds.** When it finishes a Long Rest, the blight expels 1d6 seeds into unoccupied spaces on the ground within 60 feet of itself. After 24 hours, the seeds become creatures under the blight's control. Roll 1d8 for each seed to determine the creature it becomes: on 1–4, **Twig Blight**; on 5–6, **Needle Blight**; on 7–8, **Vine Blight**.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack.** The blight makes two Slam or Thorn Volley attacks in any combination. It also uses Life-Draining Root.
- **Slam.** Melee Attack Roll: +12, reach 10 ft. Hit: 25 (4d8 + 7) Bludgeoning damage.
- **Thorn Volley.** Ranged Attack Roll: +12, range 60/180 ft. Hit: 20 (3d8 + 7) Piercing damage.
- **Life-Draining Root.** Constitution Saving Throw: DC 20, one Huge or smaller creature the blight can see within 30 feet. Failure: 14 (2d6 + 7) Necrotic damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 19) from one of six roots. Until the grapple ends, the target takes 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage at the start of each of its turns. The target's Hit Point maximum decreases by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken, and the blight regains Hit Points equal to that amount.

### d6 Signs of the Gulthias Corruption (Environmental Clues)

Blights are an actively spreading plague. When a Gulthias tree takes root, its evil spreads through the soil, poisoning healthy plants and rapidly accelerating the growth of toxic flora. _(Roll or choose one to establish the creeping corruption of the wilderness)._

|d6|Blighted Terrain|Investigation & Tactical Detail|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Smothered Ruin**|Vines and undergrowth have spread through a ruined building or logging camp with supernatural speed, completely engulfing it. The thick vegetation blocks line of sight and turns the entire structure into Difficult Terrain.|
|**2**|**Toxic Brambles**|A patch of previously healthy forest has been entirely killed off and replaced by a dense thicket of toxic weeds and brambles. Moving through it risks piercing damage and poison exposure.|
|**3**|**The Blood-Soaked Soil**|The ground here is unnaturally dark and spongy, literally contaminated by the darkness and blood the blights drink from the earth. Tracking checks made here might reveal humanoid footprints ending abruptly where roots dragged a body under.|
|**4**|**The Vanished Village**|The party finds a frontier settlement that has completely disappeared under an aggressive wave of blighted plant life in the space of only a few days.|
|**5**|**The Slaked Roots**|A small copse of seemingly ordinary trees has soil that is completely drenched in warm blood. These are Tree Blights that have recently fed to slake their roots' thirst.|
|**6**|**The Destroyed Arsenal**|Cultists or terrified locals have left an offering for the blights in the center of the path: a pile of completely destroyed axes, hatchets, and fire-making implements.|

### d6 The Vine Blight's Voice (Psychological Warfare)

Vine blights are the only blights capable of speech. Because they are connected to the evil spirit of the Gulthias tree that spawned them, they speak in a fractured, terrifying version of their dead master's voice. _(Roll or choose one to taunt the PCs from the undergrowth)._

|d6|The Voice in the Vines|Narrative Implication|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Ancient Vampire**|The blight speaks with the aristocratic, blood-hungry sneer of the vampire Gulthias himself, demanding the PCs lay down their arms and offer their necks.|
|**2**|**The Mad Druid**|The blight rambles in the crazed voice of the mad druid who originally discovered the Gulthias sapling and planted it in the dark grotto. It hints at the location of the master tree.|
|**3**|**The Dead Explorer**|The vines mimic the final, panicked shouts of a local elf woman who was recently beaten to death by thrashing, rot-scaled blights.|
|**4**|**The Petty Tyrant**|The blight speaks in the voice of an arrogant, dead warlord, attempting to strike a hollow bargain with the party for safe passage through its territory.|
|**5**|**The Spiteful Larder**|The blight angrily complains that it was passed over and not chosen to be the local settlement's "larder tree", attacking out of pure, childish spite.|
|**6**|**The Echoing Cultist**|The blight repeats the dark prayers of an occult faction that worships the forest's corruption, inadvertently revealing the cult's password or hideout.|
