# Extract — meaning-bearing dressing

**Nothing here is locked. Adam rules; this file evidences.**

**What this sheet is.** The props, decals and marks recovered by this study that **carry social
meaning** — i.e. an in-world observer reads them and learns something. Each row states *what it
says*, because that is the whole point: a prop that carries meaning is a sentence, and a prop that
does not is texture. Sorted into the three bins the sibling brief uses (**materials · decals/paint ·
props**), with a fourth bin for **temporary dressing** (festival/emergency states), which the
evidence insists on and the three-bin scheme has no home for.

**Rule of thumb this sheet argues for:** *the strongest urban dressing is a MARK APPLIED BY AN
AUTHORITY TO SOMEBODY ELSE'S PROPERTY.* Six of the highest-value items below are exactly that — a
slit ear, a red cross, a chalked door, a nailed doorplate, a painted badge, a posted name.

## Notes for the dressing census

1. **Six of the strongest items are marks applied by an authority to somebody else's property** —
   the slit ear, the red cross, the chalked door, the posted name, the painted badge, the whetstone
   round the neck. If the census can support only one new decal family for site 10, it should be
   **authority-marks**, not wear-and-grime.
2. **Several items are lies.** A street sign for a trade that has moved; a street name recording a
   right nobody remembers; a red cross left on a door after the visitation ended. **A dressing item
   that no longer matches its street is the cheapest mystery in the study.**
3. **Four items are storage locations rather than objects** — the props barn, the church steeple's
   arms, the granary in the market hall, the stone tower in the timber house. Each implies a
   custodian and a key.
4. **The temporary bin is not optional.** Two of the study's most vivid dressing recipes (Midsummer
   doors, procession arches) exist only in a state, and the site's condition/occupancy axes are
   already the right place to hang them.
5. **Nothing in bin 4 requires new geometry** — boughs, lamps, cloths, booths, marks, wands. This
   is the cheapest large visual delta available to site 10.
