# NPC Trust Lever
> **What could win this NPC's trust** — the thing that opens them up, lowers the guard, turns a closed angle into a candid one. The player's *way in*. The DM holds it behind the screen; the player discovers it by paying attention (`[[NPC Demeanor]]`, `[[NPC Want]]`, `[[NPC Fear]]` all hint at it). Counterpart to `[[NPC Honesty]]`: pulling the lever buys honesty even from the guarded. Charter: `docs/DM-CHARTER.md` §7.
>
> **Flagged for the table-improvement pass** (v1 set; expand + spice-grade against playtest).

|d20|This NPC opens up when…|
|---|---|
|1|you name a **shared enemy** — someone you both have reason to hate|
|2|you offer a **secret of your own** in kind — vulnerability for vulnerability|
|3|you **repay or forgive a debt** they thought would hang over them|
|4|you show real **respect for their craft** — and prove you understand it|
|5|you **protect someone they love** without being asked|
|6|you're **vouched for** by someone they already trust|
|7|you demonstrate plain **competence** at the thing that matters to them|
|8|you share their **faith, homeland, or origin** — one of their own|
|9|you **keep your word** on something small, where you could have cheated|
|10|you prove you can be **discreet** — you knew something and didn't tell|
|11|you **stand up to the person or power** that has its boot on them|
|12|you sit, **share a drink, and give them time** — patience, not pressure|
|13|you do them a **favor with no price named** — and mean it|
|14|you **make them laugh** — genuinely, at the right moment|
|15|you **catch them in a lie kindly** — see through them without humiliating them|
|16|you offer **coin, goods, or aid** they badly need and can't ask for|
|17|you **remember** something they told you before — you were *listening*|
|18|you **take a real risk** in front of them, for something they care about|
|19|you **deliver on a threat or promise** they didn't think you'd honor|
|20|you give them a way to **undo an old shame** — a second chance at the thing they got wrong|
^npc-trust-lever

> **Using it.** The lever is *specific to this NPC*; it won't be obvious on the surface, but their Demeanor, Want, and Fear point at it. Pulling it shifts how they read the player and unlocks candor (see `[[NPC Honesty]]` gate 3). A *failed* attempt — pulling the wrong lever, or faking the right one — can **harden** them instead. Trust earned is a continuity fact: write it to the ledger, and the NPC remembers it next time (`docs/DM-CHARTER.md` §7, continuity).
