|  d6 | Payoff Size  | What it gives |
| --: | :----------: | ------------- |
| 1 | **Micro** | Flavor + tiny benefit: clue fragment, minor utility item, 1d6 coin, scrap of map, symbol match, a name, a token. |
| 2 | **Micro** | **Situational edge:** advantage on next related check, bypass a minor hazard, identify a faction sign, gain a safe hiding spot. |
| 3 | **Standard** | **Useful cache:** 1–2 consumables (healing, antitoxin, oil, food, lamp fuel), tool kit piece, ammo, minor valuables. |
| 4 | **Standard** | **Information + leverage:** clear clue, partial map, password, schedule, weakness, permit, ledger note, or “how this place works.” |
| 5 | **Major** | **Route + reward:** meaningful shortcut/escape + solid treasure/tool (or a strong clue that changes choices). |
| 6 | **Major+** | **Signature payoff:** named item/relic hook, big truth, faction pivot, site-origin reveal, or major reward with lasting impact. |
^urban-secret-payoff-size
