**Valen Thorne (the Arcane Cabal Field Leader)** 
_Analog: Lance Reddick (Charon from John Wick) — Projects an aura of unshakable, calm competence. He speaks with absolute, articulate certainty and never raises his voice, even when discussing apocalyptic magical threats._

Notes for Play

- **Mannerism & Quirk (Quick Play):** He is the anchor in the storm. He speaks in a grounded, perfectly measured tone, treating horrifying magical anomalies as tactical puzzles to be solved. When he is thinking or issuing orders, he habitually twists a simple silver focus ring on his finger.
- **Ability & Role (Quick Play):** As the Arcane Cabal's Operational Lead, his presence immediately signals to the players that the Order is competent and powerful. His **High Intelligence** makes him a brilliant tactical planner and spellcaster, but his **Low Strength** means he relies entirely on his magic—and the player characters—to execute the physical demands of his strategies.
- **Secret & Flaw (Quick Play):** His "Formative Trauma" becomes his Flaw: having survived an uncontrolled magical disaster in his past, he is intensely controlling and terrified of a chaotic overreaction. His Secret is that he knows the local cult is terrifyingly close to achieving a "threshold event," but he is lying to the town about the severity of the threat to prevent mass panic.
- **Hook & Complication (Quick Play):** He tries to recruit the PCs because he needs capable, precise allies for a "controlled intervention" against the cult. The complication is his trauma: if the PCs fail at stealth or the cult forces his hand, his calm facade breaks, and he will double down, authorizing a devastating, scorched-earth magical strike regardless of the collateral damage.

Catchphrase

_(Twisting his silver ring, speaking in a perfectly calm, level tone)_: "Panic is a useless variable. We are going to apply a controlled intervention to this ritual, and we are going to do it exactly as I have outlined."
