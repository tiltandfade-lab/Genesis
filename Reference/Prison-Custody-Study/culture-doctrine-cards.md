## 1. Card law

A custody doctrine is a correlated answer to the same institutional questions. It is
not an ethnic label, moral alignment, palette, prop set, or named prison mesh.

Every promoted card must change at least:

- one geometry or organization fact;
- one access or interaction fact;
- one property/record or service fact; and
- one operating consequence, failure mode, or counterplay target.

The following remain invariant across every card:

- one committed topology for outside and captured starts;
- a named authority claim and current operator;
- controlled thresholds and truthful force surfaces;
- exact person/property identities and causal custody;
- finite disposition or equivalent pressure;
- service provenance;
- precommitted social/legal, routine/procedural, covert/secret, and
  force/disruption possibilities; and
- `rollerLineage` / `sourceRollRefs`.

## 2. First comparison pair

### `PR-DOC-01 — Keeper-House Civic Custody`

status: **SELECTED FOR PROOF**
best fit: Rung A/B; may survive as the old core of a promoted Rung C
evidence spine: Colonial Williamsburg Public Gaol; small lockups in Historic England;
Fluvanna County Jail measured set

**Doctrine sentence:** lawful custody is entrusted to a named keeper whose house,
livelihood, knowledge, and reputation are physically attached to the holding.

| axis | correlated answer |
|---|---|
| authority | a named sheriff, gaoler, household, or office-holder displays a commission, court seal, public notice, oath, or locally testable appointment |
| organization | keeper and small household/staff combine oversight, records, supply, and relief; few roles are redundant |
| classification | a small number of rooms/cells separates the categories the local law recognizes; the keeper knows occupants personally, but every generated child still has a stable id |
| geometry | street-facing keeper room/public desk; controlled inner threshold; compact cell room or short bank; walled yard; attached/rear household-service edge; raised keeper landing at Rung B, flat threshold at Rung A |
| circulation | public and supply movement enter the keeper side; prisoners reach cells/yard through the keeper threshold; rear household/service access is real but controlled |
| access | a concentrated key ring and keeper permission dominate; witness, kin, surety, payment, local standing, or court instruction may change access without erasing involuntary custody |
| property / records | a handwritten commitment/property book plus tagged bundle, cabinet, or staff-only closet near intake; prisoner property and crime evidence retain separate custody classes even if low volume shares one secured edge |
| services | keeper household, garden/well/cistern, external meal provider, yard privy, laundry/repair help, or named civic supplier; missing provider rejects or strains the candidate |
| routine | individual admission, meal, yard, visit, hearing/gaol-delivery, and release calls rather than an anonymous block schedule |
| force | fewer harder boundaries; the keeper threshold and cell fronts carry most direct force, while the yard/rear service edge supplies different vulnerabilities |
| legitimate counterplay | persuade or expose the keeper; prove a claim; enlist kin/witness/court; exploit a household routine; use the yard/service edge; seize or defeat concentrated keys; force a small number of honest barriers |
| failure | keeper absence, illness, corruption, divided loyalty, debt, or household crisis degrades several circuits together; this is an intended consequence, not a reason to add invisible backup staff |
| promotion | added capacity first separates intake/property/service and adds staff relief; when custody can survive a keeper change through formal handoffs, `PR-DOC-02` may layer in |
| readable signals | keeper dwelling/work overlap, personal key/tool station, public commission or notice, household service traces, walled yard, compact tagged property storage |

**Forbidden shortcut:** “rustic jail” palette on the same formal institution.

### `PR-DOC-02 — Ledger-and-Shift Civic Custody`

status: **SELECTED FOR PROOF**
best fit: Rung B/C; degrades cautiously to a record-heavy Rung A
evidence spine: Nelson Mandela Rules; current HMPPS property lifecycle; Historic
England reception/property and specialized-service evolution; larger HABS jail cohort

**Doctrine sentence:** lawful custody belongs to a recorded process that must survive
changes of staff through numbered people/places, signed handoffs, separated roles, and
scheduled movement.

| axis | correlated answer |
|---|---|
| authority | valid written commitment, named responsible authority, numbered register, and explicit release/transfer order are visible and testable |
| organization | at least two duty roles or shifts divide public intake from secure control; keys/permissions and counts have a handoff rather than living only in one keeper’s memory |
| classification | numbered cells/wards and status facts drive placement; untried/convicted, age, sex, risk, health, or other world-law categories require separate children or scheduled separation when capacity supports it |
| geometry | distinct public waiting/desk and intake-search surface; staff-only property edge; controlled inner threshold; raised landing or station observing cells and yard gate; service route that does not require opening the public front |
| circulation | admission moves public → intake/search/property → secure threshold → assigned child; staff handoff and service loops cross at controlled stations, not inside arbitrary cells |
| access | role-bound keys, seals, written permissions, schedules, countersigns, or dual acknowledgements; a single guard may operate them, but the permission survives that person |
| property / records | exact inventory at admission; attributed container, seal/tag and location; issue/transfer/release events; prisoner property, contraband, and crime evidence are separate classes with separate permissible destinations |
| services | named meal/water/waste/medical/maintenance providers operate on a schedule with countable hatches, routes, or handoffs; service failure changes routine and pressure |
| routine | admission, count, meal, yard/exercise, visit/hearing, work/medical, shift handoff, transfer, and release windows; not every fixture needs every event, but each active event has a provider and route |
| force | layered controlled thresholds spread resistance across intake, inner control, cell fronts, yard gate, and service edge; alert changes access and response without making walls magically invulnerable |
| legitimate counterplay | challenge the commitment or identity; alter/expose the record; obtain a valid release; exploit shift handoff, count discrepancy, scheduled movement, property transfer, service route, or classification conflict; force one layer while accepting response from the others |
| failure | register/person mismatch, lost or switched property, bad handoff, contradictory authority, delayed service, overcapacity, or key/permission desynchronization creates specific openings and harms rather than generic “corruption” |
| degradation | at Rung A, one officer may perform several roles, but the signed register, exact property receipt, and role-bound release remain; if those disappear, compile `PR-DOC-01` or another doctrine instead |
| promotion | Rung C separates reception/property, records, visits, services, classification blocks, and staff spine according to actual throughput; repeated wings never promote merely for spectacle |
| readable signals | numbered children, admission/search surface, separate attributed property containers, duty board or equivalent, count/handoff station, role-specific access marks |

**Forbidden shortcut:** adding paper props and cell numbers while circulation, access,
property handling, and failure behavior remain keeper-house byte-equivalent.

## 3. Comparison receipt

The first paired proof uses the same legal Rung-B obligations and same world/settlement
capacity envelope. The pair passes only if the compiled results differ coherently:

| proof dimension | `PR-DOC-01` keeper-house | `PR-DOC-02` ledger-and-shift |
|---|---|---|
| primary authority target | named keeper and appointment | commitment/release record and responsible office |
| organizational bottleneck | person/household | handoff/role/process |
| public-to-secure form | keeper room → threshold | waiting/desk → intake/search → threshold |
| property form | cabinet/closet near keeper | attributed containers in staff-only intake/property edge |
| staff movement | combined household/service | scheduled staff/service spine or controlled loop |
| social/legal plan | persuade, shame, obligate, replace, or appeal through keeper/network | challenge commitment, identity, classification, or release authority |
| routine plan | household supply, yard call, court/hearing, keeper absence | count, shift handoff, visit, service, transfer, property issue |
| covert plan | rear service/yard, household access, concentrated keys | handoff gap, scheduled crossing, property/service route, permission mismatch |
| force plan | defeat a few concentrated thresholds before local response | choose a layer, manage alert propagation and separated response |
| signature failure | keeper crisis collapses several circuits | record/handoff mismatch desynchronizes several circuits |

Palette, heraldry, writing system, clothing, and material remain supporting signals.
They cannot satisfy this comparison receipt.

## 4. Later evidence-backed candidates

These are admitted research directions, not MVP authority.

### `PR-DOC-03 — Separate Observation`

status: **EVIDENCE-BACKED CANDIDATE**
evidence: Eastern State; Historic England Pentonville/separate-system history

Custody aims to isolate communication and expose individual movement to a hub or
gallery. It promotes radial or hubbed wings, individual cells, controlled exercise,
separated schedules, and observation-heavy staff routes. Its failures are crowding,
communication through the fabric, blind hub assumptions, service burden, and the harm
of isolation. It is never the universal “high-security” upgrade.

### `PR-DOC-04 — Congregate Work-Yard`

status: **EVIDENCE-BACKED CANDIDATE**
evidence: Fremantle; Australian Convict Sites; Robben Island Museum

Custody organizes repeated movement between accommodation, count, work yard/workshop/
quarry, tools, food, and return. The custody site must own or causally connect the work
route; Site 5 owns production machinery. Tool issue/return, escort capacity, injury,
work refusal, collective organization, and shift movement become primary gameplay
facts.

### `PR-DOC-05 — Perimeter / Transport Custody`

status: **EVIDENCE-BACKED CANDIDATE**
evidence: Robben Island/nomination; Australian Convict Sites

Distance, water, wall, fortress, colony, or controlled transfer route carries much of
the containment. Harbour/gate, supply, escort, weather, work corridor, and external
authority matter as much as the cell. Escape may defeat the internal boundary yet fail
at transport or environment. This doctrine requires a real host landscape and must not
reduce colonial punishment to an adventure skin.

### `PR-DOC-06 — Debt / Liberty Custody`

status: **EVIDENCE-BACKED CANDIDATE, NOT MVP**
evidence: UK National Archives debtor-prison catalogues

The held person may live, trade, receive dependants/visitors, or move within a bounded
liberty while creditor claim and re-arrest threat control exit. Payment, account,
petition, discharge, household economy, and boundary enforcement replace much
cell-centric play. It needs broader comparative evidence before promotion.

## 5. Research-needed and fantasy-adapter cards

### `PR-DOC-07 — Hostage / Surety House`

status: **RESEARCH-NEEDED**

The earlier “surety/household” proposal was too broad and too lightly sourced to serve
as the first comparison card. Social custody remains a required plan family in any
legal composition, but a true hostage/surety doctrine needs direct evidence for:
authority, substitute liability, guest/hostage status, household boundaries, witness
and ransom procedure, property, retaliation, release, and what happens when the
sponsor defaults. Until that study lands, use a hostage **occupancy/motive overlay**
without claiming a promoted doctrine.

### `PR-DOC-08 — Quarantine / Protective Isolation`

status: **RESEARCH-NEEDED**

This card must prove that involuntary health custody—not merely locked sickroom
dressing—changes clean/dirty routes, water/waste/food/medical service, observation,
appeal/release criteria, emergency evacuation, and staff exposure. Site 4 or a medical
host may own the building; Site 6 owns the custody mutation.

### `PR-DOC-09 — Warded Exceptional Containment`

status: **LICENSED WORLD ADAPTER**

A seal, name, oath, living material, inverted gravity, suspended cage, portal, or
sentient ward may replace some keys and walls only when named world/realm/history facts
own it. The adapter must still compile:

- operator and authority;
- support/energy/life-sustainment dependency;
- access and emergency release;
- property and disposition;
- force/failure surfaces;
- staff/service route;
- social, routine, covert, and force counterplay; and
- consequences when the impossible mechanism fails.

`RC-PRISON-01`, the suspended living-ironwood solitary cell, belongs here. It is a
retained live composition, not evidence that any historical custody culture used
suspended grown crates over voids.
