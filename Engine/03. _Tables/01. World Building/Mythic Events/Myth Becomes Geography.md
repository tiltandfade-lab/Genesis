#myth-becomes-geography
> **How a myth physically marks, warps, or seeps into the land it haunts** — rolled when a legend has become attached to a *place*, to set how the ground itself carries it. Spice-graded d100, Commitment ceiling (66 Grounded · 20 Textured · 9 Strange · 4 Volatile · 1 Mythic). The Grounded band is human marks (worn paths, kept ruins, offerings); higher bands are the land itself behaving wrong. Table-improvement pass 2026-06-23: rebuilt from the thin 10-row "Mythic Landscape Effects" to a full 100-unique-row ladder; originals in `zz_Archive/`. *(Part of the Myth suite — pairs with `[[Myth Seeds]]` and `[[Myth Costs]]`; see `docs/CRIT-MAGNITUDE.md` for how the suite relates to the crit system.)*
