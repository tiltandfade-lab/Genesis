Here is the unbroken **d50 Wilderness Obstacles & Locks** table, formatted as a single continuous block for your digital dice roller or macro frame.

### Wilderness Obstacles & Locks (d50)

|d50|The Obstacle / "Lock" (Barrier)|The "Key" / Bypass Mechanic (2024 RAW)|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**01**|**Fallen Monolith / Boulder:** A massive stone slab or giant tree completely chokes a narrow ravine or cave entrance.|**Resource/Skill:** DC 20 Str (Athletics) check utilizing a lever/crank, or a spell like _Enlarge/Reduce_ or _Stone Shape_.|
|**02**|**Sheer Cliff Face:** The trail ends at an 80-foot vertical ascent of smooth rock.|**Skill:** DC 15 Str (Athletics) to climb. Requires climbing gear (pitons/rope) or spells like _Spider Climb_ or _Fly_.|
|**03**|**Caved-In Tunnel:** A necessary subterranean shortcut has collapsed, leaving tons of rubble.|**Labor/Magic:** Takes 1d4 days of manual labor to clear safely, or careful sustained use of the _Mold Earth_ cantrip.|
|**04**|**Yawning Chasm:** A 40-foot-wide, 200-foot-deep fissure bisects the landscape.|**Resource:** Requires engineering a bridge, a DC 15 Str (Athletics) jump enhanced by the _Jump_ spell, or flight.|
|**05**|**Unstable Scree Slope:** A steep incline of loose, sliding rock blocking the pass.|**Skill:** DC 15 Wis (Survival) to map a safe zigzag route. Failure means sliding down and taking 2d6 Bludgeoning damage.|
|**06**|**Petrified Forest Wall:** Ancient trees turned to solid stone, packed so tightly a Medium creature cannot squeeze through.|**Skill/Labor:** DC 16 Wis (Survival) to locate a beast-path through the stone, or hours of labor shattering the petrified wood.|
|**07**|**Sinkhole Gap:** The road has collapsed into a dark, 20-foot-wide pit.|**Skill:** DC 14 Str (Athletics) to jump with a running start, or requires laying down timber to cross.|
|**08**|**Obsidian Glass-Field:** A plain of jagged, razor-sharp volcanic glass.|**Resource:** Impassable on foot without heavy mounts, flight, or destroying it (AC 15, 10 HP per 5ft square) which takes time.|
|**09**|**Crystal Outcropping:** Fragile, razor-sharp quartz completely blocking a cave tunnel.|**Skill:** DC 15 Dex (Acrobatics) to squeeze through. Failure deals 2d4 Slashing damage and ruins non-magical clothing.|
|**10**|**Magnetic Ore Vein:** A massive lodestone deposit blocking a narrow canyon.|**Resource:** Creatures in metal armor cannot pass without a DC 18 Str (Athletics) check, or must remove their armor to cross.|
|**11**|**Washed-Out Bridge:** A torrential river blocks the path; only rotting support posts remain.|**Skill:** DC 16 Str (Athletics) to swim a tether rope across the raging water, or spells like _Water Walk_.|
|**12**|**Dammed Waterway:** A massive beaver or lizardfolk dam has flooded the valley, creating an impassable deep-water lake.|**Action:** Destroying the dam (AC 15, 50 HP) clears the path but causes a massive flash flood downstream.|
|**13**|**Frozen Waterfall:** A sheer, 60-foot vertical wall of slick ice blocking a mountain pass.|**Skill/Tool:** Requires climbing gear (ice axes) and a DC 16 Str (Athletics) check to ascend.|
|**14**|**Deep Swamp Channel:** The path dips into a 100-foot stretch of opaque, foul water.|**Resource:** Requires building a makeshift raft (1 hour) or casting _Water Breathing_. Swimming in heavy armor imposes Disadvantage.|
|**15**|**Churning Whirlpool:** A vortex blocks the only safe ford across a river.|**Skill:** DC 16 Str (Athletics) to row/pull a raft past the pull, or DC 15 Int (Nature) to predict its ebb and flow.|
|**16**|**Supernatural Fog Bank:** A thick, clinging vapor rolling off a marsh, heavily obscuring the entire valley.|**Skill/Magic:** DC 15 Wis (Survival) to navigate without getting lost. _Gust of Wind_ clears a temporary path.|
|**17**|**Flash Flood Ravine:** A sudden surge of water sweeps through the only gorge forward.|**Time/Skill:** Wait 1d8 hours for the water to recede, or attempt a DC 18 Str (Athletics) check to forge through.|
|**18**|**Ice-Choked River:** Fast-moving, grinding ice floes block a river crossing.|**Skill:** DC 15 Dex (Acrobatics) to hop across the moving floes. Failure drops the character into freezing water.|
|**19**|**Submerged Tunnel:** The only way forward dips completely underwater for 60 feet.|**Resource:** Requires _Water Breathing_, or a DC 14 Con save to hold breath while swimming in the dark.|
|**20**|**Boiling Geyser Field:** A network of vents erupting with scalding steam, blocking the trail.|**Skill/Timing:** DC 15 Dex (Acrobatics) to time the crossing perfectly. Failure deals 3d6 Fire damage.|
|**21**|**Thorny Briar Maze:** The path becomes violently constricted by razor-sharp brambles.|**Time/Tool:** Requires 1 hour of hacking with Slashing weapons/machetes, or fire magic (which creates choking smoke).|
|**22**|**Choking Web Canopy:** The trees are spun thick with phase spider or ettercap webbing, blocking movement.|**Action/Combat:** Webs have AC 10, 15 HP per square. Burning/destroying them opens the path but alerts the spiders above.|
|**23**|**Territorial Beast Den:** A sleeping apex predator (e.g., Dragon, Behir, Chimera) is slumbering directly on the path.|**Skill:** Group Dex (Stealth) check vs. the creature's Passive Perception to sneak past, or find a long detour.|
|**24**|**Massive Herd:** Thousands of migrating beasts (bison/elk) block the plains as far as the eye can see.|**Time/Skill:** Wait 1d4 hours for them to pass, or use DC 15 Wis (Animal Handling) to safely part the herd.|
|**25**|**Stubborn Treants / Awakened Trees:** Sentient trees interlock their branches, refusing passage.|**Social:** DC 15 Cha (Persuasion) to negotiate passage, or offering pure water/druidic magic as a bribe.|
|**26**|**Rotting Leviathan Corpse:** The carcass of a gargantuan beast has collapsed across the road, swelling with gas.|**Skill:** DC 14 Con save to endure the stench while climbing over, or suffer the Poisoned condition for 1 hour.|
|**27**|**Mutated Fungi Forest:** Towering, fragile mushrooms block the cavern. Touching them releases blinding spores.|**Skill:** DC 15 Int (Nature) to identify the safe path through the root system without triggering a spore collapse.|
|**28**|**Dense Insect Swarm:** A massive cloud of harmless but blinding locusts fills a narrow canyon.|**Resource:** Torches or smoke (from a campfire) clears them; otherwise, the area is Heavily Obscured and impossible to map.|
|**29**|**Poacher's Barricade:** A wall of sharpened, poisoned stakes built by local hunters to funnel game.|**Skill:** DC 14 Dex (Acrobatics) to vault, or DC 15 Dex (Sleight of Hand) to carefully dismantle the poisoned tips.|
|**30**|**Sleep-Nectar Field:** A field of blooming flowers that emit a magical soporific scent.|**Resource/Magic:** DC 13 Wis save to cross awake. Can be bypassed by covering faces with wet cloth or casting _Wind Wall_.|
|**31**|**Wall of Force:** An invisible, indestructible barrier left over from an ancient magical war bisects the landscape.|**Magic/Exploration:** Cannot be dispelled. Requires _Disintegrate_, _Dimension Door_, or exploring the perimeter to find the source.|
|**32**|**Druidic Forbiddance Line:** An ancient boundary marked by glowing runes and hanging bones.|**Magic/Lore:** _Dispel Magic_, or fulfilling a local superstition (DC 15 Int (History) to know to leave an offering of meat).|
|**33**|**Illusionary Terrain:** A _Hallucinatory Terrain_ makes a sheer cliff look like a gentle, inviting meadow.|**Skill/Magic:** DC 16 Int (Investigation) to discern the illusion, _Truesight_, or blindly testing the ground with a pole.|
|**34**|**The Lunar Path:** A treacherous mountain bridge that only becomes physically solid when moonlight touches it.|**Lore/Time:** DC 15 Int (Arcana) to deduce the lock. The party must wait until nightfall, risking nocturnal predators.|
|**35**|**Gravity Anomaly:** A 30-foot stretch of path where gravity pulls sideways toward a cliff face.|**Skill:** DC 15 Str (Athletics) to physically "climb" across the distorted gravity field without falling into the sky.|
|**36**|**Time Wrinkle:** A shimmering distortion where time moves incredibly slowly.|**Magic/Time:** Walking through takes 1d4 hours of in-game time for a 30-foot stretch, unless _Haste_ or _Expeditious Retreat_ is used.|
|**37**|**Teleportation Loop:** Walking forward past a certain pair of trees seamlessly teleports the party 50 feet backward.|**Skill/Magic:** DC 16 Int (Arcana) to deduce the trick. Bypassed by walking backward or casting _Dispel Magic_.|
|**38**|**Crystal Chime Door:** A cliff face with five massive, resonant stalactites.|**Puzzle/Skill:** Requires striking the chimes in the correct order (clues hidden nearby) or DC 16 Cha (Performance) to harmonize.|
|**39**|**The Riddle Stone:** A sentient, massive stone face blocking a tunnel.|**Social/Puzzle:** Answer its riddle correctly, or successfully use Cha (Deception/Persuasion) to trick the ancient guardian.|
|**40**|**Zone of Absolute Silence:** A 100-foot-wide crater where no sound exists.|**Resource:** Spellcasters cannot use Verbal components to cast spells (like _Fly_ or _Levitate_) to cross the gap; requires mundane solutions.|
|**41**|**Heavy Baffle Gate:** A rusted iron portcullis set into a canyon choke-point. The release bar is on the far side.|**Skill/Magic:** DC 15 Dex (Sleight of Hand) with Thieves' Tools to manually lift the pins, or use of _Mage Hand_.|
|**42**|**Massive Puzzle-Ring Door:** A circular stone vault door covered in rotating concentric rings of dwarven runes.|**Skill:** DC 15 Int (Investigation) to align the rings correctly based on environmental clues in the area.|
|**43**|**Raised Drawbridge:** A wooden bridge across a chasm, pulled up tight. The winch is visible 40 feet away on the opposite side.|**Resource:** Requires flight, teleportation, or a DC 18 ranged attack with a heavy projectile to smash the winch release lever.|
|**44**|**Stuck Chain-Hoist:** An ancient dwarven elevator platform stuck at the top of a 100-foot shaft.|**Skill:** DC 15 Dex (Acrobatics) to climb the greased, rusted chains to the top to repair the brake lever.|
|**45**|**Hunter's Snare Field:** The only path is littered with dozens of heavy, overlapping bear traps and tripwires.|**Skill/Time:** DC 15 Dex (Sleight of Hand) to slowly disarm a safe path. Failure means the rogue is Restrained and takes damage.|
|**46**|**Crank Handle (Missing Pin):** A heavy winch mechanism missing the vital shear-pin needed to open a floodgate.|**Tool/Skill:** Improvise a pin (dagger tang, piton) using DC 12 Dex (Sleight of Hand) or Tinker's Tools to force the gears to turn.|
|**47**|**Barred Keep Doors:** Iron-banded wooden doors of a ruined fortress blocking the mountain pass.|**Action:** AC 15, 30 HP to batter down, or a DC 16 Str (Athletics) check to force the rotting wood off its hinges.|
|**48**|**Crest-Sealed Gate:** A magical lock shaped like an empty family crest indentation.|**Skill/Magic:** DC 16 Int (History) to recognize the crest and forge a clay replica, or bypass with the _Knock_ spell.|
|**49**|**Acid-Etched Lock:** A highly complex lock on a smuggler's door. The interior is coated in residual acid.|**Tool:** DC 17 Dex (Sleight of Hand) with Thieves' Tools. Failing by 5 or more melts and ruins the tools.|
|**50**|**Counterweight Bridge:** A platform that only lowers if exactly 500 lbs of weight is placed on the pressure plate.|**Puzzle/Resource:** The party must use their own body weight, combined with gathered rocks or gear, to perfectly balance the scale.|
