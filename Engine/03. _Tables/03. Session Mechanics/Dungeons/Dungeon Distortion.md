|**d20**|**Distortion Type**|**Mechanical Effect**|
|---|---|---|
|1|**Non-Euclidean Shift**|The exit the players just used now leads to a completely different, unexplored room instead of the one they left.|
|2|**Temporal Lag**|All players repeat their last turn exactly (same movement, same rolls) as time "stutters." Enemies are unaffected.|
|3|**Gravity Flip**|The room rotates 90° or 180°. Ceilings become floors. Fall damage applies immediately.|
|4|**The Endless Hall**|A straight hallway loop. To escape, players must walk backward or close their eyes while moving.|
|5|**Echoes of the Future**|Players see translucent "ghosts" of themselves in the next room, revealing what they will be doing 1 minute from now.|
|6|**Mirror Plane**|The layout is perfectly mirrored. Right is left. Map-makers must flip their drawings; it's surprisingly disorienting.|
|7|**Material Swap**|Stone becomes wood, metal becomes glass, or air becomes water for 1d4 rounds.|
|8|**Expansion / Contraction**|The room grows by 2x (increasing range/movement needs) or shrinks to 1/2 size (forcing everyone into melee).|
|9|**Déjà Vu**|The current encounter "resets." Dead enemies revive with 1 HP, and all spell slots spent in this room return (once).|
|10|**Shadow Bleed**|Lighting is inverted. Torches cast shadows/darkness; darkness becomes blindingly bright light.|
|11|**Relative Velocity**|Time moves faster for players. They get 2 actions per turn, but age 1 year for every minute spent in this state.|
|12|**The Fold**|Two non-adjacent rooms on your map now have a "tear" connecting them. You can step from Room 1 to Room 8 instantly.|
|13|**Stasis Bubble**|A 10ft sphere in the room where time stops. Projectiles caught in it hang in the air until the distortion ends.|
|14|**Memory Leak**|Players forget the map. You take their physical map away (or hide the VTT fog) until they exit the room.|
|15|**Audio Displacement**|Sound travels from the future. Players hear the combat sounds of the _next_ room before they enter it.|
|16|**Magnetic Pulse**|All metal items are pulled toward the center of the room. STR save or be disarmed/dragged.|
|17|**Vertical Drift**|The floor begins rising like an elevator. Players have 3 rounds to find an exit before they are crushed against the ceiling.|
|18|**Recursive Doors**|Every door in the room leads back into the same room, but with a slight change (color, smell, temperature).|
|19|**Slow Motion**|Everything moves at 1/4 speed. Advantage on DEX saves, but -10 to Initiative and halved movement.|
|20|**Singularity**|A point in the room starts sucking in reality. Every round, one random object (or small item) is erased from existence.|
