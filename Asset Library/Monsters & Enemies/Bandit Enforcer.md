# Bandit Enforcer

- Covers retreats
- Will not talk unless cornered

- **Speed:** 30 ft.

STR 15 (+2)  
DEX 11 (+0)  
CON 14 (+2)  
INT 10 (+0)  
WIS 10 (+0)  
CHA 11 (+0)

- **Skills:** Intimidation +2
- **Senses:** passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common
- **CR:** 1/2 (100 XP)

- **Mace.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage.
