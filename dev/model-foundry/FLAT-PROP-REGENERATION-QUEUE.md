# Flat prop regeneration queue

**Pilot scope: Fantasy only. Gloom and Chrome are deferred until the Fantasy proof is accepted.**

Legacy perspective sprites are reference art only. They cannot be extruded directly. Every queued source is regenerated in a separate model call, chroma-keyed, despilled, and projection-reviewed before its manifest may say `FLAT_ORTHOGRAPHIC_QA_PASS`.

Queued: **32** (fantasy: 32).

The machine-readable prompts and per-asset QA records live in `flat-prop-regeneration-queue.json`. Do not combine them into sprite sheets during generation; contact sheets are a review output only.
