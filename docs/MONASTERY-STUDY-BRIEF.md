## Lanes (all in one pass for a background run; report once)

1. **Cloister morphology (the core lane).** The St. Gall plan (the famous ideal
   monastery drawing) · Cistercian standard plans · a built example or two with clear
   plans (public-domain/CC sources). Extract: courtyard proportions · arcade bay rhythm
   (bay width, arch count, what repeats) · the room-role ring (chapel, hall/refectory,
   cells/dormitory, garden — what sits adjacent to what and why) · where water lives
   (well, channel, fountain). Deliverable: the cloister relational grammar.
2. **The seed's stress test: hillside vs flat.** Terraced/hillside monasteries (Meteora,
   Mont-Sainte-Odile, Tibetan hillside monasteries, terraced sanctuaries) vs the flat
   valley cloister norm. Extract: does the stepped courtyard rising to the sanctuary
   have genuine precedent, and what do real terraced compounds do with their gates
   (low? lateral?)? Report honestly either way — the RULED seed stands unless evidence
   argues, and then Adam re-rules with open eyes.
3. **The institutional chassis.** The same walled-courtyard architecture serving other
   institutions: collegiate quadrangles (schools) · hospital cloisters (Hôtel-Dieu
   type) · historic prison yards · courtyard inns (REUSE `Reference/Camp-Study/`
   lane 3's caravanserai work — do not redo it; cite it). Extract: what actually
   changes between institutions on the same chassis (openings? gate treatment? yard
   contents? cell row proportions?) — the who-runs-it axis made visible in stone.
4. **Defense + the vertical accent.** Fortified monasteries (Mont-Saint-Michel type,
   militant-order houses, dzong-form compounds). Extract: bell/watch tower placement
   grammar · what a defended gate looks like on a house whose wall is "for separation,
   not war" · which defensive pieces are occupancy-licensed additions vs baseline.
5. **FFT cohort.** The corpus at
   `Reference/FFT-Guard-Post-Study/study-archive/maps/five-angle/` (121 maps × 5 views;
   `<map>_<view>.gif`; view 0 = top-down plan; the id→name table is at
   `study-archive/tools/heretic/src/map.c` — verified empirically by the lair study).
   Find the institutional subset: Orbonne Monastery (locate its id via the name table),
   the church maps (36, 95), map 59's barrel-vault arcades (repeated-bay evidence),
   Golgorand (63), and whatever else qualifies. Extract: how FFT composes institutional
   courtyards and interiors · arcade/bay rhythm at gameplay scale · raised
   chancel/platform treatment · gate framing. Exclude developer test maps (116–120,
   125).
6. **Touchstones (documentation only, no images).** BG3's Rosymorn Monastery (terraced
   mountain monastery — approach, courtyard, tower, and how its verticality plays) ·
   FFT Orbonne's opening battle staging if documented. Extract what works at gameplay
   scale.
