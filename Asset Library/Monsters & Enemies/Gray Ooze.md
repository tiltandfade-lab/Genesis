### The Gray Ooze: The Slithering Stone

Unlike the massive, gelatinous cubes that mindlessly sweep corridors, the gray ooze is a cunning, snake-like predator that perfectly camouflages itself as a wet rock formation or an oily puddle. It moves by slithering across floors, walls, and ceilings, dropping onto unsuspecting prey to rapidly dissolve their flesh and armor.

Because its highly acidic secretions exclusively target organic matter and metal, it leaves behind an eerie trail of untouched wood, stone, and bone. In subterranean societies, master metalworkers sometimes carefully capture tiny gray oozes in stone jars, utilizing their controlled acidic secretions to expertly etch and score metalwork.

A gray ooze reproduces by "budding," leaving behind a small, leathery pod in dark caverns that matures into a fully formed ooze after two to three days.

### 1. Gray Ooze

_Medium Ooze, Unaligned_

- **Armor Class:** 9
- **Hit Points:** 22 (3d8 + 9)
- **Speed:** 10 ft., Climb 10 ft.
- **Initiative:** -2 (13)
- **STR:** 12 (+1) | **DEX:** 6 (-2) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 1 (-5) | **WIS:** 6 (-2) | **CHA:** 2 (-4)
- **Skills:** Stealth +2
- **Damage Resistances:** Acid, Cold, Fire
- **Condition Immunities:** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Prone, Restrained
- **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 8
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 1/2 (100 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Amorphous.** The ooze can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.
- **Corrosive Form.** Nonmagical ammunition is destroyed immediately after hitting the ooze and dealing any damage. Any nonmagical weapon takes a cumulative -1 penalty to attack rolls immediately after dealing damage to the ooze and coming into contact with it. The weapon is destroyed if the penalty reaches -5. The penalty can be removed by casting the *Mending* spell on the weapon. The ooze can eat through 2-inch-thick, nonmagical metal or wood in 1 round.

**Actions**

- **Pseudopod.** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +3, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 10 (2d8 + 1) acid damage. Nonmagical armor worn by the target takes a -1 penalty to the AC it offers. The armor is destroyed if the penalty reduces its AC to 10. The penalty can be removed by casting the *Mending* spell on the armor.

- **Damage Resistances:** Acid, Cold, Fire, Psychic
- **Condition Immunities:** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Prone, Restrained
- **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 8
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Amorphous.** The ooze can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.

**Actions**

- **Pseudopod.** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +3, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 11 (3d6 + 1) acid damage, and the target has Disadvantage on Intelligence saving throws until the end of the ooze's next turn.
- **Psychic Crush.** _Intelligence Saving Throw:_ DC 10, one creature the ooze can see within 60 feet. _Failure:_ 13 (3d8) psychic damage.

**Reactions**

- **Mind Corrosion.** _Trigger:_ The ooze fails a saving throw against a spell or another magical effect created by a creature. _Response:_ The triggering creature takes 3 (1d6) psychic damage.

### d10 Ooze Residue & Clues (The Investigation Layer)

Because gray oozes dissolve flesh and metal but leave stone, wood, glass, and bone completely unharmed, their feeding grounds offer highly specific clues.

|d10|Discovery|Investigative Hook|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Clean Skeleton**|A skeleton rests against the wall. The bones and the wooden haft of their spear are perfectly preserved, but the spearhead and their armor are entirely missing, hinting at an ooze rather than a beast.|
|**2**|**The Glass Vials**|The PCs find an alchemist's satchel. The leather bag and protective iron casings are dissolved, but several highly volatile glass potion vials are left rolling dangerously across the floor.|
|**3**|**The Slither Trail**|A DC 12 Survival check reveals an unnatural, completely dust-free pathway winding up a stone wall and across the ceiling, indicating a gray ooze's patrol route.|
|**4**|**The Empty Stone Jars**|Several heavily sealed stone jars lay broken open from the outside. A rival guild deliberately released an artisan's captive etching oozes to sabotage their workshop.|
|**5**|**The Dropped Loot**|A small pile of gold and silver coins rests in a puddle of clear liquid. (Unlike some slimes, gray oozes corrode metal, so finding intact coins means this is either a newly arrived ooze or a completely different trap).|
|**6**|**The "Leathery" Shells**|Discarded, leathery husks are scattered in a dark corner. A DC 14 Nature check confirms these are the remnants of gray ooze "buds," meaning a new generation has recently hatched.|
|**7**|**The Melted Lock**|An iron door is completely sealed, but not by a lock. The lock mechanism has been deliberately melted into a slag-like substance by someone carefully applying ooze acid to sabotage the exit.|
|**8**|**The Psychic Static**|The air in the room feels heavy, and PCs experience a mild, throbbing headache. This environmental cue warns of a Psychic Gray Ooze preparing a _Psychic Crush_.|
|**9**|**The Preserved Map**|A wooden map case contains a completely undamaged parchment map of the dungeon, left untouched because the ooze could not digest the cellulose and wood.|
|**10**|**The False Stalagmite**|A perception check (DC 15) reveals that one of the "wet rocks" hanging from the ceiling is slowly, almost imperceptibly, undulating and shifting its shape.|
