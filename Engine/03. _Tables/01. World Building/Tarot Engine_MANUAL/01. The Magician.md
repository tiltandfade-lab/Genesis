# Dungeon Effects
#### **1. The Constant: "The Five-Element Resonance"**

The Magician represents the mastery of the world's building blocks.

- **The Effect:** Every room in the dungeon is resonant with one of the five elements: **Spirit, Air, Fire, Water, or Earth**.
    
- **The Mechanic:** When the party enters a new room, roll a d6 (1-5 corresponds to an element, 6 is the DM's choice). For the duration of that room, all spells or effects associated with that element are "Empowered" (e.g., +1 to Save DCs or +2 to damage).

#### **2. Monster Mutation Table: "Arcane Infusion"**

Whenever you place a monster from your "empty shell" into a Magician-themed dungeon, roll on this table to see how the card's energy has mutated it.

|**d6**|**Monster Mutation**|**Effect**|
|---|---|---|
|1|**Spell-Spliced**|The creature can cast one 1st-level spell (e.g., _Shield_ or _Magic Missile_) once per day.|
|2|**Elemental Skin**|The creature gains resistance to one element (Air/Lightning, Fire, Water/Cold, or Earth/Acid).|
|3|**Blinking Presence**|As a bonus action, the creature can teleport 10 feet.|
|4|**Mana-Gorged**|When the creature dies, it explodes in a 10ft radius of force damage (1d6 per CR).|
|5|**Inscrutable Mind**|The creature is immune to being charmed or frightened.|
|6|**Willpower Aura**|Allies within 10ft of this creature gain a +2 bonus to all saving throws.|

#### **3. Loot Mutation Table: "The Magician's Tools"**

When players find gold or mundane items, they might find a "Magician's Tool" instead. These are single-use "potentials."

| **d6** | **Loot Mutation**            | **Description**                                                                           |
| ------ | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1      | **Essence of Spirit**        | A small vial. When broken, it restores one expended 1st-level spell slot.                 |
| 2      | **Bottled Gale (Air)**       | When opened, it casts _Gust of Wind_ in a direction of the user's choice.                 |
| 3      | **Ever-Burning Coal (Fire)** | Can be thrown to create a 10ft wall of fire that lasts for 1 minute.                      |
| 4      | **Vitality Dew (Water)**     | A blue liquid that removes one level of exhaustion or ends one disease.                   |
| 5      | **Lodestone Core (Earth)**   | When placed on a surface, it becomes immovable (as an _Immovable Rod_) for 1 hour.        |
| 6      | **The Magician’s Coin**      | Flip it. Heads: You succeed on your next check. Tails: The next enemy succeeds on theirs. |

#### **4. Dungeon Asset Table: "The Workshop of Reality"**

Roll on this table to add flavor or "tricks" to the rooms of your empty shell.

| **d6** | **Room Asset / Trick**      | **Interaction**                                                                                                             |
| ------ | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1      | **Floating Worktables**     | Tables covered in alchemical tools hover 3 feet off the ground; they provide half-cover.                                    |
| 2      | **The Infinite Chalkboard** | A wall covered in shifting equations. A successful Arcana check reveals a secret door.                                      |
| 3      | **Elemental Pylons**        | Crystals humming with energy. If destroyed, they release an elemental burst (see Constant Effect).                          |
| 4      | **The Mirror of Potential** | A mirror showing the player as a master of a different class. Looking into it grants a temporary Skill Proficiency.         |
| 5      | **Mercury Pools**           | Floors covered in liquid metal. It is difficult terrain, but metal weapons dipped in it deal +1 damage for the next minute. |
| 6      | **Unfinished Golem**        | A half-built clay figure. If "Spirit" energy is applied, it protects the party for one encounter.                           |

# World Effects

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

_Reveals the future behavior or hidden nature of the faction involved._

**The Constant:** "Hidden Mastery." The faction is not what it appears. They are currently manipulating a resource or a person to achieve a "Grand Work."

| **d6** | **Faction Behavior (The Magician)** | **Narrative Implication**                                                                                            |
| ------ | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1      | **The Illusionist**                 | The faction is using a front or a puppet leader to hide their true intentions.                                       |
| 2      | **Elemental Monopoly**              | They are aggressively seizing control of a specific resource (e.g., all the silver, a specific ley line, or a port). |
| 3      | **The Great Work**                  | The faction is close to completing a massive project (a ritual, a super-weapon, or a new law).                       |
| 4      | **The Recruiter**                   | They will attempt to "collect" the party, offering tools and power in exchange for absolute loyalty.                 |
| 5      | **Internal Schism**                 | Two leaders are fighting for the "Magician’s Mantle"—the faction is currently paralyzed by a power struggle.         |
| 6      | **Divine Bridge**                   | The faction has successfully contacted a higher (or lower) power and is acting as their direct conduit.              |
### **Trigger: Accepting Binding Agreements**

_Defines the nature of the cost or the "gift" associated with the deal._

**The Constant:** "The Tool's Price." Every power granted by this bargain requires a piece of the user's **Spirit**. The player must permanently (until the deal ends) reduce one skill proficiency to gain the bargain's benefit.

| **d6** | **The Bargain's Mutation (The Magician)** | **The "Gift"**                                                                                                                                  |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1      | **Breath of Air**                         | You can cast _Invisibility_ once per day, but you leave no footprints and have no scent for 24 hours afterward.                                 |
| 2      | **Spark of Fire**                         | Your attacks deal an extra 1d6 fire damage, but you cannot enter a building without "extinguishing" yourself (losing 1d10 HP).                  |
| 3      | **Flow of Water**                         | You can breathe underwater and swim at your walking speed, but you must consume twice the normal amount of liquid daily.                        |
| 4      | **Weight of Earth**                       | You gain +2 to AC, but your movement speed is reduced by 10 feet as your skin becomes stone-like.                                               |
| 5      | **Echo of Spirit**                        | You can communicate telepathically with anyone you've met, but you can no longer speak aloud.                                                   |
| 6      | **The Magician’s Will**                   | You can choose to succeed on one saving throw automatically. Once used, the "patron" takes control of your next long rest (no benefits gained). |
### **Trigger: World Shifts**

_Determines the scale and type of change affecting the campaign setting._

**The Constant:** "The Great Discovery." A new law of physics or magic has been discovered. This change is permanent and affects everyone in the region.

| **d6** | **The Nature of the Shift (The Magician)** | **World Change**                                                                                                                |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1      | **Arcane Proliferation**                   | Magic becomes easier to use. Commoners begin manifesting cantrips; chaos ensues in cities.                                      |
| 2      | **The Silent Spirit**                      | Connection to the divine or outer planes is severed. Spells over 5th level no longer function for 1d4 months.                   |
| 3      | **Elemental Inversion**                    | Fire burns cold; Water becomes solid like glass; Earth becomes soft like sponge. Geography shifts overnight.                    |
| 4      | **The Clockwork Rise**                     | A sudden advancement in technology. Steam-powered or "spirit-driven" automatons begin replacing manual labor.                   |
| 5      | **The New Alphabet**                       | The language of the world changes. All written text becomes unreadable until a new "Key" is found.                              |
| 6      | **The Manifest Will**                      | Thought becomes reality in a specific area. If someone fears a monster, it appears. If they crave gold, it grows from the dirt. |
