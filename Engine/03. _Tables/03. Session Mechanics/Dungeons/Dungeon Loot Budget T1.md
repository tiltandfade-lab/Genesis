> [!info] T1 Loot Budget Reference (Lv 1–4)
> Based on DMG 2024 recommendations: 6 Common, 4 Uncommon, 1 Rare across a full T1 campaign. Budget below reflects one dungeon's share. The generator computes this from segment count; deepest rooms get rarest items. Boss always receives the top-rarity slot.

| **Dungeon Size** | **Segments** | **Common** | **Uncommon** | **Rare** |
| ---------------- | ------------ | ---------- | ------------ | -------- |
| Small | 5–8 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| Medium | 9–12 | 2 | 2 | 0 |
| Large | 13+ | 3 | 2 | 1 |

> **Distribution:** Rare → Boss room. Uncommon → deepest non-boss rooms first. Common → remaining mid-depth rooms. All other rooms receive coin only (see Dungeon Loot - Coin Cache).
