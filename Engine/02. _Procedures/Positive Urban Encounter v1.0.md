Here is a fully expanded, elaborate procedural generation engine that mirrors your **v3 Urban Encounter Engine**.

To achieve the "subverted expectations" texture you are looking for, the **Macro**, **Micro**, and **Tactical** frames are designed to feel entirely neutral or slightly threatening. They generate shadows, choke points, and gritty street features that will make your players instinctively reach for their weapons.

The "flip" only happens when you roll on **Section 4** and **Section 5**, transforming a tactically tense environment into a beautiful, peaceful, or advantageous scene.

---

## 2. The Micro Frame (The Texture)

#### d8 Area Type

|d8|Area Geometry|
|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Choke Point:** A narrow alley or bridge barely wide enough for two people abreast.|
|**2**|**The Enclosed Courtyard:** A dead-end square surrounded by high walls on three sides.|
|**3**|**The Crossroads:** A chaotic intersection of three or more twisting streets.|
|**4**|**The Vertical Drop:** A staircase, terrace, or cliff-side street with a dangerous ledge.|
|**5**|**The Sunken Path:** A drained canal or trench-like road overlooked by higher walkways.|
|**6**|**The Open Plaza:** A wide, exposed area with virtually no immediate perimeter cover.|
|**7**|**The Covered Arcade:** A street entirely roofed over by overlapping buildings or canvas awnings.|
|**8**|**The Ruined Shell:** The burned-out or collapsed foundation of a large, destroyed building.|

## 4. Encounter Determination (The Reveal)

_(When the players push into the tactical area expecting a fight, this is what they actually find)._

#### d4 Positive Encounter Type

|d4|Encounter Category|Action / Resolution|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**A. Helpful Contact / Faction Reprieve**|The PCs find an off-duty rival, a competent guard, or a grateful local resting in the cover. They offer a moment of truce, a shared drink, and a warning about a mutual threat.|
|**2**|**B. Mundane Spectacle / Art**|The PCs discover the source of the sensory details: a satirical puppet show, a masterful street musician, or a spontaneous, harmless magical anomaly. The crowd is docile and awestruck.|
|**3**|**C. Resource / Windfall**|Tucked behind the tactical cover, the PCs find a dropped coin pouch, an abandoned smuggler's stash of fine wine/rations, or an unlooted cache of medical supplies.|
|**4**|**D. The Moment of Peace (Empty)**|The area is entirely empty, but perfectly secure. The tactical terrain creates a natural fortress against the elements and city patrols. A dry hearth or clean spring is present.|

## 5. The Hidden Layer (The Boon)

_(If the players take the time to embrace the positive encounter—sitting for the performance, sharing the drink, or taking a Short Rest—reveal the Hidden Layer)._

#### d8 Secret Boon / Advantage

|d8|The Hidden Layer|
|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Unwitting Patron:** An NPC in the crowd (or the contact they spoke to) is actually a disguised noble or powerful guildmaster. By being peaceful, the PCs earn a massive political favor.|
|**2**|**The Hidden Safehouse:** A DC 14 Investigation check reveals a subtle Watcher (or local equivalent) sigil on the wall. The PCs now know the password to use this location as a permanent sanctuary.|
|**3**|**The Localized Blessing:** The area projects an ancient, residual _Consecrate_ effect. Any PC taking a Short Rest here gains Advantage on their next saving throw against a magical fear or charm effect.|
|**4**|**The Telltale Flaw:** Observing the area in peace allows the PCs to notice a structural weakness in the district's defenses. They gain a permanent +2 bonus to Initiative or Stealth checks made in this district.|
|**5**|**The Forgotten Lore:** Tucked into a crack in the landmark is a perfectly preserved journal, map, or ledger that provides a direct, risk-free clue to a major campaign mystery.|
|**6**|**Heroic Inspiration:** The sheer beauty, resilience, or humanity of the moment reminds the PCs what they are fighting for. Every character who participates in the scene immediately gains Heroic Inspiration.|
|**7**|**The Loyal Mascot:** A stray animal (a mastiff, a pseudodragon, or a fey creature) is sheltering here. If treated kindly with the windfall supplies, it bonds with a PC and becomes a loyal companion.|
|**8**|**The Restorative Aura:** The magic bleed in the area is benevolent. Any character who spends Hit Dice here treats any roll of a 1 or 2 as a 3, and their exhaustion levels are reduced by one.|
