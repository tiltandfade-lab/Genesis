## Render prompt set

All three renders use the Guard Post calibration image, Genesis camera/geometry/light
reference, Genesis standee-scale reference, and the corresponding rolled layout diagram.
Each prompt binds the same shared grammar:

- a fixed elevated orthographic three-quarter camera and compact tabletop scene tray;
- high-resolution low-poly 3D environment with crisp pixel-art character standees;
- camera-side wall omission, full visible wall height, and closed wall crowns;
- implied 5-foot construction proportions without a visible grid;
- honest ambient occlusion, contact shadows, cast shadows, and lore-native practical
  lights;
- broad material masses, sparse mechanic-bearing props, readable routes, landings,
  objectives, cover, and exits; and
- no copied Final Fantasy Tactics map, copyrighted character, logo, UI, or text.

The image-specific prompt blocks are:

- **Keeper House:** rough-hewn basalt on a narrow slate-roof hillside; street-facing
  keeper room and case-file desk; raised control landing; four cells on one side;
  debtors' pen; rear yard/cistern; property closet; one subtle inside-latch opportunity.
- **Ledger and Shift:** fieldstone-and-mortar border garrison; public waiting, intake,
  records, property lockers, lockable interview, raised shift station, four cells in
  opposing pairs, short secure corridor, rear service loop, old-city hatch.
- **Suspended Ironwood:** a keeper/winch platform and solitary grown-wood crate-cell
  suspended by chains and roots above the Shimmering Maw; narrow gangway, pulley,
  property chest, captive and keeper standees; no theatre content and no metal-cage
  substitution.

The final PNGs remain concept targets. Named repository models are production anchors,
not a claim that the generated pixels are literal screenshots of those GLBs.
