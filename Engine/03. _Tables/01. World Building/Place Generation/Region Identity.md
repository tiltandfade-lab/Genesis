> lands in an untouched region (`[[REGIONS-NAMES]]` §1) — full roll-chain on first touch: a name, a
> one-line character, and a mechanical flavor vector. Canon from the moment it's rolled (ledger
> `canon` + a codex `region` record) — never re-rolled for the same region. The flavor vector feeds
> `resolveArchetypePool` weights (`archetypeBias`), the walk-skin roll (`skinBias`), shop
> stock/lodging (`econTilt`), and assigns 2 of the 12 name cultures to the region for local NPC
> naming. `spiceTilt` nudges the region's felt intensity without breaking the shared curve. Spice-
> graded d100, Commitment ceiling (66 Grounded · 20 Textured · 9 Strange · 4 Volatile · 1 Mythic).
