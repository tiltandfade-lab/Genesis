# FFT Guard Post Study Cohort

This cohort narrows the complete 121-map/five-view corpus to ten references that can teach distinct
parts of the first Genesis golden Guard Post.

The images are copied into `guard-post-cohort/<map>/` for convenient study. The untouched originals
remain in `../maps/five-angle/`.

| Map | Reference role |

| MAP098 — Outside Castle Gate in Lesalia | Exterior approach, gate-side terrain, composed map edge |

## First analysis pass

For each map:

1. identify the five view directions before treating any image as authoritative;
2. sketch the stage envelope and large negative-space masses;
3. trace the primary route and any materially distinct secondary route;
4. label broad elevation regions and connectors;
5. label dominant, secondary, and tertiary masses;
6. record architecture/road/terrain intersections;
7. record rock and vegetation clustering;
8. record threshold, lookout, flank, refuge, exposure, and retreat relations;
9. test camera silhouette and occlusion;
10. separate observed facts from Genesis hypotheses.

## Guard Post questions for the cohort

- What makes a road feel embedded in terrain rather than painted on top?
- Where does architecture meet a slope, cut, terrace, wall, or outcrop?
- How many broad elevation masses read before the map becomes noisy?
- How do rocks support routes and silhouettes without becoming scatter?
- How much quiet grass/ground is necessary around a dominant post?
- How does a guarded structure control a route visually and tactically?
- Which foreground masses are suppressed, cut away, or composed low for readability?
- Which repeated relationships can become table-authored archetypes?

Nothing in this cohort is a production asset or a layout to copy. Its purpose is to extract
relational grammar and anti-rules.
