| Die | Segment Type | Description | Transition (d4) |
|:---:|:-------------|:------------|:-----------------|
| 1 | The Burning Building | A warehouse's top floor ignites; smoke rolls into adjacent streets and the conflagration spreads with each passing round. | 1: The fire cuts off a planned escape route—party must find a new path. 2: Fleeing refugees panic and block the party's advance. 3: A building next door collapses, trapping innocents the party must rescue or abandon. 4: The chaos attracts rival faction members scrambling to salvage goods. |
| 2 | The Ambush Funnel | A narrow alley choked by market stalls becomes a kill zone as enemies close from both ends, collapsing escape routes. | 1: One exit floods with enemy reinforcements; the party chooses fight or a dangerous rooftop sprint. 2: A stall collapses, blocking retreat but creating a temporary barrier against pursuers. 3: A faction hired the ambushers—the party stumbles onto a larger conspiracy. 4: Innocents caught in the crossfire force a moral choice that slows the party's movement. |
| 3 | The Bridge Collapse | A merchant bridge mid-crossing develops fatal cracks; it falls in 2–3 rounds, with party members still trapped on it. | 1: The party must save someone caught on the failing span, costing precious rounds. 2: Enemies pursue onto the collapsing bridge, forcing a desperate mid-crossing battle. 3: The bridge's fall blocks the only direct route forward; an alternate path leads through enemy-controlled territory. 4: Salvage and refugees pile up on the far bank, creating a bottleneck the party must push through. |
| 4 | The Mob Surge | A crowd ignited by rumor, preaching, or panic swells and turns violent, surging toward the party's position. | 1: The mob's direction changes; they flow toward a new target the party must defend or exploit. 2: Faction agitators within the crowd drive it deeper into dangerous territory, dragging the party along. 3: The party is separated in the press; rejoining costs time or a detour through unstable areas. 4: The mob's fury draws official response—armed militia or summoned guards arrive to disperse them. |
| 5 | The Hostage Standoff | A kidnapper or madman seizes innocents in a doorway, threatening escalation; time is running out. | 1: The captor's demands lead the party into a deliberately exposed position where enemies wait. 2: A failed negotiation attempt triggers a chase through dense residential blocks. 3: Another faction arrives with their own agenda, forcing the party to choose whose interests matter. 4: The hostage is released but poisoned, sick, or cursed—adding a new emergency objective. |
| 6 | The Alchemist's Eruption | A volatile shop explodes in a sequence of cascading detonations, sending shrapnel and toxic fumes across a wide district. | 1: The blasts sever a bridge or collapse a building, cutting the city into hostile fragments. 2: Survivors are injured or mad; desperate for aid or turning violent from fear. 3: A faction claims the explosion was sabotage and blames someone the party knows. 4: The chaos reveals a secret cache or hidden passage buried in the rubble—but retrieving it means crossing dangerous debris. |
| 7 | The Dam Failure | A reservoir barrier cracks and ruptures; water floods the lower city with frightening speed, drowning everything it touches. | 1: The party must reach high ground or be swept away. 2: Innocents are trapped in flooded districts; rescue attempts cost precious movement. 3: The flood reveals sunken ruins and hidden passages. 4: The catastrophe reshapes the city's geography and factions' power bases. |
| 8 | The Plague Outbreak | Sickness spreads rapidly through a district; the infected become violent or catatonic, guards begin shooting anyone who flees. | 1: Quarantine lines cut off escape routes; immunity or disguise becomes essential. 2: The sick herd toward the party, exposing them as uninfected. 3: The disease spreads to the party, changing their situation fundamentally. 4: A faction offers aid—at the cost of the party's freedom or secrets. |
| 9 | The Faction War Erupts | A simmering conflict between two or more groups suddenly ignites into open street warfare; the party is caught between advancing lines. | 1: Crossfire from multiple directions forces immediate cover-seeking. 2: One faction mistakes the party for enemies and opens fire. 3: A civilian district becomes a battleground; collateral damage is severe. 4: The party is pressed into service by a faction to survive. |
| 10 | The Summoned Disaster | A spellcaster or ritual site unleashes something: a magical storm, a demon, a void-rift that devours space itself. | 1: The phenomenon closes off normal escape routes; the party must navigate around it. 2: The entity hunts indiscriminately; survival requires understanding what drives it. 3: Multiple factions converge to stop the threat, creating secondary conflicts. 4: The disaster reveals its purpose was deliberately caused. |
| 11 | The Authority's Purge | The city guard, a noble's retinue, or an inquisitorial force moves through the district conducting mass arrests or executions. | 1: Innocent and guilty alike are taken; the party is mistaken for targets. 2: Hiding proves difficult; discovery means being swept up in the purge. 3: The purge's true target becomes clear—someone the party knows or needs. 4: The party must choose between exposure and complicity. |
| 12 | The Construct Rampage | An animated guardian, golem, or magical mechanism malfunctions and begins destroying the city; it cannot be stopped, only avoided. | 1: The construct's path intersects the party's route; detours are significant. 2: It hunts magical auras or specific types of people; the party may attract it. 3: Destruction in its wake reveals secrets or opportunities. 4: The construct is deliberately uncontrolled as a weapon. |
| 13 | The Fey Incursion | A nearby fey realm weakens its barriers; strange creatures and impossible physics spread across the affected district. | 1: The party's perception and abilities may work differently under fey logic. 2: Time becomes unreliable; minutes or hours pass strangely. 3: Fey predators hunt in the district, seeing mortals as prey or toys. 4: The incursion reveals hidden fey-touched locations or people. |
| 14 | The Undead Rising | Graves and crypts overflow; the dead rise confused or malevolent, filling streets and buildings. | 1: The undead target the living indiscriminately; flight is safer than fight. 2: A necromancer drives the dead toward specific locations or people. 3: The party discovers the rising serves a deeper purpose. 4: Holy ground or running water offers temporary refuge but not escape. |
| 15 | The Void Expansion | A location begins devouring reality around it; matter, people, and light vanish into nothing; the void expands inexorably. | 1: The party must race away from the expanding edge. 2: Those at the void's margin experience existence breaking down. 3: The void's source is a person, artifact, or ritual that must be stopped. 4: The void leaves survivors changed or partially unmade. |
| 16 | The Madness Cascade | Reality becomes unreliable; physics fails, impossible geometry manifests, and minds break under the weight of wrongness. | 1: The party's sanity is tested; failures result in erratic behavior. 2: The city reshapes itself into nightmare geometry while they traverse it. 3: Others succumb, becoming hazards themselves through their delusion. 4: The cascade originates from something intelligent watching and laughing. |
| 17 | The Devouring Swarm | A tide of magical creatures, insects, or hunger-driven beings floods through districts, consuming everything. | 1: The party is hunted actively; standing still means being engulfed. 2: The swarm is driven by a hive-mind or shepherd creature. 3: Fire or abjuration may slow it, but cannot stop it. 4: The swarm leaves nothing alive in its path. |
| 18 | The Rupture in the World | A crack in reality opens; things from beyond pour through, and the boundaries between dimensions thin catastrophically. | 1: The party is exposed to the alien; sanity checks are necessary. 2: Entities from beyond hunt with intelligence and purpose. 3: The rupture's cause is deliberate and intentional. 4: Closing it may require a sacrifice the party is unwilling to make. |
| 19 | The Apotheosis Event | A powerful being—mortal, divine, or something else—transcends or transforms; the power release reshapes the city permanently. | 1: The transformation's aftermath kills thousands; the party must survive the storm. 2: The being's final words or actions set the city on a new path. 3: The party is marked or chosen by the event in unforeseen ways. 4: The transformation reveals the city itself is alive or aware. |
| 20 | The Inevitable End | Something from deep time arrives; the city's fate becomes certain, and survival itself becomes questionable. | 1: The party glimpses what is coming and cannot prevent it. 2: Reality itself begins to fail as the end approaches. 3: All factions and peoples cease their conflicts in the face of the absolute. 4: The party must escape the city before there is no city to escape from. |
