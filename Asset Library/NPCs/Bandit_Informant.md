# Mira Kel

*(Asset: bandit_informant_moral_fracture)*

## Plot Vector

**Primary Pillar:** Bandits  
**Secondary Pillars:** Cult, Disappearances  

**Function:**  
Provides entry into bandit structure.  
Hints that raids are not random.  
Suggests someone higher is directing targets.

---

## Level 1 — Immediate Table Presence

**Race / Background:** Human; former fisher  
**Role:** Bandit scout  

**Visual Quirk:** Constantly scanning tree lines  
**Mood:** Tense, conflicted  
**Demeanor:** Avoids direct eye contact  

**Hook:** Wants out but doesn’t know how  
**Talent:** Can move silently through marsh reeds  
**Unrealized Potential:** Yes  

---

## Level 2 — Structural Pressure

**Formative Trauma:** Family lost trade license unfairly  
**Formative Grace:** Bandits fed them during hardship  

**Want:** Escape without retaliation  
**Fear:** Being marked a traitor  

**Faction Tie:** Knows which raids were “requested” by elites  
**Influence Weight:** 1 — Cell-level  
**Resource Control:** Knows upcoming raid targets  

**If Ignored (Personal):** Becomes hardened and loyal  
**If Ignored (Regional):** Raids become more surgical  

---

## Level 3 — Secrets

**Secret:** One disappearance was staged, not random  
**Leverage:** Knows meeting site between cult courier and bandit captain  
**If Cornered:** Breaks and bargains  

---

## Notes for Play

- Speaks quickly when nervous.  
- Flinches at cult symbols.  
- Not ideologically committed.
