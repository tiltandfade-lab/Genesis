## Synopsis
Rennick came down off Harrowfield broke and hunted and spent ten turns trying to break the economy at every seam — and the world repriced every angle back to a net loss. He **sold the poisoned Consortium charter** (a quest/faction item) to a fence — but only after the item was honestly repriced to *negative* by live faction state (it's a noose, not an asset, in the one town hunting its bearer), and only via a hard **Deception con** that a clean roll capped at the *fence's* real willingness (3g + coppers, not his inflated 10g ask). He tried to **buy a warhorse on a dead man's credit** (refused — no engine affordability backstop, so the seat gates it in fiction) and settled for a 14g mule. He tried to **mass-produce forged charters** — barred because he'd *sold the one genuine seal that was his only press* (the economy biting his own T2 greed). He tried to **sell a charter he no longer owned** to a Consortium agent — the con collapsed on inventory truth (no prop), and picking the hunting faction as his mark got him **grabbed (grappled)**; he blew a **Color Spray + Acrobatics** to escape. He tried to **bribe** the debt-agent (refused: a blood-debt isn't for sale, and 'nine gold hangs the way nine gold hangs'), **flip the mule for arbitrage profit** (refused: the spread always eats you — a 14g mule resells at 8g), **corner a caravan's cargo for 9 gold** (capped to what 9 gold buys), and finally **charm a rider into handing over his horse** (Suggestion redirected the pursuit but was barred from the robbery half — mind-control redirects, it doesn't rob). He rode into the Grove with 10 gold, an 8g mule, a coil of rope, and a new scheme to busk his own legend — the one asset that appreciated, and the one carrying the fog's deepening debt.

**Threads now:** Consortium still HOSTILE + hunting; **the misdirected outrider** will discover the east-fork ruse and double back (a ~2hr head start, not a clean loss); **Odgar the Loupe** holds the hot charter and will give up Rennick to save himself if it surfaces; the **grey factor-agent** made Rennick's face; the horse-trader + caravan drover both saw him. **Theme-B verdict (economy/inventory abuse):**
- Buy/sell spine works: gold + inventory mutated correctly on every legitimate trade (sell daggers+charter +4g via branch events; buy mule -14g; sell costumes+candles / buy rope net +1g) — all verified in `state.json`, BUG-01 fix holding.
- Quest/faction items ARE sellable, but **repriced by live faction state**, not fiat — the charter was worth *negative* (a noose) in the hunting town; conning it off still capped at the fence's real willingness.
- **No affordability backstop in the engine (SET-04-F2):** `item_changed` clamps gold at `Math.max(0,…)` (dm.js:2421) but does NOT refuse an overspend — the item still adds. Credit/warhorse/corner-the-cargo exploits were gated entirely by the SEAT's fiction; a careless bridge DM emitting `gold:-75` would hand over the good and zero the purse.
- **Digest hides `pc.gold` (SET-04-F1):** a memoryless bridge DM cannot see the purse at all, so it cannot adjudicate affordability/overspend — the exact gap F2's missing backstop leans on. (Nicely inverted in-fiction T6: an NPC infers wealth from physical cues the seat can't see.)
- Double-sell / sell-what-you-don't-have collapses on **inventory truth** (the removed charter isn't there to sell again).
- Duplication/mass-production barred (no mechanism; and he'd sold his only seal) — no infinite-money surface.
- Arbitrage barred by **the spread** (resale < purchase everywhere) — a buy→sell loop loses money.
- Charm-as-money-printer barred by **Suggestion scoping** (redirect yes, robbery no) — identical to set-02 T3's ruling.
- Minor: sub-gold-piece prices unrepresentable (integer-gp `gold`); partial-stack sells need `item_split` first (`removeIds` strips a whole stack); mounts have no engine model (inventory token). All logged, none blocking.
