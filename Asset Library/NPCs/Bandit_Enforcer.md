# Hestir Blackfen

*(Asset: bandit_enforcer_cult_adjacent)*

## Plot Vector

**Primary Pillar:** Bandits  
**Secondary Pillars:** Cult, Mere  

**Function:**  
Violent arm of cult-adjacent operations.  
Introduces ritual markings on raid sites.  
Pushes disappearances toward physical threat.

---

## Level 1 — Immediate Table Presence

**Race / Background:** Half-Orc; former dock brawler  
**Role:** Bandit lieutenant  

**Visual Quirk:** Wears swamp-twine around wrist  
**Mood:** Calm but predatory  
**Demeanor:** Smiles during conflict  

**Hook:** Hunting someone who “disrespected the water”  
**Talent:** Exceptional tracker in marsh terrain  
**Unrealized Potential:** No  

---

## Level 2 — Structural Pressure

**Formative Trauma:** Survived near-drowning in the marsh  
**Formative Grace:** Believes the swamp spared them  

**Want:** Prove devotion through strength  
**Fear:** Weakness  

**Faction Tie:** Coordinates with low-tier cult gatherings  
**Influence Weight:** 2 — Raider Cell  
**Resource Control:** Access to swamp hideouts  

**If Ignored (Personal):** Raids escalate  
**If Ignored (Regional):** Caravan disappearances increase  

---

## Level 3 — Secrets

**Secret:** Leaves cult markers at select raid scenes  
**Leverage:** Controls loyalty of several younger bandits  
**If Cornered:** Fights to the death

## Notes for Play

- Views violence as purification.  
- Uses cult language casually.  
- Treats death as necessary exchange.
