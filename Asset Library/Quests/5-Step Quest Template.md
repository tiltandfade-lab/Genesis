# Quest Template (5-Step Structure)

## 1. Objective (Hook + Stakes)
- What needs to be done?
- Why does it matter?
- What happens if it succeeds?
- What happens if it fails?
- Who communicates this (NPC, rumor, discovered situation)?

Clarity first. Players must understand the goal and consequences.

---

## 2. Initial Obstacle (Access Gate)
- What stands between the party and the core problem?
- Information barrier, travel hazard, minor threat, social negotiation, etc.
- Should reveal tone and stakes of the quest.

Purpose: Earn access to the real problem.

---

## 3. Core Challenge (The Engine Room)
- The central confrontation, puzzle, negotiation, heist, or combat.
- This defines the quest’s flavor.
- Design this first when creating a quest.
- Ask: “What’s the cool part?”

Everything else justifies this moment.

---

## 4. Revelation (Shift or Twist)
- New information that reframes:
  - The objective
  - The villain
  - The quest giver
  - The consequences
- May introduce a moral dilemma.
- May plant seeds for future arcs.

This prevents predictability and deepens engagement.

---

## 5. Final Confrontation (Resolution Under New Truth)
- The last meaningful obstacle.
- Often shaped by the revelation.
- Can be:
  - A boss fight
  - A moral decision
  - A betrayal
  - A timed escape
  - A negotiation

This step resolves the current quest while potentially escalating the wider world.
