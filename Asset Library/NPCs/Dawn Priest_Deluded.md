# NPC Asset – The Deluded Dawn Priest

## Name
(To be determined – leave flexible for reuse)

## Public Persona
- Soft-spoken, compassionate, visibly tired.
- Speaks in language of restoration, healing, and unity.
- Deeply affected by recent “miracle” performed by the party.
- Expresses urgency about weakening veil between living and dead.
- Frames quest as sacred responsibility.

---

## Private Belief System

### Core Conviction
“The separation between the living and the dead is not divine will — it is a wound.”

He is a [[Misguided Savior]]

He believes:
- The veil traps souls in unrest.
- The marsh is a place of unfinished passage.
- The recent miracle is proof that divine power is rising again.
- The ritual will restore balance, not unleash chaos.
- Sacrifice is necessary, but sacred.

He does **not** see himself as evil.
He sees himself as courageous.

---

## The Manipulation

### What He Has Experienced
- Repeated dreams of dawn light over black water.
- Visions of spirits smiling beyond a thin barrier.
- A voice speaking of “reunification.”
- Feelings of divine affirmation after the town miracle.

### What He Was Not Shown
- The chaos that follows boundary collapse.
- Predatory forces that exploit planar instability.
- The true nature of the power guiding him.

He is not lying about the ritual.
He is lying about his role within it.

---

## The Lie He Tells the Party

“The ward at the church responds only to living faith. I cannot break it alone. One who has touched divine power must stand within it.”

Technically true.
Intentionally incomplete.

---

## True Goal

The ritual requires:
- A living sacrifice.
- Divine potency (miracle-touched soul ideal).
- Completion inside the ritual chamber during a pulse.

He believes the sacrifice:
- Will not truly “die.”
- Will transcend.
- Will help restore cosmic balance.

---

## If Confronted

He says things like:
- “You have seen the power. You know it is rising.”
- “This is not destruction — it is reunification.”
- “The dead are not meant to be locked away.”
- “The dawn showed me this path.”
- “You were chosen.”

He fights with conviction, not malice.

---

## If Reduced to 0 HP

Options:
- Dies believing he was right.
- Lives and realizes he was manipulated.
- Survives but doubles down, blaming the party.
- Breaks down completely.

---

## Mechanical Frame (Level 2 Party Safe)

Base on Acolyte stat block (CR 1/4) with:
- Slightly increased HP.
- Ritual pulse effect granting temporary HP.
- One Skeleton ally.

Avoid overwhelming 2-PC party.
Let ritual environment create tension.

---

## Reusable Themes

- Religious reformer manipulated by higher force.
- Idealist corrupted by selective revelation.
- “Healing” framed as cosmic destabilization.
- Sacrifice justified as transcendence.
