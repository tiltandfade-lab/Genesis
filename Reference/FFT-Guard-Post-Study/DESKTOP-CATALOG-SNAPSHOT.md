# Genesis Golden Sites — Structure and Material Kit Catalog

This is the working catalog Adam and Codex are building one golden site at a time. It lives on the
Desktop so it survives the planned Genesis repository/worktree move.

This document is intentionally outside Git. It records discussion and evidence; it does not amend
the rolled tables, the Clay Proof Ladder, the art canon, or any build spec.

## Current working direction

- Begin with reusable, structure-first pieces in the retained Clayroom.
- Move to golden site 1, the Guard Post, only after the pieces that should be generic have passed
  the Clayroom bench.
- Preferred outcome: Genesis-native procedural geometry capable of FFT-grade composition.
- Working fallback: admitted, normalized, Genesis-material-treated Kenney geometry.
- A grey primitive is an honest Clayroom diagnostic or last-resort mechanics representation, but
  it is not the visual bar for the Guard Post.
- Build a material-kit catalog alongside the structure-kit catalog. Materials express canonical
  construction, culture, age, condition, and history; they never invent geometry or state.
- Most furnishings remain stable narrative/world facts with no physical model until architecture,
  portals, circulation, elevation, and tactical reservations prove the scene. A furnishing that
  affects collision, cover, sight, support, practical-light position, or exact interaction reach
  receives the smallest truthful proxy; there is no invisible tactical furniture.
- Make the first site mutation-aware without trying to build the whole mutation matrix: prove one
  universal Guard Post structure, its base realization, and one small cultural-construction A/B on the
  same canonical functional topology before spreading the pattern across all twelve sites.
- Adam selected **overgrown stone** as the first Guard Post material/history expression.
- Adam selected a **roadside post built into a hill shoulder** as the first retained golden
  composition.
- Every scene decision must replay mathematically from canonical inputs, stable ids, named seeded
  streams, and pinned compiler versions. The golden scene is a retained ordinary generator output,
  never a hand-authored map or seed-specific branch.
- Prefer tables for meaningful alternatives and describable world facts. Use a bounded engine
  roller for coordinates, candidate construction, validation, and geometric realization. The
  renderer consumes the committed plan and does not become a second roller.
- Use Kenney as a semantic-interface scaffold, topology donor, and comparison benchmark; begin the
  Genesis procedural shape language immediately for the architecture that owns the visible
  silhouette.
- Settle culturally expressive roof, opening, masonry, threshold, and repair choices from coherent
  visual reference lanes, not from terminology alone and not by mixing unrelated monuments into an
  architectural moodboard.
- Express the Guard Post in the very-low-poly construction language of an FFT map: a few broad
  elevation masses, a geometric road ribbon, continuous wall/retaining runs, deep openings, broad
  roof planes, and relational rock clusters. Masonry units, grain, joints, and quiet age belong to
  materials rather than modeled micro-geometry.
- Material Maker 1.3 is the pinned material-authoring source. Every material begins as a saved,
  versioned seed graph with reusable custom nodes, exposed parameters/configurations, export
  receipts, and explicit parentage for future mutations; no admitted graph is a throwaway.

The preference for Genesis-native geometry over Kenney is a working direction from the 2026-07-23
conversation. It should be folded into the appropriate repository authority only after Adam treats
it as a binding ruling and the repository move is complete.

## Procedural reproducibility and ownership contract

Adam's 2026-07-23 ruling: these scenes must be constructible by the walk engine; every decision must
be mathematically reproducible; anything that can usefully remain table-authored should be kept in
tables. Procedural-renderer implementation does not need to read like table prose, but the design
must explicitly consider whether each decision belongs in table prose, structured grammar data, an
engine roller, or projection.

### The four ownership layers

| Layer | Owns | Does not own |
|---|---|---|
| Canonical roll tables | Meaningful alternatives, nouns, purpose, condition, history, topology/elevation intent, and facts a DM or player could truthfully describe | Coordinates, solver tactics, mesh details, or arbitrary decorative counts |
| Structured grammar tables/config | Typed tags, ranges, weights, compatibility rules, profiles, sockets, material-role mappings, and named fallback order | A second narrative authority or seed-specific authored layouts |
| Walk/composition engine roller | Seeded selection, candidate construction, coordinates, dimensions within licensed bounds, tier assignment, route graph, validation, scoring, relaxation, and receipts | New canonical nouns, unlicensed topology, free prose, or renderer-only facts |
| Renderer/material projection | Deterministic mesh emission, UV/material projection, LOD, cutaway, visual microvariation keyed to stable ids, and presentation fallback | Independent random rolls, gameplay geometry, routes, elevations, conditions, or object existence |

The test for table authorship is not “could this technically be hard-coded in data?” It is:

- Does it name a meaningful alternative?
- Could a DM describe it without exposing implementation machinery?
- Could it change tactics, history, culture, construction, condition, or player expectations?
- Would designers benefit from adding/reweighting rows without editing solver code?

If yes, prefer a row with human-readable prose plus a typed payload. If the choice is merely where
within a legal region a rock lands, which of several equal candidates scores best, or how a run is
tessellated, it belongs to the engine.

### Seed and replay law

Every compiled site must carry:

```text
world seed
walk id and node/site id
all rolled row ids and typed payloads
site/composition seed
namespaced RNG-stream ids
walk/table/grammar/compiler/material versions
selected candidate id
rejection, relaxation, and fallback receipts
committed TacticalCompositionPlan and SurfaceAssemblyPlan ids
```

Use namespaced random streams such as `envelope`, `elevation`, `primary-route`, `secondary-route`,
`structure`, `rocks`, `vegetation`, `furnishing`, and `condition`. Adding a grass variant must not
move the road or rebuild the post.

For a fixed input capsule and pinned versions, generation must produce byte-equivalent canonical
plans. Renderer captures may differ only where the declared platform/presentation contract permits
it; no difference may alter canonical geometry or mechanics. Once a generated site becomes
persistent world canon, store its commitment capsule and committed plans so later code changes do
not silently restyle, reroll, or erase it.

### Golden-scene prohibition

The golden Guard Post may use a deliberately retained seed, but:

- no code may test for that seed, walk id, or fixture id to produce special geometry;
- no hand-placed scene data may bypass ordinary table, grammar, composition, and material paths;
- every visible major mass must have a source fact or a named derived-support rule;
- aesthetic improvement must change reusable rows, grammar parameters, constraints, scoring,
  profiles, or renderer rules—not patch the golden map;
- changed-seed and adversarial fixtures must accompany the hero seed so one lucky composition
  cannot certify the generator.

## Taste-rule foundry

Tasteful reusable rules are built by converting observed composition into typed relationships,
testing those relationships without finished art, and retaining both successes and failures. Taste
cannot be reduced to one opaque beauty score; the solver should expose a small set of named goals
and penalties that Adam can judge independently.

### 1. Measure references as relationships, not copied layouts

For each FFT study map, annotate:

- stage envelope, composed edge, and quiet negative-space masses;
- primary route and any materially distinct secondary route;
- elevation regions, signed deltas, connectors, and broad-versus-noisy distribution;
- dominant, secondary, and tertiary masses;
- building/road/terrain intersections;
- landmark, threshold, deployment, refuge, exposure, flank, and retreat relations;
- rock, vegetation, wall, and prop clustering;
- fixed-camera silhouette, focal hierarchy, and occlusion behavior.

Record observed measurements, hypotheses, and Adam's taste rulings separately. A relationship seen
once is a candidate; a repeated pattern is evidence; neither becomes a Genesis rule until a clay
test shows that it improves generated compositions.

### 2. Author composition archetypes in tables

Tables should name a small meaningful family rather than prescribe a cell layout. Provisional
Guard Post examples include:

- `shoulder-overlook`: road passes below a post embedded in the uphill shoulder;
- `switchback-watch`: the post controls a road turn and sees two approach legs;
- `road-cut-checkpoint`: terrain or rock narrows the road beside the post;
- `terrace-gate`: the road climbs onto a guarded working shelf before continuing.

Each row should have readable prose plus typed obligations such as route relation, signed elevation
intent, landmark relation, allowed connector families, secondary-opportunity license, and fallback
order. Exact coordinates remain an engine decision.

### 3. Expand each archetype through a relational grammar

Every recipe should distinguish:

- **hard invariants** — must pass: connectivity, legal footing, licensed topology, working
  threshold, usable observation, honest retreat, and no inaccessible required object;
- **soft taste goals** — ranked but relaxable: coherent broad terraces, a readable dominant mass,
  road/post interlock, a useful height silhouette, clustered rocks, protected quiet grass, and
  strong fixed-camera readability;
- **anti-rules** — named failure patterns: cell-noise elevation, ruler-straight orphan road,
  building dropped on a flat pad, rock confetti, equal-weight focal masses, decorative stairs,
  blocked doors, and foreground occlusion of the guarded threshold;
- **diversity guards** — prevent one winning template from consuming the generator: vary entry/exit
  faces, handedness, shoulder direction, archetype, tier count, road curvature, post orientation,
  and secondary opportunity through independent legal bands.

### 4. Prove rules in clay before material polish

Generate a contact sheet of many seeds using only:

- flat terrain-role colors;
- numbered elevation bands;
- road and connector overlays;
- post/landmark masses;
- reservation and route diagnostics;
- the fixed production camera.

If a composition does not read in clay, materials, vegetation, and lighting must not rescue it.
Material passes begin only after the underlying hierarchy and route/elevation relations survive.

### 5. Turn Adam's judgments into reusable causes

For every accepted or rejected seed, record a reason with a named diagnostic:

- “lovely” is not a usable rule;
- “the post and road feel causally interlocked because the post occupies the inside shoulder above
  the bend” is;
- “bad” is not a usable anti-rule;
- “three equal terraces create no dominant elevation mass” is.

Fix the reusable cause by changing a table row, grammar obligation, parameter band, constraint,
named score, penalty, relaxation, or renderer rule. Never fix only the reviewed seed. Retain rejected
seeds as regression fixtures.

### 6. Use transparent candidate comparison

A candidate report should expose separate measures rather than one magic number:

```text
hard legality
road/post control relation
elevation coherence
route distinction
focal hierarchy
negative-space quality
rock/vegetation clustering
tactical opportunity
camera visibility
complexity/budget cost
relaxations used
```

Weights may rank already-legal candidates, but no weighted sum may cancel a hard failure. Adam's
visual review calibrates these measures; it does not get replaced by them.

### 7. Promote cautiously

A taste rule becomes reusable only after it:

1. improves the retained hero seed without a special case;
2. improves or preserves a changed-seed contact sheet;
3. survives adversarial envelopes and table combinations;
4. does not collapse legitimate variety;
5. has a deterministic fallback;
6. remains truthful under clay, narrative-fact/cutaway, cultural, material, and gameplay views.

## Catalog vocabulary

### Structure kit

A reusable geometric family or deterministic assembly used by the scene compiler: floor mass,
wall run, doorway, stair, column, roof, parapet, bridge, and so on. A structure kit item is not a
finished room.

### Material kit

A coordinated semantic family across surface roles. A material kit should normally provide some
combination of floor/ground, wall, riser/retaining, trim/edge, wood/support, metal/hardware,
roof/deck, and history/condition treatments.

### Furnishing assembly

A functional recipe with required and optional roles, members, relations, sockets, footprints,
working clearances, access faces, and lawful substitutions. It is not a bag of props.

### Realization ladder

1. `GENESIS_PROCEDURAL` — preferred custom procedural profile, mesh, or deterministic assembly.
2. `KENNEY_MODULATED` — normalized donor geometry with Genesis sockets, scale, materials, and
   condition treatment.
3. `CLAY_PRIMITIVE` — truthful geometry used for admission, diagnostics, or missing coverage.
4. `MARKER_OR_CARD` — truth-preserving representation when physical realization is not yet earned.

The same semantic slot and object id must survive every rung.

### Status labels

- `LIVE` — present in the current production path.
- `CLAY-NOW` — should be proved in the retained Clayroom before Guard Post work depends on it.
- `CLAY-LATER` — belongs on the extended C1H/C1I Clayroom bench, not the current C1A truth fixture.
- `GUARD-1` — first needed by the Guard Post recipe.
- `CANDIDATE` — source exists but is not necessarily normalized, admitted, or wired.
- `DEFER` — does not need to block Guard Post 1.
- `QUESTION` — Adam's taste or scope decision is required.

## Clayroom admission gate for every reusable piece

A structure family is ready to leave Clayroom only when:

1. It snaps to the canonical 5-foot grid or declares an honest sub-cell footprint.
2. Its geometry recipe is deterministic and records version, seed, source, and fallback.
3. Normals, winding, caps, thickness, ground contact, bounds, and mount sockets are correct.
4. Straight joins, corners, endpoints, and transitions have explicit ownership.
5. It reads at gameplay scale under the fixed production camera.
6. It survives the warm/cool opposing light rig and low ambient without relying on baked lighting.
7. It does not hide grid truth, portal access, circulation, or required interaction aprons.
8. Cutaway/ghosting preserves its necessary silhouette and mechanics.
9. The custom piece and Kenney fallback occupy the same semantic slot and do not change mechanics.
10. A negative control proves the feature is load-bearing: wrong axis, missing socket, broken join,
    incorrect scale, or wrong surface role must visibly or mechanically fail the gate.

A material family is ready to leave Clayroom only when:

1. Native tile, 3×3 repeat, lit slab, and sprite-context taste cards pass.
2. It is quiet enough that the standee remains the star.
3. Physical scale is declared and consistent across floor, wall, riser, and trim.
4. Albedo is readable unlit; normal response adds form without sparkle or fake relief.
5. Corners, runs, endpoints, and trim bands do not reveal packing or repeat seams.
6. Missing normal/ORM/trim art falls back to the same canonical base material without changing the
   site identity.
7. Condition variants are selected by canonical state and remain deterministic.
8. Gate-machine frame, draw, and memory measurements are recorded.

# Part II — Golden Site 1: Guard Post

## Accepted functional definition

The first golden site is a **small operating, externally supplied Guard Post**. It is not yet a
fixed medieval tower, gatehouse, barracks, checkpoint, or wall post.

The first retained guard-room assembly must provide:

- a guard workstation and/or observation **narrative role**;
- protected approach, portal, and circulation truth;
- only the rest, storage, light, or supply support actually licensed by the selected room;
- explicit required and optional roles;
- room-relative zones, support roles, access faces, and working clearances;
- one container/holding allocation as a canonical fact;
- one aggregate search surface;
- a promotion socket and lawful minimal proxy only for any role that becomes spatially or
  tactically material;
- separate reporting of function, occupant/history evidence, tactics, and optional dressing.

## Recommended first-slice decisions

These are the recommended working dispositions from the 2026-07-23 discussion. Overgrown stone is
Adam's selection; the phasing, first form, and geometry strategy remain working recommendations
until Adam confirms them.

### GP-D01 — Mutation-aware vertical slice

Do not complete a culture-neutral/default-Fantasy version of all twelve sites before testing
cultural expression. That would risk baking supposedly neutral Fantasy assumptions into wall
profiles, apertures, proportions, support rhythms, roof edges, and repair grammar. Do not expand
the complete realm/culture matrix first either; that would multiply an unproved scene grammar.

Use this sequence:

1. Prove the universal structural and gameplay obligations in Clayroom.
2. Compile the simplest base Guard Post.
3. Apply its overgrown-stone construction/history expression.
4. Compile the locked Institutional Frontier Works versus Upland Vernacular Station cultural
   construction mutation on the **same canonical layout and
   functional program**.
5. Record which mutation hooks must be structural, material, generated-component, decal, prop, or
   dressing inputs.
6. Carry the learned interface into site 2, revisiting breadth only after multiple site types have
   tested it.

The first mutation proof should specifically test whether the grammar exposes:

- massing profile, wall thickness/batter, and corner/end language;
- opening shape, ratio, depth, frame, shutter, and lintel language;
- support spacing, construction rhythm, roof/eave/cap language, and motif/trim sockets;
- builder, occupier, and repairer as separable chronological layers;
- material-role slots and deterministic condition coverage without changing gameplay truth.

### GP-D02 — Simplest first form

Start with a **single attached/interior guard room checkpoint**: one rectangular open-top cutaway
room, one controlled door, one observation opening, and one narrative workstation/storage zone.
This is the
smallest useful step from the retained C1A 5×5 room and the first C1J guard-room assembly. It avoids
premature roof, full exterior-shell, and vertical-circulation problems while still testing guard
function rather than merely furnishing a generic chamber.

Explore larger silhouettes in this order:

1. attached/interior guard room;
2. wall or gate checkpoint with an exterior threshold;
3. freestanding roadside post with full weather shell and roof;
4. small tower with stairs, vertical sightlines, roof, and cutaway logic.

### GP-D03 — First material/history expression

The first family is **maintained overgrown stone**, not an abandoned ruin:

- old or reclaimed stone owns the primary floor, wall, riser, sill, and cap roles;
- moss, lichen, damp staining, root pressure, and small vegetation occupy crevices, shaded edges,
  endpoints, and neglected exterior bands;
- the operating post keeps the door swing, threshold, patrol path, observation station, drainage,
  and working clearances visibly usable;
- recent timber and forged-iron repairs may reveal the current externally supplied occupant;
- heavy collapse, blocked access, roof failure, or total plant capture belongs to a dormant or
  abandoned-site permutation, not Guard Post 1.

This distinguishes construction/history from culture. Overgrown stone alone must not be treated as
a cultural or realm identity.

### GP-D04 — Kenney bridge and Genesis shape language

Kenney is fruitful for learning the **interface**: grid increments, scale, pivots, sockets, joins,
end/corner/opening states, collision, variant families, fallbacks, performance, and deterministic
replacement. It will not teach Genesis its own silhouette or visual language, and prolonged use
would encourage the compiler to overfit a donor pack's modular grammar.

Use a deliberately short bridge:

1. Keep one normalized Kenney example per needed semantic family as a working reference and
   fallback.
2. Build the first Genesis procedural wall-run grammar immediately, deriving straight runs,
   corners, ends, openings, half-height edges, sill/cap treatment, and condition masks from a shared
   profile rather than authoring another finite tile pack.
3. Switch the same scene seed and semantic ids between Kenney and Genesis suppliers for comparison.
4. Let Genesis procedural geometry own continuous, high-silhouette architecture: floors, walls,
   risers, parapets, and roof masses.
5. Use Kenney longer for discrete or topology-heavy coverage—doors, stairs, brackets, furnishings,
   and emergency fallback—until each replacement earns admission.

Short version: **Kenney teaches the replacement contract, not the style.**

### GP-D05 — FFT-like terrain composition as the golden visual target

Adam wants the first retained Guard Post to feel like an FFT battle map: a composed diorama with
lovely elevation changes, a road, grass, rocks, and architecture that grows out of the terrain.
On 2026-07-23 Adam selected a **roadside post built into a hill shoulder** as the first golden
composition.
This changes the distinction between the first **component proof** and the first **golden frame**:

- the attached/interior guard room remains the easiest Clayroom, RoomProgram, and cutaway proof;
- it is not sufficient as the retained golden-site composition;
- the first golden frame should be a compact roadside or gate-side hybrid with the room embedded
  into a larger terrain composition.

The first composition should test:

- a road that enters low, changes direction and/or elevation, and clearly explains why the post is
  placed there;
- at least three readable height bands: approach, guarded working terrace, and lookout/terrain
  crown, with slopes, steps, retaining edges, or short stairs connecting them;
- overgrown-stone architecture interlocked with the terrain rather than dropped onto a flat pad;
- grass as shaped ground masses and quiet negative space, not a uniform carpet;
- rocks as elevation anchors, boundary shapers, cover, or route guidance, not random scatter;
- one dominant guarded threshold, at least one secondary tactical route or flank, and readable
  standee footing throughout;
- a compact diorama silhouette, composed map edges, selective cutaway/occlusion, and a clear camera
  hero angle.

Elevation, road, grass, and rock placement must remain projections of canonical topology and
terrain facts. “FFT-like” is a compositional and shape-language target, not permission to copy a
specific copyrighted level or import FFT art into the shipped game.
