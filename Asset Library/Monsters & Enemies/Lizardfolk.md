### Lizardfolk Geomancer

_Medium Elemental, Neutral_

- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Initiative:** +0 (10)
- **Hit Points:** 78 (12d8 + 6)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., Burrow 20 ft., Swim 30 ft.
- **STR:** 15 (+2) | **DEX:** 10 (+0) | **CON:** 13 (+1) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 15 (+2) | **CHA:** 8 (−1)
- **Skills:** Nature +2, Perception +4, Stealth +4
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
- **Languages:** Draconic, Primordial (Terran)
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Actions**

- **Multiattack.** The lizardfolk makes two Earth Burst attacks.
- **Earth Burst.** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. Hit: 9 (2d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage, and if the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.
- **Spellcasting.** The lizardfolk casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12). *At Will:* Elementalism. *1/Day Each:* Meld into Stone, Speak with Plants, Spike Growth.

### Lizardfolk Sovereign

_Medium Elemental, Neutral_

- **Armor Class:** 15
- **Initiative:** +1 (11)
- **Hit Points:** 78 (12d8 + 24)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., Swim 30 ft.
- **STR:** 17 (+3) | **DEX:** 12 (+1) | **CON:** 15 (+2) | **INT:** 11 (+0) | **WIS:** 11 (+0) | **CHA:** 15 (+2)
- **Skills:** Perception +4, Stealth +5
- **Immunities:** Frightened
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
- **Languages:** Draconic, Primordial (Terran)
- **Challenge:** 4 (1,100 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Actions**

- **Multiattack.** The lizardfolk makes one Bite attack and one Earthen Maul attack.
- **Bite.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 8 (1d10 + 3) Piercing damage. If the target is not a Construct or an Undead, the lizardfolk gains Temporary Hit Points equal to the damage dealt.
- **Earthen Maul.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.

**Bonus Actions**

- **Charge.** The lizardfolk moves up to its Speed or Swim Speed straight toward an enemy it can see.
