> roll THIS table; legacy Outlandish d300 stays the anachronism-intrusion grab-bag. Register per
> J3b: Cosmic/weird — scale that doesn't fit in a sentence; the wonder that answers back. Every
> item names its FRAME (existing `data/items.js` id); none invented. **Band shares run hotter
> than H2's baseline by construction** (see Frontier's frontmatter) — this realm's whole register
> leans Strange/Volatile by nature, so the mundane floor is deliberately kept plain and quiet by
> contrast (grounded objects from a place that otherwise doesn't sound like it should have any).

> signature wonders. Rank ladders sit on enchanted + signature rows; reality-breaking rungs floor
> at L9. d50, Commitment ceiling.
