## 30-Second Run

The highland hills are visually repetitive and magnetically wrong — the party has been wandering in circles. The geometry funnels them into a narrow wooded valley with a 5-ft exit flanked by two 20-ft stone menhirs. The ground is covered in wet autumn leaves that radiate strange heat. When the party hits the center of the funnel, arachnid beasts erupt from under the leaves and start grappling casters. Sylas the Weaver steps out from behind the menhirs, casts *Entangle*, and returns to Total Cover. The entire design is hit-and-drag until the party breaks through or retreats.

---

## Setup

**Space:** 20-ft wide path narrowing to 5-ft exit over 40 ft, bounded by impassable dense tree trunks. Twin 20-ft stone menhirs flank the narrow exit — Total Cover for anyone behind them. A 10×10 center patch is the spider ambush point.
**Atmosphere:** Bright daylight, stagnant hazy air. The ground is entirely Difficult Terrain (wet autumn leaves). Unnaturally warm underfoot. Stealth checks in the area: +2 DC (leaves crunch).
**NPCs Present:** Sylas the Weaver (behind menhirs, spellcaster), 1d4 Hulking Trapdoor Spiders (buried in center patch).
**If Ignored:** Navigation hindrance continues — compasses spin, haze thickens, trade routes swallowed. Other travelers trapped.

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Funnel Ambush

The moment the party hits the 10×10 center patch, the spiders emerge. Sylas acts on round 1 — casts *Entangle* on the center patch, returns to Total Cover.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 15 Perception:** Spot the shifting leaves before the spiders emerge — party is not Surprised.
- **DC 14 Survival:** Recognize that the magnetic anomaly and warm ground are tied to the menhirs (not the leaves).

**Spiders' Tactic — Hit and Drag:**
- Round 1: Spider uses its first attack to Grapple (DC 12 Str or Dex save). If successful, uses remaining movement to drag the target toward the tree line.
- Round 2+: While grappling, spider bites the restrained target (Disadvantage on attacks against non-grappler). Drags 15 ft per round into the brush.
- Target dragged into impassable brush: Total Cover from allies. Still attackable by the grappling spider.

**Sylas's Tactic:**
- Round 1: Casts *Entangle* (DC 12 Str save or Restrained in the center patch), steps back behind the menhirs.
- Round 2: Casts *Barkskin* on himself if the PCs break through.
- Round 3+: *Produce Flame* from behind cover — steps out, throws, steps back. If melee reaches him: *Thunderwave* to push them back into the reeds.
- Retreats (casts *Longstrider* and flees) if pets are killed or he drops to half HP.

---

### Segment 2: Magnetic Fog (if the party stalls)

If the PCs spend 10+ minutes investigating or trying to map the area without magical navigation:

Compasses spin wildly. Haze thickens to 20-ft visibility. Spiders advance from the center patch early (no Perception check to avoid Surprised). Add +1 spider immediately.

## Mechanics

1. **Difficult Terrain:** Entire funnel area. Moving 1 ft costs 2 ft of speed.
2. **Entangle (Sylas, round 1):** DC 12 Str save or Restrained. Area: 20×20 ft (centered on the patch). Concentration — attacking Sylas ends it.
3. **Grapple (Spiders):** DC 12 Str or Dex save. Grappled: Speed = 0, Disadvantage on attacks vs. non-grappler.
4. **Menhir Cover:** Any creature directly behind a menhir has Total Cover from the opposite direction. Sylas exploits this constantly.
5. **Lodestone Amulet (Sylas drops it):** Removing from the area lifts the magnetic anomaly — regional navigation checks return to normal.

## Difficulty Dial

**Tier 1 (Lvl 1–4):** As written. Sylas = Druid (CR 2). Spiders = re-flavored Apes (CR 1/2). 1d4 spiders.
**Tier 2 (Lvl 5–10):** Sylas = Mage (CR 6) with *Stinking Cloud*, *Sleet Storm*, *Counterspell*. Spiders = 1d4+1 Phase Spiders (CR 3) using *Ethereal Jaunt* to bypass the frontline. Warm leaves emit toxic spores when hit by fire or thunder — DC 12 Con save or Poisoned 1 min.
**Tier 3 (Lvl 11–16):** Sylas = Archdruid (CR 12) with *Wall of Thorns* closing the 5-ft exit. Spiders = 2–3 Driders (CR 6) clinging to the menhirs and tree canopy. Teleportation magic fails on a d6 roll of 1.
**Tier 4 (Lvl 17–20):** Sylas = Lich (CR 21). Spiders = 1–2 Nightwalkers (CR 20). Menhirs act as Lair Action nodes. Failure to defeat Sylas within 3 rounds reverses gravity in the funnel — DC 20 Str save or launched into a hostile extraplanar location.

## Stat Blocks

**Sylas the Weaver** — *Medium Humanoid (Druid), Neutral Evil* — CR 2
AC 11 (16 with *Barkskin*) | HP 27 | Speed 30 ft.
Skills: Medicine +4, Nature +3, Perception +4
**Quarterstaff.** +2 to hit (+4 with *Shillelagh*), 1d8 bludgeoning.
Spells (DC 12, +4): At will — *Druidcraft, Produce Flame, Shillelagh*. 1st (4) — *Entangle, Longstrider, Thunderwave*. 2nd (3) — *Barkskin*.
**Tactic:** Round 1: *Entangle* the center patch. Round 2: *Barkskin*. Round 3+: *Produce Flame* from cover. If cornered: *Thunderwave* to push back. Retreats at half HP or when all pets dead.

**Hulking Trapdoor Spider (×1d4)** — *Medium Beast (re-flavored Black Bear)* — CR 1/2
AC 11 | HP 19 | Speed 40 ft., Climb 30 ft.
Skills: Perception +3, Stealth +4
**Bite.** +3, 1d6+2 piercing.
**Claws.** +3, 2d4+2 slashing.
**Grapple (replaces one Multiattack strike):** DC 12 Str/Dex save or Grappled.
**Tactic:** Emerges from center patch when party enters. Grapples casters first, drags them 15 ft/round toward the impassable brush.
