#urban-pressure
> **The city as a whole exerts a mood** — a single-roll citywide-pressure oracle for *slow urban play*, when the party is in a town with no active objective and isn't closing on anything. Surfaces something the city is *doing* that the party can ignore, get pulled into, or exploit. Distinct from the `[[Urban Encounter v2.5]]` node generator (which builds one specific scene) — this is ambient, citywide, single-roll. Climbs from ordinary civic friction → organized faction tension → uncanny mood → open crisis → the city breaking its own rules. Spice-graded d100, Commitment ceiling (66 Grounded · 20 Textured · 9 Strange · 4 Volatile · 1 Mythic). New table 2026-06-23 (table-improvement pass) — fills the slot freed when the old `urban-encounters` tavern table was renamed `[[Tavern Encounters]]`. *(Natural home for the Charter's `§10.2` In-Media-Res escalation — the world acting when the player drags.)*
