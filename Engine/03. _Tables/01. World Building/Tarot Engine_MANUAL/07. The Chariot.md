### **VII. The Chariot: Trigger Effects**

The Chariot represents victory, willpower, and the mastery of opposing forces. It is the card of momentum—where the mind commands the physical world to move at its whim. Under this card, the campaign becomes a race; speed is rewarded, and hesitation is a death sentence.

---

**The Constant: "Unstoppable Momentum"**

Victory is achieved through aggressive forward motion.

- **The Effect:** If a creature moves at least 20 feet in a straight line during their turn, they gain a **+2 bonus** to the first attack roll or spell save DC they make that turn.
    
- **The Toll:** If a creature does not move at least 10 feet on their turn, they take a **-2 penalty** to their AC and Dexterity saving throws until the start of their next turn as the "stagnation" of the Chariot weighs them down.

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

**The Constant: "The Blitzkrieg Strategy"**

The faction has abandoned fortification in favor of relentless, high-speed expansion. They are not conquering territory so much as they are "passing through" it and leaving destruction in their wake.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**Faction Behavior (The Chariot)**|**Narrative Implication**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Iron Caravan**|The faction’s base is no longer a castle, but a massive, moving caravan of wagons that never stops.|
|**6-9**|Less Grounded|**The Cavalry Charge**|The faction has replaced all infantry with mounted units. They refuse to negotiate with anyone who isn't on a horse.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Great Race**|The faction believes the world is ending and they are racing to be the first to reach the "Finish Line" at the edge of the world.|

### **Trigger: Accepting Binding Agreements**

**The Constant: "The Driver’s Debt"**

The bargain grants you incredible willpower and speed, but you lose the ability to rest. You can never stay in the same town for more than 24 hours.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**The Bargain's Mutation (The Chariot)**|**The "Gift"**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**Will of Iron**|You gain a +5 bonus to all saving throws against being Charmed or Frightened, but you have -5 to all Initiative rolls.|
|**6-9**|Less Grounded|**The Ghostly Chariot**|You can summon a spectral chariot to travel at 100mph for 8 hours. The cost is one "Memory of Victory."|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Wheels of Fate**|Your legs are replaced by magical wheels. Your speed is 60ft, and you gain a "climb" speed on vertical surfaces.|

Distance shrinks. The world enters an era where travel becomes effortless, but borders become meaningless, leading to a state of perpetual migration.
