# Realm plot items — parked pointers

Extracted during the loot re-author. Each is a quest-item CONCEPT: it points at people,
places, or history and therefore bills the DM for a payoff. The plot-item unit gives the
survivors real wiring (thread capture on mint, defined payoffs); the rest die here honorably.

## Ash (extracted 2026-07-03)

- **The laminated family photo** — someone protected it before anything else; someone else might recognize the faces.
- **A settlement ration card**, three punches used — redeemable where issued, if they honor it.
- **A toll receipt for an impassable route** — proof you've been somewhere dangerous.
- **Council meeting minutes** naming six leaders, two now dead.
- **The rationing ledger** that doesn't match the harvest reports — somebody's skimming, and there's a name attached.
- **A raider's kill-tally pipe**, notches recent — its owner is feared or about to be hunted.
- **A water-rights contract signed under duress** — one well's been dry since.
- **Antibiotics prescribed for someone specific** — recovering or dead; findable either way.
- **The recruitment poster** promising more than the settlement could deliver — recruiting for something unadvertised.
- **The final newspaper edition**, headline half-legible — an ending nobody alive was told.
- **The confiscation log** for an item no longer in the locker — someone took it back out.
- **A retired raider's confession** naming an unacknowledged massacre — survivors still remember.
- **The founder's original charter** — terms that don't match how the settlement runs now.
- **The Vault Ledger** (was enchanted r44) — answers one regional-history question per rest in your own handwriting; whatever answers isn't the person holding the pen. *Strong plot-item candidate: a doer that generates pointers.*
- **The master rain-ledger** (was Mythic option 2) — one future year's column already filled in, wet ink.
- **The founder's stone** (was Mythic option 3) — carved with the town's true count, living and owed-dead alike.
- *(Location-not-item strays from the same pass: the never-dry well, the fenced soil that crops out of season — these are place-gen material, not items at all.)*
