# Caelum Virel

*(Asset: laborer_information_cultnode)*

## Level 1 — Immediate Table Presence

**Race / Background:** Half-Elf; Apprentice wizard studying tidal anomalies (operating under labor cover)  
**Role:** Porter / Dock Laborer  

**Visual Quirk:** Sleeves perpetually stained with crushed herbs and alchemical residue  
**Mood:** Calm in a way that feels deliberate and guarded  
**Demeanor:** Repeats others’ words back slowly before responding  
**Hook:** Needs protection for one night  
**Talent:** Skilled climber  
**Unrealized Potential:** Yes  

---

## Level 2 — Structural Pressure

**Formative Trauma:** Deeply inspired by a teacher  
**Formative Grace:** Granted lawful asylum or protection  

**Want:** Secure reliable income or resources  
**Fear:** Scarcity (hunger, tools, shelter)  

**Faction Tie:** Cult Tier 3 — embedded influence node  
**Influence Weight:** 4 — Town-Level (can shift policy, trade flow, militia posture, or public opinion)  
**Resource Control:** Information about recent travelers  

**If Ignored (Personal):** Deflects suspicion successfully  
**If Ignored (Regional):** coastal mountains caravan routes shift significantly  

---

## Level 3 — Secrets

**Secret:** Hides a small addiction  
**Leverage:** Controls access to a meeting space  
**If Cornered:** Moves immediately to secure key resources  

---

## Potential Quest / Adventure Tie-Ins

- Protection job escalates into exposure of a rival faction seeking access to caravan records.  
- Discovery that caravan route shifts are intentionally engineered to create artificial scarcity.  
- His former teacher resurfaces (or is revealed to be missing), tied to tidal anomaly research.  
- Meeting space becomes nexus for cult Tier 3 coordination.  
- Addiction source traces back to cult supply chain or arcane reagent.  

---

## Notes for Play

- Speaks slowly. Mirrors phrases. Buys time before answering.  
- Avoids direct lies; prefers omission and redirection.  
- Keeps mental ledger of who asks about trade, routes, or travelers.  
- Visibly uncomfortable when scarcity or supply is mentioned.  
- If threatened, immediately protects documents, logs, reagents, or meeting access — not himself.
