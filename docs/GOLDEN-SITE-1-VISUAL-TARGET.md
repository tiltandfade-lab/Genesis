# Golden Site 1 Visual Target

Status: **NONCANONICAL ART-DIRECTION TARGET**  
Asset:
`Reference/Golden-Site-1-Visual-Target/guard-post-fft-ts-genesis-target-v001.png`

This image is a tangible quality and composition target for the procedural Guard Post family. It
is not a site plan, a canon culture, a mechanics definition, or permission to copy its incidental
geometry into every seed.

## Binding takeaways

1. Natural terrain uses continuous large forms. Neighboring tactical surfaces may change angle,
   but they respond to one another as hills, shoulders, dips, switchbacks, and drainage—not as
   independent random tile pitches.
2. Constructed forms remain more planar and modular than the land. The beauty comes from the
   contrast between smooth/faceted terrain and deliberately chunky construction.
3. Height is compositional. The road climbs through several readable elevation bands and the
   tallest mass sits at the far side of the primary camera.
4. Routes read before detail: controlled road, switchback, terrain flank, deck access, and retreat
   remain visually separable.
5. Material rhythm is authored and pixel-visible. Large clusters carry the surface; fine noise
   does not.
6. Defensive masonry uses five one-foot courses per five-foot tactical rise. Wall beauty comes
   from designed base, aperture, repair, corner, cap, and storey modules rather than scale-stretched
   generic texture.
7. Condition is causal and strong: grime begins at actual earth/foundation contact; moss follows
   wet edges and the drain; ivy wraps a true corner as one rooted mass.
8. Culture reads through attachment practice, corner language, roof treatment, carving, and
   world-truth-controlled signifiers—not roof hue alone.
9. The diorama closes down to one flat tray plane. Its cut faces are presentation geometry and do
   not invent walkable terrain.
10. Quarter-turn composition is assumed. Tall elements need useful readings from all four bearings
    and should avoid occupying the near-camera edge by default.

## Inspirational, not binding

- exact building count, footprints, tower silhouette, banner emblem, tree positions, rock shapes,
  path bends, and repair location;
- the apparent culture, occupation state, heraldry, and character identities;
- the quantity of exposed rock. Production terrain may be softer, earthier, or more vegetated;
- the exact lighting direction and time of day;
- every decorative object shown by ImageGen.

## Known distance from the engine

- The concept has a denser vegetation and rock budget than the current Guard Post renderer.
- Its authored pixels are cleaner and more coherent across material boundaries than the current
  candidate library.
- The repair, drain staining, corner growth, cap language, and storey rhythm are more legible than
  the current runtime modules.
- The current procedural wall compositor has correct physical scale and deterministic placement,
  but its quiet motifs remain too much like isolated chips/dashes. They need authored families with
  larger compositional shapes.
- The concept suggests more architectural hierarchy than the current compact guardroom family
  mutations have yet proven.

Refine the preceding generated Guard Post while preserving its isometric composition, route
hierarchy, far-side tower, switchback, retaining wall, gate, guardhouse, three small units, and

stairs, soft filtering, text, UI, logos, and watermarks.
