# FICTION ORACLE — the rolled container premise

## 0. What this is

The contained game's frame — one town over a never-ending dungeon — is constant. WHY is
rolled. At world-mint, five small dice answer the five questions the container forces,
and the DM synthesizes the answers into that world's living premise. Small dice,
orthogonal axes: ~20 authored entries yield 1,024 coherent containers. The demo proves
the grand thesis in miniature — infinite variety from recombination + synthesis, not
from big tables. **Axes are architecture, entries are content**: d4 → d8 later is a
content faucet; a NEW axis is surgery and needs its own ruling.

## 1. The five axes (each is a question the container cannot leave unanswered)

1. **PEACE LAW** — why do battles go downstairs? (Not "why is there absolute peace" —
   the resolution rule only needs violence to resolve below. Town-violence consequences
   are ROLLED on the consequence ladder; banishment-to-depth is always among them.)
2. **WOUND** — what is the hole? (Skins the room families and the dungeon's register.)
3. **LICENSE** — why do people descend, and who profits? (Wires the town economy and
   why adventurers are tolerated.)
4. **BELIEF** — what does the town think is at the bottom? (Public. The hope/fear that
   colors every town conversation.)
5. **TRUTH** — what is actually at the bottom? (HIDDEN. Rolled separately from Belief.
   The belief↔truth gap is the campaign's engine and the DM's slow-drip payload.
   Sometimes they agree — the town being right is also a story.)

A sixth axis (AUTHORITY — who rules above) is deliberately folded into PEACE LAW at d4
scope; splitting it out is the first candidate when the dice grow.

## 2. Candidate entries — YOUR keep/kill pass (terse register: noun + pressure, no plots)

**PEACE LAW (d4)**
- P1 **The Watch** — unblinking warden-constructs drift the streets; violators are not
  killed but sentenced downward, deeper than their tier.
- P2 **The Truce-Stone** — every resident is oath-bound to the founding stone; blood
  spilled above marks the spiller for the deep, and the stone does not forget.
- P3 **The Tally** — the charter counts every drop shed above; debts are collected in
  descent, and the collectors are polite.
- P4 **The Quiet** — violence above WAKES something below; peace is enforced by shared
  terror, not law, and the town's silence has a texture.

**WOUND (d4)**
- W1 **The Prison** — built to hold something; every floor is a layer of restraint,
  and descent moves through locks meant never to open.
- W2 **The Buried City** — the old city, stacked and sunk; the town squats on its
  roof and sells maps to its own basement.
- W3 **The Mine** — it grows back; what they dug for is deeper than the founders ever
  admitted, and the tailings whisper.
- W4 **The Mouth** — a wound in the world that will not close; the dungeon is its
  throat, and weather comes up out of it.

**LICENSE (d4)**
- L1 **Tribute** — something demands regular delivery downward; adventurers are the
  couriers, and the schedule is not negotiable.
- L2 **The Yield** — the town's economy runs on what comes up; descent is employment,
  with guild rates and widows' funds.
- L3 **The Sentence** — the condemned descend instead of hanging; adventurers are the
  volunteers walking among convicts.
- L4 **The Pilgrimage** — the descent is holy; every floor a station, every death a
  testimony, and the church above keeps the ledgers.

**BELIEF (d4) — public**
- B1 **Salvation** — the thing at the bottom ends the peace law's cause; the town
  waits to be free.
- B2 **The Hoard** — wealth beyond ledgers; every family has a claim deed and a
  grudge.
- B3 **The End** — something that must never be reached; descent DELAYS it, and the
  town honors the delayers.
- B4 **Nothing** — the hole has no bottom; the deep is where grief goes, and they
  mourn the ones who descend.

**TRUTH (d4) — hidden; the DM's payload**
- T1 **As believed** — the belief is true, exactly and terribly.
- T2 **The Plant** — the belief is a lie authored by whoever profits from descent.
- T3 **The Protection** — worse than feared: the peace law's cause is a shield
  AGAINST the bottom, and breaking it answers the wrong prayer.
- T4 **The Door** — the bottom opens OUTWARD; the world beyond is real, and reaching
  it is how the container ends. (The expansion hook, written into lore on day one.)

## 3. Mechanics

- **Roll at world-mint**, alongside existing world identity. BELIEF is public in the
  world ledger; TRUTH is stored sealed (DM-visible, never surfaced raw to the player —
  it drips per the slow-lore doctrine).
- **Projection law (the lint rule):** every entry must declare, in its table row:
  `town:` one dialog-facing pressure · `dungeon:` one room/register pressure · `hook:`
  at least one mechanical touchpoint (a consequence-table skew, an economy knob, a
  restock bias, a trigger for the DM-summon boundary). An entry that only colors the
  opening narration fails compile.
- **Orthogonality is tested, not assumed:** the build gate includes a synthesis pass
  over the WORST 8 combinations (auto-picked: max pairwise tension) — each must yield
  a coherent premise paragraph or the offending ENTRY (never the axis) gets rewritten.
- **Spice interaction:** the rolled premise is the world's baseline; spice measures
  deviation FROM that premise. (Orbs blinking is only strange in a watched world.)
- **Compile path:** ordinary Engine markdown tables → compile-tables.py; the oracle is
  five small tables with the projection-law columns, nothing novel in the pipeline.

## 4. Non-goals and notes

- No fixed campaign story; no premise-specific engine branches (NC-GOLDEN-GENERATOR
  spirit: the premise skews data, never selects algorithms).
- IP note: warden-constructs must never be named or described as beholders (Product
  Identity, not SRD). "The Watch" as written is original and safe; keep it so.
- Open for your pass: the 20 entries above (keep/kill/rewrite in your voice — these
  are candidates in MY register, and the whole point of authorship-as-the-win is that
  the shipped entries carry YOURS); whether TRUTH gets a 5th "roll twice, both true"
  face when the dice grow; whether the premise surfaces to the player as flavor text
  at world-mint (my lean: BELIEF yes, verbatim; the rest discovered in play).
