#place-mythology
> **The town's living myth** — the founding story, local legend, or superstition the locals BELIEVE, and what believing it makes them do. Every row carries the story AND a live handle (a behavior the belief drives, or the physical thing it centers on). At Grounded/Textured the myth is probably just a story or embellished history; at Strange+ it may be true or half-true — the band grades the honest weirdness of what's actually there, not the wildness of the tale. Rolled when a PC heads toward a new town. Spice-graded d100 at 66/20/9/4/1, Commitment ceiling. Upgraded 2026-07-09 from the DMG14 gather seeds (`docs/PLACE-GATHER-DMG14-SETTLEMENTS.md`, `docs/PLACE-GATHER-DMG14-DUNGEONS.md`); rows PROVISIONAL pending Adam's red-pen; original in `zz_Archive/`. *(Pair with `[[Truth vs False]]` to set whether the myth is actually true.)*
