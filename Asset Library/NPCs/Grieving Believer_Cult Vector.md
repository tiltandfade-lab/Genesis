# Bren Tallow

*(Asset: grieving_believer_unraveling_cult_vector)*

## Plot Vector

**Primary Pillar:** The Cult  
**Secondary Pillars:** The marsh, The Disappearances  

**Function:**  
Escalation vector. Introduces instability within cult belief.  
Hints that rituals are not going as promised.  
Pushes urgency and visible consequence.

---

## Level 1 — Immediate Table Presence

**Race / Background:** Human; dock laborer from the frontier town  
**Role:** Recently unemployed laborer  

**Visual Quirk:** Mud-stained boots he refuses to clean  
**Mood:** Exhausted, volatile beneath restraint  
**Demeanor:** Talks too fast when nervous; goes silent when pressed  

**Hook:** Claims the cult can “return” someone — but something went wrong  
**Talent:** Knows hidden footpaths along the swamp perimeter  
**Unrealized Potential:** Yes  

---

## Level 2 — Structural Pressure

**Formative Trauma:** Sister disappeared near the marsh  
**Formative Grace:** Cult gathering promised she was “not lost”  

**Want:** Proof that she can still be saved  
**Fear:** That he has been lied to  

**Faction Tie:** Attended multiple low-tier cult rites  
**Influence Weight:** 1 — Personal  
**Resource Control:** Knowledge of a recent ritual site  

**If Ignored (Personal):** Attempts a reckless ritual alone  
**If Ignored (Regional):** A botched rite results in a visible disturbance in the marsh  

---

## Level 3 — Secrets

**Secret:** He has already participated in a ritual meant to “call her back.”  
**Leverage:** Knows where the next gathering will occur  
**If Cornered:** Breaks down and reveals everything he knows  

---

## Potential Quest / Adventure Tie-Ins

- Leads players to a partially collapsed ritual site.  
- Claims he heard something answer from beneath the water.  
- Begs players to attend the next gathering as protection.  
- Reveals internal cult disagreement about “how far to go.”  
- Disappearance timeline accelerates shortly after speaking with him.  

---

## Notes for Play

- Emotional swings between hope and panic.  
- Speaks of his sister in present tense.  
- Avoids looking directly at the marsh.  
- Anger surfaces if players mock the rituals.  
- Terrified that something responded.
