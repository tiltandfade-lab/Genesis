| **d20** | **Door State** | **Implication** |
| :------ | :------------- | :-------------- |
| **01** | Open / Standing Ajar | No obstruction. Passage is clear and the door's position is visible from the approach. |
| **02** | Closed, Unlocked | Closed but opens freely; no key or force required. |
| **03** | Closed, Sticking | Closed and unlocked; warped or swollen in the frame — requires a firm push but no check. |
| **04** | Locked — Key in This Zone | Locked; key is carried by a creature or hidden in an object within this dungeon section. |
| **05** | Locked — Key Elsewhere | Locked; key is in a different section of the dungeon, or held by the boss. |
| **06** | Locked — No Key | Designed to stay shut. Requires picking, forcing, or an alternate route. No key was ever meant to exist here. |
| **07** | Barred From This Side | Heavy bar across the inside face; lifted freely from this side, impossible from the other without forcing or a workaround. |
| **08** | Barred From The Far Side | Barred from the other side; cannot be opened from this approach without forcing, cutting through, or finding another route. |
| **09** | Jammed / Stuck | Warped, seized, or swollen; not locked — but requires a Strength check to force open. Loud when forced. |
| **10** | Spiked Shut | Iron spikes driven through the frame from this side; someone didn't want it opened. Requires force and time to clear. |
| **11** | Chained and Padlocked | A chain loops through the handle and a ring in the wall; padlock holds it. Chain can be broken or lock picked. |
| **12** | Mechanism Jammed | Applies to portcullises, stone slabs, and any door with a mechanical component. Roll 1d2: 1 = stuck open, 2 = stuck closed. |
| **13** | Partially Collapsed | Still in the frame but badly damaged; gap at the top or side allows a Small creature through, or can be forced wider. |
| **14** | Fully Blocked | Rubble, cave-in, or total structural failure blocks the opening; impassable without significant effort, tools, or magic. |
| **15** | Trapped — Visible | A trap element is detectable on approach: tripwire across the threshold, pressure plate, or obvious trigger mechanism. Can be disarmed. |
| **16** | Trapped — Concealed | A hidden trap; DC to detect before triggering. Effect may be alarm, damage, gas, or mechanical (portcullis drop, pit). |
| **17** | Magically Held | Arcane lock or equivalent effect; physically intact but magically reinforced. Magical means or dispelling required to open normally. |
| **18** | Magically Sealed | Longer-duration or permanent seal; requires a specific key word, object, or condition to open. Forcing damages the sealing effect. |
| **19** | Auto-Closing | Opens freely but returns to closed — and possibly locked — after one round via spring mechanism, counterweight, or magic. |
| **20** | Roll Twice | Roll twice on this table; both conditions apply simultaneously. (e.g., Barred from the far side AND trapped.) |
