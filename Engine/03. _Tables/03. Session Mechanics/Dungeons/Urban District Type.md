Urban District Type (d20)

|d20|District Type|Social Class|Vibe & 2024 Mechanical Impact|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1**|**Civic / Lord's Keep**|Upper|Heavily patrolled, clean streets. _Impact:_ Regional Guard presence is high. Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) D20 Tests due to bright streetlamps.|
|**2**|**Noble Estates**|Upper|Sprawling walled manors and private guards. _Impact:_ Cult Tier 2 (Nobles) operate here. Encounters often involve private security rather than city watch.|
|**3**|**Wealthy Residential**|Upper|Luxurious townhouses and quiet streets. _Impact:_ Low ambient noise. Easy to spot interlopers; characters stand out without appropriate high-end disguises.|
|**4**|**Magic District**|Upper|Flashy illusions and hovering architecture. _Impact:_ The Scrappy the Arcane Cabal is active here. Ambient magic might cause bizarre environmental hazards.|
|**5**|**Botanical Gardens**|Upper|Pristine nature, manicured trees, and exotic beasts. _Impact:_ Plenty of natural terrain (trees, fountains) to use as Cover during an encounter.|
|**6**|**Embassy / Diplomatic**|Upper|Clashing architectural styles and foreign dignitaries. _Impact:_ Diplomatic immunity complicates combat; high chance of triggering major faction escalations.|
|**7**|**Fine Shops & Finance**|Upper|High-end armorers, jewelers, and banks. _Impact:_ Heavily fortified doors and magically warded locks. Obstacle DCs are typically 15+.|
|**8**|**Temple District**|Middle|Massive shrines, tolling bells, and chanting. _Impact:_ High density of Clerics and Paladins. Great location for Cult Tier 3 entities hiding behind false dogmas.|
|**9**|**Guildhall District**|Middle|Massive halls displaying trade symbols. _Impact:_ Heavy wagon traffic creates _Moving Cover_. Ideal for mid-level faction extortion encounters.|
|**10**|**Marketplace / Bazaar**|Middle|Tents, barkers, and a riot of smells and colors. _Impact:_ _Difficult Terrain_ due to crushing crowds. Pickpockets and Bandits thrive here.|
|**11**|**Average Residential**|Middle|Modest homes, cramped yards, children playing. _Impact:_ The baseline standard. Standard DCs (10-15) for locks, climbing, or tracking.|
|**12**|**Garrison / Military**|Middle|Barracks, training yards, and marching boots. _Impact:_ Regional Guard epicenter. Open combat will immediately draw 1d4+1 guards per round.|
|**13**|**Caravan / Outlanders**|Middle|Wagons, animal pens, and travelers. _Impact:_ High transient population makes it easy to blend in. Smells of campfires and livestock.|
|**14**|**Theater / Entertainment**|Lower|Playhouses, street performers, and makeup. _Impact:_ Perfect for social flashpoints. High crowds at night, empty by day.|
|**15**|**Inn & Tavern District**|Lower|Clustered pubs, drunken revelers, and brawls. _Impact:_ Easy to gather rumors. Lots of improvised weapons (bottles, chairs) and narrow, muddy alleys.|
|**16**|**Waterfront / Docks**|Lower|Smell of salt and fish, creaking piers. _Impact:_ Slippery footing (requires a Dexterity [Acrobatics] D20 Test to avoid falling Prone). Ideal for Cult smuggling operations.|
|**17**|**Warehouse District**|Lower|Massive, windowless storage buildings, mostly empty streets. _Impact:_ Deep shadows, excellent for ambushes. Plenty of crates for _Total Cover_.|
|**18**|**Tannery / Industrial**|Lower|Noxious fumes, vats of chemicals, and runoff. _Impact:_ Constitution saving throws might be required to avoid being _Poisoned_ by the stench.|
|**19**|**Slum / Shantytown**|Lower|Decrepit hovels, desperate people, and trash. _Impact:_ High Bandit presence. _Hindering Terrain_ in the form of collapsing roofs or disease-ridden mud.|
|**20**|**Undercity / Sewers**|Special|Catacombs, drains, or the City of the Dead. _Impact:_ Light is heavily restricted (Dim Light or Darkness). Perfect for the Cult's undead minions.|
