|d20|Enemy Archetype|Suggested Stat Blocks (CR 1/8 to 5)|Behavior & Tactical Hook|
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**Opportunistic Scavengers**|Swarms, Giant Rats, Ghouls, Hyenas|**Feasting:** Currently distracted by a fresh kill. They will aggressively defend their meal, utilizing numbers to surround anyone who gets close.|
|**2**|**Opportunistic Scavengers**|Swarms, Giant Rats, Ghouls, Hyenas|**Starving & Desperate:** Driven mad by hunger. They attack immediately, ignoring heavily armored targets to swarm the smallest party member or a pack animal.|
|**3**|**Opportunistic Scavengers**|Swarms, Giant Rats, Ghouls, Hyenas|**Trailing:** Following the party at a distance. They won't attack head-on, but wait until the party is distracted by a hazard or attempts to take a Short Rest.|
|**4**|**Territorial Ambushers**|Giant Spiders, Gricks, Phase Spiders|**Hidden Trap:** Hiding in the canopy, under the sand, or invisible. They wait patiently for a PC to enter a _Choke Point_ or _Difficult Terrain_ before striking.|
|**5**|**Territorial Ambushers**|Giant Spiders, Gricks, Phase Spiders|**The Lure:** Using a decoy—such as a shiny dropped item, a webbed-up corpse, or mimicking a cry for help—to draw a single PC out of formation.|
|**6**|**Territorial Ambushers**|Giant Spiders, Gricks, Phase Spiders|**Hit and Drag:** They strike from cover to inflict a condition (Grappled or Poisoned), then attempt to drag the victim away into the brush or up a cliff.|
|**7**|**Mindless Horde**|Skeletons, Zombies, Oozes|**Relentless Advance:** Wandering aimlessly until triggered by sound or warmth. They do not use cover, moving in a straight, predictable line toward the party.|
|**8**|**Mindless Horde**|Skeletons, Zombies, Oozes|**Dormant / Buried:** Lying completely still beneath mud, ash, or water. They automatically gain Surprise against the first character to step into their space.|
|**9**|**Mindless Horde**|Skeletons, Zombies, Oozes|**Bottleneck Crush:** Pouring out of a narrow space or funneling the party into one, relying on sheer numbers to overwhelm the frontline.|
|**10**|**Desperate Humanoids**|Bandits, Thugs, Cultists|**The Shakedown:** Setting up a crude toll on a bridge or pass. They demand gold or rations for safe passage, but will flee if they take heavy casualties.|
|**11**|**Desperate Humanoids**|Bandits, Thugs, Cultists|**Cornered & Fleeing:** Running blindly from a much larger threat. They might attack out of sheer panic or try to steal the party's mounts/gear to escape.|
|**12**|**Desperate Humanoids**|Bandits, Thugs, Cultists|**Defending Camp:** Guarding a makeshift shelter or wagon. They utilize barricades for _Three-Quarters Cover_, readying ranged attacks against anyone approaching.|
|**13**|**Hunting Pack**|Wolves, Dire Wolves, Hell Hounds|**Skirmish & Flank:** Using _Pack Tactics_ to encircle the party. They attempt to knock the vanguard _Prone_ so the rest of the pack can gain Advantage.|
|**14**|**Hunting Pack**|Wolves, Dire Wolves, Hell Hounds|**The Chase:** Actively pursuing the party. They howl or call out to alert the rest of the pack, forcing the party to either fight or dash through hazardous terrain.|
|**15**|**Hunting Pack**|Wolves, Dire Wolves, Hell Hounds|**Bait and Switch:** One or two visibly menace the party from the front, while the bulk of the pack circles around to ambush the spellcasters in the rear.|
|**16**|**Rival Adventurers / Elite Patrol**|Scouts, Veterans, Gladiators|**Tactical Engagement:** Highly organized formation. They focus fire on spellcasters, utilize healing potions, and retreat tactically if the fight turns against them.|
|**17**|**Rival Adventurers / Elite Patrol**|Scouts, Veterans, Gladiators|**Ambuscade:** Set up in a _Sniper's Perch_ or behind _Total Cover_. They will demand surrender from a position of absolute geographical superiority.|
|**18**|**The Brute / Apex Predator**|Ogre, Manticore, Troll, Giant Boar|**Reckless Assault:** A large, highly confident threat. It charges directly into the frontline, attempting to scatter the party or smash through obstacles.|
|**19**|**The Brute / Apex Predator**|Ogre, Manticore, Troll, Giant Boar|**Territorial Display:** Currently destroying the environment (uprooting trees, smashing rocks) or a lesser creature to show dominance. Can be bypassed if the party backs away slowly.|
|**20**|**Extraplanar / Magical Threat**|Mephits, Gargoyles, Elementals|**Environmental Synergy:** Tied directly to a Landmark or Magical Zone. They use the hazard to their advantage (e.g., a Fire Elemental healing in a lava vent, or Gargoyles hiding on a ruined arch).|
