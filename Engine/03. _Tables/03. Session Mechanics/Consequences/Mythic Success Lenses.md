#mythic-success-lenses
> **The shape of a triumph that spiked.** Rolled on the *success* side of the Critical-Magnitude system (a natural 20 whose magnitude die landed 11+). Each row is a **lens** — a *kind* of permanent change — not a finished outcome; the DM fills the content to fit the exact fiction. The magnitude die sets **how many lenses fire** (count) and **how far each reaches** (intensity): 11–14 → one lens (Regional); 15–19 → two to three (a cluster); 20/20 → the full cascade, written to the World State Ledger as canon. When multiple fire, **draw distinct rows** (reroll duplicates) and **weave them into one event** that flows from a single act. See `docs/CRIT-MAGNITUDE.md`. *(Mirror: `[[Mythic Failure Lenses]]`. The "place is transformed" lens hands off to the Myth suite — `[[Myth Seeds]]` → `[[Myth Costs]]` → `[[Myth Becomes Geography]]`.)*

| d12 | Lens | The DM fills it as… |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **A place is transformed** | The ground itself is changed for good — hand off to the Myth suite (Seed → Costs → Geography). The shrine-of-light case. |
| 2 | **A person is permanently changed** | A character in the scene is turned at the root — the bandit genuinely repents, an enemy becomes a lifelong ally, a coward is made brave forever. |
| 3 | **A new thing enters the world** | Something that wasn't there now exists and persists — a spring, an heirloom item, a bloodline, a beast that breeds true. |
| 4 | **A passage opens** | A way that was shut is now open — a planar door, a hidden road revealed, a sealed vault, a line of contact to a power. |
| 5 | **A local law bends kindly** | A rule of nature softens in this area, permanently — a valley that never knows winter, ground that heals the sick, water that never fouls. |
| 6 | **A name becomes a force** | The deed crystallizes into invokable power — speaking the doer's name here opens doors, calms mobs, or wards the dark. |
| 7 | **Something lost returns** | The deed reaches backward — a curse lifts region-wide, the dead are honestly restored, a drought breaks, an exile can come home. |
| 8 | **A bond is forged that outlives the moment** | An oath, pact, or loyalty binds for good — a faction is permanently sworn, a creature owes a true debt, two peoples are reconciled. |
| 9 | **A hidden truth becomes undeniable** | Something concealed is exposed beyond deniability and stays exposed — a conspiracy laid bare, a false king unmasked, a prophecy visibly fulfilled. |
| 10 | **Fate tilts toward the doer** | The world quietly owes them — an omen marks them favored, luck attaches to their cause, powers take notice and approve. |
| 11 | **A threat is broken at the root** | A danger is ended permanently, not just defeated — a monster sealed for an age, a tyranny's spine snapped, a plague's source burned out. |
| 12 | **The doer is elevated** | The boon lands on the character — a lasting gift, a true title, a power awakened, a mark of grace others can see. |
