# NPC Role Skin — Lost-World (vanished-civilization antiquity)

> Realm skin over [[npc-role-spine]] ([[NPC-ROLE-REALMS]] approach C). Lost-World is the
> ruin-and-remnant skin: a wonder outlived the civilization that raised it, and everyone left here
> is a descendant, a guardian, or an outsider the wonder keeps luring in. It takes **one hard
> drop** — Pampered-elite (no one idle and insulated stays near a wonder this perilous; every elite
> left here has skin in the game) — and **reweights hard**: settled bulk-labor thins (Land-worker,
> Feeder, Clothier — the remnant population is small and marginal, feeding itself, not a market),
> while the relic economy swells (Delver, Thief, Wild-provider, Trader, Hired-blade, Outlaw) and
> faith/forbidden scholarship swells with it (Rite-keeper, Hidden-fanatic, Secret-scholar) — this
> realm's true demography. Soul/note/class inherit from the spine; only the LABEL changes. Blank
> Weight = spine default, `0` = drop. Kin-agnostic / recontextualize per convention. Lever/atom —
> exempt from the situation family (do NOT tag `table_family`).
