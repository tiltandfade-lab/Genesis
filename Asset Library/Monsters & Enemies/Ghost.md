*p. 131 MM 2024*

Ghosts are the spiritual remnants of the dead, anchored to the mortal world by sheer force of will, unfulfilled duty, or traumatic demise. Their appearances and demeanors vary wildly depending on their death and their purpose. Some are confused, tragic figures simply repeating their daily routines, while others are terrifying, vengeful horrors eager to possess the living.

A ghost's true threat is that it is often immune to permanent destruction through mere combat. Even if "destroyed" by spells or weapons, a ghost will typically rejuvenate and return to its haunt within a few days unless the specific reason for its existence—its "unfinished business"—is resolved.

Here are the 5e rules-as-written stat blocks for the standard Ghost and the Poltergeist variant, followed by modular d10 tables that fit a wide spectrum of campaign tones.

### 1. Ghost

_Medium Undead, Neutral_

- **Armor Class:** 11
- **Hit Points:** 45 (10d8)
- **Speed:** 5 ft., fly 40 ft. (hover)
- **STR:** 7 (-2) | **DEX:** 13 (+1) | **CON:** 10 (+0) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 17 (+3)
- **Damage Resistances:** Acid, bludgeoning, cold, fire, lightning, piercing, slashing, thunder
- **Damage Immunities:** Necrotic, poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** Common plus one other language
- **Challenge:** 4 (1,100 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Ethereal Sight:** The ghost can see 60 feet into the Ethereal Plane when it is on the Material Plane, and vice versa.
- **Incorporeal Movement:** The ghost can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The ghost makes two Withering Touch attacks.
- **Withering Touch:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 19 (3d10 + 3) necrotic damage.
- **Etherealness:** The ghost casts the Etherealness spell, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability. The ghost is visible on the Material Plane while it is in the Border Ethereal, and vice versa, but it can't affect or be affected by anything on the other plane.
- **Horrifying Visage:** Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 13, each creature in a 60-foot Cone that can see the ghost and isn't an Undead. Failure: 10 (2d6 + 3) psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the start of the ghost's next turn. Success: The target is immune to this ghost's Horrifying Visage for 24 hours.
- **Possession (Recharge 6):** Charisma Saving Throw: DC 13, one Humanoid the ghost can see within 5 feet. Failure: The target is possessed by the ghost; the ghost disappears, and the target is Incapacitated and loses control of its body. The ghost now controls the body but the target retains awareness. The ghost can't be targeted by any attack, spell, or other effect, except those that specifically target Undead. The ghost's game statistics are the same, except it uses the possessed target's Speed, as well as the target's Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution modifiers. The possession lasts until the body drops to 0 Hit Points or the ghost leaves as a Bonus Action. When the possession ends, the ghost appears in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the body. Success: The target is immune to this ghost's Possession for 24 hours.

### 2. Poltergeist (Variant)

A poltergeist is the confused, invisible spirit of an individual with no sense of how it died. It expresses its rage by hurling creatures and objects using the power of its shattered psyche.

_Change the Ghost's Challenge Rating to 2 (450 XP) and apply the following modifications:_

- **Trait - Invisibility:** The poltergeist is permanently invisible.
- _Remove_ the Withering Touch, Etherealness, Horrifying Visage, and Possession actions.
- **Action - Forceful Slam:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 10 (3d6) force damage.
- **Action - Telekinetic Thrust:** The poltergeist targets a creature or unattended object within 30 feet of it. A creature must be Medium or smaller to be affected, and an object can weigh up to 150 pounds. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 12 Strength saving throw or be hurled up to 30 feet in any direction, taking 1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet it was thrown if it hits a solid surface.

### d10 Unfinished Business (The Quest Hook)

A ghost cannot truly be destroyed by combat alone; it will rejuvenate until the PCs figure out why it is tethered to the living world.

|d10|The Unfinished Business|Campaign Tone / Resolution|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Recipe's Secret**|**Humorous:** The ghost of a baker haunts the tavern, throwing pots and pans, because a rival stole their secret pie recipe. The PCs must steal it back to let the spirit rest.|
|**2**|**The Apologetic Murderer**|**Irony/Drama:** The ghost is actually a murderer who realizes the error of their ways in death. They cannot rest until they are brought before the victim's descendants to formally apologize.|
|**3**|**The Undelivered Message**|**Lighthearted/Pulp:** A spectral courier is frantically trying to deliver a 300-year-old love letter or royal decree. The PCs must find the intended recipient (or their grave) and read it aloud.|
|**4**|**The Betrayed Comrade**|**Tragicomedy:** The ghost was left behind by an adventuring party to be eaten by a dragon. It uses minor telekinesis to untie the PCs' boots until they bring its former party members back to apologize.|
|**5**|**The Stolen Relic**|**Classic Adventure:** The ghost protects a tomb and will aggressively possess intruders. It will only rest when a stolen magical bauble is returned to the crypt's altar.|
|**6**|**The Cold-Blooded Murder**|**Mystery:** The ghost was slain in cold blood and its body hidden. It will lead the party to its corpse and demand the killer be brought to justice before it will depart.|
|**7**|**The Trap Haunt**|**Tactical:** The spirit is trapped in a dungeon loop. It believes the only way to free itself from eternal torment is to lead the PCs into the exact same trap that killed it.|
|**8**|**The Failed Guardian**|**Heroic:** The ghost is a guard who failed their duty. The party must help the ghost defend the location from a modern threat, allowing the spirit to finally redeem itself and pass on.|
|**9**|**The Drowned Jealousy**|**Gritty:** A waterlogged spirit that hates the living and seeks to drown them out of spite. It can only be laid to rest by finding its bloated corpse in the swamp and properly interring it.|
|**10**|**The Vengeful Execution**|**Dark Fantasy:** The ghost was executed unjustly by the local magistrate. The spirit refuses to leave until the magistrate's entire bloodline is ruined or destroyed.|
