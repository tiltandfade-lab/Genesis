*p. 158 MM 2024*

The wormlike grick waits unseen, blending in with the rock of the caves and caverns it haunts. Only when prey comes near does it rear up, its four barbed tentacles unfurling to reveal a hungry, snapping beak. Gricks rarely hunt. Instead, they drag their rubbery bodies to places where creatures regularly pass, lurking out of sight amid rocky rubble and debris, squeezing into burrows, holes, or crevices, climbing up to ledges, or coiling around stalactites to drop on unwary prey.

A grick consumes virtually anything that moves except other gricks. It targets the nearest prey, grabbing a fallen creature with its tentacles and dragging it off to eat alone. Gricks remain in an area until the food supply dwindles, or because sentient creatures become aware of their presence and begin plotting alternate routes. When prey is scarce, gricks venture aboveground to hunt in wilderness, lurking in trees or on cliff-side ledges. A grick pack is often led by a single well-fed, oversized Grick Alpha, around which the others congregate.

Over time, grick lairs accumulate the cast-off possessions of intelligent prey — what the MM calls "spoils of slaughter." Underdark explorers know to look for these telltale signs, and sometimes seal off the routes leading to a grick lair to starve them out before claiming the accumulated wealth of their victims.

### d10 The Ambush Position (Where Is It Hiding?)

Gricks exploit Stone Camouflage to the absolute limit. The tactical question is always: where in this environment would a grick be completely invisible? Roll or choose one to establish the ambush before combat begins.

| d10 | Position | Tactical Setup & Passive Perception DC |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **1** | **The Rubble Pile** | The grick has coiled itself into a pile of broken stone blocks in the corner of the room. It is indistinguishable from the debris at a glance. DC 14 Perception to spot it before stepping within 5 ft. |
| **2** | **The Low Ceiling** | A 10-ft-high passage. The grick clings to the ceiling directly above the doorway threshold. It drops tentacle-first on the second PC through the door, targeting the marching order's softest spot. |
| **3** | **The Crevice Wall** | A grick has wedged itself into a deep vertical crack in the cavern wall, only a few inches of its body visible. The crack is at eye level and looks like a natural shadow. DC 15 Perception to distinguish from the rock. |
| **4** | **The Stalactite Cluster** | The grick has coiled around a dense cluster of stalactites near the ceiling. At rest, it looks like an unusually thick, organic formation. DC 14 Perception to notice the formation's eyes blinking. |
| **5** | **The Statue Plinth** | In a dungeon with worked stone, the grick has coiled itself around the base of a large statue or pillar, its gray-brown skin matching the carved stone perfectly. A Grick Alpha here looks like decorative architectural detail. |
| **6** | **The Shallows** | In a flooded cavern, the grick rests on a submerged shelf with only its back breaking the surface — resembling a row of smooth river rocks. DC 15 Perception to spot. Triggers automatically if any PC wades into the water. |
| **7** | **The Mountain Pass** | Above ground variant. The grick has climbed to a cliff-side ledge 20 ft. above a mountain trail. It drops on the lead PC when they are directly below. DC 13 Perception to notice the shelf looks wrong before passing under it. |
| **8** | **The Ossuary** | Gricks in an old bone-strewn chamber benefit from extraordinary camouflage — their mottled skin resembles old calcium against the pale floor. An Alpha here cannot be distinguished from the bone floor without a DC 16 Perception check. |
| **9** | **The Patrol Route Corner** | The grick knows this corridor well. It has positioned itself around a sharp blind corner, tentacles coiled. The party can hear nothing around corners. First PC around the bend is attacked with Surprise. |
| **10** | **The Open Field** | Above-ground hunting grick variant. The grick has climbed into the canopy of a lone dead tree on a grassland or moorland, hanging upside down with Stone Camouflage helping it blend into the bark. An Alpha here is a genuinely alarming discovery. |

### d10 Spoils of Slaughter (The Accumulated Cache)

Per the Monster Manual, grick lairs are treasure-rooms by accident. Roll to determine what intelligent prey left behind in this particular hunting ground.

| d10 | The Find | Value & Hook |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **1** | **The Scout's Satchel** | A weathered leather satchel containing rations, a rolled map, and a small coded journal. The map shows the dungeon with notes — from a previous expedition that never returned. |
| **2** | **The Adventurer's Regrets** | A pile of stripped-off armor (medium quality, intact) next to a single boot. A DC 13 Medicine check: the occupant was taken alive and carried to the ceiling. |
| **3** | **The Merchant's Strongbox** | A locked iron box (DC 14 Thieves' Tools). Contains 180 gp and a promissory note addressed to a trading company. The company may not know their courier never arrived. |
| **4** | **The Dwarf's Tools** | A high-quality set of mason's and miner's tools, wrapped in oilskin to protect them. The owner valued these enough to carry them through here. They are pristine and worth 30 gp. |
| **5** | **The Spell Components** | A belt pouch of intact spell components, including a diamond worth 100 gp (for *revivify* or *raise dead*). The mage who carried these did not get to use them. |
| **6** | **The Guard's Tabard** | A torn, bloodstained city or estate guard tabard. Someone was sent into this dungeon officially. There's a name tag sewn inside. |
| **7** | **The Sealed Vial** | An intact glass vial of something faintly luminescent. DC 14 Arcana: it is a *Potion of Heroism*. The grick didn't eat it — it can't digest glass. |
| **8** | **The Chalice** | A silver chalice, dented but intact. Worth 50 gp. Bears an ecclesiastical symbol of a god of light — which raises obvious questions about what a priest was doing here. |
| **9** | **The Grick Alpha's Trophy Pile** | The Alpha has accumulated an unusually large cache against the far wall: 12 gp, 87 sp, a fine dagger (+1, not magical but exceptional quality), and a signet ring from a noble house. |
| **10** | **The Still-Living Survivor** | Behind the grick's feeding spot, wedged into a crevice too small for the creature to reach, is a living person in terrible condition — dehydrated, traumatized, but breathing. They have been here for days. |
