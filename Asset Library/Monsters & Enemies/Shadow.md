*p. 271 MM 2024*

Shadows are among the most insidious undead in the game — not because of their raw power, but because of what they do to the people they kill. A shadow arises when a creature dies in a place of profound magical darkness, or when a sufficiently powerful necromantic ritual tears the "shadow-self" from a living soul, leaving the body alive but hollow. The resulting entity hungers for Strength — specifically, it feeds on the vital force that keeps living beings upright and moving.

A creature whose Strength is drained to 0 by a shadow doesn't die immediately. It simply collapses, and then, before the body is cold, a new shadow rises from the corpse. This chain propagation is what makes shadows genuinely dangerous in enclosed spaces. One shadow that catches the party off-guard can become five shadows in a single long encounter. The standard tactics that work on skeletons and zombies — wade in, hit hard — are suicide against shadows.

The shadow has no voice, no face, and no agenda beyond the hunger. It is a predator of light and warmth, and it despises both.

### Campaign Integration: Investigation & Clues

Shadows leave a distinctive psychic residue on the places they haunt.

- **The Vitality Drain:** Animals and livestock in the vicinity of a shadow-haunted location become lethargic and weak, losing Strength and will to move without any obvious illness. Farmers notice their animals "fading." A DC 12 Nature or Medicine check confirms the symptoms match magical Strength drain, not disease.
- **The Cold Spot:** Shadows lower the ambient temperature of a space they occupy frequently. A room that is inexplicably cold, even in summer, with no draft to explain it, may be a shadow's lair.
- **The Empty Eyes:** Victims who barely survived a shadow encounter describe the experience as looking into "a hole where a person should be." Survivors often suffer lingering nightmares and mild Strength penalties until they receive clerical aid.

### 1. Shadow

A creature of living darkness that hungers for the vital force of the living.

_Medium Undead, Chaotic Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 16 (3d8 + 3)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **STR:** 6 (−2) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 13 (+1) | **INT:** 6 (−2) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 8 (−1)
- **Skills:** Stealth +4 (+6 in dim light or darkness)
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Radiant
- **Damage Resistances:** Acid, Fire, Force, Lightning, Thunder; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** Cold, Necrotic, Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 1/2 (100 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Amorphous:** The shadow can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.
- **Shadow Stealth:** While in dim light or darkness, the shadow can take the Hide action as a Bonus Action.
- **Sunlight Weakness:** While in sunlight, the shadow has Disadvantage on attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws.

**Actions**

- **Strength Drain:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 9 (2d6 + 2) necrotic damage, and the target's Strength score is reduced by 1d4. The target dies if this reduces its Strength to 0. Otherwise, the reduction lasts until the target finishes a short or long rest. If a non-evil humanoid dies from this attack, a new shadow rises from the corpse in 1d4 hours.

### 2. Greater Shadow

An older, stronger shadow — one that has fed enough to develop a malevolent cunning and the ability to temporarily sever a creature's connection to its own shadow-self.

_Medium Undead, Chaotic Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Hit Points:** 45 (10d8)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **STR:** 6 (−2) | **DEX:** 17 (+3) | **CON:** 11 (+0) | **INT:** 8 (−1) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 8 (−1)
- **Skills:** Stealth +5 (+7 in dim light or darkness)
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Radiant
- **Damage Resistances:** Acid, Fire, Force, Lightning, Thunder; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** Cold, Necrotic, Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Amorphous:** The shadow can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.
- **Shadow Stealth:** While in dim light or darkness, the shadow can take the Hide action as a Bonus Action.
- **Sunlight Weakness:** While in sunlight, the shadow has Disadvantage on attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The greater shadow makes two Strength Drain attacks.
- **Strength Drain:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 10 (2d6 + 3) necrotic damage, and the target's Strength score is reduced by 1d4. The target dies if this reduces its Strength to 0. Otherwise, the reduction lasts until the target finishes a short or long rest. If a non-evil humanoid dies from this attack, a new shadow rises from the corpse in 1d4 hours.
- **Sever Shadow (Recharge 5–6):** The greater shadow targets one creature it can see within 30 ft. The target must make a DC 13 Charisma saving throw. On a failure, the target's shadow is torn free and becomes an independent shadow under the greater shadow's control for 1 minute, or until the greater shadow is destroyed. The target has Disadvantage on Strength checks and saving throws for the duration.

| **1** | **The Darkening Room** | **Horror Buildup:** As the party explores, torches begin going out one by one — not from wind, but drained. The shadows are feeding on the light before they feed on the party. |

| **4** | **The Chain Reaction** | **Tactical Emergency:** The party arrived too late. Two bodies on the floor are already generating new shadows. The party has four rounds to destroy the original shadow before the room contains four of them. |
| **5** | **The Sunlight Gambit** | **Puzzle Combat:** The only weapon effective enough is a *sunrod* or equivalent. The party has one. They must share it, pass it, and never let the shadows knock it from their hands. |
| **6** | **The Shadow Museum** | **Atmospheric:** The room contains dozens of perfectly preserved humanoid silhouettes frozen on the walls — former victims whose shadows were peeled off and pinned. Their expressions are visible in the dark. |
| **7** | **The Tracker** | **Mounting Dread:** A shadow has been following the party through the dungeon for two rooms. It hasn't attacked. It's learned their patrol patterns. It's waiting for the party to separate. |
