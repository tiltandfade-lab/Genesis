# Garrick Blackwater

## Level 1 — Immediate Presence

**Race:** Human (Heartlander)  
**Occupation:** Supervisor of dock labor assignments  
**Secondary Role:** Controls physical dock access  

**Visual:** Wears spectacles perched low on nose.  
**Demeanor:** Speaks through clenched teeth when annoyed.  
**Mood (Default):** Anxious about being watched.  
**Hook:** Claims he is being watched.

**Talent:** Expert dart thrower.  
**Unrealized Potential:** No.

**Faction Position:** Keeps distance from formal power structures.  
**Fear:** Scarcity — hunger, tools, shelter.

**Influence Weight:** 6 — Regional Ripple

**Resource Control:**  
Controls a dock choke point (bridge/gate/dock access).

**If Ignored (Personal):**  
Trades influence for short-term security.

**If Ignored (Regional):**
