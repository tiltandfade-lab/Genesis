> [!info] Enemy Loot — Role-Keyed
> Enemy loot is **separate from the room's magic item budget.** Enemies carry mundane coin and gear — nothing from the dungeon's item budget. The Boss is the only exception: the boss carries the dungeon's top budget item on their person.
>
> **Roles:** Grunt = low-CR fodder (goblins, skeletons, bandits, cultists). Officer = mid-CR capable fighters (bugbears, gnolls, cultist priests, bandit captains). Specialist = casters, rogues, unique roles. Boss = finale enemy. Beast = non-sentient creatures (carry nothing).

| **Role** | **T1 Loot** | **T2 Loot** |
| -------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| Beast | Nothing of value. Teeth, claws, or scales worth 1d4 sp to a trader. | Nothing of value. Trophy parts worth 1d6 gp to the right buyer. |
| Grunt | 1d6 cp in a belt pouch. The weapon they were using (worn, non-magical). | 1d6 sp + worn weapon (non-magical). Possibly a crude key or token. |
| Officer | 2d6 gp + quality weapon they were using (non-magical). May carry a letter, key, or map fragment. | 2d6 × 5 gp + quality weapon. Carries a document, seal, or identifying insignia. |
| Specialist | 1d6 gp + relevant tools or materials (component pouch, alchemist's supplies, thieves' tools). | 2d6 gp + tools. May carry one consumable (roll on Dungeon Loot - Common). |
| Boss | Carries the dungeon's top budget magic item + 2d6 × 5 gp (T1) or 2d6 × 25 gp (T2). | Carries the dungeon's top budget magic item + 2d6 × 25 gp + 1 gem (50 gp). |
