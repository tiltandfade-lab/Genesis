# GOLDEN SITE WAVE 2 — FIRST END-TO-END COMPACT VIGNETTE

## 0. Outcome

Wave 2 builds the first shared, deterministic story-to-space compiler slice.
`VENUE-TAVERN-01` proves that one committed venue keeps its identity and circuits
across social and tactical projections. `GP-SHAPE-01` proves that the same compiler
can synthesize a visible compact battlefield in which connected natural ground,
constructed route control, and three viable tactical plans are one composition.

This is not a Tavern renderer plus a Guard Post generator. Both fixtures pass through
the same request, reservation, window, candidate, score, repair, surface, asset,
projection, and receipt stages. Fixture-specific program grammars provide obligations
and relational topology; they do not provide finished cell maps or seed patches.

## 1. Frozen product cut

### 1.1 Shared stages

```text
VignetteRequestV1
  -> SemanticIdentityReservation[]
  -> MaterializationWindow
  -> bounded MacroTopologyCandidate[]
  -> terrain + construction negotiation
  -> tactical/social circuit validation
  -> explicit component scores
  -> named legal repair (at most one pass)
  -> SurfaceAssemblyPlan
  -> SurfaceMaterialDemandCandidateV1[]
  -> material-family resolution
  -> SceneTray / BattleMap projections
  -> SynthesisReceiptV1
  -> AF-GV-8 proof package
```

The compiler is pure engine data. It does not read the DOM, renderer, camera state, or
world globals and does not write persistent game state. A projection consumes the
committed plan; it cannot complete missing roles or regenerate geometry.

### 1.2 Bounded search

- Four initial candidates derive from named deterministic substreams.
- Hard validation runs before scoring.
- Failed candidates remain in the receipt with exact rejection ids.
- One named repair pass may produce one child per failed candidate.
- Selection is lexicographic: hard-pass, required-circuit count, route-choice,
  tactical-tension, composition, quiet-space, identity, four-bearing, continuity,
  then lower asset cost. The individual measures remain visible; there is no opaque
  `qualityScore`.
- If no candidate passes, compilation fails. A proxy may replace appearance but never
  a required role, access route, objective, interaction, or persistent identity.

### 1.3 Golden-number and seed law

Runtime synthesis may branch on typed host/program grammar, substrate, operating
state, source obligations, and declared context. It may not branch on a Golden Site
number, fixture id, hero seed, site id, camera bearing, or projection target.

Seeds move bounded relational parameters: shoulder handedness, road bend, building
offset, route landing, threshold width, and equivalent program relationships. They do
not independently sample tile heights or tile normals.

## 2. Tavern semantic/identity microfixture

The Tavern cut uses the Wave-1 retained request for `VENUE-TAVERN-01`:
The Golden Dolphin in The Dorsal Market, typed as a Noodle Bar and retaining its
self-working-workshop former use.

Required identity reservations:

- public arrival and recognizable public common;
- service/back threshold and supply/waste circuit;
- responsible-host position;
- quiet social floor and source-backed practical light;
- former-use work trace without changing the current operating program;
- stable venue, threshold, interaction, and retreat ids.

The social SceneTray and promoted BattleMap share one committed plan fingerprint.
Promotion activates dormant tactical reservations; it does not add rooms, a basement,
an invented wing, or alternate doors.

## 3. Guard Post visible/tactical fixture

### 3.1 Defining composition

The Guard Post is an old operating post on the uphill shoulder of a through-road:

- one connected natural landform rises from lower approach to high shoulder;
- the road bends and narrows where it is cut into that shoulder;
- localized retaining masonry and exposed cut faces reveal the negotiation between
  road and land, without wrapping the map in regular strata;
- a controlled barrier crosses the road at the working apron;
- the guardroom is embedded against the middle shelf;
- the observation deck occupies the crown and has licensed counterplay;
- a second terrain route rounds or climbs the shoulder and changes timing;
- maintenance and repair are causal construction evidence, not random damage.

The high mass belongs in the camera-far band of the starting bearing. It may exceed
two storeys where composition and four-bearing review support it. There is no global
terrain or construction height cap.

### 3.2 Required zones and plans

The committed plan must expose all eleven fixture zones: entry, quiet approach,
through-road/narrowing, controlled threshold, guardroom, work/readiness, observation
face, flat deck, defensive edge, secondary flank/timing route, and exit/retreat.

It must retain three meaningfully different plans:

1. front/checkpoint — shortest and most observed;
2. terrain flank — longer, narrower, and less exposed;
3. deck/timing — vertical contest with an explicit approach and counterplay.

Routes must have real cell costs and height consequences. The post cannot merely sit
beside the road; the road, shoulder, retaining work, threshold, room, and deck must
explain one another.

### 3.3 Terrain form law

- A sparse macro control surface authors the shoulder as one landform.
- Graded corridors cut/fill the road and flank route through that landform.
- Ordinary natural neighbours negotiate complete shared edges and varied stand
  planes through the existing terrain-expression engine.
- Only licensed cut, retaining, cliff, stair, and construction boundaries make hard
  breaks.
- No independent tile terrain, diagonal zipper, repeated ridge bands, Minecraft
  stacking, or decorative terrain-angle noise is permitted.

## 4. Minimum Assetforge marriage

Wave 2 admits no new paid or generated asset by default. It first proves topology,
clay form, routes, contacts, four bearings, and battle identity using truthful proxies.

The minimum shared Assetforge applications are:

- **AF-GV-1 surface demand** — derive exact face-local demands only after stable real
  terrain/construction faces exist;
- **AF-GV-2 material family** — resolve related natural top/slope/cut and constructed
  wall/coping/deck families without making material state the owner of history;
- **AF-GV-3 world-surface projection** — bind those families to stable local frames,
  scale, masks, and trim/decal reservations;
- **AF-GV-8 proof packaging** — produce governed clay, four-bearing,
  gameplay/contact-sheet, grayscale, identity/material overlay, and changed-seed
  records from one plan.

Additional Assetforge applications specified for later admission:

- **AF-GV-9 contact witness** — verify foundations, posts, walls, props, sprites, and
  feet meet their support surfaces after projection;
- **AF-GV-10 silhouette and occlusion audit** — report role legibility and playable
  ground occlusion at each canonical bearing;
- **AF-GV-11 material-neighborhood audit** — detect scale discontinuity, repeated
  texture phase, implausible seams, and natural/constructed family leakage across
  adjacent faces;
- **AF-GV-12 causal-state channel audit** — require provenance for damage, repair,
  wetness, soot, wear, grime, and occupancy marks before projection;
- **AF-GV-13 fallback equivalence proof** — compare admitted asset and proxy for role,
  envelope, contact, interaction, collision, sight, and silhouette obligations;
- **AF-GV-14 asset-cost ledger** — attach triangle, material, texture, draw-call,
  memory, and load cost to candidate scoring without collapsing visual judgment into
  one score.

These are validators and routers inside the shared Assetforge envelope. They do not
duplicate Material Maker, ground, palette, trim, decal, sprite extrusion, renderer, or
runtime projection authority.

## 5. Complete receipt

Every successful compile emits `SynthesisReceiptV1` with:

- request, plan, mechanics, asset, SceneTray, and BattleMap fingerprints;
- named seed-chain derivations;
- retained source lineage;
- every initial and repaired candidate;
- hard rejections and component measures;
- named repairs and relaxations;
- every semantic reservation, surface demand, asset/proxy decision, and omission;
- semantic, spatial, tactical, continuity, camera, persistence, and projection
  validation;
- AF-GV proof evidence links; and
- performance and asset-cost measures.

Camera bearing and projection target are excluded from the committed plan
fingerprint. SceneTray and BattleMap must report the same plan and mechanics
fingerprints.

## 6. Verification and evidence

### 6.1 Deterministic and negative controls

- same request + seed produces byte-equivalent plan and receipt;
- changed seeds differ structurally while preserving defining obligations;
- all twelve Wave-0 negative controls fail for the expected reason;
- camera changes no plan or access fact;
- combat promotion changes no committed geometry;
- required-role omission fails even when every other asset resolves;
- fallback-only compilation still exposes all required roles.

### 6.2 Visual proof

The Guard hero and at least two changed seeds owe:

- neutral clay;
- all four canonical quarter-turn bearings;
- tactical route/zone overlay;
- identity/material overlay;
- grayscale hierarchy review;
- gameplay-scale and contact-sheet framing;
- hostile-bearing review; and
- at least one night case after the daylit form passes.

### 6.3 Play proof

- Tavern arrival → social decision → forceful escalation → exit → return;
- Guard arrival → each of the three plans → exit → return;
- PC-versus-enemy battle on the committed Guard plan;
- SceneTray-to-BattleMap fingerprint identity;
- save/load replay; and
- retained walk, combat, terrain, and manifest regressions.

## 7. Gate W2

Wave 2 passes only when:

1. no Golden number, fixture id, site id, hero seed, camera, or projection patch
   influences topology;
2. legal changed seeds pass without hand-authored rescue;
3. connected natural ground and constructed threshold read as one composition;
4. Tavern social circuits and Guard tactical plans are complete and playable;
5. all four bearings retain route, objective, and identity legibility;
6. SceneTray and BattleMap preserve the committed plan;
7. fallback preserves every required role;
8. no generated/admitted asset precedes stable geometry demand;
9. the receipt is complete and deterministic; and
10. retained game, battle, save/load, terrain, and walk gates remain green.
