### **XVI. The Tower: Trigger Effects**

The Tower represents sudden, violent upheaval, the collapse of existing structures, and the revelation of uncomfortable truths. It is the card of the "bolt from the blue"—destruction that clears the path for a new foundation. Under this card, the environment itself becomes an enemy, and stability is a memory.

---

**The Constant: "The Structural Collapse"**

The dungeon is physically reacting to the chaotic energy of the Tower.

- **The Effect:** Whenever a creature (player or monster) rolls a **Natural 1** on an attack roll or ability check, a portion of the environment (a ceiling tile, a support pillar, or a floor section) collapses.
    
- **The Toll:** The creature that rolled the 1 and anyone within 5 feet must succeed on a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw** or take **2d6 bludgeoning damage** from falling debris. The area becomes **Difficult Terrain**.

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

**The Constant: "The Internal Collapse"**

The faction’s hierarchy has been shattered. A sudden coup, a scandal, or the death of a leader has left them in a state of violent, paranoid reorganization.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**Faction Behavior (The Tower)**|**Narrative Implication**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Purge**|The faction begins executing its own members in a desperate attempt to find a "traitor."|
|**6-9**|Less Grounded|**The Radical Cleansing**|The faction burns their own headquarters to the ground, believing it was "corrupted," and moves into the wild.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Revelation**|The faction discovers that their "God" or "Leader" never existed. They lose all motivation and become a nihilistic mob.|

### **Trigger: Accepting Binding Agreements**

**The Constant: "The Tear-Down"**

The bargain requires you to destroy something you love or value to gain the power. You cannot build the new "you" until the old "you" is rubble.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**The Bargain's Mutation (The Tower)**|**The "Gift"**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**Power of Upheaval**|You can cast _Shatter_ at will (DC 14), but your own maximum HP is reduced by 5 permanently.|
|**6-9**|Less Grounded|**The Lightning Strike**|You can call down a lightning bolt once per day, but you are **Stunned** for 1 round afterward from the shock.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Total Reset**|You can choose to "Collapse." You lose all current levels and XP, but you can immediately re-build your character as a different class at the same level.|
