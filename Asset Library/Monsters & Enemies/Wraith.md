*p. 323 MM 2024 (Wraith); p. 280 MM 2024 (Specter)*

Wraiths are undead creatures born from extreme hatred and a refusal to accept death — but where wights retain memory and purpose, wraiths have been almost entirely consumed by their own negative energy. What remains is fury without focus, cold without comfort, and a Life Drain that mirrors the wraith's own experience of being emptied out. A wraith doesn't hunt prey for food or territory. It hunts because everything that still lives is an offense to what it has become.

The wraith's most dangerous quality is its incorporation — it exists partially outside the material plane, allowing it to pass through walls, floors, and ceilings freely and making it immune to nonmagical weapons entirely. A party without magical weapons or spells will find themselves fighting a creature they literally cannot damage. The wraith knows this and uses its incorporeal movement aggressively, attacking through walls and retreating into them.

Specters are lesser wraiths — shadows of the dead that retain even less coherence than a full wraith. A specter typically forms from someone who died in great anguish without the strength of will to sustain a wraith's full manifestation. They are common in sites of mass tragedy: plague houses, battle sites, collapsed mines. Where wraiths are singular and purposeful, specters cluster.

### Campaign Integration: Investigation & Clues

The presence of a wraith poisons an area on a fundamental level.

- **The Muted Warmth:** A wraith's habitual presence lowers the ambient life force of an area. Animals nearby are lethargic and skittish. Plants grow stunted and pale. Candles burn dim. This effect is subtle but cumulative — an area with a wraith for months feels profoundly wrong to anyone who stays for more than an hour.
- **The Life Drain Victim:** A creature that dies from a wraith's Life Drain rises as a specter within 24 hours. Finding specters clustered around a specific site — especially specters in different states of "age" — indicates a wraith has been active here for some time and has been generating minions.
- **The Daylight Limit:** Wraiths cannot enter areas of sunlight. Their territory is strictly nocturnal or underground. Mapping the wraith's reach reveals the shape of a dungeon even before exploration.

### 1. Specter

A lesser undead spirit of desperate anguish — a ghost that never fully cohered, trailing through the places where it died.

_Medium Undead, Chaotic Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 22 (5d8)
- **Speed:** 0 ft., Fly 50 ft. (hover)
- **STR:** 1 (−5) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 11 (+0) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 11 (+0)
- **Damage Resistances:** Acid, Fire, Lightning, Thunder; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** Cold, Necrotic, Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** understands all languages it knew in life, but cannot speak
- **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Incorporeal Movement:** The specter can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.
- **Sunlight Sensitivity:** While in sunlight, the specter has Disadvantage on attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws.

**Actions**

- **Life Drain:** _Melee Spell Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 10 (3d6) necrotic damage. The target must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or its hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the damage taken. This reduction lasts until the creature finishes a long rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its hit point maximum to 0.

### 2. Wraith

An undead creature of pure negative energy and consuming hatred, fully incorporeal and immune to nonmagical damage.

_Medium Undead, Neutral Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Hit Points:** 67 (9d8 + 27)
- **Speed:** 0 ft., Fly 60 ft. (hover)
- **STR:** 6 (−2) | **DEX:** 16 (+3) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 12 (+1) | **WIS:** 14 (+2) | **CHA:** 15 (+2)
- **Damage Resistances:** Acid, Fire, Lightning, Thunder; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** Cold, Necrotic, Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** the languages it knew in life
- **Challenge:** 5 (1,800 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +3

**Traits**

- **Incorporeal Movement:** The wraith can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.
- **Sunlight Sensitivity:** While in sunlight, the wraith has Disadvantage on attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws.

**Actions**

- **Life Drain:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 21 (4d8 + 3) necrotic damage. The target must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or its hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the damage taken. This reduction lasts until the target finishes a long rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its hit point maximum to 0.
- **Create Specter:** The wraith targets a humanoid within 10 ft. that has been dead for no longer than 1 minute and died violently. The spirit rises as a specter in the space of its corpse or in the nearest unoccupied space. The specter is under the wraith's control. The wraith can have no more than seven specters under its control at one time.

### d10 The Draining Dark (Investigation & Clues)

| d10 | Discovery | Investigative Hook |
|:----|:----------|:------------------|
| **1** | **The Stunted Plants** | All organic material in the room is desiccated and pale — not from drought, but from sustained life-draining proximity. A DC 12 Nature check confirms this is magical depletion, not environmental. |
| **2** | **The Specter Ages** | The specters in the room are in visibly different states of coherence — some fresh (weeks old), some barely present (years old). A DC 14 Arcana check dates the oldest specter to approximately when the original tragedy occurred. |
| **3** | **The Maximum HP Drop** | The party finds a body of a previous adventurer whose equipment is undamaged. A DC 12 Medicine check reveals no physical cause of death. Their hit point maximum was reduced to zero — a wraith kill. |
| **4** | **The Cold Imprint** | An impression in the air — not visible, but cold — where the wraith habitually rests. Standing in the impression feels like standing in a grave. Items left in the impression lose their warmth permanently. |
| **5** | **The Missing Memory** | An NPC who passed through this area recently cannot remember the last four hours of their life. A wraith does not simply drain life — it can briefly reach into the psyche of creatures it passes near and erode their experiences. |
| **6** | **The Specter's Object** | One specter keeps returning to the same location in the room — a specific floor tile, a specific corner. A DC 13 Investigation check reveals an object buried beneath or hidden in that location. Whatever is there was important to the specter in life. |
| **7** | **The Wraith's Name** | Carved into the floor in archaic script is a name — the wraith's name in life. A DC 14 History check traces the name to a historical figure from this region who died violently and unjustly decades or centuries ago. Using the name in the wraith's presence causes it to momentarily hesitate. |
| **8** | **The Immunity Record** | A cleric's battle notes describe a fight with this wraith. The notes detail what didn't work (nonmagical weapons) and what briefly worked (a *turn undead* that pushed it back, a *daylight* spell that forced it to retreat). The notes end mid-page — the cleric didn't survive, but their research did. |
| **9** | **The Anchor Object** | A DC 15 Arcana check identifies that the wraith is unusually tied to this specific room — it cannot move more than 60 ft. from a specific object. The object is maintaining or creating the wraith. Destroying it may destroy the wraith — or release it from the binding. |
| **10** | **The Tragedy Site** | Evidence of what created the wraith is present: records of an injustice, the site of a betrayal, the remains of a mass death. Understanding the tragedy doesn't stop the wraith. But it might suggest what would finally let it rest — if the party is willing to try that approach instead of violence. |
