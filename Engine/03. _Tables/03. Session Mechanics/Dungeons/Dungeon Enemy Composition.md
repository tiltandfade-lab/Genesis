| **d20** | **Composition** | **Roster** | **Tactical Deployment** |
| :------ | :-------------- | :--------- | :---------------------- |
| **1** | The Horde | 2d6+2 Low CR | **Overwhelm:** Rush through the entrance in mass. They chain the Help action and target whoever is bottlenecked in the doorway or corridor first. |
| **2** | The Horde | 2d6+2 Low CR | **The Wave:** Splits to flank through any alternate entrance or passage. Bypasses the armored frontline to reach the unarmored backline. |
| **3** | The Staggered Mob | 2d4 Low CR | **Reinforcements:** Half the group is present. The others arrive Round 2 through a second entrance, a hidden passage, or from the room behind the party. |
| **4** | The Staggered Mob | 2d4 Low CR | **Drip Feed:** 1d4 start on the map. An additional 1d4 arrive on Initiative 0 each round through different entry points until the roster is depleted. |
| **5** | Frontline & Shooter | 1d4 Mid CR melee + 1d4 Low CR ranged | **Combined Arms:** Melee pins the party in the doorway or at the room's narrowest point. Ranged fires from behind them or from an elevated position. |
| **6** | Frontline & Shooter | 1d4 Mid CR melee + 1d4 Low CR ranged | **Draw Them In:** Shooters fire across the room then retreat around a corner or through a door. Melee waits in ambush at the choke point for pursuit. |
| **7** | Frontline & Shooter | 1d4 Mid CR melee + 1d4 Low CR ranged | **Phalanx & Volley:** Melee forms a Dodge wall at the room's narrowest point. Shooters fire over them into the corridor or entrance. |
| **8** | The Ambush | 1d4+1 Mid CR | **Surround:** All enemies begin Hidden — in alcoves, behind pillars, under debris, or in shadows. They attack simultaneously from multiple positions. |
| **9** | The Ambush | 1d4+1 Mid CR | **High Ground:** Positioned on a balcony, ledge, or elevated platform with three-quarters cover. They open with ranged attacks before descending or dropping into melee. |
| **10** | Boss & Fodder | 1 High CR + 2d4 Low CR | **Meatshields:** Fodder forms a wall in the entrance or at the room's narrowest point, providing Half Cover to the boss behind them. |
| **11** | Boss & Fodder | 1 High CR + 2d4 Low CR | **Sacrificial Pawns:** The boss drives fodder into room hazards or uses them to bait Opportunity Attacks, deliberately creating gaps to strike through. |
| **12** | Boss & Guards | 1 High CR + 2 Mid CR | **Bodyguard Phalanx:** Guards stay adjacent to the boss, Readying actions to intercept anyone who enters their reach. The boss focuses on the highest-threat PC. |
| **13** | Boss & Guards | 1 High CR + 2 Mid CR | **Pincer Support:** Boss holds the room's center using Dodge or defensive abilities. Guards split to flank and aggressively dismantle the party's healer. |
| **14** | The Controller & Thralls | 1 Mid CR controller + 1d4 Low CR thralls | **Seek & Destroy:** Controller stays at maximum range using abilities or spells. Thralls rush forward to Grapple or Shove spellcasters out of concentration. |
| **15** | The Controller & Thralls | 1 Mid CR controller + 1d4 Low CR thralls | **The Living Wall:** Thralls take Dodge in a line blocking the room's main entrance. The controller works freely from the far side of the room. |
| **16** | The Elite Pair | 2 Mid-High CR | **Tandem Strike:** Both creatures focus all attacks on one PC at a time, eliminating them quickly before turning to the next target. |
| **17** | The Elite Pair | 2 Mid-High CR | **Divide & Conquer:** The pair splits to engage from opposite sides of the room, forcing the party to divide healing and damage output. |
| **18** | The Solo Threat | 1 High CR | **Lair Mastery:** Uses the room's architecture — darkness, alcoves, elevation, trapped sections — before engaging. Forces the party to come to it on its terms. |
| **19** | The Solo Threat | 1 High CR | **Rampage:** Ignores Opportunity Attacks to smash through the frontline and reach the most isolated or vulnerable party member. |
| **20** | Interrupted Conflict | Two factions — roll twice on Category | **Crossfire:** Two enemy groups fighting in the room when the party arrives. Both ignore the party if left alone; both turn on the PCs if they intervene. |
