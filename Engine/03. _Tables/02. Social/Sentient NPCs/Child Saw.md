#child-saw# Child Saw — the witness table (d100)

> PARTIAL-NPC HOOK ATOM ([[NPC-PARTIALS]]) — a child's contribution to the plot is what they
> **witnessed**: they see what adults miss and don't know which part matters. This is the kid's
> hook-carrier roll, kid-framed — a real hook delivered in a child's literal, significance-blind
> voice. It is a lever/witness atom, NOT a full situation: no Band/Pressure/If-Ignored, so do NOT
> tag `table_family: situation` (same exemption as [[npc-want]] / [[child-want]]). Rows run roughly
> **mundane → uncanny** as the die climbs — the low ~55 are human secrets a sharp kid could really
> notice (now including a kid's whole native register: **treasure / found things**, **secret
> geography** the child's-eye map of gaps-fences-tunnels-caves, **benign misreads & pure wonder** so
> kids aren't only quest-dispensers, **animal / natural signs**, and **secret kindness**); the strange
> tier (~56–85) leans Strange, and the **86–100 tail** goes Mythic→Cosmic for Amblin/Stranger-Things
> realms. The uncanny tail is the minority — the DM reads which part matters. **Kin-agnostic /
> recontextualize** per convention — "the widow," "the stranger," "the old line" resolve against
> rolled atoms + codex. TONE: children witness horror but are never its graphic subject
> ([[NPC-PARTIALS]] safety rule). Tags in-format.
