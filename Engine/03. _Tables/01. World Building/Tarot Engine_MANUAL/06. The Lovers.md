### **VI. The Lovers: Trigger Effects**

The Lovers represents duality, choice, and the profound connection between two entities. Under this card, the world becomes a place of intense attraction and difficult decisions. The focus shifts from the individual to the "partnership," where every action has a symmetrical reaction.

---

**The Constant: "The Resonance of Two"**

A magical thread binds individuals together, sharing both their triumphs and their pains.

- **The Effect:** Whenever a player is within 5 feet of an ally, they both gain a **+2 bonus** to AC and Saving Throws.
    
- **The Toll:** If a player is more than 30 feet away from all allies, they take a **-2 penalty** to all Attack rolls and Skill checks, feeling the weight of a spiritual "severing."

|**10**|**Insane**|**The Universal Wedding**|Every member of the faction is suddenly magically married to a member of a rival faction. War stops, but household bickering is now a national crisis.|

The bargain links your life force to the Patron or another player. You gain immense power, but if the linked person takes damage, so do you.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**The Bargain's Mutation (The Lovers)**|**The "Gift"**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**Shared Vitals**|You gain a +4 bonus to your Constitution score, but your HP is now shared with an ally. If they die, you die.|
|**6-9**|Textured|**The Unseen Companion**|An invisible "Lover" follows you. They grant you Advantage on all rolls once per day, but they are incredibly jealous of your allies.|
