# BREADTH SWEEP — Site 6 Prison/Custody

"What else could this site be" against the ten form families the original packet already
accepted (watchhouse/lockup, keeper-house civic jail, ledger-and-shift jail, courtyard/bounded
liberty, gallery/parallel wing, radial observation, congregate work-yard, perimeter/transport,
adapted host, warded exceptional holding). This lane's job is to find what that list is missing,
not to re-derive what it already covers.

## 1. Demand evidence first — is Site 6 actually asked for?

Before sweeping for new forms, the existing intel corroborates that Site 6 is real, measured
demand, not a speculative addition to the portfolio:

- `docs/intel/walk-census.md`: the dungeon-type table's "Prison / Asylum" bucket lands
  **11/253 rolls (4.35%)**, mapped as a **direct** hit to Site 6 — no reclassification needed.
  The wilderness-arrival keyword pass separately finds **9** features mapping to
  "6 Prison / Institution."
- `docs/intel/marathon-spatial-mining.md`: across an 11-set, 110-turn marathon playtest, Site 6
  is independently named the **"cleanest golden-site hit in the whole marathon"** — three
  findings (sets 08/10/12) built around one counting-house cell/corridor/warden's-stairs
  location that "did real, sustained work" across "an entire pressure-theme's worth of scenes,"
  confirming Site 6 "as demonstrably demanded, not speculative."
- `docs/intel/tiyl-starts.md`: one TIYL tragedy row explicitly generates a prison-adjacent
  origin ("You were jailed for a crime you didn't commit — 6 years of hard labor"), flagged
  there as brushing "the unresearched site 6 if that is indeed the prison site (inferred from
  incidental context in the catalog, not confirmed in the read section)." This pass confirms
  that inference directly: yes, Site 6 is exactly that site, per
  `docs/GOLDEN-SITES-CATALOG.md`'s own numbering.

Site 6 is proven demand from three independent evidence lanes. The breadth sweep below is about
completeness of the family, not justification for the site's existence.

## 2. Confirmed by this pass: the form-family table needs one more row, not a rename

`MEASURED-REFERENCES.md` §1 and §3 supply two candidates that do not fit any of the ten existing
form-family rows:

- **the yamen — custody fused permanently into the seat of judicial/administrative power**,
  rather than a dedicated jail building anywhere on the spectrum from lockup to complex; and
- **ward-and-surety custody — no dedicated building at all**, custody realized as a social/legal
  claim distributed across an ordinary residential group.

Both are drafted as the requested "next doctrine pair" in `DOCTRINE-PAIR-TWO.md` rather than as
new form-family rows, because the form-family table (`synthesis.md` §7) is explicitly a
**premise** taxonomy ("one roll chooses a coherent premise") while a doctrine card is the
correlated-answer object the campaign brief asked for. Recommendation for Codex's review: fold
`PR-DOC-10`'s premise into the existing "adapted host" row's *inverse* (custody-as-origin-
function rather than custody-as-later-insertion) and add `PR-DOC-11`'s premise as an eleventh
form-family row, tentatively named **"distributed/social custody"** — proposed, not applied to
the shared `synthesis.md` file by this pass (that file is Codex's existing packet and out of
scope for a unique-path lane; see this study's `README.md`).

## 3. Swept and found already covered — no new site or family needed

- **Asylum / involuntary mental-health custody.** `docs/intel/walk-census.md`'s own table bucket
  is literally named "Prison / Asylum," collapsing the two under one roll result. The existing
  `PR-DOC-08 — Quarantine / Protective Isolation` card (`RESEARCH-NEEDED`) is scoped to
  involuntary *health* custody broadly and already names "observation, appeal/release criteria,
  emergency evacuation, and staff exposure" as its required questions — an asylum is a plausible
  occupant of that card once it is researched, not a new card. **Flagged for Codex:** the walk-
  census bucket conflating "Prison" and "Asylum" in one roll result is itself worth a rewiring
  note (a future spawn-audit item, not a Site 6 research gap) — see
  `docs/intel-sites/SITE-6-SPAWNABLE.md` §4.
- **Workhouse / poorhouse (economic, not criminal, custody).** Overlaps `PR-DOC-04 — Congregate
  Work-Yard` (evidence-backed candidate) on the labor-movement axis, but its admission logic is
  poverty rather than offense — a genuinely different authority/record circuit (§5's Fremantle/
  Robben Island evidence spine is convict labor, not poor relief). Recorded as a **future card
  candidate**, not built out this pass — no primary source was reached for it in the time
  available, so promoting it even to `RESEARCH-NEEDED` status would overstate this pass's work.
- **Monster / creature containment.** Already explicitly named in the accepted working spec's
  "Site boundaries and stretch" section ("monster containment... only when the host's own facts
  license them") and in the occupancy list's "warded exceptional containment." No new site or
  card needed; confirmed covered.
- **Religious/monastic penance confinement.** Already resolved by the existing Site 4 boundary
  law: *"Site 4 owns communal court/range/arcade and voluntary/house-rule living. Site 6 owns
  involuntary custody mutations."* A monastery cell used to hold someone against their will is
  already, by that boundary, a Site 6 custody mutation over a Site-4-shaped host — no new
  family needed.
- **Prisoner-of-war / battlefield holding pen.** Already resolved by the existing Site 2
  boundary law: *"Site 2 Camp/Service: may host a temporary stockade, prisoner camp, staff
  bivouac, refugee/evacuation holding, or siege overlay. Site 6 owns custody facts; Site 2 owns
  temporary shelter/service organization."*
- **Shipboard / galley custody.** The Ottoman zindan source notes Baba Cafer's historical use for
  "galley slaves," and the Robben Island/Australian-convict evidence already in the original
  packet covers transport-as-containment. This is a licensed variant of the existing evidence-
  backed candidate `PR-DOC-05 — Perimeter / Transport Custody`, not a new card.

## 4. Two genuine edge cases, surfaced but not resolved — founder-adjacent, not this pass's to rule

- **Sanctuary as the inverse of custody.** Right of sanctuary (a fugitive claiming protection
  inside a church or other consecrated space, historically real in English and other European
  law) is a site where Site 6's own custody invariants are *deliberately suspended* by a
  competing claim. This reads as a `LayeredControl` (Site 8) case per
  `GOLDEN-SITE-ONTOLOGY-ENGINE-MARRIAGE.md` §5 — two persistent claims (the pursuing authority's
  custody claim and the sanctuary-granting institution's protective claim) over the same
  person — rather than a Site 6 form in its own right. Flagged for whichever session next
  advances Site 8's promised "two unlike hosts" proof (`GOLDEN-SITE-ONTOLOGY-ENGINE-MARRIAGE.md`
  §11 item 2); not built out here.
- **Property-only impoundment (no held person).** Seized ships, impounded livestock, or
  confiscated goods held by an authority without any person in custody stress-test whether
  Site 6's family requires a held *person* at all, or whether "custody" as a semantic role can
  attach to property alone. The accepted "What makes it a site family" section already implies a
  person is required ("involuntary or conditional custody... of people and their property"), so
  this reads today as **out of Site 6's scope** — a property-only impoundment is closer to an
  `BF-CUSTODY`-adjacent record inside an ordinary civic/harbor/market venue than a Golden Site.
  Recorded as a boundary clarification worth a single sentence in a future spec revision, not a
  gap this pass closes.

## 5. What this sweep changed about the site, honestly stated

Nothing in this sweep overturns the accepted family definition, the ruled PR1/PR2/PR3 build
order, or either existing doctrine card. It found: one genuinely new organizational premise pair
worth drafting as the requested second doctrine pair (§2, developed fully in
`DOCTRINE-PAIR-TWO.md`); confirmation that five other plausible "what else could this be"
candidates are already covered by existing boundary law and need no new work; and two edge
cases worth a single flagged sentence each rather than a build. This is a narrower, more honest
result than a breadth sweep that invents new site families to look thorough.
