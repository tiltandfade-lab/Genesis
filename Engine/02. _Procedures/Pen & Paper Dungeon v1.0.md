# Pen & Paper Dungeon v1.0

> A printable, dice-only dungeon generator. No software required.
> All tables use standard polyhedrals and bell curves (2d6, 3d6).
> Budget 5–6 rolls per room. Setup rolls are one-time.
>
> *Derived from Arcana Engine v0.25 · Dungeon Procedure v4.2*

---

# I. Dungeon Setup *(roll once before play)*

Roll each table once to establish the dungeon's identity. Jot the results on an index card — they inform every room you generate.

---

## 1. Dungeon Type (d10)

| d10 | Type | Original Purpose |
|:---:|:-----|:-----------------|
| 1 | Natural Cavern | No builder — carved by water, tectonics, or burrowing creatures |
| 2 | Subterranean Crypt | Burial complex — tombs, embalming halls, funerary shrines |
| 3 | Military Fortification | Defensive stronghold — barracks, armouries, kill-corridors |
| 4 | Infrastructure Hub | Functional complex — sewers, waterworks, granaries, vaults |
| 5 | Sunken Estate | Noble or mercantile residence — buried by disaster or time |
| 6 | Religious Sanctuary | Temple, monastery, or cult shrine — consecrated (or defiled) ground |
| 7 | Prison / Asylum | Containment facility — cells, wards, interrogation chambers |
| 8 | Laboratory / Workshop | Arcane or alchemical research site — experiment chambers, libraries |
| 9 | The Megastructure | Vast engineered space — dam, bridge interior, aqueduct, mine-works |
| 10 | The Living Hive | Organic or colony structure — grown, secreted, or infested |

---

## 2. Topology (d12)

*Sketch the map graph before populating rooms. Each topology tells you the shape.*

| d12 | Topology | Structure |
|:---:|:---------|:----------|
| 1 | The Spine | Rooms chain in a single sequence. 1–2 dead-end alcoves. Tension-forward. |
| 2 | The Branch | Main path forks into 2–3 parallel routes that reconverge before the finale. |
| 3 | The Loop | A ring of rooms with one or two cross-passages cutting through the middle. |
| 4 | The Figure-8 | Two connected loops sharing a central hub room. Mid-point pivot. |
| 5 | The Hub | Large central chamber with 3–5 radiating corridors to small clusters. |
| 6 | The Web | Multiple overlapping loops with cross-connections. High navigational complexity. |
| 7 | The Cascade | Rooms descend in tiers. A gatekeeper challenge separates each tier. |
| 8 | The Onion | Outer ring surrounds an inner sanctum. Access to the inner layer must be earned. |
| 9 | The Stronghold | Functional layout — barracks, kitchen, guard post — with defensible chokepoints. |
| 10 | The Ruin | Asymmetric and partially collapsed. Some routes blocked, others newly opened. |
| 11 | The Convergence | 2–3 distinct entry points funnel independently toward a single shared finale. |
| 12 | The Labyrinth Fragment | Dense cluster of short corridors and small rooms. Deliberate confusion. |

**Segment count:** Decide how many rooms (not counting the finale). 3–5 for a short delve, 6–9 for a full session dungeon. If the topology needs more rooms than you have segments, fall back: Figure-8 → Loop → Branch → Spine.

---

## 3. Threat Roster *(choose or roll)*

Pick or roll a threat identity appropriate to your tier. Write down three creature slots:

| Slot | Role | Example (T1) | Example (T2) |
|:-----|:-----|:-------------|:-------------|
| **Low CR** | Fodder, swarms, minions | Kobolds, Skeletons, Giant Rats | Gnolls, Shadows, Ghouls |
| **Mid CR** | Soldiers, specialists | Bugbears, Ogres, Wights | Trolls, Wraiths, Veterans |
| **High CR** | Boss, elite | Flameskull, Manticore, Bandit Captain | Young Dragon, Mummy Lord, Oni |

*Write your three picks on the index card. Every enemy encounter draws from these slots.*

---

## 4. Boss Archetype (d10)

| d10 | Archetype | Behavior |
|:---:|:----------|:---------|
| 1 | The Warden | Guards something. Won't pursue beyond its chamber. Fights defensively. |
| 2 | The Usurper | Seized control from the original inhabitants. Paranoid, traps everywhere. |
| 3 | The Parasite | Feeds on the dungeon itself — its magic, its dead, its prisoners. |
| 4 | The Herald | Serves a greater power. The dungeon is a staging ground for something worse. |
| 5 | The Bound Entity | Trapped here. Desperate, dangerous, possibly sympathetic. |
| 6 | The Architect | Built or reshaped the dungeon. Knows every passage. Home field advantage. |
| 7 | The Mirror | Twisted reflection of the party — similar class, opposite morality. |
| 8 | The Engine | Not a creature — a process, a machine, a ritual in progress. |
| 9 | The Broken | Once noble or powerful, now corrupted. Tragic. May plead mid-fight. |
| 10 | The Negotiator | Would rather talk. Has something the party wants. Has demands. |

# II. Per-Room Procedure *(5–6 rolls per room)*

For each room on your topology sketch, roll in order. The finale room uses Section III instead.

---

## Roll 1 — Room Shape & Size (2d6)

Roll 2d6. Read the shape, then roll the listed dice for dimensions.

| 2d6 | Shape | Dimensions | Exits | Notes |
|:---:|:------|:-----------|:-----:|:------|
| 2 | **Passage** — narrow corridor | 1d4×5' wide, 2d6×10' long | 2 | Chokepoint. Ambush territory. |
| 3 | **Passage** — wide corridor or gallery | 2d4×5' wide, 2d6×10' long | 1d4 | Doors or arches along the sides. |
| 4 | **Irregular** — L-shape, wedge, or natural alcove | 2d4×5' × 2d6×5' (longest axis) | 1d3+1 | Broken sightlines. Corners to hide behind. |
| 5 | **Circular / Domed** — rotunda or well chamber | 2d4×10' diameter | 1d3 | 75% chance domed ceiling. Central feature. |
| 6 | **Rectangular** — small chamber | 2d4×5' × 2d6×5' | 1d3+1 | Standard working room. |
| 7 | **Square** — medium chamber | 2d4×10' to a side | 1d4 | The workhorse. Most common result. |
| 8 | **Rectangular** — standard chamber | 2d6×5' × 2d6×10' | 1d4 | Functional hall, barracks, or shrine. |
| 9 | **Hexagonal / Octagonal** — unusual geometry | 2d4×10' across | 1d3+1 | Feels intentional. Purpose-built. |
| 10 | **Multi-level** — split floor, balcony, or pit | 2d6×10' × 2d4×10', height 1d4×10' | 1d4+1 | Elevation matters. Check for stairs/ladders. |
| 11 | **Grand hall** — large rectangular chamber | 3d6×10' × 2d6×10' | 1d6 | Impressive scale. Echoes. |
| 12 | **Cavern / Vault** — massive natural or engineered space | 4d6×10' × 3d6×10' | 1d6+1 | Cathedral scale. Multiple zones within. |

**Quick exits:** For each exit beyond the one you entered from, roll a d6: 1–3 = door, 4–5 = archway, 6 = passage. If door, roll d6 for state: 1 = open, 2–3 = closed, 4–5 = locked, 6 = stuck/barred.

---

## Roll 2 — Encounter Type (2d6)

Roll 2d6. This is the pacing engine — the bell curve means most rooms are exploration, with danger at the shoulders and special moments at the tails.

| 2d6 | Encounter Type | What Happens |
|:---:|:---------------|:-------------|
| **2** | **Lore** | The dungeon reveals its true nature. A mural, journal, spirit echo, or inscription that changes what the party understands about this place. Recontextualizes earlier rooms. |
| **3** | **Social / Weird Contact** *(strange)* | Something alive and willing to talk — but *wrong*. A trapped spirit, a talking head, a creature that shouldn't exist here. It knows something. It wants something. |
| **4** | **Enemy — Complication** | A creature *not* from your threat roster. An interloper, scavenger, or something displaced. Roll or pick from a different monster list. It doesn't fit — that's the point. |
| **5** | **Enemy — Threat Roster** | A fight using your threat roster creatures. Roll on the **Composition** table (Roll 3) and **Tactical Terrain** (Roll 4). This is the most common combat result. |
| **6** | **Problem / Obstacle** | The way forward is blocked, locked, or broken. A collapsed ceiling, a flooded passage, a sealed door, a mechanism that needs activating. The party must spend resources or creativity to proceed. |
| **7** | **Empty** *(atmospheric)* | No encounter — but not nothing. The room has character: old bloodstains, a cold draft, claw marks on the walls, a half-eaten meal. Roll **Atmosphere** (Roll 5) and let it breathe. Tension without threat. |
| **8** | **Problem / Obstacle** | Same as 6. A different obstacle. The dungeon pushes back. |
| **9** | **Hazard / Trap** | Environmental danger. A pit, a poison gas vent, a pressure-plate dart launcher, an unstable floor. Requires a saving throw or ability check. See the **Hazard** sub-table. |
| **10** | **Discovery** | Something valuable or useful — a cache of supplies, a map fragment, a magic item, a shortcut. Reward for exploring. The party finds something the dungeon's inhabitants missed or protected. |
| **11** | **Social / Weird Contact** *(unexpected)* | Someone who *shouldn't* be here. A rival adventuring party, a merchant, a prisoner, a faction envoy. They have information and an agenda. |
| **12** | **Lair / Set-Piece** | A mini-boss, a territorial predator in its nest, or a room that fundamentally escalates the threat. This is rare (2.8%) — when it hits, make it count. Use your High CR creature. |

---

## Roll 3 — Composition *(Enemy encounters only)* (d20)

Only roll this when Roll 2 gives you an **Enemy** result (4 or 5). This tells you *what* and *how they fight*.

| d20 | Composition | Roster | Tactic |
|:---:|:------------|:-------|:-------|
| 1 | The Horde | 2d6+2 Low CR | Rush and overwhelm. Chain Help action. Target the bottleneck. |
| 2 | The Horde | 2d6+2 Low CR | Split to flank through alternate entrance. Bypass frontline. |
| 3 | Staggered Mob | 2d4 Low CR | Half present. Others arrive Round 2 from a second entrance. |
| 4 | Staggered Mob | 2d4 Low CR | 1d4 start. Additional 1d4 arrive each round through different entries. |
| 5 | Frontline & Shooter | 1d4 Mid + 1d4 Low ranged | Melee pins at doorway. Ranged fires from behind or elevated. |
| 6 | Frontline & Shooter | 1d4 Mid + 1d4 Low ranged | Shooters fire then retreat. Melee waits in ambush at the choke. |
| 7 | Frontline & Shooter | 1d4 Mid + 1d4 Low ranged | Melee forms Dodge wall. Shooters volley over them. |
| 8 | The Ambush | 1d4+1 Mid CR | All Hidden — alcoves, pillars, shadows. Simultaneous attack. |
| 9 | The Ambush | 1d4+1 Mid CR | High ground. Three-quarters cover. Ranged opening, then drop in. |
| 10 | Boss & Fodder | 1 High + 2d4 Low | Fodder walls the entrance. Half Cover for the boss behind. |
| 11 | Boss & Fodder | 1 High + 2d4 Low | Boss drives fodder into hazards. Uses them to bait Opportunity Attacks. |
| 12 | Boss & Guards | 1 High + 2 Mid | Guards adjacent to boss, Readying intercepts. Boss targets highest threat. |
| 13 | Boss & Guards | 1 High + 2 Mid | Boss holds center with Dodge. Guards flank and target the healer. |
| 14 | Controller & Thralls | 1 Mid controller + 1d4 Low | Controller at max range. Thralls rush to Grapple casters. |
| 15 | Controller & Thralls | 1 Mid controller + 1d4 Low | Thralls Dodge in a wall. Controller works freely from behind. |
| 16 | The Elite Pair | 2 Mid-High CR | Focus-fire one PC at a time. Eliminate before switching targets. |
| 17 | The Elite Pair | 2 Mid-High CR | Split to opposite sides. Force the party to divide resources. |
| 18 | The Solo Threat | 1 High CR | Uses the room — darkness, alcoves, elevation — before engaging. |
| 19 | The Solo Threat | 1 High CR | Ignores Opportunity Attacks. Smashes through to the most vulnerable PC. |
| 20 | Interrupted Conflict | Two factions (pick two) | Two enemy groups fighting when the party arrives. Both turn on PCs if interrupted. |

---

## Roll 4 — Tactical Terrain (d10)

Roll for every room. Tells you the physical character of the space even outside combat.

| d10 | Terrain | Implication |
|:---:|:--------|:------------|
| 1 | Open kill-zone | Little cover. Positioning matters. Ranged dominates. |
| 2 | Scattered cover | A few solid pieces. Mobile fight. |
| 3 | Dense cover | Lots of cover. Ranged lines are messy. |
| 4 | Chokepoint | Doorway, bridge, or alley. Control space to win. |
| 5 | Elevation | High ground, balcony, ridge, or stairs. |
| 6 | Obscurement | Fog, dust, darkness, smoke, or crowd. |
| 7 | Difficult terrain | Mud, rubble, roots, ice. Speed and shoves matter. |
| 8 | Hazard lane | A strip or zone is dangerous — fire, collapsing edge, thorns. |
| 9 | Moving cover | Carts, crowd surge, swaying curtains, drifting debris. |
| 10 | Fragile terrain | Cover breaks. Floor gives way. Railings snap. |

---

## Roll 5 — Atmosphere (2d6)

One roll for the room's mood. Combines lighting, sensory detail, and dressing.

| 2d6 | Atmosphere |
|:---:|:-----------|
| 2 | **Unnatural silence.** No echoes. Sound dies within arm's reach. Something is absorbing it. Faint arcane residue on the walls. |
| 3 | **Pitch dark. Dripping water.** The floor is slick. The air tastes of iron. Something scratched tally marks near the entrance. |
| 4 | **Dim and dusty.** Cobwebs thick as curtains. Footprints in the dust — some old, some fresh. A toppled chair. Disturbed. |
| 5 | **Flickering torchlight.** Someone lit these recently. Smoke curls along the ceiling. Shadows jump. A faint smell of oil and sweat. |
| 6 | **Steady lantern glow.** Functional. Workmanlike. Crates stacked against a wall. A table with tools. This room was *used*. |
| 7 | **Grey and still.** Ambient light from no clear source. Stone dust. Bare walls. Nothing remarkable — just quiet weight. |
| 8 | **Warm air rising.** A thermal source below — forge, vent, or something buried. The stone is warm to the touch. Faint hum. |
| 9 | **Cold draft pulling inward.** Air moves deeper, not out. The room breathes. Frost at the edges. Something beyond draws air. |
| 10 | **Bioluminescent glow.** Fungal, mineral, or magical. Blue-green light clings to surfaces. Spore clouds drift when disturbed. |
| 11 | **Shaft of daylight.** Collapsed ceiling or vertical shaft. Plants have claimed the rubble. Birds or insects. A pocket of the surface world. |
| 12 | **Impossible geometry.** The room feels wrong — too large inside, angles that don't meet, a faint vertigo. The dungeon is *aware*. |

---

## Roll 6 — Secret Check (d6)

Roll once per room. **On a 1**, the room contains a secret. Roll d8 on the sub-table:

| d8 | Secret Type |
|:--:|:------------|
| 1 | Hidden treasure cache — non-magical valuables |
| 2 | Consumable magic item — potion, scroll, or limited-use object |
| 3 | Lore fragment — reveals dungeon origin or threat identity detail |
| 4 | Hidden passage — bypasses the next room or a known danger |
| 5 | Bound entity — spirit, construct, or trapped creature |
| 6 | Sealed object — requires a decision: open, destroy, or ignore |
| 7 | Map fragment or directional clue to a future location |
| 8 | Dormant magical device tied to the dungeon's threat |

**Reveal method:** The DM decides, or roll d6: 1–2 = Perception (visual), 3–4 = Investigation (touch/logic), 5 = Arcana/Nature (magical sense), 6 = only found by specific action (moving furniture, breaking a wall, etc.)

---

# III. Finale *(final room only)*

The last room on your topology. Use Rolls 1, 4, and 5 as normal (Shape, Terrain, Atmosphere), then replace the Encounter and Secret rolls with:

**Boss Encounter:** Use your **High CR** creature from the threat roster, played according to the **Boss Archetype** you rolled in setup. Place it in the room with tactical advantage — it's been here longest.

**Finale Type (d6):**

| d6 | Type | Description |
|:--:|:-----|:------------|
| 1 | Confrontation | Straight fight. The boss is waiting and ready. |
| 2 | Ritual in progress | The boss is doing something. Interrupt it or face consequences. |
| 3 | Prisoners / hostages | Innocents complicate the fight. Collateral risk. |
| 4 | Crumbling arena | The room is unstable. The fight has a time limit. |
| 5 | Negotiation possible | The boss will talk first. Violence is one option, not the only one. |
| 6 | Betrayal / twist | An ally turns, the objective isn't what it seemed, or the real threat is elsewhere. |

**Revelation:** After the finale resolves, reveal one piece of Lore that recontextualizes the dungeon. Tie it to the Dungeon Type and Boss Archetype. This is the "oh, *that's* what this place was" moment.

**Exit State (d6):**

| d6 | What happens next? |
|:--:|:-------------------|
| 1 | Sealed. The party must find another way out. |
| 2 | Collapses behind them. No return. |
| 3 | Opens into something deeper — a second level, a cavern, the Underdark. |
| 4 | A portal or passage to a distant surface location. |
| 5 | Clear exit. But the threat has spread — signs of it outside. |
| 6 | Clear exit. The dungeon is done. Quiet. |

| d12 | Hazard | Check / Save | Effect |
|:---:|:-------|:-------------|:-------|
| 1 | Pit trap (10' deep) | DC 12 Dex save | 1d6 bludgeoning. Climb DC 10 to escape. |
| 2 | Poison dart launcher | DC 13 Dex save | 1d4 piercing + DC 12 Con save or poisoned 1 hr |
| 3 | Tripwire alarm + falling net | DC 11 Dex save | Restrained. Escape DC 12. Alerts nearby enemies. |
| 4 | Collapsing ceiling section | DC 13 Dex save | 2d6 bludgeoning. 5' area becomes difficult terrain. |
| 5 | Pressure-plate gas vent | DC 13 Con save | Poison gas, 15' radius. 2d6 poison. Half on save. |
| 6 | Magical glyph (floor/wall) | DC 14 Dex save when triggered | 2d8 elemental damage (fire/cold/lightning — DM picks). |
| 7 | Flooded section (waist-deep) | Str (Athletics) DC 12 to wade | Half speed. Disadvantage on fire spells. Risk of drowning if knocked prone. |
| 8 | Unstable floor | DC 12 Dex save | Falls 10–20'. 1d6 per 10'. Opens a lower area or separates party. |
| 9 | Spore cloud | DC 13 Con save on entry | Poisoned. Hallucinations (disadvantage on Perception) for 1d4 rounds. |
| 10 | Swinging blade / pendulum | DC 14 Dex save to pass | 2d8 slashing. Blocks a 5' passage until disabled. |
| 11 | Extreme temperature zone | DC 12 Con save per round | 1d6 fire or cold per round. Covers 20' stretch. |
| 12 | Arcane feedback loop | DC 14 Wis save | 2d6 psychic. On fail, one random spell slot is expended. No slot = stunned 1 round. |

# V. Problem Sub-Table (d10)

*Roll when Encounter Type = Problem / Obstacle (result 6 or 8).*

| d10 | Obstacle | Bypass Options |
|:---:|:---------|:---------------|
| 1 | Collapsed passage — rubble blocks the way | Str DC 14 to clear (1 hr). Or find a crawl gap: Perception DC 13. |
| 2 | Locked iron door — no visible keyhole | Knock spell. Or find the hidden switch: Investigation DC 14. |
| 3 | Flooded room — water level chest-high | Swim (Athletics DC 12). Or drain it: find the valve elsewhere. |
| 4 | Broken bridge over chasm (20' gap) | Jump, climb down/up (Athletics DC 14), or improvise a crossing. |
| 5 | Magic barrier — shimmering ward | Dispel Magic (DC 13). Or find the anchor object in a nearby room. |
| 6 | Overgrown passage — thick roots/vines | Hack through (30 HP, resist fire). Or Persuade/Druidcraft to part them. |
| 7 | Mechanical lock — gear puzzle in the wall | Investigation DC 13 to solve, or Thieves' Tools DC 15 to bypass. |
| 8 | Alarm ward — triggers if crossed | Arcana DC 13 to detect. Dispel or Thieves' Tools DC 14 to disarm. Pass triggers Alert +1. |
| 9 | Sealed stone door — requires weight/pressure | 500+ lbs on the plate, or Str DC 16, or find a counterweight. |
| 10 | Illusory wall / misleading passage | Investigation DC 14 to notice. Walking through reveals the path. |

# VI. Loot *(simple per-room method)*

**Every room with an encounter** (not Empty or Lore): roll d6.

| d6 | Loot |
|:--:|:-----|
| 1 | Nothing beyond what the creatures carry |
| 2 | Coin — pocket change (T1: 2d6 sp / T2: 2d6 gp) |
| 3 | Coin — modest (T1: 2d6 gp / T2: 2d6×5 gp) |
| 4 | Coin + mundane useful item (rope, potion ingredients, a map, rations) |
| 5 | Minor magic item — common consumable (potion of healing, scroll, etc.) |
| 6 | Notable item — roll on your tier's magic item table or DM's choice |

**Boss room:** Always has the best magic item in the dungeon + coin. DM places deliberately.

**Deeper rooms get better loot.** If you're 3+ rooms deep, add +1 to the loot roll (max 6). Simple rule, no math.

# VII. Quick Reference — The Five Rolls

| # | Roll | Die | When |
|:-:|:-----|:---:|:-----|
| 1 | Room Shape & Size | 2d6 + dimension dice | Every room |
| 2 | Encounter Type | 2d6 | Every room |
| 3 | Composition | d20 | Enemy encounters only |
| 4 | Tactical Terrain | d10 | Every room |
| 5 | Atmosphere | 2d6 | Every room |
| 6 | Secret Check | d6 (+ d8 sub-table) | Every room (optional) |

**Encounter sub-rolls by type:**

| Encounter | Sub-Roll |
|:----------|:---------|
| Enemy (4–5) | → Composition d20, fill creature slots from Threat Roster |
| Problem (6, 8) | → Problem d10 |
| Hazard (9) | → Hazard d12 |
| Discovery (10) | → DM narrates. Loot roll at +2. |
| Social (3, 11) | → DM narrates. Roleplay encounter. |
| Lore (2) | → DM reveals dungeon truth. Tie to Type + Boss. |
| Empty (7) | → Atmosphere carries the room. |
| Lair (12) | → High CR creature. Use Boss Archetype tactics. |

# VIII. Alert Level *(optional tracker)*

| Level | State | Effect |
|:------|:------|:-------|
| 0 | Unaware | Normal activity. |
| 1 | Suspicious | Enemies cautious. Minor repositioning. |
| 2 | Searching | +1 creature to future combats. Defensive postures. |
| 3 | Mobilized | Boss fortifies. Reinforcements arrive from cleared rooms. |

**Increases when:** combat lasts 3+ rounds, loud magic, an enemy escapes, an alarm triggers.
**Resets when:** long rest outside, or narrative justification.
