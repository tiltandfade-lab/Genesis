This is a perfect logistical refinement. It creates a believable physical flow for the cult's operations and provides the players with a classic dungeon-delving choice: _Do we take the disgusting, hazardous route down the slippery body chute, or do we solve the puzzle/find the key to take the clean, secret VIP route?_

Here is the updated and finalized 2024-compatible module for Node 1, incorporating the heavy loading doors, the massive body chute, and the locked "exotic meats" walk-in closet.

# Node 1: The Prime Cut

### Header

- **Title:** Node 1: The Prime Cut
- **One-line use:** A high-end butcher shop serves as the false front for a subterranean death cult, but a clogged body chute and a paranoid owner threaten to expose the operation.
- **Adventure Promise:** The party investigates a pristine storefront where an artisan covers up damning graffiti and a drunk babbles the truth. Bypassing the paranoid butcher—socially or via the back alley loading doors—leads to a meat locker flooded with gore. They must brave the hazardous disposal chute or unlock the secret lift hidden in the exotic meats closet to descend into the cult's underworks.
- **Adventure Scale:** Levels 3–4
- **Tags:** Urban, Civic District, Investigation, Social, Stealth, Cult
- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Pressure Level:** Low/Medium (Driven by social tension and NPC paranoia; the butcher will not call the guards for fear of exposing his own crimes).

### The Set Up

- **Basic Narrative:** "The Prime Cut" is an elite butcher shop. The ground floor is a bustling retail space. In the back alley, heavy double doors receive shipments of livestock and, occasionally, trafficked humanoids. The back room contains a massive, 5-foot-wide disposal chute used to dump whole animal carcasses and bodies down to the cult below. Because the cult is currently using the lower levels for a massive ritual, this chute is clogged, and the back room is flooding with bloody sludge. The paranoid Butcher, Kinto, is panicking. Outside, a hired artisan covers up freshly painted graffiti ("The Nobility Are Eating Us"), a drunken vagabond babbles the same phrase, and a Sapphire Circle investigator sweeps the street with _Locate Object_. A Gargoyle perched on the roof silently acts as the cult's surveillance.
- **Why the PCs Care:** This is the primary intake hub for the cult. The PCs must navigate the street-level clues and the horrific back room to access Node 2 (The Processing Pens).
- **The Tracker's Branching Logic (Continuity):**
    - _If PCs arrived from the Sewers (Node 3):_ They already met Vesper the spy. The Tracker’s spell locks onto the PCs because they are carrying Vesper’s gear. The Tracker becomes an immediate ally, helping them squeeze the Butcher.
    - _If PCs started on the Surface:_ The Tracker is a quest-giver. _"My partner Vesper went into that shop three days ago and never came out. My focus points straight down into the earth beneath the floorboards."_
- **Pre-roll Tables to Define Scene:**
    - **d6 The "Exotic Meats" Closet Contents:**
        1. A heavy iron cage containing a living Rust Monster.
        2. Crates of alchemical preserving fluid that causes hallucinations if imbibed.
        3. A ledger detailing the exact cuts of humanoid meat preferred by specific nobles.
        4. A crate of perfectly preserved humanoid hands packed in salt.
        5. Several vials of _Oil of Taggit_ (used to sedate kidnappings).
        6. A silver signet ring belonging to a missing magistrate.
- **Define Maps and Tables Used:** The street level features a clean, 30-foot-wide cobblestone road and the shop's facade. The front room is a 20x20 pristine storefront. A heavy locked oak door leads to the 30x30 stone-walled Meat Locker in the back, which connects to the alley via massive loading doors. Off the main locker is a 10x10 Walk-In Closet for "exotic meats."
- **Starting NPCs:** Kinto the Butcher (Paranoid Cultist), 1 Artisan, 1 Babbling Vagabond, 1 Investigator (The Tracker), 1 Gargoyle (Watcher).

### Segment Descriptions

#### Segment 1: The Pristine Facade & The Alley

- **Narrative of segment and play flow:** The PCs arrive at the shop. They must piece together the environmental clues—the artisan, the drunk, the tracker, and the watcher on the roof—to realize the shop is a front. They must then figure out how to gain access to the back rooms.
- **What the PCs notice immediately:** A clean street. A refugee artisan pressing glass tiles over jagged red paint. A drunk muttering near a shrine. A figure sweeping a glowing crystal back and forth. A stone gargoyle on the shop's eaves. A muddy alley leading to heavy loading doors.
- **Skill Checks/revelations:**
    - _DC 13 Wisdom (Perception):_ Notice that the Gargoyle's stone eyes are physically tracking the Investigator's movements.
    - _DC 14 Wisdom (Insight):_ Looking through the shop window, the PCs see the Butcher sweating profusely and constantly glancing toward the back room. He is terrified.
    - _DC 15 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand):_ Pick the heavy locks on the back alley loading doors to enter the meat locker directly.
- **PC likely choices:**
    - Coordinate with the Investigator.
    - Walk into the front of the shop and intimidate or bluff the Butcher.
    - Sneak around to the alley and break through the loading doors.
- **Escalation and pressure keys:** The Gargoyle is under strict orders not to break its _False Appearance_ unless someone violently breaks into the shop or attempts to murder the Butcher in broad daylight.

#### Segment 2: The Meat Locker & The Chute

- **Narrative of segment and play flow:** The PCs enter the back room. The horror of the cult is revealed by the failing plumbing. The PCs must choose their descent: brave the clogged body chute or find the key to the secret door in the exotic meats closet.
- **What the PCs notice immediately:** The smell of blood and alchemical preservative is overpowering. Massive loading doors sit on the back wall. Heavy iron meat hooks hang from ceiling tracks, bearing massive animal carcasses. A 5-foot-wide iron chute in the floor is violently bubbling over with offal. A heavy, locked iron door leads to a walk-in cooling closet.
- **What does the DM read aloud?** The air in the meat locker is chilled and thick with the smell of old blood. Heavy iron meat hooks hang from tracks on the ceiling, bearing massive, butchered animal carcasses.

However, something is wrong with the plumbing. A massive, five-foot-wide disposal chute in the center of the stone floor is clogged. It is violently bubbling, slowly flooding the room with a foul, rotting sludge of animal fat and offal. Kinto the Butcher stands near a heavy iron door labeled 'Exotic Cuts', his apron stained, his hands shaking as he grips a meat cleaver.

- **Skill Checks/revelations:**
    - _DC 11 Constitution Saving Throw:_ Made by anyone entering the room. On a failure, the stench of the backed-up chute causes them to be _Poisoned_ for 10 minutes.
    - _DC 14 Charisma (Intimidation):_ Break Kinto's resolve. He drops the cleaver, hands over the heavy iron key to the Exotic Meats closet, and begs them not to tell his masters he failed.
    - _DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation):_ Search the Walk-In Closet. Behind the hanging "exotic meats," the back wall is false. A hidden keyhole requires Kinto's iron key to open the secret door to the clean lift descending to Node 2.
- **PC likely choices:**
    - Interrogate Kinto for the key.
    - Open the Walk-In Closet (Roll on the _Exotic Meats_ table).
    - Jump down the 5-foot-wide Body Chute. (Requires a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw to navigate the slick, clogged offal without taking 2d6 bludgeoning damage, depositing them violently into Node 2).
- **Escalation and pressure keys:** The floor is Difficult Terrain. If combat breaks out here, Kinto fights desperately. If blood is spilled in the sludge, 1d4 Swarms of Rats erupt from the clogged body chute on Initiative count 10.

### Clues

- **Clues & NPCs that can reveal them:**
    - _The Animal Carcasses:_ A DC 13 Wisdom (Medicine) check on the hanging meat reveals that these are legitimate animal carcasses. The cult _usually_ feeds animals to the stone; humanoids are only used for special rituals (explaining the wagon from Node 3).

### Item Stat Blocks

- **Treasures:**
    - **The Tracker's Focus:** A mundane crystal that acts as a spellcasting focus. It currently glows because the Investigator has cast _Locate Object_ to find Vesper's signet ring.
    - **Kinto's Key:** A heavy, jagged iron key that unlocks the Exotic Meats closet, the hidden door inside it, and the cages in Node 2.

### NPC Dossiers

- **Kinto the Butcher**
    - **Goal:** To maintain his lucrative, high-society lifestyle while surviving his obligations to the subterranean death cult.
    - **Leverage:** Extreme paranoia. He is terrified of the City Watch finding his basement, but even more terrified of the Cult Leader below. If the PCs prove they are more immediately dangerous than either, he will fold.
    - **Tell:** Wipes his hands obsessively on his apron; his eyes dart constantly to the massive body chute.
- **The Investigator (Tracker)**
    - **Goal:** To find their missing partner, Vesper, and expose the cult.
    - **Leverage:** Needs martial backup. Will gladly share intelligence if the PCs agree to act as muscle.

### Stat Blocks

- **Kinto the Butcher (Cult Fanatic):** _Use 2024 Core Rules Cult Fanatic._ (CR 2, Medium Humanoid. AC 13, HP 33. Multiattack: two melee attacks. _Meat Cleaver (Sickle):_ +4 to hit, 1d4+2 slashing).
- **Gargoyle:** _Use 2024 Core Rules Gargoyle._ (CR 2, Medium Elemental. AC 15, HP 52. Speed 30 ft., Fly 60 ft. _False Appearance:_ While motionless, it is indistinguishable from an inanimate statue).
- **Swarm of Rats:** _Use 2024 Core Rules Swarm of Rats._ (CR 1/4, Medium Swarm of Tiny Beasts. AC 10, HP 24. _Bites:_ +2 to hit, 2d6 piercing damage).

# Node 2: The Holding Pens

### Header

- **Title:** Node 2: The Holding Pens
- **One-line use:** A noxious underground staging area where cultists process bodies, hiding a vengeful captive, a deadly slime hazard, and a stash of skimmed loot.
- **Adventure Promise:** Descending into a mud-slicked slaughterhouse, the party discovers a caged teenager who warns them the cultists are right next door. To ambush the arguing thugs, the party must navigate hanging green slime, arm the captive, and silently operate a massive counterweight lift.
- **Adventure Scale:** Levels 3–4
- **Tags:** Dungeon, Sewers, Investigation, Hazard, Rescue, Staging Area
- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Pressure Level:** Low (No active enemies in the room, allowing the party to investigate and heal, but making noise will ruin their chance to ambush the thugs in the next zone).
- **Rewards & Consequences:**
    - _Rewards:_ Gaining Vulfio as an armed ally, discovering the thugs' stash of skimmed loot, gaining the element of surprise on the next room.
    - _Consequences:_ Taking acid damage from the slime, contracting the _Poisoned_ condition from the stench, alerting the thugs in Node 3 by making noise with the lift.

### The Set Up

- **Basic Narrative:** The PCs arrive at the bottom of the butcher's body chute or secret lift. This grim industrial holding pen is used to transfer heavy loads into the main aqueduct (Node 3). The air is thick with noxious fumes, and the floor is coated in deep, bloody mud. A rusted iron cage holds Vulfio, a 15-year-old street kid. Vulfio explains that an invisible spy in blue robes (Vesper) fell down the chute days ago, hid in the shadows, and finally snuck into the corpse wagon right before the thugs rolled it out just moments ago. A patch of deadly _Green Slime_ hangs from the groaning ceiling above the heavy counterweight winch needed to open the blocked passage forward. Just beyond the heavy iron portcullis, the PCs can hear the thugs violently arguing over a piece of dropped loot.
- **Why the PCs Care:** They need to clear the blocked passage to proceed. Vulfio provides vital exposition and offers to fight if armed, and the thugs' hidden stash of stolen valuables is buried in the mud.
- **Pre-roll Tables to Define Scene:**
    - **d6 Vulfio's Chalk Drawings (Clues drawn on the cell wall):**
        1. A crude drawing of a massive, jagged egg with teeth.
        2. Stick figures of the thugs prying gold teeth from the corpses.
        3. A drawing of the wagon, with a faint blue outline of a person hiding inside it.
        4. A warning: a stick figure melting under a green blob near the door.
        5. Tally marks counting the dozens of bodies sent down this week.
        6. A surprisingly accurate map of the Aqueduct (Node 3).
- **Define Maps and Tables Used:** A 40-by-40-foot industrial cellar. The walls feature mismatched mortar. The center of the room is dominated by a 20-foot patch of **Thick Mud** (blood and offal runoff). An iron cage sits in the southwest corner. The southern exit is blocked by a heavy iron portcullis, operated by a counterweight lift.
- **Starting NPCs:** Vulfio (Vengeful Teenager).
- **What happens if the PCs ignore it:** Vulfio is eventually retrieved by the cult and fed to the Devouring Relic in Node 5.

### Segment Descriptions

#### Segment 1: The Stench and The Stash

- **Narrative of segment and play flow:** The PCs drop into the room. They must immediately deal with the horrific stench and the difficult terrain. Investigation reveals the stash of loot the thugs have been secretly skimming from the processed bodies.
- **What the PCs notice immediately:** A choking, noxious stench. A floor coated in thick, bloody mud. Hanging animal carcasses swaying on ceiling tracks. Swarms of fat insects buzzing loudly. A heavy groaning sound from the ceiling architecture.
- **What does the DM read aloud?** Whether you took the clean lift or tumbled down the slick disposal chute, you arrive in a foul, dimly lit industrial holding pen. The choking reek of preserving chemicals and rotting meat hits you immediately, making your eyes water.

The stone floor is coated in a thick, clinging mud of blood and offal. Hanging from iron tracks on the groaning, settling ceiling are dozens of massive animal carcasses. The room appears abandoned, but fresh, heavy wagon ruts in the mud lead toward a lowered iron portcullis on the south wall.

- **Skill Checks/revelations:**
    - _DC 11 Constitution Saving Throw:_ Required upon entering the room. On a failure, the character is _Poisoned_ by the noxious fumes for 1 hour.
    - _DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Perception):_ Searching the mud near the chopping blocks uncovers a loose stone concealing a rusted iron box. This is the thugs' "skim stash" where they hide loot they steal off the bodies before giving the rest to the cult.
- **PC likely choices:**
    - Search the room and the mud.
    - Push the hanging carcasses out of the way to see the rest of the room.
- **Escalation and pressure keys:**
    - _Thick Mud (Hazard):_ The mud counts as Difficult Terrain. Because it is so clinging, any creature knocked _Prone_ in the mud must use _all_ of its movement to stand up, rather than half.

#### Segment 2: The Vengeful Teen and The Slime

- **Narrative of segment and play flow:** Moving deeper into the room, the PCs discover Vulfio in the cage. He tells them about Vesper and demands a weapon. To pursue the wagon, they must operate the winch, but a deadly hazard guards the mechanism, and noise will ruin their ambush.
- **What the PCs notice immediately:** A rusted iron cage holding a scowling teenager. Chalk drawings on the wall behind the cage. A complex counterweight lift system next to the southern portcullis. Voices echoing from the other side of the bars.
- **What does the DM read aloud?** Pushing through the hanging carcasses, you spot a rusted iron cage in the corner. Inside is a fifteen-year-old boy covered in grime, gripping the bars with white knuckles. The wall behind him is covered in frantic chalk drawings.

"Get me out of here," he whispers fiercely, pointing to the massive iron portcullis on the south wall. "The scum that run this place just rolled the corpse wagon through there. Give me a sword and some leather, and I'll help you gut them."

If you listen closely, you can hear echoing voices coming from the tunnel beyond the portcullis. Two men are violently arguing over a piece of dropped jewelry. To reach them, you'll need to use the rusted counterweight winch—but thick ropes run up to the ceiling, where a massive, quivering patch of translucent green slime hangs directly over the mechanism.

- **Skill Checks/revelations:**
    - _DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation):_ Figure out how to balance the party's weight with the heavy stone counterweights to silently operate the primitive elevator/winch without the rusted gears shrieking.
    - _DC 15 Strength (Athletics):_ Bend the bars of Vulfio's cage to free him without needing the Butcher's keys (found in Node 1).
- **PC likely choices:**
    - Arm Vulfio with spare weapons or armor.
    - Try to burn or freeze the green slime off the ceiling before touching the winch.
    - Brute-force the winch instead of solving the counterweight puzzle.
- **Escalation and pressure keys:**
    - _Green Slime Drop (Hazard):_ If a character operates the winch without removing the slime, the vibrations cause it to drop. The character must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw to dodge, or take 2d8 Acid damage.
    - _Noise:_ If the PCs fail the Investigation check on the counterweights, or choose to smash the portcullis, the resulting screech of metal alerts the arguing thugs in Node 3 (The Aqueduct), negating any chance of Surprising them.

### Clues

- **Clues & NPCs that can reveal them:**
    - _Vesper's Timeline (Vulfio):_ Vulfio explains that a "blue man" (Vesper) fell down the chute a few days ago, turned invisible, and hid in the room waiting for the wagon to move. He just slipped into the pile of corpses a few minutes ago when the thugs opened the gate.
    - _The Argonauts (Audio):_ The PCs can hear the thugs arguing: _"I saw it fall off the cart first! It's mine!" / "It's guild property, you idiot, hand it over!"_

### Item Stat Blocks

- **Treasures:**
    - _The Thugs' Skim Stash:_ A locked iron box hidden in the mud containing the loot they've stripped from the dead: 12 pieces of hematite (worth 10 gp each), 8 pieces of malachite (worth 10 gp each), a set of ivory dice with gold pips (worth 8 gp), 2 silver cups (worth 12 gp each), and a handful of bloody gold teeth (worth 15 gp total).

### NPC Dossiers

- **Vulfio (Vengeful Captive)**
    - **Goal:** To kill the thugs who locked him in a cage and get back to the surface.
    - **Leverage:** He knows exactly what the cult is doing and where the traps are. He will eagerly fight alongside the PCs if they give him a weapon (a dagger, shortsword, or crossbow) and any spare armor.
    - **Tell:** Scowls constantly, spits on the floor when mentioning the cult, hands are covered in white chalk dust.

### Stat Blocks

- **Green Slime (Hazard):** A patch of green slime drops on a creature that walks under it. DC 13 Dexterity saving throw to avoid. On a failed save, the target takes 2d8 Acid damage, and takes another 2d8 Acid damage at the end of each of its turns until the slime is scraped off (requiring an Action) or destroyed by cold, fire, or radiant damage.
- **Vulfio (If Armed):** _Use 2024 Core Rules Bandit._ (CR 1/8, Medium Humanoid. AC 12, HP 11. Speed 30 ft. _Melee/Ranged Attack:_ +3 to hit, 1d6+1 damage). _Note: If not given armor, his AC is 11._
