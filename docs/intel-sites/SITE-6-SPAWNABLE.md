## 7. Honest summary

Of roughly 35 concrete spawnables the working spec implies (comparable in scope to Site 5's
audit), **7 have a LIVE roller** (2 structural/context — Civic Center, Threshold — plus the
stocks/block/gallows trio, plus 2 occupant-role row-groups), **3 have LIVE rollers that are
generic-dungeon-prop or generic-hazard** rather than Site-6-specific state (lock/bar props;
unconfirmed hazard rows), and **the remaining majority — every site-owned structural atom
(cell/cage/deck/intake/property-store), every named mechanism, most named hooks, and all eight
operating circuits — are `NO-TABLE-YET`.** Three findings from this pass sharpen the audit
beyond what a straight re-derivation would show:

1. The Wilderness Feature table already contains a real, dimensioned disposition/execution
   structure (stocks, block, gallows — rows 105–107) that independently confirms this pass's own
   FFT finding (Golgorand Execution Site) and its Mesoamerican finding (trap-door access) without
   either research lane having known the table row existed beforehand — three unrelated sources
   converging on the same "raised platform + trapdoor" grammar is a stronger signal than any one
   of them alone.
2. Two NPC Hook/Job-Board rows (80, 189) and one Flaws-and-Secrets row (187) are already-rolled,
   already-live custody hooks that match the working spec's own hook language closely enough
   that a future wiring pass could plausibly promote them into Site-6-specific hook tables with
   comparatively little new authoring.
3. No role in the current corpus is named "gaoler," "jailer," "warden," or "keeper" as its own
   dedicated row — every custody-operator citation in this table borrows from the general City
   Guard or Watch role. This is a real, specific casting gap a future NPC-table pass could close
   cheaply, and it is distinct from (smaller than) the structural `NO-TABLE-YET` majority above.

This inventory changes no status and authorizes no build — it is the feed for a future
spawn-audit and wiring pass, matching `SITE-5-SPAWNABLE.md`'s own closing discipline.
