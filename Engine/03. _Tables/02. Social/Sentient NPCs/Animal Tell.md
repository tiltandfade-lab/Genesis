#animal-tell# Animal Tell — the living detector (d20)

> PARTIAL-NPC BREADCRUMB ATOM ([[NPC-PARTIALS]]) — an animal doesn't carry a thread, it **points at**
> one. The tell is a breadcrumb to a nearby hidden thing (a hook, a secret, a buried fact, a lie);
> it resolves into whatever it pointed at rather than ratcheting its own if-ignored drift. Lever/
> pointer atom, NOT a situation — no Band/Pressure/If-Ignored, do NOT tag `table_family: situation`.
> The DM decides *what* the tell points to from live state (rolled facts, codex, the room's hooks);
> the animal only insists something is there. Row 20 is the tell that points **past** the mundane —
> a hook into the uncanny. Tags in-format.
