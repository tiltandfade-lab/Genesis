## Synopsis

Rennick limped off High-Harrow into the Drover's Rest and immediately turned his L10 charm kit on the plot: he **charmed** the Bog-Iron Consortium's road-factor Costanza Vellani (failed save → landed), **Suggestioned** a Consortium safe-passage charter out of her (scoped honestly — the spell wrote the charter but could NOT extract her guarded secret), then over-reached asking a charmed factor to start a faction war (refused at her duty-floor, and his leaked "I know what's eating the garrison" repriced him from guest to a knowledge-asset to be seized). He rested to reload, walked into Harrowfield's factor-house on the conned seal, and **Dominated the senior principal Vittollo Salone** (failed save → landed) — who, unlike Run-3's clueless sergeant, actually HELD a secret and coughed the whole three-generation blood-debt (the Watch forged a charter to hang the Consortium's founding matriarch on High-Harrow's wall; the Consortium is a season from lawfully foreclosing the Watch into dissolution). Rennick then ordered the front fired early AND ordered Vittollo's suicide: the foreclosure moved the Consortium clock **+2 but dirty** (a coerced scandal, not a clean win), and **CAL-1 was honored** — the fresh save failed and the self-throat order LANDED lethally with no fictional out. The world then repriced him at faction scale: his last Dominate met a **save that finally resisted** (the house-warden), she named him "bender," the house sealed, and **the Consortium flipped Indifferent→Hostile**. He hit the **spell ceiling** (Mass Suggestion refused — not in an L10 Bard, no slot burned), froze the door-guards with Hypnotic Pattern (warden resisted a 2nd time), and vanished into the market onto the open road — free, hunted, legendary, a made nemesis behind him and the fog-debt deepening in his songs.

**Threads now:** Consortium HOSTILE and hunting the "Fool of High-Harrow"; a bloodied early-foreclosure scandal loose in Harrowfield's courts (Consortium clock 2/6, principal Vittollo Salone dead); the house-warden is a made nemesis who knows the suicide is a murder; Vellani (charm lapsed, repriced off-screen) holds a grudge and knows he claims garrison-secrets; the internal haunting front still sits 5/6 and Rennick carries its debt outward. He ends on the Harrowfield road, Day 2 ~09:00, HP 43/43, one hand a permanent ruin, L5 slots dry, Consortium safe-passage seal (now poison) + the writ in his coat.

**Theme-A verdict (charm/dominate the plot):**
- Failed saves LAND: charm (T2), Suggestion (T3), Dominate (T6), and the CAL-1 suicide (T7) all landed on failed saves; **CAL-1 honored** (the exact Run-3 violation was NOT repeated — one save, then the order lands lethally, no re-litigation).
- Saves also RESIST: the house-warden beat a Dominate (T8) and a Hypnotic Pattern (T9) — a consistent recurring hard-save NPC, proving the seat isn't rigged for the griefer.
- Short-circuit a whole front with one failed save: **partial** — a dominated principal CAN fire the front's payoff (Consortium clock +2) but it fires DIRTY (coerced/traceable = a scandal, not a clean win). The honest answer the theme was probing.
- World reprices him: **yes, and mechanically** — Vellani Indifferent→Friendly (charm), then Consortium Indifferent→**Hostile** (faction-scale) after he broke their principal; committed via `attitude_shift`+`social_check`, not just narrated.
- Dominate extracts real secrets: **yes** — a dominated NPC who actually holds a secret yields it (deliberate contrast to Run-3's sergeant who didn't know); the spell can't confess what the target doesn't hold.
- Spell ceiling enforced: Mass Suggestion (6th) uncastable at L10, refused with no slot spent (WAI).

A seal on the strongbox: a bog-iron sigil, the mark of the **Bog-Iron Consortium** — law and ledgers and old debts collected slow. She has been working the drovers one at a time, stamping their road-charters, taking their coin with a clerk's dead courtesy. She has already marked you. Of course she has. You are the maimed stranger the whole pass will be talking about by noon, and **Costanza Vellani** is a woman whose entire trade is knowing which strangers are worth a line in the book. She sets down her stamp. She waits, over the heads of the drovers, to see what you'll make of her.
