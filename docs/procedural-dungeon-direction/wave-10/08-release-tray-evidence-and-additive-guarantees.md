# Wave 10 — Release-Tray Evidence and Additive Guarantees

Continue the chronological Wave 10 record from
[Part 07](07-performance-device-accessibility.md). P10.8-P10.12 and additive G10.1-G10.2 are closed on their phased
bases. Wave 10 remains open pending its separate final audit and Adam's explicit closure; no build is authorized.

### 11.121 P10.12 deep-dive setup and F10.12a-F10.12j batch - corpus staging, candidates, same-state control, decision law, beauty, comprehension, performance, effort, preference, and release selection

The accepted P10.12 baseline is a same-state representation bakeoff. It inherits Wave 2's twelve named golden-site
families, eight adversarial transition traces, and deterministic/batch/human proof layers; the twenty VQ battle-
scene frames remain visual targets rather than canonical layouts. Required cases include dungeon, town/social,
wilderness/exploration, travel/map, battle, secret reveal, CrisisChain, party separation/participation roles,
tiny/huge scale, moving vehicle/platform, destruction, persistent aftermath, missing-asset fallback, low-tier
device, and accessibility. Comparison dimensions are deterministic projection/provenance, gameplay comprehension,
beauty at gameplay size, transition continuity, performance/memory, accessibility, production effort, and human
preference.

The Clay Proof Ladder has already corrected one dangerous reading: the twelve sites and eight transitions are the
eventual retained portfolio, **not the first implementation batch**. P10.12 must lock how evidence accumulates and
how it decides a release direction without demanding unavailable hardware, paid research, or a fully built engine
today.

Decision-authority labels in this batch are deliberate:

- **Codex/evidence default:** Adam need not manufacture a technical opinion now.
- **Adam taste at retained evidence:** the question returns only when real gameplay-scale captures exist.
- **Funded human evidence later:** current solo testing may prepare the seam but cannot substitute for players.
- **Adam final product decision:** the release selection cannot be delegated to a score or technical green.

#### 1. F10.12a - how should the twelve-site/eight-transition corpus be built and applied?

**Decision authority: Codex/evidence default.**

**Option A - require the entire portfolio before any renderer or tray milestone may pass.** Thorough in appearance,
but it recreates a giant engine batch: a small dungeon attack cannot pass until a mobile anomaly, urban institution,
dragon domain, cold reactivation, and every transition exist. Cost is extreme QA/content delay.

**Option B - lock the complete portfolio as the destination but accumulate retained fixtures one risk at a time
(recommended).** C1 begins with one small deterministic dungeon room; later passes retain multi-room, operating
site, relational, town, travel, vehicle, and exceptional fixtures until the twelve/eight portfolio is complete. Each
candidate renderer consumes every fixture available at its decision stage; it cannot cherry-pick. The provider-
neutral DM seat receives the same canonical snapshot and receipts in every view. Cost is medium incremental
authoring with high long-term regression value.

**Option C - use only three or four visually attractive scenes.** Affordable and useful for taste, but cannot prove
cold state, fallback, continuity, extreme scale, or causal simulation. Cost is low and false-confidence risk high.

**Prototype/MVP/ideal:** prototype uses one retained dungeon slice; MVP accumulates the representative core modes
needed for a release candidate; the ideal reaches all twelve golden sites, eight adversarial transitions, and later
statistical/human layers. Exact addition order follows uncovered risk, not content quota.

#### 2. F10.12b - which representation candidates deserve the bakeoff?

**Decision authority: Codex/evidence default now; Adam may add a genuinely distinct viable candidate.**

**Option A - compare every historical mockup and renderer experiment.** This preserves history but spends capture/
maintenance effort on superseded range strips, debug boards, and arrangements that no longer express the accepted
shell or mechanics. Cost is very high noise.

**Option B - compare only live, materially distinct candidates with an honest common contract (recommended).** The
portfolio starts with the existing full 3D theater as laboratory/control and the accepted BattleMat plus derived
EngagementLens release-direction candidate; top-down/range/relationship alternatives are evaluated as fallback/
accessibility projections, not falsely equal mechanics owners. Add a third candidate only if it can run the same
state and offers a credible product tradeoff. Every provider-neutral DM seat uses the same facts/actions. Cost is
medium capture/adapter maintenance.

**Option C - evaluate only the new BattleMat direction.** Cheapest and focused, but provides no honest control for
whether the new tray improves or regresses the existing theater. Cost is low and selection evidence weak.

**Prototype/MVP/ideal:** one same-state old/new capture pair; then the available retained core corpus; finally any
new candidate must earn admission by implementing the contract and presenting a distinct plausible release choice.

#### 3. F10.12c - what must remain identical across same-state comparisons?

**Decision authority: Codex/evidence default.**

**Option A - replay the scenario separately in each renderer and compare similar moments.** Easy, but different
rolls, timing, model prose, camera focus, or action choices can masquerade as visual quality differences.

**Option B - freeze a canonical evidence envelope and vary only permitted presentation products (recommended).**
The envelope pins world/scene versions, rolls, owners, cast/ids, positions/precision, objects/custody, hazards,
knowledge/viewpoint, actions, receipts, consequence cursor, DM fact anchors/fallback clause id, quality tier, and
capture checkpoint. Candidate adapters may change layout, camera, art binding, animation, and accessible projection
inside their contracts. If live provider prose is compared, its output is labeled a separate language test rather
than visual state. Cost is medium fixture/envelope tooling and very high experimental honesty.

**Option C - let each candidate present its best tailored version of the scene.** Attractive portfolios result, but
the renderer with an easier room, fewer citizens, or better narration may appear superior without being so. Cost is
medium curation and invalid comparison risk.

**Prototype/MVP/ideal:** one frozen attack/door/hazard/custody trace first; MVP applies the envelope to the retained
core corpus; ideal automates replay/capture while preserving inspectable provenance.

#### 4. F10.12d - how should evidence combine into a pass or selection decision?

**Decision authority: Codex/evidence default for the gate shape; Adam retains final selection.**

**Option A - calculate one weighted score and select the highest total.** Simple ranking, but exceptional beauty can
mathematically cancel lost hazards, inaccessible actions, false geometry, or unusable performance.

**Option B - use hard truth/safety vetoes followed by a transparent multidimensional comparison (recommended).** A
candidate first passes canonical/provenance equivalence, legal action availability, deterministic recovery,
truthful fallback, bounded accessibility equivalence, and the applicable sustained performance floor. Surviving
candidates are compared—not collapsed invisibly—on comprehension, beauty, continuity, frame/memory headroom,
production/maintenance effort, human preference, and promotion potential. Adam sees the tradeoffs and chooses the
release direction; the score never overrules a hard veto or founder taste. Cost is medium rubric/evidence work.

**Option C - let Adam choose only by visual preference.** Founder taste is essential, but a beautiful capture can
hide input, state, recovery, performance, or production failures. Cost is low rubric work and high product risk.

**Prototype/MVP/ideal:** prototype uses a short veto checklist plus side-by-side notes; MVP fills the locked
dimensions for retained core cases; ideal adds broader human/statistical evidence without hiding tradeoffs in one
number.

#### 5. F10.12e - what constitutes passing beauty at gameplay scale?

**Decision authority: Adam taste at retained evidence.**

**Option A - pass when a renderer implements the required lighting/material/sprite feature checklist.** Objective
to inspect, but every feature can exist while the dungeon remains muddy, toy-like, empty, noisy, badly composed, or
inferior to the accepted target.

**Option B - require native gameplay-scale retained captures to meet the accepted art-direction relationships and
receive explicit Adam visual acceptance (recommended).** The dungeon must read as one intentional tabletop stage:
clear structural composition and route, honest elevation/cover, coherent material/light hierarchy, grounded sprite
citizenship, purposeful negative space, a dominant visual story, and no debug-board or asset-collage read. The VQ
and accepted mock frames calibrate relationships and ambition, not copied layouts or mandatory identical richness
for every scene. DM prose cannot rescue a visually failed room. Cost is medium recurring capture/review iteration.

**Option C - infer beauty from player preference scores or image-quality metrics.** Helpful evidence, but neither a
small sample nor automated similarity defines Genesis's visual identity. Cost varies and authorship becomes weak.

**Prototype/MVP/ideal:** Adam judges the first real small-room and battle captures; MVP applies the accepted floor
to representative core modes; ideal expands beauty across the twelve-site portfolio and high-tier hardware. No
opinion is requested until the capture is real.

#### 6. F10.12f - what proves gameplay comprehension rather than mere mechanical completeness?

**Decision authority: funded human evidence later; Codex prepares the retained task structure.**

**Option A - verify that every fact appears somewhere in DOM, logs, or renderer data.** Necessary, but presence does
not prove a player notices the burning exit, understands reachability, finds the relevant object, or follows a
consequence.

**Option B - combine deterministic fact parity with short task-based comprehension evidence (recommended).** A
retained dungeon task asks the player to identify the immediate threat, legal route, known hazard, usable object,
target/outcome, and material aftermath without being coached by the DM. The DM seat may narrate naturally, but a
provider change cannot remove the required visual/text handles. Developer walkthroughs scaffold the prototype;
representative compensated testing is required before external usability claims. Cost is medium task/fixture work
now and high funded research later.

**Option C - ask whether players generally liked or understood the scene.** Cheap, but broad sentiment cannot locate
which projection, cue, prose, or interaction failed. Cost is low and diagnostic value weak.

**Prototype/MVP/ideal:** developer-retained tasks now; small compensated representative sessions at the relevant
playable claim; broader cohorts/modes only when funding and product stage justify them.

#### 7. F10.12g - how does performance evidence enter the bakeoff?

**Decision authority: Codex/evidence default under closed P10.10.**

**Option A - compare average FPS from one visually representative scene.** Cheap, but hides phase spikes, assembly,
input response, memory growth, and fallback behavior.

**Option B - replay identical phase-marked envelopes and apply the P10.10 law (recommended).** On the disclosed Mac
prototype, record frame distributions, sustained-under-floor runs, input-to-visible response, assembly/reveal,
memory trend where available, and provider time separately for exploration, dense battle, EngagementLens,
transition, and aftermath. A coherent low tier and forced-slow fallback are part of the evidence. Named release
hardware and high-tier cloud comparison wait for their funded triggers. Cost is medium local trace/corpus work and
approximately zero new cash now.

**Option C - exclude performance until the prettiest renderer is chosen.** Focused art direction initially, but may
select a representation whose geometry, materials, draw calls, or transition model cannot meet the accepted floor.
Cost is deferred and potentially high rewrite.

**Prototype/MVP/ideal:** local phase traces first; measured named-device gates when release claims become material;
broader/high-tier/field evidence only after the connected slice and funding justify it.

#### 8. F10.12h - how should production and maintenance effort affect the selection?

**Decision authority: Codex/evidence default now; business weighting may tune later.**

**Option A - exclude authoring/toolchain effort from visual selection.** This protects ambition but can select a
tray requiring bespoke human construction, uncached generation, or dual-renderer maintenance for every new room.

**Option B - record marginal lifecycle cost as a visible dimension without letting cheapness excuse failed quality
(recommended).** For each retained fixture, log new bespoke assets, procedural recipes, generated candidates/
rejections, manual correction, integration time, cache/storage growth, fallback coverage, capture/QA time, and
renderer-specific maintenance. A dungeon tray must pass truth, comprehension, and beauty first; among passing
options, repeatable no-human production and bounded maintenance matter strongly under the solo budget. Cost is
medium accounting discipline and high strategic value.

**Option C - always choose the cheapest passing candidate.** Sustainable, but can prematurely flatten Genesis's
distinctive visual ambition when a modest extra system investment would amortize across every world.

**Prototype/MVP/ideal:** rough per-fixture ledger now; stable marginal-cost comparison across retained core modes;
ideal field economics and staffing decisions only when real production volume/funding exist.

#### 9. F10.12i - what human preference evidence is appropriate at the current budget and later stages?

**Decision authority: funded human evidence later; Adam remains current art director, not a pretend market sample.**

**Option A - treat Adam's preference as sufficient evidence of broad player preference.** Correct for authorship but
not for accessibility, comprehension, comfort, or market response.

**Option B - separate founder art direction, developer usability checks, voluntary feedback, and compensated
representative research (recommended).** Adam accepts visual identity; local retained tasks expose obvious defects;
lightweight voluntary feedback may inform but not validate; funded participants later compare comprehension,
comfort, preference, accessibility, and sustained play without being asked to perform free professional research.
DM providers and narration variants are controlled/labeled so preference is not misattributed to the tray. Cost is
zero-to-low now and high only when claims/research promote.

**Option C - run large unpaid public polls on static screenshots.** Cheap reach, but self-selection, context loss,
unknown displays, and no actual interaction produce noisy beauty votes rather than release evidence.

**Prototype/MVP/ideal:** Adam plus internal retained review now; targeted compensated sessions for material playable
claims; broader preference evidence only near a funded product decision.

#### 10. F10.12j - what evidence permits selecting the release tray and retiring alternatives?

**Decision authority: Adam final product decision at evidence; no decision requested now.**

**Option A - automatically select the first candidate that passes the minimum technical gates.** Efficient, but the
first adequate renderer may not be the best Genesis identity or long-term production path.

**Option B - require hard-gate survival, sufficient representative retained evidence, transparent tradeoffs, and
Adam's explicit selection (recommended).** The candidate proves same-state truth/recovery/fallback, accepted input/
accessibility/performance obligations, gameplay comprehension, gameplay-scale beauty, transition continuity, and a
credible production lifecycle across the corpus appropriate to that milestone. Alternatives remain laboratories
until the retirement gate in P10.11 is met; archival evidence and rollback/repair survive retirement. No score or
DM provider selects the product. Cost is medium-high evidence work, paid incrementally rather than upfront.

**Option C - never select one release direction; permanently support every viable tray.** Preserves choice and
experimentation but multiplies asset, input, accessibility, performance, migration, and QA work indefinitely.

**Prototype/MVP/ideal:** no release selection from the first clay room; provisional convergence grows through
retained evidence; Adam selects only when the corpus is representative enough for the actual product milestone;
ideal retirement and future renderer promotion remain reversible.

**Codex recommends Option B for all ten**, with the authority split stated above. Questions 1-4, 7, and 8 can use
the recommended evidence architecture now. Question 5 returns to Adam on real captures; Questions 6 and 9 require
later human evidence appropriate to budget; Question 10 returns to Adam for explicit product selection after the
gates are populated. Answers may generate narrower questions about corpus admission, veto definitions, capture
comparability, or sufficient evidence. P10.12 and Wave 10 remain open.

### 11.123 P10.12 generated follow-up disposition - Codex-carried fixture admission, veto, capture, provider, sufficiency, iteration, human-claim, and selection-record rules

The section 11.121 batch generated narrower questions about corpus admission, veto definitions, capture
comparability, and sufficient evidence. Under the accepted authority split, these are evidence-method decisions,
not additional taste votes.

1. **Every retained fixture has an admission record.** It names its canonical seed/snapshot and versions, owning
   system/pass, coverage dimensions and why they were missing, expected receipts/terminal truth, allowed viewpoint/
   precision, capture/task checkpoints, fallback/degradation case, and deterministic rebuild procedure. A pretty
   image or hand-authored mock cannot silently become a golden fixture. Prototype records one dungeon envelope;
   later corpus tooling grows in Wave 11. Cost is low-medium metadata and fixture maintenance.
2. **New fixtures are admitted by uncovered risk, not quota or visual novelty.** The next retained case covers a
   missing size, purpose, state, group relation, Spice band, supply model, mode, transition, accessibility behavior,
   fallback, or failure class. One fixture may cover several dimensions if each is explicit. The twelve names remain
   obligations; their order stays adaptive. Cost is low planning discipline and avoids redundant content work.
3. **A representation candidate must earn bakeoff admission.** It consumes the shared SceneTray/projection and
   canonical receipts, has no shadow mechanics or candidate-only content, can mount the currently required retained
   envelopes, supplies truthful local fallback, identifies its maintenance owner, and offers a materially distinct
   plausible product tradeoff. A static concept or reskin remains reference evidence, not a live candidate. Cost is
   medium adapter/fixture proof and prevents comparison noise.
4. **Hard vetoes are typed and milestone-scoped.** Repeatable loss/duplication of material identities/state,
   incorrect legal actions or geometry, secret/viewpoint leakage, divergent terminal recovery, missing truthful
   fallback, inaccessible core interaction/equivalent, or sustained failure of the applicable P10.10 floor blocks
   that candidate at the current milestone. A tooling glitch or uncalibrated later threshold is a diagnostic, not a
   fabricated failure. Vetoes cannot be averaged away by beauty. Cost is medium test/rubric work.
5. **Comparison uses both a matched evidence view and each candidate's best legal governed view.** The matched view
   holds viewport, device/build, quality tier, scene checkpoint, known facts, active subject, and broad framing
   obligation stable so differences are inspectable. The best legal view then shows whether a candidate's actual
   camera/composition grammar adds value without revealing secrets or hiding required subjects. Captures are native,
   unretouched except labeled crops/annotations, and record DPR/resolution/camera/build/asset hashes. Cost is medium
   capture tooling and storage.
6. **DM language is controlled separately from the visual comparison.** The primary tray bakeoff uses the same
   fact anchors and frozen context-slotted fallback clause/response artifact so narration quality does not favor one
   renderer. Live supported-provider runs form a separate conformance/experience layer and are labeled by provider,
   model/configuration, latency, and response hash where lawful. No provider chooses mechanics or candidate. Cost is
   low fixture work and medium later provider matrix work.
7. **“Sufficient evidence” is milestone-specific, not one universal sample count.** The first clay decision may
   answer one uncertainty with one retained room; a playable desktop MVP requires the representative core
   movement/object/hazard/battle/EngagementLens/consequence/recovery/provider-neutral traces and their relevant
   accessibility/performance cases; release selection requires the portfolio appropriate to the actual supported
   modes and claims, ultimately growing through the twelve/eight destination. Missing coverage is listed explicitly.
   Cost scales incrementally.
8. **A visual rejection produces a systemic hypothesis and retained regression, not a one-off decoration patch.**
   Adam may reject a room for composition, material response, sprite citizenship, lighting hierarchy, camera,
   clutter, or beauty. The next iteration identifies the renderer/projection/asset-contract cause, corrects the
   reusable rule, replays the rejected fixture, and checks previously passed fixtures. Taste remains Adam's; the
   correction must remain procedural and reproducible. Cost is medium iterative art-direction work with high reuse.
9. **Human evidence supports only the tasks, cohorts, devices, and claims actually tested.** Developer completion
   and voluntary feedback remain formative. A funded study records participant/recruitment scope, compensation,
   task/version/device/assistive-technology context, failures and qualitative findings without claiming universal
   preference or accessibility. Sample size is chosen with the eventual research owner for the bounded decision,
   not guessed in Wave 10. Cost is funding-gated and potentially high.
10. **The release-selection record remains inspectable and reversible.** It lists candidates/versions, admitted
    fixtures and missing coverage, veto outcomes, dimension evidence, known limitations, Adam's visual/product
    ruling, accepted promotions/deferred goals, old-renderer retirement status, and rollback/repair artifacts. It
    does not pretend a selected tray makes every ideal feature built. Prototype may use a Markdown evidence record;
    Wave 11/12 may automate it. Cost is low current documentation and medium later tooling.

These dispositions exhaust the generated evidence-method branches without deciding outcomes that require an
unbuilt renderer, real captures, funded participants, named release hardware, or Adam's final selection.

#### Contradiction audit

1. **One-room-first versus twelve-site proof:** the first room answers one uncertainty; it never claims portfolio or
   release completeness. Passed fixtures accumulate rather than being replaced.
2. **Founder taste versus evidence:** hard semantic/safety/accessibility/performance vetoes cannot be overruled by
   beauty, while no checklist or score can overrule Adam's authorship among candidates that remain viable.
3. **VQ targets versus procedural truth:** frames establish visual relationships and ambition; candidates consume
   actual rolls/state and may not copy invented layouts or discard inconvenient nouns.
4. **Provider-neutral DM seat versus comprehension:** primary visual comparisons freeze the same fact-bound language;
   live-provider prose is separately tested so model quality neither hides nor manufactures tray performance.
5. **No-budget present versus human proof:** current structural/developer evidence remains honest and useful;
   compensated research is retained for the claims it can legitimately support rather than deleted or faked.
6. **Performance degradation versus same-state comparison:** quality tier may differ only in a labeled comparison
   lane; within a same-tier pair, canonical state and tier are fixed. No low tier may remove semantic content.
7. **Old-renderer safety versus final convergence:** the theater remains a laboratory/control until P10.11 retirement
   evidence and Adam's P10.12 selection are satisfied; permanent dual-product support is not implied.
8. **Production affordability versus beauty floor:** lifecycle effort affects choice only after truth,
   comprehension, and visual quality pass. Cheapness cannot turn a debug board into Genesis's release identity.

### 11.127 G10.1 technical disposition and generated follow-ups

G10.1 is a truth guarantee, not a demand to build every possible vehicle, split-party graph, or realm-crossing
mechanic in pre-alpha. When a canonical moving or changing thing exists, every representation must project the same
thing and its current state. If a mechanic does not yet exist, the renderer cannot invent it merely to exercise the
guarantee.

#### Accepted contract carried as a technical default

1. **Canonical owner before visual hierarchy.** A moving citizen keeps one stable id plus its canonical location,
   custody, condition, damage, and knowledge facts. A reference-frame or attachment relation identifies a legal
   parent and relative position when mechanics require one. Scene-graph parenting, animation bones, camera staging,
   or DM prose never become the owner of that relation.
2. **Distinct relations stay distinct.** Riding a lift, possessing a turtle communicator, embedding a mace in a
   wagon, and applying a realm visual grade are not one generic visual `parent` fact. Location/reference frame,
   physical attachment, custody, and visual binding remain separately typed even when a view composes them.
3. **One committed version feeds every adapter.** Mechanics commit an idempotent receipt first. BattleMat,
   EngagementLens, travel/town/aftermath views, inspectors, accessibility projections, save/load, and the provider-
   neutral DM seat consume the same resulting state/version. Mount, update, unmount, remount, and terminal rebuild
   cannot advance private adapter cursors as competing world histories.
4. **Acyclic legal attachment chains.** A citizen may depend on a bounded chain such as communicator -> goblin ->
   lift -> dungeon shaft only when each relation is mechanically owned and acyclic. A cycle, missing required
   parent, or impossible transform is a validation failure with a truthful unresolved/fallback projection. The
   renderer may not guess a new floor cell. Detachment occurs only through a canonical receipt.
5. **Precision may simplify, never falsify.** An exact lift-relative cell may derive a labeled lower/descending
   relationship band in EngagementLens or a location phrase in the DM seat. A coarse view need not expose the cell,
   but it may not substitute another exact cell, erase the moving parent, or imply fixed-floor reachability.
6. **Unmounted is not renderer-simulated.** Offscreen things persist under their canonical active/site/cold owner.
   Pre-alpha's forced separated companion remains the accepted self-inert cold record and receives only changes
   emitted by an existing owner. Later independently advancing parent/child SceneLineages must add transactional
   time/reconciliation rules before they can move anyone; G10.1 does not secretly authorize them now.
7. **Realm transition is identity continuity, not automatic realm design.** If an explicit owner later commits a
   realm crossing, the actor/item/vehicle ids, custody, damage, conditions, and relevant attachment chain survive.
   Location and licensed realm state change through the transition receipt; visual binding, materials, lighting,
   and fallback art may rebind at a safe presentation boundary. No adapter duplicates the party or rewrites facts
   because the new realm lacks exact art. G10.1 does not itself invent portals, travel rules, or realm mechanics.
8. **Provider-neutral DM-seat facts.** The seat receives viewpoint-legal canonical anchors: exact ids, current
   holder/location or relationship, precision, material state, and committed changes. For example, after the freight
   lift crashes and the communicator is knocked loose, any supported provider may narrate different fiction, but
   every provider is told that the same communicator now lies damaged on the crashed lift beside the injured goblin.
   It cannot leave the item in the goblin's hand or narrate a second copy.
9. **Truthful degradation and missing-art behavior.** Low tier, reduced motion, nonvisual navigation, or absent
   exact art may simplify presentation only. They retain identity, material state, parent/relation, reachable exits,
   hazards, and current precision through standees, markers, cards, text, sound, or static terminal state.
10. **Retained proof grows with implemented breadth.** The first moving-platform trace proves mount -> move -> drop/
    detach -> crash -> unmount/remount -> interruption/rebuild. A later feature that adds an independently moving
    party branch, nested transport family, or realm crossing must add its own retained adversarial trace before that
    breadth is claimed.

#### Concrete dungeon and provider-neutral DM-seat trace

In the prototype dungeon, a goblin carrying the turtle communicator boards a descending freight lift. BattleMat
stores the goblin relative to the lift and custody of the communicator under the goblin. EngagementLens derives
`below and descending`; it does not mint a second goblin. The fighter knocks the communicator loose, so one receipt
moves the same item id from custody to a lift-relative object location. The lift then crashes, changing elevation,
topology, passenger damage, and the communicator's condition. Skipping animation, collapsing the lens, saving, or
reloading all rebuild the same terminal facts.

The provider-neutral DM seat receives those facts and available mechanics, not renderer-local coordinates or a
Gemini-specific story. One provider might say the turtle shell rings against iron; another might describe it
skittering through dust. Both must preserve the item, its damaged state, its location on the crashed lift, the
injured goblin, and the now-blocked lower exit. A deterministic fiction-first fallback does the same when no model
is available.
