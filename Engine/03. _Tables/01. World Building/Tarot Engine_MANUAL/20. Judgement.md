### **XX. Judgement: Trigger Effects**

Judgement represents the moment of reckoning, the final call, and the profound rebirth that follows a period of transition. It is the card of absolution—where the past is weighed and the "Spirit" is awakened. Under this card, the dungeon becomes a place of high accountability, where the elements of **spirit, air, fire, water, and earth** act as the judges of the living.

---

**The Constant: "The Final Accounting"**

In the domain of Judgement, your past actions catch up to you in the present.

- **The Effect:** Whenever a player makes a roll, they may choose to "Heed the Call." They add a bonus to the roll equal to the number of encounters they have survived in the current dungeon.
    
- **The Toll:** "The Weight of the Deed." For every "Heed the Call" bonus used, the player’s "Karmic Debt" increases. The next time they take damage, they take additional damage equal to their total "Karmic Debt," and the debt then resets to zero.

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

**The Constant: "The Great Trial"**

The faction is undergoing a massive internal reckoning. They are purging their past mistakes, and their current leadership is on trial before the entire membership.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**Faction Behavior (Judgement)**|**Narrative Implication**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Public Confession**|The faction’s leaders publicly list all their crimes and offer the party a massive "Restitution" payment to be forgiven.|
|**6-9**|Less Grounded|**The Spiritual Purge**|The faction burns all their old banners and history, deciding to become a group of "Seekers" focused on the element of **Spirit**.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Ideal Rebirth**|Every member of the faction dies simultaneously and is instantly reborn as their "Ideal Self." They are now a group of perfect, terrifyingly moral beings.|

### **Trigger: Accepting Binding Agreements**

**The Constant: "The Absolution Debt"**

The bargain grants you a "Clean Slate." All your past crimes and debts are erased, but you are now a "Vessel of the Call." You must perform one task of the Patron's choosing per month, or your past returns to you with double intensity.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**The Bargain's Mutation (Judgement)**|**The "Gift"**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Second Chance**|The first time you die, you are instantly resurrected. The cost: You lose all your memories of the person you loved most.|
|**6-9**|Less Grounded|**Elemental Awakening**|You gain a "Core" of **earth, air, fire, water, or spirit**. You can cast a 5th-level spell of that element once per day.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Script-Rewrite**|You can "Erase" one NPC from history. They never existed. The world reshapes itself around their absence.|
