#place-nearby
> **What's nearby** — a notable feature or site within reach of the settlement: the thing on the horizon, the place locals warn about or profit from. Rolled when a PC heads toward a new settlement (or scouts its surroundings). Every row carries the GRID LAW payload — a concrete travel distance/direction and, for structures and bounded spaces, a footprint in feet divisible by 5 — because these rows seed actual diorama spaces and map nodes. Every row also names the live relationship the settlement has with the feature (avoided, taxed, worshiped, mined, feuded over). Spice-graded d100 at 66/20/9/4/1, Commitment ceiling. **Upgraded 2026-07-09 from DMG14 gather seeds (Dungeon Location d100 + Exotic Location d20 + settlement material); rows PROVISIONAL pending Adam's red-pen; original in `zz_Archive/`.**
