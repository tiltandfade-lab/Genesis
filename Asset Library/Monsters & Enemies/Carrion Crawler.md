*p. 66 MM 2024*

Carrion crawlers scour putrid flesh from carcasses and gobble the slimy bones that remain. They aggressively attack any creature that trespasses on their territory or disturbs their feasting. A carrion crawler follows the scent of death to its food, but it prefers not to compete with other scavengers — these foul creatures thus hunker down in territories where death is plentiful and other carrion eaters have limited mobility. Caves, sewers, dungeons, and forested marshes are their favored lairs, but carrion crawlers are also drawn to battlefields and cemeteries.

A carrion crawler roams on the hunt with its tentacles probing the air for the scent of blood or decay. In tunnels or ruins, they scurry across the ceiling to avoid contact with oozes, otyughs, and other dangerous inhabitants of the darkness, even as they surprise potential meals that don't look up. Their patience is legendary: they have been known to follow a single light source for hours through a dungeon, waiting for the smell of blood.

Once a victim goes rigid with paralysis, the crawler drags it to a high ledge or isolated passageway to kill at leisure, then resumes patrolling while waiting for its meal to ripen.

### Carrion Crawler

_Large Monstrosity, Unaligned_

- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Hit Points:** 51 (6d10 + 18)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
- **Initiative:** +1 (11)
- **STR:** 14 (+2) | **DEX:** 13 (+1) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 1 (-5) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 5 (-3)
- **Skills:** Perception +5
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 15
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Spider Climb:** The carrion crawler can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The carrion crawler uses Paralyzing Tentacles and makes one Bite attack.
- **Bite:** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +4, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 7 (2d4 + 2) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) poison damage.
- **Paralyzing Tentacles:** _Dexterity Saving Throw:_ DC 12, one creature the carrion crawler can see within 10 feet. _Failure:_ The target has the Poisoned condition and repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically. While Poisoned, the target has the Paralyzed condition.

### d10 The Crawler's Lair (Tactical Terrain & Encounter Scaffolds)

Carrion crawlers exploit three-dimensional space better than almost any monster of their CR. Their ceiling climbing and tentacle reach of 10 feet makes them punishing if the party doesn't look up.

| d10 | Lair Type | Tactical Setup & Mechanics |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **1** | **The Dripping Ceiling** | The crawler is plastered to the ceiling of a 15-ft-high corridor, directly above a pile of humanoid remains on the floor. The remains are bait. The first PC who crouches to examine the bodies is 5 ft. below the crawler's tentacles. |
| **2** | **The Sewer Junction** | A circular sewer junction with a 10-ft deep flooded center channel. The crawler clings to the curved walls above the waterline. Any creature that falls or is knocked into the channel is at the crawler's tentacle reach — and must deal with the current. |
| **3** | **The Collapsed Gallery** | Rubble creates uneven terrain (Difficult Terrain throughout). The crawler navigates the debris by clinging to the walls and ceiling, giving it free movement while the party is slow. |
| **4** | **The Bone Larder** | The ceiling of this room is crusted with the paralyzed, desiccated remains of past victims — the crawler's "pantry." One of them is still alive, barely, slowly dying 20 feet off the ground. |
| **5** | **The Lit Corridor** | Two crawlers have been following the party's light source through the dungeon for the last hour. They strike from the darkness at the rear of the marching order the moment the last PC rounds a corner away from the main group. |
| **6** | **The Flooded Passage** | Six inches of standing water covers the floor. The crawler clings to the ceiling and uses tentacle reach to paralyze PCs from above. A paralyzed PC falls face-down in the water and begins to drown immediately. |
| **7** | **The Gallery Drop** | A 30-ft-tall cavern. The crawler drops from the ceiling onto a PC as an ambush, using its body weight to knock the target Prone before the tentacle attack. (Treat as a DC 12 Strength saving throw or be Prone on landing.) |
| **8** | **The Swamp Thicket** | In a forested marsh setting, the crawler uses dense root systems and low-hanging branches to mimic its dungeon ceiling behavior, hanging just above eye level in dim conditions. Passive Perception DC 15 to spot before it strikes. |
| **9** | **The Feeding Competition** | The crawler has claimed the same corpse-heavy room as a Gray Ooze or a pack of Giant Rats. The three factions are currently staring each other down. The party's arrival breaks the standoff — violently. |
| **10** | **The Patient Stalker** | The crawler is not in the room yet. It has been following the party's blood-scent for three encounters. It arrives at the worst possible moment — mid-combat with another creature — emerging from a side passage on initiative count 10. |

### d10 What the Crawler Left Behind (Investigation & Clues)

Carrion crawlers are sloppy eaters. Their lairs and feeding corridors are rich with evidence for an observant party.

| d10 | Discovery | Investigative Hook |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **1** | **The Clean Path** | A specific corridor in the dungeon has no dust, no bones, no debris. Someone or something has been clearing it routinely. DC 12 Survival: the faint slime trail of a crawler making its patrol route. |
| **2** | **The Paralyzed Rat** | A live rat is lying completely rigid on the ground, eyes blinking in terror. It was struck by a stray tentacle swipe minutes ago. This is a fresh hunting ground. |
| **3** | **The Ceiling Cache** | Sticky resin and gnawed bone clusters are packed into a crevice 20 feet up the wall. DC 13 Perception to spot; DC 12 Athletics to reach. Contains 40 gp worth of intact equipment from past victims. |
| **4** | **The Stripped Armor** | A full suit of plate mail, completely intact, sitting in the middle of an otherwise empty room. The crawler ate the occupant but cannot digest metal. The armor is slightly damp with digestive fluid. |
| **5** | **The Scratched Boots** | A set of boot prints in the dust — moving normally, then suddenly cutting off mid-stride with drag marks continuing at ceiling height. Something plucked that person off the ground from above. |
| **6** | **The Bone Midden** | A pile of cracked, sucked-clean bones in a corner. DC 14 Medicine: the marrow has been dissolved by acid, not cracked by tools. These were digested from the outside in. |
| **7** | **The Recent Kill** | A fresh corpse, paralyzed but still warm, hung by its clothing from a ceiling hook the crawler fashioned from a broken stalactite. The person is dead — but they died within the last hour. |
| **8** | **The Feeding Groove** | A worn groove in the stone floor where the crawler repeatedly returns to eat, worn smooth over decades. Marks on the walls at ceiling height indicate it has been using this exact path for a very long time. |
| **9** | **The Warning Carving** | A previous adventuring group scratched a crude warning into the wall in Common: "IT COMES FROM ABOVE — LOOK UP." The carving is old. Someone clearly did not. |
| **10** | **The Intact Journal** | A waterlogged field journal tucked inside a metal document case — the only thing recovered from a scouting party sent ahead three weeks ago. The last entry mentions the smell of something dead getting stronger, and a strange dripping from the ceiling. |
