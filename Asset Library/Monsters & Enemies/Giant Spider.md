*p. 280 MM 2024*

The giant spider is probably the most common dungeon creature in the game — not because it's mechanically interesting on its own (it isn't particularly), but because it makes *perfect environmental sense*. Any abandoned structure that has been sitting in the dark for decades will have spiders. The giant variety simply makes the resulting encounter something more than a pest-control problem. Spider webs that create difficult terrain, poison that forces Constitution saves, and the creature's ability to sense movement through vibration in its web make the giant spider a more layered encounter than its CR 1/4 might suggest.

The real encounter design opportunity with spiders is the environment. A room webbed from floor to ceiling, with web-wrapped cocooned bodies at the corners (former victims or future meals), and spiders lurking in the upper webbing, is a classic horror tableau. The web itself is the hazard; the spiders are the consequence of being slowed by it.

The Phase Spider is an advanced variant that occupies multiple planes simultaneously — it can slip between the Material Plane and the Ethereal, making it an entirely different kind of threat. Where giant spiders are ambush predators, phase spiders are hunters. They use their phasing to appear behind a target, bite and retreat to the Ethereal, then appear again somewhere unexpected.

### Campaign Integration: Investigation & Clues

Giant spiders don't have a cultural presence — they're environmental indicators.

- **The Web Coverage:** The extent of the webs tells you how long the spiders have been here and whether anyone has been through recently. Fresh-cleared paths through old webs indicate recent passage. New strands crossing a cleared path indicate the spiders have been reweaving.
- **The Cocooned Bodies:** Spiders wrap surplus prey for later. Finding cocooned shapes in the web — some clearly humanoid — tells a story of who has come this way before. Some may still be alive inside.
- **The Vibration Sense:** Giant spiders detect movement through their webs with exceptional precision. A party that moves carefully (Stealth vs. Perception) can cross a webbed area without triggering the spider's immediate response. A party that crashes through automatically wakes everything in the network.

### 1. Giant Spider

A large predatory spider that uses its web to trap prey and its venom to immobilize and slowly digest it.

_Large Beast, Unaligned_

- **Armor Class:** 14 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 26 (4d10 + 4)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
- **STR:** 14 (+2) | **DEX:** 16 (+3) | **CON:** 12 (+1) | **INT:** 2 (−4) | **WIS:** 11 (+0) | **CHA:** 4 (−3)
- **Skills:** Stealth +7
- **Senses:** Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Spider Climb:** The giant spider can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without an ability check.
- **Web Sense:** While in contact with a web, the giant spider knows the exact location of any other creature in contact with the same web.
- **Web Walker:** The giant spider ignores movement restrictions caused by webbing.

**Actions**

- **Bite:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage, and the target must make a DC 11 Constitution saving throw, taking 9 (2d8) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. If the poison damage reduces the target to 0 hit points, the target is stable but Poisoned for 1 hour, even after regaining hit points, and is Paralyzed while Poisoned in this way.
- **Web (Recharge 5–6):** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, range 30/60 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ The target is Restrained by webbing. As an action, the Restrained target can make a DC 12 Strength check, bursting the webbing on a success. The webbing can also be attacked and destroyed (AC 10, HP 5, vulnerability to fire damage).

### 2. Phase Spider

A spider that exists partially in the Ethereal Plane, using planar phasing as a hunting technique rather than webs.

_Large Monstrosity, Unaligned_

- **Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 32 (5d10 + 5)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., Climb 30 ft.
- **STR:** 15 (+2) | **DEX:** 15 (+2) | **CON:** 12 (+1) | **INT:** 6 (−2) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 6 (−2)
- **Skills:** Stealth +6
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 3 (700 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Ethereal Jaunt:** As a Bonus Action, the phase spider can magically shift from the Material Plane to the Ethereal Plane, or vice versa.
- **Spider Climb:** The phase spider can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without an ability check.

**Actions**

- **Bite:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 7 (1d10 + 2) piercing damage, and the target must make a DC 11 Constitution saving throw, taking 18 (4d8) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. If the poison damage reduces the target to 0 hit points, the target is stable but Poisoned for 1 hour, even after regaining hit points, and is Paralyzed while Poisoned in this way.

### d10 The Webbed Room (Investigation & Clues)

| d10 | Discovery | Investigative Hook |
|:----|:----------|:------------------|
| **1** | **The Wrapped Inventory** | A cocooned body has been here long enough that its equipment is still identifiable under the silk. A DC 12 Investigation check recovers a usable item from the wrapping before it's fully consumed. |
| **2** | **The Trail of Webs** | Fresh strands lead from this room toward a specific door — thicker than exploratory webs, clearly a well-traveled route for the spider. Whatever's behind that door the spider visits regularly. |
| **3** | **The Web Clock** | The webs in this room are layered — new over old. A DC 13 Nature check estimates layers: the spiders have been here for three months. The webs over the east door are thinner — that passage is newer or less traveled. |
| **4** | **The Vibration Warning** | The web network extends into the adjacent corridor. Anyone who moves normally in the corridor ahead will vibrate the web — the spider in this room will know they're coming. The party can move at half speed (DC 14 Stealth) to prevent this. |
| **5** | **The Drider Marks** | Amid the giant spider webs, a DC 14 Investigation check finds markings made by hands rather than spinnerets — specific territorial symbols left by a drider or drow handler. These spiders have a keeper. |
| **6** | **The Phase Trail** | A faint shimmer in the air — barely visible in torchlight — marks where a phase spider transitions repeatedly. The trail shows its patrol route. Following it in the Ethereal Plane (if accessible) would reveal where it rests. |
| **7** | **The Surviving Component** | The spiders have been wrapping everything in webs, but a scroll tube with a watertight seal is intact among the cocooned debris. Whatever was in here, the original owner was protecting it from moisture and time. |
| **8** | **The Missing Patrol** | A garrison's roster in a nearby room includes a patrol that should have returned three days ago. Four of the wrapped shapes in this room match the roster count. The spiders have been here longer than the garrison knew. |
| **9** | **The High Nest** | A particularly dense cluster of webbing at the ceiling conceals a hollow space — the spider's true lair, accessible only by climbing. Inside (DC 14 Athletics to reach): previous victims' valuables and possibly a cached item from the dungeon's loot budget. |
| **10** | **The Egg Timer** | The eggsac is warm to the touch and vibrating slightly — the eggs are days from hatching. If the party leaves without dealing with it, the dungeon will have dozens of new spiders when they return. If they're coming back. |
