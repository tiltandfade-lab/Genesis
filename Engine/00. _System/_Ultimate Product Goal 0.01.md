# Tarot Vending Machine DM Engine

## Working Name (Placeholder)

**Tarot Vending Machine with Teeth**  
(A DM-facing procedural engine + future AI copilot)

---

## 1. Project Intent (Authorial North Star)

This project aims to create a **human-first Dungeon Master engine** that:

- Reduces cognitive load on the DM without replacing DM authority
    
- Preserves improvisation, judgment, and table chemistry
    
- Uses structured procedures (tarot, tables, clocks, pressure) to generate surprise without cruelty
    
- Is _AI-compatible by design_ but not _AI-dependent_
    

The long-term vision is an **AI copilot that lives alongside the DM**, operating silently behind the screen, tracking state, suggesting rolls, surfacing consequences, and condensing outputs — never narrating directly to players or adjudicating rules.

At all stages, the DM remains:

- Final arbiter of rules
    
- Sole narrator to players
    
- Ethical and tonal authority
    

---

## 2. Design Philosophy

### Core Principles

1. **Human Authority First**  
    AI may suggest, never decide.
    
2. **Procedural Surprise, Not Narrative Railroading**  
    Systems generate pressure and options, not outcomes.
    
3. **Explicit Triggers Over Vibes**  
    Events fire because conditions are met, not because it “feels right.”
    
4. **Symbol → Interpretation → Manifestation**  
    Tarot and randomization never map directly to events.
    
5. **Readable by Humans, Parsable by Machines**  
    The vault is prose-forward but structurally consistent.

## 3. The Obsidian Vault as Engine

The Obsidian vault is the **canonical source of truth**.

It functions as:

- A rules engine expressed in natural language
    
- A state database for campaigns
    
- A future AI-readable knowledge graph
    

### Vault Is NOT:

- A lore dump
    
- A linear adventure book
    
- A content generator
    

### Vault IS:

- A system of interlocking procedures
    
- A memory scaffold
    
- A testbed for future AI residency

## 4. Core Engines (In Progress / Planned)

### 4.1 Tarot Engine

**Purpose:**

- Introduce symbolic pressure and thematic distortion
    

**Key Rules:**

- Tarot never directly causes events
    
- Tarot defines _domains affected_ (power, decay, inversion, revelation, etc.)
    
- Interpretation phase always precedes manifestation
    

**Triggers (Examples):**

- Arrival in a new location
    
- Binding bargains or oaths
    
- Dungeon population
    
- World events
    

---

### 4.2 NPC Engine

**Purpose:**

- Generate NPCs with momentum, not static traits
    

**NPCs Have:**

- Wants
    
- Fears
    
- Leverage
    
- Current pressure
    

NPCs evolve due to:

- Player interaction
    
- Time passing
    
- External pressure
    

---

### 4.3 Setting / Location Engine

**Purpose:**

- Treat locations as active systems
    

**Locations Track:**

- Tags (urban, liminal, sacred, surveilled, etc.)
    
- Active factions
    
- Pressure / instability
    
- Recent events
    

---

### 4.4 Scenario / Quest Engine

**Purpose:**

- Generate situations, not plots
    

**Scenarios Emerge From:**

- NPC friction
    
- Resource scarcity
    
- Escalating clocks
    
- Player inaction or failure
    

---

### 4.5 Pressure & Clocks Engine

**Purpose:**

- Prevent stall-out
    
- Make time matter
    

Pressure increases when:

- Players hesitate
    
- Resources deplete
    
- Failures accumulate
    

Pressure releases as:

- Consequences
    
- Escalations
    
- World changes
    

---

## 5. Dice & Absurdity System

- Natural 1s and 20s trigger **Magnitude of Absurdity** rolls
    
- Second roll determines scale (from grounded to mythic)
    
- Outcomes are permanent, memorable, and world-affecting
    

This system is designed to be:

- Consistent
    
- High-impact
    
- Joyfully unbalanced

## 6. Long-Term AI Vision (Explicitly Deferred)

### What the AI Will Eventually Do

- Track campaign state
    
- Detect trigger conditions
    
- Suggest what to roll and why
    
- Identify relevant tables
    
- Condense dice results into scannable narrative options
    
- Summarize sessions and unresolved threads
    
- Suggest music / mood cues
    

### What the AI Will NEVER Do

- Adjudicate rules
    
- Narrate directly to players
    
- Override DM judgment
    
- Introduce canon without approval

## 7. MVP Definition (Critical)

### MVP Is NOT:

- An app
    
- Automation
    
- Ambient listening
    
- A marketable product
    

### MVP IS:

> A complete Obsidian vault that allows a DM to run a full campaign with reduced cognitive load, using clear procedures, tables, and outputs — _with or without AI assistance_.

### MVP Success Criteria

- The engine can be run manually
    
- The DM Screen can be maintained as a single living document
    
- Systems interlock cleanly
    
- Edge cases are documented
    
- Multiple sessions have been run successfully
