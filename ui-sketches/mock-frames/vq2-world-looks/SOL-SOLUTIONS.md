# VQ2 World Looks — Sol's solutions

These frames are visual hypotheses, not permission to copy every attractive artifact into the renderer. The walk record remains canonical; every recipe below is a deterministic projection of a stored field or state beat. The protected dungeon material language, `pl-022`, UI chrome, and the wolf/skeleton sprites remain regression targets.

## Frame notes

1. **`01-travel-grassland-daylit`** — I leaned into a brightly lit miniature in a dark studio: raking sun makes the terraces read as DAY while the tray edge retains the value law. The grammar fought the desire to expose the whole grass plate; the strongest composition is the PC/fire-ring/first riser triangle, not the entire terrain inventory.
2. **`02-travel-grassland-profiles`** — Identical geometry proves profile identity can come from key direction, fill ratio, and color temperature alone. Dark is too close to disappearance in the mock, which is useful: production needs a figure-read floor without lifting the whole tray.
3. **`03-travel-swamp-moonlit`** — The petrified dragon works when treated as one coiled hero silhouette rather than scenery. The visible moon is an out-of-bounds image-model crutch; the engine only needs its directional key and wet reflection.
4. **`04-travel-desert-night-star`** — The rolled star cleanly owns both subject and light, so this is the most economical exterior. The frame overstates the star's crystalline detail; production needs a ten-foot emissive rock recipe, not a bespoke meteor sculpture.
5. **`05-travel-arctic-lavaflow`** — The hot diagonal against a cold plate makes the table's surreal juxtaposition read intentional. The grammar resisted adding snowfield scale cues; one PC and a clipped ribbon are enough.
6. **`06-wild-arrival-crossjunction`** — Making the tray silhouette itself a cross-junction fixes the current ellipse-on-black failure before dressing does any work. The ribcage nearly steals the whole frame; its scale needs clamping so the route remains legible.
7. **`07-town-harborfront-street`** — Two facade planes, one gable cap, one road strip, one lantern, and one cart are already sufficient to read TOWN. The mock still drifts toward a complete street and dock; runtime should crop those donors into facade slices and omit the pier posts unless a dock field licenses them.
8. **`08-town-burning-building`** — Reusing frame 07's street family and changing one facade state makes the fire feel like an event in a known place. The beads read only because fire reflections pick them up; without that contrast they should remain a trace, not become extra clutter.
9. **`09-settlement-node-tray`** — A roof cluster plus one window solves the settlement noun, and full-body headroom solves the production clip. The image model made the PC slightly too sculptural and the camera slightly too overview-like; production must retain the flat standee and tighten one step.
10. **`10-kenney-dungeon-bridged`** — Donor geometry disappears into Genesis once pastel materials are replaced, edges are weathered, and one warm/cool light relationship controls the room. The bell became too monumental; keep it a wall-socket object at prop scale.
11. **`11-kenney-graveyard-gloom`** — The graveyard kit crosses the bridge with only five donor families because silhouette and palette do most of the work. The cross count is at the upper limit; more stones would revert to a Kenney showcase.
12. **`12-beat-shop-open`** — Counter + two shelves + lamp + two standees reads SHOP immediately. The mock proves a full room is unnecessary; the shopkeeper's hidden plinth is acceptable behind the counter, but both figures must remain sprite cards rather than modeled people.
13. **`13-beat-long-rest`** — Fire + tent edge + bedroll makes REST with three props and deep negative space. The grammar fought the temptation to add a forest; the darkness ring is the environment.
14. **`14-odd-glass-garden`** — Standard tree/plant silhouettes survive an exotic material override and communicate the odd roll without a bespoke kit. The mock's optical refraction and hanging glass ornaments are too expensive or unlicensed; keep the material idea, cut the ornaments and realistic caustics.
15. **`15-odd-living-megastructure`** — Industrial donor parts stop reading as buildings when buried into one broken terrain mass and joined by a coherent vein network. The mock is too dense for the normal tray budget; production should halve the pieces and retain one pulse node.
16. **`16-combat-in-room`** — This is the strongest systems proof: it is an edit of frame 10, not a new scene. Same room, same camera, same torch, same mushrooms; only three standees and a fading floor grid change. That continuity is the implementation law.

## P-A

**LAW:** Daylight is a dark-room photograph of a bright tray, not a globally bright world: the exterior key must create an unmistakable sun-facing value band while the terrain median remains mid-value and the outer 15% of the tray falls to no more than 12% display luminance. Use one directional sky source, a key-to-fill ratio of roughly 4:1, no visible sky dome, and no clipped white larger than 3% of the frame. `01-travel-grassland-daylit` proves the single frame; `02-travel-grassland-profiles` proves that direction, temperature, and ratio—not geometry swaps—separate daylit, overcast, moonlit, and dark.

**RECIPE:** Extend the current `LIGHT_PROFILES`/`applyLightProfile` seam in `src/ui/theater-boot.js` with an exterior rig row rather than another recolored ambient. For `daylit`, turn the existing `S.keyLight` into a warm directional sun at normalized direction `(0.65, 1.0, -0.45)`, intensity `1.10`, with `S.fillLight=0.18`, hemisphere `0.30`, and exterior ambient `0.30`; do not create the current decay-zero point light. Use `overcast` key `0.28`, fill `0.34`, hemi `0.36`, ambient `0.36`; `moonlit` key `0.34` cool, fill `0.07`, hemi `0.18`, ambient `0.20`; `dark` key `0.08`, fill `0.02`, hemi `0.08`, ambient `0.12` plus only a rolled practical. Give exteriors their own `EXTERIOR_AMBIENT_FLOOR=0.12` so `STAGE_AMBIENT_FLOOR=0.42` does not flatten the profiles. In `theaterBoardBuild`, stamp the profile from the stored walk/segment time and weather once that field is wired; until then use the biome bias only as an explicit diagnostic fallback. Apply the existing perimeter/rim darkening to the exterior plate with a smooth multiplier from `1.0` at the subject cluster to `0.32` at the tray edge. Gate with four fixed captures of the exact same W-GRASS tray and require thumbnail classification by image statistics plus visual review.

**RISK:** A strong directional key can turn low-poly grass into noisy glitter, and lowering the exterior ambient floor can make flat standees disappear when their card normal faces away from the sun. The cheapest early test is the existing four-profile W-GRASS capture with neutral post: record tray-edge, terrain-median, focal-95th-percentile, and standee-face luminance; fail if the daylit edge exceeds `0.12`, if any two profile medians differ by less than `0.06`, or if the PC face falls below `0.18` in any profile.

## P-B

**LAW:** Kenney is a geometry reserve, never a render style. A donor mesh is admitted only after its materials, value grouping, scale, sockets, and condition are replaced by Genesis-owned recipes; if its silhouette still reads “raw Kenney” at gameplay thumbnail size, it is CHASSIS and requires a generated face treatment or decomposition rather than a stronger tint. `07-town-harborfront-street`, `10-kenney-dungeon-bridged`, and `11-kenney-graveyard-gloom` prove that material replacement plus sparse selection can bridge ordinary architecture; none proves blanket admission of a whole pack.

**RECIPE:** Add one donor adapter at the GLTF boundary, keyed by `{pack, slug, admissionClass, semanticParts, sockets, canonicalScale}`. On load, traverse donor meshes, discard their authored pastel `MeshStandardMaterial`s, classify node/material names into `stone|wood|iron|roof|glass|cloth`, and rebuild with Genesis materials: `roughness 0.82–0.94`, `metalness 0.0` except iron `0.35`, realm-graded five-band albedo sampled through `gradeColorLocal`, nearest-filtered 32×32 deterministic grain from `interiorMaterialTexture`, and selective outline color from the realm profile. Apply naturalistic grime/wet/rust decals through the decal lane—never faceted crack noise—and add edge wear only to upward/convex faces. Start with the exact donors proven here: fantasy town `wall.glb`, `roof-gable.glb`, `road.glb`, `road-curb.glb`, `lantern.glb`, `cart.glb`; dungeon `room-small.glb`, `gate-door.glb`, `stairs.glb`; gloom `crypt.glb`, `gravestone-cross.glb`, `iron-fence.glb`, `pine-crooked.glb`, `lightpost-single.glb`, `lantern-glass.glb`. Cache the normalized GLTF by recipe hash and keep the source pack untouched.

**RISK:** Material replacement cannot rescue toy-like proportions, oversized bevels, or topology whose silhouette carries the donor style; transparent glass and thin fences also expose sorting/outline defects. The cheapest test is a three-card bridge gate using the exact town, dungeon, and graveyard sets above: raw donor / DIRECT_MODULATED / neutral-grey silhouette at gameplay size. Reject to CHASSIS if two reviewers identify the source style from the modulated thumbnail, if any material remains outside the realm palette, or if the donor creates more than one unowned bright source.

## P-C

**LAW:** A town tray is a street wedge, not a miniature city: at gameplay scale, TOWN requires no more than two facade planes, one partial roof cap, one route surface, one motivated light, one mobility/trade prop, and two to four citizens. The camera frames the active facade/road/NPC cluster and lets every unneeded building edge terminate into darkness. `07-town-harborfront-street` is the maximum ordinary grammar; `08-town-burning-building` proves that a state change should reuse that grammar; `09-settlement-node-tray` is a separate node overview with a roof-cluster budget, not permission to widen street scenes.

**RECIPE:** Have `walkSceneFrom` preserve urban `type`, `atmosphere`, `skin`, `motif`, and active segment fields, then add a deterministic `townSliceFrom(walkScene, seed)` projection that emits facade sockets rather than whole buildings. Choose `2` facade slots when a motif or event needs a second readable surface, otherwise `1`; admit at most `1` roof cap, `1` route strip, `1` curb/drain family, `1` vehicle/market prop, `1` practical, and `4` citizens. For U-TOWN, map Harborfront + Shipping/docks to wet route material and mooring-edge treatment only if the rolled field is present; map Rain-Soaked Stone to naturalistic shallow-stream decals; map Tools of the Trade to one generated mural atlas applied to a facade. Feed those provenanced instances into `shotPlanFrom`; give facade/action-center anchors more weight than tray bounds so `composeShot` crops to the street wedge. For settlement nodes, use a separate `roofCluster` recipe capped at seven masses with one lit-window practical and enforce standee headroom through `interiorFitMaxHeightFor`-equivalent exterior fit.

**RISK:** Whole-building donors will make the camera back away, and semantic aliases can silently invent “dock” props from the word Harborfront even when the segment does not license them. The cheapest test is a provenance census and capture pair: fail if any rendered instance lacks a `sourceRef`, if ordinary street scenes exceed the fixed budgets above, or if the largest facade cluster forces a medium standee below 18% frame height.

## P-D

**LAW:** A state beat earns a micro-stage, not a new level. Each beat projects one stable anchor, at most three supporting prop families, one practical light, and the minimum cast needed to explain the state; open/closed or before/after states mutate the same scene graph instead of replacing it. `12-beat-shop-open` proves SHOP with counter + shelves + lamp + two standees; `13-beat-long-rest` proves REST with fire + tent edge + bedroll + one standee; `06-wild-arrival-crossjunction` proves ARRIVAL must retain the rolled area shape and feature.

**RECIPE:** Add a small data registry consumed after `walkSceneFrom`, not event-specific branches in `theater-boot.js`: `shop_open -> anchor:counter, support:[shelf,shelf], practical:lamp, cast:[shopkeep,pc]`; `shop_closed -> same instance ids, shutter/door state closed, lamp 0, shopkeep hidden`; `long_rest -> anchor:campfire-pit, support:[tent,bedroll], practical:campfire, cast:[pc.seated]`; `walk_complete -> anchor:rolledArrivalFeature, stage:rolled areaType, cast:[pc]`. Every entry must carry the beat event and source scene as provenance; a conventional beat prop is licensed by the beat type but may not add narrative identity. Resolve props through existing part/prop recipes first, then normalized Kenney donors. Mount the practical before calling `applyLightProfile` so `S.lightPropAnchor` owns the light. Reuse stable ids across state changes and let `shotPlanFrom` target the beat anchor with one terrain ring.

**RISK:** A generic beat registry can become an authored-level back door, especially if “shop” starts implying merchandise or “rest” implies a forest not present in the walk. The cheapest test is a source ledger beside each capture: every noun must resolve to either the beat recipe's fixed non-narrative grammar or a rolled field, and each beat must render with four or fewer prop instances, one bright practical, and zero unprovenanced dressing nouns.

## P-E

**LAW:** An odd roll is realized by semantic substitution: one familiar geometry family receives an exotic material/state override, one hero silhouette explains the noun, and one accent system explains the magic. Never answer rarity with density or a bespoke kit. `14-odd-glass-garden` proves standard flora + glass override; `15-odd-living-megastructure` proves industrial donors + one vein network; the floor is passed when the frame reads as intentional wonder at thumbnail size and no asset reports a missing-material fallback.

**RECIPE:** Add `realizationClass` mappings at the visual-recipe boundary: `transparent flora -> flora geometry + pseudoGlass material`, `living megastructure -> industrial terrain assembly + veinEmissive condition`, `buried colossus -> industrial/stone chassis + partial-burial transform`. For pseudo-glass, cap the tray at `1` hero and `6` support pieces, use `MeshPhysicalMaterial` with `color 0x23383d`, `roughness 0.32`, `metalness 0`, `transmission 0.18`, `thickness 0.06`, `ior 1.35`, `opacity 0.78`, and a pale-cyan emissive edge/outline; do not attempt caustics. For veins, compose at most `8` donor pieces (`floor.glb`, `catwalk-straight.glb`, `cog-a.glb`, `building-a.glb`, `detail-tank.glb`), sink 35–60% below the plate, then generate one deterministic 64×64 branching mask from the walk seed and apply it as an emissive overlay with a single pulse phase shared by the assembly. Stamp the original odd-row `sourceRef` on every substituted instance and expose the realization class in debug view.

**RISK:** Three.js transparency sorting can break pseudo-glass, emissive masks can bloom into white noise, and substitutions can become false facts when a standard tree shape is read as a literal tree. The cheapest test is neutral-post + beauty captures for ODD-98, ODD-100, and BREACH-CHROME with a missing-material sentinel color; fail on any sentinel pixel, more than eight donor instances, more than one pulse phase, or if the hero silhouette becomes unreadable when bloom is disabled.

## P-F

**LAW:** Combat is a state layered onto the explored room, never a board swap. The room scene graph, material instances, practical lights, fog, interactable state, camera family, and provenance ids persist byte-for-byte; combat adds only unit cards, legal-space affordances, transient effects, and at most a 10% camera tighten around the action cluster. `10-kenney-dungeon-bridged` and `16-combat-in-room` prove the required before/after pair; `pl-022` remains the protected shipped regression frame.

**RECIPE:** Route combat-in-interior through the existing `trayFrom({kind:"interior"})`/`setInteriorBoard` path instead of calling flat `setBoard`. Preserve the same `walkScene`, dressed spatial plan, `activeRoomId`, `tileKit`, `data.lights`, and scene recipe hash that exploration used; enrich the input with `combat.units = theaterUnitsFrom(GS.combat).units`. Map each combat band/lane to the active room's existing `rooms[].cells` once at the contract boundary, excluding dressing-blocked cells, rather than generating a second 4×3 zone board. Mount standees through the normal sprite-citizenship path. Render the grid as thin umber lines on the existing floor geometry with `depthWrite:false`, `transparent:true`, `opacity:0.16`, `polygonOffset:true`, `polygonOffsetFactor:-1`, and radial fade to zero before wall-adjacent cells. Feed the enriched combat to `shotPlanFrom`; preserve yaw/pitch and clamp target/distance change to 10% unless an occlusion constraint rejects the prior camera.

**RISK:** The combat engine's abstract band/lane topology may not map injectively onto irregular or tiny rooms, and rebuilding the interior can reset stateful doors or practical flicker even when the frame looks continuous. The cheapest test is a deterministic exploration→combat→combat-end capture harness over rectangular, octagonal, cave, tiered, and overloaded rooms: assert identical scene recipe hash and interactable ids, zero new architecture/material/light instances, legal unique unit cells, camera delta within the clamp, and exact restoration of the exploration frame after combat end.

## Cut list

- No visible moon/sun discs, horizon paintings, landscape backdrops, or world-scale wilderness vistas. The source may exist outside frame; the tray and its response carry the light.
- No physically accurate glass caustics, ray-traced refraction, hanging glass ornaments, or per-flora bespoke transparent assets for `14-odd-glass-garden`.
- No full Kenney towns, whole pack dumps, skyline generators, or automatic admission of all 1,752 GLBs. Donor packs remain a reserve behind the adapter and visual gate.
- No 3D character models. Any mock figure that reads sculptural is an ImageGen artifact; runtime figures remain canon sprite standees on compact dark plinths.
- No bespoke Glass Garden, Living Megastructure, or Fallen Field art kit. These are the acceptance cases for semantic substitution.
- No dense industrial carpet in `15-odd-living-megastructure`; cap it at eight sunk donor pieces and one emissive network.
- No extra torches, combat spotlights, replacement combat board, or camera reset in `16-combat-in-room`.
- No inferred narrative props from atmosphere alone: Harborfront does not automatically license ships, cranes, warehouses, or dock workers; a rest beat does not license a forest.
- No realistic high-frequency grass, snow, or water simulation. Use painted material recipes, sparse silhouettes, and naturalistic non-faceted decals.
- No UI/HUD recreation in these scene frames. UI chrome is protected and composited outside the world-look acceptance images.
