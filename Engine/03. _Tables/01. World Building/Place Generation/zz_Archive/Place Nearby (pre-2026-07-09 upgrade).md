#place-nearby
> **What's nearby** — a landmark, ruin, work-site, or feature within reach of the town, good for a side-excursion. Rolled when a PC heads toward a new settlement (or scouts its surroundings). Spice-graded d100, Commitment ceiling. Table-improvement pass 2026-06-22: re-graded from the old 3-tier to the full 5-band ladder — Adam's named features preserved, a **Textured** tier added, weird tiers regraded by honest weirdness, a **Mythic** capstone added. Originals in `zz_Archive/`.
