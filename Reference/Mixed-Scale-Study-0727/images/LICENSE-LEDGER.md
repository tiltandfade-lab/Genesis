## Binding statement

**Reference evidence only.** Nothing listed here may become a Genesis production asset,
texture, mesh source, or promotional image. These images inform internal visual direction
and grammar induction. Before any image is copied, modified, redistributed, embedded, used
as a texture, or used in promotional material, its exact license and attribution
requirements must be reviewed *for that use*. The safe Genesis production path remains
original procedural geometry and original materials.

Every file below came from **Wikimedia Commons** under CC0 / public domain / CC BY / CC BY-SA
as recorded. Attribution strings are taken from each Commons file's own `extmetadata`; the
linked Commons page is authoritative if this table and the page disagree.

Files were fetched as Commons-rendered thumbnails at ~1200 px, not full resolution — enough
to read proportion and relationship, small enough to keep the study cheap.

**Retrieval is reproducible.** Every row was pulled by the Commons API
(`action=query&prop=imageinfo&iiprop=url|extmetadata&iiurlwidth=1200`) against the exact
`File:` title in column 2. No image was hand-picked from a web page.

## What was deliberately NOT downloaded

- **No FFT map images were copied.** Lane 3 read the existing untracked local corpus at
  `genesis/Reference/FFT-Guard-Post-Study/study-archive/maps/five-angle/` **in place**,
  read-only, exactly as the Lair Study did. Nothing copied, nothing committed, no contact
  sheet derived. The catalog's FFT boundary forbids importing that corpus into the repo.
- **No game screenshots of any kind** (BG3, Diablo, Shadow of the Colossus, Dragon's Dogma,
  Monster Hunter). Those would be the obvious mixed-scale touchstones, and downloading them
  into a tracked repo directory would violate the FFT boundary by construction. Lane 1
  therefore cites written documentation instead of pixels for game comparanda.
- **No measured survey drawings of a large-animal building.** Searched; none found on Commons
  at readable grade for the Hampi elephant stables or any comparable structure. Recorded as a
  declared gap in `synthesis.md`, not silently skipped.
- No images of private individuals were sought. Several kept photographs contain incidental
  people at distance, used **only for scale** — which is the entire point of this lane.
