### The Flesh Golem: The Alchemical Abomination

In a gritty, investigation-heavy campaign, a flesh golem is the terrifying culmination of a macabre supply chain. Unlike stone or iron golems built by artisans, a flesh golem requires the systematic harvesting of humanoid remains—traditionally requiring at least six different bodies to provide the torso, head, and four limbs.

These constructs are animated not by necromancy, but by binding an elemental spirit into the dead tissue using raw lightning. Because the spirit is a prisoner in a rotting cage, a flesh golem is highly unstable. When heavily damaged, the magical binding frays, and the creature goes berserk, lashing out at everything around it—including its creator.

**Campaign Integration & Clues:**

- **The Unnatural Stench:** Flesh golems emit a faint odor of freshly dug earth and decaying meat. Because of their unnatural elemental animation, natural animals are terrified of them; hounds will whimper and completely refuse to track a flesh golem.
- **The Healing Storm:** Lightning does not harm the golem; it invigorates the dead muscle, actively healing its wounds and mending its torn flesh.
- **Fire Aversion:** The elemental spirit within the golem is deeply traumatized by fire. Sudden exposure to flames can temporarily disrupt its programming and force it to recoil.

### Flesh Golem

_Medium Construct, Neutral_

- **Armor Class:** 9
- **Hit Points:** 127 (15d8 + 60)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 19 (+4) | **DEX:** 9 (-1) | **CON:** 18 (+4) | **INT:** 6 (-2) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 5 (-3)
- **Damage Immunities:** Lightning, poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Understands Common plus one other language but can't speak
- **Challenge:** 5 (1,800 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +3

**Traits**

- **Aversion to Fire:** If the golem takes fire damage, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the end of its next turn.
- **Berserk:** Whenever the golem starts its turn Bloodied, roll 1d6. On a 6, the golem goes berserk. On each of its turns while berserk, the golem attacks the nearest creature it can see. If no creature is near enough to move to and attack, the golem attacks an object. Once the golem goes berserk, it remains so until it is destroyed or it is no longer Bloodied. The golem's creator, if within 60 feet of the berserk golem, can try to calm it by taking an action to make a DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) check; the golem must be able to hear its creator. If this check succeeds, the golem ceases being berserk until the start of its next turn, at which point it resumes rolling for the Berserk trait again if it is still Bloodied.
- **Immutable Form:** The golem can't shape-shift.
- **Lightning Absorption:** Whenever the golem is subjected to lightning damage, it takes no damage and instead regains a number of hit points equal to the lightning damage dealt.
- **Magic Resistance:** The golem has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The golem makes two Slam attacks.
- **Slam:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 4 (1d8) lightning damage.

### Encounter Scaffolds & Investigation Tables

#### d6 Flesh & Composition (The Maker's Material)

Flesh golems are limited by the resources available to their creator. The type of flesh and its current state of preservation directly inform the horror of the encounter and can drastically alter the golem's appearance.

|d6|Flesh Type & Source|Composition Condition & Visuals|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**Standard Graveyard**|**Sickly & Stitched:** The classic monstrosity. The skin is a sickly green and yellow from partial decay, held together by thick, crude black sutures and iron staples.|
|**2**|**Dragonflesh (Drolem)**|**Scaly & Rigid:** Built from the harvested remains of a young dragon. Covered in mismatched, rotting scales. It obeys complex commands rigidly but violently misinterprets vague instructions.|
|**3**|**Battlefield Scavenge**|**Armored & Mismatched:** Built from fallen soldiers. Pieces of ruined chainmail and broken breastplates are literally bolted or fused directly into the swelling, necrotic tissue.|
|**4**|**Alchemical Vat**|**Bleached & Translucent:** The parts were preserved in chemical baths before assembly. The flesh is slug-white and hairless, with thick, black alchemical fluid visibly pumping through swollen surface veins.|
|**5**|**Beast-Grafted**|**Feral & Furry:** The creator ran out of human parts and substituted the heavy, muscled arms of a bugbear, an ape, or a minotaur. The golem lopes with a hunched, terrifyingly asymmetrical gait.|
|**6**|**The Embalmed**|**Leathery & Spiced:** The flesh has been perfectly preserved using expensive salts and resins. It smells of heavy spices and ozone rather than rot, looking almost like a massive, twitching doll.|

#### d4 The Laboratory Clues (The Investigation Layer)

Tracking the creator of a flesh golem requires investigating the supply chain of body parts and the massive magical infrastructure needed to harness lightning.

|d4|Crime Scene Clue|Campaign Implication|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Precision Thefts**|Local graves haven't just been robbed; they were harvested. Exactly six bodies were exhumed, but from each grave, only one specific part (a left arm, a torso) was taken.|
|**2**|**The Cowering Hounds**|The local magistrate's prize tracking dogs were brought to a crime scene, but they immediately tucked their tails, whimpered, and refused to enter the area, sensing the elemental abomination.|
|**3**|**The Lightning Trap**|The party finds a tall tower or abandoned windmill outfitted with massive copper rods and thick cables leading down into a reinforced cellar—the exact setup needed to jump-start the golem's heart.|
|**4**|**The Berserker's Wake**|A trail of utterly senseless destruction. Trees are snapped in half, and heavy stone walls are beaten to dust. A DC 13 Survival check reveals the tracks belong to a remarkably heavy, barefoot humanoid dragging a stiff limb.|
