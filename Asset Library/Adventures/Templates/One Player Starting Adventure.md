# The Old Fort Investigation — Duet-Ready Running Note (Original Outline + Agile Duet Tweaks)

> Goal: Keep your tone (frontier containment, controlled mystery, measured authority) while making Session 1 survivable and smooth for a duet (1 PC + sidekick).

---

## QUICK DUET SAFETY PRINCIPLES (Apply Anywhere, No Rewrites)

**Core duet risk:** action economy + low-level swinginess (one crit / one failed save can end the campaign).

**Fast fixes (use as needed):**
- Prefer **1 tough-ish enemy** over **3+ enemies** at once.
- Convert “save-or-suffer” into **“save-or-cost”**:
  - failures cause **noise / separation / dropped item / forced retreat** rather than hard-lock effects.
- Add **2 retreat valves** in dungeon fiction:
  - **Break line-of-sight** route (door, smoke vent, collapsed corridor)
  - **Safe pocket** route (warded alcove / old guard post)
- **Front-load a small healing buffer** (1–2 healing potions or “field dressings” equivalent).
- **One combat max** before a **short-rest opportunity** (or at least a breather scene).
- Ensure **one obstacle is solvable without fighting** (stealth, bargain, environment bypass).

---

## SIDEBAR — SIDEKICK DECISION (RECOMMENDED: START NOW)

**Recommendation:** start with a sidekick from Scene 1.
- Prevents “dies before engine turns on.”
- Keeps escort guard boundary intact.
- Adds tactics + conversation without exposition dumps.

**Fast, official scaffolding:** use **Tasha’s Sidekick classes** (Warrior / Expert / Spellcaster).
- Most duet-stable: **Warrior** (simple tank).
- If your PC is already tanky: **Expert** for skills/utility.
- If you need support: **Spellcaster** (heals/controls), but can add complexity.

**Quick sidekick identity plugs (pick one):**
- **Guard Auxiliary (Warrior):** “Assigned to keep you alive, not ask questions.”
- **the Arcane Cabal Handler (Expert/Spellcaster):** masked, containment-minded, says little.
- **Local scavenger-guide (Expert):** knows ruins, hates the marsh, paid + superstitious.

**If you insist on “meet in dungeon”:**
- Place sidekick in **Room 1–2**, injured but stable, immediate shared goal (escape).
- Make first combat **avoidable** so PC is not solo.

# ACT I — The Ride to the Ruins (ORIGINAL + DUET NOTES)

## Opening Frame (ORIGINAL)
- Session begins in motion.
- Party already mounted with a frontier guard.
- Sulfur wind from the marsh.
- Faint blue light visible near ruined watchtower.
- No explanation of party formation — assume prior credibility.

Pause early and ask: *“As you ride, what are each of you doing?”*

**Duet tweak (add 1 line of doctrine):**
- Captain’s instruction includes: **“If things go sideways, withdraw. Report matters more than heroics.”**
  - (Gives you permission to fail-soft without breaking tone.)

---

## Escort Guard (Frontier Sergeant) (ORIGINAL)
### Clearance Level
**Knows:**
- Strange blue light reported.
- Captain deemed it worth investigation.
- Guard manpower is stretched thin.
- The light hums faintly.

**Does NOT Know:**
- the Arcane Cabal structure or purpose.
- Cult infiltration within the guard.
- Node network system.

### Tone
- Practical.
- Slightly uneasy.
- Not dramatic.
- Not overly friendly.

Communicates:
- Manpower shortage.
- This is unusual.
- Magic is rare and concerning.

Style Examples:
- “Captain won’t pull men off the walls for lights.”
- “Swamp gas doesn’t hum.”

---

## Drop-Off Boundary (ORIGINAL)
- Guard stops 300 feet from the mouth of the ruin.
- Dismounts.
- Secures his horse.
- Refuses to approach further.

Clear procedural boundary:
- Orders were to escort them to the mouth.
- Orders do not include entering.

He remains behind to watch the horses.
He does not follow.
He does not interfere.

**Duet tweak (give him ONE insurance lever, still noncombat):**
Choose one:
- **Signal horn:** if blown, he won’t enter, but he **rides for help**.
- **Lantern code:** “two flashes = abort.”
- **Rope line:** he’ll hold from outside **if they insist**.

(He stays at 300 ft; this is just a fail-soft mechanism.)

---

## If the Rogue Talks to a Horse (ORIGINAL)
Horse is mundane but perceptive.

### What It Perceives
- The blue light feels wrong.
- The air smells like the swamp.
- Ground vibration is unsettling.
- Something inside is “not animal.”

### What It Does NOT Know
- Factions.
- Cults.
- Arcane terminology.
- Political structure.

### Tone of Communication
- Instinctual.
- Sensory.
- Simple imagery.

Possible phrasing themes:
- “Sky-light under earth.”
- “Stone is awake.”
- “Not cave. Not den. Wrong.”

Keep it primal. No exposition.

**Duet tweak (add 1 actionable sensory clue):**
- “Stone is awake… and it **hums where the air is cold**.”
  - (Points toward the active zone/obelisk without lore.)

# ACT II — Dungeon (ORIGINAL + DUET NOTES)

(Use the Old Fort module.)

Important:
The escort guard does not see anything inside.
Only the party has full knowledge.

This creates information leverage later.

**Duet pacing rules (do not rewrite, just steer):**
- **Reduce simultaneous enemies.** One meaningful opponent > many bodies.
- Convert “hard disables” into **costs** (noise, separation, forced retreat).
- Include **2 retreat valves**:
  - line-of-sight break
  - safe pocket
- Ensure **one encounter is avoidable** (stealth/bargain/bypass).
- Place a **breather node** early (short rest opportunity or safe pocket).
- Consider a **soft alarm** instead of a lethal ambush:
  - enemy flees to raise alert rather than fighting to the death.
- Add **small healing buffer** (potions/field dressings).

# ACT III — Return to Escort Guard (ORIGINAL + DUET NOTES)

When party exits:
- Guard listens carefully.
- Asks minimal questions.
- Does not interpret technical details.

If party uses arcane terminology, he defers:

Tone shift moment:
- “The Captain needs to hear this.”

He escorts them back to town.

**Duet tweak (add 1 human check-in line, no lore):**
- “You all still breathing?”

# ACT IV — Captain Meeting (ORIGINAL + DUET NOTES)

> NOTE: Meeting location and set dressing to be designed separately.

Escort guard is dismissed before sensitive discussion.

Door closed.
Private tone.

---

## Captain of the Guard (ORIGINAL)
### Clearance Level
**Knows:**
- the Arcane Cabal exists.
- They stabilize magical sites.
- Cult activity has increased.
- Some guards may be compromised.

**Does NOT Know:**
- Full cult hierarchy.
- the Arcane Cabal strategy.
- Exact network of nodes.
- Which guards are disloyal.

### Tone
- Controlled.
- Measured.
- Strategic.
- Careful with words.

He listens first.

He asks precise questions:
- Condition of sigils.
- Whether obelisk was disturbed.
- Whether recalibration occurred.
- Whether intruders were present.

If sigils damaged → visible tension.
If stabilized → subtle approval.

### Information He Shares (Limited)
He communicates:
- Some individuals work to contain instability.
- Guard does not interfere without cause.
- Discretion is necessary.

He does NOT:
- Say “the Arcane Cabal.”
- Explicitly state cult infiltration.
- Reveal larger political web.

He implies secrecy:
- Speak only to him about this.
- Do not discuss with other guards.

### Reward Structure (ORIGINAL)
Primary:
- Official standing with the Guard.
- Future contracts.
- Discretion remembered.

Secondary:
- Modest coin.
- Supply credit or lodging support.

No celebration. Recognition is restrained.

**Duet tweak (keep it snappy — 3 questions max):**
Pick three:
1) “Did you touch the sigils?”
2) “Was anyone else there?”
3) “Did it change while you watched?”

Everything else becomes: “We’ll talk later.”

# ACT V — Outskirts Tavern (ORIGINAL + DUET NOTES)

Tone shift:
- Firelight.
- Murmurs.
- Frontier unease.

Do not introduce factions through speeches.

Instead seed quietly:
- A well-dressed mage observing.
- Cloaked traveler leaving.
- Someone reacting to the word “ruins.”

Introduce one disappearance rumor.
No exposition dump.

End on tension, not clarity.

**Duet tweak (avoid hook overload):**
Pick **ONE** seed, not three:
- Mage observing (best for arcane tension) OR
- Disappearance rumor OR
- Reaction to “ruins”
