## What the twelve rolls proved

The live chain is good at producing a **story-bearing social place**:

- all twelve rolls retained a realm-skinned Watering-hole or Lodging context;
- all twelve minted a named venue, a proprietor record, two to four ambient people,
  a guaranteed NPC hook, and a Distant Word result;
- four produced a chance-gated ambient encounter; and
- the soft-on-approach / hard-on-contact lifecycle worked.

The typed tavern caller is not yet good at producing a **coherent hospitality
venue**:

- only **1/12** ungated typed calls selected an interior that explicitly described a
  tavern;
- **0/12** proprietor roles identified a hospitality operator;
- all **8/8** Chrome/Gloom venue names remained in the same English-fantasy
  adjective-and-creature pool despite their Noodle Bar/Diner labels;
- the rolled interior, proprietor occupation, place label, service promise, capacity,
  and current event are independent facts with no reconciliation step; and
- the rich Tavern 2.0 foundation/sensory/staff/action tables are callable but are not
  consumed by the live typed-building path.

The broad interior results are not failures of the general d300. They prove its range:
warehouse, post office, furrier, bank, mill, temple, workshop, great hall, chandler,
translator, and shopfront all arrived as coherent host/feature/occupant seeds. The
failure is the typed caller's lack of a compatibility gate, operator contract, and
reconciliation receipt. The baseline is retained without hand correction.

## Research verdict

“Tavern” is too narrow to own the generator ontology. The correct program is a
**hospitality venue** whose possible services include food, beverage, lodging,
meeting, entertainment, exchange, information, transport support, and community
services. Alcohol, a bar counter, lodging, a courtyard, and a stable are conditional.

Across the source cohort, venue form follows:

1. host relationship and route or street context;
2. service mix and schedule;
3. operator/household/institutional model;
4. clientele, recognition, law, price, credit, and access;
5. capacity and public/private/service separation;
6. supply, water, sanitation, waste, property, and animal/cargo obligations; and
7. social practice: news, ceremony, hiring, gaming, deliberation, performance,
   hospitality ritual, or community service.

Culture changes those relationships and practices, not merely the label and props.

## Preservation ruling proposed by the evidence

Keep:

- Foundation → Layout → Population → Details as progressive disclosure;
- Watering-hole and Lodging as distinct place programs;
- named venue, responsible person, ambient cast, Distant Word, and current action;
- access/recognition, locals/transients, food/drink, sensory, and distinct-object
  concepts; and
- the live codex lifecycle and realm context.

Refactor before wiring:

- Tavern Type into service/social/claim components;
- Layout Combos into obligation-aware layout recipes;
- independent population dice into capacity-derived attendance;
- arbitrary proprietor occupations into an operator/household role contract;
- generic rumors/secrets into world-bound facts, claims, debts, and evidence;
- culture-as-relabel into culture/doctrine cards that alter behavior and space; and
- the ungated use of `building-interior` into a Public-Service family chassis plus
  Hospitality-specific arrangement/current-scene/Spice and realm-doctrine layers.

Preserve the raw general d300 and its row-level combinations for untyped discovery.
Typed Tavern does not use it as the primary selector; reviewed relationships may
migrate into the family/program tables with lineage, and whole rows enter only through
explicit adaptive reuse. Current authority:
`../../docs/BUILDING-PROGRAM-TABLE-FAMILIES.md`.

- currently named `The Golden Dolphin`;

infrastructure, and rich enough to prove adaptive reuse and small layered control. Its
