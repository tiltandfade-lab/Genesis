> [!info] T2 Loot Budget Reference (Lv 5–10)
> Based on DMG 2024 recommendations: 10 Common, 17 Uncommon, 6 Rare, 1 Very Rare across a full T2 campaign. Budget below reflects one dungeon's share. The generator computes this from segment count; deepest rooms get rarest items. Boss always receives the top-rarity slot.

| **Dungeon Size** | **Segments** | **Common** | **Uncommon** | **Rare** | **Very Rare** |
| ---------------- | ------------ | ---------- | ------------ | -------- | ------------- |
| Small | 5–8 | 2 | 3 | 0 | 0 |
| Medium | 9–12 | 3 | 5 | 1 | 0 |
| Large | 13+ | 4 | 6 | 2 | 1 |

> **Distribution:** Very Rare → Boss room. Rare → second-deepest cluster. Uncommon → mid-depth rooms. Common → shallow rooms. All other rooms receive coin only (see Dungeon Loot - Coin Cache).
