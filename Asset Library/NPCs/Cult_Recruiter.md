# Tomas Rell

*(Asset: cult_recruiter_empathic_lure)*

## Plot Vector

**Primary Pillar:** The Cult  
**Secondary Pillars:** The Disappearances  

**Function:**  
Draws PCs toward low-tier gatherings through empathy and belonging.  
Frames cult as grief support and protective knowledge network.  
Softens moral barriers before ideological exposure.

---

## Level 1 — Immediate Table Presence

**Race / Background:** Human; former dock clerk  
**Role:** Organizer of small “discussion circles”  

**Visual Quirk:** Wears a simple reed ring on one finger  
**Mood:** Calm, attentive  
**Demeanor:** Asks questions more than he answers  

**Hook:** Invites players to a private gathering for those “touched by loss”  
**Talent:** Exceptional listener; mirrors emotional language  
**Unrealized Potential:** No  

---

## Level 2 — Structural Pressure

**Formative Trauma:** Lost a brother to the marsh  
**Formative Grace:** Found structure and meaning in early cult gatherings  

**Want:** Expand the circle without drawing suspicion  
**Fear:** Panic will destroy everything  

**Faction Tie:** Tier 2 cult facilitator  
**Influence Weight:** 2 — Social Influence  
**Resource Control:** Access to grief networks and ritual scheduling  

**If Ignored (Personal):** Focuses recruitment on more vulnerable targets  
**If Ignored (Regional):** Cult influence grows quietly among families of the missing  

---

## Level 3 — Secrets

**Secret:** Knows the disappearances are not random  
**Leverage:** Controls access to who gets invited deeper  
**If Cornered:** Deflects, reframes, and retreats — never fights  

---

## Potential Quest / Adventure Tie-Ins

- Invites players to observe a “remembrance rite.”
- Suggests someone the players know may need support.
- Offers information about a missing person in exchange for attendance.
- Frames cult gatherings as protection from something worse.
- Hints that authorities are lying about the marsh.

---

## Notes for Play

- Uses the players’ own words back to them.
- Never insults other beliefs.
- Avoids strong doctrinal statements early.
- Introduces ideology gradually.
- Positions the cult as compassionate, not powerful.
