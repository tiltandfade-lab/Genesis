### The Displacer Beast: The Sadistic Stalker

In a gritty, investigation-forward campaign, displacer beasts are the ultimate apex ambush predators. Originating from otherworldly realms, these six-legged, blue-black felines sport two spiked tentacles sprouting from their shoulders. They are not mere hungry animals; they are highly intelligent, malicious hunters that kill for sport and take sadistic pleasure in toying with their prey.

**Confirmed Facts & Campaign Integration:**

- **The Light-Bending Glamer:** A displacer beast projects a constant magical illusion that makes it appear to be several feet away from its actual location, making them incredibly difficult to hit.
- **The Blink Dog War:** Displacer beasts harbor a deep, instinctual hatred for all life, but they hold a specific, eternal enmity for Blink Dogs. If you have Blink Dogs in your region, the presence of a Displacer Beast turns the local woods into an invisible, teleporting warzone.
- **The Valued Corpse:** Their glowing green eyes persist even after death. Local thieves value these eyes as charms against detection, and alchemists heavily prize their hides, creating a black-market bounty on these beasts.
- **The Drag:** Displacer beasts do not eat where they kill. After crushing their prey with their tentacles, they drag the corpse to a quiet, secluded lair to feed.

### Displacer Beast

_Large Monstrosity, Lawful Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Hit Points:** 76 (9d10 + 27)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **Initiative:** +4 (14)
- **STR:** 18 (+4) | **DEX:** 15 (+2) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 6 (-2) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 8 (-1)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** Understands Sylvan but can't speak
- **Challenge:** 3 (700 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Avoidance:** If the displacer beast is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a saving throw to take only half damage, it instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if it fails. It can't use this trait if it has the Incapacitated condition.
- **Displacement:** Attack rolls against the displacer beast have **Disadvantage**, since it projects an illusion that makes it appear to be near its actual location. This trait is suppressed while the displacer beast has the Incapacitated condition.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The displacer beast makes one Rend attack and one Tentacle attack.
- **Rend:** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +6, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 9 (1d10 + 4) slashing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.
- **Tentacle:** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +6, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage.

### Encounter Scaffolds & Investigation Tables

#### d6 The Predator's Stage (Tactical Terrain)

Displacer beasts are ambush hunters that use dense terrain to mask their approach, relying on their 10-foot reach to strike from behind cover while their _Displacement_ protects them from return fire.

|d6|Lair Terrain & Hazard|2024 Mechanical Integration & Tactics|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Bramble Choke**|The woods are choked with thick briars (Difficult Terrain). The beast uses its 10-foot reach to strike PCs through the brambles, forcing martial characters to push through the punishing thorns to close the distance.|
|**2**|**The Trade Road Ambush**|Displacer beasts memorize caravan schedules. The battle takes place around an overturned wagon. The beast uses the wagon for Total Cover, popping out, striking with its 40 ft. speed, and retreating behind the wreckage.|
|**3**|**Canopy Stalkers**|The beast attacks from the heavy, interwoven branches 10 feet above the forest floor. It targets the weakest party member, attempting to grapple them and drag them up into the canopy.|
|**4**|**The Hall of Mirrors**|Encountered in a ruined noble estate or magical vault filled with reflective surfaces. The reflective light makes tracking the beast's _Displacement_ even harder. If a PC misses an attack, they shatter glass, creating a 5-foot hazard of jagged shards (1d4 piercing damage to enter).|
|**5**|**The Blink Dog's Snare**|The battlefield is littered with traps previously set by intelligent fey or Blink Dogs. The Displacer Beast knows exactly where the traps are and attempts to maneuver the PCs into concealed pits or tripwires.|
|**6**|**The Flooded Grotto**|The beast lairs in a cave with waist-deep water. Movement costs are doubled, meaning the beast's natural 40 ft. speed heavily outpaces the PCs. The beast ducks beneath the murky water to break line of sight entirely.|

#### d4 Macabre Clues (The Investigation Layer)

A displacer beast leaves a specific, terrifying signature on its environment. Tracking one is a matter of noticing the psychological toll it takes on the local wildlife.

|d4|Discovery|Clue / Campaign Hook|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Drag Marks**|A fresh kill scene where a humanoid was crushed, but the body is missing. A DC 13 Survival check reveals twin, parallel drag marks leading into the deep woods, where the beast took the corpse to feed in peace.|
|**2**|**The Thieves' Charm**|The party finds the remains of a poacher. Clutched in their hand is a small, glowing green orb—a preserved displacer beast eye. It acts as a one-time use _Amulet of Proof against Detection and Location_ for 1 hour.|
|**3**|**The Toyed Prey**|A local scout is found dead, but not eaten. They are covered in hundreds of shallow, barbed lacerations. A DC 14 Medicine check reveals the beast simply tortured them to death for hours as a game, without inflicting a lethal blow until the very end.|
|**4**|**The Subservient Beast**|The beast wears a heavy, spiked iron collar bearing the crest of a local, highly influential criminal syndicate. Intelligent evil creatures often use them as prized guard animals.|
