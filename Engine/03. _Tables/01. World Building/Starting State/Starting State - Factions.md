# Starting State — Factions
> Gives the powers in a freshly-rolled world an **agenda + method + clock**, so factions are drivers, not set-dressing — and **living things that grow, decline, splinter, merge, and change their minds** (see *Faction Turn* below). A faction pursuing a goal on a clock **is a pressure** (see `Starting State - Pressures.md`). The web is **1 dominant + 1d3 rivals** (the rivalry between them is its own pressure). Original, IP-clean content; standard pipe-table format for Track B compile-consistency. Orchestrated by `Starting State Procedure v1.0.md`. Spice-graded (`SPICE-CURVE.md`).

## Faction Agenda (Goal) — Commitment · Grounded→Volatile
> What the faction is actually driving at. The clock fills toward this.

|d20|The faction means to…|
|---|---|
|1–3|seize control of a vital resource — a mine, the water, the grain, a road|
|4–5|break, absorb, or outlast a rival faction|
|6–7|widen its territory or its reach|
|8–9|restore a lost status, title, holding, or homeland|
|10–11|amass wealth by whatever means the method allows|
|12–13|impose its creed, law, or taboo on everyone within reach|
|14|win legitimacy — to be loved, not merely feared|
|15|control who comes and goes — a border, a gate, a chokepoint|
|16|bury a dangerous truth, or drag one into the light|
|17|acquire one specific relic, secret, or person *(Textured)*|
|18|settle an old blood-debt, whatever it costs|
|19|wake, finish, or feed something old and sleeping *(Strange)*|
|20|become something other than what it is *(Volatile)*|

## Faction Relationship — Fork (each rival's stance toward the dominant)
> Generates the inter-faction pressure. Roll once per rival.

|d12|Toward the dominant power, they are…|
|---|---|
|1–2|at open war — blood in the streets|
|3|in a cold war — proxies, deniable strikes|
|4–5|rivals over one prize: a resource, a route, a person|
|6|enemies by creed — a religious or ideological rift|
|7|nursing an old grudge neither will let die|
|8–9|holding an uneasy truce, each waiting to break it first|
|10|already inside it — one has infiltrated the other|
|11|a would-be supplanter — a spurned heir or a splinter|
|12|secretly in league behind a very public feud *(Textured)*|

## Faction Turn — Fork (factions are not static; they change over time)
> The growth/change/decline roll. Fires when the world **drifts** — a montage, a rest-until-dawn that passes days, or a return after the PC has been away — once for the most active faction (or each, for a busy world). This is how the web stays alive: factions win, lose, splinter into new rivals, swallow each other, and change what they want. Ties into the change-over-time layer (`NEXT-STEPS` 10–13).

|d12|Since last we looked, the faction has…|
|---|---|
|1–2|pressed its agenda — its clock ticks one step nearer (roll a Grim Portent)|
|3|gained ground — won an asset, an ally, territory, or a windfall (it grows)|
|4|lost ground — a defeat, a betrayal, or a patron withdrawn (it declines)|
|5|achieved its goal — the clock fills; resolve the outcome, then it takes a **new Agenda**|
|6|pivoted — abandoned its goal and set a new one (roll a new Agenda)|
|7|changed hands or heart — a new leader, Method, or Tag|
|8|fractured — a splinter breaks away as a **new rival faction** (the web grows)|
|9|moved on a rival — the conflict between them shifts a notch (roll/adjust Relationship)|
|10|allied or merged with another faction (the web consolidates into a larger power)|
|11|gone quiet — lying low, hiding its assets, biding its time|
|12|overreached — a hubristic push backfires into a sudden reversal *(Textured)*|
