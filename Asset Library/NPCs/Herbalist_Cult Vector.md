**Lysa Fenmar (Swamp-Edge Herbalist)** _Analog: Evanna Lynch (Luna Lovegood from Harry Potter) meets a Swamp Hag — Projects an aura of unsettling, unshakable serenity. She is completely at peace with horrifying things and responds to aggression with gentle, pitying confusion._

Notes for Play

- **Mannerism & Quirk (Quick Play):** She never raises her voice, speaking gently and fondly about decay, rot, and loss. She smells faintly of peat and brine, and constantly fiddles with a bundle of damp reeds tied with dark twine. If the players mention someone who died or went missing, she gently corrects them, calling the victims "returned."
- **Ability & Role (Quick Play):** She is an Apothecary/Herbalist operating on the fringe of society. Her **High Wisdom** means she is incredibly perceptive, easily reading the weather, tracking safe paths through the marsh, and noticing the PCs' emotional states. However, her **Low Charisma** reflects her creepy, off-putting demeanor—she has no social graces and doesn't understand why people find her terrifying.
- **Secret & Flaw (Quick Play):** Her Secret is that she has actively participated in "honoring circles" (actual cult rites) at the exact locations where people disappeared, and she can guide the PCs to a Tier 2 cultist clearing. Her Flaw is her profound denial: she lost someone to the swamp years ago and genuinely believes she is just helping other grieving families "accept" what the marsh is doing.
- **Hook & Complication (Quick Play):** She asks the PCs to help her retrieve a rare marsh plant that happens to grow exactly where someone recently vanished. The complication is that she is a totally innocent victim of the cult's manipulation; if the PCs attack her or act aggressively toward the "harmless honoring circle" she leads them to, they will look like monsters attacking a grieving woman.

Catchphrase

_(Smiling serenely while gently tying a knot in a damp reed)_: "Oh, no, he isn't lost. The marsh just asked him to return. Decay is just the soil making room for something new. I can show you where it happened, if you'd like to understand."
