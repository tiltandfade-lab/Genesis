## 30-Second Run

The market burned. Two shopkeepers are screaming accusations at each other in the ash. A lamplighter on a shaky ladder above them is quietly using Detect Thoughts — not cleaning the lamp. The moment the guilty merchant feels cornered, corrupted hounds burst from an alley. The guilty merchant bolts. The innocent merchant panics and knocks the ladder. The PCs have one round to decide: chase, fight, or catch the falling man.

---

## Setup

**Space:** Wide cobblestone marketplace, thick black ash covering everything, charred timber frames on all sides. A tall wrought-iron streetlamp stands at center.
**Atmosphere:** Cold smoke, crunch of ash underfoot, raised voices. Bright daylight makes the ruin stark.
**NPCs Present:** Lamplighter (secret minor telepath, on a wobbly ladder), Guilty Merchant (taller, no visible shock about the fire), Innocent Merchant (genuinely furious).
**If Ignored:** The lamplighter is torn apart by hounds or falls. His psychic insight dies with him. The arsonist escapes to a fortified hideout.

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Investigation

The thick ash hides an ignition source at the epicenter. The lamplighter is reading emotional flashes from the merchants — he is no wizard, but he hears feelings.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 14 Persuasion or Intimidation:** Get the merchants to stop shouting and answer specific questions.
- **DC 15 Insight (lamplighter):** He isn't cleaning — he's staring intently, reading flashes from the argument below.
- **DC 15 Investigation (ash epicenter):** Uncover the true ignition source (see d4 True Cause table).
- **DC 12–14 Investigation:** Find it, but disturb a pocket of hot embers — take 1d4 fire damage and create a lightly obscured ash cloud.

**Escalation:** If PCs press the guilty merchant with evidence, or if the investigation stalls past 5 minutes, trigger the attack.

---

### Segment 2: The Crisis

Initiative is called. Three simultaneous problems exist at once:

1. **The hounds** (1d4+2) pour from the alley targeting the softest civilians.
2. **The ladder tips** — the lamplighter takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage if it hits the ground (4 HP total; any hit kills him). PCs have until the end of round 1.
3. **The guilty merchant dashes** — roll on the Fleeing Suspect Obstacles table.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 14 Athletics:** Catch the lamplighter or brace the ladder.
- **DC 15 Acrobatics:** Slide a cart or awning fragment under him to break the fall.
- **Standard chase rules** if the suspect breaks line of sight.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Slippery Ash:** Any creature using the Dash action must succeed on DC 12 Acrobatics or fall Prone at the end of their movement.
2. **Lamplighter's Death:** If he falls and dies, his psychic insight is lost. Recovery requires *Speak with Dead* or DC 15 Arcana (residual emotional imprint).
3. **Lamplighter Testimony (if saved):** *"I hear flashes. When the fire was mentioned, the taller merchant felt no shock — I felt sickening guilt. He knew it was going to happen."*
4. **Charred Ledger:** Found in the ash (DC 15 Investigation) or on the suspect's body — shows a massive payout from an unknown faction, dated the day before the fire.

## Difficulty Dial

**Tier 1 (Lvl 1–2):** 3 Corrupted Hounds, standard damage only.
**Tier 2 (Lvl 5–7):** 5 Corrupted Hounds. When a hound dies, it bursts into a 10-ft ash cloud — DC 14 Con save or Poisoned for 1 minute.

### Fleeing Suspect Obstacles (d4)

| d4 | Obstacle | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fallen Timber** | Charred beam blocks the alley exit. Suspect spends half movement climbing over — exposed for an extra round. |

| 3 | **Smoldering Debris** | Exit covered in hot embers. Suspect can't Dash — wastes the action navigating carefully. |
| 4 | **Panicked Scavenger** | Looter or animal collision. First 10 ft of escape route treated as Difficult Terrain. |

## Stat Blocks

**Corrupted Hound** — *Large Undead/Monstrosity, Neutral Evil* — CR 1
AC 14 | HP 37 | Speed 50 ft.
**Bite.** +5, 2d6+3 piercing + 1d6 necrotic; DC 13 Str save or Prone.
**Pack Tactics.** Advantage on attacks if an ally is adjacent to target.
**Tactic:** Knocks targets Prone with bite then follows up with advantage. Immune to Slippery Ash difficult terrain.

**Lamplighter** — *Human Commoner* — CR 0
AC 10 | HP 4 | Speed 30 ft.
**Detect Thoughts** (1/day, innate, Save DC 11): Reads surface emotional impressions.
**Tactic:** Cowers or flees. Will not fight.
