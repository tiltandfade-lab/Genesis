#in-building-complications
> **PROVISIONAL — Adam spot-check pending.** Expanded from d20 (5 building types × nested d6
> label-complications) to a single spice-graded d100 (`BATCH2-GUARDRAILS.md` H2, wave-2b
> maintenance). Roll once on entering a building the party hasn't cleared/scoped yet; the row
> gives both the building type and its complication in one draw. Band split holds the shared
> curve (66 Grounded · 20 Textured · 9 Strange · 4 Volatile · 1 Mythic); Fork's own ceiling stops
> at Strange per `[[SPICE-CURVE]]` §4 — the Volatile/Mythic rows are share-holders only, and the
> DM narrates them down to their nearest Strange-equivalent beat if one lands live. The original
> six labeled complications per building type are folded in verbatim as Grounded seeds; the rest
> extend their register. Building-type row spans: Residence 1–50 · Religious 51–60 · Tavern/Club
> 61–75 · Warehouse 76–85 · Shop 86–100.
