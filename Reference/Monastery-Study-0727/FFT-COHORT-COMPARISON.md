# FFT COHORT COMPARISON — Site 4 Monastery/Commune (evidence-thickening addendum)

The original packet's `lane-5-fft-cohort.md` (2026-07-24) already did substantial FFT work,
sampling 25 of the 121 map ids and reading five views on two of them. This addendum does not
repeat that pass. It samples **6 additional views on the institutional cohort's core ids**
(Orbonne Monastery and its chapel, plus the Church cohort) that the original lane classified by
name but did not read at all five angles, specifically to extract **proportional/measured**
evidence the original lane's synthesis flagged as missing: *"No measured section of a terraced
monastery, and no metric bay width... The seed's 'three broad steps' cannot be checked against a
real gradient from this evidence"* (original synthesis §8).

## 0. What the original lane already established (not repeated here)

Per `Reference/Monastery-Study/lane-5-fft-cohort.md` and `synthesis.md` §2 (not re-derived): id 56
= Orbonne Monastery, id 62 = Chapel of Orbonne Monastery, id 63 = Golgorand Execution Site
(raised-platform evidence), id 36 = Church Outside of Town, id 95 = Church, id 45 = Ruins of
Zeltennia Castle's Church, id 59 = a barrel-vault arcade map (the `VAULT_MODULE` candidate). This
addendum reads ids **56, 62, 36, and 59** at views not previously sampled by either the original
lane-5 or this campaign's other packets.

## 1. Orbonne Monastery (id 56) — the causeway-and-spire approach, confirmed at two new angles

**56_0** (plan/top-down): a bright grass hillside terrain with a stone-flagged path climbing
through it, flanked by patches of blue water on both sides near the base — visually confirming
the original study's "climb from a timber bridge over water" reading (original synthesis §4)
independently, from the plan view rather than an isometric angle. The stone path is a single
continuous line up the slope; there is no wrapped arcade visible anywhere on the hillside itself.

**56_1** (isometric): the same green hillside seen from the approach, with a **wooden causeway
crossing water at the base of the climb**, leading up bare turf to a stone church with a **tall,
steeply-pitched teal-blue conical spire**, lancet windows, and a red door at grade. The spire and
the ground-level door read as one continuous silhouette move — there is no intermediate terrace,
gallery, or arcade level between the water crossing and the church door.

**What this adds to the original finding.** Both new views independently confirm — from angles
the original lane's synthesis did not cite — that FFT's own terraced-sanctuary case puts **zero**
arcade geometry on the climb itself. The original study's hillside verdict already concluded this
in prose ("A continuous level walk cannot wrap a sloping yard... none of them is 'arcade around a
slope'" — original synthesis §4); these two new views are the first *direct* FFT confirmation
cited for that specific clause, closing part of the original lane's own declared sampling gap
(only 2 of 25 sampled ids had been read at all five views).

## 2. Chapel of Orbonne Monastery (id 62) — the processional axis, read as a proportion

**62_0** (plan/top-down): a narrow, deep interior — a **long red-carpeted runner**, bordered by a
dark patterned strip on each side, running the full visible length of the room toward a raised,
arched apse at the far end; a smaller side chamber with a free-standing object (plausibly a font
or lectern) opens off the room's near-right corner.

Counted against the room's own visible floor-tile pattern (not a metric survey — an honest,
declared proportion read, per THE FFT BOUNDARY's morphology-not-measurement footing): the carpet
runner occupies roughly the **center third** of the room's width, with the patterned border strips
and bare stone flanking it roughly equally on both sides. The room reads as **markedly longer than
it is wide** — a processional proportion, not a square or near-square hall.

**What this adds.** This is the first proportion read in either packet for the *interior* of a
raised-head institutional room, as opposed to the courtyard/plan proportions lane 1 and lane 5
already covered. It supports the working spec's own "processional axis" route promise (§"Route
promises," ceremonial-axis row) with a concrete interior precedent: the axis is not just a route
across the courtyard, it continues as a floor-level visual device (the carpet) inside the head
room itself — a cheap, legible dressing pattern the working spec's "processional paving" material
line (§"Material demand") could adopt without any new geometry.

## 3. Church Outside of Town (id 36) — the small/ordinary end of the ladder

**36_0** (plan/top-down): a modest exterior — a red-tiled roof section, rubble and loose fieldstone
scattered across a grass-and-cobblestone yard, and a dark timber lattice structure (most plausibly
a roofed well-head or a small timber roof section) at the upper edge of frame.

**What this adds.** Unlike Orbonne (a full institutional complex) or the chapel interior, this map
reads as a **small, rural, single-building religious site with no visible enclosure, court, or
arcade at all** — closer to the working spec's own Rung-A "small commune/house" or even a bare
wayside shrine than to the Golden Seed. This is useful negative evidence: FFT's own institutional
cohort spans from a bare rural chapel yard (36) to a fully developed monastery-on-a-hill (56),
which is independent confirmation that the working spec's five-expression ladder (small → ordinary
→ large → degraded → unusual) is a real spread FFT itself uses, not a Genesis-only invention
layered onto the source material.

## 4. Barrel-vault arcade map (id 59) — repetition confirmed as a whole-floor pattern

**59_0** (plan/top-down): the entire visible floor is covered by a dense, regular woven/latticed
pattern — repeating vertical bands at even intervals running the full width of the map, with no
visible break or irregularity across the sampled frame.

**What this adds.** The original lane classified id 59 as the `VAULT_MODULE` candidate
("freestanding pier-pair-and-vault placed on a lattice; the aisles between are the movement grid"
— original synthesis §2) from a single view. This top-down read confirms the repeat is **global**
— it is not a border treatment or a partial arcade run, but the room's entire organizing geometry,
edge to edge. This strengthens (does not newly establish) the working spec's REQUIRED invariant
"repetition remains legible... survives fixed camera, material, clutter, damage, and changed
seeds": FFT's own version of this room commits to the repeat completely rather than applying it
selectively.

## 5. What this closes, and what it does not

**Closes:** part of the original lane-5's own declared sampling gap ("only 25 maps were actually
viewed, and only two of those in all five views" — original synthesis §8) for the two ids most
load-bearing to the RULED exemplary seed (Orbonne, its chapel) plus one small/ordinary comparison

partial."
