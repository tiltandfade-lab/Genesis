# Damp Blocked Thoroughfare — Art Dispute
> *Urban Low. A street illusionist's chalk mural triggers a civic standoff — and hides smuggled contraband.*

## 30-Second Run

A chalk mural covers the thoroughfare's paving stones, still glowing faintly with hedge-magic illusion. A wealthy critic is loudly condemning it and drawing a crowd that's grinding the route to a halt. The artist gives no ground. Beneath the performance, a false-bottom barrel near a broken crank mechanism holds contraband — the crank pin is missing, and someone nearby seems very interested in whether the party finds it.

---

## Setup

**Space:** A wide cobblestone street narrowed to single-file by a hanging chain divider. A "brick plug" with suspiciously clean edges sits in the far wall — tampered, placed deliberately. A drain channel runs along one side; a crank handle for a nearby gate is missing its pin.
**Atmosphere:** Damp air, chalk dust, raised voices. Bright daylight makes everyone feel watched.
**NPCs Present:** Mira (illusionist artist, defensive), Hob (loud merchant critic, blustering — and stalling), 2–3 dockworker bystanders.
**If Ignored:** The argument resolves in 10 minutes. A figure quietly retrieves the barrel. The contraband is gone.

## Read Aloud

> *"The street ahead has ground to a stop. Twenty feet of cobblestone are covered in a shimmering chalk mural — silver fish, tangled vines, a face that shifts when you look away. A broad-shouldered man in a merchant's coat is red-faced and shouting about blocked commerce and the dangers of sorcery. The young woman with chalk on her hands shouts back just as loud. The crowd around them seems to prefer the show to wherever they were going."*

---

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Dispute

Mira's mural is legitimate — minor illusion woven into chalk makes the fish dart when stepped on. She holds a busker's permit. Hob's claim that no permit covers "road obstruction by witchcraft" is technically wrong, but his real goal is keeping attention away from the barrel.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 10 Persuasion (either side):** Calm the argument; crowd disperses, route opens.
- **DC 12 Insight (Hob):** He's not actually angry about the art. His eyes keep drifting toward the chain divider.
- **DC 13 Arcana (mural):** Harmless hedge magic — Minor Illusion, no ward, no trap.

**Escalation:** If unresolved after 5 minutes, two the frontier town Lancers arrive and begin threatening to arrest Mira for disorderly conduct. Hob will encourage this loudly.

---

### Segment 2: The Barrel

A large barrel leans against the chain divider near the broken crank. The crank's missing pin lies wedged between cobblestones 15 feet away.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 11 Perception:** Spot the missing pin in the cracks between stones.
- **DC 15 Investigation (barrel):** The bottom is false — a wax-sealed oilcloth packet inside.
- **DC 13 Sleight of Hand:** Palm the packet without drawing attention from the crowd or Hob.
- **DC 12 Athletics / DC 10 Acrobatics:** Cross the chain divider without being noticed. Failure draws eyes.

**Escalation:** If Hob sees anyone near the barrel, the argument ends abruptly and he walks away fast.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Chain Divider:** Crossing requires Athletics DC 12 or Acrobatics DC 10 (duck under). Failure attracts crowd attention — Disadvantage on subsequent Stealth checks in the scene.
2. **Crank Handle:** Inserting the found pin and cranking opens the iron gate in one action, creating a clear second exit from the area.
3. **Brick Plug:** The clean-edged brick can be pulled free with Strength DC 14. Behind it: a shallow dry niche (empty — someone got there first).
4. **Enchanted Mural:** Creatures stepping on the mural see illusory fish dart away. No mechanical effect; memorable roleplay texture.

---

## Difficulty Dial

**Tier 1 (Lvl 1–4):** As written. Hob is a civilian with something to hide — no combat.
**Tier 2 (Lvl 5–8):** Hob is the Shadow Syndicate courier. If he leaves without the barrel, two Cult Thugs emerge from a side alley to collect it. Add initiative.
**Tier 3 (Lvl 9+):** The sealed packet contains a soul vessel. Breaking it releases a Specter.
