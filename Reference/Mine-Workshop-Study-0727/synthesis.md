## 3. The one load-bearing reconciliation: Site 5 vs. `BF-SHOP-WORKSHOP`

This is the most consequential finding in the pass, so it is stated once here plainly rather
than only inside the breadth sweep.

`docs/BUILDING-PROGRAM-TABLE-FAMILIES.md` §2 defines `BF-SHOP-WORKSHOP` as the shared chassis
for Smithy, Apothecary, General Store, and Arcanist — single-building, settlement-scale,
customer-facing programs, with a named `smithy-arrangement` row explicitly covering "repair
smith with customer queue," "production forge with commission book," "armorer with fitting and
secure stock," "farrier with yard/animal route," and "machine or fabrication shop with powered
work bays." That document is `ACCEPTED DIRECTION` with live implementation deferred — it is not
in conflict with Site 5, but the two have never been reconciled in writing until this pass.

**The boundary, PROPOSED not ruled:** Site 5 owns a workshop only when it sits at the terminus
of its own production chain — physically fed by a mine or quarry's haul spine, with ore or
stone in and a distinct waste/product export edge out, sharing the site's water/power/support
circuits. `BF-SHOP-WORKSHOP` owns a workshop that is a freestanding urban or settlement
building sourcing its stock from elsewhere and selling or servicing at a customer-facing
counter or door. The tell is not appearance (both may show a forge, an anvil, a stamped-metal
product) but **connection**: does removing the building sever a real haulage/waste/water
circuit that the rest of the site still needs (Site 5), or does it just close a shop (bin b,
`BF-SHOP-WORKSHOP`)?

MW-11 (`LICENSE-LEDGER.md`) — a real Italian street-level blacksmith shop with no visible ore,
haulage, or waste chain behind it — is the photographic anchor for the `BF-SHOP-WORKSHOP` side
of that line. The working spec's own "attached workshop" stretch family (§4 Ideal expansion,
"attached crushing, washing, forging, smelting, milling, and assembly chains") is the Site 5
side. Nothing in either document currently states this test; it is offered here as a proposed
addition to the working spec's boundaries section, to be founder-ruled or revised at the next
review rather than treated as settled by this pass.
