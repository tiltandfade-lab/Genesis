*p. 106 MM 2024*

In a gritty, investigation-heavy campaign, NPC druids are rarely passive hermits sitting peacefully in the woods. They are the militant immune system of the natural world. They view the aggressive expansion of civilization, the corruption of the undead, and the presence of aberrations as literal infections that must be excised.

Because they possess a secret language (Druidic) used to leave hidden messages, and the ability to shapeshift, they operate as perfect spies, saboteurs, and guerrilla fighters within and around frontier settlements.

### Confirmed Facts & Campaign Integration

- **The Secret Network:** Druids speak and write a hidden language that allows them to leave warning signs and messages invisible to the untrained eye; outsiders require magical assistance to decipher them. Finding these marks in a city means a druidic cell has infiltrated the urban infrastructure.
- **The Great Balance:** Druids are obsessed with equilibrium. They will actively hunt down and eradicate cults of Elemental Evil, vampires, beholders, and mind flayers that encroach on their territory.
- **The Elemental Bond:** High-level druids (Archdruids) do not just turn into animals; they can physically transform into raw elemental avatars (like fire or earth elementals) to crush their enemies.

### 1. Druid (Circle Warden)

The rank-and-file agent of a druidic circle. They use magic to entangle foes and augment their own physical strikes, relying on nature to provide their armor and weaponry.

_Medium or Small Humanoid (Druid), Neutral_

- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Hit Points:** 44 (8d8 + 8)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Initiative:** +1 (11)
- **STR:** 10 (+0) | **DEX:** 12 (+1) | **CON:** 13 (+1) | **INT:** 12 (+1) | **WIS:** 16 (+3) | **CHA:** 11 (+0)
- **Skills:** Medicine +5, Nature +3, Perception +5
- **Gear:** Studded Leather Armor
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 15
- **Languages:** Common, Druidic, Sylvan
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Actions**

- **Multiattack.** The druid makes two attacks, using Vine Staff or Verdant Wisp in any combination.
- **Vine Staff.** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 7 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 2 (1d4) Poison damage.
- **Verdant Wisp.** _Ranged Attack Roll:_ +5, range 90 ft. _Hit:_ 10 (3d6) Radiant damage.
- **Spellcasting.** The druid casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (Spell Save DC 13):
    - _At will:_ `Druidcraft`, `Speak with Animals`
    - _2/day each:_ `Entangle`, `Thunderwave`
    - _1/day each:_ `Animal Messenger`, `Longstrider`, `Moonbeam`

### 2. Archdruid

Archdruids are ancient, terrifying forces of nature who watch over massive domains. They rarely interact with civilized folk unless a dire threat to the natural order arises.

_(The Archdruid stat block does not appear in the 2024 Monster Manual. The stat block below is retained from the 2014 edition as a campaign reference — treat as non-canonical until a 2024 equivalent is published.)_

_Medium Humanoid, Any Alignment_

- **Armor Class:** 14 (Hide Armor)
- **Hit Points:** 154 (28d8 + 28)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 14 (+2) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 12 (+1) | **INT:** 12 (+1) | **WIS:** 20 (+5) | **CHA:** 11 (+0)
- **Saving Throws:** Int +5, Wis +9
- **Skills:** Medicine +9, Nature +5, Perception +9
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 19
- **Languages:** Druidic plus any two languages
- **Challenge:** 12 (8,400 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +4

**Actions**

- **Spellcasting.** The archdruid casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (Spell Save DC 17):
    - _At will:_ `Beast Sense`, `Entangle`, `Speak with Animals`
    - _3/day each:_ `Animal Messenger`, `Dominate Beast`, `Faerie Fire`, `Tree Stride`
    - _1/day each:_ `Commune with Nature`, `Mass Cure Wounds`

**Bonus Actions**

- **Change Shape (2/Day).** The archdruid magically transforms into a Beast or an Elemental with a Challenge Rating of 6 or less, and can remain in that form for up to 9 hours. The archdruid reverts to its true form if it dies, falls unconscious, or uses a Bonus Action to revert.

### Encounter Scaffolds & Investigation Tables

#### d6 Druidic Traditions (The Faction's Purpose)

Druids organize themselves around specific philosophies and goals regarding the Great Balance. _(Roll or choose one to define the local circle's motivation)._

|d6|Tradition / Role|Campaign Integration & NPC Behavior|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**Avengers Against Destructive Civilizations**|These druids actively sabotage logging camps, collapse mining tunnels, and assassinate corrupt merchants who pollute the rivers. They view the PCs' city as a tumor.|
|**2**|**Navigation Guides**|Operating on the fringes of society, these druids act as highly paid escorts through deadly terrain (like deep swamps or fey-crossed forests). They demand payment in rare seeds or favors rather than gold.|
|**3**|**Hermits**|Deeply isolated, these individuals guard ancient, slumbering threats (like a sealed planar gate or a buried fiend). They will violently expel anyone who comes near their ward, refusing to explain why.|
|**4**|**Healing Menders**|Focused on the cycle of life, they operate secret clinics on the outskirts of town, using herbalism to cure magical diseases and poisons that local clerics cannot fix. They often ask the PCs to harvest dangerous monster organs as payment.|
|**5**|**Member of an Organization**|The druid has integrated into a formal, civilized guild (such as a thieves' guild or a noble spy ring), using their _Wild Shape_ to act as an undetectable scout (e.g., an urban rat or street dog).|
|**6**|**Extraplanar Wardens**|Dedicated to fighting aberrations, elementals, or undead. They monitor dimensional rifts and enlist the PCs as shock troops to hunt down creatures that do not belong on the Material Plane.|

#### d4 Druidic Signatures (The Investigation Layer)

Because druids avoid leaving conventional evidence, tracking them or identifying their handiwork requires a keen eye for manipulated nature.

|d4|Discovery|Clue / Investigative Hook|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Hidden Cipher**|A series of seemingly random scratches on a city gate or an arrangement of stones in the road. A DC 15 Perception check reveals it is a deliberate message. Without magic, the PCs know it's Druidic, but cannot read it.|
|**2**|**The Unnatural Growth**|The door to a suspect's safehouse has been completely sealed shut by thick, accelerated plant growth (_Plant Growth_ or _Entangle_ remnants), trapping whoever was inside.|
|**3**|**The Watching Wildlife**|The PCs realize that everywhere they go in the region, a specific type of common animal (a raven, a stray dog, a stag) is always sitting quietly and watching them, serving as the eyes of a Circle.|
|**4**|**The Cured Soil**|In the middle of a toxic swamp or blighted wasteland, the party finds a perfectly circular, 30-foot patch of pristine, healthy grass and clean water, marking the site where a druid recently rested and cast purification magic.|
