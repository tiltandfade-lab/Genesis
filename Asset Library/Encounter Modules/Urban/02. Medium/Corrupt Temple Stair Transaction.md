## 30-Second Run

In the temple district, odd reflected light draws the eye to a small exchange under the stairs: two regional guards and a known bandit, a barrel between them, posture too casual to be official. The stairs above are slicked with glass beads and frost. A tanning acid trough at the workshop level above is very obvious and very dangerous. If the scene stalls, a rat swarm pours from a gap in the wall and breaks everything open.

---

## Setup

**Space:** A temple staircase climbing through stone pillars toward a craftsman workshop above. Chanting and bells drift from somewhere inside. The under-stair alcove is partially screened from the street.
**Atmosphere:** Spice-and-herb smell from the workshop, uncanny frost on the steps, glass beads scattered on the risers. An odd shaft of light catches the exchange at exactly the wrong moment.
**NPCs Present:** Guard A (nervous), Guard B (brazen), Known Bandit (cool, managing the handoff), Monk Craftsman (offscreen, above).
**If Ignored:** The exchange completes. The bandit delivers the barrel to a purchaser. The route above goes unexamined.

## Segments

### Segment 1: Reading the Scene

The guards are staging the meeting to look like a sanctioned inspection. Most passersby wouldn't question it. The barrel is the transaction — contraband being held under guard protection until a purchaser collects it.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 13 Insight:** The posture, pacing, and way they handle the barrel don't match inspection or seizure — this is a payoff.
- **DC 10 Perception:** The reflected light isn't accidental — an upstairs window was opened at just the right moment. Someone is watching.
- **DC 15 Investigation (barrel):** False bottom. Contains contraband (roll d6 Guarded Contraband).

**Escalation:** If any PC is identified as a threat, Guard B signals Guard A — they position to block both ends of the alcove. The bandit goes still.

---

### Segment 2: The Stairs

Getting above the exchange — either to observe from height, access the workshop, or flank the guards — requires navigating the staircase.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 12 Acrobatics:** Navigate the glass beads without slipping. Failure: fall Prone on the stairs, loud clatter (everyone looks up).
- Uncanny frost on the steps: Difficult Terrain throughout.
- **DC 12 Investigation (workshop level):** The netting hung as a divider above partially screens the tanning acid trough. The trough is open and full.

**Escalation:** The tanning acid trough can be toppled (DC 14 Athletics to push, 10-ft line, DC 12 Dex save or 2d8 acid damage, area becomes Difficult Terrain). Loud, obvious, absolutely ruins the stealth option.

---

### Segment 3: The Rats

If the scene stalls — no confrontation, no investigation, no exit — a gap low in the wall disgorges a swarm of rats that panics the bandit and scatters the guards. Everything resets to chaos.

**Mechanics:** The rat gap is also a narrow access point — DC 12 Investigation to recognize it leads to underworks or a cache behind the wall.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Stair Hazard:** Glass beads on ascending stairs = Difficult Terrain + DC 12 Acrobatics check to avoid falling Prone. Frost adds an additional −5 ft to movement on the staircase.
2. **Tanning Acid Trough:** Hazard, obvious. Knockover requires Athletics DC 14. Effect: 10-ft line, DC 12 Dex save, 2d8 acid on fail. Area becomes Difficult Terrain (acid puddle).
3. **Rat Gap:** Swarm of Rats emerges on round 3 if scene hasn't resolved. The gap itself is a separate clue: DC 12 Investigation to see it leads somewhere.
4. **Contraband Barrel:** DC 15 Investigation to find false bottom. Guards notice a PC spending more than one action near the barrel.

## Difficulty Dial

**Tier 1 (Lvl 1–3):** As written. Guards are complicit civilians, not fighters. Bandit is cautious.
**Tier 2 (Lvl 4–6):** Guards are trained soldiers. The bandit is a Cult Thug. A fourth figure — the purchaser — arrives at the end of round 2 to collect.
**Tier 3 (Lvl 7+):** The barrel contains something alive (see result 3 or 6 on the d6 table). The purchaser is a cult lieutenant.
