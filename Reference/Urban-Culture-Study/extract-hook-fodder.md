# Extract — arrival-hook fodder for the urban institution

**Nothing here is locked. Adam rules; this file evidences.**

**What this sheet is.** 44 arrival-hook candidates for site 10, sorted by spice band, each one
traceable to a sourced cultural fact in the lane notes. Per the arrival hook law, every rolled
site arrival presents an active hook, band-appropriate; per SPICE-RAISE, the band is picked from
the region's tier weights first and the row second, so **all five bands need real coverage** and
this sheet supplies it.

**Banding discipline used here** (following SPICE-RULER as summarised in WORLD-TURN §7's worked
reclassifications): *Grounded* = concrete human pressure, never filler · *Textured* = the social
world visibly bent · *Strange* = a present oddity or an aftermath the town has absorbed ·
*Volatile* = an active force in present-tense motion · *Mythic* = permanence and remaking.

**Counts: Grounded 12 · Textured 11 · Strange 10 · Volatile 6 · Mythic 5 = 44.**

Column key — **trace** names the lane and the sourced fact the hook is built from. A hook with no
trace does not belong on this sheet.

## Notes for whoever wires this

1. **Every row above is an ACTIVE hook, not a description.** Per the arrival hook law the hook is
   a rolled fact the DM expresses, not DM invention; each row here should survive being handed to
   the DM as a single sentence of world-state.
2. **Bands are proposals.** Several rows sit on a boundary and I have said so where it matters
   (M1 especially — the accommodation is what lifts it out of Strange; if the rota is removed it
   drops a band). SPICE-RULER is the labelling authority, not this sheet.
3. **Time-of-day and calendar are hook multipliers, not hooks.** G3, G7, G11 and T8 all *are* the
   clock; the same site at another hour has a different hook from the same table. This is the lane-1
   result: time of day is a rules parameter.
4. **Six rows scale by occupancy rather than band** — G4, G5, G6, T2, T3, V5 all get sharper or
   softer depending on whether the institution is functioning, corrupt, faction-seized, abandoned,
   or a front (the standard site-10 occupancy axis).
5. **Nothing here needs a fantasy element to work.** Every Strange-band row is a real historical
   fact placed in front of a player who does not know it is real. That is the study's central
   argument for the band system: the archive is already weirder than invention.
