## 30-Second Run

The air changes the moment the party enters — something alchemical, or arcane, or just wrong. A low vibration hums through the floor. Guards or mercenaries are around the sharp corner ahead, but they're not standing down — they're already fighting something. A brazier of pale ash has been set back too neatly. A wet ankle-deep trench runs along one edge. Fragile terrain everywhere: cover breaks, footing fails, railings snap. And one fake brick, if pressed, disarms whatever alarm is keeping the whole corner locked down.

---

## Setup

**Space:** Sharp corner — tight turn limiting all sightlines. A wet trench runs along one wall (ankle-deep). A refuse pile narrows the approach. A brazier of pale ash sits set back too precisely — recently moved. A woven cloth gate-piece hangs on the bars ahead, being actively vandalized by someone.
**Atmosphere:** Flickering light — fire, reflected off wet stones. A low vibration in the wall when touched. Air smells different on entry — alchemical, not natural. Dial disc with worn markings is visible on the wall near the brazier.
**NPCs Present:** Guards or Mercenaries (4, already in combat around the corner), Unknown Enemy (currently engaging the guards — nature depends on campaign context), Vandal (defacing the gate-piece, background NPC).
**If Ignored:** The guards lose or win without the party's help. The fake brick is never found. The alarm remains active for the next approach.

## Segments

### Segment 1: What Are They Fighting?

The guards aren't waiting for the party. They're already committed to something around the corner. The party can use this.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 13 Perception (from behind the corner):** Hear the nature of what the guards are fighting — undead groaning, animal snarls, cultist chanting, or nothing audible (something magical, silent).
- **DC 12 Insight (guards, at a glance):** They're losing. Or holding. Or barely maintaining. This determines whether intervening helps them or just adds the party to the casualty list.
- **DC 14 Stealth:** Pass through the corridor without either side noticing the party — use the trench and refuse pile for cover.

**Escalation:** Entering line of sight of either faction immediately draws attention. The unknown enemy pivots to assess the party as a new threat. The guards may or may not see the party as backup.

---

### Segment 2: Fragile Terrain

The whole space is structurally compromised and full of improvised hazards.

**Mechanics:**
- **Wet trench (ankle-deep, along one wall):** Difficult Terrain. Creatures shoved toward it: DC 12 Acrobatics or fall in — Prone + Speed halved next turn.
- **Refuse pile:** Provides half cover. Climbing through it: DC 10 Athletics, loud.
- **Brazier:** Can be knocked over — 10-ft cone of hot ash, DC 12 Dex save or 1d6 fire + Blinded until end of next turn.
- **Fragile railing:** Any heavy impact (grapple/shove against it): Breaks if creature fails DC 10 Str save — 15-ft drop to lower area below.
- **Flickering light:** Disadvantage on ranged attacks at targets 30+ ft away.

---

### Segment 3: The Fake Brick

One stone in the wall near the dial disc depresses slightly when pushed.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 15 Perception:** Notice the slightly mismatched stone — it doesn't crumble like the others; it gives, slightly.
- **Pressing it:** Immediately disarms the nearest mechanical trap or alarm in the area. The dial disc was controlling it — the disc's worn markings now make sense as an override sequence.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Three-Way Problem:** Guards are already engaged. The party can join, exploit, flee, or observe. Only one approach prevents all parties from eventually turning hostile.
2. **Alarm/Trap:** Active until fake brick found and pressed. While active: any creature moving faster than half speed in the corridor triggers it (see the dial disc — DC 13 Investigation to identify the alarm's nature and scope).
3. **Dial Disc:** Worn unevenly. DC 13 Investigation: it controls an old civic mechanism — lock, vent, or ward-linked alarm. Turning it to the correct worn position (DC 12 Dexterity or Intelligence) opens or closes the mechanism.
4. **Vandal NPC:** Low-stakes background presence. Tears the cloth gate-piece for unknown reasons. If approached: a thief checking for something hidden in the weave. Will bolt if combat starts.

---

## Difficulty Dial

**Tier 1 (Lvl 2–4):** Guards are fighting 3 Bandits around the corner. Fragile terrain is visual flavor only.
**Tier 2 (Lvl 4–6):** Guards are fighting 3 Shadows or a Cult cell. The guards lose in 2 rounds without help. The unknown enemy turns to the party on round 3.
**Tier 3 (Lvl 7+):** Guards are fighting a Cult Lieutenant with command over the thugs. If the party reveals themselves, both sides target them first.
