#place-ruler-status

Every row is a runnable situation: who rules, plus the live tension a DM can pull on. **Full d100 — 100 unique rows**, spice-graded 66/20/9/4/1, Commitment ceiling. Upgraded 2026-07-09 from the DMG14 gather seeds (Ruler's Status d20, Forms of Government d100, Local Leaders d12); rows PROVISIONAL pending Adam's red-pen. Original in `zz_Archive/`.
