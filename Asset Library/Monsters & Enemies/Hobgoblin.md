*p. 157 MM 2024*

Hobgoblins are what goblins aspire to and bugbears look down on — the disciplined, martial middle-ground of the goblinoid hierarchy. Where goblins are chaotic and cowardly and bugbears are brutish and solitary, hobgoblins form armies. They drill. They maintain formations. They have a chain of command. In a war-shaped region, encountering hobgoblins means encountering an *organization* — there is always a plan, always a captain, always a supply line somewhere.

Hobgoblin society is built entirely around the concept of the *Blood War* — an eternal struggle against weakness, against disorder, against every enemy that doesn't submit. They hold no grudges against individuals; they simply categorize the world into those who serve Maglubiyet and those who will be conquered. This makes them more dangerous than any goblinoid: they are predictable, which sounds like a weakness, but a predictable enemy that never retreats, never panics, and never abandons their formation is terrifying.

Hobgoblin captains are tacticians first and fighters second. They don't engage in heroic duels — they position their troops, call retreats when necessary, and treat every combat as a resource allocation problem. A captain who is losing will spend the lives of hobgoblin soldiers to buy time for the plan, not to save themselves.

### Campaign Integration: Investigation & Clues

Hobgoblin activity looks nothing like goblin or bugbear activity. It looks like a military presence.

- **The Supply Cache:** Hobgoblin warbands maintain organized supply caches — weapon racks, ration stores, marked maps. Finding one indicates a planned, sustained operation, not a raiding party.
- **The Watch Rotation:** Hobgoblin sentries rotate on a schedule. A party that scouts an area will find guards in the same position at the same time every four hours. This predictability can be exploited — or it can be a trap, since hobgoblin captains know their patrols are regular and may deliberately leave a "gap."
- **The Battle Standard:** Hobgoblins carry unit banners into every operation. Finding a planted standard means a specific unit has claimed this territory. A DC 13 History check can identify the unit, its commander, and its usual operating area.

### 1. Hobgoblin Soldier

The backbone of any hobgoblin warband. Disciplined, armored, and trained to fight as a unit rather than as an individual.

_Medium Humanoid (Goblinoid), Lawful Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 18 (chain mail, shield)
- **Hit Points:** 11 (2d8 + 2)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 13 (+1) | **DEX:** 12 (+1) | **CON:** 12 (+1) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 9 (−1)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common, Goblin
- **Challenge:** 1/2 (100 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Martial Advantage:** Once per turn, the hobgoblin can deal an extra 7 (2d6) damage to a creature it hits with a weapon attack if that creature is within 5 ft. of an ally of the hobgoblin that isn't incapacitated.

**Actions**

- **Longsword:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 5 (1d8 + 1) slashing damage, or 6 (1d10 + 1) slashing damage if used with two hands.
- **Longbow:** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +3 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage.

A specialized hobgoblin trained in stealth and martial arts — a rare elite unit used for assassination, infiltration, and disrupting enemy spellcasters.

- **Languages:** Common, Goblin
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The iron shadow makes four attacks, each with a different weapon: longsword, shortsword, unarmed strike, or hand crossbow.

If the target is a creature, the iron shadow can choose one of the following additional effects: the target must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or be knocked Prone; the target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 ft. away; the target can't take reactions until the start of the iron shadow's next turn.
- **Hand Crossbow:** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.

### 3. Hobgoblin Captain

A battle-hardened officer who commands a warband through a combination of tactical genius and iron discipline. Captains rarely engage until they have positioned their troops.

_Medium Humanoid (Goblinoid), Lawful Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 17 (half plate)
- **Hit Points:** 52 (8d8 + 16)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 15 (+2) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 14 (+2) | **INT:** 14 (+2) | **WIS:** 11 (+0) | **CHA:** 13 (+1)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common, Goblin
- **Challenge:** 3 (700 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Martial Advantage:** Once per turn, the captain can deal an extra 10 (3d6) damage to a creature it hits with a weapon attack if that creature is within 5 ft. of an ally of the captain that isn't incapacitated.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The captain makes two Greatsword attacks.
- **Greatsword:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 9 (2d6 + 2) slashing damage.
- **Javelin:** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.
- **Leadership (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest):** For 1 minute, the captain can utter a special command or warning whenever a nonhostile creature within 30 ft. that can hear the captain makes an attack roll or saving throw. That creature can add a d4 to its roll, provided it can hear and understand the captain. A creature can benefit from only one Leadership die at a time.

|:----|:------------------|:--------------------------|
| **1** | **The Checkpoint** | **Tense Social:** A hobgoblin patrol has set up a checkpoint on the only road out of the dungeon. They are demanding to inspect cargo — including any prisoners the party might be escorting. The captain is politely menacing. |

| **3** | **The Defector** | **Intrigue:** A hobgoblin soldier approaches the party under a white flag. They have information about the captain's plan — in exchange for passage out of the dungeon and away from certain execution for losing a patrol. |
| **4** | **The Supply Disruption** | **Objective-Based:** The party needs to reach the other side of the dungeon, but a hobgoblin warband is blocking the route. They won't attack unless threatened — they're on a supply mission and are counting on the party not wanting a fight. |

### d10 The Garrison Scene (Investigation & Clues)

| d10 | Discovery | Investigative Hook |
|:----|:----------|:------------------|
| **1** | **The Roster** | A precisely kept list of names, unit designations, and daily assignments. A DC 12 Insight check notes two names have been crossed out and replaced — deserters, defectors, or the recently deceased. |
| **2** | **The Tactical Map** | A detailed map of the surrounding area, with patrol routes marked in red, civilian settlements marked in black, and a location three days' march away circled and labeled "rally point." |
| **3** | **The Execution Order** | A sealed scroll contains a signed execution order for a named individual — a merchant, a hedge wizard, or a frontier official. The hobgoblins weren't here to raid; they were here to kill someone specific. |
| **4** | **The Training Dummy** | A human-shaped training dummy in the corner is dressed in a crude approximation of a local guard's uniform. The hobgoblins were specifically practicing how to fight the local garrison. |
| **5** | **The Letters** | A bundle of personal letters — written by hobgoblin soldiers to family in a distant fortress, never sent. They describe the mission in vague terms but hint at a timeline. The mission was supposed to be over by now. |
| **6** | **The Interrogation Notes** | A captain's log of prisoner interrogations. Names, dates, questions asked, answers received. The most recent entry describes information about a hidden safe house or an underground contact network. |
| **7** | **The Defaced Symbol** | A religious shrine in the room has been methodically defaced — not vandalized in rage, but carefully and deliberately destroyed. Hobgoblins follow Maglubiyet and eradicate competing divine presences in occupied territory. |
| **8** | **The Broken Formation Marker** | Chalk marks on the floor show a complex battle formation that was drilled here. The formation includes a gap on the eastern side — a deliberate weakness, possibly a trap for enemies who think they've found an opening. |
| **9** | **The Prisoner Manifest** | A list of captives taken over the past two weeks. Most names are farmers or merchants. One name is underlined three times — someone the captain considers important. That person may still be alive somewhere in this dungeon. |
| **10** | **The Counter-sign** | A cipher key and partial message are found. A DC 15 Investigation check decodes enough of it to reveal a rendezvous time and location — the party can impersonate the patrol at that meeting, or arrive to ambush whoever is coming to meet them. |
