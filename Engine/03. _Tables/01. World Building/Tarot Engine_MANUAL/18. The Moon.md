### **XVIII. The Moon: Trigger Effects**

The Moon represents the realm of the subconscious, illusions, and the hidden fears that surface in the dark. It is the card of shifting tides and wild, untamed nature. Under this card, the dungeon becomes a place where the senses cannot be trusted and the boundary between the material world and the world of dreams is thin.

---

**The Constant: "The Silver Delusion"**

Under the Moon’s gaze, the environment fluctuates like a mirage.

- **The Effect:** Any creature that relies on sight has **Disadvantage** on Wisdom (Perception) checks made to detect hidden creatures, objects, or traps.
    
- **The Toll:** "The Lunar Fever." At the start of each combat, every creature must succeed on a **DC 13 Wisdom saving throw** or be **Confused** (as the spell) until the start of their next turn, overwhelmed by shifting shadows and internal anxieties.

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

**The Constant: "The Silver Conspiracy"**

The faction is shrouded in secrets and erratic behavior. Their goals shift with the phases of the moon, making long-term prediction impossible.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**Faction Behavior (The Moon)**|**Narrative Implication**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Hidden Saboteurs**|The faction has replaced 10% of a city’s leadership with doppelgangers or shapechangers.|
|**6-9**|Textured|**The Lunar Cult**|The faction believes the moon is an eye. They refuse to do anything "unobserved" by the night sky.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Collective Nightmare**|The faction has stopped speaking. They now communicate by projecting their dreams into the minds of the populace.|

### **Trigger: Accepting Binding Agreements**

**The Constant: "The Cost of the Moon"**

The bargain grants you access to hidden truths and shadows, but you lose your grip on the sun. You take 1d10 radiant damage every hour you spend in direct sunlight.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**The Bargain's Mutation (The Moon)**|**The "Gift"**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Shadow-Self**|You can create an illusory duplicate of yourself as a bonus action, which mimics your movements exactly.|
|**6-9**|Textured|**Dream-Walker**|You can enter the dreams of any sleeping creature. The cost: You no longer gain the benefits of a long rest.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Author’s Pen**|You can change one established "Fact" about the world (e.g., "The King has a secret twin"). The world reshapes to make it true.|

The sun dims, and the world enters an era of perpetual twilight. Wild nature reclaims the roads, and secrets become the primary global currency.
