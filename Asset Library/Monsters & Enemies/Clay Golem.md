### The Clay Golem: The Unforgiving Warden

In a gritty, investigation-forward campaign, a Clay Golem is not merely a monster; it is a walking curse left behind by ancient priests or fanatical cults. Unlike flesh golems (which are mockeries of science) or iron golems (which are weapons of war), a clay golem is driven by raw, unyielding divine purpose. It is a hulking, misshapen brute that never tires, never negotiates, and never questions the morality of its orders.

**Confirmed Facts:**

- **Unmendable Wounds:** The clay golem’s fists inflict a terrible magical affliction. Wounds caused by its slam attacks fester and refuse to heal, actively reducing the victim's maximum hit points until they can fully rest.
- **The Breaking Point:** Clay is a weak vessel for the elemental spirit bound inside it. When heavily damaged, the binding cracks, and the golem is highly likely to go berserk, abandoning its programming to destroy everything around it.
- **Acid Affinity:** Corrosive substances do not harm the golem; they actually heal its clay body, melting away cracks and restoring its mass.

**Campaign Assumptions (Integration into your Setting):**

- **The Lost Creators:** The clerics or druids who animated these golems are long dead, but their rigid, extreme laws are still being enforced by these tireless statues.
- **The Literal Mind:** Golems cannot be tricked with clever arguments. They understand their creator's commands literally and perfectly, but have no grasp of context or language beyond those parameters.

### Clay Golem

_Large Construct, Unaligned_

- **Armor Class:** 14 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 123 (13d10 + 52)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 20 (+5) | **DEX:** 9 (-1) | **CON:** 18 (+4) | **INT:** 3 (-4) | **WIS:** 8 (-1) | **CHA:** 1 (-5)
- **Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing
- **Damage Immunities:** Acid, poison, psychic
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9
- **Languages:** Common plus one other language
- **Challenge:** 9 (5,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +4

**Traits**

- **Acid Absorption:** Whenever the golem is subjected to acid damage, it takes no damage and instead regains a number of hit points equal to the acid damage dealt.
- **Berserk:** Whenever the golem starts its turn Bloodied, roll a d6. On a 6, the golem goes berserk. On each of its turns while berserk, the golem attacks the nearest creature it can see. If no creature is near enough to move to and attack, the golem attacks an object. Once the golem goes berserk, it continues to do so until it is destroyed or it is no longer Bloodied.
- **Immutable Form:** The golem can't shape-shift.
- **Magic Resistance:** The golem has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The golem makes two Slam attacks, or it makes three Slam attacks if it used Hasten this turn.
- **Slam:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 10 (1d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage plus 6 (1d12) acid damage, and the target's Hit Point maximum decreases by an amount equal to the Acid damage taken.

**Bonus Actions**

- **Hasten (Recharge 5–6):** The golem takes the Dash and Disengage actions.

### Encounter Scaffolds & Investigation Tables

#### d6 The Golem's Directive (Rules of Engagement)

A golem possesses no initiative of its own. It acts entirely based on a simple, unbreakable program set by its creator. Discovering this program is the key to surviving or bypassing the encounter without a lethal fight.

|d6|The Programmed Order|Tactical Behavior & Bypass Condition|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**Pacification Protocol**|_“Block the path of anyone with weapons drawn.”_ The golem stands idle if the party approaches with empty hands. If a weapon is drawn or a spell focus is presented, it immediately attacks until the weapons are dropped.|
|**2**|**The Sanctuary Warden**|_“Defend members of the community.”_ The golem protects anyone wearing a specific holy symbol or bearing a specific local bloodline. It will ignore the PCs unless they take hostile action against the "community" members in the room.|
|**3**|**The Inquisitor**|_“Fight fiends and the corrupted.”_ The golem actively hunts down anything with the Fiend type, or anyone carrying desecrated/evil magic items. It will ruthlessly prioritize these targets over all others.|
|**4**|**General Security**|_“Patrol the perimeter and crush intruders.”_ The golem marches a continuous, predictable route. It attacks anyone who does not speak a specific, ancient passphrase.|
|**5**|**The Alarm Bell**|_“Ring the gong and hold the line.”_ Upon spotting an intruder, the golem's first action is to strike a massive bronze bell to alert the rest of the dungeon. It then focuses entirely on Grappling the largest PC to hold them in place for reinforcements.|
|**6**|**The Relic Keeper**|_“Guard the altar.”_ The golem completely ignores the PCs, even if they walk right past it or loot the rest of the room. It only animates and attacks if a specific, central object (like a relic or locked chest) is touched.|

#### d4 The Stage (Tactical Terrain)

Clay golems are often stationed in areas that complement their unique immunities, turning the environment into a weapon against the players.

|d4|Lair Terrain & Hazard|2024 Mechanical Integration & Tactics|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Acid Vats**|The room is lined with pools of alchemical acid or caustic sludge. The golem will deliberately grapple a PC and attempt to drag them into a vat, taking the acid damage itself in order to heal via its _Acid Absorption_ trait while melting the PC.|
|**2**|**Crumbling Tombs**|The ceiling is unstable. Due to the golem's _Magic Weapons_ and massive strength, every time it misses a Slam attack and hits a wall, 1d4 chunks of masonry fall. Creatures within 5 feet of the impact must succeed on a DC 14 Dex save or take 1d6 bludgeoning damage.|
|**3**|**Narrow Catacombs**|The hallways are only 5 feet wide. The Large-sized golem completely blocks the passage, forcing martial characters to engage it one-on-one in melee while the golem's HP-reducing Slams slowly whittle the vanguard to death.|
|**4**|**Magical Darkness**|The creator warded the room with permanent _Darkness_. The golem's 60 ft. Darkvision allows it to see perfectly, forcing the PCs to fight a regenerating, HP-draining juggernaut completely blind unless they have magical light sources.|

#### d4 Macabre Clues (The Investigation Layer)

Tracking a golem is about noticing the sheer, unthinking application of mass and the lingering effects of its cursed clay.

|d4|Discovery|Clue / Campaign Hook|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Festering Corpse**|The party finds a dead tomb robber. A DC 14 Medicine check reveals that the massive bludgeoning wounds on their chest are surrounded by rotting, gray necrosis—a signature of the clay golem's unhealing strikes.|
|**2**|**The Perfect Footprints**|Deep, incredibly heavy footprints pressed into the dirt. A DC 13 Survival check notes that the stride length never alters, even over difficult terrain, indicating a machine rather than a living creature.|
|**3**|**The Acid Trap**|A hallway filled with spraying acid vents that reset automatically. The party finds heavy tracks walking straight through the acid spray without hesitation, suggesting the guardian is immune or healed by the trap.|
|**4**|**The Extracted Materials**|A local potter's shop or riverbank has been entirely excavated. Cultists recently stole over 1,000 pounds of raw, purified clay and 30,000 gp worth of enchanted vestments to build the construct.|
