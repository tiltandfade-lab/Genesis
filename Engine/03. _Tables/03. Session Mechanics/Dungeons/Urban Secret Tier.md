| d20 | Secret Tier | Result |
| --: | :---------: | ------ |
| 1 | Micro | **Loose board / stone stash.** Trifle + clue fragment (scrap, token, wax seal, chalk mark). |
| 2 | Micro | **Hairline seam.** A panel or compartment is almost hidden; opening it is simple if noticed. |
| 3 | Micro | **Misleading asymmetry.** False shutter, uneven wall, mismatched paving, or altered trim points to a deeper secret. |
| 4 | Micro | **Hidden message in plain sight.** Warning, tally, prayer, route mark, or coded symbol. |
| 5 | Micro | **Dropped evidence.** Footprints + a small object (button, cloth scrap, bead, receipt, seal fragment). |
| 6 | Micro | **Tiny cache.** 1–2 consumables or mundane resources (oil, chalk, bandages, food, twine, spikes). |
| 7 | Minor | **Hollow feature compartment.** Inside a post, shrine, bench, basin, crate base, or wall niche. |
| 8 | Minor | **Pocket behind a feature.** Behind hanging cloth, beneath a counter, under stairs, inside a hearth, under a sill. |
| 9 | Minor | **Environmental trigger reveal.** Move debris, turn a valve, shift a sign, lift a board → a compartment opens. |
| 10 | Minor | **Secret bypass (small).** Crawl gap, roof access, narrow alley slit, service passage, or drain route. |
| 11 | Minor | **Hidden tool advantage.** A useful item staged here (rope, prybar, lantern, key, wedge, disguise piece). |
| 12 | Standard | **Hidden compartment with meaning.** Clue + usable reward (map scrap, key, antitoxin, pass token, coded note). |
| 13 | Standard | **Concealed utility room.** Store room, watch nook, office, shrine prep room, records space—contains info + something useful. |
| 14 | Standard | **Concealed route room.** Optional alternate entrance/exit point within the site (never the only way). |
| 15 | Standard | **Secret encounter space.** Witness, informant, scavenger, prisoner, sentry, or lone threat in a hidden nook. |
| 16 | Standard | **Faction evidence cache.** Ledger, contraband, uniforms, coded letters, offerings, marked goods. |
| 17 | Major | **Major secret chamber tied to the site’s origin.** Founding mark, ward node, hidden office, old shrine, sealed archive. |
| 18 | Major | **The site lies.** A room or storefront is a façade hiding the true function behind or beneath it. |
| 19 | Major | **Major route revelation.** Shortcut to a key area, escape path, roof link, underworks stair, or hidden crossing. |
| 20 | Mythic | **Signature secret.** A unique revelation + reward that becomes campaign glue (named relic, key truth, recurring motif). |
^urban-secret-tier
