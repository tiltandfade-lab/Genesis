# Camp / Service — evidence-thickening study (0727)

## What this packet is for

The 2026-07-27 campaign brief for Site 2 is explicit: **do not self-rule.** The hosted-camp
mechanism and the camp's build orders are `PROPOSED`, pending Adam. This packet's job is to
make the founder's decision easier and better-informed — not to make the decision.

It serves the working spec's founder-packet section

3. the guest-family property (hosted camps borrowing a host's floor, with host-governed caps).

## One-paragraph summary

Three independent, historically unrelated service-and-lodging systems — the Roman
`cursus publicus` (mansio/mutatio), Scottish drove-road stances/inns, and the American
Pony Express/stagecoach relay/home-station network — each converged on the same two-tier
shape the ladder already proposes (an unstaffed, minimally-marked stop vs. a staffed, roofed,
provisioned one), which is strong convergent evidence for the rung 1 / rung 2 distinction.
The tent-kit build order's first two forms (bell/cone, Roman troop tent) are real,
well-documented, standardized military shelters exactly as described; the third (the low
open span) matches the Bedouin black tent's real open-front, adjustable-wall behavior almost
exactly. The guest-family mechanism's clearest real analog — Jordan's Zaatari refugee camp —
supports the core claim (a bounded, host-governed camp really can grow a rooted service
economy without becoming architecture) but also shows the mechanism's population/permanence
caps may need a harder upper bound: Zaatari's decade-long drift toward a de facto permanent
city is the live, real-world version of the exact boundary tension
`SETTLED-LIFE-SITES-PROGRAM.md` already flags as unclaimed by any of the twelve sites.
