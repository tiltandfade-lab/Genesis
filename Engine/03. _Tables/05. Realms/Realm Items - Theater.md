#realm-items-theater
> **PROVISIONAL — Adam spot-check pending.** The THEATER realm's item universe (docs/BREACH.md
> §2d, BATCH3-GUARDRAILS.md J2 restructure). d50; breach draws inside a `theater`-tagged walk
> roll THIS table; legacy Outlandish d300 stays the anachronism-intrusion grab-bag. Register per
> J3b: War, any war — never named, never flagged; shapes without nations, real atrocity never
> loot (docs/BREACH.md §2d content-safety, BATCH-GUARDRAILS G0/G9). This realm carries the
> **era-lens** sub-table (`data/realms.js` REALMS.theater.eraLens` — trench/hedgerow/legion/
> musket/longship/jungle/siege): every row below is written era-AGNOSTIC (no named nations, no
> named conflicts, no real atrocity depicted or looted) so a DM can present it through whichever
> lens the current breach rolled; where an item's flavor leans toward one lens more than another
> it's noted, but nothing here requires a specific lens to make sense. Every item names its FRAME
> (existing `data/items.js` id); none invented. **Band shares run hotter than H2's baseline by
> construction** (see Frontier's frontmatter). Rank ladders sit on enchanted + signature rows;
> reality-breaking rungs floor at L9. d50, Commitment ceiling.
