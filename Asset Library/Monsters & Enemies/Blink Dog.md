*p. 46 MM 2024*

In a gritty, high-pressure campaign, **Blink Dogs** shouldn't be treated as mere animals. With an Intelligence of 10, they are just as smart as the average human. They are fey creatures bound to a canine form, communicating in a complex language of yaps, growls, and whines.

Because they are Lawful Good, they can serve as unexpected allies, but their primary motivation is an ancient, brutal shadow-war against their mortal enemies: the Displacer Beasts.

Here is the 5e rules-as-written stat block, followed by campaign-agnostic hooks and modular tables for running a highly tactical fey pack.

### Campaign Integration Hooks

- **The Invisible War:** The party finds a trail of strange, intermittent blood splatters and severed tentacles. The local woods aren't haunted; they are the active battleground between a pack of Blink Dogs and a stalking Displacer Beast.
- **The Unseen Escorts:** While navigating a highly dangerous fey-corrupted forest, the party realizes they are being tracked. The trackers never trigger traps and leave no continuous footprints. The dogs are actually screening the party, waiting to see if they are a threat or if they can be pushed into attacking a local coven of hags or evil fey.

_Medium Fey, Lawful Good_

- **Hit Points:** 22 (4d8 + 4)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **STR:** 12 (+1) | **DEX:** 17 (+3) | **CON:** 12 (+1) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 13 (+1) | **CHA:** 11 (+0)
- **Skills:** Perception +5, Stealth +5
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 15
- **Languages:** Blink Dog; understands Elvish and Sylvan but can't speak them
- **Challenge:** 1/4 (50 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Actions**

- **Bite:** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage.

**Bonus Actions**

- **Teleport (Recharge 4–6):** The dog teleports up to 40 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.

### d6 Tracking the Pack (The Investigation Layer)

Because Blink Dogs teleport short distances, tracking them using normal survival methods is baffling to the uninitiated. _(Roll or choose one to provide a clue that the party is in Blink Dog territory)._

|d6|Environmental Clue|Narrative Implication|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**Broken Tracks**|A set of heavy canine footprints abruptly stops in the middle of a muddy path, only to resume perfectly 30 feet away, bypassing a tripwire or concealed pit entirely.|
|**2**|**The Complex Chorus**|The party hears wolves howling, but a DC 13 Nature or Insight check reveals it is far too structured—it sounds like a coordinated, multi-part conversation or military cadence.|
|**3**|**The Displacer's Bane**|The rotting, heavily chewed corpse of a Displacer Beast. The area is covered in fey dog tracks, confirming the pack successfully hunted down an apex predator.|
|**4**|**The Watched Camp**|During a long rest, the party's campfire catches the reflection of amber eyes in the brush. If the party approaches aggressively, the watcher vanishes with a faint "pop" of displaced air.|
|**5**|**The Safe Haven**|The dogs have deliberately corralled a lost child or wounded traveler into a defensible cave, blinking in occasionally to drop fresh game at the entrance to keep them fed.|
|**6**|**The Stolen Weapon**|An evil mercenary was found dead, their throat torn out, but their magical or silvered weapon is missing. The dogs teleported away with the weapon to keep it out of enemy hands.|

### d4 Blink Dog Tactics (Combat Behavior)

Blink Dogs do not fight like normal animals. They possess human-level intelligence, coordinate perfectly, and understand tactical positioning. They refuse to fight fair against evil foes.

| d4    | Pack Tactic              | 2024 Mechanical Integration & Execution                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| :---- | :----------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1** | **The Flanking Jump**    | The pack rarely charges head-on. They use their movement to close the distance, then use their _Teleport_ action to pop directly behind the party's weakest spellcasters to strike with Advantage (Flanking).                   |
| **2** | **Hit and Fade**         | Because the _Teleport_ action allows a bite attack _before_ or _after_ the jump, a dog will run up, Bite (+3 hit), and immediately teleport 40 feet away to high ground, completely avoiding Opportunity Attacks.               |
| **3** | **Vertical Superiority** | Blink dogs cannot teleport into solid objects, but they can teleport to any unoccupied space they can _see_. They will blink onto the roofs of cottages, thick tree branches, or the tops of boulders to scout or escape melee. |
| **4** | **The Tactical Retreat** | If a pack loses 25% or more of its numbers, they recognize the battle is lost. The entire pack will simultaneously use their Teleport action to disengage and flee into the heavy brush, preserving their lives.                |
