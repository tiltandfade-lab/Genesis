# Captain Bramwell Harth

*(Asset: guard_honorable_stability)*

## Level 1 — Immediate Table Presence

**Race / Background:**  
Human (Lowlander) / Veteran of the frontier town Reclamation

**Role:**  
Captain of the frontier town Guard

**Visual Quirk:**  
Keeps his armor impeccably maintained except for a single dent near the left collarbone he refuses to repair.

**Mood:**  
Measured and quietly vigilant.

**Demeanor:**  
Direct. Speaks plainly. Listens fully before responding.

**Hook:**  
Concerned about increased magical disturbance near the ruined obelisk site.

**Talent:**  
Reads people accurately without intimidation; strong tactical awareness.

**Unrealized Potential:**  
Could become a regional stabilizing leader if given broader authority.

---

## Level 2 — Structural Pressure

**Formative Trauma:**  
Lost several guards during the early rebuilding of the frontier town due to poor reconnaissance in the Drowned Marsh.

**Formative Grace:**  
Was once spared by a wandering adventurer who chose mercy over vengeance during a border skirmish in his youth.

**Want:**  
A stable the frontier town that does not fall back into chaos.

**Fear:**  
That unseen forces are moving beyond his ability to detect or contain.

**Faction Tie:**  
The Guard (Honorable Core)

**Influence Weight:**  
High within the Guard; moderate civic influence.

**Resource Control:**  
Access to patrol routes, basic guard deployment, holding cells, and official investigation authority.

**If Ignored (Personal):**  
Becomes overworked and increasingly cautious, tightening patrols and restricting access to dangerous areas.

**If Ignored (Regional):**  
Guard response slows; corruption and cult influence gain quiet footholds.

---

## Level 3 — Secrets

**Secret:**  
Suspects that something within the Guard ranks is compromised but lacks proof and refuses to make accusations without evidence.

**Leverage:**  
Respects competence and honesty; will quietly support those who prove reliable.

**If Cornered:**  
Protects civilians first. Will sacrifice reputation before integrity.

---

## Potential Quest / Adventure Tie-Ins

- Sends the party to investigate magical disturbance at the Obelisk Ruins.
- Requests discreet inquiry into irregular patrol reports near the marsh.
- Asks party to escort a detained cult suspect quietly to avoid public panic.

---

## Notes for Play

- Speaks in calm, practical sentences. No grand speeches.
- Makes eye contact when asking questions; does not interrupt.
- Rewards results, not flattery.
- Does not tolerate cruelty or recklessness.
- Pays fairly and on time.
