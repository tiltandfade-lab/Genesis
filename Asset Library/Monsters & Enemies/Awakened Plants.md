*p. 23 MM 2024*

In a gritty, investigation-forward campaign, awakened plants are much more than just whimsical woodland oddities. The magic to awaken a plant is highly deliberate: it requires tracing magical pathways within a precious gemstone, consuming the gem in the process. When targeted by this magic, a plant with no prior Intelligence gains an Intelligence of 10, the ability to speak one language known by its creator, and human-like senses. It also gains the ability to uproot itself and move its limbs, roots, and vines.

Because the newly awakened plant is charmed by its creator for exactly 30 days—after which it chooses whether to remain friendly based on how it was treated—an awakened plant is an incredible investigative tool, a perfect spy, or a localized guardian with a flawless memory.

Here are the Rules-As-Written stat blocks for awakened plants, along with modular integration hooks for your campaign.

### Campaign Integration Hooks

- **The Courtyard Spy (Awakened Shrub):** A local druid or faction spy awakened a seemingly ordinary topiary or shrub in the courtyard of a corrupt official. Because it possesses the _False Appearance_ trait, it has perfectly eavesdropped on every clandestine meeting for the last three weeks. The players must find a way to interrogate the shrub without alerting the estate guards.
- **The Vengeful Guardian (Awakened Tree):** An ancient, magically suffused forest or ruined grove contains a tree that was awakened decades ago by a now-dead hermit. It fiercely protects its woodland demesne from outside threats and loggers. It remembers exactly who killed its creator and is willing to trade safe passage for vengeance.

### Awakened Shrub

_Small Plant, Unaligned_

An awakened shrub is an ordinary shrub given sentience and mobility by the _awaken_ spell or similar magic. It is incredibly fragile but practically invisible when standing still.

- **Armor Class:** 9
- **Hit Points:** 10 (3d6)
- **Speed:** 20 ft.
- **STR:** 3 (-4) | **DEX:** 8 (-1) | **CON:** 11 (+0) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 6 (-2)
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire
- **Damage Resistances:** Piercing
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common plus one other language
- **Challenge:** 0 (10 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **False Appearance:** While the shrub remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a normal shrub.

**Actions**

- **Rake:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +1 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 1 slashing damage.

### Awakened Tree

_Huge Plant, Neutral_

An awakened tree is an ordinary tree given sentience and mobility. It is slow and highly vulnerable to fire, but its immense size makes it a highly effective ambush predator or barricade, dealing massive bludgeoning damage to anything that gets too close.

- **Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 59 (7d12 + 14)
- **Speed:** 20 ft.
- **STR:** 19 (+4) | **DEX:** 6 (-2) | **CON:** 15 (+2) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 7 (-2)
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire
- **Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, Piercing
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common plus one other language
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Actions**

- **Slam:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 14 (3d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

### d6 Awakened Plant Clues & Demands (Social Interaction)

If the players figure out a plant is awakened, it might be willing to share its secrets. However, awakened plants have bizarre priorities based on their stationary origins. _(Roll or choose one when the PCs attempt to interrogate an awakened plant)._

|d6|The Demand or Observation|
|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Water Tribute:** The plant is parched. It refuses to speak until the PCs provide it with clean, purified water (or cast _Create or Destroy Water_), complaining about the bitter taste of local rain.|
|**2**|**The Rooted Perspective:** The plant cannot recognize faces well, but it perfectly recognizes footfalls, the weight of a creature's tread, or the specific smell of the mud on an assassin's boots.|
|**3**|**The Pruning Request:** An Awakened Shrub will trade the password to a smuggler's den if the PCs carefully prune away a rotting, diseased branch that it cannot reach itself.|
|**4**|**The Time Dilation:** Because trees experience time differently, the Awakened Tree answers questions accurately but struggles to specify if an event happened "yesterday" or "three years ago."|
|**5**|**The Missing Master:** The 30-day _charmed_ condition has just ended. The plant's creator promised to return but hasn't. The plant is lonely and will trade its creator's secrets to the PCs if they agree to escort it to a safer grove.|
|**6**|**The Elemental Grudge:** The plant demands the PCs destroy a local Fire Elemental or a careless wizard who recently scorched its leaves before it will reveal the location of a buried treasure cache.|
