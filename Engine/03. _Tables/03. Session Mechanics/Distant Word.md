#distant-word
> **PROVISIONAL — Adam spot-check pending.** Rolled on tavern/crier contact, downtime, or session
> start (`[[WORLD-TURN]]`'s rumor arm; `[[TABLE-GAPS-070126]]` §2) — drift stops being lazy-local
> and the world becomes audible from a barstool. **Kind = Distortion Lens** (~60% of rows, salience-
> weighted to a real ledger `clock`/`outcome`/`drift` entry from a NON-current node/faction; the
> engine binds the actual fact at roll time and the DM alone holds it — the row below only supplies
> HOW the telling garbles it: exaggerated, misattributed, inverted, or delayed). **Kind = Far
> Color** (~40%, pure unverifiable atmosphere, no bound fact). The lens keeps rumors anti-drift: the
> FACT is engine-owned canon, only the distortion is flavor. Spice-graded d100, Commitment ceiling
> (66 Grounded · 20 Textured · 9 Strange · 4 Volatile · 1 Mythic). Five band-anchor rows (marked ★)
> are Adam's approved samples from `TABLE-GAPS-070126.md` §6, included verbatim; the rest extend
> their register.
