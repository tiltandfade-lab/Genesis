### II. The Adventure Framework (Macro Level)

**Premise & Hook** The PCs are navigating a fetid bog when they stumble into a surprisingly bright, breezy, and bountiful clearing shaped like a figure-eight. To cross the deep, rushing mud that surrounds this oasis, they must pass through an ancient, dilapidated 5x5 stone sluice gate. However, this seemingly safe passage is a cunning trap cultivated by a Green Hag who uses a cursed coin and a petrified frog to lure greedy travelers into a crushing stone prison.

**What Happens if the PCs Ignore It** If the heroes find a magical way around the clearing or retreat, they leave the hag's trap active. Another wandering traveler or innocent local will inevitably be crushed and consumed by the hag.

**The Factions & Cast**

- **Matilda (Green Hag):** A wicked swamp fey who cultivates the area with insect-repelling pheromones to give travelers a false sense of security. She watches from the murky waters, waiting for the trap to spring.
- **The Sluice Prisoners:** Previous victims or transformed enemies of the hag, currently serving as petrified props or bait for her traps.

**Dynamic Pacing: "The Events"**

- **The Pheromone Shift:** If the PCs spend more than 10 minutes investigating without making a move, the wind shifts. The chemical pheromone scent fades, and a hostile _Swarm of Insects_ descends upon the party, forcing them into the sluice gate for cover.

### III. The Encounter Nodes (Micro Level)

### Node 1: The Sluice Gate Chokepoint

**The Set Up** _[Encounter Map Placeholder: A 60' x 90' figure-eight path of solid, mossy ground. Everything outside the figure-eight is deep, untraversable rushing mud. The center chokepoint connecting the two loops is a 5x5 stone sluice gate ruin. A 20' diameter algae-choked pond sits in the lower loop.]_

![[Pasted image 20260312112921.png|488]]

#### **The Run Surface (Segments)**

- **Adventure Promise:** An _Indiana Jones_-style weight-replacement puzzle set in a deceptive wilderness oasis, where greed leads to a claustrophobic trap.
- **Pressure Level:** Medium (The environment is pleasant, but the trap triggers an immediate life-or-death crisis, followed by an ambush).
- **Rewards & Consequences:** Overcoming the trap yields the "gold coin" and potentially a rescued ally. Failure leaves a PC trapped and battered while the hag attacks the separated party.
- **Basic Narrative:** The party arrives at the center of the figure-eight. The only way across the rushing mud is through the 5x5 stone frame of the sluice gate. Inside the frame sits a massive gold coin on a wooden pressure-stump, and hanging beside it is a wooden birdcage containing a petrified frog.

#### **Define Physical Space**

- **The Space:** A 60'x90' figure-eight. The terrain outside the figure-eight is entirely untraversable hazardous mud and deep rushing water.
- **The Footing:** The outer perimeter of the figure-eight is coated in Slick Algae (Entering requires a DC 10 Dexterity/Acrobatics check or fall Prone).
- **The Atmosphere:** The sky is bright and harmless gusts of wind kick up loose dirt. The air smells strongly of chemical pheromones, which repels normal insects and allows edible, nutrient-dense fungi to grow bountifully (No foraging penalties).
- **The Feature:** The 5x5 stone sluice gate housing in the center of the path. A heavily obscured, algae-choked pond (20' diameter) sits in the southern loop.

#### **Starting NPCs**

1. **Matilda (Green Hag):** Submerged in the algae-choked pond, using her _Invisible Passage_ to watch the PCs approach the sluice gate.

#### **Segment 1: The Counterweight Puzzle**

- **Narrative Flow:** The PCs must navigate the 5x5 sluice gate. Taking the coin without replacing it with an object of equal weight (the petrified frog in the birdcage) triggers the crushing walls. If the trap is sprung, the hag attacks the remaining party members outside the gate while one PC is trapped inside.

- **Read Aloud:** 
  >"The oppressive swamp gives way to a breezy, sunlit figure-eight of solid ground, completely surrounded by an impassable torrent of deep, rushing mud. In the center of the path stands the ruined, 10-foot-tall stone frame of an ancient sluice gate. Resting conspicuously on a stump inside the 5-foot-wide gate is a massive gold coin. Dangling from a rusted hook right next to it is a wooden birdcage containing a heavy, petrified frog."
  
  
- **Skill Checks & Revelations:**
    - **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation):** Inspecting the stump reveals it is a pressure plate tied to the stone walls of the sluice housing. It requires exact counterbalancing to avoid triggering.
    - **DC 15 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand):** A PC attempting to quickly swap the petrified frog for the coin must succeed on this check. On a failure, the weight difference is detected, and the trap springs.

- **Escalation & Pressure Keys:** If the trap springs, the heavy stone plates (5'x2') slam shut. A PC inside must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw to drop beneath the 2-foot plates. Regardless of the save, the exit and entrance are completely sealed off by the mechanism, trapping them inside the 5x5 space until released from the outside.

### IV. Encounter Mechanics & Appendices

#### **Difficulty Dial: Scaling by Tier**

**Tier 1: Local Heroes (Levels 1–4)**

- _The Base Encounter:_ This tier utilizes the encounter as written in the primary document.
- **The Controller:** Matilda uses a standard **Green Hag** stat block (CR 3).
- **The Pressure:** The crushing wall trap deals 4d10 bludgeoning damage on a failed save.

**Tier 2: Heroes of the Realm (Levels 5–10)**

- **The Controller:** Upgrade Matilda to a **Green Hag** in a Coven (CR 5), granting her access to coven spells like _Phantasmal Killer_ and _Polymorph_. She is joined by 2 **Giant Crocodiles** (CR 5) hiding in the mud.
- **Tactical Shift:** The hag uses _Polymorph_ on a PC outside the trap, turning them into a helpless swamp creature, while the crocodiles grapple and drag the others into the untraversable mud.
- **The Pressure:** The algae on the perimeter now acts as _Grease_ (DC 14), and the crushing wall trap deals 8d10 bludgeoning damage.

**Tier 3: Masters of the Realm (Levels 11–16)**

- **The Controller:** Upgrade Matilda to a **Night Hag** in a Coven (CR 7) accompanied by a **Froghemoth** (CR 8).
- **Tactical Shift:** The hag uses _Etherealness_ to remain completely immune to attacks until she strikes. The Froghemoth uses its tentacles to grab PCs and forcefully shove them into the sluice gate, intentionally triggering the trap on them.
- **The Pressure:** The sluice gate is warded with _Antimagic Field_. PCs trapped inside cannot use _Misty Step_ or _Dimension Door_ to escape the housing.

**Tier 4: Masters of the World (Levels 17–20)**

- **The Controller:** The Hag is an **Archfey**, accompanied by a **Black Dragon** (Ancient, CR 21) slumbering beneath the mud.
- **Tactical Shift:** The dragon awakens, creating lair actions that cause the untraversable mud to surge over the safe figure-eight paths.
- **The Pressure:** The crushing walls are laced with Prismatic magic. Failing the save against the trap subjects the PC to the effects of a _Prismatic Spray_ (DC 19).

#### **Mechanic: The Sluice Gate Trap (2024 Core Rules)**

1. **The Trigger:** Removing the coin without perfectly counterbalancing the stump (such as using the petrified frog) triggers the trap.
2. **The Effect:** Two 5'x2' stone plates slam into the 5x5 space. Any creature inside must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 22 (4d10) Bludgeoning damage. On a successful save, they drop prone beneath the plates and take no damage.
3. **The Prison:** Regardless of the save, the stone plates seal the 5x5 sluice housing. The creature is trapped.
4. **The Release:** A creature outside the trap can climb the 10-foot stone housing and pull the rusted emergency release lever as an Action. Alternatively, a trapped creature can attempt a DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) or DC 18 Strength (Athletics) check to force the rusted gears open from the inside.
