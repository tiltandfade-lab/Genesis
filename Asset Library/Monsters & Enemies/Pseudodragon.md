### 2. The Pseudodragon

*p. 249 MM*

Pseudodragons are quiet, elusive, and defensive. Because mages often seek them out as familiars, finding a lone pseudodragon in a gritty frontier town is an immediate clue that something has gone wrong. They are highly selective and abhor manipulative or cruel masters, abandoning them without warning.

**Campaign Integration Hooks:**

- **The Orphaned Familiar:** A pseudodragon is found hiding in the rafters of an abandoned library or an entertainment district theater. It is the surviving familiar of a local mage or prominent noble who recently became a "missing person."
- **The Selective Scout:** The players can attempt to win over a wild pseudodragon with gifts of food or treasure. It can serve as a telepathic scout to safely observe cultist activity in the cramped, dangerous slums. It vocalizes using animal noises: a purr for pleasure, a hiss for unpleasant surprise, and a growl for anger.

#### Pseudodragon

_Tiny Dragon, Neutral Good_

- **Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 10 (3d4 + 3)
- **Speed:** 15 ft., fly 60 ft.
- **STR:** 6 (-2) | **DEX:** 15 (+2) | **CON:** 13 (+1) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 10 (+0)
- **Skills:** Perception +3, Stealth +4
- **Senses:** Blindsight 10 ft., darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** Understands Common and Draconic but can't speak
- **Challenge:** 1/4 (50 XP)

**Traits**

- **Keen Senses:** The pseudodragon has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight, hearing, or smell.
- **Magic Resistance:** The pseudodragon has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
- **Limited Telepathy:** The pseudodragon can magically communicate simple ideas, emotions, and images telepathically with any creature within 100 feet of it that can understand a language.
- **Variant - Familiar:** If the pseudodragon willingly serves a spellcaster as a familiar, they form a telepathic bond. The companion can sense what the pseudodragon senses within 1 mile. _Crucially, while the pseudodragon is within 10 feet of its companion, the companion shares the pseudodragon's Magic Resistance trait._ The pseudodragon can end this service at any time.

**Actions**

- **Bite:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.
- **Sting:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or become poisoned for 1 hour. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the target falls unconscious for the same duration, or until it takes damage or another creature uses an action to shake it awake.
