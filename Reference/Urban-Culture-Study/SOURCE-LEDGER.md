# SOURCE LEDGER — Urban Culture Study

**Binding statement.** Every text listed here is **reference evidence only**. Nothing here is, or
may become, a Genesis production asset. No text has been copied into the repo; the lane notes quote
short passages for evidential purposes and attribute every one. **Before any passage longer than a
quotation is reproduced, embedded, or redistributed, its exact licence status must be reviewed for
that use.** The safe Genesis production path remains original prose and original procedural content
(`ATTRIBUTION.md`; the GOLDEN-SITES-CATALOG import-audit precedent).

**This is a ledger of TEXTS, not images.** Per the brief, images were optional and secondary, and
**no image was retrieved, viewed, or stored for this study.** Nothing in the lane notes rests on
visual evidence. This is a deliberate difference from the sibling Camp / Lair / Monastery studies
and it should be understood as a limitation: **this study has read about pre-industrial urban life
and has not looked at it.**

**Provenance and method.** Fifteen Project Gutenberg plain-text files were downloaded on 2026-07-24
to a session scratchpad **outside the repo** (they are not retained here) and searched with a
regex/context extractor; passages were then read in place. Encyclopedic and reference material was
retrieved by targeted web fetch on the same date. Every quotation in the lane notes was read by me
in the source text or in the fetched article — **with the explicit exceptions listed in §3 below,
which are quotations at second hand and are flagged in place.**

**Reading-depth key.**
`DEEP` = read extensively, multiple passages, load-bearing across several lanes ·
`WORKED` = specific passages located and read, contributing sourced facts ·
`TOUCHED` = one or two passages used ·
`HELD` = obtained and **not** read; evidences nothing.
