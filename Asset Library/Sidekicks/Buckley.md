## Bogwarden Bullywug (Warrior Sidekick 1)
*Small humanoid (bullywug), neutral (or neutral evil, if you want the swamp edge)*

**Armor Class** 15 (hide armor, shield)  
**Hit Points** 11 (2d8 + 2)  
**Speed** 20 ft., swim 40 ft.

STR 12 (+1) | DEX 12 (+1) | CON 13 (+1) | INT 7 (-2) | WIS 10 (+0) | CHA 7 (-2)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

**Saving Throws** Con +3 *(Warrior bonus proficiency — pick Str/Dex/Con; Con is the “survive” choice)*  
**Skills** Stealth +3; Athletics +3; Perception +2 *(Warrior gives 2 skills; Stealth is from base bullywug)*  
**Senses** passive Perception 12  
**Languages** Bullywug (and optionally a few rough words of Common, if you want easier table talk)  
**Challenge** — (Sidekick)

---

### Traits (Bullywug — kept RAW)
**Amphibious.** The bullywug can breathe air and water.  
**Speak with Frogs and Toads.** The bullywug can communicate simple concepts to frogs and toads when it speaks in Bullywug.  
**Swamp Camouflage.** The bullywug has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in swampy terrain.  
**Standing Leap.** The bullywug’s long jump is up to 20 ft. and its high jump is up to 10 ft., with or without a running start.

---

### Martial Role (Warrior Sidekick — Defender)
**Defender.** When a creature the bullywug can see makes an attack roll against a target **other than** the bullywug that is within **5 feet** of the bullywug, the bullywug can use its **reaction** to impose **disadvantage** on the attack roll.

*(This is the “keep the PC alive” button.)*

---

## Actions (Bullywug — kept RAW)
**Multiattack.** The bullywug makes two melee attacks: one with its **bite** and one with its **spear**.

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
*Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) piercing damage.

**Spear.** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target.  
*Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage, or 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage if used with two hands to make a melee attack.
