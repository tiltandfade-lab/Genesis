#mythic-failure-lenses
> **The shape of a disaster that spiked.** Rolled on the *failure* side of the Critical-Magnitude system (a natural 1). The magnitude die runs **inverted** here — lower is worse: 7–10 → one lens (the cost spreads locally); 2–6 → two to three (a spreading catastrophe); 1/1 → the full cascade, permanent and dark, written to the World State Ledger as canon. Each row is a **lens** — a *kind* of permanent cost — not a finished outcome. **Register switch (CRIT-MAGNITUDE §2):** low stakes → memorable comedy; high stakes → as dark and permanent as the fiction allows. The lens sets *what* breaks; the DM reads the stakes for *how hard*. When multiple fire, **draw distinct rows** (reroll duplicates) and **weave them into one event**. See `docs/CRIT-MAGNITUDE.md`. *(Mirror: `[[Mythic Success Lenses]]`. The "place is scarred" lens hands off to the Myth suite — esp. `[[Myth Becomes Geography]]`.)*

| d12 | Lens | The DM fills it as… |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **A place is scarred** | The ground is wronged for good — hand off to the Myth suite (esp. Geography). The hell-wound case. |
| 2 | **A person is broken or corrupted** | A character is ruined at the root — an ally turned enemy, a sane mind cracked, a good NPC pulled toward something dark. |
| 3 | **Something wrong enters the world** | A thing that shouldn't exist now does and persists — a monster born, a plague seeded, a cult coalesces around the moment. |
| 4 | **A door opens that should be shut** | A barrier fails — a planar breach, a sealed thing freed, a bargain that lets something *through*. |
| 5 | **A local law bends wrong** | A rule of nature curdles here, permanently — ground that won't hold the dead, a wood where sound dies, water that remembers. |
| 6 | **A name becomes a curse** | The deed brands the doer — their name here means disaster, draws blame, or summons what hunts them. |
| 7 | **Something is lost beyond recovery** | The failure takes something that does not come back — a sense, a memory, a person erased from more than life. |
| 8 | **A debt comes due** | A cost attaches to the doer or their line — a curse, an owed soul, a pursuer that will never stop, a bill the bloodline inherits. |
| 9 | **A truth that should have stayed buried gets out** | A protective ignorance breaks — knowledge that maddens, a sleeping thing's name spoken, a seal of forgetting undone. |
| 10 | **Fate turns against the doer** | The world quietly marks them ill — an omen of ruin, luck souring around their cause, powers taking offended notice. |
| 11 | **A threat is unleashed or amplified** | The danger gets worse, not better — the thing they fought ascends, escapes, or draws the attention of something far larger. |
| 12 | **The doer is diminished** | The cost lands on the character — a permanent wound, a stripped power, a brand others can see and shun. |
