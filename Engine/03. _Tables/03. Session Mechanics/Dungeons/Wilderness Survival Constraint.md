|d20|Constraint Type|Mechanical Impact & Flavor|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**Bountiful (Small Game & Rock Springs)**|**No Penalties.** Plentiful small game (rabbits, marmots, or local equivalents) dart among bushes laden with tart, edible berries. Clear, cold spring water pools in natural rock indentations. Standard DC 10 Wisdom (Survival) for foraging/navigating.|
|**2**|**Bountiful (Herd Tracks & Riverways)**|**No Penalties.** Fresh tracks of a large grazing herd (deer, antelope, or giant seahorses) crisscross the area. A steady, easily accessible river or stream flows nearby. Standard DC 10 Wisdom (Survival) for foraging/navigating.|
|**3**|**Bountiful (Nesting Fauna & Rain Basins)**|**No Penalties.** Abundant nesting birds or tree-dwelling mammals fill the canopy/crags. Broad-leafed plants or natural stone basins have caught plenty of fresh, clean rainwater. Standard DC 10 Wisdom (Survival) for foraging/navigating.|
|**4**|**Bountiful (Aquatic Life & Deep Ponds)**|**No Penalties.** A thriving ecosystem of fish or edible amphibians swims in a deep, pristine natural pond. The banks are rich with edible, starchy roots. Standard DC 10 Wisdom (Survival) for foraging/navigating.|
|**5**|**Bountiful (Nut Yields & Heavy Dew)**|**No Penalties.** The ground is littered with edible nuts, seeds, or hardy pods. Heavy morning dew or saturated moss makes water collection a trivial task. Standard DC 10 Wisdom (Survival) for foraging/navigating.|
|**6**|**Bountiful (Large Grazers & Waterfalls)**|**No Penalties.** Sightings of slow-moving, large grazing beasts (elk, oxen, or manatees). A rushing waterfall or set of rapids provides highly oxygenated, pure water. Standard DC 10 Wisdom (Survival) for foraging/navigating.|
|**7**|**Bountiful (Fungi & Porous Seeps)**|**No Penalties.** Edible, nutrient-dense fungi, lichens, or mosses cover the shaded terrain. Water seeps cleanly through porous rock walls into perfectly drinkable pools. Standard DC 10 Wisdom (Survival) for foraging/navigating.|
|**8**|**Bountiful (Migratory Flocks & Meltwater)**|**No Penalties.** A seasonal migration of plump waterfowl or small herd beasts is resting in this segment. Clean snowmelt or natural condensation provides ample hydration. Standard DC 10 Wisdom (Survival) for foraging/navigating.|
|**9**|**Bountiful (Grubs & Flora Sap)**|**No Penalties.** High-protein grubs, large beetles, or edible locusts abound beneath loose rocks. Sweet, hydrating sap can be safely tapped from local flora or cacti. Standard DC 10 Wisdom (Survival) for foraging/navigating.|
|**10**|**Bountiful (Shellfish & Sand Filters)**|**No Penalties.** Slow-moving waterways or mudflats teem with freshwater clams, crayfish, or snails. Water here is easily purified or naturally filtered through fine sand. Standard DC 10 Wisdom (Survival) for foraging/navigating.|
|**11**|**Bountiful (Wild Shoots & Bubbling Springs)**|**No Penalties.** Sweet, edible blossoms, bamboo-like shoots, or wild grains grow thick here. Small, bubbling ground springs provide a limitless supply of fresh water. Standard DC 10 Wisdom (Survival) for foraging/navigating.|
|**12**|**Bountiful (Burrowers & Catchments)**|**No Penalties.** Plentiful burrowing mammals or large, meaty lizards dart between holes. Deep rocky catchments shaded from the sun hold gallons of perfectly still, clean water. Standard DC 10 Wisdom (Survival) for foraging/navigating.|
|**13**|**Disorienting Terrain**|**Navigation Hindrance.** The geography is twisting, visually repetitive, or magnetically anomalous. Wisdom (Survival) checks to navigate or map the area are made with **Disadvantage**.|
|**14**|**Barren / Blighted**|**Foraging Scarcity.** The area has been stripped of life, over-hunted by monsters, or struck by disease. The DC to forage for food and water increases to **20**.|
|**15**|**Contaminated Supply**|**Toxic Environment.** Water sources are stagnant, brackish, or tainted; local flora is mildly toxic. Anyone relying on foraged food/water here must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be **Poisoned** for 24 hours.|
|**16**|**Exhausting Footing**|**Pacing Hindrance.** Deep snow, sucking mud, shifting sand, or dense undergrowth. The entire segment is **Difficult Terrain**. Traveling at a normal pace requires a DC 10 Constitution saving throw to avoid 1 level of **Exhaustion**.|
|**17**|**Extreme Temperature**|**Exposure Threat.** A brutal heatwave or bitter cold snap grips the area. Characters without appropriate gear or magical protection must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw at the end of the segment or gain 1 level of **Exhaustion**.|
|**18**|**Predator Territory**|**Resting Hindrance.** The area is actively prowled by highly aggressive natural fauna. Attempting to take a Short or Long Rest here requires a DC 15 Wisdom (Survival) check to camouflage the camp; on a failure, a combat encounter occurs during the rest.|
|**19**|**Thin Air / Choking Vapors**|**Severe Exposure.** High altitude, volcanic venting, or thick swamp miasma makes breathing incredibly taxing. All Strength and Dexterity ability checks are made with **Disadvantage**.|
|**20**|**Supernatural Hazard**|**Lethal Environment.** The area is infused with negative energy, planar bleeding, or magical radiation. Characters cannot regain Hit Points from spending Hit Dice during Short Rests, and foraging yields only inedible, foul-tasting ash.|
