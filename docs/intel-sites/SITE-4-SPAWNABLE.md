## 7. Honest summary

Of roughly 35 concrete spawnables the working spec implies, **17 have a LIVE roller** — 6
zone/scene-frame rows (§1), 2 bundled mechanism facts (§2), 4 role rows across realm skins (§3),
and the 11-row context/adjacent-world set (§4) — but **every LIVE zone/scene-frame row in §1 is
`AUTHORED-UNWIRED` against this specific working spec**: none of them has ever been cross-
referenced against the working spec's zones, generation order, or M1–M11 rulings before this
pass (`Reference/Monastery-Study-0727/MEASURED-REFERENCES.md` §5 is the first such
cross-reference, done this pass, for the Monastery Cloister and Gate House rows specifically).
No monastic *rank* (abbot, monk, novice, cult leader) has a dedicated role row anywhere in the
Engine tables searched — every occupant hit is a realm-skinned general role (Rite-keeper,
Secret-scholar, Recluse) standing in for one. All seven operating circuits, the arcade bay/
corner piece, the processional head platform, the institution-circulation mutation contract, and
every named hook are `NO-TABLE-YET`. This matches the working spec's own generator contract,
which describes a `TARGET-ADAPTER` translating world/place/building facts into the monastery's
communal-routine grammar; that adapter has not been built. This inventory changes no status and
authorizes no build — it is the feed for a future spawn-audit and wiring pass.
