## 30-Second Run

Charlatan Elias uses magic chalk on a Civic District street, expecting a living mural. Instead the cobblestones dissolve into a glowing necrotic pit — the chalk reacted with a cult ward. Below: a sewer aqueduct, a wagon of corpses, a draft horse sinking into mud, and five cult thugs arguing over a dropped casket. Hidden in the wagon, invisible Vesper (Sapphire Circle spy) cast *Faerie Fire* on initiative 10 — the thugs glow blue. Elias is running. The City Watch arrives in 1d4+2 rounds.

---

## Setup

**Space — Surface:** Brightly lit Civic District plaza. The dissolved square is 10×10 ft. A dry ornate fountain sits 15 ft away with a misaligned stone. Disadvantage on Stealth checks in bright daylight.
**Space — Sewers:** 20-ft wide × 60-ft long straight aqueduct, 15-ft ceiling. Hole from street at north end. Wagon in center. Southern 20 ft = Sinking Mud.
**Atmosphere:** The hole glows necrotic green at the edges. Below: sewage, stagnant water, the frantic thrashing of a horse.
**NPCs Present:** Elias (fleeing, chalk in hand), Pampered Mastiff (barking at hole), 4 Cult Bandits + 1 Cult Thug (below, arguing), Invisible Vesper (in the wagon), Draft Horse (trapped in sinking mud).
**If Ignored:** Elias escapes. Guards cover the hole. Thugs secure the casket, silence the horse, execute Vesper.

The performer stares at what he's done — and sprints.*

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Surface

Three things compete immediately: the fleeing charlatan, the dead drop fountain, and the hole.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 13 Acrobatics or Athletics (×3 successes):** Chase Elias through the panicked crowd. If caught, he hands over the chalk immediately. He bought it from a trickster wizard — he had no idea it would do this.
- **DC 14 Investigation (fountain):** Misaligned stone in the base. Behind it: a sealed tube containing 50 gp and a signet ring — a corrupt magistrate's dead drop, paying the cult for the bodies.
- **DC 15 Arcana (hole edge):** The chalk reacted with a pre-existing cult ward. The glowing perimeter is a Cult Sigil — 1d6 necrotic to anyone passing through without a cult medallion.
- **Watch Timer:** The Regional Guard arrives in 1d4+2 rounds. Anyone visible at the hole is detained.

**Escalation:** The drop requires DC 10 Acrobatics. Fail: 1d6 bludgeoning + land Prone in the algae below.

---

### Segment 2: The Illuminated Ambush

The party drops into the aqueduct. The horse's thrashing masks their arrival. On initiative count 10, Vesper (invisible, in the wagon) casts *Faerie Fire* — the five thugs glow blue.

**Read Aloud (sewer):**
> *"The algae-slicked tunnel stinks of sewage and stagnant water. The corpse wagon is bogged in thick muck. Twenty feet ahead, a massive draft horse is sinking in a quagmire, screaming. Five armed thugs stand at the wagon's rear, shoving each other over an ornate tin casket that's spilled into the mud.*
>
> *Then: an arcane syllable from inside the wagon. A burst of blue light. The five thugs are outlined in glowing magical radiance."*

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 13 Arcana:** Recognize *Faerie Fire* — glowing targets grant Advantage on attacks.
- **DC 14 Animal Handling:** Calm and guide the draft horse out of the sinking mud.
- **DC 12 Medicine:** Quick check of the wagon — victims were killed by ritualistic bleeding.

**Vesper (if PCs hesitate):** *"I painted them for you! Put them down before they call the rest of the cult!"*

**Escalation:** Thugs use the wagon for cover and attempt to shove charging PCs into the Sinking Mud.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Cult Sigil (hole perimeter):** 1d6 necrotic to cross without cult medallion. *Dispel Magic* (DC 13) removes it.
2. **Sinking Mud (southern 20 ft):** Entering: Speed = 0. DC 13 Athletics to pull free (action required). On fail: restrained, sinks 1d4 ft per round.
3. **Faerie Fire:** Activates initiative count 10, round 1. All five thugs outlined — Advantage on all attacks against them until end of their next turn (or until they use an action to extinguish it with a DC 14 Dex save).
4. **Draft Horse:** If not calmed, thrashes — any creature within 5 ft takes 2d4+2 bludgeoning (DC 13 Dex save for half) on the horse's turn.
5. **Watch Arrival:** If the party is still on the surface when the Watch arrives, they are detained for questioning — this costs them time and the sewer scene.

## Difficulty Dial

**Level 1:** 2 Bandits + 1 Thug below. Sigil deals 1d4 necrotic.
**Levels 2–4:** As written.
**Levels 4–5:** The Thug is a Veteran. 1d4 Giant Rats emerge from the mud on round 2. Elias uses a *Potion of Invisibility* to flee.

## NPC Dossiers

**Elias the Charlatan**
Goal: Fake artistic talent for glory. Fleece the wealthy.
Leverage: Deeply cowardly. Hands over the chalk immediately if cornered.
Tell: Speaks very fast. Constantly wipes chalk dust on his trousers.

**Vesper (Sapphire Circle Spy)**
Goal: Map the cult's lair, expose the corrupt nobles funding it.
Leverage: Pragmatic. Offers safe haven at a faction safehouse for an escort out.
Tell: Brushes dirt off tattered dark blue robes. Clipped, educated accent.
