# Genesis — Re-Authoring Rubric

*The shared yardstick for the whole-corpus pass. Two axes: a **quality floor** (every row up to par and
pointed at an intriguing outcome) and an **explosive ceiling** (genuinely wild high-band rows with real
teeth). Benched on the corpus's own best rows. Use it per table: read against this, agree a strategy,
rewrite, approve.*

> **The corpus reality this pass addresses (from `CORPUS-INTENSITY-MAP.md`):** of 238 content tables,
> only ~27 are graded-and-at-the-bar; **~210 have no band column at all** — their spice is unrecorded.
> So most of the work is twofold per table: **(1) grade the bands honestly, (2) author the explosive tail**
> the class permits. A handful are copy-paste-inflated (DUPED) and want a structural die-collapse first.

---

## Axis 1 — THE FLOOR: every row must be up to par

A row is **up to par** when it is a *situation with a hook*, not a label with a gloss. The test: **could
the DM read this row and immediately have something to DO with it?** If the row only names a category, it
fails.

- **Up to par** (Plot Item #1): *"A small iron key, blackened, the bow worn smooth. — Someone hid it
  rather than carry it — that choice is the whole story. — A strongbox three streets over that its owner
  swears is empty."* → a concrete object + an implication + a thread to pull.
- **Not up to par** (NPC Fear #1): *"Authority — Being punished or noticed."* → a label and a definition.
  No image, no scene, nothing to do. This is the failure mode the floor pass fixes.

**Floor checklist (every row):**
1. **A graspable image, not an abstraction.** A blackened iron key, not "a valuable item." The body,
   the object, the gesture — concrete and singular.
2. **Sensory-first where `voice_critical`** (it feeds the player Fragment). Lead with
   smell/sound/texture/light/the body before sight-and-exposition; survive being read as a 6–10-word line.
3. **It points somewhere.** A quiet hook even at Grounded — *"an old woman who was plainly expecting
   someone else."* The row implies a next beat.
4. **Range across the table.** Some warm, some cold, some grim, some absurd — so the DM can match any
   player energy. Don't write every row the same temperature.
5. **No filler tells.** Kill "DM discretion," "Effect unknown," keyword stubs, and copy-paste
   "minor variation" strings. If a table is mostly these, it's a DUPED structural fix, not a polish.

---

## Axis 2 — THE CEILING: let it get explosive (and make the explosion real)

The 5 bands are a **craft ladder**, not just rarity. Each band is a *kind of pressure on reality*. Author
each band to its true register; never let a high band be just a slightly-stranger Grounded row (the
flat-spice flaw). **Reach the ceiling your class permits — and not past it:** Spark ≤ Textured · Fork ≤
Strange · Commitment may place Volatile/Mythic.

| Band | What it does to the world | Exemplar row |
|---|---|---|
| **Grounded** | Mundane — but carries a quiet hook. | *"The only safe harbor on a brutal stretch of coast."* / *"A stamped customs receipt with the goods column left blank."* |
| **Textured** | The world tilts; a wrongness you can name. | *"The Impossible Heir: a child raised to repeat a deed that depended on circumstances that will never come again, and the whole town waits to see them try."* |
| **Strange** | Uncanny, still explicable; reality TILTS. | *"Every map agrees the town is here; the town disagrees about its own century, and all the records check out."* / *"The Fey Census — they count the town by criteria no human census uses; nobody has found out what the count is for."* |
| **Volatile** | Reality STRAINS; consequences escalate, locally. | *"Settled to contain a thing the founders couldn't kill; the town is the lid, and the lid is loosening."* / *"The Completing Legend: the myth has begun arranging events toward its own ending, using the living as its material."* |
| **Mythic** | Reality BREAKS; world-marking, written to the Ledger as canon. | *"The Retroactive Author: the myth grows true enough to edit its own past — the world quietly rearranges so it was always so, leaving a handful of people with headaches and memories that no longer match a single stone."* |

### What makes a high-band row EXPLODE (not just "intense")

Reverse-engineered from the corpus's best Mythic/Volatile rows. The great ones do **most of these at once**:

1. **A reframe that resets everything you assumed.** The original comes *after* the copy; the town is older
   than its own people; the people are a memory being replayed. *"A brass key, utterly ordinary, except
   every lock it has opened opened first, before the key was made… The First Door."*
2. **Cosmic scale delivered through one concrete, sensory image.** Not "reality unravels" — *"headaches and
   memories that no longer match a single stone in the town."* The bigger the idea, the smaller and more
   graspable the detail that carries it.
3. **It implies a scene, a clock, or an action — it isn't a static fact.** *"the lid is loosening"* ·
   *"opening, slowly, this whole time"* · *"waiting for the town to reach a specific size before
   introducing itself."* The player can push on it.
4. **It honors world-seriousness.** Grim/severe/hilarious *braided*; the joke lives in the texture, the
   stakes stay real (DM-CHARTER §1). Never a wink that breaks the world.
5. **Specific and singular — it has a name/face**, not a generic adjective. *"The Memory Sustains It,"* not
   "something eldritch happens."

**Explosive seed-patterns** (reach for these when a table's tail is flat — they're the engine under the
exemplars, not a checklist to copy literally):
- **The Retroactive** — it was always so; the past quietly edits to match.
- **The Lid** — something was contained here, and the containment is failing *now*.
- **The Replacement** — what you're looking at isn't the thing; it's a copy / memory / reconstruction, and
  it's starting to slip.
- **The Anticipation** — something laid this out in advance and has been *waiting* for a condition to be met.
- **The Concrete-Cosmic** — an utterly ordinary object that is secretly load-bearing for the world.
- **The Recursive Space** — it loops, circles, contains more than it should; the exit is a trick of attention.

### Anti-patterns at the ceiling (these read as fake-explosive)
- A "Strange" row that's just a weirder adjective on a Grounded image (no actual tilt in reality).
- Gore/shock with no reframe — intensity without a hook is noise.
- Cosmic vocabulary ("eldritch," "reality itself," "ancient evil") standing in for a concrete image.
- Tone-break — a Mythic row that turns the world into a joke, or railroads the table's tone.

## Per-table working loop (how we use this)

1. **Map → pick.** Take the next table off `CORPUS-INTENSITY-MAP.md` (FLAT/UNGRADED/DUPED flags + `vc`
   prioritize). Read it against the two axes.
2. **Agree a strategy** (one of): *grade-only* (add honest bands, content fine) · *floor-fix* (rewrite
   bland rows) · *ceiling-author* (add/strengthen the explosive tail) · *die-collapse* (DUPED) ·
   *merge/retire* (true duplicate — see the sweep plan) · *promote class* (the content earns a higher
   ceiling). Most tables = grade + ceiling-author.
3. **I rewrite** to the rubric; archive the original first (`zz_Archive/`), keep the `^block-anchor`/`id`
   exact, add the Band column, state the band split in a header note.
4. **You approve / edit.** Then **compile** (`compile-tables.py --emit`) and move on. Rinse and repeat.

### The band split to aim for (honest static rarity, clamped at the class ceiling)
Anchor on the canonical d100 curve and scale to the die: **~70 Grounded · ~20 Textured · ~9 Strange ·
~1 Volatile · ~1 Mythic** (Commitment). Fork stops at Strange (no Volatile/Mythic rows). Spark stops at
Textured. The explosive rows are **rare by design — and that's exactly why each one must have real teeth.**

## The ★BAR study set (read these for *what makes them great*)
The graded-and-at-ceiling exemplars to benchmark against, by domain:
- **Place Generation:** Place History · Place-Secret (d200) · Building Interior (d300) · Place Mythology ·
  Place Master-Setting · the Place-Traits/Nearby/Relevancy/Ruler/Race-Relations family.
- **Mythic Events:** Myth Costs · Myth-Becomes-Geography.
- **Quests/NPCs (Commitment d300s):** Plot Item · Plot Lock · NPC Bonds · NPC Flaws-Secrets · NPC
  Immediate-Motivation · NPC Job-Board.
- **Pressure:** Urban Pressure (d100) · the Starting-State opening-bundle family.
- **Recently-built anti-drift (the new bar at d20):** Puzzle Type/Mechanism/Solution/Failsafe ·
  Supernatural Charm/Blessing · Creature Parley-Wants · Morale Outcome · Patron Archetype · NPC Opening-Attitude.
