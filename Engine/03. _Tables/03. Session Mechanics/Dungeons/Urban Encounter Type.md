Urban Encounter Type (d20)

| d20 | Encounter Type                           | Narrative Glue / Execution                                                                                                           |
| --- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | **Enemy / Faction Encounter**            | An immediate, hostile clash. Roll initiative or react quickly.                                                                       |
| 2   | **Enemy / Faction Encounter**            | The party spots enemies setting up an ambush or preparing to attack.                                                                 |
| 3   | **Enemy / Faction Encounter**            | A tense standoff or a chance to use stealth against an opposing faction.                                                             |
| 4   | **Hazard / Trap / Threat**               | A sudden environmental danger that demands quick reflexes or a saving throw.                                                         |
| 5   | **Hazard / Trap / Threat**               | A lingering or hidden environmental threat that requires careful navigation to bypass.                                               |
| 6   | **Social / Weird Contact**               | An unexpected NPC interaction offering a rumor or a brief conversation.                                                              |
| 7   | **Social / Weird Contact**               | A strange or eccentric local presents a minor dilemma, warning, or request.                                                          |
| 8   | **Social / Weird Contact**               | The party witnesses a curious vignette or social argument unfolding nearby.                                                          |
| 9   | **Problem / Lock / Obstacle**            | A physical barrier blocking the path, requiring brute force or finesse to clear.                                                     |
| 10  | **Problem / Lock / Obstacle**            | A logistical puzzle or locked passage demanding investigation or thieves' tools.                                                     |
| 11  | **Empty (but not nothing)**              | The area is quiet, but sensory details strongly hint at recent passage or danger.                                                    |
| 12  | **Empty (but not nothing)**              | An eerie silence or strange architectural feature builds immediate tension.                                                          |
| 13  | **Empty (but not nothing)**              | The party finds minor clues, discarded items, or highly specific mundane set dressing.                                               |
| 14  | **Urban Art Encounter**                  | A localized piece of art and its creator or audience. Roll on the 3-part Art Table.                                                  |
| 15  | **Combat Complication (Enemy + Hazard)** | Enemies are taking advantage of a hazard, or both sides are caught in it (e.g., Thugs attacking through a _Smoke Cloud_).            |
| 16  | **Ambush / Toll (Enemy + Problem)**      | Enemies are using an obstacle to trap, slow, or extort the party (e.g., Bandits guarding a _Crowd Bottleneck_).                      |
| 17  | **Bystander in Peril (Social + Hazard)** | An NPC is currently threatened by an active hazard or trap and needs immediate help (e.g., a merchant caught in _Corrosive Runoff_). |
| 18  | **Shared Obstacle (Social + Problem)**   | An NPC is stuck at the exact same obstacle as the party, offering a chance to cooperate, bribe, or compete to get through.           |
| 19  | **Lethal Barrier (Hazard + Problem)**    | An obstacle that is actively dangerous to bypass (e.g., a _Locked Door_ in a hallway filling with _Bad Air_).                        |
| 20  | **Complex Scene**                        | Combine as many elements as you can into an intense encounter!                                                                       |
