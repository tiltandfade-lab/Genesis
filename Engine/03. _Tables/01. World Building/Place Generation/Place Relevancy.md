#place-relevancy
> **Why this place matters** — the settlement's role in the wider region right now: the reason a traveler, a merchant, or the story itself would come here (or be pulled through here), plus who profits by it and who resents it. Rolled when a PC heads toward a new settlement. **Full d100 — 100 unique rows**, spice-graded by row count (66 Grounded / 20 Textured / 9 Strange / 4 Volatile / 1 Mythic), Commitment ceiling.
