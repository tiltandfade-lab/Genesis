# NPC Role Skin — Suburb (sleep-stalker 80s cul-de-sac)

> Realm skin over [[npc-role-spine]] ([[NPC-ROLE-REALMS]] approach C). Soul/note/class inherit from
> the spine — this skin only relabels. Blank Weight = spine default, `0` = drop. Kin-agnostic /
> recontextualize per convention. Lever/atom — exempt from the situation family (do NOT tag
> `table_family`). Suburbia specializes rather than sprawls: it **drops Wild-provider outright** — a
> tract development has no true wild economy, only the treeline behind the last lot, which is a
> threat here, not a livelihood. It **reweights hard toward the genre's real demography** — orderly
> authority (patrol cops, crossing guards), domestic service in the nicer houses (housekeepers), the
> real-estate churn that built the place (realtors, the subdivision developer), and above all the
> **signature wrongness**: the new family nobody's vetted, the neighbor whose devotion runs a little
> too deep, the shut-in at the end of the cul-de-sac. The lawns stay cut and the wrongness keeps to
> the curfew.
