Bugbears are the apex predators of the goblinoid host. In a high-stakes frontier campaign, they should not be run as mere oversized foot soldiers; they are boogeymen and slasher-villains. Despite their hulking, bear-like appearance and shambling gait, they move through the shadows with the terrifying, absolute silence of a stalking cat.

They are incredibly lazy, preferring to bully and enslave smaller goblins into doing their daily chores, rousing themselves only for the thrill of a hunt or when bribed with the promise of bloodshed and treasure.

### Campaign Integration: Investigation & Clues

Bugbear activity in an area leaves a specific, macabre signature. They do not fight fair, and they view terror as a weapon.

- **The Decapitation Signature:** Bugbears frequently decapitate their victims to honor their patron deity, Hruggek. Local folklore suggests that if a bugbear takes your head, your soul remains trapped inside it, allowing the bugbear to use the head to magically command your grieving allies. _(DM Note: Whether this is literal magic or just psychological warfare is up to your setting's lore, but finding a frontier camp where all the bodies are missing their heads is a confirmed bugbear clue)._
- **The Bait Tactic:** Bugbear warriors are surprisingly cunning; they will deliberately send out terrified, lesser goblin slaves to act as bait, leading overeager adventurers or guards directly into a lethal ambush.
- **The Silent Approach:** A confirmed fact of bugbear physiology is that their tough, leathery feet and dense fur allow them to achieve near-perfect stealth. If a heavily fortified outpost goes completely dark without a single alarm bell or sign of forced entry, a bugbear strike force is the likely culprit.

### 1. Bugbear Warrior

Bugbear warriors serve as the champions and heavy muscle for goblinoid warbands. They wield oversized weapons, relying on their massive upper-body strength and sheer brutality to crush enemies before they can react.

_Medium Humanoid (Goblinoid), Chaotic Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 16 (Hide Armor, Shield)
- **Hit Points:** 45 (6d8 + 18)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 17 (+3) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 8 (-1) | **WIS:** 11 (+0) | **CHA:** 9 (-1)
- **Skills:** Intimidate +3, Stealth +6, Survival +2
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common, Goblin
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Ambusher:** In the first round of a combat, the bugbear has Advantage on attack rolls against any creature it has surprised.
- **Surprise Attack:** If the bugbear hits a creature with an attack roll and has Advantage on the roll (such as from being Hidden), the attack deals an extra 7 (2d6) damage.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The bugbear makes two melee attacks.
- **Oversized Morningstar:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 12 (2d8 + 3) piercing damage. _(Note: Bugbears wield weapons one size category larger without penalty)._
- **Skullthumper (Recharge 5-6):** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 12 (2d8 + 3) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or become **Dazed** until the end of its next turn.
- **Javelin:** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage.

### 2. Bugbear Stalker (Strangler)

The stalker (often referred to as a strangler) embodies the true nightmare of the bugbear mythos. They prefer to stay completely hidden for the first few rounds of an engagement, waiting until the enemy frontline is distracted. They then step out of the shadows to isolate and choke the life out of the most vulnerable target.

_Medium Humanoid (Goblinoid), Chaotic Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 15 (Studded Leather)
- **Hit Points:** 52 (8d8 + 16)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 16 (+3) | **DEX:** 16 (+3) | **CON:** 14 (+2) | **INT:** 10 (+0) | **WIS:** 14 (+2) | **CHA:** 10 (+0)
- **Skills:** Athletics +5, Intimidate +4, Stealth +7 (Expertise)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** Common, Goblin
- **Challenge:** 3 (700 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Surprise Attack:** If the bugbear hits a creature with an attack roll and has Advantage on the roll, the attack deals an extra 7 (2d6) damage.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The stalker makes two attacks: one with its Morningstar and one to Strangle, or two with its Morningstar.
- **Morningstar:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 12 (2d8 + 3) piercing damage.
- **Strangle (Garrote):** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 8 (1d10 + 3) bludgeoning damage, and the target is **Grappled** (escape DC 13). Until this grapple ends, the target cannot speak or cast spells with verbal components, and the stalker cannot strangle another target.

**Bonus Actions**

- **Nimble Escape:** The stalker takes the Disengage or Hide action.

**Reactions**

- **Meat Shield (Body Shield):** When the stalker is targeted by a melee or ranged attack, it can use its reaction to force a creature it is currently Grappling to take the attack instead. The stalker swaps places with the grappled creature, and the attack roll is compared to the grappled creature's AC to determine if it hits.
