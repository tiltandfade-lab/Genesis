## 7. Honest summary

Of roughly 35 concrete spawnables the working spec implies, **6 have a LIVE roller** (2
structural/prop hits, 4 occupant-role hits), **5 have a LIVE-but-generic or LIVE-but-degraded
roller** that does not yet carry Site-2-specific rung/state facts (trough, ruined
wagon/caravan/palisade, abandoned-campsite snapshot, oversized campfire curiosity), **6 have
a LIVE roller that is atmosphere/skin or extreme-tier flavor rather than the ordinary
spatial case** (dungeon Walk Skin rows, wilderness Walk Breach rows), and the remaining
majority — every site-owned structural atom, every named mechanism, all seven operating
circuits, and nearly every named hook — are `NO-TABLE-YET`. This matches the working spec's
own generator contract, which describes a `TARGET-ADAPTER` that has not been built. Two new
roster items are proposed by this pass (a sutler/vivandière reading of the existing Trader-
or-Peddler role, and a trade-rendezvous/fair occupancy variant); neither is wired, and both
are offered as proposals, not rulings. This inventory changes no status and authorizes no
build — it is the feed for a future spawn-audit and wiring pass.
