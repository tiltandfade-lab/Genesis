## 30-Second Run

The party follows Vesper into a dark, reeking antechamber hung with origami birds. A loose floorboard fires a crossbow trap — Vesper goes down. In the northeast corner, a glowing Clairvoyance sensor watches everything and the Cult Leader is on the other end. A red Flumph is wrapped around the winch, projecting panicked warnings into everyone's minds. If the PCs calm it, they get intelligence on the boss room. If they don't, the origami birds unfold into Stirges.

---

## Setup

**Space:** 20×20 ft stone room. Slick floor (guano + water — Difficult Terrain, Disadvantage on Acrobatics). Iron portcullis blocks the southern exit. Rusted winch at center. Clairvoyance sensor hovering in the northeast corner.
**Atmosphere:** Rot smell (Disadvantage on all Perception checks while in the room). Dozens of folded paper birds hang from the ceiling. Weak light — damp, cold air.
**NPCs Present:** Vesper (Sapphire Circle spy, currently visible), 1 Flumph (glowing red, wrapped around the winch), 1d4+2 Stirges (disguised as origami — dormant).
**If Ignored (retreat):** Vesper bleeds out. The Flumph is eventually consumed by the Cult Leader.

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Mark, The Watcher, and the Flock

The trap fires on entry. The sensor is already active. The PCs have seconds to react.

**The Trap Roll (open, not hidden):** Ranged attack against Vesper — +6 to hit, AC 13. Hit: 1d10 piercing + 2d10 poison. Vesper has 22 HP. If dropped to 0: PCs have 3 rounds to stabilize via healing magic or DC 10 Medicine before Vesper dies.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 13 Arcana (sensor):** *Clairvoyance* — the caster sees and hears everything. Obscuring it (e.g., *Darkness*, a cloak thrown over it) prevents the Cult Leader from reading the party's tactics.
- **Sensor consequence:** If not obscured, the Cult Leader cannot be surprised in the boss room and gains +2 to Initiative against the party.
- **DC 13 Medicine:** Assess and stabilize Vesper.

**Escalation:** Roll or choose what triggers the origami flock (see d4 Trigger table). Once triggered: 1d4+2 Stirges unfold and attack.

---

### Segment 2: The Red Flumph

The Flumph is wrapped around the winch, glowing violent red.

**Read Aloud:**
> *"Wrapped tightly around the handles of the heavy iron winch is a bizarre creature — a flattened jellyfish with eyestalks and dangling tentacles, glowing a violent, pulsing red. As you approach, a frantic voice erupts directly inside your minds: 'No! Do not open! The stone hungers! The dark shell cracks! Death is below!'"*

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 12 Nature:** Identify as a Flumph — peaceful, telepathic, Underdark denizen. Red = extreme terror.
- **DC 14 Persuasion:** Calm the Flumph. Telepathic communication grants Advantage.
- **Forced removal (Athletics):** Pulls it off the winch. If the Stirge trap hasn't triggered yet, it triggers immediately via *Psychic Backlash*. The Flumph flees through the portcullis bars.

**On successful calm:** The Flumph turns green, releases the winch, and grants 1 roll on the Psychic Flashes table. It also warns the party of the Cult Leader's specific magical defenses before floating to safety.

## Mechanics

1. **Difficult Terrain:** Slick floor throughout — Disadvantage on Acrobatics checks, Difficult Terrain.
2. **Rot Stench:** Disadvantage on all Perception checks while in the room.
3. **Portcullis:** Opens when the winch is operated freely. Forcing it (Flumph still attached): DC 13 Athletics + triggers Stirge flock.
4. **Trap Bolt:** If carefully extracted from the wall or from Vesper, the coating poison can be applied — next creature struck must make DC 13 Con save or be Poisoned for 1 hour.
5. **Vesper's Ledger (if Vesper dies):** Contains the full supply chain proving Civic District nobles are funding the cult's corpse-smuggling operation.

## NPC Dossier

**Vesper** — Sapphire Circle Spy
Goal: Deliver the party safely to the Cult Leader's chamber and survive.
Leverage: Holds the map out of the dungeon. Death = no easy exit after the boss fight.
Tell: Flinches slightly when passing doors (combat veteran, not fearless).

**The Refugee Flumph**
Goal: Escape the evil radiating from the boss room. Prevent anyone else from dying here.
Leverage: Responds to kindness, healing, and telepathic reassurance.
Boundary: Will not enter the boss room under any circumstances.
Tell: Color = emotional state (Red = terror, Green = calm, Blue = sadness).

---

## Stat Blocks

**Flumph** — *Small Aberration, Lawful Good* — CR 1/8
AC 12 | HP 7 | Speed 5 ft., Fly 30 ft.
**Telepathy 60 ft.**
**Stench Spray (Recharge 6):** 15 ft cone, DC 10 Dex save or Poisoned 1d4 hours.
**Tactic:** Flees through portcullis bars if threatened. Does not fight.

**Stirge** — *Tiny Beast* — CR 1/8
AC 14 | HP 2 | Speed 10 ft., Fly 40 ft.
**Blood Drain.** +5, 1d4+3 piercing; attaches to target. While attached: target loses 1d4+3 HP at start of each Stirge turn.
**Tactic:** Attaches to nearest warm body and drains. Detaches only when full or killed.

**Assassin's Mark (Trap)**
Detect: DC 15 Investigation (loose floorboard + firing slit). Trigger: stepping on floorboard.
Effect: +6 ranged attack. Hit: 1d10 piercing + 2d10 poison.
