#realm-items-chrome
> **PROVISIONAL — Adam spot-check pending.** The CHROME realm's item universe (docs/BREACH.md
> §2d, BATCH3-GUARDRAILS.md J2 restructure). d50; breach draws inside a `chrome`-tagged walk
> roll THIS table; the legacy Outlandish d300 stays the anachronism-intrusion grab-bag (division
> of labor, J2). Register per J3b: Tech/sci-fi — clean hard surfaces, cheap miracles, a battery
> bar always dropping. Every item names its FRAME (existing `data/items.js` id); none invented.
> Tech consumables are CHARGE items (cells) and rust-EXEMPT — they ride the existing
> enchantment-immunity check (`rustImmune`), no engine change needed. **Band shares run hotter
> than H2's 66/20/9/4/1 by construction** (this table is ~half enchanted/signature items by the
> brief's own recipe — see Frontier's frontmatter for the full reasoning); Volatile/Mythic stay
> rare and earned against `[[SPICE-RULER]]`, not forced to a ratio. **"Lance Pistol" and
> "Falling-Star Visor" are the J3b-anchored ladders**, landed verbatim among the signature
> wonders. Rank ladders (2–4 rungs, level prereqs) sit on enchanted + signature rows;
> reality-breaking rungs floor at L9. d50, Commitment ceiling.
