## Binding statement

**Reference evidence only.** Nothing listed here may become a Genesis production asset,
texture, mesh source, or promotional image. These images inform internal visual direction
and grammar induction. Before any image is copied, modified, redistributed, embedded, used
as a texture, or used in promotional material, its exact license and attribution
requirements must be reviewed *for that use*. The safe Genesis production path remains
original procedural geometry and original materials.

Every image below is from **Wikimedia Commons**. Attribution strings are as supplied by the
Commons file's own `extmetadata`; the linked Commons page is authoritative if this table and
the page ever disagree. Images were fetched at ~1100–1280 px (Commons-rendered thumbnails),
not full resolution — enough to read morphology, small enough to keep the study cheap.

**License mix:** 9 × CC BY-SA 2.0 · 2 × CC BY-SA 4.0 · 1 × CC BY 4.0. No CC0 and no public
domain in this set — every image in this packet carries an attribution obligation, and two
carry a share-alike obligation at 4.0. That is a stricter mix than the Lair study's and is
worth knowing before anybody reuses a frame.

## What was deliberately NOT downloaded

- **No FFT map images were downloaded.** Lane 3 read the in-repo corpus at
  `Reference/FFT Battle Maps/` in place after materializing it from git-LFS. Nothing was
  copied, derived, or added.
- **No game screenshots** (BG3, Diablo, Darkest Dungeon, etc.). The FFT boundary in
  `docs/GOLDEN-SITES-CATALOG.md` keeps game screenshots to local comparison only, never
  redistributed and never committed; this study lives *in* the repo, so downloading them here
  would break that boundary by construction.
- No images of private individuals were sought. Three frames contain incidental modern
  fittings (an electrical conduit in D4-02, a steel drum in D5-01, a graded track in D5-02);
  those are read as evidence of the *state*, not as design targets.
