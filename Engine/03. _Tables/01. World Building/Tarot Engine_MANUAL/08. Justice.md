### **VIII. Justice: Trigger Effects**

Justice represents the objective truth, the law of cause and effect, and the cold, impartial nature of the universe. When this card is drawn, the "Chaos of the Dungeon" is replaced by a rigid system of accountability. Every action has an equal and opposite reaction, and the scales must always be balanced.

---

**The Constant: "The Scales of Equilibrium"**

The universe demands a zero-sum outcome. Power is never created, only moved.

- **The Effect:** Whenever a creature (player or monster) deals damage, they gain a "Karmic Debt" equal to half the damage dealt. The next time they take damage, the debt is added to the total, and the debt is cleared.
    
- **The Toll:** "Cold Impartiality." Luck is suspended. No creature in the dungeon can have **Advantage** or **Disadvantage**. All rolls are flat d20s.

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

**The Constant: "The Great Audit"**

The faction has stopped all expansion to "balance their books." They are hunting down anyone who owes them money, service, or blood.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**Faction Behavior (Justice)**|**Narrative Implication**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Arbitrators**|The faction offers to mediate a conflict between the party and another group, but their fee is 50% of the party's total wealth.|
|**6-9**|Textured|**The Inevitable Law**|The faction begins arresting people for "Future Crimes" based on the prophecies of a blind judge.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Simulation Audit**|The faction realizes the world’s math is wrong. They begin deleting "redundant" people to save on universal resources.|

### **Trigger: Accepting Binding Agreements**

**The Constant: "The Vow of Fairness"**

The bargain is perfectly fair. For every blessing you receive, you must accept a curse of equal magnitude. You cannot choose the curse; the universe will "balance" it.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**The Bargain's Mutation (Justice)**|**The "Gift"**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**Eye for an Eye**|Whenever you take damage, the attacker takes the same amount. The cost: You can never be healed by magic.|
|**6-9**|Textured|**The Soul-Debt**|You can cast any spell without a slot, but you lose a number of maximum HP equal to the spell's level until your next long rest.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Sentence**|You become immortal, but you are physically bound to a 10ft radius of the spot where you signed the agreement.|
