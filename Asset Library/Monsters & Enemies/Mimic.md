*p. 207 MM 2024*

The mimic is the dungeon's ultimate argument against complacency. It doesn't attack like a monster — it waits until the player character has their hand on a chest, a door, or a piece of furniture that they want, and then it strikes at its most vulnerable moment. The mimic exploits greed, habit, and the fundamental architecture of dungeon exploration itself. After the first mimic encounter, every subsequent chest becomes a moment of genuine hesitation. That hesitation is the mimic doing its job whether it's present or not.

Mimics are extraordinarily patient. They can hold their form for months or years, subsisting on vermin and small creatures that wander into their adhesive grasp. A mimic that has been in one location for years will have learned the smell, temperature, and acoustic signature of that location perfectly — it knows how to look exactly right. A DC 10 Investigation check is the bare minimum to suspect something, and a mimic that has been in place for longer can inflate that DC at the DM's discretion.

The relationship between mimics and the dungeon's other inhabitants is complicated. Some monsters use mimics deliberately — placing them at the entrance to a treasure vault to eliminate thieves. Others avoid certain rooms because they know one of the strange furniture pieces is hungry. The mimic doesn't discriminate. It will consume anything that touches it and is small enough to be food.

### Campaign Integration: Investigation & Clues

Mimics are harder to detect before the encounter than after it. The clues are retrospective.

- **The Absence of Vermin:** A room with a mimic has no rats, no insects, no small animals. Anything that would normally nest in dungeon furniture has learned not to approach this piece. An unnaturally clean room with no sign of vermin near a specific piece of furniture is a quiet flag.
- **The Inconsistent Dust:** Mimics shift their position slightly to catch prey. A chest or door that has left a different dust outline than its current position was moved — recently. DC 13 Investigation to notice the dust pattern mismatch.
- **The Survivor's Warning:** NPCs who survived an encounter with a mimic in a previous part of the dungeon will warn the party about "the room with the gold-banded chest." By the time the party gets there, the mimic has moved. The warning is technically useless. But it's there.

### 1. Mimic

An ambush predator that has adopted the shape of a dungeon fixture to catch unwary prey.

_Medium Monstrosity (Shapechanger), Neutral_

- **Armor Class:** 12 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 58 (9d8 + 18)
- **Speed:** 15 ft.
- **STR:** 17 (+3) | **DEX:** 12 (+1) | **CON:** 15 (+2) | **INT:** 5 (−3) | **WIS:** 13 (+1) | **CHA:** 8 (−1)
- **Skills:** Stealth +5
- **Damage Immunities:** Acid
- **Condition Immunities:** Prone
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Shapechanger:** The mimic can use its action to Polymorph into an object or back into its true, amorphous form. Its statistics are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed. It reverts to its true form if it dies.
- **Adhesive (Object Form Only):** The mimic adheres to anything that touches it. A Huge or smaller creature adhered to the mimic is also Grappled by it (escape DC 13). Ability checks made to escape this grapple have Disadvantage.
- **False Appearance (Object Form Only):** While the mimic remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary object.
- **Grappler:** The mimic has Advantage on attack rolls against any creature Grappled by it.

**Actions**

- **Pseudopod:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage. If the mimic is in object form, the target is subjected to its Adhesive trait.
- **Bite:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) acid damage.

### 2. Greater Mimic

A mimic that has grown to considerable size, capable of imitating large furniture, statues, doors, or entire sections of floor or wall. Rarely encountered — its presence suggests an older, more established dungeon.

_Large Monstrosity (Shapechanger), Neutral_

- **Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 102 (12d10 + 36)
- **Speed:** 20 ft.
- **STR:** 20 (+5) | **DEX:** 10 (+0) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 7 (−2) | **WIS:** 13 (+1) | **CHA:** 8 (−1)
- **Skills:** Stealth +4
- **Damage Immunities:** Acid
- **Condition Immunities:** Prone
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** —
- **Challenge:** 4 (1,100 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Shapechanger:** The mimic can use its action to Polymorph into an object or back into its true form.
- **Adhesive (Object Form Only):** Any creature that touches the greater mimic while it is in object form must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be Grappled and Restrained (escape DC 14). Creatures that fail the saving throw have Disadvantage on checks to escape.
- **False Appearance (Object Form Only):** While the mimic remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary object. Large objects (door, statue, section of wall, altar) are within its range.
- **Crushing Grasp:** A creature that is Grappled by the greater mimic takes 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage at the start of each of the mimic's turns.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The greater mimic makes two attacks: one Pseudopod and one Bite.
- **Pseudopod:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage. The target is subjected to the Adhesive trait.
- **Bite:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one Grappled creature. _Hit:_ 14 (2d8 + 5) piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) acid damage.
- **Engulf (Recharge 5–6):** The mimic attempts to engulf a Medium or smaller creature Grappled by it. The target must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or be swallowed. While swallowed, the target is Blinded and Restrained, it has total cover from attacks outside the mimic, and it takes 14 (4d6) acid damage at the start of each of the mimic's turns. If the mimic takes 20 damage or more on a single turn from the swallowed creature, the mimic must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures. A swallowed creature is dropped Prone in a space within 10 ft. of the mimic if the mimic dies.

| d10 | Encounter Premise | Campaign Tone / Mechanics |
|:----|:------------------|:--------------------------|
| **1** | **The Expected Chest** | **Classic Dread:** There is exactly one chest in the room. It is slightly newer than everything else. It is the only item in the room. Everything about it says "loot me." Everything about it says "don't loot me." |
| **2** | **The Stuck Door** | **Awful Realization:** The fighter has been trying to force open the stuck door for two rounds. Then the "door" bites them. |
| **3** | **The Furniture Room** | **Paranoid Standoff:** The room contains four chairs, two tables, a wardrobe, and a chest. One of them is a mimic. The party has to identify which one without touching any of them. |
| **4** | **The Mimic Keeper** | **Dark Humor / Tactical:** A dungeon faction is using a trained mimic (as much as a mimic can be trained) as a guardian. Its keeper is standing in the back of the room, holding a long pole with a scrap of meat on it. They direct the mimic with the meat. |
| **5** | **The Second Chest** | **Escalation:** The party finds a treasure room. There is one chest, obviously real — good lock, quality craftsmanship. There is a second chest in the corner, slightly dusty. The second one is the mimic. The real chest is the decoy. |
| **6** | **The Party Split Problem** | **Tactical:** The room has two exits, and the party splits to investigate. The mimic chooses the moment the group is divided to reveal itself, targeting whoever is alone. |
| **7** | **The Trap-Within-A-Trap** | **Nested Deception:** There is a chest. It is obviously trapped (pressure plate, wire, everything). The trap is real. But the chest is *also* a mimic. Disarming the trap triggers the mimic. |
| **8** | **The Stuck Companion** | **Emergency Combat:** An NPC companion opened a chest before the party could stop them. They are now stuck, screaming, as the mimic bites and squeezes. The mimic won't release unless destroyed or killed. |
| **9** | **The Ancient Mimic** | **Boss-Level Flavor:** The greater mimic has been here so long it has become a room. The party stepped *onto* it. The floor is the mimic. |
| **10** | **The Mimic Nest** | **Horror:** This room has three mimics — two chests and a wardrobe. They have been living here together for decades, subsisting on the dungeon's vermin and the occasional unlucky adventurer. ### d10 The Adhesive Scene (Investigation & Clues)

| d10 | Discovery | Investigative Hook |
|:----|:----------|:------------------|
| **1** | **The Dissolved Boot** | Near the base of a chest, a single boot is partially dissolved into gray sludge. The owner of the boot did not escape. |
| **2** | **The Dust Ring** | The dust around a chest or piece of furniture shows a slightly different pattern than expected — the furniture was in a different position three days ago. It moved. |
| **3** | **The Animal Silence** | No rats, no insects, no spider webs near the "furniture." Everything nearby has learned not to approach it. |
| **4** | **The Adventurer's Warning** | Scratched into the wall near the entrance in common: "Don't touch the red chest. DON'T." The chest is red. |
| **5** | **The Sticky Spot** | A faint film of adhesive residue is detectable on the floor near the object — dried and harmless now, but distinctly organic. DC 13 Nature check identifies it as a secretion from a shapeshifting predator. |
| **6** | **The Perfect Object** | The chest (or door, or statue) is *too perfect*. No scratches, no age patina, no matching dust layer with the rest of the room. It was placed here recently and has been posing since. |
| **7** | **The Half-Eaten Map** | A partially dissolved parchment is stuck to the side of a piece of furniture. Most of it is gone, but a fragment remains — a map piece or a partial note from a previous adventurer who got too close. |
| **8** | **The Irregular Weight** | Attempting to lift the chest (without triggering the mimic) reveals it weighs significantly more than an empty chest should — it has been feeding and has undigested mass inside. DC 14 Investigation to notice before full contact. |
| **9** | **The Mimic's Memory** | In the greater mimic's gullet (after it is destroyed), the party finds the preserved remnants of its last meal — an adventurer's pack, including a journal, a map, or a letter that provides significant dungeon intelligence. |
| **10** | **The Long Presence** | Carbon scoring on the wall above a chest matches a torch bracket long since removed. The chest has been here longer than the torch brackets — which means it predates the dungeon's current occupants. Whoever put it here is long gone. The mimic is all that remains of the original installation. |
