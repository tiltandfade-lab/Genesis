> d50; breach draws inside a `cosmic`-tagged walk roll THIS table; legacy Outlandish d300 stays
> the anachronism-intrusion grab-bag. Register per J3b: Cosmic/weird — scale that doesn't fit in
> a sentence; the wonder that answers back. Every item names its FRAME (existing `data/items.js`
> id); none invented. **Band shares run hotter than H2's baseline by construction** (see
> Frontier's frontmatter) — this realm's register leans Strange by nature, so the mundane floor
> is deliberately kept plain and quiet by contrast (grounded objects from a place that otherwise
> doesn't sound like it should have any). **"The Left Gauntlet of the Choir" is the J3b-anchored
> ladder**, landed verbatim among the signature wonders. Rank ladders sit on enchanted +
> signature rows; reality-breaking rungs floor at L9. d50, Commitment ceiling.
