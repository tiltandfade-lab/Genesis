### **XIII. Death: Trigger Effects**

The Death card represents finality, the end of a cycle, and inevitable transformation. It is rarely about literal expiration and more about the clearing of the old to make way for the new. Under this card, the dungeon becomes a place where nothing stays the same, and the "End" is a constant, looming presence.

---

**The Constant: "The Finality"**

Under the gaze of Death, there are no second chances. Resources are spent and gone forever.

- **The Effect:** Any healing received (spells, potions, or features) is halved. However, any creature (player or monster) that is reduced to 0 HP is instantly and cleanly "Transitioned"—they do not roll death saving throws and cannot be stabilized; they are simply dead (or incapacitated for the duration of the crawl).
    
- **The Rebirth:** To balance this, once per encounter, a player may choose to "Shed their Skin." They lose all current Temporary HP and half their maximum HP to instantly end any one condition or curse affecting them.

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

**The Constant: "The Great Collapse"**

The faction is in its death throes. They are not fighting to win; they are fighting to ensure they are remembered, or to take their enemies down with them.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**Faction Behavior (Death)**|**Narrative Implication**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Scorched Earth**|The faction begins destroying its own infrastructure and resources to prevent anyone else from taking them.|
|**6-9**|Less Grounded|**The Ghost Pact**|The faction’s members are committing ritual suicide to return as an army of vengeful ghosts.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Total Erasure**|The faction is systematically deleting its own history, burning all books and killing all witnesses to its existence.|

### **Trigger: Accepting Binding Agreements**

**The Constant: "The Final Bargain"**

The bargain is absolute and cannot be broken by anything short of a _Wish_ spell. It grants immense power, but sets a "Deadline."

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**The Bargain's Mutation (Death)**|**The "Gift"**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Reaper’s Eye**|You can see the exact HP of any creature. The cost: You can no longer see the faces of the living (they appear as skulls).|
|**6-9**|Less Grounded|**The Borrowed Time**|You gain a +10 bonus to all rolls for 1 hour. After that, you fall into a coma for 1 week.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Appointment**|You choose the exact date, time, and method of your death. Until that moment, you are literally unkillable.|

### **Trigger: World Shifts**

**The Constant: "The Turning of the Age"**

An era ends. A major institution, religion, or empire vanishes overnight, leaving a vacuum that nature—and chaos—is eager to fill.
