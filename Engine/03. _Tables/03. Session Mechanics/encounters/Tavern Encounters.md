> **Tavern / interior pressure** — quick single-roll beats for when the party is sitting in a pub and the scene needs a jolt. NOT the citywide encounter tool (that's the `Urban Encounter v2.5` node generator). A future `urban-pressure` table will cover citywide slow-play pressure; this one stays tavern-scoped. Renamed from "Urban Encounters" 2026-06-22 — it was always a tavern table.

### Random Tavern Encounters (d12 + d8)
| Roll | Encounter |
|:---:|:---|
| 2 | Animals on the loose (A circus bear or pack of baboons bursts into the tavern) |
| 3 | Announcement |
| 4 | Brawl (Rival gangs or factions suddenly clash inside) |
| 5 | Bullies (Thugs start harassing someone at the bar) |
| 6 | Companion |
| 7 | Contest (An impromptu drinking or arm-wrestling contest starts) |
| 8 | Corpse |
| 9 | Draft (The city watch bursts in and drafts the PCs to help with a crisis) |
| 10 | Drunk (A drunk staggers over and mistakes a PC for a hated rival) |
| 11 | Fire (The tavern kitchen goes up in flames!) |
| 12 | Found trinket |
| 13 | Guard harassment |
| 14 | Pickpocket (A thief tries to lift a PC's coin purse while they are distracted) |
| 15 | Procession |
