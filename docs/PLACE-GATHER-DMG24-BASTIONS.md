# DMG 2024 — Bastions (Ch.8) — Place-Gen Gather

**What this is.** Vision-verified, purpose-scoped gather of the DMG 2024 Bastion chapter for the Genesis PLACE-GEN spec: the full building-function taxonomy (6 basic + 29 special facilities with their physical flavor, sizes, staffing, and function verbs) plus the Bastion Events d100 table and all facility option sub-tables verbatim. The player-subsystem economics (bastion points, attack math, turn cadence) are deliberately compressed to a paragraph each — this document treats the chapter as raw material for a place-type spine and interior composition, not as a rules port.

## Chapter frame (compressed)

A Bastion is a location belonging to a PC — home, stronghold, place of power — gained at level 5; shape/style are player-determined (wizard tower, cleric shrine, fighter keep, rogue guildhall/lodge; classes can combine forms, and multiple PCs can fuse Bastions into one structure) (p.333). **Turn cadence (compressed):** a Bastion turn happens every 7 in-game days; the owner issues orders to special facilities or a whole-Bastion Maintain order; absent owners default to Maintain, which triggers an event roll (p.333). **Attack/defender math (compressed):** when attacked, roll 6d6 and each 1 kills a Bastion Defender; at zero defenders a random special facility is damaged and shut down until repaired free next turn; stocked Armory upgrades dice to d8, full enclosure by defensive walls drops 2 dice, housed War Room lieutenants each drop 1 die (pp.334, 339, 349–350). **Acquisition economy (compressed):** special facilities aren't bought — they arrive by level (2 at L5, 4 at L9, 5 at L13, 6 at L17), and each level-up allows swapping one facility for another qualified one (p.335). Facilities generate enough income to pay their own hirelings (p.336).

## Bastion map features (p.334)

Free structural connective tissue any Bastion can have: **Closets** (washrooms/enclosures inside a facility, no area increase), **Corridors, Ramps, and Staircases**, **Doors and Windows** (every facility comes with one or more doors and shuttered windows; locked/secret doors and portcullises allowed), and **Defensive Walls** (20 ft high, optional walkway with ladder/lift access; each 5-ft square takes 10 days and 250 GP).

## Facility space definitions (p.335)

Area is in 5-foot squares, configurable on the map and stackable across multiple levels/stories.

| Space | Maximum area |
|---|---|
| Cramped | 4 squares (i.e. up to 100 sq ft) |
| Roomy | 16 squares (up to 400 sq ft) |
| Vast | 36 squares (up to 900 sq ft) |

Adding a basic facility: Cramped 500 GP / 20 days · Roomy 1,000 GP / 45 days · Vast 3,000 GP / 125 days. Enlarging: Cramped→Roomy 500 GP / 25 days · Roomy→Vast 2,000 GP / 80 days (p.335).

## Basic facilities (p.335)

A Bastion starts with two free basic facilities (one Cramped, one Roomy); duplicates allowed. Nonmagical furnishings and decor, no game effects — pure verisimilitude and roleplay stage-setting.

**Bedroom · Courtyard · Dining Room · Kitchen · Parlor · Storage**

## Orders vocabulary (p.336)

The function verbs facilities run on: **Craft** (hirelings make an item; facility is tied up for the duration), **Empower** (facility confers a temporary empowerment on you or someone else), **Harvest** (gather a resource the facility produces), **Trade** (hirelings buy/sell goods or services stored/produced there), **Research** (hirelings gather information), **Recruit** (hirelings recruit creatures, incl. Bastion Defenders), **Maintain** (whole-Bastion upkeep order; triggers a Bastion Events roll).

## Special facility descriptions

Format: **Name** (level · space · hirelings · order) — what the space physically is, then a digest of function.

**Arcane Study** (L5 · Roomy · 1 · Craft) — A place of quiet research containing one or more desks and bookshelves. Confers a weekly charm to cast *Identify* free after a Long Rest; Craft options: an Arcane Focus (7 days, free), a blank book (7 days, 10 GP), or (L9+) a Common/Uncommon Arcana magic item (p.336).

**Archive** (L13 · Roomy · 1 · Research) — A repository of books, maps, and scrolls, usually attached to a Library behind a locked or secret door. Research: Helpful Lore commissions a 7-day lore search that works like *Legend Lore*. Holds one reference book (below); enlarging to Vast (2,000 GP) adds two more (p.338).

*Archive Reference Books (verbatim options, p.338):*
| Book | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Bigby's Handy Arcana Codex | Advantage on Int (Arcana) Study checks to recall lore about spells, magic items, eldritch symbols, magical traditions, and planes of existence |
| The Chronepsis Chronicles | Advantage on Int (History) Study checks to recall lore about historical events, legendary people, ancient kingdoms, past disputes, wars, and lost civilizations |
| Investigations of the Inquisitive | Advantage on Int (Investigation) Study checks to make deductions from clues/evidence or recall lore about traps, ciphers, riddles, and gadgetry |
| Material Musings on the Nature of the World | Advantage on Int (Nature) Study checks to recall lore about terrain, plants, animals, and the weather |
| The Old Faith and Other Religions | Advantage on Int (Religion) Study checks to recall lore about deities, rites and prayers, hierarchies, holy symbols, and the practices of secret cults |

**Armory** (L5 · Roomy · 1 · Trade) — Contains mannequins for displaying armor, hooks for Shields, racks for storing weapons, and chests for ammunition. Trade: Stock Armory fills it with armor/Shields/weapons/ammunition (100 GP + 100 GP per Defender; halved with a Smithy); while stocked, defenders are harder to kill (d8s instead of d6s on attack loss rolls), and stock is expended per attack (p.339).

**Barrack** (L5 · Roomy · 1 · Recruit) — Sleeping quarters furnished for up to twelve Bastion Defenders (a Bastion can have several). Recruit adds up to four Defenders free. Vast enlargement (2,000 GP) sleeps twenty-five (p.339).

**Demiplane** (L17 · Vast · 1 · Empower) — A door up to 5 ft wide and 10 ft tall appears on a flat solid surface in another facility, leading to an extradimensional stone room; relocatable; only owner and hirelings can open it; can't be dispelled. Empower: wall runes grant Temp HP = 5× level after a Long Rest inside; Fabrication conjures a nonmagical object (≤5 ft, ≤5 GP, common materials) once per Long Rest (p.340).

**Gaming Hall** (L9 · Vast · 4 · Trade) — Offers recreational activities like chess and games of darts, cards, or dice. Trade: Gambling Hall turns it into a gambling den for 7 days, yielding a d100-rolled cut of house winnings (p.340).

*Gambling winnings (verbatim, p.340):* 01–50 → 1d6 × 10 GP · 51–85 → 2d6 × 10 GP · 86–95 → 4d6 × 10 GP · 96–00 → 10d6 × 10 GP.

**Garden** (L5 · Roomy · 1 · Harvest) — An outdoor/cultivated plot; pick a type at creation (changeable in 21 days); multiple Gardens allowed. Harvest collects the type's yield in 7 days, free. Vast (2,000 GP) = two Roomy gardens (same or different types) + 1 extra hireling (p.340).

*Garden Types (verbatim, p.340):*
| Garden Type | Description | Harvest |
|---|---|---|
| Decorative | Aesthetically pleasing garden full of flowers and topiaries | Ten exquisite floral bouquets (worth 5 GP each), ten vials of Perfume, or ten Candles |
| Food | Garden of delicious mushrooms or vegetables | 100 days worth of Rations |
| Herb | Garden of rare herbs, some of which have medicinal uses | Herbs used to create either ten Healer's Kits or one *Potion of Healing* |
| Poison | Garden stocked with plants and fungi from which poisons and antitoxin can be extracted | Plants used to create either two vials of Antitoxin or one vial of Basic Poison |

**Greenhouse** (L9 · Roomy · 1 · Harvest) — An enclosure where rare plants and fungi are nurtured in a controlled climate. Always bears three magical Fruits of Restoration (eat = *Lesser Restoration*; replaced daily at dawn). Harvest: Healing Herbs (a *Potion of Healing (greater)*) or Poison (one application of Assassin's Blood, Malice, Pale Tincture, or Truth Serum), 7 days, free (p.341).

**Guildhall** (L17 · Vast · 1 · Recruit) — Comes with a guild (you're guild master) and a meeting room where ~fifty skilled members — who live and work outside the Bastion, usually in nearby settlements — can gather in your presence. Recruit sends guild members on the guild-type's assignment (p.341–342).

*Sample Guilds (verbatim, p.341):*

**Laboratory** (L9 · Roomy · 1 · Craft) — Contains storage space for alchemical supplies and workspaces for crafting various concoctions (a cauldron is standard equipment). Craft: anything makeable with Alchemist's Supplies, or a vial of poison (Burnt Othur Fumes, Essence of Ether, or Torpor; 7 days, half the poison's cost) (p.342).

**Library** (L5 · Roomy · 1 · Research) — A collection of books plus one or more desks and reading chairs. Research: Topical Lore — 7 days on a topic (legend, event, location, person, creature type, famous object) yields up to three accurate previously-unknown pieces of information (p.342).

**Meditation Chamber** (L13 · Cramped · 1 · Empower) — A relaxing space that helps align one's mind, body, and spirit. Empower: Inner Peace lets you roll twice on the next Bastion event and choose. Fortify Self: 7 days of meditation grants Advantage on two randomly determined kinds of saving throws for 7 days (p.342).

*Fortify Self saving throw d6 (verbatim, p.342):* 1 Strength · 2 Dexterity · 3 Constitution · 4 Intelligence · 5 Wisdom · 6 Charisma. (Reroll duplicates.)

**Menagerie** (L13 · Vast · 2 · Recruit) — Enclosures big enough to contain up to four Large creatures (four Small/Medium fit in one Large slot). Recruit adds a creature from the table below (7 days, listed cost); menagerie creatures count as Bastion Defenders unless you opt them out, in which case they act per their nature (p.343).

**Observatory** (L13 · Roomy · 1 · Empower) — Situated atop the Bastion; contains a telescope aimed at the night sky, used to peer into the far corners of Wildspace and the Astral Plane. Confers a weekly charm to cast *Contact Other Plane* free. Empower: Eldritch Discovery — 7 nights of eldritch stargazing; on an odd roll an unknown power bestows a Charm of Darkvision, Heroism, or Vitality (pp.343–344).

**Pub** (L13 · Roomy · 1 · Research) — Where folks come to consume tasty beverages and socialize; might be a bar, coffee shop, or tea room with a colorful name (the Rusty Flagon, the Dragon's Loft). The bartender-hireling maintains a network of spies in nearby communities. Research: Information Gathering locates any familiar creature within 50 miles via the spy network. One magical beverage on tap (switchable each turn); Vast (2,000 GP) = two taps + three server hirelings (p.344).

*Pub Special options (verbatim, p.344):*
| Beverage | Effect of a pint |
|---|---|
| Bigby's Burden | The "enlarge" effect of an *Enlarge/Reduce* spell for 24 hours (no saving throw allowed) |
| Kiss of the Spider Queen | The effect of a *Spider Climb* spell for 24 hours |
| Moonlight Serenade | Darkvision out to 60 feet for 24 hours (existing Darkvision extended by 60 feet instead) |
| Positive Reinforcement | Resistance to Necrotic damage for 24 hours |
| Sterner Stuff | For 24 hours, automatically succeed on saves to avoid or end the Frightened condition |

**Reliquary** (L13 · Cramped · 1 · Harvest) — A vault that holds sacred objects. Confers a weekly charm to cast *Greater Restoration* free. Harvest: Talisman produces a specially prepared talisman — usually an amulet, rune-carved box, or statuette, but any Tiny nonmagical object of religious significance — that substitutes for one spell's material components costing ≤1,000 GP (p.344).

**Sacristy** (L9 · Roomy · 1 · Craft) — A preparation and storage room for sacred items and religious vestments. Craft: Holy Water (potency boostable with GP) or a Common/Uncommon Relic magic item. Spell Refreshment: regain one expended spell slot of level 5 or lower on a Short Rest in the Bastion (pp.344–345).

**Sanctuary** (L5 · Roomy · 1 · Craft) — Icons of your religion are displayed here; a quiet place for worship. Confers a weekly charm to cast *Healing Word* free. Craft: Sacred Focus — a Druidic Focus (wooden staff) or Holy Symbol, 7 days, free (pp.345–346).

**Sanctum** (L17 · Roomy · 4 · Empower) — A place of solace and healing. Confers a weekly charm to cast *Heal* free. Empower: Fortifying Rites — daily rites grant a named beneficiary Temp HP = your level per Long Rest for 7 days. Sanctum Recall: *Word of Recall* always prepared with the Sanctum as an optional destination; one arriving creature gets a *Heal* (p.346).

**Scriptorium** (L9 · Roomy · 1 · Craft) — Contains desks and writing supplies for crafting books, scrolls, and other paperwork. Craft: Book Replica (copy a nonmagical book, needs a blank book, 7 days), Spell Scroll (Cleric/Wizard spell ≤L3, has the Calligrapher's Supplies), or Paperwork (up to fifty copies of a broadsheet/pamphlet, 1 GP per copy, distributable within 50 miles) (p.346).

**Smithy** (L5 · Roomy · 2 · Craft) — Contains a forge, an anvil, and other tools needed to craft weapons, armor, and other equipment. Craft: anything makeable with Smith's Tools, or (L9+) a Common/Uncommon Armament magic item (p.347).

**Stable** (L9 · Roomy · 1 · Trade) — Big enough to house three Large animals (two Medium = one Large); comes with one Riding Horse or Camel plus two Ponies or Mules; multiple Stables allowed. Beasts stabled 14+ days grant Advantage on Animal Handling checks about them. Trade: Animals buys/sells mounts (sale profit +20%, scaling to +50% at L13, +100% at L17). Vast (2,000 GP) houses six Large animals (p.347).

**Storehouse** (L5 · Roomy · 1 · Trade) — A cool, dark space meant to contain trade goods. Trade: Goods — hireling spends 7 days buying nonmagical items (≤500 GP total, rising to 2,000 GP at L9 and 5,000 GP at L13) or selling stored goods (+10% profit, +20% at L9, +50% at L13, +100% at L17) (p.347).

**Teleportation Circle** (L9 · Roomy · 1 · Recruit) — A permanent teleportation circle (as the *Teleportation Circle* spell) inscribed on the floor. Recruit: Spellcaster invites a Friendly NPC spellcaster (50% chance of accepting) who stays 14 days and will cast one Wizard spell ≤L4 (≤L8 if you're L17+) for you; won't defend the Bastion (pp.347–348).

**Theater** (L9 · Vast · 4 · Empower) — Contains a stage, a backstage area where props and sets are kept, and a seating area for a small audience. Empower: Theatrical Event — 14 days rehearsal + 7+ days performances; contributors (composer/writer, conductor/director, performer) who pass a DC 15 Cha (Performance) check gain a Theater die (d6; d8 at L13, d10 at L17) to add to one future D20 Test (p.348).

**Training Area** (L9 · Vast · 4 · Empower) — Might be an open courtyard, a gymnasium, a music or dance hall, or a cleverly built gauntlet of traps and hazards; may contain inanimate targets and padded mats. One hireling is an expert trainer, the rest training partners. Empower: Training — 7 days (8 hrs/day) grants the current trainer's benefit for 7 days; trainer swappable each turn (pp.348–349).

*Expert Trainers (verbatim, p.348):*
| Trainer Type | Empower Effect |
|---|---|
| Battle Expert | When you take damage from an attack made with an Unarmed Strike or a weapon, you can take a Reaction to reduce this damage by 1d4 |
| Skills Expert | You gain proficiency in one of the following skills of your choice: Acrobatics, Athletics, Performance, Sleight of Hand, or Stealth |
| Tools Expert | You gain proficiency with one tool of your choice |
| Unarmed Combat Expert | When you hit with your Unarmed Strike and deal damage, the attack deals an extra 1d4 Bludgeoning damage |
| Weapon Expert | Choose a kind of Simple or Martial weapon, such as Spear or Longbow. If you aren't proficient with the weapon, you gain proficiency with it. If you already have proficiency with the weapon, you can use its mastery property |

**Trophy Room** (L9 · Roomy · 1 · Research) — Houses a collection of mementos, such as weapons from old battles, the mounted heads of slain creatures, trinkets plucked from dungeons and ruins, and trophies passed down from ancestors. Research: Lore (as Library, the trophies providing clues to a wide variety of subjects) or Trinket Trophy (7-day search; even roll finds a Common Implement magic item) (p.349).

**War Room** (L17 · Vast · 2+ · Recruit) — Where you plan military actions with an inner circle of loyal lieutenants (each a battle-hardened Veteran Warrior); contains war memorabilia plus a large table surrounded by enough chairs for you and your lieutenants (up to ten). Recruit: Lieutenant (add one) or Soldiers (each lieutenant musters one hundred Guards in 7 days — twenty if mounted; 1 GP/day/head upkeep; army disbands if unfed or unled) (p.349).

**Workshop** (L5 · Roomy · 3 · Craft) — A creative space where useful items can be crafted; equipped with six kinds of Artisan's Tools chosen from: Carpenter's, Cobbler's, Glassblower's, Jeweler's, Leatherworker's, Mason's, Painter's, Potter's, Tinker's, Weaver's, Woodcarver's. Craft: Adventuring Gear (anything makeable with the chosen tools) or (L9+) a Common/Uncommon Implement magic item. Source of Inspiration: a Short Rest here grants Heroic Inspiration once per Long Rest. Vast (2,000 GP) adds two hirelings + three more tool kits (pp.349–350).

### Event descriptions (pp.350–352)

**All Is Well.** Nothing significant happens; roll on the flavor table:

| 1d8 | Details |
|---|---|
| 1 | Accident reports are way down. |
| 2 | The leak in the roof has been fixed. |
| 3 | No vermin infestations to report. |
| 4 | You-Know-Who lost their spectacles again. |
| 5 | One of your hirelings adopted a stray dog. |
| 6 | You received a lovely letter from a friend. |
| 7 | Some practical joker has been putting rotten eggs in people's boots. |
| 8 | Someone thought they saw a ghost. |

**Attack.** A hostile force attacks but is defeated. Roll 6d6; each 1 kills a Bastion Defender; at zero defenders, one random special facility is damaged and shut down (repaired free by the next turn after).

**Criminal Hireling.** A hireling's criminal past comes to light when officials or bounty hunters arrive with an arrest warrant. Retain them with a 1d6 × 100 GP bribe, or the hireling is arrested and taken away (facility unusable next turn if left unstaffed; replaced free).

**Extraordinary Opportunity.** The Bastion may host an important festival or celebration, fund a powerful spellcaster's research, or appease a domineering noble. Pay 500 GP to seize it → influx of recognition (DM rolls again on the events table, rerolling this result); decline and nothing happens.

**Friendly Visitors.** Visitors seek brief use of one of your special facilities, offering 1d6 × 100 GP (a knight wanting the Smithy for a horseshoe or armor repair; sages needing the Arcane Study to settle a dispute). Doesn't interrupt issued orders.

**Guest.** A Friendly guest comes to stay:

| 1d4 | Guest |
|---|---|
| 1 | An individual of great renown who stays 7 days and leaves you a letter of recommendation (see "Marks of Prestige" in chapter 3). |
| 2 | The guest requests sanctuary while avoiding persecution for their beliefs or crimes; departs after 7 days with a gift of 1d6 × 100 GP. |
| 3 | A mercenary — one additional Bastion Defender needing no housing, staying until sent away or killed. |
| 4 | A Friendly monster, such as a brass dragon or a treant; if the Bastion is attacked during their stay, it defends the Bastion and you lose no Defenders; leaves after one defense or when dismissed. |

**Lost Hirelings.** One random special facility loses its hirelings (cause is yours to invent); facility unusable next turn; hirelings replaced free afterward.

**Magical Discovery.** Your hirelings discover or accidentally create an Uncommon magic item (Potion or Scroll) at no cost.

**Refugees.** 2d4 refugees fleeing a monster attack, natural disaster, or other calamity seek refuge; they camp just outside if no basic facility can house them; they pay 1d6 × 100 GP for hospitality and stay until rehomed or a hostile force attacks.

**Request for Aid.** A local leader needs help (a missing-person search, brigand trouble). Dispatch Defenders and roll 1d6 each: total ≥10 → solved, 1d6 × 100 GP reward; total <10 → solved anyway but reward halved and one dispatched Defender dies.

**Treasure.** The Bastion acquires an art object or magic item — an inheritance, a guest's or admirer's gift, a theft, or a fortunate discovery:

| 1d100 | Treasure |
|---|---|
| 01–40 | Roll on the 25 GP Art Objects table |
| 41–63 | Roll on the 250 GP Art Objects table |
| 64–73 | Roll on the 750 GP Art Objects table |
| 74–75 | Roll on the 2,500 GP Art Objects table |
| 76–90 | Roll on a Common Magic Items table of your choice (Arcana, Armaments, Implements, or Relics) |
| 91–98 | Roll on an Uncommon Magic Items table of your choice |
| 99–00 | Roll on a Rare Magic Items table of your choice |

## Fall of a Bastion (compressed, p.352)

Three loss modes: **Divestiture** (voluntary abandonment — Bastion is vacated, eventually looted, maybe burned), **Neglect** (no orders for consecutive turns equal to the character's level — hirelings abandon it, site eventually looted; a returning owner can rebuild amid the ruins), **Ruination** (the Ruin card from the Deck of Many Things — player picks the terrible fate: sacked by enemies, destroyed by an earthquake). A replacement Bastion starts fresh per the acquisition table plus two basic facilities (one Cramped, one Roomy). A Bastion Tracker sheet (p.353) records facilities, hirelings, and Defenders.
