*p. 100 MM 2024*

In a gritty, investigation-forward campaign, doppelgangers are the ultimate social predators. They are devious shapeshifters who survive by infiltrating humanoid societies, spreading fear, suspicion, and deceit. Because they are fundamentally hedonistic swindlers, they avoid hard work; they prefer to target wealthy merchants, nobles, or successful adventurers, murdering them and stepping seamlessly into their comfortable lives.

A doppelganger is not merely a physical mimic. It uses innate telepathy to constantly read the surface thoughts of those around it, allowing it to glean names, desires, and fears to perfectly tailor its disguise and interactions.

### Doppelganger

_Medium Monstrosity, Neutral_

- **Armor Class:** 14
- **Hit Points:** 52 (8d8 + 16)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Initiative:** +4 (14)
- **STR:** 11 (+0) | **DEX:** 18 (+4) | **CON:** 14 (+2) | **INT:** 11 (+0) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 14 (+2)
- **Skills:** Deception +6, Insight +3
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** Common plus three other languages
- **Challenge:** 3 (700 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The doppelganger makes two Slam attacks and uses Unsettling Visage if available.
- **Slam:** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +6 (with Advantage during the first round of each combat), reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
- **Read Thoughts:** The doppelganger casts *Detect Thoughts*, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12).
- **Unsettling Visage (Recharge 6):** _Wisdom Saving Throw:_ DC 12, each creature in a 15-foot Emanation originating from the doppelganger that can see the doppelganger. _Failure:_ The target has the Frightened condition and repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically.

**Bonus Actions**

- **Shape-Shift:** The doppelganger shape-shifts into a Medium or Small Humanoid, or it returns to its true form. Its game statistics, other than its size, are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.

### 2024 Mechanical Variants (Escalations)

To keep veteran players on their toes, you can introduce advanced doppelganger castes that have honed their murderous craft.

- **The Greater Doppelganger (Mirrorkin):** A terrifyingly perfect infiltrator. Unlike a common doppelganger, a greater doppelganger absorbs the complete mind, personality, and memories of a victim by consuming its brain. _Mechanical Adjustment:_ It can maintain up to eight distinct, permanent identities at once, retaining all of the victim's proficiencies, class features, and even spells up to 2nd level when wearing that specific form.
- **The Doppelganger Assassin:** A specialized killer that uses its psychic abilities to erase itself from a victim's perception. _Mechanical Adjustment:_ Challenge Rating increases to 8. Wields a dagger (+13 to hit, 1d4+5 damage, deals an extra 2d6 damage with Combat Advantage/Sneak Attack). Gains the **Cloud Mind** action: _Close burst 15 ft; +11 vs. Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the doppelganger assassin is completely invisible to the target as long as it sustains concentration, until it attacks, or until it is hit_.

### d6 The Long Con (Encounter Hooks)

Doppelgangers rarely attack head-on unless cornered; they work alone or in small groups to orchestrate elaborate cons. _(Roll or choose one to establish the doppelganger's current operation)._

|d6|The Scheme|Campaign Integration & Complication|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Subverted Noble**|The doppelganger has taken the place of a local merchant or noble. It is keeping the real victim alive and chained in a cellar, visiting them daily to probe their mind and learn how to speak authentically before finally disposing of them.|
|**2**|**The Saboteur**|Hired by an evil faction (like a beholder or a thieves' guild), the doppelganger has orders to trail the PCs. It will attempt to eliminate the newest or most isolated party member, replace them, and sabotage the party's objectives from the inside.|
|**3**|**The Masquerade**|The doppelganger runs a local information network, spy ring, or mercenary company. Because they can wear a dozen different faces, the PCs believe they are dealing with a massive organization, when in reality, the entire guild is just three doppelgangers sharing identities.|
|**4**|**The Changeling's Parent**|Doppelgangers are notoriously lazy parents. The doppelganger assumed an attractive form, seduced a local human, and vanished, leaving the mother to raise its progeny. The child is just now reaching adolescence and discovering its terrifying true nature.|
|**5**|**The "Rescued" Prisoner**|The PCs find a sympathetic prisoner (like a deep gnome or fellow adventurer) in a dungeon. It is actually a doppelganger using its _Read Thoughts_ ability to perfectly mimic the kind of person the PCs would want to rescue, waiting until they are weak to strike.|
|**6**|**The False Mirror**|The PCs enter a room with a massive, ornate mirror. The doppelgangers are hiding behind an illusory wall, perfectly mimicking the PCs' appearances and matching their movements to make the party believe it is just a reflection—until the "reflections" step out and attack.|

### d4 Identifying Tells (The Investigation Layer)

Because doppelganger transformations do not impart memories or duplicate equipment, paranoid PCs can catch them in a lie if they know what to look for.

|d4|The Clue|Investigative Vulnerability|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Wardrobe Cache**|A doppelganger cannot transform the equipment it wears. If the PCs find a hidden stash containing multiple sets of perfectly tailored clothes, guard uniforms, and mundane gear in an otherwise empty lair, a doppelganger is operating nearby.|
|**2**|**The Lead Room**|A doppelganger's _Read Thoughts_ ability is blocked by a thin sheet of lead. If a PC wears a lead-lined helmet or the party conducts an interview in a lead-lined vault, the doppelganger suddenly loses its Advantage on Deception checks and begins making glaring social errors.|
|**3**|**The Untranslated Document**|While a doppelganger can copy a form perfectly, it does not gain the creature's languages or proficiencies. Handing an impersonator a document written in their "native" racial language and asking them to read it aloud will instantly expose the fraud.|
|**4**|**The Death State**|If the PCs suspect an assassination has occurred but cannot find the creature responsible, examining the corpse provides a clue: a doppelganger immediately reverts to its true, hairless, gray-skinned monstrous form the moment it dies.|
