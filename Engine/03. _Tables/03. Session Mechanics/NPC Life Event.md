#npc-life-event
> **PROVISIONAL — Adam spot-check pending.** Rolled per known NPC on revisit-after-absence at
> `elapsed >= 14d` (`[[WORLD-TURN]]` §4) — known faces move through time whether or not the
> player is present to see it. Death or vanishing of a thread-linked NPC auto-mints a successor
> thread (cause-shape / what-remains / who-inherits) — nothing load-bearing disappears without
> leaving a door. Spice-graded d100, Commitment ceiling (66 Grounded · 20 Textured · 9 Strange ·
> 4 Volatile · 1 Mythic). Five band-anchor rows (marked ★) are Adam's approved samples from
> `WORLD-TURN.md` §7, included verbatim; the rest extend their register. **2026-07-03 Effect-column
> pass** (ADAM-REVIEW-1 §1 / ADAM-REVIEW-2 §7C): added the trailing `Effect` column (closed
> vocabulary, matching Place Drift) and rebound the 8 undefined-referent rows (70/73/76/77/78/84/86/99)
> so each now names a real, mintable referent (the world's own factions) instead of an unnameable
> "someone"/"something."
