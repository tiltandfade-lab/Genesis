# 5-Step Quest Generation Procedure (d10 Robust Version)

Use when you need momentum with depth.
Roll to ignite. Refine deliberately.

---

## Step 1 – Objective & Stakes

### 1A. Objective (d10)
1. Recover a lost relic
2. Destroy a corrupted presence
3. Rescue or retrieve a missing person
4. Investigate strange activity
5. Seal or stabilize a magical anomaly
6. Negotiate with a hostile force
7. Escort something dangerous to safety
8. Prevent a ritual from completing
9. Claim a site before a rival faction does
10. Survive and escape a hostile location

### 1B. Stakes (d10)
1. Local villagers in danger
2. Religious or factional conflict
3. Environmental corruption spreading
4. Valuable trade route threatened
5. Ominous omen tied to larger arc
6. Personal connection to a PC
7. Political instability
8. Something ancient begins awakening
9. A fragile peace will collapse
10. A contained horror may be released

Write 2–3 sentences combining results.

---

## Step 2 – Initial Obstacle (Access Gate)

### 2A. Barrier Type (d10)
1. Travel hazard
2. Territorial creatures
3. Social resistance
4. Environmental instability
5. Magical ward
6. Rival adventuring party
7. Corrupted wildlife
8. Haunted terrain
9. Locked mechanism requiring clues
10. Faction checkpoint or blockade

### 2B. Pressure Twist (d10)
1. Time-sensitive
2. Resource drain (light, water, rations)
3. Noise attracts danger
4. Moral complication
5. Hidden observer
6. Escalating weather
7. Magical interference
8. Structural collapse risk
9. Unstable ally
10. Something is following them

Build 1 meaningful complication or encounter here.

---

## Step 3 – Core Challenge (Design With Intent)

Roll if stuck. Then refine manually.

### 3A. Core Type (d10)
1. Boss combat
2. Multi-wave defense
3. Ritual interruption
4. Environmental survival
5. Social confrontation
6. Puzzle under pressure
7. Corruption containment
8. Split-party scenario
9. Chase through hazardous terrain
10. Protect an object while it activates

### 3B. Environmental Modifier (d10)
1. Rising water
2. Collapsing structure
3. Darkness/magical obscurity
4. Rotting unstable floor
5. Necrotic aura
6. Wild magic bleed
7. Extreme cold or heat
8. Unpredictable magical pulses
9. Toxic spores or fumes
10. Shifting terrain (walls, water levels, platforms)

Design this scene deliberately.
This is the engine room.

---

## Step 4 – Revelation

### 4A. Truth Revealed (d10)
1. Quest giver lied
2. Evil is contained, not active
3. A greater force is manipulating events
4. The threat was summoned accidentally
5. The relic is sentient
6. The site is part of something larger
7. The “monster” is guarding something worse
8. The corruption is recent, not ancient
9. Someone is feeding the threat
10. The threat is trying to escape, not attack

Write one reframing sentence.
Decide whether it changes the ending or seeds a future arc.

---

## Step 5 – Final Confrontation / Resolution

### 5A. Resolution Form (d10)
1. Climactic fight
2. Desperate escape
3. Moral decision
4. Bargain with dangerous entity
5. Sealing ritual
6. Collapse with treasure scramble
7. Public revelation with political fallout
8. Containment but not destruction
9. Sacrifice required
10. Apparent victory with subtle lingering consequence

Resolve based on Step 4.

---
