### **XII. The Hanged Man: Trigger Effects**

The Hanged Man represents sacrifice, suspension, and the power of letting go. It is the card of the paradox—where victory is found through surrender and the world is best understood by looking at it upside down. Under this card, the dungeon becomes a place of stillness, reversal, and shifting perspectives.

---

**The Constant: "The Weight of Perspective"**

The Hanged Man flips the logic of combat. Action is hindered, but contemplation is empowered.

- **The Effect:** Any creature that takes the **Ready** or **Dodge** action gains a **+4 bonus** to their next attack roll or ability check.
    
- **The Toll:** "The Blood Rush." Being right-side up feels unnatural. Any creature that moves more than half its speed in a single turn must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution save or be **Poisoned** (representing vertigo and nausea) until the start of its next turn.

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

**The Constant: "The Great Wait"**

The faction has ceased all aggression. They have entered a state of radical martyrdom or "Passive Resistance," making them impossible to fight with traditional weapons.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**Faction Behavior (The Hanged Man)**|**Narrative Implication**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Hunger Strike**|The faction’s members refuse to eat, work, or fight until their demands are met. The populace is starting to pity them.|
|**6-9**|Less Grounded|**The Inverted Spy**|The faction begins sending "Spies" who do nothing but watch and report the _good_ things the party does.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Time-Freeze**|The faction magically "pauses" their entire city in time. They are statues until a specific "sacrifice" is made by the players.|

### **Trigger: Accepting Binding Agreements**

**The Constant: "The Price of Stillness"**

The bargain grants you power through sacrifice. You gain a powerful ability, but you can only use it while you are **Restrained, Incapacitated, or Prone**.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**The Bargain's Mutation (The Hanged Man)**|**The "Gift"**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Martyr’s Vitality**|You can take damage intended for an ally. In exchange, you gain 1 extra Hit Die during every short rest.|
|**6-9**|Less Grounded|**The Out-of-Body View**|You can see through the eyes of any creature within 100ft, but your own body falls into a catatonic coma while doing so.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Inverted Soul**|You are technically "Dead," but your spirit continues to pilot your body. You are immune to all physiological needs, but you smell like a corpse.|

### **Trigger: World Shifts**

**The Constant: "The Great Inversion"**

The values of the world flip. The poor become wealthy, the powerful lose their influence, and the very laws of physics begin to behave "backwards."

| **d10** | **Magnitude** | **The Nature of the Shift (The Hanged Man)** | **World Change**                                                                                                 |
| ------- | ------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1-5** | Grounded      | **The Seasonal Flip**                        | Winter and Summer swap places instantly. Crops fail, and the world must adapt to a "Frozen Summer."              |
| **6-9** | Less Grounded | **The Reversed Sun**                         | The sun now rises in the West and sets in the East. Time feels like it is moving backward for 1 hour every day.  |
| **10**  | **Insane**    | **The Gravity Failure**                      | For 1 hour a day, gravity simply stops. Everything not bolted down floats into the sky until the "Wait" is over. |
