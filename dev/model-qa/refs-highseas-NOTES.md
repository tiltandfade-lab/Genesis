# refs-highseas-NOTES — the high-seas artist brief (Wave 0, 2026-07-08)

Distilled from 8 Wikimedia Commons references (Howard Pyle pirate plates ×4; MET tricorne
DP-642-001; RMG naval-captain portrait BHC4242; Ryder's *Flying Dutchman*; a naval cutlass
close-up). Images viewed then DELETED per Adam's standing rule — these notes are the artifact.

## What reads at miniature scale

- **The SASH is the pirate's identity mass.** In every Pyle plate the wide waist-sash (crimson)
  is the single loudest color block on the figure — wrapped broad (waist to hip), knotted, with a
  pistol/dagger shoved through it. At distance the red sash reads before the face does. Author it
  as a proud (slightly larger-radius) band, hot accent hex, one knot-tail quad.
- **Loose shirt = a billowed LIGHT mass; slops = a pale bell.** Sailcloth shirt balloons at the
  chest/sleeves; wide "petticoat" slops flare like a skirt and stop at MID-CALF — thin bare
  ankles/flat shoes below. The deckhand silhouette is: narrow head → billowed torso → cinched
  sash → flared slops → sticks for ankles.
- **Bandana = a bright skull-knob with a nape tail.** Pyle's crews at distance are rows of
  red/white head-dots. One dome band over the crown + a small trailing knot quad at the back
  of the neck sells it.
- **Tricorn = a dark TRIANGLE plate with a light rim, point forward.** The brim folds up into
  three flat cocks; from above it's a triangle, front point over the face; edge trimmed pale
  (braid). The crown barely shows — at mini scale the hat is the brim, not the crown.
- **Cutlass: the BOWL GUARD is the identifier, more than the curve.** Short, broad, shallow-curve
  blade; a huge shell/bowl guard swallowing the whole fist. Author a fist-sized guard knob +
  a stubby wide blade with a slight sweep — that's the read.
- **Captain = a dark coat COLUMN framing a light center.** Knee-length navy coat hangs open:
  pale waistcoat/breeches make a bright vertical column down the middle, coat tails flare behind
  the legs, brass button rows dot the lapel edges, white stock at the throat. Value structure
  (dark frame / light core) is the entire read; the tricorn tops it.
- **Baldric/cross-belt is the cheapest rank signal** — one diagonal strap across the chest
  reads at any distance (Pyle's captains carry it; mooks don't).
- **The drowned read is VALUE + SLUMP, not detail.** Ryder's Dutchman figures are waterlogged
  dark lumps melted into the swell under a sickly green-amber glow. So: brine-pale grey-green
  skin, cloth darkened to kelp tones, posture slumped (head dropped, shoulders caved), kelp/rag
  streamers hanging off the arms — silhouette sags where the living sailor billows.
- **Palette anchor (BLENDER-MODEL-SPEC §4.2 high-seas, author-ready under the 0.85-sat /
  #3c7888-tint grade):** tar hull `#2c2a26`, sailcloth `#d0c6a8`, brine oak `#7a5f3e`, navy coat
  `#2e3a56`, sea teal `#3c7888`, rope hemp `#a89060`, brass `#b08d46`, blood `#a3241e`. Accents
  authored HOT — the grade halves chroma.
