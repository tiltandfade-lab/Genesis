# ADVENTURE: The Devouring Relic (v1.5)

![[Pasted image 20260311122537.png]]

## Node 1: The Prime Cut

##### Header

- **Title:** Node 1: The Prime Cut
- **One-line use:** A high-end butcher shop serves as the false front for a subterranean death cult, but a clogged body chute and a paranoid owner threaten to expose the operation.
- **Adventure Promise:** The party investigates a pristine storefront where an artisan covers up damning graffiti and a drunk babbles the truth. Bypassing the paranoid butcher leads to a meat locker flooded with gore. They must brave the hazardous disposal chute or unlock the secret lift hidden in the exotic meats closet to descend into the cult's underworks.
- **Adventure Scale:** Levels 3–4
- **Tags:** Urban, Civic District, Investigation, Social, Stealth, Cult
- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Pressure Level:** Low/Medium (Driven by social tension and NPC paranoia; the butcher will not call the guards for fear of exposing his own crimes).
- **Rewards & Consequences:**
    - _Rewards:_ Access to the cult's underground supply chain, an alliance with a magical investigator.
    - _Consequences:_ Triggering the gargoyle's assassination protocols, alerting the cult below to the party's infiltration.

##### The Set Up

- **Basic Narrative:** "The Prime Cut" is an elite butcher shop. The ground floor is a bustling retail space. In the back alley, heavy double doors receive shipments of livestock and, occasionally, trafficked humanoids. The back room contains a massive, 5-foot-wide disposal chute used to dump whole animal carcasses and bodies down to the cult below. Because the cult is currently using the lower levels for a massive ritual, this chute is clogged, and the back room is flooding with bloody sludge. The paranoid Butcher, Kinto, is panicking. Outside, a hired artisan covers up freshly painted graffiti ("The Nobility Are Eating Us"), a drunken vagabond babbles the same phrase, and a Sapphire Circle investigator sweeps the street with _Locate Object_. A Gargoyle perched on the roof silently acts as the cult's surveillance.
- **Why the PCs Care:** This is the primary intake hub for the cult. The PCs must navigate the street-level clues and the horrific back room to access Node 2.
- **Pre-roll Tables to Define Scene:**
    - **d6 The "Exotic Meats" Closet Contents (Variant Table):**
        1. A heavy iron cage containing a living Rust Monster.
        2. Crates of alchemical preserving fluid that causes hallucinations if imbibed.
        3. A ledger detailing the exact cuts of humanoid meat preferred by specific nobles.
        4. A crate of perfectly preserved humanoid hands packed in salt.
        5. Several vials of _Oil of Taggit_ (used to sedate kidnappings).
        6. A silver signet ring belonging to a missing magistrate.
- **Define Maps and Tables Used:** The street level features a clean, 30-foot-wide cobblestone road and the shop's facade. The front room is a 20x20 pristine storefront. A heavy locked oak door leads to the 30x30 stone-walled Meat Locker in the back, which connects to the alley via massive loading doors. Off the main locker is a 10x10 Walk-In Closet for "exotic meats."
- **Starting NPCs:** Kinto the Butcher (Paranoid Cultist), 1 Artisan, 1 Babbling Vagabond, 1 Investigator (The Tracker), 1 Gargoyle (Watcher).

##### Segment Descriptions

###### Segment 1: The Pristine Facade & The Alley

- **Narrative of segment and play flow:** The PCs arrive at the shop. They must piece together the environmental clues to realize the shop is a front, then figure out how to gain access to the back rooms.
- **What the PCs notice immediately:** A clean street. A refugee artisan pressing glass tiles over jagged red paint. A drunk muttering near a shrine. A figure sweeping a glowing crystal back and forth. A stone gargoyle on the shop's eaves. A muddy alley leading to heavy loading doors.
- **What does the DM read aloud?** The Civic District is immaculate, but the atmosphere outside "The Prime Cut" butcher shop is decidedly tense. A weary-looking artisan is frantically pressing a beautiful glass mosaic over the shop's exterior wall, trying to cover up jagged red graffiti. You can just make out the remaining words: "ARE EATING US."

Near a pristine corner shrine, a disheveled vagabond slumps against the stone, quietly and drunkenly babbling that exact same phrase to himself. Meanwhile, a figure in a dark cloak paces the street, waving a glowing crystal focus back and forth. Above it all, perched on the shop's eaves, a stone gargoyle silently watches the street.

- **Skill Checks/revelations:**
    - _DC 13 Wisdom (Perception):_ Notice that the Gargoyle's stone eyes are physically tracking the Investigator's movements.
    - _DC 14 Wisdom (Insight):_ Looking through the shop window, the PCs see the Butcher sweating profusely and constantly glancing toward the back room. He is terrified.
    - _DC 15 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand):_ Pick the heavy locks on the back alley loading doors to enter the meat locker directly.
- **PC likely choices:** Coordinate with the Investigator; walk into the front of the shop to intimidate Kinto; sneak around to the alley.
- **Escalation and pressure keys:** The Gargoyle is under strict orders not to break its _False Appearance_ unless someone violently breaks into the shop or attempts to murder the Butcher in broad daylight.
- **The Tracker's Branching Logic (Continuity Secret):** If PCs arrived from the Sewers (Node 3 first), the Tracker’s spell locks onto them because they are carrying Vesper’s gear, making the Tracker an immediate ally. If starting on the surface, the Tracker is a quest-giver looking for Vesper.

###### Segment 2: The Meat Locker & The Chute

- **Narrative of segment and play flow:** The PCs enter the back room. The horror of the cult is revealed by the failing plumbing. The PCs must choose their descent: brave the clogged body chute or find the key to the secret door.
- **What the PCs notice immediately:** The smell of blood and alchemical preservative. Heavy iron meat hooks hang from ceiling tracks. A 5-foot-wide iron chute in the floor is violently bubbling over with offal. A heavy, locked iron door leads to a walk-in cooling closet.
- **What does the DM read aloud?** The air in the meat locker is chilled and thick with the smell of old blood. Heavy iron meat hooks hang from tracks on the ceiling, bearing massive, butchered animal carcasses.

However, something is wrong with the plumbing. A massive, five-foot-wide disposal chute in the center of the stone floor is clogged. It is violently bubbling, slowly flooding the room with a foul, rotting sludge of animal fat and offal. Kinto the Butcher stands near a heavy iron door labeled 'Exotic Cuts', his apron stained, his hands shaking as he grips a meat cleaver.

- **Skill Checks/revelations:**
    - _DC 11 Constitution Saving Throw:_ Made by anyone entering the room. On a failure, the stench of the backed-up chute causes them to be _Poisoned_ for 10 minutes.
    - _DC 14 Charisma (Intimidation):_ Break Kinto's resolve. He drops the cleaver, hands over the key to the Exotic Meats closet, and begs for his life.
    - _DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation):_ Search the Walk-In Closet. Behind the hanging meats, a hidden keyhole requires Kinto's key to open the secret door to a clean lift descending to Node 2.
    - _DC 13 Dexterity Saving Throw:_ Required to jump down the clogged Body Chute without taking 2d6 bludgeoning damage.
- **PC likely choices:** Interrogate Kinto for the key, search the closet, or jump down the chute.
- **Escalation and pressure keys:** The floor is Difficult Terrain. If blood is spilled in the sludge, 1d4 Swarms of Rats erupt from the clogged body chute on Initiative count 10.

##### Clues & Treasures

- **The Animal Carcasses:** A DC 13 Wisdom (Medicine) check reveals these are legitimate animal carcasses. The cult usually feeds animals to the stone; humanoids are only used for special rituals.
- **The Tracker's Focus:** A mundane crystal that acts as a spellcasting focus, glowing via _Locate Object_.
- **Kinto's Key:** A heavy, jagged iron key unlocking the Exotic Meats closet, the secret lift, and the cages in Node 2.

##### NPCs & Stat Blocks

- **Kinto the Butcher:** Goal: Maintain his cover and survive. Leverage: Extreme cowardice. _Use 2024 Cult Fanatic._
- **Gargoyle:** _Use 2024 Gargoyle._
- **Swarm of Rats:** _Use 2024 Swarm of Rats._

## Node 2: The Holding Pens

![[Pasted image 20260311122820.png]]

##### Header

- **Title:** Node 2: The Holding Pens
- **One-line use:** A noxious underground staging area where cultists process bodies, hiding a vengeful captive, a deadly slime hazard, and a stash of skimmed loot.
- **Adventure Promise:** Descending into a mud-slicked slaughterhouse, the party discovers a caged teenager who warns them the cultists are right next door. To ambush the arguing thugs, the party must navigate hanging green slime, arm the captive, and silently operate a massive counterweight lift.
- **Pressure Level:** Low (No active enemies in the room, allowing the party to investigate, but making noise will ruin their chance to ambush the thugs in Node 3).

##### The Set Up

- **Basic Narrative:** The PCs arrive at the bottom of the butcher's body chute or secret lift. This grim industrial holding pen is used to transfer heavy loads into the main aqueduct (Node 3). The air is thick with noxious fumes, and the floor is coated in deep, bloody mud. A rusted iron cage holds Vulfio, a 15-year-old street kid. Vulfio explains that an invisible spy in blue robes (Vesper) fell down the chute days ago, hid in the shadows, and finally snuck into the corpse wagon right before the thugs rolled it out just moments ago. A patch of deadly _Green Slime_ hangs from the ceiling above the heavy counterweight winch needed to open the blocked passage forward.
- **Pre-roll Tables to Define Scene:**
    - **d6 Vulfio's Chalk Drawings (Variant Clue Table):**
        1. A crude drawing of a massive, jagged egg with teeth.
        2. Stick figures of the thugs prying gold teeth from the corpses.
        3. A drawing of the wagon, with a faint blue outline of a person hiding inside it.
        4. A warning: a stick figure melting under a green blob near the door.
        5. Tally marks counting the dozens of bodies sent down this week.
        6. A surprisingly accurate map of the Aqueduct (Node 3).
- **Starting NPCs:** Vulfio (Vengeful Teenager).

##### Segment Descriptions

###### Segment 1: The Stench and The Stash

- **Narrative of segment and play flow:** The PCs drop into the room. They must immediately deal with the horrific stench and the difficult terrain. Investigation reveals the stash of loot the thugs have been secretly skimming.
- **What the PCs notice immediately:** A choking, noxious stench. Thick, bloody mud. Hanging animal carcasses swaying on ceiling tracks.
- **What does the DM read aloud?** Whether you took the clean lift or tumbled down the slick disposal chute, you arrive in a foul, dimly lit industrial holding pen. The choking reek of preserving chemicals and rotting meat hits you immediately.

The stone floor is coated in a thick, clinging mud of blood and offal. Hanging from iron tracks on the groaning ceiling are dozens of massive animal carcasses. The room appears abandoned, but fresh, heavy wagon ruts in the mud lead toward a lowered iron portcullis on the south wall.

- **Skill Checks/revelations:**
    - _DC 11 Constitution Saving Throw:_ Required upon entering. On a failure, the character is _Poisoned_ by the fumes for 1 hour.
    - _DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation):_ Searching the mud uncovers a loose stone concealing a rusted iron box (the thugs' skim stash).
- **Escalation and pressure keys:** The thick mud counts as Difficult Terrain. Any creature knocked _Prone_ in the mud must use _all_ of its movement to stand up, rather than half.

###### Segment 2: The Vengeful Teen and The Slime

- **Narrative of segment and play flow:** Moving deeper, the PCs discover Vulfio. To pursue the wagon, they must operate the winch, but a deadly hazard guards the mechanism.
- **What the PCs notice immediately:** A rusted iron cage holding a scowling teenager. Chalk drawings on the wall. A complex counterweight lift system. Voices echoing from the other side of the bars.
- **What does the DM read aloud?** Pushing through the hanging carcasses, you spot a rusted iron cage in the corner. Inside is a fifteen-year-old boy covered in grime, gripping the bars with white knuckles.

"Get me out of here," he whispers fiercely, pointing to the massive iron portcullis. "The scum that run this place just rolled the corpse wagon through there. Give me a sword, and I'll help you gut them."

If you listen closely, you can hear echoing voices from the tunnel beyond. Two men are violently arguing over dropped jewelry. To reach them, you'll need to use the rusted counterweight winch—but a massive, quivering patch of translucent green slime hangs directly over the mechanism.

- **Skill Checks/revelations:**
    - _DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation):_ Figure out how to balance the counterweights to silently operate the winch without the rusted gears shrieking.
    - _DC 15 Strength (Athletics):_ Bend the bars of Vulfio's cage to free him without the Butcher's keys.
- **Escalation and pressure keys:**
    - _Green Slime Drop (Hazard):_ If a character operates the winch without removing the slime, they must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw to dodge, or take 2d8 Acid damage.
    - _The Noise Penalty:_ Failing the Investigation check or smashing the portcullis alerts the arguing thugs in Node 3, negating Surprise.
    - _The Wheelbarrow Timer (Secret):_ If the PCs spend more than 10 minutes in this room, two thugs from Node 3 open the portcullis to retrieve wheelbarrows, initiating combat at the gate.

##### Clues & Treasures

- **Vesper's Timeline:** Vulfio explains an invisible "blue man" fell down the chute days ago, hid, and just slipped into the corpse wagon.
- **The Thugs' Skim Stash:** 12 pieces of hematite (10 gp each), 8 pieces of malachite (10 gp each), a set of ivory dice (8 gp), 2 silver cups (12 gp each), and a handful of bloody gold teeth (15 gp).

##### NPCs & Stat Blocks

- **Vulfio (Vengeful Captive):** Goal: Kill the thugs and escape. Leverage: He knows the layout. _Use 2024 Bandit if given a weapon._
- **Green Slime (Hazard):** Drops from ceiling. DC 13 Dex save. 2d8 Acid damage, and 2d8 at the start of each turn until scraped off (requires an Action) or destroyed by cold/fire/radiant damage.

## Node 3: The Illuminated Ambush

![[Pasted image 20260311123028.png]]

##### Header

- **Title:** Node 3: The Illuminated Ambush
- **One-line use:** A bottleneck encounter where a massive corpse wagon is stuck in the mud, causing an argument between cult thugs and trapping an invisible spy.
- **Pressure Level:** High (Combat engagement is immediate; the thugs have cover and the high ground if the party is spotted).

##### The Set Up

- **Basic Narrative:** This massive, vaulted sewer aqueduct connects the upper processing pens (Node 2) to the inner sanctum (Node 4). A draft horse pulling a massive wagon of corpses has become hopelessly stuck in the thick mud. Because the wagon cannot physically fit through the small door to Node 4, five cult thugs are violently arguing about having to carry the bodies by hand. Hiding invisibly within the pile of corpses is Vesper, a Sapphire Circle spy.
- **Pre-roll Tables to Define Scene:**
    - **d4 What fell off the wagon? (Variant Table - The source of the thugs' argument):**
        1. A heavy iron casket spilling stolen silver.
        2. A corpse wearing an expensive, recognizable noble's signet ring.
        3. A sealed urn containing a volatile Alchemist's Fire.
        4. A spell scroll case that broke open, scattering magical parchment in the muck.

##### Segment Descriptions

###### The Ambush & The Reveal

- **Narrative of segment and play flow:** How the PCs enter dictates the start of combat. Once combat begins, Vesper realizes they have allies and drops a massive debuff on the enemies to aid the party.
- **Entry Vectors & Read Aloud:**
    - _If entering silently from Node 2:_ "You raise the portcullis without a sound. Ahead, a massive wooden wagon is sunk to its axles in the sewer mud. Five thugs have their backs to you, screaming at each other over an object dropped in the muck. A draft horse whinnies nervously." (Grants Surprise).
    - _If entering loudly from Node 2:_ "The portcullis shrieks as it rises. The five thugs in the tunnel ahead immediately stop arguing, draw their crossbows, and take cover behind a massive, mud-stuck corpse wagon."
    - _If entering from the Surface (The Chalk Portal):_ "The magical chalk portal dissolves the ceiling, dropping you 20 feet directly into a massive wagon filled with rotting corpses. Five thugs staring at the falling debris draw their weapons in shock." (The Ceiling Collapse hazard occurs naturally in round 2 if PCs didn't enter this way).
- **Escalation and pressure keys:**
    - _Vesper's Reveal:_ On Initiative Count 10 of Round 1, the pile of corpses shifts. Vesper drops their invisibility and casts _Faerie Fire_ (DC 13 Dex save) covering the thugs and the wagon in glowing blue light, shouting, "Take the ones on the left!"
    - _Environmental Hazard (The Chalk Collapse):_ If the PCs entered from Node 2, the unstable ceiling above the wagon collapses on Initiative Count 20 of Round 2. Anyone within 10 feet of the wagon must make a DC 13 Dex save or take 2d6 bludgeoning damage.

##### Clues & Treasures

- **The Dropped Loot:** (Roll on the d4 table above).
- **Vesper's Intel:** Once combat ends, Vesper confirms they are a spy. Vesper explains the inner sanctum is just ahead, but they don't know the layout because they've been stuck in Node 2 for days. Vesper volunteers to walk point to prove their loyalty.

##### NPCs & Stat Blocks

- **Vesper (Sapphire Spy):** _Use 2024 Spy._ (Currently visible, out of spell slots except cantrips).
- **Cult Thugs (5):** _Use 2024 Thug._

## Node 4: The Watcher's Gate

![[Pasted image 20260311123642.png]]

##### Header

- **Title:** Node 4: The Watcher's Gate
- **One-line use:** A lethal marching-order trap tests the party's guide under the gaze of a magical sensor, leading to a tense social encounter with a telepathic aberration and a volatile magical hazard.
- **Pressure Level:** High (The party must manage an injured ally, avoid triggering a secondary ambush, and calm a panicked creature while under active surveillance).

##### The Set Up

- **Basic Narrative:** The PCs follow Vesper into a small, dark, waterlogged chamber that reeks of rot. The ceiling is covered in guano, and paper origami birds hang from the walls. Hovering near the ceiling is a glowing _Clairvoyance_ sensor—the Cult Leader is watching, waiting for the delayed bodies. As the party steps forward, a sunken pressure plate triggers a heavy crossbow trap aimed perfectly at chest-height. To open the heavy iron portcullis leading to Node 5, the PCs must use a rusted winch, but a telepathic Flumph—glowing red with terror—is physically blocking it, desperately trying to warn the PCs about the "the Devouring Relic" below.
- **Pre-roll Tables to Define Scene:**
    - **d4 Origami Trap Trigger Table (Variant Table):**
        1. **Psychic Backlash:** If the Flumph is attacked or intimidated, its panic releases psychic static, animating the paper flock into **1d4+2 Stirges**.
        2. **Spite:** If PCs attempt to blind or dispel the _Clairvoyance_ sensor, the Cult Leader speaks a command word, animating **1d4+2 Stirges**.
        3. **Blood Scent:** The paper bats are painted in ritual blood. If any PC takes piercing/slashing damage in the room, the flock unfolds into **1d4+2 Stirges**.
        4. **Obeisance:** Forcing the iron winch clockwise without a cult medallion causes a loud clank that animates **1d4+2 Stirges**.
- **Starting NPCs:** 1 Flumph, 1d4+2 Stirges (Disguised as origami).

##### Segment Descriptions

###### Segment 1: The Marching Order Trap & The Watcher

- **Narrative of segment and play flow:** As the party enters, the DM asks for the marching order. The character on point triggers the trap while the Cult Leader watches.
- **What does the DM read aloud?** The disturbed dust on the floor leads you into a small, dark, waterlogged chamber that reeks of rotting meat. The stone beneath your boots is slick with water and bat guano, and dozens of strange, folded paper birds hang suspended from the ceiling.

At the exact same moment a sharp mechanical _TWANG_ echoes from a narrow slit in the wall, you notice a faintly glowing, translucent eye hovering near the ceiling, silently watching the room.

- **Escalation and pressure keys:**
    - _The Trap Roll:_ The DM rolls a ranged attack (+6 to hit) against the point character (usually Vesper, proving their loyalty when they take the hit). _Hit:_ 1d10 piercing and 2d10 poison damage.
    - _Slick Surface Hazard:_ Any creature taking damage while standing on this slick guano floor must succeed on a DC 13 Dex saving throw or fall Prone.
    - _The Sensor:_ If the PCs do not obscure the _Clairvoyance_ sensor, the Cult Leader cannot be surprised in Node 5 and gains a +2 bonus to Initiative.

###### Segment 2: The Red Flumph

- **Narrative of segment and play flow:** The PCs move to the winch to open the portcullis. A bizarre creature is clinging to it, projecting warnings directly into their minds.
- **What does the DM read aloud?** Wrapped tightly around the handles of the heavy iron winch is a bizarre creature resembling a floating jellyfish. It is glowing a violent, pulsing red. As you approach, a frantic voice erupts inside your minds. _"No! Do not open! The stone hungers! The dark shell cracks! Death is below!"_
- **Skill Checks/revelations:**
    - _DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion):_ Calm the Flumph. (Advantage if communicating telepathically). It turns green, unwraps from the winch, and grants psychic intel about the Shadow Dragon Egg.
- **Escalation and pressure keys:** Attacking the Flumph triggers its _Stench Spray_ and forces it to flee.

##### Clues & Treasures

- **The Flumph's Warning:** Telepathy confirms the "the Devouring Relic" is actually an unhatched, shadow-corrupted dragon egg.
- **The Trap's Bolt:** The heavy crossbow bolt is coated in Drow poison. Can be applied to one melee weapon; next creature struck must succeed on a DC 13 Con save or be _Poisoned_ for 1 hour.

##### Stat Blocks

- **Flumph:** _Use 2024 Flumph._
- **Stirge:** _Use 2024 Stirge._

## Node 5: The Devouring Relic

![[Pasted image 20260311123956.png]]

##### Header

- **Title:** Node 5: The Devouring Relic
- **One-line use:** A high-pressure boss room where the PCs must stop a synchronized occult ritual that is slowly annihilating the physical environment in a growing sphere of necrotic ash.
- **Pressure Level:** High (Environmental Timer / Trap).

##### The Set Up

- **Basic Narrative:** The PCs enter an oval room lit by flickering lamps. Heavy iron meat hooks hang from the ceiling, bearing the butchered remains of sacrificed animals—though one hook holds a live, whimpering captive. In the center of the room, a jagged black stone (the Dragon Egg) rests upon a pedestal. The Cult Leader and a priest stand at opposite ends of the room, maintaining a synchronized chant. The Devouring Relic pulses, and a sphere of corruption begins eating the floor, the ceiling, and the meat hooks, expanding outward.
- **Pre-roll Tables to Define Scene:**
    - **d6 The Captive (Variant Table - What is on the hook?):**
        1. **The Loyal Mastiff:** A mundane, terrified Mastiff. _Reward:_ A dependable guard dog.
        2. **The Injured Druid:** Appears to be a wounded wolf; reverts to a humanoid Druid if saved.
        3. **The Blink Dog:** An intelligent fey canine. _Reward:_ Can use teleportation to help PCs bypass the ash.
        4. **The Stolen Mount:** An undersized pony in expensive barding. _Reward:_ Massive gold bounty.
        5. **The Innocent Deer:** Uncorrupted nature. _Reward:_ A supernatural boon (_Charm of Animal Friendship_).
        6. **The Weird Sidekick:** A captive Pseudodragon. _Reward:_ A telepathic scout/familiar.
    - **d8 True Nature of the Devouring Relic (Variant Table):** 1-4: The Celestial Tear, Verdant Heart, Echo Stone, or Gravitic Lodestone. **5: Shadow Dragon Egg (Canon for this run):** A petrified dragon egg exposed to the Shadowfell. 6-8: Abyssal Shard, Archfiend's Vessel, or Death Rock.
- **Starting NPCs:** 1 Cult Leader, 1 Cult Priest, 4 Cult Guards, 1 Captive Animal.

##### Segment Descriptions

###### The Ambush & The Timer

- **Narrative of segment and play flow:** The priests chant. A sphere of necrotic corruption expands every round, destroying the floor and dividing the room.
- **What does the DM read aloud?** Heavy iron meat hooks hang from the ceiling, bearing the butchered remains of animals—though one hook near the far wall holds a live, whimpering creature. In the center of the room, a jagged black stone rests upon a pedestal.

Two identical priests take up positions at opposite ends of the room. Holding smaller black stones aloft, they chant in perfect, synchronized unison. The central stone pulses with sickening violet light. The floor around the pedestal instantly turns to gray ash. A spherical sinkhole of corruption begins creeping outward toward the edges of the room.

- **Skill Checks/revelations:**
    - _DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana or Religion):_ Realize the priests are acting as symmetrical conduits; breaking their symmetry, breaking line of sight, or killing them disrupts the ritual.
    - _DC 13 Strength (Athletics):_ Leap over the corrupted floor to unhook the captive without dropping it into the ash.
- **Escalation and pressure keys:**
    - _The Sphere of Annihilation (Hazard):_ Acts on Initiative count 20. Starts at a 10-foot radius and expands by 5 feet each round. Any creature starting its turn in the zone or entering it takes 1d6 necrotic damage and treats it as Difficult Terrain.
    - _The Interruption:_ If a priest takes damage, they must make a Constitution saving throw (as if concentrating on a spell). On a failure, the sphere's expansion pauses for that round.

##### Stat Blocks

- **Cult Leader & Priest (2):** _Use 2024 Cult Fanatic._
- **Cult Guards (4):** _Use 2024 Thug._ (They actively Shove/Grapple PCs into the ash zone).

## Node 6: The Velvet Vestry

![[Pasted image 20260311124202.png]]

##### Header

- **Title:** Node 6: The Velvet Vestry
- **One-line use:** An opulent preparation room where the cult's wealthy patrons don their masks, hiding a secret stairwell up to a noble's wine cellar and an impossibly heavy treasure hoard.
- **Pressure Level:** Low/Variable (The new pressure is logistical: capturing the fleeing noble and smuggling 200+ pounds of stolen silver out of the heavily patrolled Civic District).

##### The Set Up

- **Basic Narrative:** Directly connected to the rear of Node 5, this worked-stone chamber is an opulent robing room. It smells strongly of expensive perfume meant to mask the scent of blood. the local lord, the cult's primary financial backer, is here in a panic. Hearing the carnage, he is frantically packing the cult's ledgers and fleeing up a secret spiral staircase hidden behind a false wardrobe. The stairs lead to his heavily guarded surface estate. Vane is a coward who will trade the contents of his hidden vault for his life.
- **Pre-roll Tables to Define Scene:**
    - **d6 The Cult Masks (Variant Table):**
        1. Bone mask carved into an eyeless frog.
        2. Gilded tiger mask with actual feline fangs.
        3. Heavy iron mask depicting a weeping skeletal child.
        4. Rat mask crafted from stitched human leather.
        5. Cracked porcelain mask of a five-jawed skull.
        6. Featureless oval of black glass that adheres to the face.
- **Starting NPCs:** the local lord (Cult Paymaster), 2 Private Guards.

##### Segment Descriptions

###### Segment 1: The Perfumed Antechamber

- **Narrative of segment and play flow:** The PCs enter, catch the local lord packing evidence, and must stop him from escaping.
- **What does the DM read aloud?** Stepping through the heavy stone doors, the grime of the underground vanishes. You stand in an opulent, brilliantly lit dressing chamber. Plush rugs cover the stone floor, and the air is thick with a nauseating mix of lilac perfume and old blood.

Near a row of mahogany wardrobes, a pale, wealthy man is frantically stuffing velvet robes and a heavy ledger into a sack. Two armored guards stand nearby. The noble drops a gold-leaf mask and screams, "Kill them! Buy me time!"

- **Skill Checks/revelations:**
    - _DC 13 Wisdom (Insight):_ Vane is a coward. If his guards fall, he will surrender.
    - _DC 14 Dexterity (Initiative):_ The PCs must beat Vane's initiative to prevent him from Disengaging and reaching the secret door on his first turn.

###### Segment 2: The Wardrobe Stairwell & The Vault

- **Narrative of segment and play flow:** Vane attempts to buy his life with his hidden vault located at the top of the stairs.
- **What does the DM read aloud?** The middle wardrobe's back panel is swung open, revealing a narrow spiral staircase winding sharply upward.

If cornered, the pale noble falls to his knees. "Take the ledger! I have gold in my cellar! I'll open the vault for you, just don't kill me!"

- **Escalation and pressure keys (The Logistical Trap):** If the PCs access the vault in Vane's cellar, they find a massive hoard. However, the 7,000 silver pieces alone weigh 140 pounds, and the gold weighs 42 pounds. Vane's estate is in the pristine Civic District; walking out with sacks of jingling coins will immediately attract the City Watch.

##### Clues & Treasures

- **The Ledger (Secret):** Details the financial transactions. However, the notes are amateurish—it becomes clear these nobles are foolish dabblers who had absolutely no idea what the Dragon Egg actually was.
- **Vane's Vault (Treasure Hoard):** Hidden behind a false wine cask. Contains: 2,100 gp, 7,000 sp, 105 pp, 2 Art Objects (250gp each), _Potion of Healing (Greater)_, _Scroll of Invisibility_.

##### NPCs & Stat Blocks

- **the local lord:** _Use 2024 Noble._ (Relies entirely on guards; takes Dodge/Disengage actions).
- **Private Guards (2):** _Use 2024 Guard._
