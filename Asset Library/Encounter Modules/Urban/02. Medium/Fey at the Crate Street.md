## 30-Second Run

A carved doorway on a dim street has drawn a crowd, and the bottleneck is making movement impossible. In the dim light, a glamour-touched fey is here — working on something, not waiting to fight. A crate stack breaks sightlines. A door is wedged open with a scorched wedge. Someone tripped a tripwire before the party arrived. A crank handle missing its pin hints at a mechanism no one's operated yet.

---

## Setup

**Space:** A straight, open street narrower than expected. Dim light. A stack of crates on one side. A long carved doorway lintel that has drawn spectators — the street is jammed.
**Atmosphere:** Water drips somewhere. Sooty candle bundle by the wedged-open door. The crowd murmur is louder than it should be for the space. Carved stone faces on the doorway, worn smooth.
**NPCs Present:** Fey Presence (distracted, occupied with food or a task — not yet engaged with the party), Old Master Carver (working at the doorway, ignoring the crowd), Crowd (spectators, bottleneck).
**If Ignored:** The fey finishes its task and leaves. The snapped tripwire is never examined. The mechanism is never operated. The access point stays hidden.

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Crowd and the Fey

The fey — a minor glamour-touched presence — is occupied: eating, repairing something, or quietly observing the crowd. It has no immediate interest in the party but will react if cornered or approached quickly.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 14 Perception:** Spot the fey among the crowd shadows. It's partially obscured by crates and dim light.
- **DC 15 Insight:** The fey isn't hostile — it's distracted. It notices the party before they notice it, but doesn't consider them a threat yet.
- **DC 13 Persuasion or Animal Handling (approaching calmly):** Make contact without triggering a defensive reaction.

**Escalation:** Moving fast, drawing weapons, or calling attention to the fey in the crowd triggers a chase or fey mischief. The crowd reacts unpredictably.

---

### Segment 2: What Came Before

A snapped tripwire hangs from an iron fitting near the doorway. It was triggered before the party arrived.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 12 Perception:** Notice the snapped wire — frayed end, slightly warm iron.
- **DC 13 Investigation:** The wire was connected to the crank mechanism. Whatever it controlled — a gate, a bell, a latch — fired already. Someone came through here first.
- **DC 13 Investigation (crank handle):** The pin is missing. The mechanism requires improvised repair before it can be used again. A bent nail or wire from a nearby fence will do.

**Escalation:** The missing-pin mechanism, once repaired and operated, opens a concealed access panel in the wall behind the crate stack — a hidden route or storage space. The fey knows this is here and may react if the party gets close.

---

### Segment 3: The Crowd Bottleneck

The carved doorway has created a 10-ft wide pinch point. Anyone trying to move through quickly takes a crowd penalty.

**Mechanics:** Moving through the crowd at full speed requires DC 10 Athletics or DC 10 Acrobatics. Failure: movement halved. High ground — a wall bracket, a crate stack ledge — bypasses the crowd entirely and provides tactical advantage.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Dim Light:** Disadvantage on Perception checks that rely on sight throughout the scene.
2. **Crate Stack:** Provides half cover. Climbing to the top (DC 10 Athletics) gives high ground and a clear view over the crowd.
3. **Scorched Door Wedge:** The wedge has been burned before — not recently. The open door leads to a dim corridor (smell of old smoke and wax).
4. **Snapped Tripwire:** Frayed wire — already triggered. The alarm is gone. But whoever tripped it is somewhere ahead.
5. **Missing-Pin Crank:** The mechanism requires one action to improvise a pin (nail, wire) and one action to operate. Opens a concealed niche behind the crates.

## Difficulty Dial

**Tier 1 (Lvl 1–3):** Fey is a Sprite or Pixie — mischievous, not dangerous. Turns hostile only if attacked.
**Tier 2 (Lvl 4–7):** Fey is a Dryad or Satyr with fey charm abilities. May try to redirect the party away from the concealed access point.
**Tier 3 (Lvl 8+):** The fey is a Green Hag in disguise. The "prior intruder" is dead in the hidden niche.
