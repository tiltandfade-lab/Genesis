## Urban Lighting — d50

| d50 | Lighting State | Minor Variation (flavor prompt) |
|---:|----------------|----------------------------------|
| 1-10 | Bright | Clear light from daylight, open windows, or multiple sources. |
| 11-25 | Dim | The area is lit, but details are easy to miss. |
| 26-33 | Shadowed | Light is present, but strong shadows break sightlines. |
| 34-39 | Flickering | Firelight, reflected light, or unstable light shifts constantly. |
| 40-43 | Localized | Light is strong in one part of the area and weak elsewhere. |
| 44-46 | Dark | There is little to no useful light here. |
| 47-48 | Filtered | Light passes through smoke, mist, cloth, or dirty glass. |
| 49 | Unusual | The light draws attention for some reason. |
| 50 | Uncanny | The light behaves in a way that feels wrong. |
^urban-lighting
