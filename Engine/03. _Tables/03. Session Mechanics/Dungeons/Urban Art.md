# Urban Street Art Generator

Roll once on each table, or choose results that fit the district.

---

## 1. Local Art Medium (d10)
*What does the art actually look like in the street?*

| d10 | Medium & Location |
|---:|---|
| 1 | **Chalk Mural:** A temporary mural drawn directly onto cobblestones or paving stones. |
| 2 | **Painted Shop Sign:** A business sign embellished with extra images, symbols, or caricatures. |
| 3 | **Scrap Sculpture:** A figure or structure made from discarded metal, wood, tools, or cart parts. |
| 4 | **Magical Watercolor:** Glowing or shifting paint applied to a wall, shutter, or plaster surface. |
| 5 | **Paper Flock:** Folded paper shapes strung between buildings, fences, or poles. |
| 6 | **Glass & Tile Mosaic:** Broken glass, pottery, and colored fragments pressed into mortar or plaster. |
| 7 | **Carved Doorway:** Decorative carving added to a lintel, post, threshold, or doorframe. |
| 8 | **Political Broadside:** An illustrated poster, print, or pasted sheet mocking a figure or faction. |
| 9 | **Woven Gate Piece:** Dyed cloth, cord, or ribbon woven through bars, fencing, or latticework. |
| 10 | **Illusory Loop:** A repeating minor illusion hovering in an alley, arch, doorway, or window. |
^urban-art-medium

## 2. Local Artist (d10)
*Who made this, and what are they like if the party meets them?*

| d10 | The Artist |
|---:|---|
| 1 | **The Urchin:** A street kid with real talent and very questionable methods of getting supplies. |
| 2 | **The Disgruntled Apprentice:** A guild-trained artisan tired of making respectable work for respectable people. |
| 3 | **The Illusionist Performer:** A minor mage, bard, or entertainer testing visual tricks in public. |
| 4 | **The Obsessive:** Someone compelled to keep making images tied to a feeling, memory, or place. |
| 5 | **The Activist:** A masked local using art to expose corruption, shame power, or rally sympathy. |
| 6 | **The Old Master:** An elder artist quietly working in public, known by some and ignored by many. |
| 7 | **The Fence:** A criminal using artistic marks as codes for safehouses, routes, or exchanges. |
| 8 | **The Seer:** A strange figure whose work may be prophetic, symbolic, or half-mad. |
| 9 | **The Refugee:** A displaced outsider preserving stories, gods, or customs from a lost homeland. |
| 10 | **The Charlatan:** A fraud, hustler, or self-invented genius selling junk as priceless art or relics. |
^urban-art-artist

## 3. Immediate Encounter (d10)
*What is happening right now around the art?*

| d10 | Event / In Media Res |
|---:|---|
| 1 | **In the Act:** The artist is still working, with tools and materials spread around them. |
| 2 | **Guard Harassment:** Watchmen, wardens, or hired enforcers are trying to stop or remove it. |
| 3 | **Hidden Clue:** The piece contains a hidden message, symbol, or directional clue. |
| 4 | **The Critic:** A wealthy local, merchant, priest, or official is loudly condemning the work. |
| 5 | **The Spectacle:** A crowd has gathered, clogging the street and turning the site into a bottleneck. |
| 6 | **The Vandal:** Someone is actively defacing, tearing down, or painting over the piece. |
| 7 | **The Patron:** A business owner, local notable, or gang intermediary is arguing over payment or terms. |
| 8 | **The Cipher:** The arrangement of symbols, figures, or colors functions as a code or password. |
| 9 | **The Theft:** The artist’s tools, pigments, or payment have just been stolen. |
| 10 | **Magical Mishap:** The artwork is reacting strangely; painted or shaped elements are becoming briefly real. |

^urban-art-encounter

## Quick Use
- Roll **1 Medium**
- Roll **1 Artist**
- Roll **1 Immediate Encounter**
- Then decide:
  - Is this **flavor**, **social scene**, **clue**, or **full encounter**?
  - Does it connect to a **district**, **faction**, **secret**, or **job board hook**?
