### The Dryad: The Captive Warden

In a gritty, investigation-forward campaign, dryads are not merely peaceful forest nymphs; they are tragic, fiercely territorial fey bound to a specific tree (usually a massive oak). According to fey lore, lesser spirits are sometimes bound to these trees as a punishment for falling in forbidden love with mortals.

Because they are literally rooted to the world through this single tree, their psychology is extreme. They are highly reclusive guardians, but they are also profoundly lonely. This combination makes them dangerous: a dryad struck by the beauty or high Charisma of a traveler will often use her magic to charm them, pulling them into the deep woods to serve as "amorous slaves" and protectors.

**Campaign Assumptions & Investigation Hooks:**

- **The Distance Tether:** A dryad cannot easily leave her home. If she wanders more than 300 to 360 yards from her tree, she becomes violently ill and will eventually die.
- **The Missing Years:** Victims taken by a dryad's charm are often subjected to fey time dilation. If a victim manages to escape the charm and return to civilization, they often find that 1 to 4 years have passed while they felt they were only gone for days.
- **The Madness of the Felled Tree:** A dryad's life force is directly tied to her tree. If the tree is harmed, she suffers physical damage. If the tree is destroyed, she does not always die immediately; instead, she descends into a violent, psychotic madness, sometimes transforming into a corrupted "Briar Witch" or undead threat.

### Dryad

_Medium Fey, Neutral_

- **Armor Class:** 15
- **Hit Points:** 22 (5d8)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Initiative:** +1 (11)
- **STR:** 10 (+0) | **DEX:** 12 (+1) | **CON:** 11 (+0) | **INT:** 14 (+2) | **WIS:** 15 (+2) | **CHA:** 18 (+4)
- **Skills:** Perception +4, Stealth +5
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
- **Languages:** Elvish, Sylvan
- **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Magic Resistance:** The dryad has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
- **Speak with Beasts and Plants:** The dryad can communicate with Beasts and Plants as if they shared a language.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The dryad makes one Vine Lash or Thorn Burst attack, and it can use Spellcasting to cast *Charm Monster*.
- **Vine Lash:** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +6, reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage.
- **Thorn Burst:** _Ranged Attack Roll:_ +6, range 60 ft. _Hit:_ 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage.
- **Spellcasting:** The dryad casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):
    - _At Will:_ *Animal Friendship*, *Charm Monster* (lasts 24 hours; ends early if the dryad casts the spell again), *Druidcraft*
    - _1/Day Each:_ *Entangle*, *Pass without Trace*

**Bonus Actions**

- **Tree Stride:** If within 5 feet of a Large or bigger tree, the dryad teleports to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of a second Large or bigger tree that is within 60 feet of the previous tree.

### Encounter Scaffolds & Investigation Tables

#### d6 The Dryad's Motive (The Missing Persons Case)

Dryads rarely attack caravans head-on. They are typically encountered because the PCs are tracking someone who went missing, or because a local settlement has encroached on the dryad's tethered radius.

|d6|The Encounter Setup|Investigation & Resolution|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Enraptured Scout**|A local ranger or noble with high Charisma went missing a week ago. The PCs find them peacefully guarding a massive oak tree, fully under the dryad's _Fey Charm_. They will violently attack the PCs to protect the "beautiful forest spirit."|
|**2**|**The Deceptive Veil**|The dryad is using illusion magic to disguise herself as a wounded elven woman. She begs the PCs to escort her, deliberately leading them into a labyrinth of _Entangle_ spells and traps to test if they are worthy protectors of the grove.|
|**3**|**The Dying Tree**|The dryad's oak has been infected by a magical blight, a burrowing monster, or a Gulthias root. She is slowly dying and will offer her accumulated treasure (often gifts from past charmed victims) if the PCs can cure the tree without harming her.|
|**4**|**The Mad Guardian**|Loggers recently cut down the dryad's tree, unaware of what it was. The dryad has descended into violent madness. She is now stalking the logging camp, dragging one worker into the woods each night to brutally murder them.|
|**5**|**The Stolen Relic**|The dryad possesses a powerful magic item or a piece of crucial information. She will only trade it if the PCs agree to hunt down a local band of hobgoblins or evil clerics who have been using fire magic near her grove.|
|**6**|**The Fey Court's Prisoner**|The PCs discover the dryad weeping. She confesses she was bound to this tree centuries ago by a cruel Eladrin noble for loving a mortal. The PCs must find a way to break the ancient fey curse to set her free.|

#### d4 Fey-Touched Evidence (Crime Scene Clues)

Tracking a dryad or her victims requires noticing the subtle, supernatural manipulations of the forest.

|d4|Environmental Clue|Narrative Implication|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Impossible Footprints**|The tracks of a missing person simply walk directly into the solid trunk of a massive tree and vanish. The dryad cast _Pass Without Trace_ and pulled them through the wood using her _Tree Stride_ network.|
|**2**|**Unnatural Regrowth**|A campsite or logging path that was cleared only yesterday is already completely overgrown with thick, mature vines and blooming flowers.|
|**3**|**The Watching Animals**|The PCs realize that local wildlife (birds, squirrels, deer) are acting as an organized perimeter alarm. The dryad is using _Speak with Beasts_ to monitor the party's exact movements.|
|**4**|**The Time-Lost Camp**|The party finds a rusted, decades-old camp. Hidden inside is a journal written by an adventurer who claims they arrived in the woods "yesterday." They are currently a captive of the dryad, experiencing fey time dilation.|

#### d4 Tactical Terrain & Forest Defenses

In combat, dryads are evasive skirmishers. They refuse to fight fair, using the environment to divide the party and incapacitate heavy hitters.

| d4    | Tactic / Hazard          | Mechanical Execution                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| :---- | :----------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1** | **The Entangling Choke** | The dryad opens combat from total cover, casting _Entangle_ on the party's vanguard. She relies on allied plant monsters or charmed animals to attack restrained PCs while she stays out of range.                       |
| **2** | **Hit-and-Run Strides**  | She never ends her turn in the open. She casts _Shillelagh_, moves out of a tree to strike a weak PC, and then uses her Bonus Action _Tree Stride_ to instantly teleport 60 feet away into another tree.                 |
| **3** | **Charming the Tank**    | The dryad targets the party's lowest-Wisdom fighter with _Fey Charm_. If successful, she commands the fighter to grapple and hold the party's spellcaster, turning the PCs against each other without lifting a finger.  |
| **4** | **The Treant's Wrath**   | If reduced to half hit points, the dryad lets out a piercing, supernatural scream. 1d4 rounds later, an allied Treant or Awakened Tree arrives, tearing through the canopy to crush the intruders threatening the grove. |
