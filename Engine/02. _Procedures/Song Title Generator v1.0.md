
# Song Title Generator

When a bard plays, a crowd sings along, a broadside is pinned to a tavern wall, or an NPC hums a tune — roll a song title on the fly.

> [!tip] Use at table
> Roll **d50 twice** — once for First Part, once for Second Part.
> Combine as written. Swap articles or tense if it reads awkward.
> For a darker or more comic title, reroll either column.

## Notes

- **Grammar swap**: If "Song of the Lament" feels off, try "Song of Lament" or swap columns.
- **Canon hook**: When a rolled title resonates with current canon (e.g., "The Drowned Bells" in a cult arc), let the song carry a rumor, warning, or prophecy in its verses.
- **Bard-improvised titles** (pre-table): Log in Playtest Notes under 🟡 Untriggered Improv. Backfill to this table if the title still feels good after a session.
