### Brain-Crawler

_Tiny Aberration, Lawful Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Initiative:** +2 (12)
- **Hit Points:** 21 (6d4 + 6)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.
- **STR:** 6 (−2) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 13 (+1) | **INT:** 14 (+2) | **WIS:** 11 (+0) | **CHA:** 10 (+0)
- **Skills:** Perception +2, Stealth +4
- **Resistances:** Psychic
- **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft., passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** Understands Deep Speech but can't speak; telepathy 60 ft.
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Devour Intellect.** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 12, one creature the brain-crawler can see within 5 feet. *Failure:* 11 (2d10) Psychic damage, and the target has the Stunned condition until the end of the brain-crawler's next turn.
- **Steal Body.** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 12, one Small or Medium creature that has the Incapacitated condition, is a Humanoid or Beast, and has 10 Hit Points or fewer. *Failure:* The brain-crawler possesses the target, consumes its brain, and teleports inside its skull. While there, the brain-crawler has Total Cover against attacks and other effects originating outside its host. The brain-crawler retains its Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma; uses its understanding of Deep Speech; its telepathy; and its Detect Intelligence trait. It otherwise adopts the target's game statistics. It knows everything the target knew, including spells and languages. If the host body dies, the brain-crawler must leave it. The brain-crawler is also forced out if the target regains its devoured brain by means of a Wish spell.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack.** The brain-crawler makes one Claw attack and uses Devour Intellect.
- **Claw.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (2d4 + 2) Slashing damage.
