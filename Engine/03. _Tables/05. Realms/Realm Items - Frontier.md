#realm-items-frontier
> **PROVISIONAL — Adam skim pending.** The FRONTIER realm's item universe (docs/BREACH.md §2d
> "DEEP realm kits", BATCH3-GUARDRAILS.md J2 "realm-kits → REALM TABLES" restructure).
> **Re-authored 2026-07-03 to ADAM-REVIEW-2 §3/§3b** (doers-only — pointers extracted to
> `docs/REALM-PLOT-ITEMS-PARKED.md`; coherence laws: wired physics only, cross-breach R1s, no
> undefined referents, Volatile = live-danger items). d50; breach draws inside a
> `frontier`-tagged walk roll THIS table; the legacy Outlandish d300 keeps its
> anachronism-intrusion role (division of labor per J2). Register per J3b: Western — dust, debt,
> and a line nobody enforces till somebody does. Every item names its FRAME, an existing id in
> `data/items.js` — frameless would be a spec bug, so none are invented here. THE RESKIN
> BOUNDARY: mechanics stay D&D, presentation is Western. **Band shares run hotter than the H2
> 66/20/9/4/1 baseline by construction** (an item table is ~half enchanted/signature by the
> brief's own recipe; a functioning magic item is legitimately Strange-band under
> `[[SPICE-RULER]]`); the mundane rows still anchor Grounded/Textured, and Volatile/Mythic stay
> RARE and ruler-earned. **"Peacemaker, Blued" and "Trail-Iron Shoes" are the J3b-anchored
> ladders, landed verbatim among the signature wonders.** Rank ladders (2–4 rungs,
> attunement-level prereqs, 3-slot cap governs) sit on enchanted + signature rows;
> reality-breaking rungs floor at L9. Realm consumables (ammunition variants) are rust-EXEMPT
> via the existing enchantment-immunity check (`rustImmune`, `src/world/durability.js`). d50,
> Commitment ceiling.
