*p. 298 MM 2024*

The troll is a pure engine of violence and regeneration. It exists to eat, to grow, and to tear apart anything that gets between it and the next meal. There is no strategy, no culture, no social structure — trolls are barely self-aware, operating on a level of cognitive engagement closer to a hungry crocodile than a goblin. What they have instead of intelligence is an almost supernatural ability to not die.

The regeneration is the encounter's defining mechanical threat. A troll that has taken 40 damage is back to fighting strength within two rounds unless the party has something that deals acid or fire damage. This means that a party without those damage types will exhaust themselves damaging a troll that simply keeps getting up, becoming more frantic each round, wondering why the troll won't stay down. The DM should make the regeneration *visible* — the wounds close in real-time, the severed fingers crawl back to reattach, the blood stops flowing mid-battle.

Trolls fear exactly two things: fire and acid. A lit torch changes troll behavior immediately — they become wary, hesitant, and will withdraw from a sustained fire source. A party that knows this and maintains it will find the troll manageable. A party that doesn't will find themselves fighting a near-unkillable nightmare.

### Campaign Integration: Investigation & Clues

Troll territory looks like a war zone.

- **The Splintered Wood:** Trolls smash rather than open doors, break rather than move obstacles, tear rather than cut their food. Environments with extreme structural damage — ripped-off door hinges, collapsed sections of wall, broken furniture reduced to splinters — indicate troll activity.
- **The Stench:** Trolls smell appalling. Their regenerating flesh produces a persistent reek of rot and raw meat. The smell is noticeable 100 feet away in enclosed spaces, 30 feet in open air. A party can smell a troll before they see one.
- **The Bone Pile:** Trolls don't eat cleanly. They leave heaps of cracked bones and scraps wherever they feed. The age of the bones indicates how long the troll has been in the area.

### 1. Troll

A massive, nearly unkillable predator that regenerates in combat unless damaged by acid or fire.

_Large Giant, Chaotic Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 15 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 84 (8d10 + 40)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 18 (+4) | **DEX:** 13 (+1) | **CON:** 20 (+5) | **INT:** 7 (−2) | **WIS:** 9 (−1) | **CHA:** 7 (−2)
- **Skills:** Perception +2
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** Giant
- **Challenge:** 5 (1,800 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +3

**Traits**

- **Keen Smell:** The troll has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.
- **Regeneration:** The troll regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn. If the troll takes acid or fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the troll's next turn. The troll dies only if it starts its turn with 0 hit points and doesn't regenerate.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The troll makes three attacks: one with its Bite and two with its Claws.
- **Bite:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage.
- **Claws:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.

### 2. Troll Amalgam

An ancient troll that has survived long enough — and been injured and regenerated often enough — that detached pieces of itself have fused back wrong. The amalgam is bigger, stranger, and more dangerous than a normal troll.

_Large Giant, Chaotic Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 16 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 110 (10d10 + 55)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR:** 20 (+5) | **DEX:** 10 (+0) | **CON:** 21 (+5) | **INT:** 5 (−3) | **WIS:** 9 (−1) | **CHA:** 5 (−3)
- **Skills:** Perception +2
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** Giant (barely)
- **Challenge:** 7 (2,900 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +3

**Traits**

- **Keen Smell:** The amalgam has Advantage on Perception checks that rely on smell.
- **Regeneration:** The amalgam regains 15 hit points at the start of its turn. Acid or fire damage suppresses this trait. The amalgam dies only if it starts its turn with 0 hit points and doesn't regenerate.
- **Wrong Geometry:** The amalgam's mismatched limbs give its attacks an unpredictable reach. Its attacks have Reach of either 5 ft. or 10 ft. (its choice each attack).

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The amalgam makes four attacks: two with its Bites and two with its Claws.
- **Bite:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 8 (1d6 + 5) piercing damage.
- **Claws:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, reach 5 or 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 12 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage.
- **Fling (Recharge 5–6):** The amalgam grabs and hurls a Medium or smaller creature within 5 ft. up to 30 ft. The target must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be thrown and land Prone, taking 14 (4d6) bludgeoning damage. On a success, the target doesn't move.
