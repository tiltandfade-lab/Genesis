# Corin Vask

*(Asset: bandit_intermediary_shadow_logistics)*

## Plot Vector

**Primary Pillar:** Bandits  
**Secondary Pillars:** Cult, Political Elite  

**Function:**  
Connects factions quietly.  
Moves goods and information between groups that “shouldn’t” meet.  
Hints that bandits are structured, not chaotic.

---

## Level 1 — Immediate Table Presence

**Race / Background:** Human; former caravan guard  
**Role:** Logistics coordinator for outlaw network  

**Visual Quirk:** Immaculate boots despite muddy surroundings  
**Mood:** Relaxed, almost bored  
**Demeanor:** Speaks plainly, avoids threats  

**Hook:** Needs discreet transport for sealed crates  
**Talent:** Knows every unofficial road within 30 miles  
**Unrealized Potential:** No  

---

## Level 2 — Structural Pressure

**Formative Trauma:** Betrayed by a noble employer  
**Formative Grace:** Was spared by a rival outlaw  

**Want:** Maintain stable shadow trade  
**Fear:** Disorder and amateur violence  

**Faction Tie:** Works between cult couriers and merchant elites  
**Influence Weight:** 3 — Trade Network  
**Resource Control:** Safe transit corridors  

**If Ignored (Personal):** Routes shift unpredictably  
**If Ignored (Regional):** Bandit activity becomes more chaotic and violent  

---

## Level 3 — Secrets

**Secret:** Has transported ritual materials for the cult  
**Leverage:** Knows who funds certain raids  
**If Cornered:** Negotiates immediately; never fights if avoidable  

---

## Notes for Play

- Never raises voice.  
- Treats crime as business.  
- Refers to “efficiency” often.  
- Hates reckless bandits.
