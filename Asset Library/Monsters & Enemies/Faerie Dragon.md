### 1. The Faerie Dragon

*p.117 MM*

In a high-pressure frontier setting, Faerie Dragons represent unpredictable, chaotic anomalies. They are not mindless beasts; they possess sharp minds and are driven by a puckish sense of humor. In a gritty campaign, they are rarely found in pristine forests; instead, they are likely refugees fleeing the creeping corruption of the surrounding marshlands, or opportunists feeding on the magical distortions of a wealthy urban district.

**Campaign Integration Hooks (Young / Red):**

- **The High-End Dock Thief:** A mated pair of Faerie Dragons is responsible for a string of inexplicable thefts from locked merchant cash boxes in the city's elite trade district.
- **The Bribable Informant:** Faerie Dragons watch invisibly as victims contend with their pranks. If the PCs play to their draconic nature by offering "treasure" (sweets, baked goods, baubles), the dragons can be bribed into providing vital information or safe passage through a magically unstable district.

#### Faerie Dragon (Red / Young)

_Tiny Dragon, Chaotic Good_

- **Armor Class:** 15
- **Hit Points:** 20 (4d4 + 4)
- **Speed:** 10 ft., fly 60 ft.
- **STR:** 3 (-4) | **DEX:** 20 (+5) | **CON:** 13 (+1) | **INT:** 14 (+2) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 16 (+3)
- **Skills:** Arcana +4, Perception +3, Stealth +7
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** Draconic, Sylvan
- **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP)

**Traits**

- **Superior Invisibility:** As a bonus action, the dragon can magically turn invisible until its concentration ends (as if concentrating on a spell). Any equipment the dragon wears or carries is invisible with it.
- **Limited Telepathy:** Using telepathy, the dragon can magically communicate with any other faerie dragon within 60 feet of it.
- **Magic Resistance:** The dragon has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
- **Innate Spellcasting:** The dragon's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 13). It can innately cast a number of spells based on its color. _Red (1/day each): dancing lights, mage hand, minor illusion_.

**Actions**

- **Bite:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 1 piercing damage.
- **Euphoria Breath (Recharge 5-6):** The dragon exhales a puff of euphoria gas at one creature within 5 feet of it. The target must succeed on a DC 11 Wisdom saving throw, or for 1 minute, the target can't take reactions and must roll a d6 at the start of each of its turns to determine its behavior during the turn (typically wandering aimlessly or standing dazed).

**Campaign Integration Hooks (Adult / Violet):**

- **The Civic Anomaly:** A Violet Faerie Dragon has taken up residence near an abandoned wizard's tower or central city monument. It is using its _Hallucinatory Terrain_ and _Major Image_ spells to unintentionally terrorize the district, masking the actual creeping occult corruption with disorienting fey illusions.
- **The Polymorphed Prisoner:** A prominent local "missing person" isn't actually dead—they angered an Adult Faerie Dragon and have been temporarily _Polymorphed_ into a stray dog or mud-crab currently wandering the poorest slum district.

#### Faerie Dragon (Violet / Adult)

_As Faerie Dragons age, their scales move through the colors of the rainbow, culminating in Violet at 51+ years of age. They gain immense magical capabilities as they mature._

_Tiny Dragon, Chaotic Good_

- **Armor Class:** 15
- **Hit Points:** 35 (10d4 + 10)
- **Speed:** 10 ft., fly 60 ft.
- **STR:** 3 (-4) | **DEX:** 20 (+5) | **CON:** 13 (+1) | **INT:** 14 (+2) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 16 (+3)
- **Skills:** Arcana +4, Perception +3, Stealth +7
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** Draconic, Sylvan
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP)

**Traits**

- **Superior Invisibility:** As a bonus action, the dragon can magically turn invisible until its concentration ends (as if concentrating on a spell). Any equipment the dragon wears or carries is invisible with it.
- **Limited Telepathy:** Using telepathy, the dragon can magically communicate with any other faerie dragon within 60 feet of it.
- **Magic Resistance:** The dragon has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
- **Innate Spellcasting:** The dragon's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 13). It can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
    - _1/day each: dancing lights, mage hand, minor illusion, color spray, mirror image, suggestion, major image, hallucinatory terrain, polymorph_.

**Actions**

- **Bite:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 1 piercing damage.
- **Euphoria Breath (Recharge 5-6):** The dragon exhales a puff of euphoria gas at one creature within 5 feet of it. The target must succeed on a DC 11 Wisdom saving throw, or for 1 minute, the target can't take reactions and must roll a d6 at the start of each of its turns to determine its behavior during the turn (typically wandering aimlessly or standing dazed).

**Tactics (The Windgleam Approach):** When forced into conflict, an adult faerie dragon prefers to remain completely invisible. It avoids melee, utilizing its _Euphoria Breath_ and vast array of illusion spells (_Major Image, Hallucinatory Terrain_) to disorient and separate its enemies. If cornered, it uses _Polymorph_ defensively to turn a dangerous martial attacker into a harmless beast, buying time to flutter away to safety.

### Faerie Dragon Youth

_Tiny Dragon, Chaotic Good_

- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Initiative:** +3 (13)
- **Hit Points:** 21 (6d4 + 6)
- **Speed:** 10 ft., Fly 60 ft.
- **STR:** 3 (-4) | **DEX:** 16 (+3) | **CON:** 13 (+1) | **INT:** 12 (+1) | **WIS:** 12 (+1) | **CHA:** 14 (+2)
- **Skills:** Arcana +3, Perception +3, Stealth +5
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** Draconic, Sylvan; telepathy 60 ft. (faerie dragons only)
- **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Magic Resistance.** The dragon has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Actions**

- **Bite.** Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) Psychic damage.
- **Euphoria Breath (Recharge 5–6).** Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 12, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. Failure: The target has the Incapacitated condition and uses all of its movement on its turn to move in a random direction. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. Success: 2 (1d4) Psychic damage only.
- **Spellcasting.** The dragon casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 12). At Will: Dancing Lights, Mage Hand, Minor Illusion.

**Bonus Actions**

- **Superior Invisibility.** The dragon casts Greater Invisibility on itself, requiring no spell components and using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
