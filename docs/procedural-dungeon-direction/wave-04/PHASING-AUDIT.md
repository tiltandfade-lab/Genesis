# Wave 4 Prototype/MVP Phasing Audit

This audit separates Wave 4's canonical connection, access, secret, vertical, circulation, group-traversal,
mutation, map, and projection goals from the smallest honest proofs and playable-MVP obligations. The chronological
authority remains [Wave 4's running record](01-questionnaire-and-triage.md). It follows the global
[phasing framework](../PHASING-FRAMEWORK.md), [Clay Proof Ladder](../CLAY-PROOF-LADDER.md), and
[Feature-Promotion Ledger](../FEATURE-PROMOTION-LEDGER.md). No implementation or dependency is authorized.

## Pre-alpha-critical foundation

| Decision family | Minimum critical form | Why it cannot wait |
|---|---|---|
| One connection owner | Every implemented edge has one stable id/version owning endpoints, direction, state, access, capacity used by play, knowledge, lifecycle, and provenance; exits/apertures/maps/cards are projections | Independent half-doors or provider-authored state corrupt traversal, persistence, and return. |
| Typed access and solvability | Implemented gates use actual predicates/certified approaches; required dependencies have a validated solution plus recovery/failsafe or supported continuing failure branch | A solo campaign cannot depend on a required key placed behind its own gate or lost without recovery. |
| Knowledge-safe discovery | Hidden topology is eager canon while evidence, belief, noticed/located/opened/traversed state, maps, and actor knowledge remain separate | Secret edges cannot leak because geometry, UI, or DM prose knows they exist. |
| Vertical and fall truth | Implemented elevations, landings, headroom, supports, fall space, and positions are canonical; fall damage uses the retained D&D rule and other outcomes require their own causal receipts | Flat adjacency or improvised damage would make rescue, pursuit, custody, and remount disagree. |
| Traversal transaction | Material crossings preserve ordered actors, objects/custody, load/capacity used by the supported slice, time, pursuit, and partial completion; forced persistent separation obeys the cold-record law | Party members, items, or enemies may not teleport, duplicate, disappear, or gain offscreen turns. |
| Connection mutation | Opening, closing, barring, spiking, cutting, repairing, creating, stabilizing, or destroying an implemented route uses certified authority and one typed receipt | Renderer physics and provider narration cannot create durable topology independently of the game. |
| View and recovery parity | Tactical, map, text, accessibility, DM-seat, save/load, and rebuild paths consume the same viewpoint-legal version | A correct semantic connection is insufficient if another view leaks or falsifies it. |

## Explicit prototype/MVP exclusions

- The first proof does not implement every connection noun, full portal networks, multi-landing lifts, broad vehicles,
  continuous physics, every spell/tool interaction, or an independently advancing split party.
- The playable MVP is not permitted to use prose-only locks, ropes, falls, maps, or secret doors for its supported
  representative families. Narrow breadth must still have real canonical mechanics and persistence.
- Corridor compaction changes presentation cadence, not geometry, time, visibility, sound, access, or encounter
  truth. Material promotion cannot retroactively invent the corridor.
- Every-room discovery opportunity does not mean one full independent secret or one explicit search prompt per room.
  Closed Wave 1 search aggregation and attention laws remain binding.
- Fall damage does not receive Wave 4-specific tuning. The retained D&D rule owns numeric damage; separate outcomes
  require separate causal facts and receipts.
- Exact schemas, weights, incidence, balance, UI layout, solver budgets, and content catalogs remain later
  specification/workbench evidence. They may not weaken the accepted authority and fairness laws.
