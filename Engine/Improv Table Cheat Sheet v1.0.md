## 1) Fast Combat & Rulings

**1. Improvising Damage (DMG)**  
Use when: “How much damage does _that_ do?”  
Look up: DMG “Improvising Damage” + “Damage Severity and Level.”  
Quick anchor: the DMG provides a damage ladder (1d10 → 24d10) with examples, and a severity-by-level guide (Setback/Dangerous/Deadly).

**2. Damage Severity by Level (DMG / XGE)**  
Use when: you’ve chosen a damage number and want to sanity-check lethality by tier.  
Look up: DMG “Damage Severity and Level.” (XGE also has encounter-balance tools; see Quick Matchups.)

**3. Object Armor Class (DMG)**  
Use when: “What’s the AC of this door/statue/lockbox?”  
Look up: DMG “Objects” → Object Armor Class (by material).

**4. Object Hit Points (DMG)**  
Use when: “How many hits until it breaks?”  
Look up: DMG “Objects” → Object Hit Points (by size + fragile/resilient).

**5. Targets in Areas of Effect (DMG)**  
Use when: theatre-of-the-mind fireball/cone math.  
Look up: DMG “Targets in Areas of Effect” (Running the Game).

**6. Mob Attacks (DMG)**  
Use when: 12 goblins all stab at once and you refuse to roll 12 d20s.  
Look up: DMG “Handling Mobs” / “Mob Attacks.”

**7. Quick Matchups (XGE)**  
Use when: you need a “good enough” CR pick instantly (1v1, 2v1, 4v1).  
Look up: XGE Ch. 2 “Encounter Building” → **Quick Matchups (p. 91).**
