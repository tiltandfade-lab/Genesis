The useful cross-tradition constant is a custody system: an authority claim, controlled
thresholds, people and property intake, classification, operator routine, services,
disposition, release/transfer, and precommitted counterplay.

1. `PR-DOC-01 — Keeper-House Civic Custody`; and
2. `PR-DOC-02 — Ledger-and-Shift Civic Custody`.

access, property handling, routine, failure behavior, and escape targets. They are
institutional answers cultures may weight, not real-world culture labels.
