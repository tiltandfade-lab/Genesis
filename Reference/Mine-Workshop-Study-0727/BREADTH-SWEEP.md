# BREADTH SWEEP — "What else could this site be?"

Guidelines step 0.5 requires asking, before adding depth to a family, whether the proposed
scope is genuinely a host program, a cross-host transform, a scale/relationship stress case, a
substrate case, or an ordinary venue fixture that does not deserve independent geometry. This
sweep tests Site 5's boundaries against real-world and cross-media alternatives the working
spec has not yet had to answer, plus web research to widen the measured/cultural base the
status table names as thin.

## 1. Alternative resources and extraction modes the working spec has not stress-tested

The working spec's form-family table (`synthesis.md` in the original packet, §5) already
covers drift/slope/shaft/room-and-pillar/stope/open-cut/gradient-works/attached-workshop. This
sweep adds resource *kinds*, not forms, because a different material can force a different
operating circuit even inside an already-licensed form:

- **Salt (rock salt, mined dry, not evaporated).** [Wieliczka Salt Mine](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/32/)
  (Poland, 13th century–2007) is the standard-setting real example: over 300 km of galleries
  on multiple levels, "protected against collapse by timber cribs, roof supports and braces, in
  some instances of very elaborate design," reaching chambers as large as 50 m long × 15 m wide
  × 12 m high (the Chapel of the Blessed Kinga). Critically, Wieliczka's workers carved chapels,
  statues, and an entire cathedral from the salt itself over centuries — production waste
  becomes monumental art in situ, because the material removed IS the building material. This
  is a genuine boundary stress case for step 0.5: does a salt mine that becomes a working
  cathedral cross into Site 4 (Monastery/Commune, "communal institution, ritual/service
  hierarchy") or Site 10 (Urban Institution)? **Verdict: it stays Site 5 host with a Site 4/10
  guest relation**, exactly the pattern the working spec's boundaries section already allows
  ("Site 10 may consume workshop/frontage vocabulary, while Site 5 remains the authority for
  the production and support-flow grammar") — the production/support-flow chain (extraction →
  haulage → the carved chambers as a *byproduct destination*, not the primary product) still
  organizes the site. This is a PROPOSED reading, not a ruling.
- **Gem/crystal seams (licensed, "unusual" family member).** The working spec's five-expression
  family already names "crystal seam" under the unusual/realm-owned rung; this sweep confirms
  it belongs there rather than as a new host, because gem mining still runs the same
  source→extraction→haulage→sort→export chain — only the sorting step (visual/hardness
  grading rather than crushing) and the security posture (small, extremely valuable, easily
  concealed product) change. Security-of-product becomes a new *occupancy* pressure, not a new
  host.
- **Peat.** The intel evidence (`docs/intel/tiyl-starts.md` line 293, "The Bog-Iron Camp") shows
  a real rolled result pairing "a mud-tent camp beside a peat mine" — the working spec's
  Site 2×Site 5 composite the intel doc calls out directly (§3.2 mapping table: "direct, and
  doubly so"). Peat is cut from open bog rather than shafted or drifted, so it stresses the
  form-family table with a shallow, wet, horizontal-strip extraction mode the current table
  does not name. **PROPOSED:** peat-cutting licenses under the existing "prospect/open-cut"
  family rather than requiring a new row — its footprint (shallow trenches, stacked drying
  turves, a wet working surface) is closer to a quarry bench than to a drift, and the working
  spec's stretch-analysis language ("a quarry swaps the worked void for benches") already
  covers it.

## 2. Cross-media comparison (games) — what "mine as a level" solves elsewhere

The concepting guidelines ask what else the site could be relative to other design traditions,
not only real-world alternatives. A short, disclosed sample:

- **Deep Rock Galactic** (Ghost Ship Games) generates cave/mine missions procedurally across
  nine biomes, with a fully destructible environment (players tunnel new routes at will) and a
  class-based toolset (Scout platforms, Engineer turrets, Driller tunnels, Gunner ziplines) that
  turns "make your own route" into the core strategic lever. Lighting is explicitly scarce and
  costly — a limited flare stash that fizzles out — which independently confirms the working
  spec's "Grounded mine light is local and costly" verdict from an unrelated design tradition.
  **What Genesis should NOT copy:** DRG's core loop is player-authored tunnelling through fully
  destructible rock. Site 5's `SpatialPlanV2`/clay-room construction model commits geometry
  before play; the working spec's "engineered ceiling profile" and "last-safe-support boundary"
  are meaningful specifically because the player cannot dig a new route on a whim. This is a
  genuine design-tradition fork, recorded so nobody proposes destructible mining later without
  knowing it contradicts the site's committed-geometry model.
- **Dwarf Fortress / Valheim** (not independently re-searched this pass beyond the initial
  query; the search tool returned Deep Rock Galactic results only) remain open comparison
  points for a future pass — flagged as a genuine, not a false, gap rather than silently
  dropped.

## 3. "Is this actually a new host?" — the step-0.5 test applied to two edge cases

- **Quarry alone, with no underground element at all.** Already RULED accepted stretch in the
  working spec ("the family may extend into quarries… when each version preserves an honest
  source-to-export chain"). This sweep confirms via MW-12 (`LICENSE-LEDGER.md`) that a real
  disused quarry face (Ballachulish slate) shows the same "worked void distinct from natural
  terrain" read the working spec's identity section requires — the void is legible as *cut*,
  not *found*, which is the same test Site 7 (Natural Lair) uses to exclude worked mine voids
  from its own family (`GOLDEN-SITES-CATALOG.md` Site 5 boundaries: "Site 7 owns natural and
  feral voids. Site 5 owns deliberate cuts").
- **Freestanding urban smithy with no extraction chain behind it (a shop, not a mine).** This is
  the genuine boundary question the working spec has not yet answered explicitly, and it maps
  directly onto `docs/BUILDING-PROGRAM-TABLE-FAMILIES.md`'s `BF-SHOP-WORKSHOP` family (Smithy,
  Apothecary, General Store, Arcanist — "customer edge, transaction, stock, work/preparation
  where present, secure material, delivery/waste, operator position"). MW-11
  (`LICENSE-LEDGER.md`, the Castelmezzano blacksmith shop) is real photographic evidence of
  exactly this case: a small, retail-facing, single-operator forge opening onto a street, with
  no visible ore, waste, or haulage chain behind it. **Verdict, PROPOSED:** this is bin (b) —
  an ordinary venue fixture consumed by the shared `BF-SHOP-WORKSHOP` chassis and the typed
  building generator, not a Site 5 host expression. Site 5 owns the workshop only when it sits
  at the *end of its own production chain* (mill/forge/wash floor physically fed by the mine's
  haul spine, with ore in and waste/product out); `BF-SHOP-WORKSHOP`'s "production forge with
  commission book" arrangement owns the freestanding urban smith who *buys* stock rather than
  extracting it. Full reconciliation in `synthesis.md` §5.
