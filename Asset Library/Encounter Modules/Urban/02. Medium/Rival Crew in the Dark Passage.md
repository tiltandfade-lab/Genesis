## 30-Second Run

A narrow dark passage in the entertainment district — lawless, no witnesses. A rival crew is already here, working the same lead. A jammed door wedge, a newly-fastened wall bracket, and a deliberately tied sack suggest they've been here a while and they've set this space up. A carved slate engraving of a battle scene is built into the far wall. The hollow behind it is the prize — and the rival crew doesn't know about it yet.

---

## Setup

**Space:** A constricted narrow passage, 5 ft wide in places. Dark — almost no useful light. A blocked opening at one end; a window or gap high in the wall lets in minimal outside sound. The passage runs through the back of an entertainment strip.
**Atmosphere:** A distant bell rings. Cold air and soot. Old wrought-iron vines and ironwork on the wall, weathered and corroded. A carved slate slab shows a battle — hobgoblin horde, elf king falling. Nobody's looked at it in years.
**NPCs Present:** Rival Crew (3 members — leader, muscle, lookout at the window).
**If Ignored:** The rival crew completes their work and leaves. The hollow behind the slate is emptied.

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Social Standoff

The rivals are doing the same kind of work. They know it. The party knows it. This is a conversation first — unless someone pushes it.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 12 Insight:** The leader is cautious, not aggressive. She'll deal if she thinks the party has something she doesn't.
- **DC 13 Persuasion:** Negotiate — offer a trade of information, a split of what's found, or a pledge not to interfere with each other's operations.
- **DC 14 Intimidation:** Assert dominance. Works, but they leave resentful and may reappear as complications later.
- **DC 13 Deception:** Claim false authority or false information to redirect them.

**Escalation:** Any violence in the passage triggers Cramped Route conditions. One of them is at the window — the lookout calls out if things go loud, bringing a CR ~1 veteran in 3 rounds.

---

### Segment 2: The Slate and the Hollow

While the social negotiation plays out, an attentive party member can notice what the rival crew has missed.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 14 Perception:** Notice the slate engraving on the far wall — the surface is cold but the temperature around one edge is slightly different. Condensation pattern at the lower right corner.
- **DC 13 Investigation (slate):** The base of the slate is a false front. Behind it: a shallow hollow, dry, concealed by the weight of the slab. Contents inside (roll below).
- **DC 12 Sleight of Hand:** Extract the contents without the rival crew noticing during the conversation.

**Escalation:** If the crew sees the party go for the slate, the negotiation breaks down immediately. The leader wants whatever's in there.

---

### Segment 3: The Jammed Route

The blocked opening at the passage end is a jammed grate.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 13 Athletics:** Bend the grate open.
- **DC 12 Thieves' Tools:** Remove the fasteners quietly.
- Beyond it: a secondary route through the underworks that bypasses the entertainment strip entirely.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Cramped Route:** In the passage, Speed is halved and creatures have Disadvantage on Dexterity checks. Two creatures cannot occupy the same 5-ft space.
2. **Dark:** Heavily Obscured without Darkvision or a light source. Disadvantage on sight-based Perception checks.
3. **Lookout:** The figure at the window has line of sight to the alley approach. Stealth check DC 14 to avoid being spotted on entry.
4. **Veteran Reinforcement:** If combat starts and goes loud, a CR 1 veteran investigates from the entertainment strip end of the passage in 3 rounds.

---

## Difficulty Dial

**Tier 1 (Lvl 1–3):** Rival crew are Bandits (CR 1/8). The veteran doesn't arrive unless loud noise continues for 3+ rounds.
**Tier 2 (Lvl 4–6):** The rival crew is a Cult cell — they recognize the party and the negotiation starts at hostile.
**Tier 3 (Lvl 7+):** The crew has already opened the hollow. The cache is gone. Now they want to know how the party knew about it.
