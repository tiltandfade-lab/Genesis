## 30-Second Run

Purple lightning cracks overhead. Two mages are dueling on a rooftop, invisible to the party — but their fallout isn't. Undead stagger through a T-junction. A courier is pounding on a stuck wooden door, panicking. Paper shapes folded between buildings have come alive as paper daggers, lashing at the undead like a defensive mishap. Deep potholes and mucky footing make every movement risky. A draft from the wrong direction reveals a hidden passage behind an illusory wall.

---

## Setup

**Space:** Modest residential T-junction. Bright daylight. The street is riddled with deep potholes — Difficult Terrain throughout. A well or stone cistern anchors the center. Dense cover from keg racks and debris.
**Atmosphere:** Purple lightning flashes above the rooftops. The smell of perfume or soap from an overturned cart. Paper shapes between buildings flap and dart with strange purpose.
**NPCs Present:** Courier (panicking at stuck door, message in hand), Mage (unseen, rooftop — not interactable), Necromancer (unseen, opposing rooftop), 3–5 Lingering Undead (Shadows or Ghouls pushing into the junction).
**If Ignored:** The undead overrun the junction. The courier is killed or flees. The hidden passage is never found. The duel ends eventually — one mage falls.

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Junction

Three things compete for the party's attention at once: the undead, the courier, and the fallout from above.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 13 Arcana:** The purple lightning is a *Chromatic Orb* or *Chaos Bolt* exchange — two mages at war, not targeting the street.
- **DC 10 Athletics / DC 10 Acrobatics:** Move through the potholes without losing speed (Difficult Terrain requires check or half movement).
- **DC 12 Insight (courier):** The message is urgent and the address is real. The courier is not a threat — just stuck.
- **DC 10 Athletics / DC 12 Investigation (stuck door):** Shoulder through or identify the lift-from-frame technique.

**Escalation:** Every round the undead are not neutralized, one more drifts into the junction from an alley. They target the courier before the party — he's slower.

---

### Segment 2: Paper Daggers and Undead

The illusionist's paper flock has become briefly real — sharp, darting forms that attack undead but cannot distinguish friend from foe.

**Mechanics:** On initiative count 10 each round, one random creature in the junction (ally, enemy, or civilian) is targeted by a paper dagger — +4 to hit, 1d4 piercing damage.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 14 Arcana:** Recognize the flock as a Magical Mishap — the enchantment is unstable. A *Dispel Magic* (DC 13) or physically scattering the paper shapes (DC 10 Athletics, action) ends the hazard.
- **DC 12 Sleight of Hand:** Grab a paper dagger as it passes — it remains real for 1 minute, then returns to ordinary paper.

---

### Segment 3: The Hidden Passage

A draft comes from the wrong direction near the well. The wall behind the cistern is an illusion.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 15 Perception:** Feel the draft where the air should be still — coming from the south wall.
- **DC 15 Investigation:** The wall is a permanent illusion. Behind it: a narrow passage leading away from the junction.
- The small urn sitting near the cistern base: DC 12 Investigation — it's hollow. Inside: a folded note or minor salvage (roll below).

**Escalation:** If the party uses the passage while the mage duel is active overhead, one stray bolt hits the junction — everyone outside the passage makes DC 13 Dex save or 2d6 lightning damage.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Deep Potholes:** Difficult Terrain throughout. DC 10 Athletics or DC 10 Acrobatics to avoid speed penalty each round.
2. **Deep Muck Hazard (potholes, specific):** DC 10 Athletics to escape once restrained. On fail: Restrained and sinks 1d4 ft.
3. **Paper Daggers:** +4 to hit vs. random creature in junction on initiative count 10. 1d4 piercing. Can be ended (see above).
4. **Hidden Passage:** Illusory wall, DC 15 Perception/Investigation to find. Bypasses the junction entirely.
5. **Courier Message:** If the party delivers it (the recipient is 2 blocks away, DC 10 Investigation to determine), the courier becomes a minor recurring contact.

---

## Difficulty Dial

**Tier 1 (Lvl 1–3):** 3 Shadows (undead). Paper daggers deal 1 piercing. Mage duel causes no direct hits.
**Tier 2 (Lvl 4–6):** 5 Ghouls (undead). Paper daggers deal 1d4 piercing. One stray bolt per round hits the junction (DC 13 Dex save or 1d6 lightning).
**Tier 3 (Lvl 7+):** The mages crash into the street at round 3 — one dead, one badly wounded. Introduce a second enemy faction here.

## Stat Blocks

**Shadow** — *Medium Undead, Chaotic Evil* — CR 1/2
AC 12 | HP 16 | Speed 40 ft.
**Strength Drain.** +4, 2d6+2 necrotic; target's Str reduced by 1d4 until long rest.
**Tactic:** Targets isolated or armored PCs (drain strength to debilitate). Vulnerable to sunlight (Disadvantage on attacks in bright light).

**Ghoul** — *Medium Undead, Chaotic Evil* — CR 1
AC 12 | HP 22 | Speed 30 ft.
**Claws.** +2, 2d4+2 slashing; DC 10 Con save or Paralyzed until end of next turn.
**Tactic:** Targets the courier first (paralysis on civilians is efficient). Piles on paralyzed targets.
