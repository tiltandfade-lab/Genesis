## Synopsis

The set that finally put Sella's body on the line — and the dice answered with the coldest run of the marathon, five straight failures (nat-1, 2, 9, 7, 3, several at advantage), every one honored without a fudge. Opening on the exact hinge of set-03's close (mid dusk flood-rescue, freshly struck off the book), she leaned into the physical danger her three prior social sets never touched — and **fumbled a nat-1** hauling a paralytic (Halder) out of a drowning room, taking the **first HP damage of her whole campaign** (−5 of 9), losing her lantern, and being saved only by the network she'd built (the fail LANDED, no fictional second-out). Chastened, she pivoted from muscle back to mind: scouted the flooding row (Insight 20) and spotted the true human threat — a **Circle clerk** working the flood, marking down every dead-man room the struck row had reoccupied, building Corran a list of who "came back from the dead" with her own new drowned-name next up. She took the clerk from surprise (Stealth 13) — a real ambush whose fail branch would have opened combat — but couldn't flip him (**Persuasion 2**: the Circle is the deeper fear, a Frederick-echo), so she made the campaign's **first on-camera kill**, drowning him in the flood as its own alibi (no wound, `fact_canonized`, +1 XP, no Circle front tick because a clean flood-death leaves no evidence). She saved the collapsing north-footspan crossing with her T1 lesson applied — rope + the network's arms, not her back — but **misread the rotten piling (Investigation 9)**: the surge took the span, her rope-line still saved the two on it, and the flood gained a reach (external front 3→4). Running the boat-extraction of the cut-off dozen as pure coordination (her arc's mastery), the enforcer **Vesk** — hunting his vanished clerk-partner — intercepted her at the choke, and her brilliant ball-bearings-and-the-flood rogue gambit **failed (Acrobatics 7 at advantage)**: Vesk dropped her to 0 and took her **alive (nonlethal capture — the BUG-04 fix verified live: 0-stable, no death spiral)**. Held overnight, roused to 1 HP, she tried to talk her way out on her true home turf (Vesk's off-books grab is his liability) — and the dice came up **3 at advantage**, so his fear of Corran beat her and he dragged her bound to the ironwood door at dawn. The unbeaten patient man wins the sum, as foretold across all three sets — but the Circle (now 5/6, one from doom) has taken a **mole into its own house and called it an asset.**
