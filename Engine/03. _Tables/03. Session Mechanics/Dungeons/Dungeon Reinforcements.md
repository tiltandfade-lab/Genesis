| d12 | Reinforcement Result |
|----|----------------------|
| 1  | 1 Guard from the same faction arrives from an adjacent segment. |
| 2  | 2 Minions (CR 1/8–1/4 each) arrive cautiously. |
| 3  | 1 Veteran/Elite (CR ~1) arrives to investigate the disturbance. |
| 4  | Defensive lockdown instead of creatures: doors barred, cover established, trap activated. |
| 5  | Patrol heard approaching — arrives at start of next round. |
| 6  | Boss sends a scout (weak creature) to assess situation. |
| 7  | 3 Minions (CR 1/8–1/4 each) arrive at a dead run, already shouting the alarm onward. |
| 8  | 1 Specialist (CR ~1/2, ranged or caster) arrives and holds position at the room's edge instead of closing. |
| 9  | The faction's alarm signal fires (bell, horn, ward-chime) — no reinforcement this round, but every future roll on this table is made with advantage for the rest of the encounter. |
| 10 | 2 Veterans/Elites (CR ~1 each) arrive together, already coordinating a pincer. |
| 11 | Reinforcements arrive, but for the WRONG faction — a rival force shows up to exploit the noise, hostile to both sides. |
| 12 | The boss arrives in person, underprepared and furious — treat as a Veteran/Elite (CR ~1) with one boss-tier trait active early. |
