## 30-Second Run

The party enters an oval chamber. Meat hooks hang from the ceiling — one holds something alive. A jagged black stone sits at the center on a pedestal. The doors slam shut. Two priests step into symmetrical positions at opposite ends and begin a synchronized chant. On initiative count 20, the stone starts eating the floor in an expanding ash zone. The guards actively shove PCs into it. The priests must be broken to stop the expansion. And someone needs to grab that captive before the ash reaches the hook.

---

## Setup

**Space:** Oval underground chamber, hidden beneath a wealthy estate. Dual iron-banded doors on opposite ends (currently locked, AC 19 HP 30 to smash). Meat hooks hanging from the ceiling. Central pedestal.
**Atmosphere:** Flickering, unreliable lamplight. Heavy moisture-slicked brickwork. The smell of rot and copper.
**NPCs Present:** 2 Cult Priests (opposite ends), 4 Cult Guards (edges of the room), 1 Captive (on a hook, alive — see d6 table).
**If Ignored:** The entire room is consumed, collapsing the streets above. The captive dies. Evidence is destroyed.

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Ambush and the Timer

On initiative count 20 each round: the corruption zone expands 5 ft from the center. It starts at 10-ft radius.

Any creature starting its turn in the zone or entering it takes **1d6 necrotic damage** (Tier 1: 1d4; Tier 2: 2d6).

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 14 Arcana or Religion:** The priests are symmetrical conduits. Breaking their symmetry, their concentration, or killing them disrupts the ritual.
- **DC 13 Athletics or Acrobatics:** Leap over the corrupted floor to reach the opposite priest (requires movement through or over the zone).
- Guards' tactic: grapple and shove PCs into the ash zone.

**Escalation:** When a priest takes damage: DC 13 Con save (as concentration). Fail: ritual falters, expansion pauses for that round. When a priest dies: remaining guards gain reckless attacks (Advantage to hit; attacks against them also have Advantage).

---

### Segment 2: The Captive

The live captive hangs from a hook in the far portion of the chamber. The ash expansion reaches the hook's position at round 4 (based on start radius + 5 ft per round).

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 13 Athletics or Acrobatics:** Jump or climb to the hook and unhook the creature safely before the ash arrives.

---

### Segment 3: Reversing the Ritual

Once both priests are dead, the ash stops expanding — but doesn't recede. The doors remain locked. Reversal is required to escape.

**How to reverse:** Two creatures stand symmetrically across the central pedestal (equidistant, opposite sides) and each uses an Action to channel a lesser stone inward. **DC 14 Arcana or Religion.** On success: corruption retreats, doors unlock. On fail: try again next round. The lesser stones grant immunity to the ash zone while held.

**Boar Carcass Secret:** One hanging carcass (the gutted boar) has a watertight leather satchel sewn into its chest cavity — DC 15 Investigation or Medicine (or *Detect Magic*) to find it.

**Priest's Missive:** DC 14 Investigation of the lead priest's body. Mixed success (DC 11–13): find it but a vial of acid in the pocket burns the letter edges — location intact, some names obscured. Fail (DC 10): letter ripped, falls into the ash.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Expanding Corruption Zone:** Initiative count 20, +5 ft radius each round. 1d6 necrotic to start/enter. Creatures immune while holding lesser stones.
2. **Locked Doors:** AC 19, HP 30 each. Smashing alerts whatever is above.
3. **Lesser Stones:** Grant ash immunity while held. Needed for ritual reversal.
4. **Guards' Tactic:** Grapple check (Str vs Str/Dex) to shove PCs into the ash. Grappled creature moves when guard moves.
