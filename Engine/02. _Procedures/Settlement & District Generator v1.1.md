## Naming Logic (after rolls)
- **Function name** (e.g., Tanners’ Run, Lantern Market)
- **Landmark name** (e.g., Leaning Bell Ward, Cistern Quarter)
- **Founder/house name** (e.g., Arden Quarter, Vell’s Reach)
- **Slang name** (locals vs officials)
- **Old-name / new-name split** (rebuilt-town history)

### Quick Name Seed Formula
**[Landmark/Texture] + [Function Word]**  
Examples: *Gullhook Docks*, *Bellshadow Ward*, *Reedmire Row*, *Cinderforge Quarter*
