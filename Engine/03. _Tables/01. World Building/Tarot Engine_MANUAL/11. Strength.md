### **XI. Strength: Trigger Effects**

The Strength card represents internal fortitude, courage, and the mastery of the "beast within" through compassion and control rather than brute force. Under this card, the dungeon becomes a place where physical prowess is amplified, and the line between the wild and the civilized begins to blur.

---

**The Constant: "Unshakable Resolve"**

The presence of Strength grants the party a quiet, immovable confidence.

- **The Effect:** Players have **Advantage** on saving throws against being **Frightened** or **Charmed**.
    
- **The Toll:** This inner focus makes it harder to react to external, chaotic shifts. All players take a **-2 penalty** to **Initiative** rolls.

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

**The Constant: "The Sovereign Pride"**

The faction has reorganized into a meritocracy based purely on physical and spiritual resilience. They are purging the weak and recruiting those who show "True Strength."

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**Faction Behavior (Strength)**|**Narrative Implication**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Great Hunt**|The faction organizes a global competition to hunt the world's most dangerous beast.|
|**6-9**|Textured|**The Martial Vow**|The faction’s soldiers stop using magic and ranged weapons, believing only melee combat is "honorable."|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Ascent of Titans**|The faction’s leaders undergo a ritual to grow to 20 feet tall, becoming literal giants to rule their territory.|
