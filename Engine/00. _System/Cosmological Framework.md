  
## The Firmament & The Vale  
### Statement of Intent

This campaign engine operates within a dual-layer cosmology:

- **The Firmament** — the structured, historically continuous world.
- **The Vale** — the procedural, emergent mythic plane.

Both layers are canon. Both are real. They obey different governing principles.

---

## I. The Firmament

The Firmament is the stable, causally consistent layer of reality.

It is:

- Historically anchored  
- Geographically mapped  
- Governed by Rules As Written  
- Consequence-driven  
- Persistent across campaigns  

All events in the Firmament become permanent canon upon session resolution.

If a village burns, it remains burned.  
If a faction falls, it remains fallen.  
If a dragon dies, it remains dead.

Multiple campaigns may occur within the Firmament at different times or in parallel strata, but:

- Parties cannot interact directly  
- No retroactive edits are allowed  
- Historical continuity must be preserved  

The Firmament rewards structure, tone, intentional narrative arcs, and faction evolution.  
It is the ledger of the world.

---

## II. The Vale

The Vale is the procedural layer of mythic instability.

It is:

- System-forward  
- Tarot-reactive  
- Table-driven  
- Tonally volatile  
- Symbolically responsive  

The Vale may:

- Bend geography  
- Warp causality  
- Escalate absurdity  
- Reflect player psychology  
- Generate unexpected myth  

However:

- Events become canon only after session resolution  
- Consequences still ripple outward  
- Internal consistency must hold once established  

The Vale is not random nonsense.  
It is emergent reality under pressure.

It exists either as:

- A parallel plane  
- A metaphysical echo  
- A reality undergoing formation  
- A mythic fracture adjacent to the Firmament  

Its precise metaphysical explanation may evolve over time.

---

## III. Shared Canon Rules

Both layers share a common historical ledger.

- Actions in either layer may influence the other through rumor, planar bleed, faction shifts, or mythic distortion  
- Direct player crossover between campaigns is not permitted  
- Time flows consistently unless a defined anomaly explicitly alters it  
- No event is erased once finalized  

This world accumulates.

---

## IV. Design Philosophy

The Firmament allows for:

- Deep structured arcs  
- Thematic cohesion  
- Intentional dramatic pacing  
- Faction politics and long-term consequence  

The Vale allows for:

- Procedural chaos  
- Mythic escalation  
- High-entropy surprise  
- Legendary absurdity  

Neither mode invalidates the other.

Together they create tectonic tension.

The goal is not control.  
The goal is coherence under emergence.

---

## V. Long-Term Vision

Over time, both layers will:

- Develop independent histories  
- Accumulate myth  
- Generate factional memory  
- Create legends that echo across campaigns  

If ever a convergence event occurs between the Firmament and the Vale, it must be:

- Rare  
- Structured  
- World-altering  
- Permanent  

Such an event is not default.  
It is earned.
