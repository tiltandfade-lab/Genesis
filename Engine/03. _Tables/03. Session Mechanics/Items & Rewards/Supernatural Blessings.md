#supernatural-blessing
> **A lasting supernatural gift — a Blessing.** Where a [[#supernatural-charm|Charm]] is a small favor spent once, a Blessing is a standing favor from a power: a god, a dead saint, a thing in the deep that took an interest, the land itself. It **persists** — until it is used up, until its condition is broken, or until you prove unworthy and it leaves. The **DM rolls this instead of inventing** when the fiction grants a real, lasting boon (a shrine's true gift, a patron-power's mark, surviving the Mythic). Commitment ceiling — the top rolls are **world-marking**, and a Blessing taken should be written to the World State Ledger as canon about this soul. Genesis-native, IP-clean (structure mined from XGtE/TCoE supernatural gifts, authored fresh 2026-06-28).
