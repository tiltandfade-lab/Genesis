# NPC Role Skin — Frontier (western coast)

> Realm skin over [[npc-role-spine]] ([[NPC-ROLE-REALMS]] approach C). Frontier is the **base skin**:
> it relabels every spine archetype with the current `npc-role` job-name (migration parity — a
> frontier world reproduces today's distribution), takes **no drops** (the frontier has a bit of
> everything), and **adds** the western-genre flourish roles that exist nowhere else. Soul/note/class
> inherit from the spine; only the LABEL changes. Weight blank = spine default. Lever/atom — exempt
> from the situation family (do NOT tag `table_family`).
