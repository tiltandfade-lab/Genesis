| **d20** | **Composition** | **Roster** | **Tactical Deployment** |
| :------ | :-------------- | :--------- | :---------------------- |
| **1** | The Crowd | 2d6+2 Low CR | **Mob Mentality:** Civilians or rioters surge forward in mass. They chain the Help action and scatter if their apparent leader falls first. |
| **2** | The Crowd | 2d6+2 Low CR | **The Surge:** Splits around the party and flows into flanking positions down adjacent alleys; some grab improvised weapons mid-encounter. |
| **3** | The Gang | 2d4 Low CR | **Reinforcements:** Half present at the start. The others arrive Round 2 from an adjacent alley, building, or rooftop access. |
| **4** | The Gang | 2d4 Low CR | **Drip Feed:** 1d4 start on the map. An additional 1d4 arrive on Initiative 0 each round from different entry points until the roster is depleted. |
| **5** | Enforcers & Watch | 1d4 Mid CR melee + 1d4 Low CR ranged | **Combined Arms:** Melee blocks the street or doorway; ranged units fire from rooftops, windows, or the far end of the alley. |
| **6** | Enforcers & Watch | 1d4 Mid CR melee + 1d4 Low CR ranged | **Funnel & Fire:** Ranged units open fire then retreat indoors. Melee moves to cut off the party's retreat, trapping them in the kill zone. |
| **7** | Enforcers & Watch | 1d4 Mid CR melee + 1d4 Low CR ranged | **Checkpoint Formation:** Melee holds a chokepoint — gate, bridge, or doorway — using Dodge. Ranged fires over them into the party's approach. |
| **8** | The Ambush | 1d4+1 Mid CR | **Alley Surround:** All enemies begin Hidden, deployed across multiple entry points. They attack simultaneously from two or more directions. |
| **9** | The Ambush | 1d4+1 Mid CR | **Rooftop Drop:** Positioned above on ledges, eaves, or balconies with three-quarters cover. They open with ranged attacks before dropping into melee. |
| **10** | Boss & Muscle | 1 High CR + 2d4 Low CR | **Meatshields:** Muscle forms a wall around the boss, providing Half Cover. The boss uses ranged attacks or abilities from behind them. |
| **11** | Boss & Muscle | 1 High CR + 2d4 Low CR | **Sacrificial Distraction:** Boss immediately throws muscle at the party to buy time, then uses the chaos to reposition, flee, or access a resource. |
| **12** | Boss & Guard | 1 High CR + 2 Mid CR | **Close Protection:** Guards interpose themselves and Ready actions to intercept. The boss focuses fire on ranged attackers and spellcasters. |
| **13** | Boss & Guard | 1 High CR + 2 Mid CR | **Escape & Contain:** Boss flees the moment combat begins. Guards hold the party at the chokepoint to buy time for the escape. |
| **14** | The Spellcaster & Allies | 1 Mid CR caster + 1d4 Low CR | **Magical Cover:** Caster uses a doorway, alley corner, or window for total cover. Allies engage at street level to keep the party occupied. |
| **15** | The Spellcaster & Allies | 1 Mid CR caster + 1d4 Low CR | **Living Barrier:** Allies block the street in a line using Dodge. The caster casts area spells over them into the compressed party. |
| **16** | The Elite Pair | 2 Mid-High CR | **Tandem Strike:** Both targets focus on one PC at a time, communicating and coordinating kills before moving to the next. |
| **17** | The Elite Pair | 2 Mid-High CR | **Divide & Conquer:** The pair splits the party by forcing them into separate alleys, rooms, or buildings, then engages each group separately. |
| **18** | The Solo Threat | 1 High CR | **Rampage:** Does not slow down for anything. Smashes through obstacles to reach the weakest or most isolated target. |
| **19** | The Solo Threat | 1 High CR | **Stalk & Strike:** Uses the urban environment — fog, shadows, rooftops, crowd cover — to remain unseen until the optimal moment to strike. |
| **20** | Factional Conflict | Two factions — roll twice on Category | **Crossfire:** Two groups already fighting when the party arrives. Both ignore the party if left alone; both turn on the PCs if anyone picks a side. |
