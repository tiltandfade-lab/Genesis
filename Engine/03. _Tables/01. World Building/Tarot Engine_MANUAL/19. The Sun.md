### **XIX. The Sun: Trigger Effects**

The Sun represents success, vitality, joy, and the burning light of truth. It is the card of peak energy and total visibility. Under this card, the dungeon is no longer a place of shadows and secrets; it becomes a radiant, blistering environment where everything—both good and bad—is exposed to the light.

---

**The Constant: "The Blinding Zenith"**

In the domain of the Sun, there is no place for shadows to hide.

- **The Effect:** The entire dungeon is considered "Bright Light." All creatures have **Advantage** on Wisdom (Perception) checks and **Saving Throws against being Frightened**.
    
- **The Toll:** "Overexposure." The light is physically taxing. All creatures have **Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth)** checks. Additionally, any creature that starts its turn in a room without "Cover" takes **1 radiant damage** as the heat of the Sun begins to bake the environment.

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

**The Constant: "The Era of Transparency"**

The faction has moved beyond secrecy. They are now operating with "Aggressive Honesty," making their plans public and demanding total transparency from all allies and enemies.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**Faction Behavior (The Sun)**|**Narrative Implication**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Great Audit**|The faction publishes all their secrets, debts, and crimes, and demands the same from the party.|
|**6-9**|Less Grounded|**The Solar Expansion**|The faction begins a massive construction project to "burn away the night," building lighthouses in every dark forest.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Universal Truth**|The faction casts a spell that makes it physically impossible for anyone in their territory to speak anything but the absolute truth.|

### **Trigger: Accepting Binding Agreements**

**The Constant: "The Vow of Day"**

The bargain grants you the vitality of the sun, but you lose the ability to exist in shadows. You have **Advantage on all rolls in Bright Light**, but you are **Blinded** whenever you are in Dim Light or Darkness.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**The Bargain's Mutation (The Sun)**|**The "Gift"**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**Aura of Health**|You are immune to disease and poison. The cost: You can never again benefit from a Long Rest unless it is taken outdoors.|
|**6-9**|Less Grounded|**The Radiant Heart**|You can cast _Daylight_ at will. The cost: You lose your shadow; people find your presence "Uncanny" and have Disadvantage on Persuasion.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Main Character Spotlight**|A literal beam of light follows you from the sky. You gain +5 to all stats, but the DM gets to roll for a "Random Encounter" every 10 minutes.|

### **Trigger: World Shifts**

**The Constant: "The Long Summer"**

The seasons stop. The world enters a period of eternal growth and blistering heat. Food is plentiful, but water becomes the world's most valuable currency.

| ------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1-5** | Grounded      | **The Age of Harvest**                | Crops grow three times as fast. Famine is eradicated, but the world's forests begin to catch fire from the heat. |
| **6-9** | Less Grounded | **The Vanishing Stars**               | The sun becomes so bright it is visible through the earth at night. There is no more darkness on the planet.     |
| **10**  | **Insane**    | **The Smiling Sun**                   | The sun gains a face and begins to commentate on the world’s events in a voice that can be heard by everyone.    |
