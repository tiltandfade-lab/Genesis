### **IV. The Emperor: Trigger Effects**

The Emperor represents the ultimate authority—structure, law, stability, and the iron-fisted rule of the material world. When this card is drawn, the world loses its fluidity. Chaos is suppressed, and everything is forced into a rigid, uncompromising hierarchy.

---

**The Constant: "The Iron Law"**

The Emperor demands order. Randomness is a crime within his domain.

- **The Effect:** Any roll that results in a **Natural 1** is treated as a **10**. There is no "fumbling" under the Emperor’s gaze.
    
- **The Toll:** Sovereignty has a price. Any roll that results in a **Natural 20** is treated as an **11**. Exceptional success is prohibited; only consistent, standardized performance is allowed.

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

**The Constant: "Total Annexation"**

The faction has ceased all diplomacy. They are now focused on absolute unification and the absorption of all smaller groups into their "Grand Empire."

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**Faction Behavior (The Emperor)**|**Narrative Implication**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Law-Givers**|The faction begins enforcing strict new laws in the region (curfews, taxes, weapon bans).|
|**6-9**|Textured|**The Great Wall**|The faction starts building a massive, magically-accelerated wall that divides the map in two.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Hive Sovereignty**|The faction's leader gains the ability to speak through the mouths of every citizen simultaneously.|

The world settles. Civil wars end, borders are drawn in stone, and the unpredictable forces of magic are heavily regulated or suppressed.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**The Nature of the Shift (The Emperor)**|**World Change**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Continental Peace**|All wars end. Trade flourishes, but personal freedoms are restricted by a global police force.|
|**6-9**|Textured|**The Stone Sky**|The clouds turn into solid, unmoving slabs of grey granite. It never rains again; it only "sweats."|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Grid**|The entire world is divided into a perfect 100x100ft grid. You can only move in straight lines and right angles.|
