### The Crawling Claw: The Macabre Sentries

In an investigation-heavy campaign, a crawling claw is rarely a random encounter. Because these undead are deliberately created by spellcasters from severed hands or paws, their presence is a direct indicator of necromantic activity, a hidden laboratory, or an active assassination plot.

**Confirmed Facts:**

- **The Batch Mind:** Claws have no culture or society, but they possess a basic telepathic link with all other claws created in their specific "batch". When one claw detects a victim or target, all other claws in the area immediately move to assist it.
- **Unnatural Strength & Leaping:** Despite their size, crawling claws are incredibly strong (Strength 13) and can leap up to 15 feet to ambush prey.
- **Turn Immunity:** Unlike standard undead, crawling claws are completely immune to effects that turn undead.

**Campaign Assumptions (Integration into your Setting):**

- **The Perfect Infiltrator:** Because they are Tiny and possess a climb speed, crawling claws are the ultimate thieves and assassins. They can easily slide down chimneys, crawl through sewer grates, and unlock doors from the inside for their masters.
- **Literal Executioners:** Claws follow a single, brief instruction from their creator. They cannot adapt to complex situations, making their behavior highly predictable once the players figure out their programmed "rule."

### Crawling Claw

_Tiny Undead, Neutral Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 2 (1d4)
- **Speed:** 20 ft., climb 20 ft.
- **STR:** 13 (+1) | **DEX:** 14 (+2) | **CON:** 11 (+0) | **INT:** 5 (-3) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 4 (-3)
- **Damage Immunities:** Necrotic, Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Incapacitated, Poisoned
- **Senses:** Blindsight 30 ft. (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Understands Common but can't speak
- **Challenge:** 0 (10 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Actions**

- **Slam:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 2 necrotic damage.

**Tactics (The Ceiling Drop):** Crawling claws rely on their blindsight and climb speed to hide in absolute darkness on ceilings or the undersides of furniture. Because of their "batch mind," they do not attack one at a time. They wait until an intruder is fully in the room, then drop simultaneously, using their unnatural strength to crush throats or gouge eyes.

### Encounter Scaffolds & Investigation Tables

#### d6 The Maker's Directive (Rules of Engagement)

A batch of claws is given a specific condition under which they activate or a target they must destroy. _(Roll or choose one to establish how the PCs can trigger or bypass the encounter)._

|d6|Programmed Command|Tactical Behavior & Bypass Condition|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**"Strangle the uninitiated."**|The claws ignore anyone wearing the specific holy symbol or guild signet of their creator. They drop from the ceiling onto anyone else.|
|**2**|**"Retrieve the stolen ledger."**|The claws are not trying to kill the PCs; they are actively attempting to grapple the character holding a specific quest item, rip it from their grasp, and flee up the walls.|
|**3**|**"Lock the vault."**|The claws wait until the PCs enter a room, then simultaneously leap onto the door mechanism, using their combined strength to lock it and barricade the exit.|
|**4**|**"Silence the witness."**|The claws were sent to assassinate an NPC in their sleep. The PCs arrive just as the claws are crawling through the NPC's window.|
|**5**|**"Guard the arcane focus."**|The claws form a literal, dormant pile around a wizard's spellbook or staff. They only animate if the item is touched.|
|**6**|**"Dismantle the light."**|The claws are programmed to target light sources first. They will leap at torches, lanterns, and characters casting _Light_ to plunge the room into darkness so their Blindsight gives them the advantage.|

#### d4 Macabre Clues (The Investigation Layer)

Tracking crawling claws requires looking for tiny, localized disturbances rather than large footprints.

|d4|Discovery|Clue / Campaign Hook|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Inside Job**|The party finds a murdered merchant in a perfectly locked room. A DC 13 Investigation check reveals tiny, bloody smudges on the _inside_ of the chimney flue and the door's deadbolt.|
|**2**|**The Moving Object**|While watching a suspect's house, the PCs see a heavy iron key or a small lockbox slowly sliding across the floor in the shadows. A batch of claws is working in concert to carry it away (capable of lifting 5 lbs per claw).|
|**3**|**The Graverobber's Camp**|The remains of a camp where the only things missing from the excavated skeletons are the hands and animal paws.|
|**4**|**The Alchemical Preservative**|A shattered glass jar smelling of formaldehyde. The claws were dormant inside it, waiting to be unleashed when the jar was broken.|
