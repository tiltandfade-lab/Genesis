| d12 | Distortion | How NPCs Tell It | DM Prompt |
|-----|-----------|-------------------|-----------|
| 1 | The Blame Shifted | The real cause is ignored; locals blame someone easier to hate — a foreigner, a rival guild, an old enemy. | *Who do locals wrongly blame? How does confronting the scapegoat reveal the real trail?* |
| 2 | The Numbers Grew | One became many. A single incident became a wave. A lone actor became a conspiracy. The scale is wildly exaggerated. | *What do locals claim the scale is? How does the exaggeration create false urgency or misdirect resources?* |
| 3 | Cause and Effect Reversed | People think the consequence caused the catalyst. The symptom is treated as the disease. | *What do locals think caused this? How does following their logic lead to a dead end or a different problem?* |
| 4 | A Detail Became the Story | One vivid detail — a color, a symbol, a sound — overwhelmed the actual event. Locals fixate on the detail, not the truth. | *What detail are locals obsessed with? What does it actually mean vs. what they think it means?* |
| 5 | The Victim Became the Villain | Whoever was harmed is now suspected of having caused it — through recklessness, greed, or secret wickedness. | *Why do locals distrust the victim? What evidence (real or imagined) supports their suspicion?* |
| 6 | The Supernatural Explanation | A mundane cause has been attributed to magic, divine punishment, curses, or planar influence. People are praying instead of investigating. | *What supernatural cause do locals believe? How does this prevent them from seeing the real (possibly mundane) explanation?* |
| 7 | The Cover-Up Is the Story | The actual event is forgotten. What locals are angry about is the perceived cover-up — the silence, the missing witnesses, the sealed records. | *What do locals think is being hidden? Is there actually a cover-up, or just incompetence mistaken for conspiracy?* |
| 8 | The Timeline Collapsed | Events from different days or weeks have been compressed into a single dramatic night. Separate incidents are now one big event. | *What separate events have locals merged? How does untangling the timeline reveal the real pattern?* |
| 9 | The Hero Was Invented | Someone who wasn't there — or who played a minor role — has become the central figure of the story. They may be playing along. | *Who is the invented hero? Are they exploiting the false credit, or are they as confused as everyone else?* |
| 10 | The Motive Was Romanticized | The actual motive (greed, spite, accident) has been replaced with something nobler or more dramatic — revenge, love, honor, prophecy. | *What do locals think the motive was? How does the romantic version make the real perpetrator harder to identify?* |
| 11 | The Location Moved | Locals place the event somewhere more dramatic, important, or familiar — not where it actually happened. The real site is ignored. | *Where do locals think it happened? What evidence at the real location has gone unexamined?* |
| 12 | Someone Was Erased | A key participant has been forgotten or deliberately removed from every retelling. Their role is attributed to someone else or to fate. | *Who was erased? Why — were they protected, or did they arrange their own disappearance from the story?* |
