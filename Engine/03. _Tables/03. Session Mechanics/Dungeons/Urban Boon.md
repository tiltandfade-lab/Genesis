| d20 | Boon | Description | Mechanical Benefit |
|-----|------|-------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Grateful Healer | A back-alley herbalist or retired cleric patches the party up free of charge after hearing their story. | Each PC may spend one Hit Die and add their proficiency bonus to the roll. |
| 2 | Insider's Shortcut | A local kid or street sweeper shows the party a route that bypasses the next obstacle entirely. | Skip the next Problem or Hazard encounter; the party arrives at the following segment without complication. |
| 3 | Sympathetic Guard | A watch member who owes the party (or hates the threat faction) looks the other way and whispers a warning. | Reveal one upcoming Enemy encounter's composition and location before it triggers. Heat -1. |
| 4 | Safe House Offered | A nervous shopkeeper, priest, or retired adventurer offers a hidden room for the party to rest undisturbed. | The party may take a Short Rest here with no encounter roll. One PC gains Inspiration. |
| 5 | Lucky Find | A dropped pouch, forgotten stash, or unlooted cache turns up in the party's path. | Gain 3d6 gp and one common consumable (potion of healing, antitoxin, or oil of slipperiness). |
| 6 | Merchant's Favor | A trader the party helped (or who hates the threat faction) offers goods at half price or a single free item. | One PC may purchase any common magic item or mundane equipment at 50% cost, or gain one free potion of healing. |
| 7 | Faction Defector | A low-ranking member of the threat faction approaches the party wanting out — and brings information. | Reveal the threat faction's current plan, next move, or boss location. The defector can serve as a guide for 1d4 segments. |
| 8 | Street Performer's Distraction | A musician, juggler, or fire-eater creates a crowd that draws enemy attention away from the party. | The party has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks for the next segment. Heat -1. |
| 9 | Shared Meal | A tavern keeper, street cook, or generous family invites the party to eat. The food is surprisingly good and the company warm. | Each PC gains 1d4+1 temporary hit points. One PC gains Inspiration. The cook knows one useful local rumor (free Rumor roll). |
| 10 | Warded Ground | The party stumbles into a consecrated courtyard, old ward circle, or protective rune zone that still holds. | While here, creatures have advantage on saving throws against magic. Undead and fiends cannot enter willingly. Safe Short Rest. |
| 11 | Retired Adventurer | A scarred veteran recognizes fellow troublemakers and shares hard-won advice about the local threat. | Gain advantage on the next ability check or saving throw made against the threat faction. One PC gains Inspiration. |
| 12 | Timely Rainfall | A sudden downpour clears the streets, douses fires, and washes away tracks — both the party's and their enemies'. | Heat resets to 0. All Wisdom (Perception) checks to track the party are made with disadvantage for 1d4 hours. |
| 13 | Urchin Network | A gang of street kids adopts the party as their current heroes and volunteers to run messages, scout, and cause diversions. | The party gains a free Investigation or Perception check (with advantage) at any point in the next 3 segments. Heat -1. |
| 14 | Temple Sanctuary | A priest or acolyte offers the party sanctuary and minor healing, asking nothing but a prayer or small donation. | Each PC may recover one spent Hit Die. One creature is cured of one disease or one condition (Poisoned, Blinded, or Deafened). |
| 15 | Blackmarket Tip | A fence, smuggler, or information broker offers a freebie to build goodwill — or because they hate the same people. | Reveal one secret about the current segment's location or the threat faction's operations. Gain access to a black market for this district. |
| 16 | Unexpected Ally | Someone the party helped earlier (or a stranger who witnessed their good deed) shows up at a critical moment to help. | Gain a temporary ally (use Commoner, Guard, or Spy stat block) for the next 2 segments. They act on their own initiative. |
| 17 | Fortuitous Collapse | A wall crumbles, a gate jams open, or a locked door breaks — pure luck clears a barrier the party was about to face. | Automatically bypass one locked door, barricade, or physical obstacle in this segment. No check required. |
| 18 | Master Craftsman's Gift | An artisan gives the party a custom tool, modified weapon, or protective charm as thanks for their work in the district. | One PC receives a minor magical boon lasting 24 hours: +1 to one ability score, resistance to one damage type, or darkvision 60ft. |
| 19 | Prophetic Dream | During rest, one PC receives a vivid dream showing a crucial detail about the finale — a weakness, a layout, or a betrayal. | Gain one concrete piece of information about the finale encounter: the boss's weakness, the location layout, or a hidden escape route. |
| 20 | The City Itself Helps | A statue's pointing hand, a wind-blown sign, a stray cat leading the way — the city seems to want the party to succeed. | The party automatically succeeds on their next navigation, investigation, or social check related to the main objective. One PC gains Inspiration. |
