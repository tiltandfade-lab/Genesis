#realm-items-cosmic
> **PROVISIONAL — DRAFT re-key, Adam taste pass pending (docs/COSMIC-REKEY.md).** The COSMIC realm's
> item universe (docs/BREACH.md §2d, BATCH3-GUARDRAILS.md J2 restructure). **Re-keyed 2026-07-08**
> from Lovecraft-void into **Egyptian funerary cosmology + Western hermetic esoterica (Agrippa-style
> correspondence) + Enochian angelic myth** (Adam's ruling). The SPINE survives the re-key intact —
> *symbols are load-bearing and the wonder answers back*: unfinishable names became **true names**,
> the drowned language became **the tongue of the Aethyrs / the language of Thoth**, arguing geometry
> became **sacred geometry that actively casts**. Doers-only (ADAM-REVIEW-2 §3/§3b; pointers in
> `docs/REALM-PLOT-ITEMS-PARKED.md`; coherence laws: wired physics only, cross-breach R1s, no
> undefined referents, Volatile = live-danger items). d50; breach draws inside a `cosmic`-tagged walk
> roll THIS table; legacy Outlandish d300 stays the anachronism grab-bag. Every item names its FRAME
> (existing `data/items.js` id); none invented (frames unchanged by the re-key — render is untouched).
> **Band shares run hotter than H2's baseline by construction** — this realm's register leans Strange
> by nature, so the mundane floor is kept plain and quiet by contrast (grounded objects from a place
> that otherwise doesn't sound like it should have any). **Four new-key hero items** live in the
> Strange band (d21–24): the scarab that weighs choices, the deck with one extra card (the Tarot-Engine
> tie-in — see docs/COSMIC-REKEY.md §Tarot), the squared circle, and the chalk that draws doors.
> **"The Left Gauntlet of the Choir" is the J3b-anchored ladder** ("Choir" now reads as the Enochian
> angelic choirs of the Aethyrs), reflavored not restatted, landed among the signature wonders. Rank
> ladders sit on enchanted + signature rows; reality-breaking rungs floor at L9. d50, Commitment ceiling.
