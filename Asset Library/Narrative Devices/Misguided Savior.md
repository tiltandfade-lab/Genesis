# Narrative Device – Misguided Savior

## Core Concept

A character who believes they are saving others, restoring balance, or healing a wound — but whose actions cause harm due to incomplete knowledge, manipulation, or flawed assumptions.

The Misguided Savior is not driven by greed, cruelty, or power.
They are driven by conviction.

---

## Structural Function

This device creates:
- Moral tension instead of simple villainy.
- Emotional weight in confrontation.
- Opportunities for persuasion, redemption, or tragedy.
- Conflict rooted in belief, not malice.

It transforms a fight from “defeat the evil” into:
“Stop someone who believes they are right.”

---

## Key Elements

### 1. A Wound
The character identifies a real problem:
- Spiritual separation
- Social injustice
- Cosmic imbalance
- Corruption or decay

The wound must be real.  
Their response is what is flawed.

---

### 2. A Revelation (Incomplete or Manipulated)
They receive:
- A vision
- A prophecy
- A divine sign
- Forbidden knowledge
- A whispered promise

The revelation is:
- Partial
- Selectively curated
- Misinterpreted
- Or deliberately manipulated

They are not lying — they are convinced.

---

### 3. A Justified Sacrifice
They believe:
- The ends justify the means.
- Loss is temporary.
- Suffering is necessary.
- Sacrifice leads to transcendence.

They frame harm as healing.

---

### 4. A Blind Spot
They cannot see:
- The full consequences.
- The deeper force exploiting them.
- The arrogance in assuming control.
- That the “wound” may serve a purpose.

This blind spot sustains the delusion.

---

## Player Experience

The Misguided Savior creates space for:
- Insight checks revealing withheld truth.
- Persuasion attempts mid-conflict.
- Moral decision points.
- Regret or redemption arcs.
- Tragic deaths that feel heavy.

Combat becomes ideological as well as physical.

---

## Variations

- **Manipulated Idealist** – Acting under influence of higher force.
- **Overcorrecting Reformer** – Trying to fix injustice through extreme measures.
- **Faithful Extremist** – Believes divine will demands radical action.
- **Grieving Redeemer** – Trying to undo a personal loss at any cost.

---

## Design Warnings

- Do not remove player agency by hiding all clues.
- Seed subtle signs of instability or omission.
- Avoid cartoon villain monologues.
- Keep motivations coherent and emotionally grounded.

The power of this device lies in plausibility.

---

## Reusability

Applicable to:
- Clerics and prophets
- Political reformers
- Arcane scholars
- War heroes
- Cult leaders
- Planar guardians

Any character who believes:
“I am doing this for the greater good.”

---

## Short Definition

The Misguided Savior is a tragic antagonist whose desire to heal or restore balance leads them to cause harm through incomplete understanding or manipulation.

They are not evil.

They are wrong.
