# Jax Rusk

*(Asset: bandit_charmer_moral_entangler)*

## Plot Vector

**Primary Pillar:** Bandits  
**Secondary Pillars:** Cult, Economic Elite  

**Function:**  
Lures PCs into morally gray operations.  
Normalizes shadow trade.  
Creates “fun” jobs that escalate into cult-adjacent consequences.

---

## Level 1 — Immediate Table Presence

**Race / Background:** Human; born in the frontier town  
**Role:** Independent smuggler and fixer  

**Visual Quirk:** Always carries a polished coin he flips casually  
**Mood:** Upbeat, amused by everything  
**Demeanor:** Leans in when speaking, laughs easily  

**Hook:** Offers a simple, well-paying courier job  
**Talent:** Exceptional at reading people  
**Unrealized Potential:** Yes  

---

## Level 2 — Structural Pressure

**Formative Trauma:** Grew up watching family lose everything to political maneuvering  
**Formative Grace:** Learned to survive through charm and negotiation  

**Want:** Stay untouchable  
**Fear:** Being pinned down by one faction  

**Faction Tie:** Works with bandits but sells to merchants and cult couriers alike  
**Influence Weight:** 2 — Social Network  
**Resource Control:** Access to off-ledger contracts  

**If Ignored (Personal):** Aligns more firmly with darker forces  
**If Ignored (Regional):** Smuggling routes become harder to track  

---

## Level 3 — Secrets

**Secret:** Has unknowingly delivered ritual components for the cult  
**Leverage:** Knows which elites quietly hire bandits  
**If Cornered:** Smiles, lies, and disappears before violence starts  

---

## Potential Quest / Adventure Tie-Ins

- Pays players to move sealed crates through a checkpoint.
- Hires them to escort someone “who talks too much.”
- Asks them to retrieve goods from a “misplaced caravan.”
- Later reveals one job tied to a disappearance site.
- Introduces them to a bandit gathering that feels like a party.

---

## Notes for Play

- Never threatens directly.
- Makes everything feel like an opportunity.
- Calls players by friendly nicknames quickly.
- Avoids discussing the marsh seriously.
- If exposed, reframes wrongdoing as “everyone’s making a living.”
