### Monster Response to Being Hunted (roll 1d10)

| d10 | Response |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Flees immediately |
| 2 | Lures party toward hazard |
| 3 | Splits group using terrain |
| 4 | Calls or signals others |
| 5 | Plays dead or hides |
| 6 | Defends nest or food cache |
| 7 | Bargains through display or mimicry |
| 8 | Attacks weakest-looking target |
| 9 | Retreats to digest/recover |
| 10 | Observes first; strikes later |
^monster-behavior-if-hunted
