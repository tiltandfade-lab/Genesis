### **XIV. Temperance: Trigger Effects**

Temperance represents alchemy, moderation, and the perfect blending of opposites. Under this card, the dungeon becomes a place of stabilization and synthesis, where the five elements (**spirit, air, fire, water, and earth**) are no longer distinct forces but are being fused into a singular "Great Work."

---

**The Constant: "The Golden Mean"**

In the domain of Temperance, extremes are suppressed in favor of the middle ground.

- **The Effect:** Any d20 roll that results in a **10 or 11** is treated as a **Natural 20**. Consistency and moderation are divinely rewarded.
    
- **The Toll:** "The Dampening." Any **Natural 1 or Natural 20** is treated as a **10**. The universe refuses to allow catastrophic failure or miraculous success; it demands the steady path.

### **Trigger: Faction Arc Begins**

**The Constant: "The Great Reconciliation"**

The faction has ceased its extremist views. They are currently acting as mediators, attempting to blend rival ideologies or even rival species into a single, stable society.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**Faction Behavior (Temperance)**|**Narrative Implication**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**The Alchemical Guild**|The faction begins a massive project to turn lead into gold, threatening to collapse the regional economy.|
|**6-9**|Textured|**The Peace-Makers**|The faction forcibly "blends" two warring villages, magically binding the citizens so they cannot harm one another.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The World-Soup**|The faction believes that "Diversity is Chaos" and begins a ritual to melt all living things into a single, peaceful biomass.|

### **Trigger: Accepting Binding Agreements**

**The Constant: "The Vow of the Middle"**

The bargain grants you alchemical mastery, but you lose the ability to feel "Highs" or "Lows." You are immune to being **Charmed** or **Frightened**, but you can no longer gain the benefits of **Inspiration**.

|**d10**|**Magnitude**|**The Bargain's Mutation (Temperance)**|**The "Gift"**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1-5**|Grounded|**Elemental Synthesis**|You can spend a bonus action to merge two damage types into one (e.g., Fire-Water damage).|
|**6-9**|Textured|**The Third State**|You can turn your body into a "Slush" of elements, becoming immune to critical hits and grapples.|
|**10**|**Insane**|**The Average Hero**|All of your Ability Scores become 10. However, you are magically incapable of failing any check with a DC of 15 or lower.|

### **Trigger: World Shifts**

**The Constant: "The Great Blending"**

The world settles. Elemental extremes vanish—volcanoes cool, oceans calm, and the winds become a steady breeze. The world is safe, but strangely uniform.

| ------- | ------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1-5** | Grounded      | **The Global Truce**                     | Every war in the world stops for one year. Trade and education flourish as the "Great Work" of civilization begins.                   |
| **6-9** | Textured | **The Elemental Merge**                  | The planes begin to bleed together. You might find a forest where the trees are made of **Water** and the rain is made of **Earth**.  |
| **10**  | **Insane**    | **The Universal Beige**                  | All colors in the world begin to fade into a neutral, harmonious grey. Everyone begins to look and speak exactly like their neighbor. |
