# Table Gaps — the tables the new systems now want

## §0. Provenance + lanes (Adam, 2026-07-01)

Census: zero referenced-but-missing table ids; the gaps are CONCEPTUAL — tables no system had,
that tonight's specs created demand for. Adam approved all four gap sets for speccing, samples-first
(§6). **Thin-but-high-traffic expansions** (in-building-complications ×5 rows, the six walk-on job
d6s, dungeon-reinforcements, dungeon-lore-art/-content, myth-truth-vs-false, d100-air):
**Sonnet-drafts to the existing rows' voice, PROVISIONAL, 10-row spot-checks by Adam** — his deep
craft sweep still owns the 210 ungraded. **Compile hygiene:** the 1-row packed-format artifacts
(`birth-order-spark` class) get the name-megatable treatment — type-stamp or parser fix so fake
tables leave the compiled set.

## §1. Chase Complications (d100, Fork-class, spice-graded; env-flavored)

**Demand:** MONSTER-TACTICS made morale-flee binding — fleeing foes now happen every fight, and
nothing rolls when someone runs. **Mechanics (thin):** a chase = a **gap clock** (3 segments;
pursuer closes on won opposed checks, quarry opens on lost). Each round rolls ONE complication
(this table), flavored by env kind (urban/wild/dungeon variants can be columns, not three tables);
the complication sets that round's check or cost. Ends: gap closed (contact — combat/parley),
gap opened ×3 (away — the fled foe persists soft, recall fodder), or either side yields.
Player rolls their own dice always; foe side script-rolled like enemy attacks.

## §2. Distant Word (d100, Commitment-class; the World Turn's rumor arm)

**Demand:** drift is lazy-local — no news ever leaves a place. **Mechanics:** on tavern/crier
contact, downtime, or session start, roll: **~60% of rows are DISTORTION LENSES bound to a real
ledger fact** (script picks a salience-weighted `clock`/`outcome`/`drift` entry from a NON-current
node/faction; the row says HOW the telling garbles it — exaggerated, misattributed, inverted,
delayed); **~40% pure far-off color** (unverifiable, atmospheric). The lens keeps rumors
anti-drift: the FACT is engine-owned canon, only the distortion is flavor — and the DM knows both
(`dm` holds the true entry). Feeds the criers/pilgrims real content; the world becomes audible
from a barstool.

## §3. Downtime Ledger (d100, Commitment-class; the week-unit)

**Demand:** lodging now costs, montage passes days, and a week in town does nothing. **Mechanics:**
player declares intent (work / carouse / research / train / lie low) → montage passes the week
(lodging charged) → ONE roll here for what the week actually yielded; rows carry payout hooks
(gold ± scaled by place tier, a Distant-Word roll, a contact minted from the ambient pool, a
condition, a thread). Intent biases interpretation, not the die (one table, not five).

## §4. Festival / Holy-Day (d100, Commitment-class) + §5. Shrine & Omen (d100, Commitment-class)

**Demand:** the urban life beat (skins flirt with it; economy ties — prices, full inns) and the
audit's "Myth content thin" flag (world-genesis rolls a myth play never touches). **Festival
mechanics:** rolled on arrival drift (a Textured+ drift row can BE a festival) or downtime; carries
market effects (`price ±`, lodging full) + a custom + a complication hook. **Shrine & Omen
mechanics:** a walk/interior/place dressing lane; rows bind to the WORLD'S OWN rolled myth + taboo
by reference (the row is a frame; the world's myth fills it) — the myth finally circulates.

## §6. 5-BAND SAMPLES — FOR ADAM'S REVIEW (the gate on all five tables)

**Chase Complications**
- *Grounded* — **A cart swings out of the wheelwright's.** Vault it (Athletics) or lose the gap; the carter's curses mark your route for anyone asking.
- *Textured* — **A funeral procession fills the lane wall to wall.** Barge through and the quarter remembers; go around and the quarry gains a street.
- *Strange* — **The alley you both entered is not the alley you exit** — and the quarry ran it like they knew.
- *Volatile* — **The rooftop tiles shear loose underfoot.** Whoever fails falls with them — two storeys, a market awning, and the crowd's full attention.
- *Mythic* — **For six heartbeats the chase crosses somewhere thin** — black sand, a bell, no sky — and something there notices the running and joins in.

**Distant Word**
- *Grounded* — **True, mostly — the numbers doubled in the telling.** (Lens: scale exaggerated; the fact beneath is ledger-real.)
- *Textured* — **The deed is real; the doer is swapped** for whoever the teller already hated. (Lens: misattributed.)
- *Strange* — **The news arrived twice, from opposite roads, with different endings.** One teller is lying, or one road is longer than it should be.
- *Volatile* — **A survivor tells it themselves, mid-market, wounds still open** — and the telling starts a panic with legs of its own. (Lens: true, too soon, unbearable.)
- *Mythic* — **The word arrived before the event** — dated three days hence, in a dead man's handwriting, and it names a place you were planning to be.

**Downtime Ledger**
- *Grounded* — **Honest wages.** Coin scaled to the place, sore hands, and one useful acquaintance who now knows your name and work.
- *Textured* — **The carouse left a ledger.** You're owed a favor OR owe one — dice decide which, the town remembers at the worst moment.
- *Strange* — **Your research answered a different question** — one nobody had asked out loud, filed under a name you don't recognize in your own hand.
- *Volatile* — **The week is missing.** You remember paying for it and nothing else; someone wearing your face kept appointments you're now expected to honor.
- *Mythic* — **You trained under someone who died years ago.** The skill is real and stays. The grave is undisturbed. The lessons continue in dreams, and there is a fee.

**Festival / Holy-Day**
- *Grounded* — **The harvest weighing.** Scales in the square, loud bragging, a pie court with genuine stakes; prices dip for a week after.
- *Textured* — **The Lantern Debt.** Every household floats one light per debt forgiven this year; dark windows get noticed, and yours is dark.
- *Strange* — **The Quiet Day.** No one speaks until dusk, by old compact. Nobody remembers who the compact is WITH — only that once, someone broke it.
- *Volatile* — **The effigy year.** The town burns its worst citizen in effigy, by vote. This year the effigy wears a face you know.
- *Mythic* — **The god attends.** Everyone pretends this is normal. The etiquette is life-critical, unwritten, and no one will explain it to a stranger.

**Shrine & Omen**
- *Grounded* — **A wayside shrine, tended.** Fresh offerings, a plinth worn smooth where hands rest. Leave something; the road feels shorter.
- *Textured* — **The offerings are all the same object, decades deep** — keys, or teeth, or spoons. Nobody will say why it must be that.
- *Strange* — **The icon's face is freshly wet** in a dry season, and the cloth someone left to dry it is already sodden.
- *Volatile* — **The shrine is upside down** — re-consecrated in deliberate inversion by someone who knew exactly what they were doing. The local taboo runs backward within sight of it.
- *Mythic* — **Prayers here are answered in advance.** The granted thing arrives before the asking. The debt is presumed, and collection is patient.
