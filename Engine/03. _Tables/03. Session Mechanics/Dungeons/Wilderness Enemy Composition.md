| **d20** | **Composition** | **Roster** | **Tactical Deployment** |
| :------ | :-------------- | :--------- | :---------------------- |
| **1** | The Swarm / Horde | 2d6+2 Low CR | **Overwhelm:** Rush in a tight mass. They use the Help action to grant Advantage to each other and target the smallest or least-armored party member. |
| **2** | The Swarm / Horde | 2d6+2 Low CR | **The Enveloping Wave:** The horde splits into two pincers immediately, bypassing the armored frontline to swarm the unarmored backline. |
| **3** | The Staggered Mob | 2d4 Low CR | **Reinforcements:** Half the group is present. The other half arrives Round 2 from a different direction — behind the party or from a flanking position. |
| **4** | The Staggered Mob | 2d4 Low CR | **Drip Feed:** Only 1d4 start on the map. An additional 1d4 arrive on Initiative 0 each round from varying edges until the roster is depleted. |
| **5** | Frontline & Artillery | 1d4 Mid CR melee + 1d4 Low CR ranged | **Combined Arms:** Melee closes immediately to pin the party down while ranged units fire from cover behind them. |
| **6** | Frontline & Artillery | 1d4 Mid CR melee + 1d4 Low CR ranged | **Draw Them In:** Artillery fires once then retreats behind total cover. Melee waits in ambush at a choke point, ready for the party to pursue. |
| **7** | Frontline & Artillery | 1d4 Mid CR melee + 1d4 Low CR ranged | **Phalanx & Volley:** Melee forms a tight line using Dodge to block a choke point. Artillery fires over them into the party's backline. |
| **8** | The Ambush Ring | 1d4+1 Mid CR | **Surround:** All enemies begin Hidden. They deploy in a full ring around the party and attack simultaneously from all sides. |
| **9** | The Ambush Ring | 1d4+1 Mid CR | **The Drop:** Enemies are positioned above — in trees, on cliffs, or on ledges — with three-quarters cover. They open with ranged attacks before dropping into melee. |
| **10** | Boss & Fodder | 1 High CR + 2d4 Low CR | **Meatshields:** Fodder forms a physical wall in front of the boss, providing Half Cover. The boss uses ranged attacks or abilities from behind them. |
| **11** | Boss & Fodder | 1 High CR + 2d4 Low CR | **Sacrificial Pawns:** The boss aggressively drives fodder into hazards or uses them to bait Opportunity Attacks, intentionally creating gaps to strike the party. |
| **12** | Boss & Bodyguards | 1 High CR + 2 Mid CR | **Bodyguard Phalanx:** Guards stay adjacent to the boss, Readying actions to intercept anyone who enters their reach. The boss focuses on the highest-threat PC. |
| **13** | Boss & Bodyguards | 1 High CR + 2 Mid CR | **Pincer Support:** Boss holds the center using Dodge or defensive abilities. Guards split to aggressively flank and dismantle the party's healer. |
| **14** | The Controller & Pets | 1 Mid CR caster + 1d4 Low CR beasts | **Seek & Destroy:** The controller stays at maximum range using spells or abilities. Pets rapidly close to Grapple or Shove spellcasters. |
| **15** | The Controller & Pets | 1 Mid CR caster + 1d4 Low CR beasts | **The Living Wall:** Pets take the Dodge action in a line to block the party's advance. The controller casts unimpeded from the far side. |
| **16** | The Elite Duo | 2 Mid-High CR | **Tandem Strike:** Both creatures focus all attacks on one PC at a time, eliminating them quickly before moving to the next target. |
| **17** | The Elite Duo | 2 Mid-High CR | **Divide & Conquer:** The duo splits to engage the party from opposite flanks, forcing divided damage output and healing resources. |
| **18** | The Solo Apex | 1 High CR | **Rampage:** A single massive threat. It accepts Opportunity Attacks to push past the frontline and reach the most vulnerable party member. |
| **19** | The Solo Apex | 1 High CR | **Hit & Run:** Uses superior mobility, flight, or burrowing to strike a PC then retreat out of range or into total cover, forcing Ready actions. |
| **20** | Interrupted Conflict | Two factions — roll twice on Category | **Crossfire:** Two groups are already fighting when the party arrives. They ignore the party if left alone but turn on the PCs if anyone intervenes or tries to push through. |
