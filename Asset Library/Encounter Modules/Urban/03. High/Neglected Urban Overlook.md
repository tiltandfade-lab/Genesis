# Neglected Urban Overlook
> *Urban High. A structurally compromised balcony in bright daylight — someone is about to fall, and something below smells wrong.*

## 30-Second Run

A raised overlook or balcony — neglected, rusting iron straps, wind howling through the gaps. Bright daylight makes the whole thing feel exposed and wrong. A bystander is in immediate danger from active structural failure. The charcoal route sketch on the wall suggests someone planned to use this spot. The low hum vibrating through the floor suggests something buried beneath it. And something in this area smells like brimstone or rot — forewarning of a hazard that hasn't shown itself yet.

---

## Setup

**Space:** Raised overlook or balcony (15 ft above the street or alley floor). Rusted iron keg rack still bolted to the railing — the rust is the tell that the structure is compromised. A cart or wagon sits below on the street, usable as landing pad or cover.
**Atmosphere:** Wind howling through gaps in the broken parapet. Bright exposed light. Signs of long neglect: crumbling stone, discolored iron. A charcoal sketch on the wall surface — a route or symbol, not decorative.
**NPCs Present:** Bystander in Peril (standing on compromised section), Old Master Painter (just off-scene — their sign was just stolen, they're distracted).
**If Ignored:** The bystander falls — 2d10 bludgeoning. The charcoal route goes unread. The hazard warning goes unnoticed until whatever it's warning about attacks.

## Segments

### Segment 1: The Structural Hazard

The bystander is in active danger. The structure will fail on its own if disturbed.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 13 Investigation:** Spot the structural weakness — the crack in the stone, the failing iron strap. If unnoticed and the area is disturbed (combat, heavy movement, a shove), the section collapses: DC 13 Dex save or 2d10 bludgeoning damage.
- **DC 12 Athletics (reaching the bystander):** Cross the compromised section without triggering a collapse.
- **DC 13 Persuasion or a clear warning:** Get the bystander to move away from the edge before the structure fails.
- If they fall before rescue: the cart below serves as a partial break — DC 11 Dex save for half damage (bystander takes 1d10 instead of 2d10).

**Escalation:** Any creature on the overlook during a combat shove, an AoE spell, or a heavy fall check: DC 11 Str save or the keg rack pulls free and the attached section fails — 15-ft drop, Dex DC 13 or 2d10 bludgeoning.

---

### Segment 2: The Charcoal Sketch

The route sketch on the wall is deliberate — not decorative graffiti.

**Skill Checks**
- **DC 11 Investigation:** Recognize it as a path sketch — a route marked from this point to somewhere else. Destination unclear.
- **DC 13 Investigation:** Recognize specific landmarks in the sketch — this route passes through areas the party knows (or should). Someone was planning an approach to a location.
- **DC 14 History or Streetwise:** The symbol in the corner is a faction mark — identify which one.

---

### Segment 3: The Smell

**DC 13 Perception:** Detect the smell — brimstone, ozone, or rotting meat — coming from the alley below or from behind the wall.

This is a forewarning: something magical or undead is in the immediate vicinity. The party has a round or two to act on this information before whatever it is becomes apparent.

- If explored (DC 12 Investigation of the alley below or the wall): find a recently disturbed scorch mark, a glyph barely visible on stone, or cold air from a crack suggesting undead presence.

---

## Mechanics

1. **Structural Hazard:** Marked section on overlook. DC 13 Investigation to spot. Triggered by heavy impact: DC 13 Dex save or 2d10 bludgeoning + fall 15 ft.
2. **Low Hum Point:** The floor vibrates when touched at a specific point — buried civic mechanism, arcane fault, or old sealed infrastructure. DC 13 Arcana to determine which.
3. **High Ground Advantage:** The overlook gives advantage on Perception checks spotting ground-level activity, and ranged attacks have superior angle (no cover from below).
4. **Cart/Wagon Landing:** Breaking fall on the cart reduces fall damage by 1d10. DC 10 Dex check to land on it intentionally.
5. **Old Master's Theft:** A street artist nearby is distressed — their tools or payment were just stolen. Minor social thread; can be used to distract a pursuer or recruit a contact.

## Difficulty Dial

**Tier 1 (Lvl 1–3):** Structural collapse deals 1d10 bludgeoning. The odor is a false alarm — the cart covers a harmless drainage vent.
**Tier 2 (Lvl 3–6):** As written. The odor warns of 2 Shadows lurking in the alley below.
**Tier 3 (Lvl 7+):** The hum point is a Planar Seal — barely holding. The odor warns of a Wraith preparing to emerge. The bystander has unknowingly been standing over it for an hour.

## Stat Blocks

AC 10 | HP 4 | Speed 30 ft.
No combat ability. Will not move without encouragement.

**Shadow (×2, Tier 2)** — *Medium Undead, Chaotic Evil* — CR 1/2
AC 12 | HP 16 | Speed 40 ft.
**Strength Drain.** +4, 2d6+2 necrotic; Str reduced 1d4. Vulnerable to sunlight (Disadvantage in bright light).
**Tactic:** Attacks from alley below if the odor is ignored and the party lingers. Retreats to darkness if the overlook provides sunlight coverage.
