#realm-items-chrome
> **PROVISIONAL — Adam taste-pass pending (CHROME RE-KEY 2026-07-08).** The CHROME realm's item
> universe. **RE-KEYED from generic hard-sci-fi to neon-slum megacity** — subway trains, street
> gangs with theatrical identities, graffiti-as-territory, mutants in the storm drains, corporate
> super-cops, and enforcement mechs (The Warriors × Ninja Turtles × RoboCop, serial numbers filed
> off). This is a RE-KEY of the ADAM-REVIEW-2 §3/§3b doers-only table: **mechanics (frames, ranks,
> DCs, bands, damage) are unchanged — only flavor moved from space-habitat to megacity.** Doers-only
> (pointers extracted to `docs/REALM-PLOT-ITEMS-PARKED.md`; coherence laws: wired physics only,
> cross-breach R1s, no undefined referents, Volatile = live-danger items). d50; breach draws inside
> a `chrome`-tagged walk roll THIS table; the legacy Outlandish d300 stays the anachronism-intrusion
> grab-bag. Register: neon-slum megacity — subway thunder, turf sprayed in code, a badge that
> answers to a boardroom. Every item names its FRAME (existing `data/items.js` id); none invented.
> Tech consumables are CHARGE items (cells) and rust-EXEMPT — they ride the existing
> enchantment-immunity check (`rustImmune`), no engine change needed. **Band shares run hotter than
> H2's 66/20/9/4/1 by construction; Volatile/Mythic stay rare and earned against `[[SPICE-RULER]]`,
> not forced to a ratio. "Lance Pistol" and "Falling-Star Visor" are the J3b-anchored ladders**,
> landed verbatim among the signature wonders. Rank ladders (2–4 rungs, level prereqs) sit on
> enchanted + signature rows; reality-breaking rungs floor at L9. d50, Commitment ceiling.
> **New-key signature grounded items (Adam's ruling): subway token (row 12), gang colors (row 5),
> spray cans (row 30).**
