#realm-items-lost-world
> **PROVISIONAL — Adam skim pending. DRAFT RE-KEY 2026-07-08 (`craft/lost-world-rekey-draft`).** The
> LOST WORLD realm's item universe (docs/BREACH.md §2d, BATCH3-GUARDRAILS.md J2 restructure).
> **Re-keyed to the deep-time saurian dominion** (docs/LOST-WORLD-REKEY.md) — the old "vanished
> antiquity / elegy" key is retired; three strata now stack: **(1) the scaled court** rules the
> valley in the present and does not consider mammals people, yet; **(2) the ones before the
> scales** — the ruins are THEIR works, demoted one stratum down (even the great lizards walk on
> somebody's bones); **(3) the impossible layer beneath** — pre-scale wonders of a refinement no
> hand in this world can match, seeded high-band only, never named. **Mechanics/frames/bands are
> FROZEN from the pre-key table** (ADAM-REVIEW-2 §3/§3b compliance carried forward — doers-only;
> pointers still parked in `docs/REALM-PLOT-ITEMS-PARKED.md`; coherence laws: wired physics only,
> cross-breach R1s, no undefined referents, Volatile = live-danger items). Only fiction reflavored;
> four new-key rows appended (51–54): obsidian court-tech doers, a court passage-token, and the
> mount seam. d54; breach draws inside a `lost-world`-tagged walk roll THIS table; legacy Outlandish
> d300 stays the anachronism-intrusion grab-bag. Every item names its FRAME (existing `data/items.js`
> id); none invented. **Band shares run hotter than H2's baseline by construction** (see Frontier's
> frontmatter). Rank ladders sit on enchanted + signature rows; reality-breaking rungs floor at L9.
> Carries a scenario anchor (BATCH3-GUARDRAILS J2's Fenced Valley — techWorks + huntRules, now the
> scaled court's dinosaur game-preserve) reflected in item 45. d54, Commitment ceiling.
