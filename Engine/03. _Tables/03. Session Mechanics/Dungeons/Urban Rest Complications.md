| **d20** | **Result** | **Narrative & Tactical Impact** |
| ------- | ---------- | ------------------------------- |
| 1 | **Raided** | The rest is violently interrupted by a nearby Threat. No recovery benefits. |
| 2 | **Encroaching Presence** | A Threat has set up an ambush, cordon, or blockade just outside your current site. |
| 3 | **Marked** | You wake to find a symbol, chalk sign, or token left nearby; you are being tracked. |
| 4 | **The Watch Fails** | A Threat is already inside the site when you wake, having slipped past the sentry. |
| 5 | **Street Shift** | A barricade, fire, crowd surge, or patrol pattern has blocked the usual exit. You must find a new way out. |
| 6 | **Temporal Drag** | You slept far longer than intended. Roll on the Spatial/Temporal table. |
| 7 | **Stale Air** | Poor ventilation, smoke, or sewer stink makes breathing difficult. Regain only half of your usual health/energy. |
| 8 | **Local Pests** | Fleas, rats, roaches, or biting insects infested your gear. Disadvantage on your next check. |
| 9 | **Echoing Disturbance** | Distant bells, arguments, machinery, or screams prevented deep sleep. Partial recovery only. |
| 10 | **The Bone-Chill** | You wake stiff and shivering from damp, draft, or cold stone. Penalty to your next physical action. |
| 11 | **Heightened Paranoia** | Every creak sounded like footsteps. You cannot be surprised in the next segment. |
| 12 | **Auditory Hallucinations** | You hear voices through pipes, vents, or walls. Gain a cryptic lore clue, but no mental recovery. |
| 13 | **Stiff Joints** | Sleeping in a cramped loft, cellar, or alley has left you clumsy. Disadvantage on your next initiative or reflex. |
| 14 | **Vivid Omens** | Dreamt of the site’s past. Gain an "Insight" but lose your next Boon. |
| 15 | **Damaged Notes** | Rain, condensation, soot, or grime has blurred some of your recent journal entries or sketches. |
| 16 | **Heavy Silence** | The district is unnaturally still. The rest is successful, but the atmosphere is tense. |
| 17 | **Secure Position** | Your barricades, lookout, or hidden spot held perfectly. You wake feeling refreshed and safe. |
| 18 | **Unexpected Comfort** | You found a surprisingly dry loft, warm cellar, or sheltered nook. Regain one additional hit die or resource. |
| 19 | **Hidden Curiosity** | While clearing the floor to sleep, you found a small trinket, token, coin, or old key. |
| 20 | **Inspiring Respite** | The rest was perfect. Gain a temporary Boon for the duration of the next segment. |
