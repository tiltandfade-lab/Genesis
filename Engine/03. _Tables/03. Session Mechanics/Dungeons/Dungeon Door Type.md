| **d50** | **Door Type** | **Description / Skin Notes** |
| :------ | :------------ | :---------------------------- |
| **001** | Open Archway | No door. Stone-framed opening, 8' wide, 8' high. *Cave skin: rough natural arch, 6' x 5' irregular.* |
| **002** | Open Archway, Wide | No door. 12' wide dressed stone arch, 10' high. *Ruin skin: partially collapsed, one keystone missing.* |
| **003** | Collapsed Opening | No door — wall has partially given way; 4' x 5' gap, rough edges, passable with care. *Cave skin: treat as a natural fissure.* |
| **004** | Natural Cave Mouth | No door. Irregular 6' x 7' cave opening, edges damp and rough. *Fortress skin: treat as open archway with a rusted hingepost.* |
| **005** | Single Wooden Door | Standard 3' x 7' plank door, iron hinges, no window. |
| **006** | Single Wooden Door, Rotted | 3' x 7' plank door; swollen in the frame and soft at the base; opens with effort or a shoulder. |
| **007** | Wooden Door, Reinforced | 3' x 7' plank door banded with iron straps; iron ring handle; noticeably sturdier than standard. |
| **008** | Double Wooden Doors | Two 3' x 7' plank doors sharing iron hinges; one hanging slightly ajar. *Noble skin: carved panels, brass fittings.* |
| **009** | Wooden Door, Peephole | 3' x 7' door with a sliding iron cover at face height; can be opened to look through without unlatching the door. |
| **010** | Wooden Door, Low | 3' x 5' — frame deliberately shortened; must duck or crouch to pass. *Cave skin: fitted into a natural low opening.* |
| **011** | Double Wooden Doors, Damaged | One door intact, one off its upper hinge and propped against the frame; can still be barred. |
| **012** | Wooden Postern | Small 2' x 6' utility door set beside or independent of a larger opening; iron latch, no lock. *Fortress skin: iron-banded.* |
| **013** | Single Iron Door | Heavy 3' x 7' iron door, flat face, iron ring handle; hinges intact and functional. |
| **014** | Double Iron Doors | Two 3' x 7' iron doors with a center bar receiver; plain or worked depending on skin. *Noble skin: relief-carved faces.* |
| **015** | Iron Door, Grated Window | 3' x 7' iron door with a 6" x 12" barred window at face height; cover may or may not be present. |
| **016** | Iron Door, Riveted | Thick riveted iron plate construction; noticeably heavier than standard; hinges are oversized. *Cave skin: treat as stone slab.* |
| **017** | Iron Door, Corroded | Surface heavily rusted; hinges seized; opens with sustained force or may require breaking. |
| **018** | Iron Door, No Handle | Flat iron face, no visible latch. Push-pressure mechanism, or handle has been deliberately removed. |
| **019** | Iron Door, Inscribed | Surface etched with warnings, ward sigils, a faction mark, or a maker's stamp. Mechanically standard. |
| **020** | Iron Door, Knee Hatch | Full 3' x 7' iron door plus a separate 18" square iron hatch at knee height, separately latched. |
| **021** | Stone Slab, Pivoting | Single stone slab pivots on a central pin; 3' x 7', flush with the surrounding wall when closed. |
| **022** | Double Stone Slabs | Two 3' x 7' stone slabs on heavy iron hinges; bar socket carved directly into the stone frame. |
| **023** | Stone Door, No Handle | No external latch or handle visible; opens via push-pressure, counterweight, or hidden trigger. |
| **024** | Stone Door, Disguised | Carved to match the surrounding wall; findable on close inspection but not immediately obvious. |
| **025** | Stone Door, Massive | Single slab, 5' x 8', visibly enormous; requires significant strength or a lever mechanism to move. |
| **026** | Stone Door, Barred | Standard stone slab with an iron bar socket cut into the frame on one side. *Bar may or may not be present — see state roll.* |
| **027** | Iron Portcullis | Single iron portcullis, 8' wide, 8' high; winch or counterweight mechanism mounted on the near wall. |
| **028** | Double Portcullis | Two portcullises 10' apart in a short passage, forming a kill-box; each on a separate mechanism. |
| **029** | Portcullis + Wooden Door | Iron portcullis as the outer layer; a wooden door set 5' behind it. *Fortress skin: iron door behind the portcullis.* |
| **030** | Portcullis, Rusted | Iron portcullis; mechanism frozen with age — roll 1d2: 1 = stuck open, 2 = stuck closed; ignores state roll. |
| **031** | Stone Portcullis | Heavier than iron; mechanism is a large counterweight system; raises slowly and loudly. |
| **032** | Main: Portcullis / Side: Wooden Postern | Primary entrance is a portcullis; a 2' x 6' wooden postern set in the same wall for personnel access. |
| **033** | Main: Iron Door / Side: Crawlspace Hatch | Primary iron door, plus a 2' x 2' iron hatch set at floor level in the adjacent wall; separate latch. |
| **034** | Main: Double Wooden / Flanking: Iron Gates | Double wooden main doors; two narrow iron-barred alcove gates flank the opening on both sides. |
| **035** | Main: Iron Door / Alt: Collapsed Section | Primary iron door, plus a 4' x 4' collapsed wall section 10' away offering an alternate route. |
| **036** | Main: Stone Slab / Side: Wooden Postern | Massive stone slab as main door; a small 2' x 6' wooden postern beside it. *Fortress skin: iron postern.* |
| **037** | Main: Double Iron / Secondary: Floor Hatch | Double iron main doors plus a 2' x 2' floor hatch 5' away leading to a crawl passage below. |
| **038** | Natural Cave Mouth, Narrow | Irregular 4' x 5' cave opening; requires turning sideways or ducking to pass. *Fortress skin: rough-cut with an iron frame.* |
| **039** | Natural Fissure | Vertical crack in the rock, 2' wide, 6' high; tight but passable. |
| **040** | Cave Mouth, Submerged | Opening partially or fully below water level; requires swimming or draining to use. *Fortress skin: treat as a sluice gate.* |
| **041** | Cave Mouth, Stalactite Fringe | Wide irregular cave opening (8' x 6'); stalactites reduce effective clearance to 5'. |
| **042** | Heavy Curtain | Thick fabric or hide hanging, floor to ceiling; no mechanical door, but announces movement when disturbed. *Noble skin: rich tapestry. Cave skin: matted moss or hide.* |
| **043** | Chain or Bead Curtain | Hanging chains or pierced metal discs; no barrier to movement but audible when passed through. |
| **044** | Iron Grille, Fixed | Fixed iron bars across the full opening, 3" gaps; bars are mortared in place; requires force or magic to pass. |
| **045** | Wooden Lattice Screen | Decorative or functional wooden screen filling the opening; relatively easy to force or dismantle. |
| **046** | Arcane Barrier | Shimmering field across the opening; no physical door frame. Requires a specific condition, key, or word to dismiss. |
| **047** | Concealed Panel | No visible door. A section of wall depresses, slides, or rotates on a hidden mechanism; DC to detect and locate trigger. |
| **048** | False Wall, Full | No seam visible without close inspection. Section swings or pivots; higher DC to detect than a concealed panel. |
| **049** | Trapdoor, Concealed | Access point in the floor; disguised with fitted stone or boards matching the surrounding surface; DC to detect. |
| **050** | Rotating Fixture | A bookcase, weapon rack, or mounted statue rotates on a hidden pivot to reveal a passage; DC to detect the mechanism. |
