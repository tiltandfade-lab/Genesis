## Material kit: current status

### Locked headline

- maintained overgrown old/reclaimed stone;
- operational paths, threshold, observation, drainage, and clearances remain maintained;
- overgrowth is condition/history, not automatically Lost World, abandonment, culture, or realm.

### Strong derived family

- coursed operational stone, local-rubble operational stone, and dressed operational stone;
- packed road/working apron with site-specific traffic clearing kept out of the base tile;
- quiet grass/soil with broad fields;
- geological rock/cliff visibly distinct from masonry;
- structural timber and forged iron;
- one roof/weather parent seed pending the exact roof choice;
- maintained-overgrowth affinity masks for damp, moss/lichen, crevice growth, cleared use, and
  repair suppression;
- cutaway/interior cap derivative and trim-role source strips;
- exact first trim layout: plain, base-course, cornice-belt, coping-cap, stair-nosing, and
  curb-retaining bands in one stable 1024 master/512 runtime fold;
- deterministic exposure, drainage, slope, traffic, and repair inputs;
- pinned Material Maker 1.3 graph, custom-node, mutation, export, and taste-card contract.

### Still missing

- roof material and weather cover;
- exact repair narrative;
- overgrowth intensity band;
- lighting/weather/time condition;
- palette/value script after gameplay-scale material cards;
- taste-pass evidence and calibrated parameter ranges for every specified graph family.
- trim diagnostic, packed-sheet, corner/endpoint, mip/gutter, changed-seed, and cultural A/B
  evidence.

### Historical-reference narrowing

The visual pack in `../historical-reference/` makes one hybrid precise enough to test without
turning it into arbitrary mixture: irregular/rubble wall fields with dressed stones reserved for
openings, corners, caps, drains, thresholds, and other load/wear-critical roles. It remains
`WORKING`, not `LOCKED`, until it is compared with a more coursed family at gameplay scale.

Maintained-overgrowth presentation must not copy the failed roofs, unsafe edges, blocked access, or
wholesale collapse visible in some surviving monuments. Reference damage may teach chronological
layers; the operating Guard Post must show clearing, drainage, weatherproofing, and concentrated
recent repair.

## Layout and generation order: current status

### Locked principle

Meaning is rolled first; geometry cannot invent it. Generation is deterministic, versioned,
bounded, replayable, and persistent after commitment.

### Working construction sequence

1. Roll site meaning and relational archetype.
2. Resolve the stage envelope and composed edges.
3. Resolve road entry/exit continuations.
4. Generate road-corridor candidates.
5. Generate broad elevation/ground masses relative to the road.
6. Score causal post anchors.
7. Derive retaining walls, connectors, foundations, and rock-boundary slots.
8. Reserve gameplay footing, circulation, deployment, objective, flank, cover, and retreat.
9. Assemble surface frames, material roles, sockets, and chronological layers.
10. Project deterministic maintained-overgrowth fields.
11. Validate the fixed production view and derive cutaway.
12. Select, receipt, version, commit, and persist one legal candidate.

This ordering is technically consistent with current Genesis authority and the FFT translation
study, but Adam has not explicitly locked it as the Guard Post build specification.
