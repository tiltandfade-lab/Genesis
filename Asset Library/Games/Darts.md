**Playing a Game of Darts** If your characters are participating in a literal game of darts (which would be a perfect fit for the "raucous dive" tavern you just generated!), the _Dungeon Master's Guide_ states that you can simply use **attack rolls** to resolve noncombat activities such as a game of darts or an archery contest. You would have the players roll their standard ranged attack (1d20 + Dexterity or Strength modifier + proficiency bonus if they are proficient with darts) against a set AC for the dartboard to see who gets the best score.
