#supernatural-charm
> **A minor supernatural gift — a Charm.** A small, finite magical favor, granted by a thing the world owes you: a touched shrine, a discovery earned, a debt paid by something not-quite-mortal, a sip from the wrong cup. Distinct from loot (it has no form to steal) and from a [[#supernatural-blessing|Blessing]] (which lasts). Most charms are **one-use, then spent** — the world settles its account and the magic is gone. The **DM rolls this instead of inventing a reward** when the fiction grants a small boon. Spice-graded (Fork ceiling — never Volatile/Mythic; for world-marking favor roll a Blessing). IP-clean Genesis-native set (structure mined from XGtE supernatural gifts, authored fresh 2026-06-28).
