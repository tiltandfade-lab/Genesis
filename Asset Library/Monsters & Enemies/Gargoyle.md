*p. 128 MM 2024*

In any campaign setting—whether it’s a terrifying gothic metropolis, a whimsical wizard’s academy, or a treacherous dungeon—gargoyles serve as the ultimate ambush predators. Originating from the Elemental Planes, these creatures are malicious, winged terrors made of living stone. They delight in the fear they cause by breaking their motionless facades to strike unsuspecting prey.

Because they do not need to eat or drink, gargoyles have infinite patience. They can stand motionless for days, years, or even centuries, perfectly blending in with standard masonry. While they are inherently evil and love to inflict pain, their varying levels of intelligence mean they are frequently hired, bound, or bullied into serving as guardians for stronger villains or powerful mages.

Here is the 2024-compatible 5e stat block, followed by modular d10 tables to help you adapt their appearances and ambush methods to fit any campaign tone.

### Gargoyle

_Medium Elemental, Chaotic Evil_

- **Armor Class:** 15 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 67 (9d8 + 27)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., fly 60 ft.
- **STR:** 15 (+2) | **DEX:** 11 (+0) | **CON:** 16 (+3) | **INT:** 6 (-2) | **WIS:** 11 (+0) | **CHA:** 7 (-2)
- **Skills:** Stealth +4
- **Damage Immunities:** Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Exhaustion, petrified, poisoned
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Primordial (Terran)
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

**Traits**

- **Flyby:** The gargoyle doesn't provoke an Opportunity Attack when it flies out of an enemy's reach.

**Actions**

- **Multiattack:** The gargoyle makes two Claw attacks.
- **Claw:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 7 (2d4 + 2) slashing damage.

### d10 Camouflage & Ambush Methods

Because of their _False Appearance_, gargoyles almost always strike with the element of surprise. They love to toy with their prey and use their environment to deadly—or occasionally hilarious—effect.

|d10|Ambush Tactic|Narrative & Mechanical Execution|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**1**|**The Drop Attack**|**Aggressive:** The gargoyle waits on a rooftop 60 feet up. When the party walks below, it drops off the ledge, using gravity to crash into the weakest-looking party member.|
|**2**|**Musical Pedestals**|**Paranoia/Humorous:** The party enters a room with several empty pedestals and a few statues. Every time the party looks away or the lights flicker, the gargoyles silently move to different pedestals to mess with the players' heads before attacking.|
|**3**|**The Phony Replacement**|**Investigation:** The PCs find a destroyed, mundane statue shoved into a closet. The gargoyle smashed the real artwork and took its place in the grand hall to ambush the noble of the house.|
|**4**|**The Cathedral Choir**|**Suspense:** The party must climb the exterior of a massive temple lined with exactly 50 identical stone gargoyles. Only 1d4 of them are alive, forcing the party to nervously edge past every single one.|
|**5**|**The Fountain Trap**|**Tactical:** The gargoyles are submerged at the bottom of a murky, decorative fountain. They wait until someone reaches into the water to grab a coin before striking to pull the victim under.|
|**6**|**The Door Knocker**|**Jump Scare:** A Small-sized gargoyle is curled up tightly, holding a heavy iron ring in its mouth, posing as an ornate door knocker. When a PC reaches for the ring, it bites them.|
|**7**|**The Plunging Bridge**|**Lethal Terrain:** The gargoyles are carved into the support pillars of a narrow stone bridge over a chasm. They attempt to Grapple the PCs and shove them over the edge into the abyss.|
|**8**|**The Silent Follower**|**Horror:** The gargoyle refuses to move while being looked at. If the party leaves it behind, it silently stalks them through the dungeon, freezing into a decorative pose the absolute second a PC turns around with a torch.|
|**9**|**The Rubble Pile**|**Deceptive:** The gargoyle pretends to be a broken, defeated statue lying in a pile of debris. It waits for opportunistic adventurers to start looting the area before it reassembles itself and strikes.|
|**10**|**The Courier's Bane**|**Urban Intrigue:** The gargoyles have been hired by a local thieves' guild to act as a silent rooftop surveillance network, tracking the party's movements across the city and reporting back to their master.|
