| d12     | Urban Exit State                                                                                          |
| :------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1**   | **The district returns to normal uncomfortably fast** — shopkeepers sweep blood from their storefronts by evening. Merchants laugh off what happened. No one asks questions. It's almost insulting how quickly people move on. |
| **2**   | **A power vacuum emerges and someone obvious fills it** — a rival gang, merchant house, or corrupt official. You see their banners going up within days. The district trades one tyrant for another. |
| **3**   | **You become the neighborhood legend — for better or worse** — children play-act your deeds in the streets. Your arrival at a tavern causes conversations to stop. Favors are owed, and everyone remembers. |
| **4**   | **The district is scarred but defiant** — buildings are scorched, storefronts boarded, streets cracked. People rebuild with grim determination. There's a toughness in the air now, not quite healing. |
| **5**   | **Trust shatters like glass** — neighbors eye each other with suspicion. The authority that was supposed to protect them failed. Informal networks form. People lock their doors. The community fractures. |
| **6**   | **Someone else claims the victory** — a local noble, the city guard, a merchant consortium. Your name fades while others grow fat on the credit. The district celebrates the wrong heroes. |
| **7**   | **An eerie quiet settles in** — fewer people on the streets than before. Shops close early. Conversations happen in whispers. It's as if the entire district is holding its breath, waiting for something worse. |
| **8**   | **A strange alliance forms in the aftermath** — former rivals work together. A merchant house pairs with the street syndicate. Self-preservation has redrawn every line. The political landscape shifts in unexpected ways. |
| **9**   | **The district becomes a pilgrimage site for the desperate** — the sick, the seeking, the broken come hoping for miracles or answers. Your actions have given the place an almost sacred weight. |
| **10**  | **Authorities clamp down hard** — new checkpoints, more guards, curfews. The district becomes a tinderbox. Resentment builds. Whatever peace you restored will come at the cost of freedom. |
| **11**  | **Life returns, but it's different** — new faces moving in, old families leaving. The district shifts its character. What was once familiar is slowly becoming foreign. The old world is already fading. |
| **12**  | **The wound never quite closes** — a persistent wrongness lingers. Strange occurrences. Unexplained noises at night. Maybe something was released, or maybe the district simply remembers. Either way, unfinished business hangs in the air. |
^urban-exit-state
